# Arab

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the second group of the hill country (Joshua 15:52), one of the *nine cities with their villages* of the second hill-country group (52–54). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
