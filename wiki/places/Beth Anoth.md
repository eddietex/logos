# Beth Anoth

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the fourth group of the hill country (Joshua 15:59), one of the *six cities with their villages* of the fourth hill-country group (58–59). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
