# Gibeath

## Overview

Named among Benjamin’s second group of fourteen cities with their villages (28). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied. It is not silently merged with Gibeah in Judah (Joshua 15:57). Nor is it merged with [[Gibeah (Benjamin)]], the town of Judges 19, though the two are commonly identified; the wiki keeps the listed name and the narrated town apart until the text joins them.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.
