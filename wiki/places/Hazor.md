# Hazor

## Overview

### Jabin king of Hazor at peace with Heber — Judges 4:17

*There was peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite* (4:17); 4:23–24
then report Jabin subdued and destroyed. Nothing is said of the city itself. See [[Jael and Sisera]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:17–24.

### Jabin reigns again — Judges 4:2

*Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor* (4:2). Joshua had burned Hazor and killed its king,
also called Jabin (Joshua 11:1, 10–13). Judges does not say the city was rebuilt; it has a king there
again, with nine hundred chariots of iron. See [[Jabin (of Deborah's time)]] and [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Joshua 11:1–13.

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

A Hazor is listed among Naphtali’s fortified cities (36). Whether it is Jabin’s city, burned in 11:10–13, and whether it had been rebuilt, the verse does not say; the notice is kept here because both are northern, without asserting identity.

See [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48; comparisons as cited.

Joshua 15:23–25 lists a southern Hazor, Hazor Hadattah, and Kerioth Hezron *also called Hazor*; they have their own pages, starting with [[Hazor (Judah)]], and are not this city.

Jabin’s royal city and the former head of the allied kingdoms (1, 10). Joshua kills its king and inhabitants and burns it (10–11). Among the cities standing on their mounds, Hazor alone is burned (13).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Its king, Jabin, is counted (12:19). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
