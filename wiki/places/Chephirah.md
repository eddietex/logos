# Chephirah

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

Included in Benjamin’s second city group (26), alongside other treaty cities from Joshua 9:17. Territorial assignment does not narrate a breach of the oath.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

One of the four neighboring cities whose identity exposes the envoys’ claim of distant origin
(16–17). The chapter gives no independent description or modern location.
