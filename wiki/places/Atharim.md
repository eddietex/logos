# Atharim

## Overview

The way by which Israel approaches in Numbers 21:1. The king of [[Arad]] hears of
the approach and attacks. This page records the WEB's place-name without assigning a
translation, modern location, or exact route. The passage locates the king in [[The Negev]],
but gives no itinerary for Atharim itself.
