# Beer (well in Moab)

## Overview

The well where Yahweh tells Moses to assemble the people and promises water (16). Israel sings of
the well dug by princes and nobles (17–18). This page does not identify it with another similarly
named biblical well.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:10–20.

**Renamed at Judges 9:21.** This page was `Beer` until Jotham fled *to Beer* (Judges 9:21). Nothing in
the text places that Beer in Moab or ties it to this well, so the two have separate pages; see
[[Beer (Jotham's refuge)]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 9:21.
