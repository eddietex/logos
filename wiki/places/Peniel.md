# Peniel

## Overview

*"Face of God"* — ***pānîm*** plus ***ʾēl***. Jacob names it himself, in the naming formula this wiki
tracks throughout Genesis, immediately after the wrestling ends: *"I have seen God face to face, and
my life is preserved"* (32:30). **It is the one place-name in the family's story given for what has
just happened rather than for what has not** — contrast [[Mahanaim]], named a few verses earlier for
an arithmetic Jacob had not yet performed.

**The Hebrew spells the name two ways within two verses**: ***Pənîʾēl*** at the naming itself (32:30)
and ***Pənûʾēl*** in the very next verse, describing Jacob's departure (32:31) — the same site later
called *Penuel* without variation (Judges 8:8–9, 17; 1 Kings 12:25). The WEB renders both occurrences
*Peniel*; this page keeps that spelling as the page title and records the fluctuation as a feature of
the Hebrew text itself, not of the translation. Its exact location, like Mahanaim's, is not fixed by
archaeology, only placed generally on the Jabbok in Gilead.

Later, at Judges 8:8–9 and 17, Gideon destroys the tower of Penuel for refusing his army bread —
Genesis 33:17 and Judges 8 are the only two texts that put anyone back at this site after this night.
