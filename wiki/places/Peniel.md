# Peniel

## Overview

*"Face of God"* — ***pānîm*** plus ***ʾēl***. Jacob names it himself, in the naming formula this wiki
tracks throughout Genesis, immediately after the wrestling ends: *"I have seen God face to face, and
my life is preserved"* (32:30). **It is the one place-name in the family's story given for what has
just happened rather than for what has not** — contrast [[Mahanaim]], named a few verses earlier for
an arithmetic Jacob had not yet performed.

**The Hebrew spells the name two ways within two verses**: ***Pənîʾēl*** at the naming itself (32:30)
and ***Pənûʾēl*** in the very next verse, describing Jacob's departure (32:31) — the same site later
called *Penuel* without variation (Judges 8:8–9, 17; 1 Kings 12:25). The WEB renders both occurrences
*Peniel*; this page keeps that spelling as the page title and records the fluctuation as a feature of
the Hebrew text itself, not of the translation. Its exact location, like Mahanaim's, is not fixed by
archaeology, only placed generally on the Jabbok in Gilead.

Later, at Judges 8:8–9 and 17, Gideon destroys the tower of Penuel for refusing his army bread, and
Jeroboam *built Penuel* again (1 Kings 12:25, WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested).
**Correction (2026-11-15):** this page used to say that *Genesis 33:17 and Judges 8 are the only two texts
that put anyone back at this site after this night*. Genesis 33:17 is about Succoth, not Peniel, and
1 Kings 12:25 also names Penuel. The sentence was withdrawn.

### The tower of Penuel — Judges 8:8–9, 17

*He went up there to Penuel, and spoke to them in the same way; and the men of Penuel answered him as the
men of Succoth had answered* (8:8). Gideon threatens, *When I come again in peace, I will break down this
tower* (8:9), and on his return *He broke down the tower of Penuel, and killed the men of the city* (8:17).
The second half was not threatened. The Hebrew spelling in Judges is *Pənûʾēl* throughout (WLC). Gideon
had said of the angel *I have seen Yahweh's angel face to face* (6:22), with Jacob's *pānîm ʾel-pānîm*
(WLC), and the place Jacob named for seeing God face to face is the one he damages. Judges does not draw
the link. See [[Ephraim, Succoth, and Penuel]] and
[[Penuel and Succoth - Jacob's Road and Gideon's Pursuit]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 6:22; 8:8–9, 17;
Genesis 32:30–31; 33:17; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
