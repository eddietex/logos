# The Wilderness of Sin

## Overview

**The stretch of wilderness *"between Elim and Sinai"*, where the manna first fell and the first Sabbath was
kept.**

> **1** They took their journey from Elim, and all the congregation of the children of Israel came to the
> wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after their
> departing out of the land of Egypt. (Exodus 16:1)

**The page is titled *The Wilderness of Sin* rather than *Sin* because the theme page [[Sin]] holds the bare
title.** The Hebrew place-name ***sîn*** has nothing to do with the English word.

**It is named four times in the Torah** — Exodus 16:1; 17:1; and Numbers 33:11–12 (WLC lemma search,
Genesis–Deuteronomy) — **always as a stage of the march.** Numbers 33 puts a camp *"by the Red Sea"* between
Elim and the wilderness of Sin, and a stop at Dophkah after it (Numbers 33:10–12; by reference, *not yet
ingested*); Exodus goes straight from Elim to Sin and from Sin, *"by their journeys"*, to Rephidim (17:1). The
wiki records the two itineraries without harmonizing.

**The name has the same consonants as *Sinai* (*sînay*), and its first verse names both.** The WLC files them
under separate numbers (5512, 5514). **Many readers take the wilderness as named for the mountain, or both
for a common word** (reported, not checked in this pass); the wiki records the resemblance and draws nothing
from it. It is not to be confused with *"the wilderness of Zin"* (*ṣin*, with a different first letter) of
Numbers 13:21; 20:1; 27:14, near Kadesh (WLC lemma search, Numbers; *not yet ingested*).

**Everything Exodus says happened there is in one chapter**: the murmuring against Moses and Aaron, the
first appearance of *"Yahweh's glory … in the cloud"*, the quail, the manna, the omer, the first *Sabbath*,
and the jar laid up for the generations (16:1–36). See [[Manna and Quail]].

**Its date is the first exact date since the Passover.** *"The fifteenth day of the second month"* is a month
and a day after the lamb was killed on the fourteenth of the first (12:6), and Israel reaches Sinai *"in the
third month"* (19:1; *not yet ingested*).

**Where it is is not known.** It is usually placed on the western side of the Sinai peninsula, on the
assumption that Sinai lies in the south of it (reported, not checked in this pass); the wiki adopts no
location for either. See [[Horeb]].

## Events here

- The congregation murmurs against Moses and Aaron for bread — Exodus 16:2–3
- Yahweh's glory appears in the cloud, *"toward the wilderness"* — Exodus 16:10
- Quail cover the camp; manna lies with the dew — Exodus 16:13–15
- The first Sabbath: *"So the people rested on the seventh day"* — Exodus 16:30
- *Ahead*: Israel journeys on to Rephidim — Exodus 17:1; Numbers 33:12–14
