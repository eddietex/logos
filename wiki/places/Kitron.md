# Kitron

## Overview

One of Zebulun's two cities whose inhabitants were not driven out; *the Canaanites lived among them, and became subject to forced labor* (Judges 1:30, stored WEB). It is often identified with Kattath of Zebulun's list (Joshua 19:15), but the WLC's lemma tagging treats the two names as different words, and the identification is not made here; see [[Kattath]].

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:30–33; Joshua 19:15, 24–31;
the Hebrew name (*qiṭrôn*) read in the WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb).
