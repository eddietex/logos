# Tob

## Overview

### Where Jephthah fled — Judges 11:3, 5

*Then Jephthah fled from his brothers, and lived in the land of Tob. Outlaws joined up with Jephthah,
and they went out with him* (11:3). The elders of Gilead *went to get Jephthah out of the land of Tob*
(11:5). The Hebrew is *ʾereṣ ṭôb* (WLC). In the WLC files read for this ingest (Genesis, Numbers,
Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges), the place is named only in these two verses. The text gives no location
or direction; it is a place outside Gilead's community, and far enough away to be called a flight.
*Ṭôb* is also the ordinary Hebrew word *good*; the text makes nothing of it.

Its site is not given in the text, and no identification is made here.

See [[Jephthah Called from Tob]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 11:3, 5; Hebrew from the Westminster
Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
