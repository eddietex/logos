# Goiim in Gilgal

## Overview

Royal seat whose king is counted in Joshua 12:23, *the king of Goiim in Gilgal*. No battle is narrated and the phrase occurs nowhere else in the stored WEB. The page takes the whole phrase as its title to avoid two identifications the text does not make: with the Goiim whose king Tidal joins [[Chedorlaomer]] in Genesis 14:1, 9, and with [[Gilgal]], Israel's camp by the Jordan. Gilgal is linked below only as the name the phrase shares.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12:23 and Genesis 14:1, 9. No modern site is assigned.
