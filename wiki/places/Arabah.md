# Arabah

## Overview

### Leave Horeb and possess the land — Deuteronomy 1:6–8

Named again in Yahweh's remembered command (7), now as one region of the land to be entered rather than as part of the setting of the address. The list does not define its boundaries.

See [[Leave Horeb and Possess the Land]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:6–8.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

The wilderness setting of the address includes the Arabah opposite Suf (1). The verse gives regional
bearings rather than a modern map; no precise boundaries are assigned here.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.
