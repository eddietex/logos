# Arabah

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

The south border passes the side facing the Arabah northward and descends to it (18), before passing north of Beth Hoglah (19).

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

### The whole land taken — Joshua 11:16–23

The Arabah is one of the regions named in the summary of land Joshua took (16).

See [[The Whole Land Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23.

### The northern coalition — Joshua 11:1–15

The summons includes kings in the Arabah south of Chinneroth (2). This describes coalition members rather than supplying an additional march itinerary.

See [[The Northern Kings at the Waters of Merom]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.

### The capture of Ai — Joshua 8:1–29

The king of Ai leads his men out *at the time appointed, before the Arabah* (14) — facing east toward
the Jordan rift as he pursues. Israel's feigned flight goes *by the way of the wilderness* (15). The
exact sense of *before the Arabah* (a direction, or a meeting place) is not explained.

See [[The Capture of Ai]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 8:1–29; WLC (morphhb) where Hebrew is named.

### The Canaanites of the Arabah — Deuteronomy 11:18–32

Gerizim and Ebal are located *"in the land of the Canaanites who dwell in the Arabah"* (30). The mountains stand in the central hill country, and the verse does not explain how the Arabah locates them.

See [[Set These Words Before You]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 11:18–32.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

The summary includes all the Arabah east of the Jordan as far as its sea below Pisgah (49). These are territorial bearings rather than a narrated journey.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

The grant includes the Arabah and the Jordan’s border from Chinnereth to the Salt Sea beneath
Pisgah’s eastern slopes (17).

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

### Protected neighbors and the wilderness generation — Deuteronomy 2:1–23

The Arabah road is named with Elath and Ezion Geber in the movement past Seir toward Moab (8). No
exact modern route is assigned.

See [[Around Seir and Past Moab and Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.

### Leave Horeb and possess the land — Deuteronomy 1:6–8

Named again in Yahweh's remembered command (7), now as one region of the land to be entered rather than as part of the setting of the address. The list does not define its boundaries.

See [[Leave Horeb and Possess the Land]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:6–8.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

The wilderness setting of the address includes the Arabah opposite Suf (1). The verse gives regional
bearings rather than a modern map; no precise boundaries are assigned here.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** The east-bank Arabah belongs to Sihon's realm down to the Salt Sea (12:1, 3), and *the Arabah* is also one of the western regions Joshua gives the tribes (8). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
