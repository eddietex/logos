# The Ascent of Akrabbim

## Overview

### The Amorite border — Judges 1:36

*The border of the Amorites was from the ascent of Akrabbim, from the rock, and upward* (36), the last verse of Judges 1. Why a southern border landmark closes a passage about Dan and the Amorites is not explained. A reading *Edomite* is reported elsewhere and has not been checked.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

### Judah’s borders — Joshua 15:1–12

The south border runs south of this ascent before passing along Zin (3), repeating a landmark of Numbers 34:4.

See [[The Borders of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12.

The southern border turns south of this ascent before passing to Zin.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34:4. No precise modern site is assigned here.
