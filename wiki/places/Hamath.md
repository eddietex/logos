# Hamath

## Overview

A city-state of Syria on the Orontes, and in the Bible the northern reference point of the land.
Numbers 13:21 sends the spies as far as *the entrance of Hamath*, *ləbōʾ ḥămāt* (WLC). The same
phrase marks the land's north border at Numbers 34:8 and Solomon's kingdom at 1 Kings 8:65: *from
the entrance of Hamath to the brook of Egypt*. Whether *ləbōʾ* is a common word, *the way into*, or
the name of a town, *Lebo*, is debated (recalled). The tags here treat it as the verb *come*. Rahlfs
has *εἰσπορευομένων Εφααθ*.

**From the Table of Nations on.** Genesis 10:18 has the Hamathites among the sons of Canaan, one of
the families of [[Canaan (son of Ham)]]. So verse 21's far point lies in the Canaanite family of
Genesis 10, though it lies beyond any border Israel held for long. The spies are said to have walked
the whole promised frontier and back in forty days. See tension 3 of [[The Twelve Spies]].

Later texts (WEB and tags; *not yet ingested*): David's dealings with Toi king of Hamath (2 Samuel
8:9), Jeroboam II's restoration from the entrance of Hamath (2 Kings 14:25), Assyrian conquest (2
Kings 18:34; Isaiah 10:9), and Ezekiel's northern border (Ezekiel 47:16–20).
