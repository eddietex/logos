# Valley of Achor

## Overview

### The capture of Ai — Joshua 8:1–29

The next chapter matches Achor's heap with another: the king of Ai is covered with *a great heap of
stones that remains to this day* (29), in the same Hebrew words as 7:26 (WLC). With Achor dealt
with, the campaign resumes at Ai in the first verse of the chapter.

See [[The Capture of Ai]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 8:1–29; WLC (morphhb) where Hebrew is named.

The valley where [[Achan]] and his household were stoned and burned, and where a great heap of stones
was raised over him *that remains to this day* (Joshua 7:24–26). The name is explained by the event:
*Why have you troubled us? Yahweh will trouble you today* (7:25), and *Therefore the name of that place
was called "The valley of Achor" to this day* (7:26). *ʿĒmeq ʿāḵôr* (WLC 7:24, 26) means *valley of
trouble*, from the verb *ʿāḵar* used in verse 25. See [[Names and Naming]].

**Location.** The text says only that Israel *brought them up* to it from the camp near Jericho (7:24).
Joshua 15:7 later names it on Judah's northern border (stored WEB, not yet ingested); a precise modern
identification is not attempted here. Proposed sites are debated and none is asserted.

**The valley's second life.** The prophets take the name of judgment and reverse it. Hosea 2:15:
*the valley of Achor for a door of hope* (WEB, verified at bible-api.com, 2026-11-10). Isaiah 65:10:
*the valley of Achor a place for herds to lie down in, for my people who have sought me* (same). See
[[The Valley of Achor - Trouble Turned to a Door of Hope]].
