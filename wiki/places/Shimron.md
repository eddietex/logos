# Shimron

## Overview

### Three tribal lots — Joshua 19:1–23

Named in Zebulun’s city register, whose stated total is twelve cities with villages (15–16). No individual capture or modern identification is supplied.

See [[The Lots of Simeon, Zebulun, and Issachar]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23; comparisons as cited.

Royal city whose unnamed king receives Jabin’s summons (1). The collective capture of the allied cities follows in verse 12, without an individual siege account.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** *The king of Shimron Meron, one* (12:20) is linked here as probably the same royal city as 11:1's Shimron, since both stand beside Madon, Hazor, and Achshaph. The text does not equate the two names. Joshua 19:15 names a Shimron among Zebulun's cities; it is not identified here. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
