# Valley of Elah

## Overview

### Israel's camp, and the valley between — 1 Samuel 17:1–11

*Saul and the men of Israel were gathered together, and encamped in the valley of Elah, and set the battle in array
against the Philistines* (17:2). The Hebrew is *bəʿēmeq hāʾēlâ* (WLC). The Philistines hold the mountain on one side
and Israel the mountain on the other, *and there was a valley between them* (17:3); the verse calls that gap *haggay*,
a different word from the *ʿēmeq* of verse 2 (WLC). Whether they name the same ground is not stated. Into it comes
[[Goliath]], *the man of the between* (17:4). *ʾĒlâ* is the word for a terebinth or large tree (recalled, not checked);
in the Hebrew files checked, morphhb tags the same lemma (425) as the Edomite chief Elah (Genesis 36:41) and this
valley (17:2, 19; 21:10 WLC = 21:9 WEB). No modern site is assigned.

The valley is remembered by name when [[Ahimelech (son of Ahitub)]] at [[Nob]] gives David *the sword of Goliath the
Philistine, whom you killed in the valley of Elah* (21:9; see [[David at Nob]]).

### The battle line — 1 Samuel 17:19–24

*Saul, and they, and all the men of Israel, were in the valley of Elah, fighting with the Philistines* (17:19). David
arrives at *the place of the wagons*, *hammaʿgālâ* (17:20, WLC), as the army goes out shouting to the line, and
*Israel and the Philistines put the battle in array, army against army* (17:21). There he hears Goliath, and Israel
flees from the man (17:23–24). *Maʿgālâ* occurs in the Hebrew files checked otherwise only of Saul's camp in 26:5, 7
(WLC). See [[David Comes to the Camp]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 17:1–3, 19–24; 21:9. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-21.

### The fight, and the pursuit west — 1 Samuel 17:41–53

Goliath comes near, David runs *toward the army*, *hammaʿărāḵâ*, the battle line, to meet him (17:48, WLC), and the
stone fells the champion between the two lines (17:49). From the valley Israel and Judah pursue the Philistines *as
far as Gai and to the gates of Ekron*, along the road to Shaaraim, to Gath (17:52), out of the hill country toward the
Philistine plain. The priest at [[Nob]] later names the place: *the sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you killed in
the valley of Elah* (21:9; see [[David at Nob]]). See [[David Kills Goliath]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 17:41–53; 21:9. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-21.

### After the fight — 1 Samuel 17:55–18:5

The narrator returns to the field at the moment *Saul saw David go out against the Philistine* (17:55): Abner brings
David to Saul *as David returned from the slaughter*, with the head in his hand (17:57). The place is not named again in
the pericope, and 18:5 moves David out to wherever Saul sends him. See [[Jonathan Loves David]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 17:55–18:5.
