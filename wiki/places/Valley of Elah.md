# Valley of Elah

## Overview

### Israel's camp, and the valley between — 1 Samuel 17:1–11

*Saul and the men of Israel were gathered together, and encamped in the valley of Elah, and set the battle in array
against the Philistines* (17:2). The Hebrew is *bəʿēmeq hāʾēlâ* (WLC). The Philistines hold the mountain on one side
and Israel the mountain on the other, *and there was a valley between them* (17:3); the verse calls that gap *haggay*,
a different word from the *ʿēmeq* of verse 2 (WLC). Whether they name the same ground is not stated. Into it comes
[[Goliath]], *the man of the between* (17:4). *ʾĒlâ* is the word for a terebinth or large tree (recalled, not checked);
in the Hebrew files checked, morphhb tags the same lemma (425) as the Edomite chief Elah (Genesis 36:41) and this
valley (17:2, 19; 21:10 WLC = 21:9 WEB). No modern site is assigned.

The valley is remembered by name when the priest at Nob gives David *the sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you
killed in the valley of Elah* (21:9, stored WEB; not yet ingested).
