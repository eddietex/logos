# The Winepress of Zeeb

## Overview

*Zeeb they killed at Zeeb's wine press* (Judges 7:25). The place where the Midianite prince [[Zeeb]] was
killed by Ephraim. The noun is *yeqeb*, the wine vat (WLC), not the *gat* of 6:11 where Gideon hid his
wheat; WEB has *wine press* for both. The text does not say whether the place was named for him. The
site is not identified here.

Source: stored WEB Judges 6:11; 7:25; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
