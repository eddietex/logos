# Kittim

## Overview

The coast from which ships come to afflict Asshur and Eber in Numbers 24:24. The oracle does not
identify a fleet, ruler, or date. No precise later empire is assigned to the name in this ingest.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.
