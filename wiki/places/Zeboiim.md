# Zeboiim

## Overview

Zeboiim is one of the four cities whose overthrow illustrates the threatened devastation of Israel’s land (Deuteronomy 29:23). The verse names it alongside Sodom and Gomorrah; Genesis 19:24–25 narrates the overthrow of the cities and plain but does not name Zeboiim individually. No precise modern location is assigned here.
