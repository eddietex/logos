# Lehi

## Overview

### Where the jawbone was used — Judges 15:9–19

The Philistines go up, camp in [[Judah]], and *spread themselves in Lehi* (Judges 15:9). The men of Judah
bring [[Samson]] there bound, and *When he came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted as they met him* (14).
The Spirit comes on him, the ropes melt, and he kills a thousand men with *a fresh jawbone of a donkey*
(14–16). He throws the jawbone away, *and that place was called Ramath Lehi* (17). Then he is very thirsty
and calls on Yahweh, and *God split the hollow place that is in Lehi, and water came out of it*; the spring
is [[En Hakkore]], *which is in Lehi, to this day* (18–19).

**The name is the word for *jaw*.** *Leḥî* (WLC lemma 3896 as a place, 3895 as the common noun) is
spelled the same as *the jawbone*, *ləḥî ḥămôr* (15). The place is already called Lehi in 9 and 14,
before Samson picks up the jawbone, so the story does not explain the name; it gives a second one,
*Ramath Lehi* (17, lemma 7437, tagged as one proper noun). That is usually rendered *height of the jaw*;
the rendering is reported, not checked. The wiki treats Ramath Lehi as another name for Lehi, not a
separate place.

**Where the hollow is.** In 19 the WLC data tags the first *balleḥî*, *in Lehi*, as the common noun
*jaw*, and the second as the place. The WEB reads both as the place. The wiki records the tagging and
does not decide.

The site is in or near Judah (9), within reach of the rock of Etam (11–14). It is not identified here.
At 2 Samuel 23:11 some propose reading *laḥayyâ*, *into a troop*, as *at Lehi*; that is reported, not
checked, and the WLC tags the word as *company* (lemma 2416).
