# Ashkelon

## Overview

### A golden tumor for Ashkelon — 1 Samuel 6:17

*These are the golden tumors which the Philistines returned for a trespass offering to Yahweh: for Ashdod
one, for Gaza one, for Ashkelon one, for Gath one, for Ekron one* (6:17). Ashkelon is a city the ark never visited; the offering
counts all five lordships, *for one plague was on you all, and on your lords* (6:4). See
[[The Ark Returns to Israel]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 6:4, 17.

### Thirty men for their clothes — Judges 14:19

*Yahweh's Spirit came mightily on him, and he went down to Ashkelon, and struck thirty men of them, and
took their plunder* (14:19). The city is Philistine; Judah's taking of it at 1:18 has not held. Samson
pays the solvers of his riddle with the garments of the dead. The text does not say why Ashkelon was
chosen; it lies some distance from Timnah, toward the coast (reported, not checked). See [[Samson's Riddle]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 1:18; 14:19.

### Taken by Judah — Judges 1:18

*Also Judah took Gaza with its border, and Ashkelon with its border, and Ekron with its border* (18).
Joshua 13:2–3 had listed the city with Gaza and Ekron among *the land that still remains*. The next verse
says Judah could not dislodge the people of the valley, and later Judges has two of them in Philistine
hands (Ashkelon at 14:19, Gaza at 16:1–2). It is often said that the Septuagint reads verse 18 as a
negative; that claim has not been checked here. The WLC reads *wayyilkōd*, *and he took*.

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

The Ashkelonites appear among the five Philistine lordships in land still remaining (Joshua 13:3). Ashkelon is the city-name used for this page; the verse itself names its inhabitants. No siege or modern site identification is supplied here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.
