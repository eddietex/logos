# Naioth

## Overview

### Where David stays with Samuel — 1 Samuel 19:18–24

*He and Samuel went and lived in Naioth* (19:18). David has fled from Saul's house to Samuel *at Ramah*, and the two
stay together here. Saul is told *Behold, David is at Naioth in Ramah* (19:19) and sends three groups of messengers to
seize him. Each group sees *the company of the prophets prophesying, and Samuel standing as head over them*, and
prophesies (19:20–21). Saul then comes himself. The Spirit of God comes on him on the road *until he came to Naioth in
Ramah* (19:23), and there he strips, prophesies before Samuel, and lies naked all day and night (19:24). See
[[Saul Among the Prophets at Naioth]].

**Name and location.** In the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, Ruth; WLC), the name occurs in five
verses, 1 Samuel 19:18, 19, 22, 23, and 20:1, and nowhere else. Each time the consonantal text and the vocalization
differ: written *nwyt* (and *nwwt* at 20:1), read *nāyôṯ*. The text always calls it *Naioth in Ramah* (19:19, 22, 23;
20:1), which suggests a place inside or beside Samuel's town rather than a separate town. The name is often taken to
mean *dwellings* or *pastures*, and the place as a settlement of the prophets Samuel leads (recalled, not checked). The
text says only that the prophets were there with Samuel at their head (19:20). No site is identified.
