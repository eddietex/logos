# Pirathon

## Overview

*Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite died, and was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in
the hill country of the Amalekites* (Judges 12:15). The judge's town, given twice as his epithet,
*the Pirathonite* (12:13, 15), and once as his grave.

In the WLC books of Genesis, Numbers, Joshua, and Judges, *pirʿātôn* (lemma 6552) and the gentilic
*pirʿātônî* (6553) occur only in this notice. Its site is not given in the text. Outside these books, *Benaiah a
Pirathonite* is listed among David's mighty men (2 Samuel 23:30, WEB checked at bible-api.com
2026-11-16; not yet ingested).

*In the hill country of the Amalekites*, *bəhar hāʿămālēqî* (WLC), places it in a district of
Ephraim's hill country carrying an Amalekite name. The Song of Deborah's *Those whose root is in
Amalek came out of Ephraim* (5:14) is the only other link in Judges. What the name means, the text
does not say. See [[Amalek]].

See [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 5:14; 12:13–15; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-16.
