# Hazor Hadattah

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the farthest cities of Judah toward the border of Edom in the South (Joshua 15:25), one of the names in the southern group that verse 32 totals as *twenty-nine* cities with their villages, though thirty-six names are listed (21–32). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Listed as a separate entry beside [[Hazor (Judah)]] (23) and [[Kerioth Hezron]] (25). The wiki assigns no meaning to the second element and does not merge the names.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
