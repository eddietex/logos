# Peor

## Overview

### Baal Peor — Numbers 25:1–18

Numbers 25 uses Peor for the affair associated with Baal Peor (18). The height in Numbers 23:28,
the deity Baal Peor, and Israel’s named campsite Shittim are related names and settings, not
interchangeable locations.

See [[Baal Peor and the Zeal of Phinehas]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 25:1–18.


The height overlooking the desert to which Balak brings Balaam for the third sacrificial station
(Numbers 23:27–30). The name here denotes a height, not a personal deity. Its exact modern location
is not established by this text.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.
