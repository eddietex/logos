# Mount Halak

## Overview

The southern limit of Joshua's conquest summary: *from Mount Halak, that goes up to Seir* (11:17). Joshua 12:7 repeats it as the other end of the same span, from Baal Gad *even to Mount Halak*. The verse places it on the ascent toward Seir, the country linked on this wiki to [[Edom]]; it does not say the mountain lay inside Edom.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23. No modern identification is assigned.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** The southern limit of the western list, *Mount Halak, that goes up to Seir* (12:7), repeating 11:17 in reverse order. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
