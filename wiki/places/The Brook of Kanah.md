# The Brook of Kanah

## Overview

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

Manasseh’s boundary lies north of the brook and reaches the sea; Ephraim has cities among Manasseh’s
cities (9–10).

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

Watercourse along the westward boundary from Tappuah toward the sea (8). The text gives a direction and terminus but no modern identification.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.
