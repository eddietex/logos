# Rephidim

## Overview

**The last camp before Sinai, and the place where two things happen in one chapter: water from the rock and
Israel's first battle as a people.** Israel comes to it from the wilderness of Sin, *"by their journeys,
according to Yahweh's commandment … but there was no water for the people to drink"* (Exodus 17:1). There the
people quarrel with Moses and test Yahweh, and water comes out of *"the rock in Horeb"* (17:6). In the same
camp *"Amalek came and fought with Israel"* (17:8). From it they go on to the wilderness of Sinai (19:2).

***Rəpîdîm*** is plural in form. A meaning such as *resting places* or *supports* is sometimes proposed from
the root *r-p-d*, *to spread out* or *support* (a lexical suggestion not checked here); **the wiki records the suggestion and adopts nothing from it**,
and Exodus draws no wordplay from the name. **It is named five times in the Torah** — Exodus 17:1, 8; 19:2;
Numbers 33:14, 15 (WLC lemma search, Genesis–Deuteronomy).

**Numbers 33 reaches it in three stages that Exodus skips**: Sin, Dophkah, Alush, Rephidim — *"where there was
no water for the people to drink"* (Numbers 33:12–14; WEB; *not yet ingested*). **Numbers 33 mentions water at only two
camps** — Elim's *"twelve springs of water"* (33:9) and Rephidim's lack (WLC).

**Its location is not known**, and depends on where Sinai is placed. Under the traditional southern location,
Wadi Refayid or Wadi Feiran in the south-west of the Sinai peninsula are commonly proposed (reported from secondary
commentary, not checked here). The wiki records the options and adopts none (see [[Horeb]]).

**The geography of 17:6 is a puzzle the text leaves open**: the rock Moses strikes is *"the rock in Horeb"*,
but Israel leaves Rephidim before it reaches the wilderness of Sinai (19:2). See [[Water from the Rock]] for
the readings.

**The battle has no name of its own and is fought nowhere more specific than *"the top of the hill"***
(17:9–10). **The altar Moses builds after it is named, but the place is not** — unlike the rock, which gave the
camp *Massah* and *Meribah*. See [[The Battle with Amalek]].

**The place of the water was given its own names** — *"Massah, and Meribah"* (17:7). **Rephidim is the camp;
Massah and Meribah are what the people did there.** See [[Massah and Meribah]].
