# Mizpah (Gilead)

## Overview

### Jephthah made head — Judges 11:11

*Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people made him head and chief over them. Jephthah
spoke all his words before Yahweh in Mizpah* (11:11). The text does not say what the words were; the
wiki reads them as the agreement of 11:9–10 said again in Yahweh's presence. The elders swore by
Yahweh as *witness*, *šōmēaʿ*, *one who hears* (11:10, WLC). Laban's oath at the Mizpah of Genesis 31
used *ʿēd* (31:50, WLC). The two Mizpahs are still not identified here. See
[[Jephthah Called from Tob]].

### Israel's camp against Ammon — Judges 10:17

*The children of Israel assembled themselves together and encamped in Mizpah* (10:17), while Ammon
camps in Gilead. It is the place where the princes of Gilead ask *Who is the man who will begin to
fight against the children of Ammon?* (10:18). The Hebrew is *bammiṣpâ*, *in the watchpost* (WLC).

The next chapter makes it Jephthah's town. He speaks *all his words before Yahweh in Mizpah* (11:11),
and the text names it *Mizpah of Gilead* (11:29). Those verses are not yet ingested; they are quoted
from the stored WEB.

**Is it Jacob's Mizpah?** Genesis gives a *Mizpah* in the hill country of Gilead, the name added to
the heap of witness Jacob and Laban raised: *Yahweh watch between me and you* (Genesis 31:49). Judges
does not say that Israel's camp is at that heap, and the wiki does not identify them. *Miṣpâ* is a
common place name. See [[Galeed]]. Other Mizpahs in the wiki are different places:
[[Mizpah (under Hermon)]], [[Mizpah (lowland)]] of Judah, and [[Mizpeh]] of Benjamin. The site is
not given in the text.

See [[Israel's Repentance Under Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 31:49; Judges 10:17–18; 11:11,
29; Hebrew of Judges 10:17 from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
