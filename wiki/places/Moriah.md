# Moriah

## Overview

**The name occurs twice in the Bible, in books six hundred years apart, and the second occurrence is
the reason anyone knows where the first one is.**

- **Genesis 22:2** — *"go into the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the
  mountains which I will tell you of"*: a **land**, containing mountains, one of which will be
  indicated later.
- **2 Chronicles 3:1** — *"Solomon began to build Yahweh's house at Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where
  Yahweh appeared to David his father"*: a **mountain**, in Jerusalem, and the temple platform.

Genesis names a region and never names the mountain — the peak gets a name only after the fact, and
the name Abraham gives it is not *Moriah* but *Yahweh Will Provide* (22:14). **The land is named
before the event; the mountain is named by it.**

### Where it is, and how confident anyone can be

**The identification with the temple mount in Jerusalem rests on one verse and is otherwise
unsupported by Genesis.** 2 Chronicles 3:1 makes it explicitly, and every later Jewish and Christian
tradition follows; Genesis itself gives only a three-day walk from [[Beersheba]], which is about
fifty miles and fits Jerusalem well enough without singling it out. The chapter never says
*Jerusalem*, never mentions [[Salem]] or Melchizedek, and does not connect Moriah to 14:18 — which
would have been the obvious link to make if the author had it in view.

**The Samaritan Pentateuch reads a different word.** Where the Masoretic text has *ha-Môrîyâ*, the
Samaritan has *ha-Môreh*, **the land of Moreh** — which is the oak of Shechem where Abram built his
first altar (12:6) and which sits beside **Gerizim**, the Samaritan holy mountain. One consonant, and
the whole geography of the chapter moves ninety miles north. **The variant is old, it is not obviously
polemical in origin even though both communities used it polemically, and the wiki records it rather
than choosing.** See [[The Altar at Shechem and the Covenant at Shechem]].

### The etymology, and Genesis's own wordplay

*Môrîyâ* has no settled derivation. The three live proposals:

| reading | root | what it would mean |
|---|---|---|
| the one the chapter plays on | *rāʾâ*, to see | *seen by Yah* / *Yah provides* |
| the one Chronicles' *appeared* suggests | *rāʾâ*, passive *yērāʾeh* | *place of appearing* |
| the one grammar prefers | *yārâ*, to teach, to point | *place of teaching* or *of pointing out* |

**The name as spelled fits *yārâ* better than *rāʾâ*, and the chapter behaves as though it were
*rāʾâ*.** Genesis 22 conjugates *see* five times in fourteen verses and lands on *YHWH yirʾeh* and on
the proverb *"On Yahweh's mountain, it will be provided"* — so whatever the name's origin, its use in
the text is built on seeing. That is the ordinary situation with biblical place-names: the narrative
etymology is a play, not a philology. See
[[Yahweh Will Provide - Moriah and the Temple Mount]].

### What later readers did with it

**Jewish tradition made Moriah the fixed point of sacred geography** — the site of Adam's altar, of
Noah's, of Abraham's, and of the temple; the *Akedah* is read into the Rosh Hashanah liturgy and the
ram's horn of 22:13 becomes the shofar. **Christian tradition read the mountain typologically**, as
the place where a father did not withhold a son, and located Calvary in the same city if not the same
rock. **Samaritan tradition put the whole episode on Gerizim.** All three are reading one verse of
Genesis through one verse of Chronicles, and the wiki says so.

## Appears in

- [[The Binding of Isaac]] — Genesis 22:1–19; the land Abraham is sent to on three days' notice, and
  the unnamed mountain in it that he names *Yahweh Will Provide*

## Related places

- [[Beersheba]] — where the journey starts and ends, about three days' walk away
- [[Salem]] — Melchizedek's city at 14:18, and Jerusalem if the Chronicles identification holds;
  Genesis never connects the two
- [[Canaan]] — the land the region sits in, none of which Abraham owns
- [[Shechem]] — beside the oak of *Moreh*, the Samaritan Pentateuch's reading of this name

## Events here

- **Genesis 22:1–19** — Abraham builds an altar, binds his son, is stopped by a voice out of the sky,
  and offers a ram *"instead of his son"*; he names the place *Yahweh Will Provide*
- **2 Chronicles 3:1** — Solomon begins the temple on Mount Moriah, at the threshing floor of Ornan
  the Jebusite *(not yet ingested)*
