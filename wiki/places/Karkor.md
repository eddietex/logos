# Karkor

## Overview

*Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their armies with them, about fifteen thousand men* (Judges
8:10). The place where the kings of Midian rest after the rout, east of the Jordan. Gideon reaches it
*by the way of those who lived in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah* and strikes the camp, *for the
army was secure* (8:11). *Qarqōr* (7174) occurs in Genesis–Judges only here (WLC). The site is not
identified here.

Source: stored WEB Judges 8:10–12; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
