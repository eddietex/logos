# Jutah

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Judah and Simeon to Aaron’s descendants (21:16).  WEB here spells the name Juttah; identification with Jutah in Joshua 15:55 remains likely, not expressly stated.

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the hill country (Joshua 15:55), one of the *ten cities with their villages* of the third hill-country group (55–57). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Joshua 21:16 names a *Juttah* among the priests' cities; the spelling differs in the WEB and the identification is likely but not stated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
