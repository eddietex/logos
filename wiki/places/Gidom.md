# Gidom

## Overview

### The end of the pursuit — Judges 20:45

As Benjamin flees toward [[The Rock of Rimmon]], Israel *gleaned five thousand men of them in the
highways, and followed hard after them to Gidom, and struck two thousand men of them* (Judges 20:45).
That is all the text says of it.

*Gidʿōm* (45, WLC) occurs only in this verse in the WLC files of Genesis through 1 Samuel and Hosea
searched (lemma 1440). The name shares its three consonants with *gādaʿ*, *to cut down*; the wiki notes this and draws
nothing from it. No site is assigned. Some readers take it as a common noun, *until they were cut off* —
reported, not checked.

See [[The War Against Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 20:45; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-17.
