# Ashdod

## Overview

### The land that remains — Joshua 13:1–7

The Ashdodites are among the five Philistine lordships in unpossessed territory (3), continuing the qualification already attached to the Anakim remnant in 11:22.

See [[The Land That Remains]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.

One of three cities where some of the Anakim remain after Joshua cuts them off from the hill country (11:22). Joshua 13:3 counts *the Ashdodites* among the Philistine peoples whose land still remains, and 15:46–47 later includes Ashdod in Judah's allotted list. Allotment and possession are not the same claim.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23. No modern identification is assigned.
