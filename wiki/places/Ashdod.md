# Ashdod

## Overview

### The house of Dagon — 1 Samuel 5:1–7

The Philistines bring the captured ark *from Ebenezer to Ashdod* and into *the house of Dagon* (5:1–2).
Dagon falls before it on two mornings, the second time broken *on the threshold*, and the narrator notes that
Dagon's priests and worshippers do not step on *the threshold of Dagon in Ashdod, to this day* (3–5). Then
*Yahweh's hand was heavy on the people of Ashdod … even Ashdod and its borders* (6), and its men refuse to
keep the ark (7). The first of three Philistine cities in the chapter, and the first in which the narrative
of 1 Samuel is set outside Israel. See [[The Ark Among the Philistines]] and [[Dagon]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 5:1–7.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Allotted to Judah with its towns and villages (46–47). 11:22 leaves Anakim in Ashdod and 13:3 counts it among the Philistine lordships that remain, so allotment here is not occupation.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### The land that remains — Joshua 13:1–7

The Ashdodites are among the five Philistine lordships in unpossessed territory (3), continuing the qualification already attached to the Anakim remnant in 11:22.

See [[The Land That Remains]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.

One of three cities where some of the Anakim remain after Joshua cuts them off from the hill country (11:22). Joshua 13:3 counts *the Ashdodites* among the Philistine peoples whose land still remains, and 15:46–47 later includes Ashdod in Judah's allotted list. Allotment and possession are not the same claim.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23. No modern identification is assigned.
