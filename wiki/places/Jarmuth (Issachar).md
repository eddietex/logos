# Jarmuth (Issachar)

## Overview

Allocated with its suburbs from Issachar to Gershon’s descendants (21:29).  Kept distinct from [[Jarmuth]] in Judah. Identification with Remeth in Joshua 19:21 is not asserted.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42. No precise modern site is assigned.
