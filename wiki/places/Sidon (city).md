# Sidon (city)

## Overview

**The Phoenician port on the Lebanese coast, and the northernmost place Genesis names.**

**It enters the book twice in one chapter and once at the end.** **Genesis 10:15 makes it a person**
— *"Canaan became the father of Sidon (his firstborn), Heth"* — **and 10:19 makes it a border**:
*"The border of the Canaanites was from Sidon—as you go toward Gerar—to Gaza."* **Then nothing for
thirty-nine chapters, until [[Jacob]]'s deathbed poem gives it to a son as a limit**: *"Zebulun will
dwell at the haven of the sea. He will be for a haven of ships. His border will be on Sidon"*
(49:13).

**Genesis therefore uses the name three ways** — **an ancestor, a frontier, and a tribal boundary** —
**and never as a city anyone visits.** **No patriarch goes there, nothing happens there, and the
place is always a marker for how far something reaches.**

**The qualifier is in the filename because the person is coming.** [[The Table of Nations]] names
Sidon the firstborn of [[Canaan (son of Ham)]] and the wiki has not yet given him a page; **when it
does he will be `Sidon (son of Canaan)`, and this page keeps `(city)`** — the convention that already
separates [[Dan (city)]] from [[Dan (person)]] and [[Shechem (city)]] from
[[Shechem (son of Hamor)]].

**It is a real and very old city**, attested in Egyptian and Akkadian sources long before Israel
exists, and the senior of the two Phoenician ports until Tyre overtakes it. **Scripture afterwards
treats the two together** — *"Tyre and Sidon"* is a standing pair in Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Joel
and the Gospels. **Jezebel is a Sidonian king's daughter** (1 Kings 16:31), **which makes the city
the channel by which Baal worship enters the northern kingdom**; **Ezekiel 28:20–23 prophesies
against it**; **and Jesus goes into its region and is met by a Syrophoenician woman** (Mark 7:24–30),
**and names it in a judgement on Galilean towns** — *"if the mighty works had been done in Tyre and
Sidon which were done in you, they would have repented long ago"* (Matthew 11:21–22).

## Appears in

- [[The Table of Nations]] — Genesis 10:15, 19; **named twice in five verses, first as
  [[Canaan (son of Ham)]]'s firstborn and then as the top of the Canaanite frontier.** **It is the
  only city in the Table that is also a person in the Table**
- [[Jacob Blesses His Sons]] — Genesis 49:13; ***"His border will be on Sidon"*** — **the northern
  limit of [[Zebulun]]'s saying, and the last place-name Genesis adds to its map.** **The allotment
  Joshua 19:10–16 actually gives Zebulun lies inland in lower Galilee and reaches neither the sea nor
  Sidon**, a mismatch neither book remarks on. The wiki records it and does not harmonise it

## Related places

- [[Canaan]] — the land whose northern border Genesis 10:19 fixes at Sidon
- [[The Land of the Philistines]] — the coast south of it; **Genesis's two seaboard references sit at
  opposite ends of the same shoreline**
- [[Egypt]] — the other end of the coastal road, and where the man who names Sidon at 49:13 is dying

## Events here

- **Nothing.** **Genesis names Sidon three times and narrates no event in it** — **the only named
  settlement in the book of which that is true apart from the cities of the Table of Nations
  themselves**
