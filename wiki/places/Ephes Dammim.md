# Ephes Dammim

## Overview

### The Philistine camp — 1 Samuel 17:1

The Philistines gather *at Socoh, which belongs to Judah, and encamped between Socoh and Azekah, in Ephesdammim*
(17:1). The Hebrew is *bəʾep̄es dammîm* (WLC). In the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, and Ruth) the
name occurs only here. *ʾEp̄es* means *end* or *ceasing*, and *dammîm* is the plural of *blood* (recalled, not checked);
whether the name was understood that way, or meant anything, the text does not say. The verse locates it between two
towns of Judah's lowland, [[Socoh (lowland)]] and [[Azekah]] (Joshua 15:35). 1 Chronicles 11:13 has David's man *with David at Pasdammim, and there the Philistines were gathered together to
battle* (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not stored); whether *Pasdammim* is the same place is not stated, and the
Hebrew of Chronicles was not checked. No modern site is assigned.
