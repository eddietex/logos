# The Sea of Chinnereth

## Overview

**The city.** Joshua 19:35 lists a fortified city Chinnereth in Naphtali; see [[Chinnereth (city)]]. The verse does not mention the sea.

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Its extremity marks the northern reach of Gad’s Jordan-bank description (27).

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

Chinnereth marks the northern end of the Jordan boundary described in verse 17. The verse uses the
shorter name; Numbers 34:11 explicitly names the sea. No distinct city is identified here.

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

The eastern border reaches the east side of this sea and then goes down to the Jordan and the Salt Sea.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34:11–12. No precise modern site is assigned here.
