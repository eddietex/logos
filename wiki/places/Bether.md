# Bether

## Overview

Named once in the ingested text, in a comparison. At the close of [[Rise Up, My Love]] the woman
asks the beloved to *"be like a roe or a young deer on the mountains of Bether"* (Song 2:17). The
WEB treats *Bether* as a proper name. No location is given in the verse, and the wiki makes no
identification.

It may not be a place name at all. The Hebrew *hārê ḇāter* (Sefaria, *Miqra according to the
Masorah*) uses the root *b-t-r*, *to cut in two*, which appears at Genesis 15:10, where Abram
*"divided them in the middle, and laid each half opposite the other"* (*wayəḇattēr*, *biṯrô*; see
[[The Covenant Between the Pieces]]). On that reading the phrase means *mountains of division*,
*cleft* or *rugged* mountains. The parallel line at Song 8:14 has *hārê ḇəśāmîm*, *"the mountains of
spices"* (Sefaria; stored WEB, not yet ingested). The page follows the WEB's proper name and keeps
the question open. Whether the mountains are also a figure for the woman herself is debated and is
left open on the passage page.

This page is filed as a place because the WEB names it as one, following the [[Kedar]],
[[En Gedi]], and [[Sharon]] precedent for places named in comparisons.

Sources: stored WEB Song of Solomon 2:17; 8:14 and Genesis 15:10; Hebrew of Song 2:17; 8:14 and
Genesis 15:10 read from Sefaria's *Miqra according to the Masorah*, 2026-10-28.
