# Gaza

## Overview

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Allotted to Judah with its towns and villages, *to the brook of Egypt* (47). 10:41 reaches Gaza, 11:22 leaves Anakim there, and 13:3 counts it as remaining; the allotment does not resolve those notices.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### The land that remains — Joshua 13:1–7

The Gazites belong to the five Philistine lordships in land still remaining (3). This explicitly qualifies the campaign reaching Gaza in 10:41 and accords with the remnant there in 11:22.

See [[The Land That Remains]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.

### The whole land taken — Joshua 11:16–23

Gaza is one of three cities where some of the Anakim remain (22), with [[Gath]] and [[Ashdod]]. Joshua 13:3 lists the Gazites among the land remaining.

See [[The Whole Land Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23.

### The southern campaign — Joshua 10:28–43

Gaza marks the reach from Kadesh Barnea in verse 41, without a separate capture narrative. Joshua 13:1–3 still lists Philistine territory, including the Gazites, as remaining; comprehensive language here does not settle permanent possession.

See [[The Southern Cities Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43; comparisons as cited.

Named as the extent of the Avvim’s villages in the account of their displacement by the Caphtorim
(Deuteronomy 2:23). Israel does not conquer Gaza in this pericope.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.
