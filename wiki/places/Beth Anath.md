# Beth Anath

## Overview

### Not driven out — Judges 1:33

Naphtali did not drive out its inhabitants, but they *became subject to forced labor* (33).

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (38), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). No account of its inhabitants is given here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
