# Bozez

## Overview

One of two rocky crags at the pass between Michmash and Geba: *Between the passes, by which Jonathan sought to go
over to the Philistines' garrison, there was a rocky crag on the one side, and a rocky crag on the other side: and the
name of the one was Bozez, and the name of the other Seneh. The one crag rose up on the north in front of Michmash,
and the other on the south in front of Geba* (1 Samuel 14:4–5, stored WEB). The verse does not say in so many words
which name belongs to which crag; the usual reading takes the order of naming and the order of placing together, so
Bozez is the northern crag facing [[Michmash]] (an inference, not stated).

*Bôṣēṣ* (WLC) occurs only here in the WLC files of Genesis–2 Samuel (morphhb lemma 949). It is commonly glossed
*shining* or *slippery* (recalled, not checked). *Crag* is *šēn hassela*ʿ, literally *tooth of the rock* (14:4, WLC),
and in verse 5 *māṣûq*, a word morphhb also tags at *the pillars of the earth* in Hannah's song (2:8). See
[[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].
