# Rehoboth by the River

## Overview

**The home town of Shaul, the sixth king of Edom, and not the well Isaac dug.**

> **37** Samlah died, and Shaul of Rehoboth by the river, reigned in his place.

**One of the eight royal seats in Edom's king list, and one of the six that cannot be located.** See
[[The Descendants of Esau]].

## Why it has its own page

**[[Rehoboth]] already exists in this wiki, and it is a different place.** Isaac's third well in the
valley of Gerar, named at 26:22 for the room Yahweh made — *"For now Yahweh has made room for us"* —
is in the western Negev, several days' travel from Edom, and it is a well rather than a town.

***Rəḥōbôt*** is simply the plural **broad places, open spaces**, from *rāḥab*, to be wide, and it is
exactly the sort of name that gets used more than once. **The two pages are kept separate and named
distinctly because Obsidian resolves wikilinks by basename**: two pages called `Rehoboth.md` would
pass `scripts/link-check.sh` and then be silently collapsed into one by the vault. The disambiguation
is built into this page's title, so nothing about the existing [[Rehoboth]] needs to change.

**A third Rehoboth** — *Rehoboth Ir*, in Nimrod's Assyrian city list at Genesis 10:11 — is also
distinct, and has no page.

## Which river

***Han-nāhār***, **the River**, with the article, normally means the **Euphrates** in biblical usage —
which would put Shaul's town some four hundred miles north-east of Edom and make the entry very odd.
The two standard resolutions:

- **A local wadi.** The article need not be exclusive, and a king list compiled in Edom would call its
  own watercourse *the river*. The Wadi el-Hesa, Edom's northern boundary, is the usual candidate.
- **The Euphrates after all**, with an Aramean Rehoboth — there is a *Raḥabu* attested in Assyrian
  records near the middle Euphrates — meaning that one of Edom's kings came from outside the country
  entirely. **That is not as strange as it sounds**, given that no son succeeds a father anywhere in
  the list and every king comes from a different town.

**Neither is provable and the wiki adopts neither.** See [[The Euphrates]].
