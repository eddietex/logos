# Pisgah

## Overview

### The first two oracles — Numbers 22:36–23:26

The field of Zophim on Pisgah’s summit becomes the second sacrificial station (23:14). Another set
of seven altars produces another blessing, not the reversal Balak seeks.

See [[Balaam's First Two Oracles]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 22:36–23:26.


The top of Pisgah overlooks the desert at the end of this itinerary (20), in the description of the
valley in the field of Moab. The verse does not narrate Moses’ later death or a conquest here.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:10–20.
