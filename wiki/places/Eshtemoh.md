# Eshtemoh

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the hill country (Joshua 15:50), one of the *eleven cities with their villages* of the first hill-country group (48–51). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Joshua 21:14 names an *Eshtemoa* among the priests' cities; the spelling differs in the WEB and the identification is likely but not stated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
