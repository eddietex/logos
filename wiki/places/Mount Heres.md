# Mount Heres

## Overview

One of three places where *the Amorites would dwell*, with Aijalon and Shaalbim, until *the hand of
the house of Joseph prevailed* and they became forced laborers (Judges 1:35, stored WEB). The Hebrew is
*bəhar-ḥeres* (WLC), *Mount Heres*; *ḥeres* is usually glossed *sun* (the standard lexical gloss, not checked against a lexicon in this pass), and it is often noted that the
neighboring Beth Shemesh and Ir-shemesh (Joshua 19:41) are also sun names. That observation is
recorded as commonly made; the text does not equate the places.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:34–35; Joshua 19:41; WLC Judges
1:35.
