# Beth Tappuah

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the second group of the hill country (Joshua 15:53), one of the *nine cities with their villages* of the second hill-country group (52–54). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Distinct from [[Tappuah (lowland)]] (34) and the royal [[Tappuah]] (12:17); no relation is stated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
