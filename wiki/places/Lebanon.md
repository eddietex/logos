# Lebanon

## Overview

The mountain range north of Israel, along the coast behind Sidon and Tyre, and the forested
country around it. In Scripture it is known above all for its trees. The Hebrew name, *lǝḇānôn*,
has the consonants *l-b-n*, the root for *white*. The name is usually explained by snow on its
peaks, which is a common explanation but is not stated by any verse the wiki has read.

**Its first appearance in the wiki is in [[Solomon's Wedding Procession]].** *"King Solomon made
himself a carriage of the wood of Lebanon"* (Song of Solomon 3:9). The Hebrew is *ʿăṣê
hallǝḇānôn*, *trees of Lebanon* (Sefaria, *Miqra according to the Masorah*); the WEB does not name
the kind of tree, and neither does the Hebrew. Lebanon here is a source of fine timber for a king.
Nothing happens there.

The same picture appears in 1 Kings. Solomon asks Hiram of Tyre to *"command that cedar trees be
cut for me out of Lebanon"* for the temple (1 Kings 5:6), and he builds *"the House of the Forest of
Lebanon"* on *"four rows of cedar pillars"* (1 Kings 7:2). Both were checked at bible-api.com
(`translation=web`) on 2026-10-28; 1 Kings is not yet fetched or ingested.

**The Song uses Lebanon more than any other place name.** The stored WEB has it at 3:9; 4:8 (twice),
11, 15; 5:15; and 7:4. Later, it becomes a place the bride is called away from (*"Come with me from
Lebanon, my bride"*, 4:8), a fragrance (4:11), a source of streams (4:15), a picture of the man's
appearance, *"excellent as the cedars"* (5:15), and a tower *"which looks toward Damascus"* (7:4).
These are recorded here from the stored text and will be described as each unit is ingested.

**Lebanon, frankincense, and the moon.** *Lǝḇônâ*, frankincense (Song 3:6), and *lǝḇānâ*, the moon
(6:10), share the same three consonants (Sefaria). Readers often see wordplay among them in the
Song. The wiki notes it without claiming it was intended. See [[Solomon's Wedding Procession]].

Sources: Song of Solomon 3:6, 9; 4:8, 11, 15; 5:15; 6:10; 7:4 from the stored WEB. 1 Kings 5:6 and
7:2 checked at bible-api.com, 2026-10-28. Hebrew of Song 3:6, 9; 4:8; 6:10 from Sefaria's *Miqra
according to the Masorah*.
