# Debir (Gad)

## Overview

The border of Debir is a terminus of Gad’s boundary from Mahanaim (26). Its eastern context is distinct from the southern Debir described with Hebron in Joshua 10:38–39. The verse does not identify its kind of settlement or a modern site.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33.
