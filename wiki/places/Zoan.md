# Zoan

## Overview

A city of the Nile delta, named in Numbers 13:22 only to date another: *Hebron was built seven years
before Zoan in Egypt*. It is the one thing in the Torah said about it, by the tags. Rahlfs has
*Τάνιν*, so the Greek translators took it as Tanis, the Delta city.

**The date's point.** The aside says that the town of Abraham's grave is older than a city of Egypt.
Which founding is meant is unknown, and the wiki does not turn it into a date.

**Later uses** (WEB checked; *not yet ingested*). Psalm 78:12 places the plagues *in the land of
Egypt, in the field of Zoan*, and 78:43 repeats it (tags). Isaiah 19:11 has *The princes of Zoan are
utterly foolish*, and 19:13 and 30:4 name it too. Ezekiel 30:14 says *will set a fire in Zoan*. So
after Numbers it is a name for Egypt's seat of power. Whether it is linked with [[Rameses]] is a
modern question about ruins (recalled), and the text does not address it.
