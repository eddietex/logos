# Ebenezer

## Overview

### Israel's camp before Aphek — 1 Samuel 4:1b

*Israel went out against the Philistines to battle, and encamped beside Ebenezer; and the Philistines
encamped in Aphek* (4:1). The WLC writes the name with the article, *hāʾeḇen hāʿēzer*, *the stone of the
help* (4:1; 5:1), and morphhb lemmatizes all three of its occurrences in 1 Samuel together (4:1; 5:1; 7:12).
From here Israel sends to Shiloh for the ark, and here it loses two battles, four thousand men and then
thirty thousand, with the ark (4:2–11). The Philistines carry the ark *from Ebenezer to Ashdod* (5:1); see
[[The Ark Among the Philistines]].

The name is explained only later: after Samuel's victory, *Samuel took a stone, and set it between Mizpah and
Shen, and called its name Ebenezer, saying, "Yahweh helped us until now"* (7:12; see below). Read in order, the book names the camp of the defeat by the name of the later victory. Whether
the two are one site, 4:1 using the later name by anticipation, or two places that share a name, the text
does not say, and readers differ. No modern site is assigned here. See [[The Ark Is Captured]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 4:1–11; 5:1; 7:12. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### The stone of help — 1 Samuel 7:12

After the victory at Mizpah, *Samuel took a stone, and set it between Mizpah and Shen, and called its name
Ebenezer, saying, "Yahweh helped us until now"* (7:12). The WLC reads *ʾeḇen hāʿāzer* (pausal), where 4:1 had
*hāʾeḇen hāʿēzer*, and the name is explained here for the first time: *ʿaḏ-hēnnâ ʿăzārānû YHWH*. *Until now*,
*ʿaḏ-hēnnâ*, can be read of time or of place (*thus far*). The battle of 7:10–11 returns the defeat of 4:2 in the
same words: there *Israel was struck before the Philistines*, here the Philistines *were struck down before
Israel* (WLC). The question already recorded above, whether this is the site of the camp of 4:1, stays open:
7:12 locates the stone between Mizpah and Shen and says nothing of Aphek. See [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]]
and [[The Pillar Jacob Raised and the Pillars the Law Forbids]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 4:1–2; 7:10–12. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
