# Ebenezer

## Overview

### Israel's camp before Aphek — 1 Samuel 4:1b

*Israel went out against the Philistines to battle, and encamped beside Ebenezer; and the Philistines
encamped in Aphek* (4:1). The WLC writes the name with the article, *hāʾeḇen hāʿēzer*, *the stone of the
help* (4:1; 5:1), and morphhb lemmatizes all three of its occurrences in 1 Samuel together (4:1; 5:1; 7:12).
From here Israel sends to Shiloh for the ark, and here it loses two battles, four thousand men and then
thirty thousand, with the ark (4:2–11). The Philistines carry the ark *from Ebenezer to Ashdod* (5:1, stored
WEB; not yet ingested).

The name is explained only later: after Samuel's victory, *Samuel took a stone, and set it between Mizpah and
Shen, and called its name Ebenezer, saying, "Yahweh helped us until now"* (7:12, stored WEB; not yet
ingested). Read in order, the book names the camp of the defeat by the name of the later victory. Whether
the two are one site, 4:1 using the later name by anticipation, or two places that share a name, the text
does not say, and readers differ. No modern site is assigned here. See [[The Ark Is Captured]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 4:1–11; 5:1; 7:12. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
