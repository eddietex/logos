# Harosheth of the Gentiles

## Overview

Sisera's base: he *lived in Harosheth of the Gentiles* (Judges 4:2). The chariots set out from it to
the river Kishon (4:13), and Barak pursues them back to it, where *all the army of Sisera fell by the
edge of the sword* (4:16). The Hebrew is *ḥărōšet haggôyim* (WLC), *Harosheth of the nations*. It
occurs only in Judges 4 in the WLC files read (Genesis through Judges and 1 Samuel). The text does
not locate it beyond these movements; no modern site is assigned.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.
