# Gibeon

## Overview

### The whole land taken — Joshua 11:16–23

Gibeon is the single exception in the summary: *There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, except the Hivites, the inhabitants of Gibeon* (19). The peace was obtained by deception (9:3–15), and it held.

See [[The Whole Land Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23.

### The southern campaign — Joshua 10:28–43

The city is a geographical limit in the campaign summary (41), not a newly destroyed city. Its protected status from Joshua 9 and 10:1–15 remains distinct.

See [[The Southern Cities Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43; comparisons as cited.

### At Gibeon — Joshua 10:1–15

A great city with mighty men, greater than Ai, is attacked because it made peace with Israel (1–5).
Its inhabitants appeal as Joshua’s servants, and Israel comes to defend them (6–10). The sun is
addressed over Gibeon (12). The comparison with royal cities (2) does not name a king of Gibeon.

See [[The Sun Stands Still at Gibeon]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15.


The community whose inhabitants organize the deceptive embassy after hearing of Jericho and Ai (3).
Its envoys are identified as Hivites (7); it is one of four cities protected by the oath (17–20).
