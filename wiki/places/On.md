# On

## Overview

**The Egyptian centre of the worship of the sun, and the home of Joseph's wife.**

> **45** Pharaoh called Joseph’s name Zaphenath-Paneah; and he gave him Asenath, the daughter of
> Potiphera priest of On as a wife. Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.

**Genesis names it three times** (41:45, 50; 46:20) **and always in the same phrase** —
*Potiphera priest of On* — **so the city enters Scripture as an address attached to a priesthood.** The
text says nothing else about it: no size, no description, no journey to it, and **no comment on what was
worshipped there.**

### What it was

***ʾŌn*** transliterates Egyptian ***iwnw***, *the pillar place*, **at the apex of the Delta a few miles
north-east of modern Cairo.** **The Greeks called it Heliopolis, *sun city*** — which is a translation of
what it was for: **the principal cult centre of Ra**, and the home of the Heliopolitan Ennead, one of the
oldest and most influential theologies in Egypt. **Its high priest bore the title *wr-mȝw*, *greatest of
seers*.**

**It was, in other words, not an incidental town.** **It was to Egyptian religion roughly what Jerusalem
would become to Israel's**, and **Genesis marries Joseph into its priesthood in half a verse without a
word of qualification.** See [[Potiphera]], [[Asenath]], [[Priesthood]], and [[Idolatry]].

**The obelisk still standing on the site belongs to Senusret I**, of the Twelfth Dynasty, **which puts a
functioning sanctuary there well before any date proposed for Joseph** — though Genesis supplies no date
and the wiki declines to import one (see [[Pharaoh (of Joseph's time)]]).

### Elsewhere in the Bible

**The prophets return to it, and never kindly.**

- **Jeremiah 43:13** — *"He will also break the pillars of Beth Shemesh that is in the land of Egypt"* —
  ***Bêt Šemeš***, *house of the sun*, **which is Heliopolis translated into Hebrew**, and *the pillars*
  are its obelisks.
- **Ezekiel 30:17** — the WEB's *"Aven"*, ***ʾĀwen***, *wickedness* or *emptiness* — **the name ʾŌn
  repointed into a slur**, exactly as *Bethel* becomes *Beth Aven* at Hosea 4:15. **A city named for the
  sun is renamed for nothing.**
- **Isaiah 19:18** — one of five Egyptian cities that *"speak the language of Canaan"* is called, in the
  Hebrew, *ʿÎr haHeres* or *ʿÎr haḤeres*, **read either as *city of destruction* or as *city of the sun*
  depending on one letter**, and the ancient witnesses divide. **If it is *city of the sun* then Isaiah
  has On swearing allegiance to Yahweh.**

**So the place Genesis mentions three times without evaluation is, in the prophets, the standing example
of what Egyptian religion is** — **and possibly also of what it might become.**

## Appears in

- [[Pharaoh's Dreams and Joseph's Rise]] — Genesis 41:45, 50; **named twice, as the seat of the
  priesthood Joseph marries into.** It arrives in the same verse as his Egyptian name and his Egyptian
  wife, **the three of them together making up the chapter's act of naturalisation.** **Genesis offers no
  comment at all on a patriarch's household being joined to the cult of the sun** — the most conspicuous
  silence in the chapter, and the wiki records it as a silence
