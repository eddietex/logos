# Tyre

## Overview

Asher’s border turns *to Ramah, to the fortified city of Tyre* (29). The verse treats Tyre as a boundary point and calls it fortified; it does not narrate a capture, and Joshua 13:4–6 still counts the Sidonians among those to be driven out. The later prophetic pairing of Tyre and Sidon lies outside the stored books and is not quoted here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
