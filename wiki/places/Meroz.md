# Meroz

## Overview

### Cursed for not coming — Judges 5:23

*'Curse Meroz,' said Yahweh's angel. 'Curse bitterly its inhabitants, because they didn't come to help
Yahweh, to help Yahweh against the mighty'* (5:23). That verse is all the Bible gives of Meroz. In the
WLC files read for this ingest (Genesis through Joshua, Judges, and Psalms) the name occurs only here
(OpenScriptures lemma 4789), and its site is unknown. The Song does not say which tribe it belonged to
or where it lay; that it was near enough to have come is implied by the curse.

The curse is spoken by [[The Angel of Yahweh]], who also spoke at Bochim (2:1–5). Its charge is the
Song's charge against Reuben, Gilead, Dan, and Asher (5:15–17), but harder: those tribes are asked
*why*; Meroz is cursed. The verb is *ʾārar*, three times in one line (*ʾôrû … ʾōrû ʾārôr*, WLC).

Judges returns twice to towns that did not help: Succoth and Penuel (8:5–17) and Jabesh Gilead
(21:8–12). See [[Those Who Did Not Come - Meroz, Succoth and Penuel, and Jabesh Gilead]] and
[[The Song of Deborah]].
