# Beth Jeshimoth

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Listed in Reuben’s portion (20), alongside Beth Peor and Pisgah’s slopes.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

One limit of the final encampment by the Jordan, with Abel Shittim as the other (49).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49. No modern site is assigned.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** *The way to Beth Jeshimoth* locates Sihon's Arabah toward the Salt Sea (12:3). Deuteronomy 3:17's otherwise close description does not name it. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
