# The Hill of Phinehas

## Overview

Where Eleazar the son of Aaron is buried: *the hill of Phinehas his son, which was given him in the
hill country of Ephraim* (Joshua 24:33). The verse does not say who gave it, when, or whether it was
a town, a field, or a burial site, and no modern site is assigned here.

It sits uneasily with the statements that Levi received no land inheritance (Joshua 13:14, 33) and
that the priests' cities lay in Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin (21:4). The tension is recorded on
[[The Burials of Joshua, Joseph, and Eleazar]] and [[Phinehas (son of Eleazar)]], not resolved.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:14, 33; 21:4; 24:33.
