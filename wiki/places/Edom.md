# Edom

## Overview

**The nation Israel is related to and cannot get along with** — the one neighbour the Hebrew Bible
consistently calls *brother* and consistently fights.

The country lies south-east of the Dead Sea, along the rift and the highland spine running down to
the Gulf of Aqaba: a rugged, defensible strip astride the King's Highway, with copper in the Arabah
and a reputation for wisdom (Jeremiah 49:7; Obadiah 8; and see [[Teman]]). Its other biblical name is
**Seir**, from *śēʿār*, **hair** — the word used of Esau's appearance at birth.

**Genesis gives it its name in the middle of a meal.** *"Please feed me with that same red stew, for
I am famished." Therefore his name was called Edom"* (25:30). ***ʾĔdôm*** is *ʾādōm*, **red** — the
colour of the stew, and also of the newborn *ʾadmônî* five verses earlier, and also, as it happens, of
the sandstone the region is made of. **Genesis attaches the etiology to the soup rather than to the
skin or to the rock**, which puts the nation's name permanently next to the moment its ancestor sold
his inheritance for one meal. See [[Jacob and Esau and the Sold Birthright]] and [[Esau]].

### The oracle is about this place

*"Two nations are in your womb"* (25:23) — ***šənê gôyîm***, and Edom is one of them. **The oracle at
Rebekah's inquiry is not in the first instance about two men**; its terms are peoples, its content is
a ranking, and the ranking is political. The Bible records the relation being what the oracle said and
then ceasing to be:

- **2 Samuel 8:13–14** — David garrisons Edom, *"and all the Edomites became servants to David."*
- **1 Kings 11:14–22** — Hadad the Edomite, an adversary raised up against Solomon.
- **2 Kings 8:20–22** — *"In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and made a king over
  themselves."* The subjection lasts roughly a century and a half and then ends.

See [[The Elder Will Serve the Younger - Genesis 25.23 in Malachi and Romans]].

### Brother, and enemy

**The Torah protects Edom and the prophets prosecute it, and both do it on the same ground.**

- **Numbers 20:14–21** — Moses sends to *"your brother Israel"* asking passage on the King's Highway,
  and Edom refuses and comes out with an army.
- **Deuteronomy 2:4–5** — Israel is forbidden to take any of Seir: *"I have given Mount Seir to Esau
  for a possession."* The land grant to the passed-over brother is explicit and permanent.
- **Deuteronomy 23:7–8** — *"You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother."*
- **Amos 1:11; Obadiah 10–14; Psalm 137:7** — the prophets on Edom's conduct at the fall of
  Jerusalem, and the charge in every case is fratricide: *"because he pursued his brother with the
  sword."*
- **Malachi 1:2–3** — *"I loved Jacob; but Esau I hated, and made his mountains a desolation"*, spoken
  of the nation, and quoted by Paul at Romans 9:13.

**The word that recurs across the whole file is *brother*.** It is what makes the protections
generous and the accusations unbearable, and it comes from Genesis 25.

Later the Idumeans are forcibly incorporated into Judea under John Hyrcanus, and Herod the Great —
the king in Matthew 2 — is an Idumean. The last political appearance of Esau's line in the biblical
story is on the throne of Jacob's.

## Appears in

- [[Jacob and Esau and the Sold Birthright]] — Genesis 25:23, 25, 30; forecast as one of the *two
  nations*, seeded by the *ʾadmônî* and *śēʿār* of the birth notice, and named outright from the red
  stew
- [[The Stolen Blessing]] — Genesis 27:39–40; **its charter, spoken over its ancestor as what is left
  after the blessing has been spent.** Fertile country or arid country depending on a single Hebrew
  preposition — *min* is both *some of* and *away from*, and the WEB, KJV, and LXX take the first
  while most modern versions take the second, which decides whether Esau is blessed at all. Then
  *"By your sword will you live, and you will serve your brother"*, and *"when you will break loose,
  … you shall shake his yoke from off your neck"* — **both halves of which the historical books
  record**, in that order

## Related places

- [[Teman]] — an Edomite town or district, proverbial for wisdom, and the home of Job's first friend
- [[Uz]] — Job's country, placed by Lamentations 4:21 in Edomite association, and the wiki's other
  Transjordanian wisdom setting
- [[Canaan]] — the land on the other side of the rift, and the one the covenant is about
- [[The Salt Sea]] — the northern boundary of the Edomite highlands
- [[Kadesh]] — where Israel is camped when it asks Edom for passage (Numbers 20:14–16)
- [[Moab (place)]] — its northern neighbour, and the Bible's other *brother* nation by descent from
  Lot
