# Keilah

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the lowland (Joshua 15:44), one of the *nine cities with their villages* of the third lowland group (42–44). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
