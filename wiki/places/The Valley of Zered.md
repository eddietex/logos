# The Valley of Zered

## Overview

### Protected neighbors and the wilderness generation — Deuteronomy 2:1–23

The brook Zered is crossed at Yahweh’s command (13). Thirty-eight years from Kadesh Barnea to this
crossing mark the end of the condemned military generation (14–16), adding a chronological
interpretation absent from Numbers 21:12.

See [[Around Seir and Past Moab and Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.

Israel camps in this valley after Iyeabarim and before the Arnon stage (12–13). No crossing event or
date is described here.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:10–20.
