# Aphek

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:18. No battle is narrated. Joshua names an Aphek again in the land still remaining, *to Aphek, to the border of the Amorites* (13:4), and among Asher's cities (19:30). Whether these are one place or several is not stated; this page covers the king list only, and later ingests decide whether to merge or qualify.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
