# Aphek

## Overview

### The land that remains — Joshua 13:1–7

Joshua 13:4 names Aphek in the remaining northern territory, after Sidonian Mearah and before the Amorite border. This notice is kept distinct from the royal city in 12:18: the shared name does not establish identity. The page now records both references without claiming a single site.

See [[The Land That Remains]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:18. No battle is narrated. Joshua names an Aphek again in the land still remaining, *to Aphek, to the border of the Amorites* (13:4), and among Asher's cities (19:30). Whether these are one place or several is not stated; the distinct notices are retained here without assuming they describe the same site.
