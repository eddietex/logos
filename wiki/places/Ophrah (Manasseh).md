# Ophrah (Manasseh)

## Overview

*Ophrah of the Abiezrites* (Judges 6:24), Gideon's town in Manasseh. Its first scene has three
landmarks: the oak belonging to Joash under which Yahweh's angel sits, the winepress where Gideon
threshes wheat out of Midian's sight (6:11), and the altar Gideon builds and names *Yahweh is Peace*.
The narrator adds that the altar *To this day … is still in Ophrah of the Abiezrites* (6:24).

The town also has Joash's altar to Baal, with an Asherah beside it. Gideon throws them down by night and
builds an altar to Yahweh *on the top of this stronghold* (6:25–26), so within one day the town has
two new altars to Yahweh. In the morning *the men of the city* demand his death, *Bring out your son,
that he may die* (6:30). It is an Israelite town defending a Baal altar against an Israelite. Later the
town is the site of Gideon's ephod, which *all Israel* worships *there* (8:27), of his burial (8:32), and
of Abimelech's killing of the seventy sons of Jerubbaal *at Ophrah* (9:5). Those belong to later
pericopes. See [[Gideon Throws Down Baal's Altar]]. Its modern location is disputed and is not
checked here.

It is a different town from the Ophrah in Benjamin's allotment (Joshua 18:23); see
[[Ophrah (Benjamin)]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 6:11, 24–32; 8:27, 32; 9:5; Joshua 18:23.

**The ephod and the tomb (8:27, 32).** Now ingested. Gideon puts his ephod *in Ophrah, his city*, and
*all Israel played the prostitute with it there* (8:27). He is buried *in the tomb of Joash his father,
in Ophrah of the Abiezrites* (8:32), the phrase of the altar *Yahweh is Peace* (6:24). So the town holds
the altar of his call, the ephod that became *a snare*, and his grave. See [[Gideon's Ephod and Death]]. Source: stored WEB Judges
6:24; 8:27, 32.

**The seventy killed (9:5).** Now ingested. Abimelech *went to his father's house at Ophrah, and
killed his brothers the sons of Jerubbaal, being seventy persons, on one stone*; only Jotham was left,
*for he hid himself* (9:5). The town of Gideon's call, ephod, and grave becomes the place where his
house is destroyed. See [[Abimelech Made King at Shechem]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 9:5.

## Events here
- The angel under the oak; the call of Gideon; fire from the rock; the altar *Yahweh is Peace*
  (Judges 6:11–24).
- Joash's Baal altar and Asherah thrown down by night; an altar to Yahweh on the stronghold; the town
  demands Gideon's death; the name *Jerub-Baal* (Judges 6:25–32).
- Gideon's ephod set up, and *all Israel played the prostitute with it there* (Judges 8:27).
- Gideon buried in the tomb of Joash (Judges 8:32).
- Abimelech kills the seventy sons of Jerubbaal *on one stone*; Jotham hides and survives (Judges 9:5).
