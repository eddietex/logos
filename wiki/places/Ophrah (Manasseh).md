# Ophrah (Manasseh)

## Overview

*Ophrah of the Abiezrites* (Judges 6:24), Gideon's town in Manasseh. Its first scene has three
landmarks: the oak belonging to Joash under which Yahweh's angel sits, the winepress where Gideon
threshes wheat out of Midian's sight (6:11), and the altar Gideon builds and names *Yahweh is Peace*.
The narrator adds that the altar *To this day … is still in Ophrah of the Abiezrites* (6:24).

The town also has Joash's altar to Baal, with an Asherah beside it. Gideon throws them down by night and
builds an altar to Yahweh *on the top of this stronghold* (6:25–26), so within one day the town has
two new altars to Yahweh. In the morning *the men of the city* demand his death, *Bring out your son,
that he may die* (6:30). It is an Israelite town defending a Baal altar against an Israelite. Later the
town is the site of Gideon's ephod, which *all Israel* worships *there* (8:27), of his burial (8:32), and
of Abimelech's killing of the seventy sons of Jerubbaal *at Ophrah* (9:5). Those belong to later
pericopes. See [[Gideon Throws Down Baal's Altar]]. Its modern location is disputed and is not
checked here.

It is a different town from the Ophrah in Benjamin's allotment (Joshua 18:23); see
[[Ophrah (Benjamin)]].
