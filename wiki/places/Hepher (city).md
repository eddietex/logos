# Hepher (city)

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:17. No battle is narrated, and the stored WEB names the city nowhere else. The qualifier distinguishes it from Hepher the man of Manasseh's line, father of [[Zelophehad]] (Numbers 26:32–33; Joshua 17:3), who has no page yet; when he receives one it will take the matching person qualifier. The wiki does not connect the city and the clan; Joshua 19:13's *Gath Hepher* is likewise a separate name.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12 and 17, Numbers 26. No modern site is assigned.
