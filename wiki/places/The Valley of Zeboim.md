# The Valley of Zeboim

## Overview

*Gêʾ haṣṣəḇōʿîm* (1 Samuel 13:18, WLC). The third Philistine raiding company from [[Michmash]] turns *the way of
the border that looks down on the valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness*. The name is commonly glossed *valley of
hyenas* (recalled, not checked). The text locates it only by the border overlooking it and the direction *toward
the wilderness*; a ravine running east from the Benjamin plateau toward the Jordan valley is the usual
identification (recalled, not checked).

Not [[Zeboiim]], *ṣəḇōyim*, the city of the plain overthrown with Sodom and Gomorrah (Genesis 10:19; 14:2, 8;
Deuteronomy 29:23). The Hebrew names differ in a consonant, *ʿ* against *y*, and morphhb gives them different
lemmas (6650 and 6636). The English spellings are close enough to confuse, and the WEB keeps them apart.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:18; Deuteronomy 29:23. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-19.
