# Zelzah

## Overview

### At Rachel's tomb — 1 Samuel 10:2

*You will find two men by Rachel's tomb, on the border of Benjamin at Zelzah* (10:2). *Bəṣelṣaḥ* (WLC) occurs only
here in the WLC files searched (lemma 6766), and nowhere else does the Bible name the place. The WEB and the WLC
lemma both treat it as a place name. Whether it is a town, a landmark, or something else, and where it was, cannot
be settled from the text. Some readers take the word as corrupt or as a common noun. That is reported in
[[Rachel Weeping - Her Grave, Ramah, and Bethlehem]] and is recalled, not checked here. No modern site is assigned.
See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]] and [[Rachel's Tomb]].
