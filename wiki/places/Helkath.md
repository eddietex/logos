# Helkath

## Overview

Named first in Asher’s border (25), in Asher’s register, whose stated total is twenty-two cities with their villages (30–31). Joshua 21:31 names a Helkath again among Levitical cities; that verse is not yet ingested.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
