# Bezek

## Overview

### Saul counts the army — 1 Samuel 11:8

*He counted them in Bezek; and the children of Israel were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty
thousand* (11:8). *Bəḇāzeq* (WLC). Saul musters here between the summons of the cut oxen and the night march on
Jabesh Gilead. In the WLC files searched (Genesis through 2 Samuel) the name occurs only here and at Judges
1:4–5. Whether the two are one place is not stated, and the location is not verified here; the usual placement
north of Shechem, west of the Jordan opposite Jabesh, is recalled and not checked. See
[[Saul Rescues Jabesh Gilead]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 11:8; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-18.

### Judah goes up first — Judges 1:1–7

The site of the first battle after Joshua's death. Judah and Simeon strike *ten thousand men in
Bezek* (4), find [[Adoni-Bezek]], *lord of Bezek*, there, and defeat the Canaanites and Perizzites
(5). The verse writes the name twice: the king's name and then *in Bezek*, the place.

The text gives no location. 1 Samuel 11:8 names a Bezek where Saul *counted them* before relieving
Jabesh Gilead (now ingested; see above). That Bezek is usually
placed north of Shechem, outside Judah's lot. Whether the two are the same place, and where either
lay, has not been verified here. If they are one, Judah's first campaign began well to the north of
its own territory.

See [[Judah Goes Up First]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:4–5; comparisons as cited.
