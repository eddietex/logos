# Bezek

## Overview

### Judah goes up first — Judges 1:1–7

The site of the first battle after Joshua's death. Judah and Simeon strike *ten thousand men in
Bezek* (4), find [[Adoni-Bezek]], *lord of Bezek*, there, and defeat the Canaanites and Perizzites
(5). The verse writes the name twice: the king's name and then *in Bezek*, the place.

The text gives no location. 1 Samuel 11:8 names a Bezek where Saul *counted them* before relieving
Jabesh Gilead (WEB checked at bible-api.com; 1 Samuel not yet ingested). That Bezek is usually
placed north of Shechem, outside Judah's lot. Whether the two are the same place, and where either
lay, has not been verified here. If they are one, Judah's first campaign began well to the north of
its own territory.

See [[Judah Goes Up First]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:4–5; comparisons as cited.
