# Shamir (Ephraim)

## Overview

### Tola's town — Judges 10:1–2

Tola the son of Puah, *a man of Issachar*, *lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim*, judged
Israel twenty-three years, *and died, and was buried in Shamir* (10:1–2). The town is named only in
these two verses of Judges. Its site is not given and is not identified here.

The text does not say why a man of Issachar lived in Ephraim's hills, outside his tribe's northern
lot (Joshua 19:17–23).

Qualified because Judah also has a hill-country town named Shamir (Joshua 15:48), now
[[Shamir (Judah)]]. The WLC spells both *šāmîr* (lemma 8069). Nothing in either text connects them.

See [[Tola and Jair]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 10:1–2; Joshua 15:48; 19:17–23; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
