# Carmel (mountain)

## Overview

Carmel supplies the comparison for the woman's head in [[Return, Return, Shulammite]]
(Song 7:5). This page takes the likely mountain reading without claiming that the verse
explicitly says mountain or that the lovers travel there.

1 Kings 18:19 explicitly names Mount Carmel. Joshua 15:55 also lists a Carmel among
Judah's towns. The qualifier keeps that town distinct; a future page for it should be
Carmel (town). No claim about the precise feature compared to her head is required.

Sources: stored WEB Song 7:5; 1 Kings 18:19 and Joshua 15:55 checked at bible-api.com
(`translation=web`), 2026-10-28, with single-verse requests; neither book added to raw.
