# Kamon

## Overview

### Jair's grave — Judges 10:5

*Jair died, and was buried in Kamon* (10:5). That is the whole record. In the WLC books of Genesis
through Judges, *qāmôn* (lemma 7056) occurs only here. The verse does not locate it. Because Jair is
*the Gileadite* and his sons' cities are *in the land of Gilead* (10:3–4), it is commonly placed in
Gilead, but that is an inference, not a statement of the text.

See [[Tola and Jair]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 10:3–5; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-15.
