# Gai

## Overview

### *As far as Gai* — 1 Samuel 17:52

After Goliath falls, *the men of Israel and of Judah arose and shouted, and pursued the Philistines as far as Gai and to
the gates of Ekron* (17:52, stored WEB). The Hebrew is *ʿaḏ-bôʾăḵā gayʾ*, *until you come to a valley* (WLC).
*Gayʾ* (morphhb lemma 1516) is the ordinary noun for *valley*, the word in *the valley of Hinnom* (Joshua 15:8, WLC),
and nothing else in the verse marks it as a name. The WEB prints it as one, so the wiki gives it a page; whether a
place called Gai is meant, or simply *a valley* on the way west, is not settled by the text. The Septuagint is
reported to read *Gath* here (recalled, not checked; no Septuagint text was consulted), which would pair it with the
*Gath* and *Ekron* at the end of the same verse. No site is assigned. See [[David Kills Goliath]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 17:52; Joshua 15:8. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-21.
