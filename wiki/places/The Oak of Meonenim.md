# The Oak of Meonenim

## Overview

At dawn Gaal sees Abimelech's men coming: *Behold, people are coming down by the middle of the land,
and one company comes by the way of the oak of Meonenim* (Judges 9:37). *ʾĒlôn məʿônənîm* is *the oak of
the soothsayers*. *Məʿônənîm* is the participle of *ʿānan* (WLC), the practice the law forbids among the
diviners' arts (Leviticus 19:26; Deuteronomy 18:10, 14). The tree's name says it was a place of
divination. The text says nothing more about it.

Shechem has had many named trees: Abram's *oak of Moreh* (Genesis 12:6), the oak where Jacob hid the
household gods (Genesis 35:4), the oak by which Joshua set up the covenant stone (Joshua 24:26), and *the
oak of the pillar* where Abimelech was made king (Judges 9:6). Whether the oak of Meonenim is one of
these is not said. The wiki does not identify it with any of them.
