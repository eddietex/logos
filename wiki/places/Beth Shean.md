# Beth Shean

## Overview

### Not driven out — Judges 1:27

First of Manasseh's five cities not driven out (27), as in Joshua 17:11–12.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

### Joseph asks for more — Joshua 17:14–18

The children of Joseph identify this city and its dependent towns as a center of Canaanite iron chariots (16). Its allocation to Manasseh in 17:11 therefore does not establish secure possession.

See [[The Children of Joseph Ask for More]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:14–18.

City with dependent towns assigned to Manasseh within the Asher/Issachar territorial setting (11).
The collective non-removal notice covers these cities (12–13). No modern site identification is
attempted.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.
