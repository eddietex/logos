# Beth Shemesh (Naphtali)

## Overview

Last of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (38), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). Distinct from [[Beth Shemesh]] on Judah’s border (15:10) and [[Beth Shemesh (Issachar)]] (19:22).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
