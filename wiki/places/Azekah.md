# Azekah

## Overview

### The Philistine camp — 1 Samuel 17:1

The Philistines camp *between Socoh and Azekah, in Ephesdammim* (17:1), and Israel faces them from the valley of Elah
(17:2). The pair Socoh and Azekah stand side by side in Judah's lowland list (Joshua 15:35). Azekah was the limit of
Joshua's pursuit (Joshua 10:10–11); here it marks the edge of a Philistine camp inside Judah. See
[[Goliath Defies Israel]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:10–11; 15:35; 1 Samuel 17:1–2.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Listed in the first lowland group (35). The pursuit of 10:10–11 reached it; the allotment now assigns it to Judah.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

A limit named in the pursuit (10) and in the hail-stricken flight from Beth Horon (11). This does
not yet narrate a siege of the city.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No modern identification is assigned.
