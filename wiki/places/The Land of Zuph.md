# The Land of Zuph

## Overview

### Where the search ends — 1 Samuel 9:5–6

*When they had come to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant … "Come, and let us return"* (9:5), and the
servant answers that *there is in this city a man of God* (9:6). *Ṣûp̄* (lemma 6689) occurs elsewhere in the WLC
files searched only at 1:1, as the name of Elkanah's ancestor, *Zuph, an Ephraimite*, in the verse that places
Elkanah at *Ramathaim Zophim*. The district therefore seems to be Samuel's own country, and the unnamed city of the
seer is often identified with [[Ramah]], where Samuel lived (7:17; 8:4). The text of chapter 9 never names the city,
and the identification is reported, not asserted. The page is titled *The Land of Zuph* rather than *Zuph* so that a
later page for Elkanah's ancestor will not collide with it. See [[Saul Comes to the Seer]].
