# Jericho

## Overview

### The fall of Jericho — Joshua 6:1–27

The formerly shut city is encircled, its wall falls, its inhabitants and livestock are destroyed except for Rahab’s household, and it is burned (1–25). Joshua’s curse targets its rebuilding (26). The text provides no archaeological date or mechanism for the wall’s collapse.

See [[The Fall of Jericho]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 6:1–27; comparisons identified above.


### The commander by Jericho — Joshua 5:13–15

Joshua encounters the armed commander by Jericho (13). The ground on which he stands
is called holy (15), but the text gives no exact site and does not declare the whole
city a sanctuary. Jericho has not yet fallen in this unit.

See [[The Commander of Yahweh's Army]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 5:13–15; comparisons as cited.

### Passover and the end of manna — Joshua 5:10–12

The plains of Jericho locate the Passover at Gilgal (10). Israel eats the land’s produce
before the city’s capture; this is not a celebration inside a conquered Jericho.

See [[Passover at Gilgal and the End of the Manna]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 5:10–12; comparisons as cited.

### The twelve stones — Joshua 4:1–24

The eastern contingent reaches its plains ready for battle (13), and Gilgal is on its east border
(19). These are arrival notices, not a report of the city’s conquest.
See [[The Twelve Stones from the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 4:1–24.

### Crossing the Jordan — Joshua 3:1–17

Israel crosses the Jordan near Jericho (16). The city locates the crossing, but no siege or entry into Jericho occurs in this pericope.

See [[Crossing the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 3:1–17.


### Rahab and the spies — Joshua 2:1–24

The spies enter Rahab’s house in Jericho; its king orders their surrender, but she conceals them (1–7). Her house is on the city wall, allowing escape through a window (15). This is now an event within the city, unlike its earlier use as a distant geographical landmark; the city has not yet fallen.

See [[Rahab and the Spies]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 2:1–24.

### The death of Moses — Deuteronomy 34:1–12

Nebo is *over against Jericho* (1), and the view ends with *the Plain of the valley of Jericho the city of palm trees* (3).

See [[The Death of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 34:1–12.

### Commanded to ascend Nebo — Deuteronomy 32:48–52

Jericho locates Mount Nebo across from it (49). The city is a geographical reference point; no entry
into or conquest of it occurs here.

See [[Moses Commanded to Ascend Nebo]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 32:48–52.

### Marriage and tribal inheritance — Numbers 36:1–13

Jericho is the landmark used to locate the plains of Moab by the Jordan (13), not a city entered
or conquered in this unit.

See [[The Marriage of the Daughters of Zelophehad]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 36; comparisons as cited.

### Levitical cities and refuge — Numbers 35:1–34

Jericho locates the speech in the plains of Moab beside the Jordan (1). It is not named as a refuge city or reported conquered here.

See [[The Levitical Cities and the Cities of Refuge]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 35; comparisons as cited.

### The borders of the land — Numbers 34:1–29

Jericho locates the already received inheritance beyond the Jordan eastward (15); no conquest of Jericho is narrated here.

See [[The Borders of the Land]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 34.

### Drive out the inhabitants — Numbers 33:50–56

Jericho locates the speech by the Jordan in the plains of Moab (50). It remains a geographical
reference point, not a city reported conquered here.

See [[Drive Out the Inhabitants]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:50–56.

### The stages of the journey — Numbers 33:1–49

The final camp is located by the Jordan at Jericho (48), not inside a conquered city.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49.

### The second census — Numbers 26:1–65

The city is a geographical reference for the census across the Jordan (3, 63), not a city entered in
this account.

See [[The Second Census]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 26.
