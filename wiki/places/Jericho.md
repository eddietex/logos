# Jericho

## Overview

### The second census — Numbers 26:1–65

The city is a geographical reference for the census across the Jordan (3, 63), not a city entered in
this account.

See [[The Second Census]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 26.


Named as the geographical reference for Israel’s camp beyond the Jordan in the plains of Moab (1).
Israel does not enter or conquer Jericho in this unit.
