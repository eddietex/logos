# Baal Tamar

## Overview

### Where Israel formed for the third day — Judges 20:33

*All the men of Israel rose up out of their place, and set themselves in array at Baal Tamar. Then the
ambushers of Israel broke out of their place, even out of Maareh Geba* (Judges 20:33). The main army,
which had drawn Benjamin out onto the highways toward Bethel and Gibeah (31–32), turns and forms its
line here while the ambush breaks out behind Benjamin.

*Baʿal tāmār* (33, WLC) is *lord* or *Baal of the palm*. The name (lemma 1193) occurs only in this verse
in the WLC files of Genesis through 1 Samuel and Hosea searched. Whether the name preserves a shrine of
Baal, the text does not say. The verse places it near Gibeah and the Bethel road and no more; no modern
site is assigned. Deborah judged *under Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and Bethel* (4:5) in the same
district, a different word for the palm (*tōmer*, WLC); the text does not connect them, and neither does
the wiki.

See [[The War Against Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 4:5; 20:31–33; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-17.
