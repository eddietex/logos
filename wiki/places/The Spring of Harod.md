# The Spring of Harod

## Overview

### Gideon's camp — Judges 7:1–8

*Then Jerubbaal, who is Gideon, and all the people who were with him, rose up early, and encamped
beside the spring of Harod. Midian's camp was on the north side of them, by the hill of Moreh, in the
valley* (7:1). The spring is Gideon's camp, south of Midian's, with the enemy *beneath him in the
valley* (7:8). It is also, by the narrative's logic, the water to which Yahweh has the ten thousand
brought down to drink (7:4–6). The text does not say so in as many words; it says only *the water*.

The Hebrew is *ʿên ḥărōd* (WLC), *the spring of Harod*. The name has the consonants of *ḥārēd*,
*trembling*, the word of the proclamation two verses later: *Whoever is fearful and trembling, let him
return* (7:3). A play on the name is often noted; the narrator does not draw it. In the WLC of
Genesis–Deuteronomy, Joshua, and Judges, *Ḥărōd* occurs only at Judges 7:1. No modern site is assigned
here.
