# Madon

## Overview

Jobab’s royal city, named in Jabin’s summons (1). It belongs to the coalition whose cities Joshua captures (12); no separate siege is narrated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Its king, Jobab, is counted (12:19). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
