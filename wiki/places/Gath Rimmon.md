# Gath Rimmon

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Dan to the remaining Kohathites (21:24). Allocated with its suburbs from half-Manasseh to the remaining Kohathites (21:25).  WEB repeats Gath Rimmon in Dan’s grant (24) and half-Manasseh’s grant (25). This page records both without deciding whether two towns, shared rights, or a textual problem explains the repetition.

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

Named in Dan’s border list (41–46), for which no city total is given (45). Joshua 21:24 names a Levitical Gath Rimmon among Dan’s cities. Distinct from the Rimmon pages.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
