# Pihahiroth

## Overview

**The camp by the sea, where Israel is overtaken and from which it crosses.**

> **2** "Speak to the children of Israel, that they turn back and encamp before Pihahiroth, between Migdol
> and the sea, before Baal Zephon. You shall encamp opposite it by the sea." (Exodus 14:2)

**It is reached by turning back.** From [[Etham]], God tells the people to *"turn back"* — *wəyāšūbû* — and
camp here, so that Pharaoh will say *"They are entangled in the land. The wilderness has shut them in"*
(14:3). **The camp is chosen as bait**, and the reason is given in advance: *"I will harden Pharaoh's heart,
and he will follow after them; and I will get honor over Pharaoh"* (14:4). Pharaoh's army overtakes them
*"encamping by the sea, beside Pihahiroth, before Baal Zephon"* (14:9).

***Pî haḥîrōt*** — **the WLC writes it as two words, *pî*, *mouth (of)*, and *haḥîrōt*, with the article**
(14:2, 9), and Numbers 33:8 shortens it to *"Hahiroth"* (*haḥîrōt*; WEB, WLC). **The first element is the
Hebrew for *mouth*, and the name has been read as *mouth of the canals* or as a Hebrew rendering of an
Egyptian name**; both are reported, not checked in this pass. Numbers 33:7 places it *"before Baal Zephon"*
and the camp *"before Migdol"* (WEB; *not yet ingested*).

**Where it is is not known**, and depends on which body of water [[The Red Sea]] names; the wiki adopts no
location.
