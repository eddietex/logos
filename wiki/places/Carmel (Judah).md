# Carmel (Judah)

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the hill country (Joshua 15:55), one of the *ten cities with their villages* of the third hill-country group (55–57). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

A town in Judah's hill country, distinct from [[Carmel (mountain)]] on the northern coast.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.

**Saul's monument — 1 Samuel 15:12.** Returning from Amalek, *Saul came to Carmel, and behold, he set up a monument
for himself, and turned, and passed on, and went down to Gilgal*. The verse names no region; this Carmel, on a
southern route near Maon, is the likely one, and 1 Samuel 25:2 places Nabal's flocks at *Carmel* with Maon (stored WEB;
not yet ingested). The *monument* is *yāḏ*, a *hand* (WLC). See [[Saul Rejected over Amalek]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 15:12; 25:2. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-20.
