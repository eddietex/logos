# The Valley of Iphtah El

## Overview

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

Asher’s border reaches Zebulun and the valley of Iphtah El northward to Beth Emek and Neiel (27). The valley that ended Zebulun’s boundary (14) is thus a shared landmark of two lots.

See [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48; comparisons as cited.

The endpoint of Zebulun’s northern boundary after Hannathon (14). Not equated with the town Iphtah in Judah (15:43).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23. No precise modern site is assigned.
