# Heshbon

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Gad to Merari’s descendants (21:39).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Sihon’s royal center (10, 21), listed with Reuben’s cities (17) and used again as a starting point in Gad’s boundary (26). A boundary reference need not be an exclusive grant of the same city twice.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### The Gibeonite deception — Joshua 9:1–27

Sihon is named as its king in the envoys’ account of Yahweh’s victories (10); the embassy does not
originate here.

See [[The Gibeonite Deception]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27; comparisons as cited.


### The covenant in Moab — Deuteronomy 29:1–29

Sihon is identified as king of Heshbon in the recollection of victory (7). The present assembly is in Moab, not a new battle here.

See [[The Covenant in Moab and the Hidden Things]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 29:1–29.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

Sihon is identified as having lived here (46). The instruction itself takes place in the valley opposite Beth Peor, not in Heshbon.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

### Og’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

Sihon is identified by his residence here (2) and as its king (6); his defeat is the precedent for
Og’s.

See [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.

### Sihon’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 2:24–37

Sihon is repeatedly identified as its king (24, 26, 30). The account moves from a request to pass
through his land to the conquest of his cities.

See [[The Defeat of Sihon Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:24–37.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

Named as Sihon’s residence in the retrospective notice of his defeat (4).

See [[The Words of Moses Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

### Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan — Numbers 32:1–42

Named in the request (3) and built by Reuben (37). Sihon's former capital becomes a Reubenite town.
Joshua 13 names Heshbon in Reuben's border and as the start of Gad's (13:17, 26; WEB via bible-api.com, not
yet ingested).

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.

### Sihon and Og — Numbers 21:21–35

Sihon’s city and an earlier possession of Moab (25–26). Israel occupies it after defeating him; the
poem celebrates its strength and then speaks of its ruin (27–30). The historical account supplies a
different setting from the pools imagery in Song of Solomon 7:4.

See [[Sihon and Og]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.


A city whose pools beside the gate of [[Bathrabbim]] supply the comparison for the
woman's eyes in [[Return, Return, Shulammite]] (7:4). The poem gives no account of a visit.
Numbers 21:26 identifies Heshbon as Sihon's city, taken from the former king of Moab;
that political history is not invoked by the love poem.

Sources: stored WEB Song 7:4; Numbers 21:26 checked at bible-api.com
(`translation=web`), 2026-10-28, by a single-verse request. Numbers was not added to raw.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Sihon's residence, recalled at the head of the list of kings (12:2); verse 5 calls him *Sihon king of Heshbon*. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
