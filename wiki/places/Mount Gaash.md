# Mount Gaash

## Overview

A mountain in the hill country of Ephraim, named only as a landmark: Joshua is buried at
Timnathserah, *on the north of the mountain of Gaash* (Joshua 24:30). Judges 2:9 repeats the
location for *Timnath Heres* (stored WEB; see [[Another Generation Arose]]). The text
says nothing else about it, and no modern site is assigned here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 24:30.
