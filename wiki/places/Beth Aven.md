# Beth Aven

## Overview

The northern boundary ends this stage at the wilderness of Beth Aven (12). The passage names a wilderness bearing, not a new occupation or an equation with Bethel.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.

### The Philistine camp — 1 Samuel 13:5

The Philistines *encamped in Michmash, eastward of Beth Aven*, *qiḏmaṯ bêṯ ʾāwen* (13:5, WLC). The text uses it as
a bearing for [[Michmash]], as Joshua 18:12 used it for a boundary; it does not equate it with Bethel, which 13:2
names separately. See [[Saul's Sacrifice at Gilgal]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:2, 5; Joshua 18:12; Hebrew
WLC, 2026-11-18.
