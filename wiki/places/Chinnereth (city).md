# Chinnereth (city)

## Overview

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (35), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). Because this verse lists it as a city, it has its own page; it is not merged with [[The Sea of Chinnereth]] or with the open referent of [[Chinneroth]] (11:2), though the shared name suggests the region.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
