# Maacath

## Overview

The land of the Maacathites, named with Geshur as a border of Og's kingdom of Bashan (Joshua 12:5) and in Deuteronomy 3:14, where Jair takes Argob *to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites*. Joshua 13:13 says Israel *didn't drive out* the Maacathites, and *Geshur and Maacath live within Israel to this day*. The title follows the WEB's spelling in 13:13. The place is not connected here with any person named Maacah; no such identification is made in the text.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 and Deuteronomy 3 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
