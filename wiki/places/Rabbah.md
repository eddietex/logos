# Rabbah

## Overview

Rabbah of the children of Ammon is where the text points to Og’s iron bedstead (11). The notice
supplies dimensions but no account of its transport, discovery, or independent archaeological
verification. It does not say Israel conquered Rabbah.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.
