# Elon (city)

## Overview

Named with Timnah and Ekron in Dan’s border list (41–46), for which no city total is given (43). The qualifier keeps the town distinct from [[Elon the Hittite]] and from a son of Zebulun named Elon in Genesis 46:14, who does not yet have a page.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
