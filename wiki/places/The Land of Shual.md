# The Land of Shual

## Overview

*ʾEreṣ šûʿāl* (1 Samuel 13:17, WLC), the destination of the first of the three Philistine raiding companies that
go out from [[Michmash]], on *the way that leads to Ophrah* ([[Ophrah (Benjamin)]]). The name is commonly glossed
*fox land* or *jackal land* (not checked against a lexicon). It occurs only here in the WLC of Genesis–2 Samuel
(morphhb lemma 7777). The text gives no location beyond the road to Ophrah; a district north of Michmash is the
usual placement (recalled, not checked).

Not [[Hazar Shual]], *fox village*, the town of Judah and Simeon in the far south (Joshua 15:28; 19:3). The page
is titled *The Land of Shual* so that it cannot collide with a later person named Shual.
