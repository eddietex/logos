# The Brook of Egypt

## Overview

The southern border reaches this brook from Azmon and ends at the sea. This page does not equate the brook with the Nile or identify it with Genesis 15:18’s river of Egypt.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34:5. No precise modern site is assigned here.
