# Mount Hor (northern border)

## Overview

A northern boundary landmark between the great sea and the entrance of Hamath. Its role differs from [[Mount Hor]], the mountain beside Edom where Aaron died (Numbers 20:22–29). The shared name does not justify merging these geographical descriptions.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34:7–8. No precise modern site is assigned here.
