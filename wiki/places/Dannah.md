# Dannah

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the hill country (Joshua 15:49), one of the *eleven cities with their villages* of the first hill-country group (48–51). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
