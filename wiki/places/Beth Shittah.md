# Beth Shittah

## Overview

*The army fled as far as Beth Shittah toward Zererah* (Judges 7:22). The first point named on the line
of Midian's flight from the valley, after Yahweh *set every man's sword against his fellow*. The name
means *house of the acacia*, *šiṭṭâ* being the acacia wood of the ark and tabernacle (gloss reported,
not checked). It occurs in Genesis–Judges only here (WLC). The site is not identified here; the flight
runs toward the Jordan, since the Ephraimites are sent to hold its waters (7:24).
