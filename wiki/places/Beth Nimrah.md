# Beth Nimrah

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Named in Gad’s valley portion (27), corresponding to its building by Gad in Numbers 32:36.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

A town in the Jordan valley built by the children of Gad (Numbers 32:36). The request of 32:3 names a
*Nimrah*; the wiki takes it as the same town, since the two lists overlap so closely, but the text does not
say so. Joshua 13:27 lists Beth Nimrah in Gad's portion *in the valley* (WEB via bible-api.com, now ingested).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
