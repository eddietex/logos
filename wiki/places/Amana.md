# Amana

## Overview

Named once in the ingested text. In [[A Garden Locked and Opened]] the man calls the bride to come
with him from Lebanon and to *"look from the top of Amana"* (Song of Solomon 4:8). The Hebrew is
*mērōʾš ʾămānâ* (Sefaria, *Miqra according to the Masorah*). It is named with Senir and Hermon, so it
is usually taken as a summit of the Anti-Lebanon range. The verse gives no location, and the wiki
does not fix one.

**A river of Damascus.** At 2 Kings 5:12 Naaman asks, *"Aren't Abanah and Pharpar, the rivers of
Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel?"* (checked at bible-api.com, `translation=web`,
2026-10-28). The WEB follows the written Hebrew form, *ʾăḇānâ*. The Masoretic text marks *ʾămānâ*,
*Amana*, as the form to be read (Sefaria shows the written and read forms). So the same name may
belong both to a mountain and to a river flowing from the mountains toward Damascus. That is a
reasonable connection, not one the texts state.

Sources: Song of Solomon 4:8 from the stored WEB; 2 Kings 5:12 at bible-api.com on 2026-10-28,
without fetching the book. Hebrew of both from Sefaria's *Miqra according to the Masorah*.
