# Debir (Judah border)

## Overview

The north border ascends to Debir from the valley of Achor (7). This boundary occurrence is kept distinct from Debir near Hebron in Joshua 15:15 and Debir in Gad (13:26); no identification is supplied by this verse.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12. No precise modern location or unstated biography is asserted.
