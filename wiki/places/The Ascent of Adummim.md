# The Ascent of Adummim

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

Geliloth stands opposite this ascent (17). Joshua 15:7 places Gilgal opposite it; the two landmark names are not silently equated.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

The ascent faces the Gilgal bearing on Judah’s north border and is south of the river mentioned in verse 7. The river is unnamed in that verse.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12. No precise modern location or unstated biography is asserted.
