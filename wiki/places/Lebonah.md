# Lebonah

## Overview

### A landmark for Shiloh — Judges 21:19

The elders locate Shiloh's yearly feast by three bearings: *on the north of Bethel, on the east side of
the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah* (Judges 21:19). Lebonah is
the northern one. That is all the text says of it.

*Ləḇônâ* (19, WLC) occurs only in this verse in the WLC files of Genesis through 1 Samuel searched (lemma
3829). The name is close to *ləḇōnâ*, *frankincense* (Leviticus 2:1, lemma 3828, WLC); the wiki notes the resemblance and draws nothing from
it. No modern site is assigned here.

See [[Wives for Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 21:19; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-17.
