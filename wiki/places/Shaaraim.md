# Shaaraim

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the lowland (Joshua 15:36), one of the *fourteen cities with their villages* of the first lowland group (33–36). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.

### The road of the fallen — 1 Samuel 17:52

After Goliath's death *the wounded of the Philistines fell down by the way to Shaaraim, even to Gath and to Ekron*
(17:52). In the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, and Ruth) the name occurs only here and at Joshua
15:36 (WLC; lemma 8189), so the lowland town of Judah's list is the likeliest referent, on the road west from the
valley of Elah. That *šaʿărayim* means *two gates*, beside *the gates of Ekron* in the same verse, is commonly said
(recalled, not checked). See [[David Kills Goliath]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 17:52; Joshua 15:36. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-21.
