# Jokneam

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:22, *Jokneam in Carmel*. No battle is narrated. Zebulun's border reaches *the brook that is before Jokneam* (19:11), and the city is given to the Merarite Levites from Zebulun (21:34). *In Carmel* is linked to [[Carmel (mountain)]] because the setting is northern and Asher's border reaches *to Carmel westward* (19:26); the verse itself says only *Carmel*.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
