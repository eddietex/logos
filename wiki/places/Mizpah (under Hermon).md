# Mizpah (under Hermon)

## Overview

The land of Mizpah under Hermon locates the Hivites in the summons (3); the valley of Mizpah eastward marks the pursuit (8). These notices are grouped provisionally, without equating this northern Mizpah with other places of the same name.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.
