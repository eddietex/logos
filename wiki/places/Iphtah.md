# Iphtah

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the lowland (Joshua 15:43), one of the *nine cities with their villages* of the third lowland group (42–44). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Distinct from the valley of Iphtah El on Zebulun's border (19:27).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
