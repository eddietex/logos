# Tirzah (city)

## Overview

The city used beside [[Jerusalem]] as a comparison for the woman's beauty in
[[My Dove Is Unique]] (Song of Solomon 6:4). Joshua 12:24 lists a king of Tirzah, confirming
the place-name context independently. The Song gives no travel, building, siege, or exact
location here; its contribution is a city whose beauty can praise a beloved.

The qualifier distinguishes this city from the woman Tirzah, named among Zelophehad's
daughters in Numbers 26:33. That person is not part of this pericope and has no page yet;
when she is ingested, her page will take the matching person qualifier. No personal name
is inferred for the Song's woman from the city comparison.

Sources: stored WEB Song of Solomon 6:4. Joshua 12:24 and Numbers 26:33 verified on
2026-10-28 through the repository's bible-api.com WEB source (`translation=web`), as two
single-verse requests; neither book was added to raw. No archaeological identification or
composition date is asserted.
