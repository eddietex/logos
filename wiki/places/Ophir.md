# Ophir

## Overview

**The Bible's gold country, a byword for the finest gold, and never located.**

Ophir enters Scripture as a name in a genealogy: a son of Joktan, in the Shemite line of
[[The Table of Nations]] (Genesis 10:29), listed just before [[Havilah]] — another gold country — and
Jobab. Like the other names in that list it is a people and a territory before it is a man, and the
Bible never treats it as a person again. From Solomon's reign onward it is a *place you sail to*:
Hiram's and Solomon's fleets bring gold from Ophir to Jerusalem (1 Kings 9:28; 10:11), Jehoshaphat's
fleet is wrecked trying to reach it (1 Kings 22:48), and *"the gold of Ophir"* becomes a stock phrase
for gold of the highest grade (Psalm 45:9; Isaiah 13:12; 1 Chronicles 29:4).

**Where it was is unknown.** The proposals are south-western Arabia, which suits Genesis 10's placing
of it among Joktan's sons beside Sheba and Havilah; the East African coast (Somalia, or the *Punt* of
Egyptian texts); and India, which suits the cargoes of 1 Kings 10:22 and the Septuagint's spellings.
The voyage *"once every three years"* of 1 Kings 10:22, if it refers to the Ophir fleet, suggests a
long one. An ostracon from Tell Qasile reading *gold of Ophir to Beth-horon* shows the phrase was a
real trade designation, not only a poetic one. The wiki adopts none of the locations.

**Naming.** The page carries the bare name because, like [[Havilah]], [[Uz]] and [[Tema]], the
Genesis 10 eponym has no person page of his own. If a later pass makes one for Joktan's son, the
person/place rule in `AGENTS.md` applies and both pages take a qualifier.

## Appears in

- [[The Table of Nations]] — Genesis 10:29, Ophir son of Joktan, followed by Havilah and Jobab.
- [[Eliphaz's Third Speech]] — Job 22:24, **its first appearance in Job, and a byword rather than a
  location:** *"Lay your treasure in the dust, the gold of Ophir among the stones of the brooks. The
  Almighty will be your treasure."* Eliphaz tells Job to throw the finest gold in the world into a
  wadi bed, on the assumption that wealth had become his god. Job answers under oath at 31:24–25.
- [[Where Shall Wisdom Be Found]] — Job 28:16, wisdom *"can't be valued with the gold of Ophir"*: the
  same byword, now as the measure of what cannot buy wisdom. Six verses earlier the miner of 28:1–11
  could have dug it out; the hymn's point is that what he can dig is not what is needed. See
  [[Better Than Gold - The Price of Wisdom]].

## Related places

- [[Havilah]] — the other gold country of Genesis, named beside Ophir among Joktan's sons.
- [[Cush]] — whose *"topaz"* is named three verses after Ophir's gold in Job 28:19, the far south
  beside the far east.
- [[Uz]] — Job's land, whose name is also a genealogical entry in Genesis 10 and 36.
