# Bamoth Baal

## Overview

Listed in Reuben’s portion between Dibon and Beth Baal Meon (17). The passage does not expressly identify it with Bamoth in Numbers 21:19 or the high places of Baal in Numbers 22:41; this page keeps the allotment name separate.
