# Moseroth

## Overview

### Renewed tablets and onward journey — Deuteronomy 10:1–11

Compare Moserah in Deuteronomy 10:6, where Aaron dies after Israel travels from Beeroth Bene Jaakan.
Numbers 33:30–31 places Moseroth before Bene Jaakan, and 33:38 places Aaron’s death later at Mount
Hor. The possible name correspondence does not resolve the route and death-location tensions.

See [[New Tablets and the Journey Onward]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 10:1–11.

The itinerary places this stage after [[Hashmonah]] and before [[Bene Jaakan]].

Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49. No modern site is assigned.
