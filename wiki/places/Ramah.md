# Ramah

## Overview

**Other Ramahs.** Joshua 19 names a Ramah on Asher’s border (29) and one among Naphtali’s cities (36); they have their own pages, [[Ramah (Asher)]] and [[Ramah (Naphtali)]], and are not Benjamin’s Ramah.

Named among Benjamin’s second group of fourteen cities with their villages (25). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.
