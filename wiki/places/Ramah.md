# Ramah

## Overview

### The anointing at the edge of the city — 1 Samuel 9:26–27

The next morning Samuel walks Saul *down at the end of the city* and anoints him there (9:27–10:1). The city is
still unnamed. If it is Ramah, as is often proposed (see below), the first king is anointed outside Samuel's own
town. The route of the signs, past Rachel's tomb *on the border of Benjamin* (10:2), is one reason some place the
city elsewhere. The identification is recorded, not asserted. See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]] and [[Rachel's Tomb]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 9:26–10:2.

### The seer's unnamed city — 1 Samuel 9:5–25

Saul's search ends in *the land of Zuph*, where his servant knows of *a man of God* in *this city* (9:5–6). Samuel is
found there, at a sacrifice on the high place, and has a house with a housetop in it (9:12–25). The chapter never
names the city. Samuel's home is Ramah (7:17; 8:4), Elkanah's town is *Ramathaim Zophim* (1:1), and Zuph is the
name of Elkanah's ancestor (1:1), so the city is often identified with Ramah; but the text does not say so, and some
place the episode elsewhere because the route of chapter 10 runs past Rachel's tomb (10:2, stored WEB; not yet
ingested). The identification is recorded, not asserted. See [[Saul Comes to the Seer]] and [[The Land of Zuph]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:1; 7:17; 8:4; 9:5–25; 10:2.

### The elders come to Ramah — 1 Samuel 8:4

*All the elders of Israel gathered themselves together and came to Samuel to Ramah* (8:4), *hārāmāṯâ* (WLC),
to his house (7:17), and there ask for *a king to judge us like all the nations* (8:5). See [[Israel Asks for a King]].
Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 8:1–22. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Ramathaim Zophim, Elkanah's home — 1 Samuel 1:1, 19

Elkanah is *a certain man of Ramathaim Zophim, of the hill country of Ephraim* (1:1), and after Shiloh the
family *came to their house to Ramah* (1:19). The WLC has *hārāmāṯayim ṣôp̄îm* in 1:1, a dual form, *the
two heights*, with *Zophim*, and *hārāmāṯâ*, *to Ramah*, in 1:19; the narrative treats them as one town.
*Zophim* matches the ancestor named at the end of the verse, *Zuph*, and *the land of Zuph* of 9:5 (not yet
ingested). This is Samuel's Ramah: his birthplace here, and later his home, his altar, and his grave (7:17;
25:1; the grave not yet ingested).

**Which Ramah — flagged.** The wiki files Samuel's Ramah on this page, beside Benjamin's Ramah of Joshua
18:25, because 1 Samuel calls it simply *Ramah* and gives no other qualifier. Whether the two are the same
town is disputed: 1:1 places Ramathaim *of the hill country of Ephraim*, and Benjamin's Ramah lies in
Benjamin's allotment. Deborah's palm, already filed here, also stands *between Ramah and Bethel in the hill
country of Ephraim* (Judges 4:5), so the hill country's name may reach into Benjamin. Proposed sites differ
(recalled, not verified); if a later passage makes the distinction clear, this page will be split. See
[[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:1, 19; 7:17; 9:5; 25:1; Joshua 18:25; Judges 4:5; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### *Gibeah, or Ramah* — Judges 19:13

Near Jebus at evening the Levite says, *Come and let us draw near to one of these places; and we will lodge
in Gibeah, or in Ramah* (19:13). They reach Gibeah first. Ramah is in Benjamin's list (Joshua 18:25), and the
narrator places Gibeah in Benjamin too (19:14). See [[The Levite's Concubine at Gibeah]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 19:13; Joshua 18:25.

### Deborah's palm — Judges 4:5

Deborah sits *under Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim*
(4:5). Paired with Bethel, it is most naturally Benjamin's Ramah (Joshua 18:25), and the wiki files
it here; the verse itself places the palm *in the hill country of Ephraim* and does not name a tribe. See [[Deborah's Palm Tree]] and [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Joshua 18:25.

**Other Ramahs.** Joshua 19 names a Ramah on Asher’s border (29) and one among Naphtali’s cities (36); they have their own pages, [[Ramah (Asher)]] and [[Ramah (Naphtali)]], and are not Benjamin’s Ramah.

Named among Benjamin’s second group of fourteen cities with their villages (25). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.

### Samuel's house and altar — 1 Samuel 7:17

*His return was to Ramah, for his house was there; and he judged Israel there; and he built an altar to Yahweh
there* (7:17). *Ûṯəšuḇāṯô hārāmāṯâ* (WLC): Ramah is the fixed point of the circuit of Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah
(7:16), and *there* is said three times. The ark is at Kiriath Jearim and Shiloh is not mentioned; the altar is
Samuel's own, and the narrator neither commends nor questions it. Deborah judged *between Ramah and Bethel*
(Judges 4:5), which strengthens, without settling, the filing of Samuel's Ramah with Benjamin's. The elders will
come *to Samuel to Ramah* to ask for a king (8:4, stored WEB; not yet ingested). See
[[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]] and [[Between Ramah and Bethel - Deborah and Samuel as Judges]].
Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 7:16–17; 8:4; Judges 4:5. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
