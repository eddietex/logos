# Ramah

## Overview

### Deborah's palm — Judges 4:5

Deborah sits *under Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim*
(4:5). Paired with Bethel, it is most naturally Benjamin's Ramah (Joshua 18:25), and the wiki files
it here; the verse itself places the palm *in the hill country of Ephraim* and does not name a tribe. See [[Deborah's Palm Tree]] and [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Joshua 18:25.

**Other Ramahs.** Joshua 19 names a Ramah on Asher’s border (29) and one among Naphtali’s cities (36); they have their own pages, [[Ramah (Asher)]] and [[Ramah (Naphtali)]], and are not Benjamin’s Ramah.

Named among Benjamin’s second group of fourteen cities with their villages (25). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.
