# Bezer

## Overview

Bezer is the city Moses sets apart for the Reubenites, in the wilderness and plain
country east of the Jordan (Deuteronomy 4:43). It offers refuge to the unwitting killer
without prior hatred (4:42). The verse gives no precise modern identification.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:41–43.
