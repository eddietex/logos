# Bezer

## Overview

### Refuge cities appointed — Joshua 20:1–9

Bezer in the wilderness plain is assigned from Reuben as one of the three eastern refuges (8). This repeats Moses’ designation in Deuteronomy 4:43 within the completed six-city list.

See [[The Cities of Refuge Appointed]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 20:1–9.

Bezer is the city Moses sets apart for the Reubenites, in the wilderness and plain
country east of the Jordan (Deuteronomy 4:43). It offers refuge to the unwitting killer
without prior hatred (4:42). The verse gives no precise modern identification.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:41–43.
