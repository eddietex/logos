# Bashan

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

The half-tribe of Manasseh supplies Gershonite cities here, including Golan (6, 27).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Refuge cities appointed — Joshua 20:1–9

Golan is the refuge named in Bashan, assigned from Manasseh (8). The appointment adds judicial protection to the account of eastern settlement.

See [[The Cities of Refuge Appointed]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 20:1–9.

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

Named with Gilead as Machir’s eastern holding, outside the ten parts described for western Manasseh
(1, 5).

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Og’s former kingdom extends to Salecah (11–12); the Manassite allocation includes all Bashan and sixty villages of Jair (30).

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### The Gibeonite deception — Joshua 9:1–27

Og is recalled as its king (10). The region belongs to the envoys’ remembered evidence of Yahweh’s
power, not the route of a verified journey.

See [[The Gibeonite Deception]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27; comparisons as cited.


### Moses blesses the tribes — Deuteronomy 33:1–29

Dan’s lion cub leaps from Bashan (22). The image does not by itself narrate a conquest or identify a
later city.

See [[Moses Blesses the Tribes]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 33:1–29; comparisons as cited.


### The Song of Moses — Deuteronomy 31:30–32:47

Rams of Bashan appear in the catalogue of rich provision (14). The region supplies an image of abundance, not the location of the song’s delivery.

See [[The Song of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 31:30–32:47.


### The covenant in Moab — Deuteronomy 29:1–29

Og is named as its king when the two defeated rulers and the eastern inheritance are recalled (7–8).

See [[The Covenant in Moab and the Hidden Things]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 29:1–29.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

Og’s land is included in the conquered territory where the law is framed (47). This is a territorial summary, not another campaign or a fresh tribal allocation.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

### Eastern refuge cities — Deuteronomy 4:41–43

Golan in Bashan is set apart for the Manassites as a refuge for unwitting killing
without previous hatred (42–43). The inherited territory receives a protective city.

See [[Three Cities Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:41–43.

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

All Bashan, Og’s former kingdom and a land of Rephaim, goes to half of Manasseh (13). Jair’s taking
and naming of Argob is recalled within this allocation (14).

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

### Og’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

Og’s kingdom includes sixty fortified cities in Argob and many unwalled villages (4–5). Israel takes
the kingdom; the territorial summary reaches Salecah and Edrei (10).

See [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

Og is identified as king of Bashan in the retrospective notice (4). The introduction assumes the
victory already accomplished.

See [[The Words of Moses Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

### Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan — Numbers 32:1–42

*The kingdom of Og king of Bashan* is given to Gad, Reuben, and half of Manasseh (33). Deuteronomy 3:13
and Joshua 13:30 assign Bashan to the half-tribe of Manasseh and place Havvoth Jair there (WEB via
bible-api.com, not yet ingested).

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.

Og’s kingdom, approached after the Jazer campaign (33). Following battle at Edrei, Israel kills Og,
his sons, and his people and possesses his land (35).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Og's kingdom is recalled *to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites* (12:5); see [[Geshur]] and [[Maacath]] and [[The Kings Struck Down]].
