# Adamah

## Overview

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (36), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). Not equated with [[Adami-nekeb]] on the border (33).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
