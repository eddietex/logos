# Argob

## Overview

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

Jair’s acquisition extends to the border of the Geshurites and Maacathites (14), and belongs within
Manasseh’s Bashan allotment (13). The verse does not claim conquest of those neighboring peoples.

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

Region containing the sixty cities of Og’s kingdom in Bashan (4). Verse 5 describes fortified cities
with high walls, gates, and bars, alongside many unwalled villages. No precise modern boundary is
assigned.
