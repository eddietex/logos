# Argob

## Overview

Region containing the sixty cities of Og’s kingdom in Bashan (4). Verse 5 describes fortified cities
with high walls, gates, and bars, alongside many unwalled villages. No precise modern boundary is
assigned.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.
