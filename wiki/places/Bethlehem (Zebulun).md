# Bethlehem (Zebulun)

## Overview

Named in Zebulun’s city register, whose stated total is twelve cities with villages (15–16). No individual capture or modern identification is supplied. Kept distinct from Bethlehem, also called Ephrath in Genesis 35:19.

**Ibzan's town?** Judges 12:8–10 names *Ibzan of Bethlehem* without a tribe, while every other
Bethlehem in Judges is *Bethlehem Judah* (17:7–9; 19:1–2, 18). The next judge, Elon, is a Zebulunite
buried *in the land of Zebulun* (12:11–12). This town is therefore one candidate for Ibzan's home and
grave; the text does not decide. See [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]] and [[Bethlehem]]. Source: stored WEB
Judges 12:8–12; 17:7–9; 19:1–2, 18; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23. No precise modern site is assigned.
