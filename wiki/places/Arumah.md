# Arumah

## Overview

*Abimelech lived at Arumah; and Zebul drove out Gaal and his brothers, that they should not dwell in
Shechem* (Judges 9:41). It is where Abimelech lives during the revolt, while his officer [[Zebul]] holds
[[Shechem (city)]] for him. *ʾĂrûmâ* (WLC) is named only here in Genesis–Judges. Its site is not given.
Some read *bətormâ*, *craftily*, in 9:31 as a place name, *in Tormah*, and identify it with Arumah
(reported, not checked). The wiki keeps WEB's reading.

That Abimelech does not live in the town that made him king says something about his rule. The text
does not say why.

See [[The Fall of Abimelech]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 9:31, 41; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-15.
