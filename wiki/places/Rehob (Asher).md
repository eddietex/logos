# Rehob (Asher)

## Overview

### Not driven out — Judges 1:31

Last of Asher's seven cities not driven out (31). Only one Rehob is named here, against two notices in Joshua 19:28, 30.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Asher to Gershon’s descendants (21:31).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

Asher’s register names Rehob twice, in verse 28 before Hammon and again in verse 30 with Ummah and Aphek. Whether these are one town or two is not stated; this page holds both notices without deciding. Joshua 21:31 later names a Levitical Rehob from Asher. Kept distinct from [[Rehob]], the northern limit of the spies’ search (Numbers 13:21).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
