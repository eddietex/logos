# Rehob (Asher)

## Overview

Asher’s register names Rehob twice, in verse 28 before Hammon and again in verse 30 with Ummah and Aphek. Whether these are one town or two is not stated; this page holds both notices without deciding. Joshua 21:31 later names a Levitical Rehob from Asher (not yet ingested). Kept distinct from [[Rehob]], the northern limit of the spies’ search (Numbers 13:21).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
