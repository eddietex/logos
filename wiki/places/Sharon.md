# Sharon

## Overview

A region of Israel, known in Scripture for pasture and for its growth. In
[[Delight and the Unawakened Love]] the woman says, *"I am a rose of Sharon, a lily of the
valleys"* (Song 2:1). The flower is not securely identified, and the WEB's *rose* is followed
without a botanical claim. Whether the phrase is modest (a common local flower) or
proud (the flower of a famous place) is left open on the passage page.

Elsewhere, checked in the WEB but not yet ingested: herds feed in Sharon under an appointed
overseer, *"Shitrai the Sharonite"* (1 Chronicles 27:29). Isaiah sets *"the excellence of Carmel and Sharon"* beside
Lebanon's glory (Isaiah 35:2). In judgment *"Sharon is like a desert"* (33:9), and in
restoration *"Sharon will be a fold of flocks"* (65:10). Pasture and fertility are the region's
standing associations, which suits the Song's image of a flower.

The region's location and extent are not set out here; no geographical claim is made beyond
the verses cited.
