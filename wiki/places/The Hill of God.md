# The Hill of God

## Overview

### The garrison and the prophets — 1 Samuel 10:5, 10

*After that you will come to the hill of God, where the garrison of the Philistines is* (10:5). There Saul is to meet
a band of prophets coming down from the high place, and *Yahweh's Spirit will come mightily on you* (10:5–6).
It happens as Samuel said: *When they came there to the hill, behold, a band of prophets met him* (10:10).

*Giḇʿaṯ hāʾĕlōhîm* (WLC) occurs only here in the WLC files searched. The noun is *giḇʿâ*, *hill* (lemma 1389). It is
not the place-name lemma used for [[Gibeah (Benjamin)]] at 10:26, where Saul goes home (lemma 1390). The place
has a city (*when you have come there to the city*, 10:5) and a high place (10:5, 13), and a Philistine *nəṣîḇ*,
a garrison or post, or possibly an officer. A Philistine *nəṣîḇ* is struck *in Geba* at 13:3 (stored WEB; not yet
ingested). Identifications of the hill of God with Gibeah, with [[Geba]], or with another height are commonly
proposed. They are recalled, not checked, and the text makes none of them. No modern site is assigned.
See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].
