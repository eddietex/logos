# Iron (city)

## Overview

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (38), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). The qualifier keeps the town distinct from the metal named elsewhere, as in Deuteronomy 33:25.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
