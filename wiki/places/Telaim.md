# Telaim

## Overview

The place where [[Saul]] musters the army for the war against Amalek: *Saul summoned the people, and counted them
in Telaim, two hundred thousand footmen, and ten thousand men of Judah* (1 Samuel 15:4). The Hebrew is
*baṭṭəlāʾîm* (WLC), with the article. Telaim is named nowhere else in the stored books, and the verse gives no
region.

The muster is the first step of a march south: Saul goes on to *the city of Amalek* (15:5) and fights *from
Havilah as you go to Shur* (15:7). An identification with [[Telem]], a town in Judah's southern group *toward the
border of Edom in the South* (Joshua 15:21, 24), is commonly proposed (recalled, not checked); it fits the
direction of the campaign, and the text neither makes nor excludes it. This page keeps the two apart.

The verb of the muster, *wayyip̄qəḏēm*, *counted them* (15:4, WLC), is the verb Yahweh uses two verses earlier,
*pāqaḏtî*, *I remember* (15:2, WLC). See [[Saul Rejected over Amalek]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 15:2–7; Joshua 15:21–24. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-20. No modern
site is assigned.
