# Ain (Negev)

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the farthest cities of Judah toward the border of Edom in the South (Joshua 15:32), one of the names in the southern group that verse 32 totals as *twenty-nine* cities with their villages, though thirty-six names are listed (21–32). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Qualified to keep it apart from [[Ain]], the landmark beside Riblah in Numbers 34:11. Simeon's list names *Ain, Rimmon* (19:7) and the priests' cities include an Ain (21:16). Whether *Ain, and Rimmon* here are two towns or one is not settled by the WEB; the count discrepancy of verse 32 is noted on the passage page.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
