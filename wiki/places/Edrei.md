# Edrei

## Overview

**Naphtali’s Edrei** (19:37) now has its own page, [[Edrei (Naphtali)]].

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Og’s royal city (12), assigned with Ashtaroth to Machir’s descendants (31); no fresh battle is narrated.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### Og’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

Og brings his people to battle here (1). It is also named with Salecah among the cities marking the
extent of his kingdom (10).

See [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

Named in the compressed notice of Og’s defeat (4). Numbers 21:33 explicitly makes it the battle
site; this introduction does not narrate another engagement.

See [[The Words of Moses Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

Site of the battle to which Og king of Bashan comes with his people (33). Yahweh’s assurance to
Moses precedes Israel’s victory (34–35).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** One of Og's two residences, with Ashtaroth (12:4). Joshua 19:37 also names an Edrei among Naphtali's cities; it is not identified with this one here. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
