# Shiloh

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

The Levite heads petition Eleazar, Joshua, and the tribal heads here in Canaan (1–2).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Joshua’s own inheritance — Joshua 19:49–51

The closing allotment summary locates the lot before Yahweh at the door of the Tent of Meeting here (51), confirming the sanctuary setting introduced in Joshua 18:1–10.

See [[Joshua's Own Inheritance]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:49–51.

The place where the whole congregation assembles and sets up the Tent of Meeting in Joshua 18:1. The
land surveyors return to the camp here, and Joshua casts the lots before Yahweh (8–10). The passage
establishes Shiloh as a sanctuary and administrative center without giving a modern identification
or describing a stone temple.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:1–10.
