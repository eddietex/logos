# Shiloh

## Overview

The place where the whole congregation assembles and sets up the Tent of Meeting in Joshua 18:1. The
land surveyors return to the camp here, and Joshua casts the lots before Yahweh (8–10). The passage
establishes Shiloh as a sanctuary and administrative center without giving a modern identification
or describing a stone temple.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:1–10.
