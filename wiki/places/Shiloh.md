# Shiloh

## Overview

### The ark sent for, and the news brought back — 1 Samuel 4:3–22

After the first defeat at [[Ebenezer]], the elders say *Let us get the ark of Yahweh's covenant out of Shiloh*,
and *the people sent to Shiloh*; Hophni and Phinehas go with it (4:3–4). The same day *a man of Benjamin* runs
back *and came to Shiloh*, and *all the city cried out* (12–13). Eli dies at *the side of the gate* (18), and
Phinehas' wife dies giving birth to Ichabod (19–22). The narrative never returns the ark to Shiloh: it comes
back to Beth Shemesh and Kiriath Jearim (6:12–7:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested). Psalm 78:60 and Jeremiah
7:12–14 read this as the day Yahweh *abandoned the tent of Shiloh*; see
[[Shiloh Forsaken - 1 Samuel 4, Psalm 78.60-64, and Jeremiah 7.12-14]] and [[The Ark Is Captured]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 4:3–22; 6:12–7:2. Psalm 78:60 and Jeremiah 7:12–14 WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17.

### The lamp, the ark, and the doors — 1 Samuel 3:1–21

Samuel lies *in Yahweh's temple, where God's ark was*, before *God's lamp* has gone out (3:3), and Yahweh
calls him there. In the morning he *opened the doors of Yahweh's house* (3:15). *Hêḵal YHWH* (3:3, WLC) is
the word of 1:9; a sanctuary with doors is a building, while 2:22 still names *the Tent of Meeting*, and
the text does not reconcile the two. The chapter ends with the place renewed as a seat of revelation:
*Yahweh appeared again in Shiloh; for Yahweh revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh by Yahweh's word* (3:21).
The next chapter takes the ark out of it (4:3–4, stored WEB; not yet ingested), and Jeremiah later points
to Shiloh as a sanctuary Yahweh judged (Jeremiah 7:12, cited by reference only). See
[[Yahweh Calls Samuel]] and
[[Both His Ears Will Tingle - 1 Samuel 3.11, 2 Kings 21.12, and Jeremiah 19.3]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:9; 2:22; 3:3, 15, 21; 4:3–4. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### *My habitation* — 1 Samuel 2:27–36

The man of God's oracle against Eli speaks of *my sacrifice and … my offering, which I have commanded in my
habitation* and forecasts *the affliction of my habitation* (2:29, 32). *Māʿôn*, *habitation*, occurs in
the WLC files of Genesis through 1 Kings elsewhere only of Yahweh's *holy habitation, from heaven*
(Deuteronomy 26:15); here it most likely means the sanctuary at Shiloh. 2:32 is textually difficult, and
many read it as a forecast of the ark's capture and Shiloh's eclipse (chapter 4). 1 Kings 2:27 later
recalls *Yahweh's word, which he spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh* (WEB checked at
bible-api.com). See [[A Man of God Against the House of Eli]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:29, 32; Deuteronomy 26:15. 1 Kings 2:27 WEB checked at bible-api.com,
2026-11-17. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### What they did in Shiloh — 1 Samuel 2:12–26

*So they did in Shiloh to all the Israelites who came there* (2:14): the priest's servant forks meat out
of the worshippers' pots, and demands raw meat before the fat is burned (2:13–16). The pericope names the
sanctuary *the Tent of Meeting* (2:22), where Eli's sons lie with *the women who served at the door*, the
phrase of Exodus 38:8. In chapter 1 the same sanctuary was *Yahweh's temple* (1:9); the text does not say
how the two names relate. Here too the boy Samuel ministers *before Yahweh* in a linen ephod, and Hannah
comes up yearly with his robe (2:18–19). See [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]] and
[[The Women Who Served at the Door - Exodus 38.8 and 1 Samuel 2.22]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:9;
2:12–26; Exodus 38:8.

### Yahweh's temple, and Eli's seat — 1 Samuel 1:3–28

Elkanah *went up out of his city from year to year to worship and to sacrifice to Yahweh of Armies in
Shiloh*, where *the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas* were priests (1:3). The sanctuary here is called
*Yahweh's house* (1:7, 24) and *Yahweh's temple*, *hêḵal YHWH*, with a *doorpost* beside which Eli sits on
his *seat* (1:9, WLC). *Hêḵal* occurs in the WLC files of Genesis through 1 Samuel only at 1:9 and 3:3.
Joshua set up *the Tent of Meeting* at Shiloh (Joshua 18:1); 1 Samuel 2:22 still names *the Tent of
Meeting* there. The text does not explain how tent and *temple* relate. Here Hannah
prays and vows, and here she brings the weaned Samuel to *appear before Yahweh, and stay there forever*
(1:9–28). The yearly visit continues the *feast of Yahweh from year to year in Shiloh* of Judges 21:19;
see [[From Year to Year - Jephthah's Daughter, the Feast at Shiloh, and Elkanah]] and [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]]. Source: stored
WEB 1 Samuel 1:3–28; 2:22; Joshua 18:1; Judges 21:19; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### The camp, the feast, and the dances — Judges 21:12, 19–23

The four hundred virgins of Jabesh Gilead are brought *to the camp to Shiloh, which is in the land of
Canaan* (21:12), the wording of Joshua 22:9 (WLC). Then the elders point to *a feast of Yahweh from year to
year in Shiloh*, and give directions to it — *on the north of Bethel, on the east side of the highway that
goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah* (21:19) — as if the place of *God's house*
(18:31) needed finding. Benjamin lies in wait in the vineyards and seizes *the daughters of Shiloh* as they
dance (21:20–23). The next Shiloh scene in reading order is Elkanah going up *from year to year* to
sacrifice there (1 Samuel 1:3, WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). See [[Wives for Benjamin]],
[[From Year to Year - Jephthah's Daughter, the Feast at Shiloh, and Elkanah]], and [[Lebonah]]. Source:
stored WEB Joshua 22:9; Judges 18:31; 21:12, 19–23.

### *Yahweh's house*? — Judges 19:18

The Levite says *I am going to Yahweh's house* (19:18; the Greek is reported to read *my house* — not
checked). Shiloh, *God's house* in 18:31, is in the hill country of Ephraim where he lives, but the text
does not name it here. See [[The Levite's Concubine at Gibeah]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 18:31; 19:18.

### God's house in Shiloh — Judges 18:31

The Danites keep Micah's image at Dan *all the time that God's house was in Shiloh* (18:31). The
chapter's last words set the house where the tent was set up (Joshua 18:1) beside the shrine a tribe made
for itself. *Bêt-hāʾĕlōhîm*, with the article (WLC). See [[The Danite Migration]] and
[[The Tent Established at Shiloh - Exodus 40 and Joshua 18]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:1; Judges
18:31.

### The altar of witness — Joshua 22:10–34

The whole congregation gathers here to go to war over the eastern altar (12). Shiloh's tabernacle
is the reference point of the dispute: the altar *before his tabernacle* (29) is the only place
of sacrifice, and the eastern altar claims only to copy it.

See [[The Altar of Witness by the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 22:10–34; comparisons as cited.

### The eastern tribes sent home — Joshua 22:1–9

The eastern tribes depart from the children of Israel here in Canaan toward Gilead (9). The place of
the allotment becomes the departure point for the authorized return.

See [[The Eastern Tribes Sent Home]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 22:1–9; comparisons as cited.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

The Levite heads petition Eleazar, Joshua, and the tribal heads here in Canaan (1–2).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Joshua’s own inheritance — Joshua 19:49–51

The closing allotment summary locates the lot before Yahweh at the door of the Tent of Meeting here (51), confirming the sanctuary setting introduced in Joshua 18:1–10.

See [[Joshua's Own Inheritance]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:49–51.

The place where the whole congregation assembles and sets up the Tent of Meeting in Joshua 18:1. The
land surveyors return to the camp here, and Joshua casts the lots before Yahweh (8–10). The passage
establishes Shiloh as a sanctuary and administrative center without giving a modern identification
or describing a stone temple.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:1–10.
