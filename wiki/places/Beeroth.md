# Beeroth

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

Included with Gibeon and Ramah in Benjamin’s second city group (25). The allotment does not narrate removal of the inhabitants protected in Joshua 9.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

One of the four cities belonging to the people protected by Israel’s oath (17–20). It is not
identified here with Beeroth Bene Jaakan in the wilderness itinerary.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27.
