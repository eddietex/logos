# Timnathserah

## Overview

The city Joshua asks for and receives from Israel according to Yahweh’s command, in the
hill country of Ephraim (Joshua 19:49–50). He builds the city and lives there. The notice
does not identify a modern site, report a capture, or explain the nature of the building work.

Joshua is buried here, *in the border of his inheritance*, *on the north of the mountain of Gaash*
(24:30). Judges 2:9 gives the name as *Timnath Heres* in the same location (stored WEB). The
Hebrew has *timnat-seraḥ* in Joshua 24:30 and *timnat-ḥeres* in Judges 2:9: the same three consonants
in reverse order (WLC). Whether that is a scribal transposition is not settled here. See [[Another Generation Arose]] and
[[Joshua's Death Told Twice - Joshua 24.28-31 and Judges 2.6-9]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:49–51; 24:30; Judges 2:9. Hebrew of Joshua 24:30 and Judges 2:9 from the
Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13. The spelling follows this stored WEB text.
