# Massah and Meribah

## Overview

**The name given to the place at Rephidim where water came out of the rock — two names, both for what the
people did**: *"He called the name of the place Massah, and Meribah, because the children of Israel quarreled,
and because they tested Yahweh, saying, 'Is Yahweh among us, or not?'"* (Exodus 17:7). ***Massâ*** is from
***nissâ***, *to test*; ***mərîbâ*** from ***rîb***, *to quarrel* or *contend* — the two verbs of Moses'
question in 17:2, *"Why do you quarrel with me? Why do you test Yahweh?"* **Marah was named for its water;
this place is named for the people's sin.** See [[Water from the Rock]].

**The two names travel separately.** *Massah* alone is the place of the commandment *"You shall not tempt
Yahweh your God, as you tempted him in Massah"* (Deuteronomy 6:16; WEB), and of the list of provocations in
Deuteronomy 9:22 — *"At Taberah, and at Massah, and at Kibroth Hattaavah, you provoked Yahweh to wrath"*
(WEB). *Meribah* alone is also the name of a **second place**: the water at Kadesh, late in the wilderness years,
where Moses struck the rock twice — *"These are the waters of Meribah; because the children of Israel strove
with Yahweh"* (Numbers 20:13; WEB) — and which Numbers 27:14 calls *Meribah of Kadesh* (*mərîbat qādēš*; WLC).
**The wiki files that second Meribah under [[Kadesh]]** and keeps this page for the place of Exodus 17;
where a later text could mean either, it says so. **Psalm 81:7's *"waters of Meribah"*** and **Psalm 106:32's**
are the clearest such cases; Psalm 106:32–33, with *"Moses was troubled for their sakes … he spoke rashly with
his lips"*, points to Numbers 20.

**Deuteronomy 33:8 and Psalm 95:8 put the names together**, as Exodus does. Psalm 95:8 — *"Don't harden your
heart, as at Meribah, as in the day of Massah in the wilderness"* (WEB) — writes them in a form the WLC tags as
the common nouns *quarrel* and *testing* (*kimrîbâ kəyôm massâ*); Hebrews 3:8 quotes the psalm in Greek as
*parapikrasmos*, *rebellion* or *provocation*, and *peirasmos*, *trial* (SBLGNT). **The Greek Psalm Hebrews
quotes was not checked.** See [[Do Not Test Yahweh - Massah in Deuteronomy, the Psalms, and Hebrews]].

**Its location is Rephidim's**, and not known. See [[Rephidim]].
