# Elim

## Overview

**The oasis after Marah: twelve springs and seventy palm trees.**

> **27** They came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water, and seventy palm trees: and they
> encamped there by the waters. (Exodus 15:27)

**It is the first place in the wilderness that is described rather than named for an event.** Marah is
named for its bitter water (15:23); Massah and Meribah will be named for a quarrel (17:7). Elim gets a
count — ***šətêm ʿeśrēh ʿênōt mayim wəšibʿîm təmārîm*** (WLC) — and a camp *"by the waters"*. **Nothing
happens there.** No complaint, no miracle, and no word from God are recorded; the next verse leaves it
(16:1).

***ʾÊlim*** **is usually taken to mean *trees* — terebinths or large trees** (reported, not checked in this
pass); the WLC files it as a proper name. **Its consonants nearly match the *ʾēlim*, *gods*, of the Song**
— *"Who is like you, Yahweh, among the gods?"* (15:11) — **which the WLC spells without the place-name's
*yod***. The wiki records the resemblance and draws nothing from it.

**Twelve and seventy are the book's numbers for Israel** — *"the twelve tribes of Israel"* (Genesis 49:28),
the *"seventy souls"* who went down to Egypt (Exodus 1:5), and ahead the *"seventy of the elders of Israel"*
(24:1, 9; *not yet ingested*). **Many readers take Elim's springs and palms as a picture of the tribes and
the elders** (reported, not decided); the text gives only the numbers. Numbers 33:9 repeats them exactly:
*"In Elim, there were twelve springs of water, and seventy palm trees; and they encamped there"* (WEB;
*not yet ingested*).

***Tāmār*, *palm*, occurs here for the first time in Scripture** (WLC lemma search, Genesis–Deuteronomy).
The same letters are the name of [[Tamar]] (Genesis 38), which the WLC files under a separate number.

**Its date is bounded from the other side.** Israel leaves Elim for the wilderness of Sin *"on the
fifteenth day of the second month after their departing out of the land of Egypt"* (16:1) — a month after
the Passover of the fourteenth day of the first month (12:6). How long they camped there is not said.

**Where it is is not known.** Identifications with oases on the western side of the Sinai peninsula have
been proposed; they are reported, not checked in this pass, and none is adopted.
