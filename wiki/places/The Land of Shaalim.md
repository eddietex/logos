# The Land of Shaalim

## Overview

### The third district of the search — 1 Samuel 9:4

*Then they passed through the land of Shaalim, and there they weren't there* (9:4). *Šaʿălîm* (lemma 8171) occurs
only here in the WLC files searched (Genesis through 2 Samuel, Ruth). Its location is unknown; the route places it
between Shalishah and the land of Benjamin. Any link with [[Shaalabbin]] of Dan's lot (Joshua 19:42) is a resemblance
of spelling only, not an identification the text makes. See
[[Saul Comes to the Seer]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 9:4; Joshua 19:42. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
