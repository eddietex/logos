# En Tappuah

## Overview

Boundary locality whose inhabitants mark the turn from Michmethath before Shechem (7). The adjacent
verse distinguishes Tappuah’s land from its town; no precise identification or equation of En
Tappuah with that town is asserted.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.
