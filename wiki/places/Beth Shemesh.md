# Beth Shemesh

## Overview

### The ark comes home, and the town is struck — 1 Samuel 6:9–21

The Philistine priests make Beth Shemesh the test: if the unyoked cows go *up by the way of its own border to
Beth Shemesh*, the plague was Yahweh's (6:9). They go straight there, and *the people of Beth Shemesh were
reaping their wheat harvest in the valley … and saw the ark, and rejoiced to see it* (13). The cart stops in
*the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh* by a great stone; the cart becomes firewood and the cows a burnt
offering; *the Levites* set the ark on the stone, and the men of the town sacrifice (14–15). That the
Levites are at hand fits Joshua 21:16, which gives the town to Aaron's descendants. The stone *remains to
this day in the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh* (18). Then Yahweh *struck of the men of Beth Shemesh,
because they had looked into Yahweh's ark* (19); the number is disputed (see the passage page). The town
asks *Who is able to stand before Yahweh, this holy God?* and sends to Kiriath Jearim (20–21).

**Joshua of Beth Shemesh**, *yəhôšuaʿ bêṯ-haššimšî* (14, 18, WLC), is named only as the owner of the field;
the wiki gives him no page of his own. The town lies on the way *up* from Philistine territory to Israel's
border (9, 12), in *the valley* (13).

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Judah and Simeon to Aaron’s descendants (21:16).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

**Other Beth Shemeshes.** Issachar’s and Naphtali’s registers each name one (19:22, 38): [[Beth Shemesh (Issachar)]] and [[Beth Shemesh (Naphtali)]]. Dan’s [[Irshemesh]] (19:41) shares the second element but is not equated with this town.

The boundary descends here after Mount Jearim and passes by Timnah (10). No identification with another similarly named town is imposed.
