# Deborah's Palm Tree

## Overview

*She lived under Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim; and the
children of Israel came up to her for judgment* (Judges 4:5). A tree named after the person who sat
under it, used as a court. The Hebrew is *tōmer dəbôrâ* (WLC); *tōmer* is an unusual form, occurring
only here in the WLC files read, where the usual word for palm is *tāmār*.

It is the second tree near Bethel to carry the name Deborah. Genesis 35:8 buries *Deborah, Rebekah's
nurse* *below Bethel under the oak*, which was named *Allon Bacuth*. The two women are different and
the text does not connect them or their trees. See [[The Trees of the Patriarchs]], where Deborah's
palm is listed among the trees under which Israel's leaders ruled.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:5; Genesis 35:8; Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-13. No modern site is assigned.
