# The Hill Country of Ephraim

## Overview

### Two graves — Joshua 24:29–33

Joshua is buried at Timnathserah *in the hill country of Ephraim, on the north of the mountain of
Gaash* (30), and Eleazar in *the hill of Phinehas*, also *in the hill country of Ephraim* (33).
Shechem, where Joseph's bones are buried (32), is also in this hill country (20:7).

See [[The Burials of Joshua, Joseph, and Eleazar]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 24:29–33; comparisons as cited.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Shechem, the refuge supplied by Ephraim, is located here (21).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Joshua’s own inheritance — Joshua 19:49–51

Timnathserah, Joshua’s requested and granted city, is located here (50). He builds and inhabits it; the verse supplies neither a modern site nor an expanded boundary for the region.

See [[Joshua's Own Inheritance]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:49–51.

The hill country described as too narrow for Joseph’s numerous descendants (Joshua 17:15).
Joshua directs them to clear the forest and promises its farthest extent (18). The passage
does not draw a precise boundary or equate every surrounding forest with the whole region.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:14–18.
