# The Hill Country of Ephraim

## Overview

### The hidden men come out — 1 Samuel 14:22

*All the men of Israel who had hidden themselves in the hill country of Ephraim, when they heard that the Philistines
fled, even they also followed hard after them in the battle* (14:22). The verb is *hammiṯḥabbəʾîm*, the form used when
the people *hid themselves in caves, in thickets, in rocks, in tombs, and in pits* (13:6, WLC), and the Philistines'
taunt was *the Hebrews are coming out of the holes* (14:11). As in Gideon's day, men of the hill country of Ephraim
join the pursuit of a rout they did not start (Judges 7:24). See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:6; 14:11, 22; Judges 7:24. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-19.

### The first district of Saul's search — 1 Samuel 9:4

Saul and his servant begin the search for Kish's donkeys here: *He passed through the hill country of Ephraim, and
passed through the land of Shalishah, but they didn't find them* (9:4). It is the district of Elkanah's
*Ramathaim Zophim* (1:1), and the search ends in *the land of Zuph* (9:5), the name of Elkanah's ancestor. See
[[Saul Comes to the Seer]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:1; 9:4–5.

### Ramathaim Zophim — 1 Samuel 1:1

1 Samuel opens as Judges 17 and 19 did, with a man of this hill country: *a certain man of Ramathaim
Zophim, of the hill country of Ephraim, and his name was Elkanah* (1:1). Micah was *a man of the hill
country of Ephraim* (Judges 17:1) and the Levite lived *on the farther side of the hill country of
Ephraim* (19:1). The three stories share the region and the opening. See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]] and [[Ramah]]. Source:
stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:1; Judges 17:1; 19:1.

### The far side, and the old man — Judges 19:1, 16–18

The Levite of Judges 19 lives *on the farther side of the hill country of Ephraim*, *bəyarkəṯê har-ʾep̄rāyim*
(19:1, WLC), and is heading home there when he stops at Gibeah (19:18). His host is *from the hill country of
Ephraim* too, sojourning in Gibeah (19:16). See [[The Levite's Concubine at Gibeah]] and [[The Old Man of Gibeah]]. Source: stored
WEB Judges 19:1, 16–18; Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-17.

### *The hill country of the Amalekites* — Judges 12:15

Abdon the son of Hillel is buried *in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of the
Amalekites* (12:15). Within Ephraim's hills the text names a district by an Amalekite name; what that
name preserves, it does not say. See [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]], [[Pirathon]], and [[Amalek]].
Source: stored WEB Judges 12:15.

### Tola at Shamir — Judges 10:1–2

Tola, *a man of Issachar*, *lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim* and was buried there
(10:1–2). A judge from a northern tribe lives in the central hills, where Deborah had judged (4:5).
The text does not say why. See [[Shamir (Ephraim)]].

See [[Tola and Jair]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 10:1–2.

### Gideon's messengers — Judges 7:24

*Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill country of Ephraim* (7:24) to cut off Midian at the
Jordan. Ehud had raised Israel from the same hills with a trumpet before taking the fords (3:27–28).
See [[The Sword of Yahweh and of Gideon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:27–28; 7:24.

### Deborah's court — Judges 4:5

Deborah's palm is *between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim*, and Israel *came up to
her for judgment* (4:5). After Joshua's grave (2:9) and Ehud's trumpet (3:27), the region now holds
the book's one court. See [[Deborah's Palm Tree]] and [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16.

### Ehud's trumpet — Judges 3:27

*When he had come, he blew a trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim; and the children of Israel went
down with him from the hill country, and he led them* (27). A Benjamite judge musters Israel in
Ephraim's hills, and leads them *down* to the Jordan fords.

See [[Ehud and Eglon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:12–30.

### Joshua's grave retold — Judges 2:6–23

Judges 2:9 places Joshua's grave at *Timnath Heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, on the north of
the mountain of Gaash*, repeating Joshua 24:30 with the city's name spelled differently.

See [[Another Generation Arose]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 2:6–23.

### Two graves — Joshua 24:29–33

Joshua is buried at Timnathserah *in the hill country of Ephraim, on the north of the mountain of
Gaash* (30), and Eleazar in *the hill of Phinehas*, also *in the hill country of Ephraim* (33).
Shechem, where Joseph's bones are buried (32), is also in this hill country (20:7).

See [[The Burials of Joshua, Joseph, and Eleazar]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 24:29–33; comparisons as cited.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Shechem, the refuge supplied by Ephraim, is located here (21).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Joshua’s own inheritance — Joshua 19:49–51

Timnathserah, Joshua’s requested and granted city, is located here (50). He builds and inhabits it; the verse supplies neither a modern site nor an expanded boundary for the region.

See [[Joshua's Own Inheritance]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:49–51.

The hill country described as too narrow for Joseph’s numerous descendants (Joshua 17:15).
Joshua directs them to clear the forest and promises its farthest extent (18). The passage
does not draw a precise boundary or equate every surrounding forest with the whole region.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:14–18.
