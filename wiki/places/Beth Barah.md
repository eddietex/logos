# Beth Barah

## Overview

*Take before them the waters, as far as Beth Barah, even the Jordan!* (Judges 7:24). The limit of the
waters Gideon tells Ephraim to seize ahead of the fleeing army; the order is given and carried out in
the same words. It occurs in Genesis–Judges only here (WLC). Whether *the waters* are streams running
down to the Jordan or the Jordan's own crossings is not said, and the site is not identified here.

The tactic repeats Ehud's, who *took the fords of the Jordan against the Moabites* (3:28), and Jephthah's
Gileadites will do it against Ephraim (12:5). See
[[The Fords of the Jordan - Joshua 2, Judges 3, 7, and 12]].
