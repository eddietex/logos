# Mount Zalmon

## Overview

When the men of the tower of Shechem shelter in the stronghold of El Berith, *Abimelech went up to Mount
Zalmon, he and all the people who were with him; and Abimelech took an ax in his hand, and cut down a
bough from the trees, and took it up, and laid it on his shoulder* (Judges 9:48). His men do the same,
and the boughs are piled against the stronghold and set on fire (9:49). *Har ṣalmôn* (WLC) is named only
here in Genesis–Judges. It is near enough to Shechem for wood to be carried from it to the tower, and
the text locates it no further. It is not [[Zalmonah]], the wilderness stage of Numbers 33.

The mountain's trees supply the fire Jotham's fable had threatened: *let fire come out of the bramble,
and devour the cedars of Lebanon* (9:15).
