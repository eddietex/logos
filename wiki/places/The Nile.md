# The Nile

## Overview

**The river both of Pharaoh's dreams come up out of, and the reason Egypt has bread when nobody else
does.**

> **1** At the end of two full years, Pharaoh dreamed: and behold, he stood by the river.
> **2** Behold, there came up out of the river seven cattle, sleek and fat, and they fed in the marsh
> grass.

**The WEB's *"the river"* is ***hayʾōr***, and the word is not Hebrew.** It is **Egyptian *itrw*,
*river*, borrowed into Hebrew as a near-proper noun for this one river** — *the* river, with the article,
meaning the Nile and nothing else. **It enters Scripture at Genesis 41:1 and occurs five times in this
chapter** (41:1, 2, 3, 17, 18), **and it is the standard biblical word for the Nile from here to the
prophets.**

**Genesis has referred to Egypt a great deal before this without ever naming its river.** [[Abraham]]
went down in a famine (12:10) and came back up; [[Isaac]] was forbidden to go (26:2); Joseph was sold
there (37:36). **The river appears the moment the text is inside the country rather than looking at it
from Canaan.**

### Why a dream about cows in a river is a dream about famine

**Egypt is a rainless country fed by one flood.** The Nile's annual inundation, arriving in late summer,
**deposits the silt that is the whole of Egyptian agriculture** — the country's own name for itself,
*kmt*, *the black land*, is a description of that silt. **A high flood is a harvest; a low flood is a
famine; a run of low floods is a catastrophe**, and the Egyptian state measured the river with nilometers
because the tax assessment depended on the number.

**So a dream in which fat cattle and lean cattle both climb out of the water is a dream about the flood
gauge**, and its subject is the one variable an Egyptian king could neither predict nor control. **The
cow was also the form of Hathor**, a standing Egyptian image of fertility and nourishment.

**The dream is composed entirely in the dreamer's own agricultural and religious vocabulary** — **and
[[Joseph]] credits it to a God the dreamer does not know** (41:16, 25, 28). See
[[Pharaoh's Dreams and Joseph's Rise]] and [[Prophecy and Vision]].

### The same river later

**Every major thing the Bible does with Egypt happens at this water.**

- **Exodus 1:22** — *"You shall cast every son who is born into the river"*
  ([[The Oppression in Egypt]]), and **Exodus 2:3–5**, where one is put into it in a basket and drawn out of it by a princess.
- **Exodus 7:17–21** — **the first plague turns it to blood** ([[Water Turned to Blood]]), which is a judgment aimed precisely at the
  thing Genesis 41 shows the country depends on.
- **Isaiah 19:5–10; Ezekiel 30:12** — the prophets' image of Egypt's end is **the river drying up.**
- **Ezekiel 29:3** — Pharaoh as *"the great monster that lies in the middle of his rivers, that has
  said, 'My river is my own, and I have made it for myself'"* — **the sentence Genesis 41 quietly denies
  by having the river's own message arrive from elsewhere.**

**Genesis 15:18's *"the river of Egypt"* is a different phrase** — *nəhar Miṣrayim*, and usually taken as
the Wadi el-Arish on the border rather than the Nile; see
[[From the River of Egypt to the Euphrates]].

## Appears in

- [[Pharaoh's Dreams and Joseph's Rise]] — Genesis 41:1–3, 17–18; **the setting of both halves of the
  dream and the chapter's first Egyptian word.** Pharaoh stands *by* it in the narrator's telling and
  *on the brink of* it in his own; **the fat cattle and the thin cattle both come up out of it**, which
  is not how cattle behave and is exactly how a Nile flood behaves. **The seven years of plenty and the
  seven of famine are, in the dream's own terms, seven good inundations and seven bad ones**
- [[The Oppression in Egypt]] — Exodus 1:22; ***hayəʾōrâ*, *"the river"*, made a grave**: *"You shall cast every son
  who is born into the river."* Egypt's source of life becomes the instrument of the decree — and
  the river will be where Moses is laid and drawn out (2:3–10), the first water turned to blood (7:20),
  and its counterpart the sea where *"Pharaoh's chariots and his army"* are cast (15:4)
- [[The Birth of Moses]] — Exodus 2:3–10; **the decree's grave becomes the child's ark.** His
  mother puts him into the river as ordered, in a sealed papyrus *tēbâ*, *"in the reeds by the
  river's bank"* — *sûp*, the reed of the *yam sûp* he will lead Israel through; Pharaoh's daughter
  comes down to bathe in it and draws him out. The papyrus (*gōmeʾ*) is the Nile marsh's own plant
- [[The Burning Bush]] — Exodus 4:9; **the first plague, in a cup.** *"The water which you take out of
  the river will become blood on the dry land"* — the third sign for the elders, and the preview of
  7:17–21
- [[Water Turned to Blood]] — Exodus 7:14–25; **struck.** Pharaoh *"goes out to the water"* in
  the morning, and Moses stands *"by the river's bank"* — *śəpat hayəʾōr*, the phrase of Pharaoh's dream
  (Genesis 41:3, 17) and of the basket (2:3). The river is struck in Pharaoh's sight; its fish die,
  it stinks, and the Egyptians dig around it for water. **The river Egypt lived by, and the river of
  1:22, is the first thing struck**

## Related places

- [[Egypt]] — the country the river is, in any practical sense
- [[On]] — Heliopolis, at the apex of the Delta where the river fans out
- [[The Euphrates]] — the other river Genesis names as a boundary, and the far edge of the promise at
  15:18
- [[Canaan]] — a rain-fed country, which is why it starves when Egypt does not
- **Goshen** — the Delta pasture Jacob's household is given, further downstream; Genesis 47:6 *(not yet
  ingested, and no page yet)*

## Events here

- **Pharaoh's two dreams** — seven fat cattle and seven lean coming up out of the water, Genesis 41:1–7,
  17–24, [[Pharaoh's Dreams and Joseph's Rise]]
- Made the instrument of Pharaoh's decree: every son born to be cast into it — Exodus 1:22,
  [[The Oppression in Egypt]]
- The infant Moses set among the reeds and drawn out — Exodus 2:1–10, [[The Birth of Moses]]
- Its water to be poured out as blood, the third sign given at the bush — Exodus 4:9,
  [[The Burning Bush]]
- Turned to blood, the first plague — Exodus 7:14–25, [[Water Turned to Blood]]
