# Secu

## Overview

### The great well on the road to Naioth — 1 Samuel 19:22

When three groups of his messengers have prophesied instead of seizing David, Saul *also went to Ramah, and came to the
great well that is in Secu: and he asked, "Where are Samuel and David?" One said, "Behold, they are at Naioth in
Ramah"* (19:22). It is the only stop named on Saul's way, and the only place in the pericope where anyone answers him.
See [[Saul Among the Prophets at Naioth]].

**The well.** *Bôr haggāḏôl ʾăšer baśśeḵû* (WLC). WEB's *well* renders *bôr*, which in the Hebrew files checked is a
cistern or pit, the word for the pit Joseph was thrown into (Genesis 37:20–29) and for the pits the Israelites hid in
(1 Samuel 13:6); a well is usually *bǝʾēr*. The text does not say whether the *great* cistern was a landmark, or why
Saul stopped there.

**Name and location.** In the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, Ruth; WLC), *Secu* (7906) occurs only in
this verse. The narrative puts it on Saul's way *to Ramah*, so presumably between Gibeah and Ramah, but the text does
not place it. Some ancient versions read the verse differently (recalled, not checked). No site is identified.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 19:22; Genesis 37:20–29; 1 Samuel 13:6. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-28.
