# Shittim

## Overview

Israel’s campsite at the opening of Numbers 25:1. Here sexual infidelity and participation in
foreign worship lead to the Peor crisis. The preceding setting is the plains of Moab (Numbers 22:1);
this verse supplies no precise modern location.
