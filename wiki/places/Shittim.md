# Shittim

## Overview

### Rahab and the spies — Joshua 2:1–24

Joshua sends the two spies from Shittim (1). The camp previously associated with the Peor crisis becomes the departure point for a mission that returns in confidence.

See [[Rahab and the Spies]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 2:1–24.

### Comparison with Numbers 33

Numbers 33:49 names [[Abel Shittim]] as one end of the final encampment. Its relationship to Shittim in 25:1 is plausible, but the verses do not expressly equate the names.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]].

Israel’s campsite at the opening of Numbers 25:1. Here sexual infidelity and participation in
foreign worship lead to the Peor crisis. The preceding setting is the plains of Moab (Numbers 22:1);
this verse supplies no precise modern location.
