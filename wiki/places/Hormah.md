# Hormah

## Overview

### The sentence and the ascent — Deuteronomy 1:34–46

Deuteronomy 1:44 recalls the rout reaching Hormah, locating the beating in Seir and naming Amorites
as the pursuers. Numbers 14:45 names Amalekites and Canaanites instead; the different enemy
designations remain explicit.

See [[The Sentence and the Presumptuous Ascent]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:34–46; Numbers 14 and 20 for comparisons.

### Arad and Hormah — Numbers 21:1–3

Numbers 21:3 now supplies the ingested naming account: Israel destroys the Canaanites
and their cities after Yahweh hears its vow. The earlier defeat at Numbers 14:45 and
the distinct naming of Zephath in Judges 1:17 remain unreconciled accounts; Arad and
Hormah are not explicitly equated in this unit.

See [[Arad and Hormah]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:1–3.

The place in the far south of Canaan where the rout of the first generation ends, and later a town of
Judah and Simeon. Numbers uses the name at 14:45, before it has been explained.

*Ḥormâ* is the root of *ḥērem*, the word for what is devoted to destruction, and two different
naming stories attach to that sense. At Numbers 21:1–3 Israel vows to destroy the cities of the king
of Arad, and *the name of the place was called Hormah*. At Judges 1:17 Judah and Simeon destroy
Zephath, and *the name of the city was called Hormah*. So the name of Israel's defeat is also the name
of its later victory near the same border. See
[[Even to Hormah - Numbers 14, Numbers 21, Deuteronomy 1, and Judges 1]].

Joshua 12:14 lists its king beside the king of Arad. Joshua 15:30 and 19:4 place it in the towns of
Judah and of Simeon, and 1 Chronicles 4:30 keeps it among Simeon's. At 1 Samuel 30:30 David sends a
share of plunder there. The site is not fixed by the text, and modern identifications were not
checked.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** A king of Hormah is counted among those *Joshua and the children of Israel struck* west of the Jordan (12:14) — between the naming in Numbers 21:3 and the naming in Judges 1:17, and fitting neither easily. See [[Even to Hormah - Numbers 14, Numbers 21, Deuteronomy 1, and Judges 1]] and [[The Kings Struck Down]].
