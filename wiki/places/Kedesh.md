# Kedesh

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:22, among the northern cities of the list. No battle is narrated. Joshua names a Kedesh among Judah's southern towns (15:23), a Kedesh among Naphtali's cities (19:37), and *Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali* as a city of refuge (20:7; 21:32). The list's northern neighbors make a northern Kedesh likely for 12:22, but the verse does not say which; this page covers the king list only, and later ingests decide whether to merge or qualify. Distinct from [[Kadesh]], the wilderness oasis.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
