# Kedesh

## Overview

### Kedesh Naphtali — Judges 4:6–11

Barak comes *out of Kedesh Naphtali* (4:6), *qedeš naptālî* (WLC), the name that settles which Kedesh
is meant. Deborah goes there with him, and Barak calls Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh (4:9–10).
Heber the Kenite's tent is at *the oak in Zaanannim, which is by Kedesh* (4:11). See [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Hebrew WLC.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Naphtali to Gershon’s descendants (21:32).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Refuge cities appointed — Joshua 20:1–9

Kedesh in Galilee in Naphtali’s hill country is set apart as a western refuge (7). This locates the refuge explicitly; identification with the unqualified royal city in Joshua 12:22 remains uncertain.

See [[The Cities of Refuge Appointed]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 20:1–9.

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

A Kedesh is listed among Naphtali’s fortified cities (37). Joshua 20:7 will name *Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali* as a city of refuge. Identification with the king-list Kedesh (12:22) remains unstated.

See [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48; comparisons as cited.

Joshua 15:23 lists a southern Kedesh among Judah's cities; it has its own page, [[Kedesh (Judah)]], and is not identified with this one.

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:22, among the northern cities of the list. No battle is narrated. Joshua names a Kedesh among Judah's southern towns (15:23), a Kedesh among Naphtali's cities (19:37), and *Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali* as a city of refuge (20:7; 21:32). The list's northern neighbors make a northern Kedesh likely for 12:22, but the verse does not say which; this page now also covers Naphtali’s town and refuge, while keeping the king-list identification provisional. Distinct from [[Kadesh]], the wilderness oasis.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
