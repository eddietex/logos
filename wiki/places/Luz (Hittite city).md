# Luz (Hittite city)

## Overview

The city built by the man of Luz after the house of Joseph took Bethel. He showed the spies the way
in, was let go with his family, *went into the land of the Hittites, built a city, and called its name
Luz, which is its name to this day* (Judges 1:26, stored WEB). It is the only notice of this city in
the texts the wiki has ingested, and its location is not given beyond *the land of the Hittites*.

Distinct from the old Luz, which is [[Bethel]] under its earlier name (Genesis 28:19; Judges 1:23). The
qualifier keeps the two apart: the old name is recorded on Bethel's page, and this page holds the
second city the name was carried to.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 28:19; Judges 1:23–26; Hebrew
of 1:26 (*ʾereṣ haḥittîm*) read in the WLC.
