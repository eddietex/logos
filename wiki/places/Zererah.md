# Zererah

## Overview

*As far as Beth Shittah toward Zererah* (Judges 7:22). Named only as the direction of Midian's flight;
the Hebrew has the directional ending, *ṣərērātâ*, *toward Zererah* (WLC). It occurs in Genesis–Judges
only here (WLC). The site is not identified here.
