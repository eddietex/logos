# Kadesh

## Overview

The great oasis at the southern edge of the land — the place Israel spends most of the wilderness
years, and where nearly everything that goes wrong on the way to Canaan goes wrong.

*Qādēš* means **holy**, or **set apart**, and it is the same root as *qōdeš*, sanctuary. The fuller
name **Kadesh Barnea** distinguishes it from the Kadesh on the Orontes in Syria. The standard
identification is **ʿAin el-Qudeirat** in the northeastern Sinai, the largest spring in the region,
with the nearby ʿAin Qadeis preserving the ancient name; together they are the best water for many
days' travel in any direction, which is why a nomadic people could hold there for a generation.

**In Genesis it is a marker rather than a place anyone does anything in.** It is given first under
another name — *"En Mishpat (also called Kadesh)"*, the spring of judgment, where Chedorlaomer's
coalition turns after striking the Amalekites (14:7); it is one bound of the country [[Hagar]] is
found in, since her well lies *"between Kadesh and Bered"* (16:14); and at 20:1 it is the eastern
marker of where Abraham settles, *"between Kadesh and Shur"*. **Between Kadesh and Shur is as far
south as any patriarch lives.**

**What it becomes** is out of all proportion to that. Israel camps here after the exodus and sends
the twelve spies into the land from it; the refusal to go up, and the sentence of forty years, happen
here (Numbers 13:26; 14:1–35). Moses strikes the rock here instead of speaking to it and is told he
will not enter the land (Numbers 20:1–13), and Miriam is buried here in the same passage. The
embassies to Edom go out from here (Numbers 20:14–21). Deuteronomy 1:46 sums it up in one line:
*"So you stayed in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you remained."* It is the address of
the wilderness generation.

Later it fixes the southern border of the land in the boundary lists (Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:3;
Ezekiel 47:19), so that the place where the first generation refused to enter becomes the survey
point for what the second one receives.

## Related places

- [[Shur]] — the frontier toward Egypt, and the other half of Genesis 20:1's bearing
- [[The Negev]] — the dry country between them
- [[Beer Lahai Roi]] — the well *"between Kadesh and Bered"* that Hagar names
- [[Egypt]] — what lies beyond Shur, and what Israel comes out of before camping here
- [[Canaan]] — the land the spies are sent into from here, and refused

## Events here

- Chedorlaomer's coalition strikes the Amalekites at En Mishpat, that is Kadesh — Genesis 14:7,
  [[The Rescue of Lot and Melchizedek]]
- Hagar's well is located between Kadesh and Bered — Genesis 16:14, [[Hagar and Ishmael]]
- Abraham settles between Kadesh and Shur and sojourns in Gerar — Genesis 20:1,
  [[Abraham and Abimelech]]
- The spies are sent into Canaan from here, and the generation is sentenced not to enter —
  Numbers 13:26; 14:1–35
- Miriam dies and is buried here; Moses strikes the rock at Meribah — Numbers 20:1–13
- Messengers go from here to the king of Edom — Numbers 20:14–21
