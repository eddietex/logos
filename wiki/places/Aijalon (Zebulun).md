# Aijalon (Zebulun)

## Overview

*Elon the Zebulunite died, and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun* (Judges 12:12). Named
only here. It is not in Zebulun's city register (Joshua 19:10–16), and its site is unknown.

The text's own qualifier, *in the land of Zebulun*, separates it from the better-known
[[Aijalon]] of Dan (Joshua 19:42; 21:24; Judges 1:35). In the WLC both are *ʾayyālôn*, lemma 357.

**The judge's name.** The town is spelled with the same consonants, *ʾylwn*, as the judge's name at
12:11 (*ʾêlôn*). Whether the town took its name from him, the two are variant spellings of one name,
or the likeness is coincidence, the text does not say. Some ancient translations are reported to read
*Elon* here; that is recalled, not verified.

See [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 12:12; Joshua 19:10–16; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.
