# Arad

## Overview

### The Negev of Arad — Judges 1:16

The Kenites settle *in the wilderness of Judah, which is in the south of Arad* (16). Arad marks the
region; nothing is said of the city itself.

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

### The stages of the journey — Numbers 33:1–49

The king hears of Israel’s coming (40); unlike Numbers 21:1–3, this notice gives no battle outcome.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49.

The city or royal center identifying the Canaanite king in Numbers 21:1. He lives in
[[The Negev]] and attacks Israel as it comes by [[Atharim]]. His people's cities are
destroyed after Israel's vow. The text does not explicitly equate Arad with [[Hormah]],
and no modern site identification is adopted here.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** A king of Arad is counted among those Joshua struck (12:14), after Numbers 21:1–3's battle with Arad's king in Moses' time. The list does not say whether it is the same king. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
