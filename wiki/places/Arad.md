# Arad

## Overview

### The stages of the journey — Numbers 33:1–49

The king hears of Israel’s coming (40); unlike Numbers 21:1–3, this notice gives no battle outcome.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49.

The city or royal center identifying the Canaanite king in Numbers 21:1. He lives in
[[The Negev]] and attacks Israel as it comes by [[Atharim]]. His people's cities are
destroyed after Israel's vow. The text does not explicitly equate Arad with [[Hormah]],
and no modern site identification is adopted here.
