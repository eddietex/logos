# The Great Sea

## Overview

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

The sea closes Manasseh’s western boundary (9–10). No new coastal conquest is narrated.

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

### Ephraim’s inheritance — Joshua 16:1–10

The sea terminates the westward lines in verses 3 and 8. Identification as the western Great Sea follows the boundary’s direction and broader setting; these verses call it only the sea.

See [[The Inheritance of Ephraim]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

The Philistine-plain section of Judah's cities ends at *the great sea with its coastline* (47), echoing the west border of 15:12.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### Judah’s borders — Joshua 15:1–12

The southern and northern boundary routes terminate at the sea, whose shore forms Judah’s west border (4, 11–12).

See [[The Borders of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12.

### The Gibeonite deception — Joshua 9:1–27

Its shore in front of Lebanon defines part of the coalition’s territory (1). The description gives
the scope of opposition rather than an episode at sea.

See [[The Gibeonite Deception]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27; comparisons as cited.


### Circumcision at Gilgal — Joshua 5:1–9

The Canaanite kings live by the sea (1), understood geographically as the western sea already named in Joshua 1:4. Verse 1 itself supplies only “the sea,” not the fuller place-name.

See [[The Second Circumcision at Gilgal]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 5:1–9; comparisons as cited.

### The border given — Joshua 1:1–9

*The great sea toward the going down of the sun* (4), the western limit of the land, where Deuteronomy 11:24 had *the western sea*.

See [[After the Death of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 1:1–9.

The sea defines the western border and provides the starting point for the northern line.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34:5–7. No precise modern site is assigned here.

### The western sea — Deuteronomy 11:18–32

Deuteronomy 11:24 names *"the western sea"* as the end of the border; Joshua 1:4 has *"the great sea toward the going down of the sun"* (WEB, verified from bible-api.com). The wiki files both with this page.

See [[Set These Words Before You]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 11:18–32.


### The death of Moses — Deuteronomy 34:1–12

*The western sea* is the limit of the view westward (2); the wiki files the phrase here as with 11:24.

See [[The Death of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 34:1–12.
