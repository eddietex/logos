# Gibeah (Benjamin)

## Overview

### *Gibeah of Saul* — 1 Samuel 11:4–7

*The messengers came to Gibeah of Saul*, *giḇʿaṯ šāʾûl* (11:4, WLC), the first time the town is named after its
man. The people weep at the news from Jabesh Gilead, and Saul comes in from the field (11:4–5). From here he
sends the pieces of a yoke of oxen *throughout all the borders of Israel* (11:7), the words of Judges 19:29, where
a Levite sent the pieces of a woman abused in this town. In Judges the pieces brought Israel against Gibeah; here
they bring Israel out for Jabesh Gilead. See [[Saul Rescues Jabesh Gilead]] and
[[Cut Limb by Limb - The Burnt Offering, the Concubine, and Saul's Oxen]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 11:4–7;
Judges 19:29; Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-18.

### Saul's home — 1 Samuel 10:26

*Saul also went to his house to Gibeah; and the army went with him, whose hearts God had touched* (10:26). The WLC
has *giḇʿāṯâ*, *to Gibeah*, the place name (lemma 1390), not the common noun of *the hill of God* at 10:5. This is
the first time the stored text makes Gibeah Saul's town. In the same verse the men who reject him are called *sons
of bəliyyaʿal* (10:27, WLC), the phrase used of Gibeah's men in Judges 19:22 and 20:13. See
[[Saul Chosen by Lot at Mizpah]] and [[Sons of Belial - Worthless Men from Deuteronomy to Samuel]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 10:5, 26–27; Judges 19:22; 20:13. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Besieged, entered, and burned — Judges 20

The assembly at Mizpah resolves *this is the thing which we will do to Gibeah* (20:9) and demands *the
wicked fellows, who are in Gibeah* (13). Benjamin gathers there instead, with *seven hundred chosen men*
of Gibeah's own (15). Twice its army comes out and defeats Israel (21, 25). On the third day Israel sets
ambushes *all around Gibeah* (29), draws its defenders out onto the highways *of which one goes up to
Bethel, and the other to Gibeah* (31), and the ambush *rushed on Gibeah … and struck all the city with
the edge of the sword* (37). Smoke is the agreed sign, and *the whole city went up in smoke to the sky*
(40) — *kəlîl hāʿîr* (WLC), with the *kālîl*, *whole* or *whole burnt offering*, of Deuteronomy 13:16
(Hebrew 13:17) for the condemned city. The text does not say Gibeah was rebuilt; it is Saul's town in
1 Samuel 10:26. In verse 10 the WLC reads *Geba of Benjamin* where the WEB has *Gibeah*; see [[Geba]].

See [[The War Against Benjamin]], [[Drawn Away from the City - Ai and Gibeah]], and
[[The Whole City Went Up - Deuteronomy 13 and Gibeah]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 20:9–48; Deuteronomy
13:16; Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-17.

### The night at Gibeah — Judges 19:12–28

*Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin* (19:14), a town just north of Jebus on the road from Bethlehem to the
hill country of Ephraim, named with [[Ramah]] as a place to lodge (19:13). The Levite passes by Jebus,
*the city of a foreigner*, to reach it (19:12). In its street *there was no one who took them into his
house to stay* (19:15); the one host is an old man *from the hill country of Ephraim* who *lived in
Gibeah; but the men of the place were Benjamites* (19:16). *The men of the city, certain wicked fellows*,
surround the house and demand the guest, and the concubine is abused all night and found at the threshold
(19:22–27). The scene repeats Sodom's; see [[Gibeah and Sodom - Judges 19 as a Retelling]].

*Gibʿâ* means *hill*. The name is kept apart in the wiki from [[Gibeah (Judah)]] (Joshua 15:57) and from
[[Gibeath]] in Benjamin's allotment list (Joshua 18:28). The identification of this Gibeah with Gibeath is
commonly proposed and not made here, and no modern site is assigned.

### After the stored text

Gibeah's war is told in [[The War Against Benjamin]] (Judges 20) and set beside Ai in
[[Drawn Away from the City - Ai and Gibeah]]. It becomes Saul's home at 1 Samuel 10:26 (above), and *Gibeah of Saul* at 11:4 (stored WEB; not yet ingested). Outside
the stored text it is Hosea's byword, *as in the days of
Gibeah* (9:9; 10:9) (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not in raw).
