# Gibeah (Benjamin)

## Overview

### The night at Gibeah — Judges 19:12–28

*Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin* (19:14), a town just north of Jebus on the road from Bethlehem to the
hill country of Ephraim, named with [[Ramah]] as a place to lodge (19:13). The Levite passes by Jebus,
*the city of a foreigner*, to reach it (19:12). In its street *there was no one who took them into his
house to stay* (19:15); the one host is an old man *from the hill country of Ephraim* who *lived in
Gibeah; but the men of the place were Benjamites* (19:16). *The men of the city, certain wicked fellows*,
surround the house and demand the guest, and the concubine is abused all night and found at the threshold
(19:22–27). The scene repeats Sodom's; see [[Gibeah and Sodom - Judges 19 as a Retelling]].

*Gibʿâ* means *hill*. The name is kept apart in the wiki from [[Gibeah (Judah)]] (Joshua 15:57) and from
[[Gibeath]] in Benjamin's allotment list (Joshua 18:28). The identification of this Gibeah with Gibeath is
commonly proposed and not made here, and no modern site is assigned.

### After the stored text

Gibeah is the town of the war that follows (Judges 20, not yet ingested; see
[[Drawn Away from the City - Ai and Gibeah]]). Outside the stored text it is Saul's home — *Saul also went
to his house to Gibeah* (1 Samuel 10:26), *Gibeah of Saul* (11:4) — and Hosea's byword, *as in the days of
Gibeah* (9:9; 10:9) (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not in raw).
