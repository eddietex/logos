# Dibon

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Listed in Reuben’s portion (17), though Gad builds it in Numbers 32:34. Building and allotment are different actions, but neither account narrates a transfer; the attribution tension remains.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### Comparison with Numbers 33

Numbers 33:45–46 names [[Dibon Gad]], plausibly related to the Dibon built by Gad in 32:34. The itinerary does not expressly identify the two; separate pages preserve that uncertainty.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]].

### Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan — Numbers 32:1–42

Named in the request of Reuben and Gad (3) and built by Gad (34). Joshua 13:17 lists Dibon in Reuben's
border (stored WEB; now ingested); the passage page records the disagreement without
resolving it.

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.

Named as the extent of Heshbon’s ruin in the quoted poem (30). The passage does not separately
narrate a battle at Dibon.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.
