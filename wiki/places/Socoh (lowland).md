# Socoh (lowland)

## Overview

### *Socoh, which belongs to Judah* — 1 Samuel 17:1

*The Philistines gathered together their armies to battle; and they were gathered together at Socoh, which belongs to
Judah, and encamped between Socoh and Azekah* (17:1). This is the first event narrated at the town. The wiki takes it
to be the lowland Socoh because Joshua 15:35 lists *Socoh, Azekah* together in the lowland; the hill-country Socoh
(15:48) is not near Azekah in the list. The verse's *which belongs to Judah*, *ʾăšer lîhûḏâ* (WLC), does not by itself
decide between them. See [[Goliath Defies Israel]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:35, 48; 1 Samuel 17:1. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-21.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the lowland (Joshua 15:35), one of the *fourteen cities with their villages* of the first lowland group (33–36). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated there.

The list names a second Socoh in the hill country (48), [[Socoh (hill country)]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
