# Socoh (lowland)

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the lowland (Joshua 15:35), one of the *fourteen cities with their villages* of the first lowland group (33–36). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

The list names a second Socoh in the hill country (48), [[Socoh (hill country)]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
