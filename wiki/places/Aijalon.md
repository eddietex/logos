# Aijalon

## Overview

A town in Dan’s border list (41–46), for which no city total is given (42). Related by name to [[The Valley of Aijalon]] of Joshua 10:12, but this verse lists a city and does not mention the valley.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
