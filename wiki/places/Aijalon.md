# Aijalon

## Overview

### Held by the Amorites — Judges 1:35

*The Amorites would dwell in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim*, until the house of Joseph subjected them to forced labor (35). Aijalon was in Dan's list (Joshua 19:42), and Joshua 10:12 had the moon stand still in *the valley of Aijalon* on the day *Yahweh delivered up the Amorites*.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Dan to the remaining Kohathites (21:24).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

A town in Dan’s border list (41–46), for which no city total is given (42). Related by name to [[The Valley of Aijalon]] of Joshua 10:12, but this verse lists a city and does not mention the valley.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
