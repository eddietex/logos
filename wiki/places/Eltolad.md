# Eltolad

## Overview

### Three tribal lots — Joshua 19:1–23

Listed among Simeon’s towns within Judah’s inheritance (19:4); verse 9 explains the shared territory by Judah’s oversized portion. No new capture is narrated.

See [[The Lots of Simeon, Zebulun, and Issachar]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23; comparisons as cited.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the farthest cities of Judah toward the border of Edom in the South (Joshua 15:30), one of the names in the southern group that verse 32 totals as *twenty-nine* cities with their villages, though thirty-six names are listed (21–32). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Simeon's list in 19:4 also names Eltolad; see [[Simeon's Lot Within Judah - Joshua 15 and 19]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
