# The Wilderness of Zin

## Overview

### The waters of Meribah — Numbers 20:1–13

The congregation arrives in the first month and stays at Kadesh (1), where Miriam dies.
The verse supplies no year. Numbers 13:26 locates Kadesh in Paran; this geographic tension
remains explicit, without imposing an unprovided boundary between the wildernesses.

See [[The Waters of Meribah]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 20:1–13.

The southern edge of the land. Numbers 13:21 gives it as the start of the search, *from the
wilderness of Zin to Rehob*. It is *ṣin* (6790 in the tags), not the *sîn* of
[[The Wilderness of Sin]] (5512), which lies on the far side of Sinai. Rahlfs writes both alike
here, *Σιν*.

**It holds Kadesh, in some verses.** Numbers 20:1 brings the whole congregation there and has the
people stay at Kadesh, and 33:36 says they *encamped at Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin*, with
Hebrew *hiwʾ qādēš*, *that is Kadesh* (WLC). But Numbers 13:26 has *the wilderness of Paran, to
Kadesh*. So either the two wildernesses meet at Kadesh or the names overlap. The text does not
settle it. See [[Kadesh]] and [[Paran]].

**It is a border.** Numbers 34:3–4 draws the land's south side from *the wilderness of Zin along by
the side of Edom*, and Joshua 15:1, 3 uses it for Judah's border (by the tags). So the spies start
at what will be the frontier. Numbers 27:14 and Deuteronomy 32:51 name it for the sin at Meribah
(tags; *not yet ingested*).
