# Chezib

## Overview

**Named once, in a clause that does nothing else — and the name means *lie*.**

> **5** She yet again bore a son, and named him Shelah: and he was at Chezib, when she bore him. (38:5)

***Kəzîb*** is from ***kāzab***, **to lie, to deceive, to prove false, to fail** — the root behind
*"God is not a man, that he should lie"* (Numbers 23:19) and behind the image of a stream that dries up
when you need it, *"a deceitful brook"* (Jeremiah 15:18). **Genesis attaches it to the birth of the one
son Judah will withhold from [[Tamar]] behind an excuse**, and makes no remark. See
[[Shelah (son of Judah)]] and [[Deception]].

**The wiki records the wordplay as a feature of the Hebrew and does not claim the narrator intended
it** — the same footing [[Er]]'s name and [[Enaim]] are recorded on in this chapter. **What can be said
is that the clause is otherwise pointless.** The birthplace of no other son in the chapter is given,
and nothing in the story ever happens at Chezib.

## Identification

**Almost certainly the Achzib of Joshua 15:44**, listed in the Shephelah district of Judah with Keilah
and Mareshah, and **the Achzib of Micah 1:14** — where the prophet makes the pun that Genesis leaves
unspoken: *"the houses of Achzib will be a deceitful thing (*ʾakzāb*) to the kings of Israel."* **Micah
is punning on exactly the root this verse's name comes from**, in a passage that puns its way through
half a dozen Shephelah towns. A third reference, **1 Chronicles 4:22**, names *Cozeba* among the
settlements of [[Shelah (son of Judah)]]'s descendants — **the same consonants again, attached to the
same man's clan**, which is the strongest evidence that all three are one place.

**Not to be confused with the Achzib of Joshua 19:29 and Judges 1:31**, which is a coastal town in
Asher's territory, far to the north.
