# Zobah

## Overview

### *The kings of Zobah* — 1 Samuel 14:47

Among the enemies Saul fought *on every side* are *the kings of Zobah*, *malḵê ṣôḇâ* (14:47, WLC), plural. It is
Zobah's first appearance in Scripture, and the only one in 1 Samuel; in Genesis–2 Samuel the name recurs only in 2
Samuel (8:3, 5, 12; 10:6, 8; 23:36, WLC). No battle with Zobah is narrated in Saul's reign. See
[[Saul's Wars and Household]].

In 2 Samuel, David strikes *Hadadezer the son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as he went to recover his dominion at the
River*, and *the Syrians of Damascus came to help* him (2 Samuel 8:3, 5, WEB at bible-api.com), so Zobah is an
Aramean kingdom with reach toward the Euphrates, and Hadadezer is one king where Saul fought *kings*. 2 Samuel 10:6,
8 has the spelling *ṣôḇāʾ* (WLC). Its usual placement is in Syria, north of Damascus, in or near the Beqaa valley
(recalled, not checked); the text gives no location. See
[[Saul's Wars and David's Wars - 1 Samuel 14.47-52 and 2 Samuel 8]].
