# Nebo (landmark)

## Overview

### Commanded to ascend Nebo — Deuteronomy 32:48–52

Deuteronomy 32:49 explicitly names Mount Nebo, now covered by [[Mount Nebo]]. This distinguishes the named mountain from the unqualified landmark in Numbers 33:47; the latter’s referent is not settled merely by sharing a name.

See [[Moses Commanded to Ascend Nebo]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 32:48–52.

The mountains of Abarim are described as before Nebo (47). This page retains the ambiguous landmark without deciding between the city and the mountain; compare [[Nebo (city)]].

Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49. No modern site is assigned.
