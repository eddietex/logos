# Jattir

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Judah and Simeon to Aaron’s descendants (21:14).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the hill country (Joshua 15:48), one of the *eleven cities with their villages* of the first hill-country group (48–51). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Joshua 21:14 names Jattir among the cities given to the priests out of Judah and Simeon.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
