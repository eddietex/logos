# Kiriath Jearim

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

Kiriath Baal is explicitly Kiriath Jearim, a city of Judah, at the end of Benjamin’s west border (14). The south border starts at its farthest part (15). Kiriath in verse 28 is not explicitly identified with it.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Listed in the hill country as *Kiriath Baal (also called Kiriath Jearim)* (60), a third name beside Baalah (15:9). Benjamin's border also ends at it, *a city of the children of Judah* (18:14).

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### Judah’s borders — Joshua 15:1–12

The text expressly identifies Baalah with Kiriath Jearim (9). The boundary reaches it from Mount Ephron and turns westward toward Mount Seir (9–10).

See [[The Borders of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12.

One of the four cities reached when Israel discovers that the treaty partners are neighbors (16–17).
No later sanctuary history or modern site identification is asserted by this chapter.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27.
