# The Rock of Oreb

## Overview

*They killed Oreb at Oreb's rock* (Judges 7:25). The place where the Midianite prince [[Oreb]] was killed
by Ephraim. The Hebrew is *ṣûr-ʿôrēb*, *rock of Oreb*, or *raven's rock* (WLC). The text does not say
whether it was named for him or already had the name. Isaiah remembers it: *as in the slaughter of Midian
at the rock of Oreb* (Isaiah 10:26, WEB checked; not yet ingested). The site is not identified here.

See [[The Day of Midian - Oreb and Zeeb in Psalm 83 and Isaiah 9-10]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 7:25;
Isaiah 10:26 checked at bible-api.com (WEB); Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
