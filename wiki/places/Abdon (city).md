# Abdon (city)

## Overview

Allocated with its suburbs from Asher to Gershon’s descendants (21:30).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42. No precise modern site is assigned.

**Not the judge.** *Abdon* is also the name of the judge *Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite*
(Judges 12:13–15). In the WLC both are *ʿabdôn* and carry the same lemma (5658), so the page is
qualified as a city and the man as [[Abdon (son of Hillel)]]. The judge is buried in Pirathon in
Ephraim, not here; nothing in the text connects him with this Asherite town.
