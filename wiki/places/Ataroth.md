# Ataroth

## Overview

First in the list of nine towns Reuben and Gad name when they ask for the land east of the Jordan
(Numbers 32:3), and one of the towns the children of Gad build (32:34). The Hebrew is *ʿăṭārôt* (Miqra according
to the Masorah), which looks like the plural of *ʿăṭārâ*, *crown*; the text gives no meaning.

The Mesha Stele, a ninth-century BC Moabite inscription, is usually read as saying that the men of Gad
had long lived in the land of Ataroth before Mesha took it. That is a paraphrase from memory of standard
translations, not checked against an edition in this pass.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
