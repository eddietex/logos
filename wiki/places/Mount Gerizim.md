# Mount Gerizim

## Overview

### Jotham's fable — Judges 9:7–21

*He went and stood on the top of Mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice, cried out, and said to them,
"Listen to me, you men of Shechem, that God may listen to you"* (9:7). The first narrated event on the
mountain. Deuteronomy and Joshua made Gerizim the place of the blessing (Deuteronomy 11:29; 27:12;
Joshua 8:33). Jotham stands there to tell the fable of the trees and to lay a curse on Abimelech and
Shechem, which 9:57 calls *the curse of Jotham*. The text does not comment on the reversal. In the WLC
of Genesis–Judges *Gerizim* occurs only in those four verses. See [[Jotham's Fable]] and
[[Blessing on Gerizim and Curse on Ebal - Deuteronomy 11, 27, and Joshua 8]]. Source: stored WEB
Deuteronomy 11:29; Joshua 8:33; Judges 9:7, 57; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### The law read at Ebal — Joshua 8:30–35

Half the assembly stands in front of Gerizim, opposite those before Ebal and on either side of the ark (33). Joshua reads blessing and curse (34); the narrative does not reproduce separate liturgical formulas or list the tribes again.

See [[The Law Read at Mount Ebal]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 8:30–35.

### The responsive curses — Deuteronomy 27:11–26

Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin must stand here to bless the people (12). No corresponding blessing formulas are recorded in this unit.

See [[The People Answer Amen to the Curses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 27:11–26.


The mountain on which Israel is to *"set the blessing"* once it is in the land (Deuteronomy 11:29).
It stands opposite [[Mount Ebal]], with the town of [[Shechem (city)]] in the pass between them, in
the central hill country. Deuteronomy 11:30 locates the pair by the oaks of Moreh, the landmark at
Shechem where Abram built his first altar in the land (Genesis 12:6–7).

Deuteronomy 11 gives no reason why Gerizim takes the blessing and Ebal the curse. Deuteronomy
27:12 assigns Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin to stand on Gerizim *"to bless
the people"*. In Joshua 8:33 half of Israel stands *"in front of Mount Gerizim"* while the law is
read (WEB, now checked in stored Joshua).

Later, Gerizim became the Samaritans' holy mountain. In John 4:20 the Samaritan woman says, *"Our
fathers worshiped in this mountain"* (WEB, verified from bible-api.com). That history lies outside
Deuteronomy and is noted only so it is not lost; see
[[The Altar at Shechem and the Covenant at Shechem]].
