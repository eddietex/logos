# Minnith

## Overview

### The end of the twenty cities — Judges 11:33

Jephthah *struck them from Aroer until you come to Minnith, even twenty cities, and to Abelcheramim, with
a very great slaughter* (11:33). *Until you come to Minnith*, *wəʿad-bôʾăkā minnît* (WLC), is the
narrator's phrase for a route: *as far as you go to Minnith*. The twenty cities lie between Aroer and
Minnith, and the pursuit goes on to [[Abel Keramim]].

It is the only place in the WLC of Genesis–Judges where Minnith is named. The text gives no other
detail, and the site is not given. If the [[Aroer]] here is the one *near Rabbah* (Joshua 13:25), the
pursuit runs into Ammon's own land. The text does not say which Aroer is meant.

See [[Jephthah's Vow]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 11:33; Joshua 13:25; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-15.
