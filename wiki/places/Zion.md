# Zion

## Overview

The hill of [[Jerusalem]], and in poetry often the city itself. It is a name that will grow through
the Psalms and the Prophets into the city of God, the place of his dwelling, and the people who live
there. The wiki has not yet ingested any of those books, so this page records only what has been
read so far.

**Its first appearance in the wiki's ingested text is in [[Solomon's Wedding Procession]].** *"Go
out, you daughters of Zion, and see king Solomon"* (Song of Solomon 3:11). This is the only time the
Song names Zion. The phrase, *bǝnôṯ ṣiyyôn* (Sefaria, *Miqra according to the Masorah*), stands in
parallel with *"the daughters of Jerusalem"* in the verse before it (3:10). So Zion here is another
name for Jerusalem, and it locates the audience, not the scene. The wiki reads the daughters of Zion
as the same group as [[The Daughters of Jerusalem]], and marks that as a reading.

Zion is not a setting in the Song. The procession of 3:6–11 comes up from the wilderness, and the
poem does not say where it arrives.

How Zion came to be part of Jerusalem is told in 2 Samuel 5: *"David took the stronghold of Zion.
This is David's city"* (5:7). Micah 4:8 calls [[The Tower of Eder]] *"the hill of the daughter of
Zion"*, as quoted on that page; Micah is not yet ingested.

Sources: Song of Solomon 3:10–11 from the stored WEB; Hebrew from Sefaria's *Miqra according to the
Masorah*, 2026-10-28. 2 Samuel 5:7 checked at bible-api.com (`translation=web`), 2026-10-28; Samuel
was not fetched.
