# Rameses

## Overview

**The best land in Egypt, given as a gift — and the address of the bondage.**

> **11** Joseph placed his father and his brothers, and gave them a possession in the land of Egypt,
> in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded. (47:11)

**It enters Scripture here, in the pericope where [[Jacob]]'s household is settled**, and the verse is
generous in every clause: *a possession*, *the best of the land*, *as Pharaoh had commanded*.
**Genesis uses the name once and never again.**

**The rest of the Pentateuch uses it twice more and both times it is about slavery or escape.**
**Exodus 1:11 makes Raamses one of the two store-cities Israel builds for Pharaoh under forced
labour**, with Pithom; **Exodus 12:37 makes Rameses the place the nation marches out of** on the night
of the Passover, on the first stage to Succoth. **Numbers 33:3–5 repeats it as the first station of
the wilderness itinerary.**

**So the district Genesis hands over as a favour is the district Exodus will describe as a work camp,
and then as a starting line.** **The wiki records the sequence and does not moralise it**: nothing in
Genesis 47 suggests a trap, and [[Pharaoh (of Joseph's time)]] is not the king of Exodus 1:8. **What
the text does is name the place three times across two books and let the three uses stand next to each
other.**

### On the name

**It is an anachronism inside Genesis and the book is not troubled by it.** **The name belongs to a
pharaoh of the Nineteenth Dynasty**, centuries after any date usually proposed for [[Jacob]], **and it
is being used of the region around his capital in the eastern delta.** **The straightforward reading
is that a later name has been used for an older place** — the same convention by which Abram at 14:14
*"pursued as far as Dan"*, a town not called Dan until Judges 18:29. **The wiki notes the usage and
does not treat
it as evidence for or against anything else.**

**[[Goshen]] and Rameses are almost certainly the same country under two names.** 47:6 grants Goshen,
47:11 settles them in *the land of Rameses*, and 47:27 will say simply *in the land of Goshen*.
**Genesis treats them as interchangeable without saying so.**
