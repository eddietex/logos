# The Valley of Sorek

## Overview

*He loved a woman in the valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah* (Judges 16:4). It is named once in the
stored text, as [[Delilah]]'s home, and nothing happens there except the story told in
[[Samson and Delilah]]. The Hebrew is *naḥal śōrēq* (WLC); *naḥal* is a wadi or stream valley, the word
of *the brook Kishon* (Judges 4:7, 13; 5:21, WLC).

**Where.** The valley is usually identified with the Wadi es-Sarar, running from the hill country west
past Beth Shemesh toward the coastal plain, between Samson's home towns of [[Zorah]] and [[Eshtaol]] and
Philistine [[Timnah]]. That identification is reported, not checked; the text places it nowhere. The
lords of the Philistines *came up* to it (16:5, 18).

**The name.** *Śōrēq* is commonly connected with *śōrēq*, *choice vine*, as in Genesis 49:11, *his
donkey's colt to the choice vine*. The WLC lemma data gives the place (7796) and the vine there (8321)
different numbers, so the connection is reported, not verified. If it holds, the Nazirite, who *shall eat nothing that is made
of the grapevine* (Numbers 6:4), loses his hair in the vine valley. The wiki
builds nothing on it.

Source: stored WEB Genesis 49:11; Numbers 6:4; Judges 4:7, 13; 5:21; 16:4–5, 18; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-16.
