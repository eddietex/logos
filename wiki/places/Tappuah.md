# Tappuah

## Overview

Joshua 15:34's lowland Tappuah has its own page, [[Tappuah (lowland)]], because the list does not say it is this royal city; [[Beth Tappuah]] (15:53) is distinct again.

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:17. No battle is narrated. Joshua later names a Tappuah among Judah's lowland towns (15:34) and a Tappuah on the border of Ephraim and Manasseh (16:8; 17:8, with *En Tappuah* at 17:7). Which, if either, is the royal city of 12:17 is not stated; this page covers the king list only. The later towns have separate qualified pages, without asserting that the royal city must be a third distinct site.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
