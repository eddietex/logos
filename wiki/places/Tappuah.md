# Tappuah

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:17. No battle is narrated. Joshua later names a Tappuah among Judah's lowland towns (15:34) and a Tappuah on the border of Ephraim and Manasseh (16:8; 17:8, with *En Tappuah* at 17:7). Which, if either, is the royal city of 12:17 is not stated; this page covers the king list only, and the ingest that reaches chapters 15–17 decides whether the later names join this page or take qualified pages of their own.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
