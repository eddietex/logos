# Shen

## Overview

### Between Mizpah and Shen — 1 Samuel 7:12

*Samuel took a stone, and set it between Mizpah and Shen, and called its name Ebenezer* (7:12). The WLC has
the article, *haššēn*, *the tooth* (lemma 8129), which suggests a landmark, a crag or tooth of rock, rather than
a town; that is a reading of the article, not something the verse explains. The name occurs only here in the
WLC files of Genesis through 2 Kings, Ruth, and Psalms. Some ancient versions are commonly reported to read a
different name here (recalled, not checked). No site is assigned. See [[Ebenezer]] and
[[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]].
