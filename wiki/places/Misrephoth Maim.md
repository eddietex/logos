# Misrephoth Maim

## Overview

A limit of Israel’s pursuit (8), alongside Sidon and the valley of Mizpah. This does not establish permanent possession: Joshua 13:6 still describes inhabitants in the region to be driven out.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.
