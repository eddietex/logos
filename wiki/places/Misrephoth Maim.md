# Misrephoth Maim

## Overview

### The land that remains — Joshua 13:1–7

The hill-country inhabitants from Lebanon to Misrephoth Maim remain to be driven out (6). The earlier pursuit reaching this place (11:8) and the present promise describe different stages, without supplying a chronology of settlement.

See [[The Land That Remains]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.

A limit of Israel’s pursuit (8), alongside Sidon and the valley of Mizpah. This does not establish permanent possession: Joshua 13:6 still describes inhabitants in the region to be driven out.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.
