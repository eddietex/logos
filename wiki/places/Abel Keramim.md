# Abel Keramim

## Overview

### The farthest point of the pursuit — Judges 11:33

*He struck them from Aroer until you come to Minnith, even twenty cities, and to Abelcheramim, with a
very great slaughter* (11:33). The WEB writes the name as one word. The WLC has two, *ʾābēl kərāmîm*,
both tagged as the place name. The first word is the *Abel* of [[Abel Meholah]], [[Abel Mizraim]] and
[[Abel Shittim]], usually taken to mean *meadow* or *watercourse*. The second is spelled like
*kərāmîm*, *vineyards*, as in *come out of the vineyards* (21:21, *min-hakkərāmîm*, WLC). The gloss
*meadow of vineyards* is an inference from those two words. The text does not explain the name.

It is the only place in the WLC of Genesis–Judges where it is named, and the site is not given.

See [[Jephthah's Vow]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 11:33; 21:21; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-15.
