# Ibleam

## Overview

### Not driven out — Judges 1:27

One of Manasseh's five cities not driven out (27), as in Joshua 17:11–12.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

City with dependent towns in Manasseh’s list (11). The inhabitants persist, and the subsequent
notice records forced labor without complete removal (12–13).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.
