# Mount Ebal

## Overview

The mountain on which Israel is to *"set … the curse"* once it is in the land (Deuteronomy 11:29).
It stands opposite [[Mount Gerizim]], with [[Shechem (city)]] in the pass between them, and
Deuteronomy 11:30 locates both by the oaks of Moreh.

The curse mountain is also the altar mountain. Deuteronomy 27:4–7 commands that the law be
written on plastered stones on Mount Ebal and an altar of uncut stones be built there, and
Deuteronomy 27:13 assigns Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali to stand on Ebal *"for
the curse"*. Joshua 8:30–35 narrates the altar and the reading of *"the blessing and the curse"*
(Joshua 8:34, WEB, verified from bible-api.com). The wiki records that the altar, offerings, and
written law are placed on the mountain of the curse, not of the blessing; none of these texts
explains the choice.
