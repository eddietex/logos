# Bochim

## Overview

### The angel's verdict — Judges 2:1–5

*Yahweh's angel came up from Gilgal to Bochim* (2:1). There he charges Israel with not listening to
his voice and says Yahweh will not drive out the peoples of the land. The people *lifted up their
voice, and wept*. *They called the name of that place Bochim, and they sacrificed there to Yahweh*
(2:4–5, stored WEB).

See [[The Angel at Bochim]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 2:1–5.

**The name.** *Bōkîm* is the plural participle of *bākâ*, *to weep*: *weepers*, *those who weep*.
Judges 2:1 has it with the article, *habbōkîm*, and calls the place by that name before verse 5 says
how it got it (WLC, OpenScriptures morphhb, 2026-11-13). This is the only place in the stored text
named for a whole people's weeping. *Allon Bacuth*, *oak of weeping*, below Bethel, is the other name
in Scripture from the same root, and it was a tree named at a funeral (Genesis 35:8).

**Location unknown.** The text does not say where Bochim was, except that one goes *up* to it from
Gilgal. The Septuagint of 2:1 is often said to add *and to Bethel*; that is reported as a claim, not
checked here. If correct, it would put Bochim near Bethel and near the *Allon Bacuth*. The wiki does
not identify Bochim with either. See
[[The Place of Weepers - Allon Bacuth, Bochim, and Bethel]].

**Sacrifice here.** The people sacrifice at Bochim (2:5). The tent of meeting was at Shiloh (Joshua
18:1, stored WEB). The text does not comment on the difference.
