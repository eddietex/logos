# Achzib (Asher)

## Overview

Asher’s border *ended at the sea by the region of Achzib* (29). Distinct from the lowland Achzib of Judah (15:44), which the wiki covers at [[Chezib]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
