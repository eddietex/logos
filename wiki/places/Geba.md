# Geba

## Overview

### The Philistine garrison struck — 1 Samuel 13:3

*Jonathan struck the garrison of the Philistines that was in Geba*, *bəḡeḇaʿ* (13:3, WLC, lemma 1387), while his
thousand were posted at *Gibeah of Benjamin* (13:2, lemma 1390); the two names stand in consecutive verses. After
Samuel leaves Gilgal, Saul and Jonathan are at *Geba of Benjamin*, facing the Philistines at [[Michmash]] (13:16,
stored WEB; the next pericope). See [[Saul's Sacrifice at Gilgal]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:2–3, 16; Hebrew
WLC, 2026-11-18.

### Saul and Jonathan at Geba — 1 Samuel 13:16

*Saul, and Jonathan his son, and the people who were present with them, stayed in Geba of Benjamin* (13:16), facing
the Philistines at [[Michmash]]. The six hundred hold the post unarmed except for the king and his son (13:22). The
next chapter puts Saul at *the uttermost part of Gibeah* (14:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested); the Geba/Gibeah
question recorded below stays open. See [[No Smith in Israel]].

### Facing the crag Seneh — 1 Samuel 14:5

The southern of the two crags at the pass rises *in front of Geba* (14:5), *mûl gāḇaʿ* (WLC). The narrator's Israelite
positions in the chapter are *the uttermost part of Gibeah* (14:2) and *Gibeah of Benjamin* (14:16), where Saul's
watchmen stand (WLC *haggiḇʿâ*, *giḇʿaṯ binyāmin*); Geba here marks the edge of the pass. Isaiah also sets Geba just
past the pass: *They have gone over the pass. They have taken up their lodging at Geba* (Isaiah 10:29, checked at
bible-api.com, WEB, not stored). The Geba/Gibeah question below stays open. See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 14:2, 5, 16. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-19.

### Geba in Judges 20:10 and Maareh Geba — Judges 20:33

Two verses of the war against Benjamin have this name in the Hebrew. In verse 10 the assembly plans what it
will do *when they come to Gibeah of Benjamin* (WEB), but the WLC reads *ləḡeḇaʿ binyāmin*, *to Geba of
Benjamin* (lemma 1387). In verse 33 *the ambushers of Israel broke out of their place, even out of Maareh
Geba*, *mimmaʿărê-ḡāḇaʿ* (WLC), which the morphhb data tags with Gibeah's lemma (1390), not Geba's. Geba and
Gibeah differ in Hebrew only in their ending, and in both verses the story is about Gibeah. The wiki records
the Hebrew, follows the WEB in reading Gibeah at verse 10, and files *Maareh Geba* here as an alias rather
than as a separate place. *Maʿărê* may be a common noun, *the open* or *bare place* of Geba — reported, not
checked.

See [[The War Against Benjamin]] and [[Gibeah (Benjamin)]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 20:10, 33; Hebrew
WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Benjamin to Aaron’s descendants (21:17).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

Named among Benjamin’s first twelve cities with their villages (24). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.
