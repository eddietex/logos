# Bozkath

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the second group of the lowland (Joshua 15:39), one of the *sixteen cities with their villages* of the second lowland group (37–41). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
