# Jarmuth

## Overview

### The five kings at Makkedah — Joshua 10:16–27

Its king, [[Piram]], is brought from the cave and killed at Makkedah (23–27). The city is named to identify its ruler; the king’s death is not itself a report that the city has been captured.

See [[The Five Kings in the Cave at Makkedah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:16–27.


Piram’s royal city, whose army joins the attack on Gibeon (3–5). No capture of the city is narrated
in this unit.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No modern identification is assigned.
