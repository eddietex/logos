# Geshur

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

The eastern border population remains within Israel, not driven out (11, 13). The conquered-kingdom summary of verse 12 does not erase this explicit exception. Its relation to the southern Geshurites of 13:2 remains unspecified.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### The land that remains — Joshua 13:1–7

Joshua 13:2 names Geshurites alongside the Philistine regions among land remaining. This southern context differs from the Geshurites beside Bashan in 12:5 and 13:11–13. This page records the two notices without identifying the populations or asserting that their territories are the same; the text does not settle their relationship.

See [[The Land That Remains]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.

The land of the Geshurites, named as a border of Og's kingdom of Bashan (Joshua 12:5) and earlier in [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]], where Jair takes Argob *to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites* (Deuteronomy 3:14). Joshua 13:11 repeats the border, and 13:13 adds the qualification: *the children of Israel didn't drive out the Geshurites, nor the Maacathites: but Geshur and Maacath live within Israel to this day.* Joshua 13:2 also lists *all the Geshurites* among the land still remaining, beside the Philistines; whether that is the same people as the Geshurites by Bashan is not stated, and the wiki does not decide it here.
