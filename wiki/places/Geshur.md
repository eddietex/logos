# Geshur

## Overview

The land of the Geshurites, named as a border of Og's kingdom of Bashan (Joshua 12:5) and earlier in [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]], where Jair takes Argob *to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites* (Deuteronomy 3:14). Joshua 13:11 repeats the border, and 13:13 adds the qualification: *the children of Israel didn't drive out the Geshurites, nor the Maacathites: but Geshur and Maacath live within Israel to this day.* Joshua 13:2 also lists *all the Geshurites* among the land still remaining, beside the Philistines; whether that is the same people as the Geshurites by Bashan is not stated, and the wiki does not decide it here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 and Deuteronomy 3 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
