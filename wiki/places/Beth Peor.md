# Beth Peor

## Overview

### The death of Moses — Deuteronomy 34:1–12

Moses is buried *in the valley in the land of Moab opposite Beth Peor*, though *no man knows where his tomb is to this day* (6). This is the same valley setting as 3:29 and 4:46.

See [[The Death of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 34:1–12.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

The valley opposite Beth Peor is the setting of Moses’ instruction (46), continuing the stay recorded in 3:29. The text does not say that Israel entered the settlement.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

The closing notice locates Israel’s stay in the valley near Beth Peor (29). It does not say that
the people entered the settlement or identify the valley with a particular modern site.
