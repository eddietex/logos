# Ashan

## Overview

### Three tribal lots — Joshua 19:1–23

Listed among Simeon’s towns within Judah’s inheritance (19:7); verse 9 explains the shared territory by Judah’s oversized portion. No new capture is narrated.

See [[The Lots of Simeon, Zebulun, and Issachar]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23; comparisons as cited.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the lowland (Joshua 15:42), one of the *nine cities with their villages* of the third lowland group (42–44). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Simeon's list in 19:7 also names Ashan; see [[Simeon's Lot Within Judah - Joshua 15 and 19]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
