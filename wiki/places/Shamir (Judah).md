# Shamir (Judah)

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the hill country (Joshua 15:48), one of the *eleven cities with their villages* of the first hill-country group (48–51). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.

**Qualified because Judges 10:1–2 has a second Shamir**, *in the hill country of Ephraim*, where
the judge Tola lived and was buried. Same name, *šāmîr*, same lemma (8069) in the WLC, but a
different hill country. The Judges town is [[Shamir (Ephraim)]]. Nothing in either text connects
the two. This page was renamed from `Shamir` in the ingest of [[Tola and Jair]].
