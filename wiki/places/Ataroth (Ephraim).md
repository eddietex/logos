# Ataroth (Ephraim)

## Overview

Ataroth occurs on the Josephite boundary by the Archites (2) and on the descent from Janoah toward Naarah (7). These mentions are collected here without asserting that both designate one site. Neither is identified with Gad’s eastern [[Ataroth]] or explicitly equated with [[Ataroth Addar]] in verse 5.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.
