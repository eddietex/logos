# En Hazor

## Overview

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (37), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). Its name shares an element with [[Hazor]] (36), listed separately just before it; they are not equated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
