# Mahaneh Dan

## Overview

*Maḥănēh-dān*, *the camp of Dan* (WLC). Named twice in Judges, in two locations.

**Judges 13:25** — *Yahweh's Spirit began to move him in Mahaneh Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.* The
place of Samson's first stirring by the Spirit, between the two towns at the head of Dan's border list
(Joshua 19:41).

**Judges 18:12** — the six hundred Danites on their way north *went up and encamped in Kiriath Jearim,
in Judah. Therefore they called that place Mahaneh Dan, to this day. Behold, it is behind Kiriath
Jearim.* This verse explains the name and places it *behind Kiriath Jearim*.

So the name appears in chapter 13 before chapter 18 tells how it arose, and the two verses locate it
differently. Whether they describe one site, two camps of the same name, or a narrator using the later
name in the earlier story, the text does not say. The wiki records both and chooses neither. No modern
site is assigned.
