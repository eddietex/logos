# The Tower of Shechem

## Overview

*When all the men of the tower of Shechem heard of it, they entered into the stronghold of the house of
Elberith* (Judges 9:46). *Of it* is the destruction of the city the day before (9:45). *Migdal šəkem*
(WLC), *the tower of Shechem*, is named three times (9:46, 47, 49), and its people are distinct from the
city's. Its men are *baʿălê migdal-šəkem*, *the lords of the tower of Shechem*, as the city's were *the
lords of Shechem* (9:2). They shelter in *the stronghold*, *ṣərîaḥ* (only 9:46, 49 in Genesis–Judges), of
*the house of El Berith*, *God of the covenant*. On the god's name, see [[Baal Berith]].

Abimelech cuts boughs on [[Mount Zalmon]], his men copy him, and *they put them at the base of the
stronghold, and set the stronghold on fire on them; so that all the people of the tower of Shechem died
also, about a thousand men and women* (9:48–49). It is the first half of Jotham's curse made literal:
*let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the men of Shechem* (9:20).

Some identify the tower with *the house of Millo* (9:6, 20) (reported, not checked). The text does not.
See [[Beth Millo (Shechem)]]. The wiki keeps the two pages apart.

Gideon had *broken down the tower of Penuel, and killed the men of the city* (8:17). His son burns this
tower with its people inside. See [[Penuel and Succoth - Jacob's Road and Gideon's Pursuit]].

See [[The Fall of Abimelech]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 8:17; 9:2, 6, 20, 45–49; Hebrew WLC
