# Pethor

## Overview

### Balaam's home recalled — Deuteronomy 23:4

*"Balaam the son of Beor from Pethor of Mesopotamia"* (4). Deuteronomy places Pethor in *ʾăram
nahărayim* (WLC 23:5), where Numbers 22:5 had *"by the River"*. See [[Aram Naharaim]].

See [[The Assembly and the Holy Camp]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 23:1–14; Numbers 22:5.

Balaam’s location, by the River in the land of his people (5). The WEB capitalizes River but does
not name it in this verse. No precise modern site or river identification is asserted here. The
embassies lodge with Balaam before his departure.
