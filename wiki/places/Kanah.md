# Kanah

## Overview

Named in the run of names leading *even to great Sidon* (28), in Asher’s register, whose stated total is twenty-two cities with their villages (30–31). Distinct from [[The Brook of Kanah]] on the Ephraim–Manasseh line (16:8; 17:9): the register gives no reason to identify them.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
