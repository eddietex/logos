# Jerusalem

## Overview

The city named in the heading of [[Ecclesiastes]] as the location of the Preacher's kingship.
In [[Vanity of Vanities]], the city gives the speaker a royal setting; no journey, building, temple
ceremony, or event in the city's political history is narrated. In [[The Burden of Wisdom]] the
first-person Preacher is king over Israel in Jerusalem (1:12) and measures his wisdom against all
who were before him there (1:16), which implies predecessors in the city without naming them.

[[Salem]] retains its page for the Genesis 14 name and its identification history. The new
page records the explicit Jerusalem name in Ecclesiastes 1:1. Ecclesiastes does not itself
identify Salem with Jerusalem, so the two textual occurrences are not collapsed.

Source: stored WEB Ecclesiastes 1:1–18 and Genesis 14:18; the earlier identification discussion
and its sources remain on [[Salem]].

## Appears in

- [[Vanity of Vanities]] — Ecclesiastes 1:1; city of the kingship named in the heading.
- [[The Burden of Wisdom]] — Ecclesiastes 1:12, 16; seat of the kingship and measure of the Preacher's wisdom.

## Events here

- The heading locates [[The Preacher (Ecclesiastes)]]'s kingship here (Ecclesiastes 1:1).
  The opening poem supplies observations about all human life, not a scene set in a named street.
- The Preacher reigns here over Israel and surpasses in wisdom all who were before him in the city
  (Ecclesiastes 1:12, 16).
