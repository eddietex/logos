# Ashtaroth

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Og’s royal city (12), assigned with Edrei and half Gilead to half the children of Machir (31).

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### The Gibeonite deception — Joshua 9:1–27

The envoys locate Og here in their recollection of the two Amorite kings (10). No new battle or
visit at Ashtaroth is narrated.

See [[The Gibeonite Deception]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27; comparisons as cited.


Named as Og’s residence in the WEB introduction (4), with Edrei also named. Numbers 21:33 identifies
Edrei as the battle site. No identification with another similarly named site is imposed here.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** One of Og's two residences, with Edrei (12:4). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].

**The name and the goddess — Judges 2:13.** *ʿaštārôt* is also the plural of the goddess's name:
Israel *served Baal and the Ashtaroth* (stored WEB; WLC). Judges 2 means the goddesses, not this city,
and they have their own page, [[Ashtoreth (deity)]]. Whether the city was named for the goddess is
not said in the text.
