# Ashtaroth

## Overview

Named as Og’s residence in the WEB introduction (4), with Edrei also named. Numbers 21:33 identifies
Edrei as the battle site. No identification with another similarly named site is imposed here.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.
