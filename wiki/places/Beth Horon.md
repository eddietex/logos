# Beth Horon

## Overview

The ascent lies on the route of pursuit from Gibeon (10); the descent is the setting of the hail
that strikes the fleeing army toward Azekah (11). No distinction between upper and lower settlements
is made here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No modern identification is assigned.
