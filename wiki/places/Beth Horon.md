# Beth Horon

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

The mountain south of lower Beth Horon locates Ataroth Addar (13) and the turn of the western border (14). Upper and lower Beth Horon remain distinguished.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

### Ephraim’s inheritance — Joshua 16:1–10

Lower Beth Horon is named on the Josephite boundary (3), upper Beth Horon on Ephraim’s boundary (5). These are distinguished locations within the shared Beth Horon page; the earlier pursuit account did not specify upper and lower.

See [[The Inheritance of Ephraim]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.

The ascent lies on the route of pursuit from Gibeon (10); the descent is the setting of the hail
that strikes the fleeing army toward Azekah (11). No distinction between upper and lower settlements
is made here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No modern identification is assigned.
