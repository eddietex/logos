# Megiddo

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:21, paired with Taanach. No battle is narrated. Joshua 17:11–12 places it among the towns whose inhabitants Manasseh *couldn't drive out*, and Judges 1:27 (WEB checked, not yet ingested) repeats it. See [[Kings Struck and Cities Not Driven Out - Joshua 12, 15-17, and Judges 1]]. No later history or modern site is asserted here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited; Judges 1:27 checked at bible-api.com (`translation=web`). No modern site is assigned.
