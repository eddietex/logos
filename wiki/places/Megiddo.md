# Megiddo

## Overview

### Not driven out — Judges 1:27

Last of Manasseh's five cities not driven out (27), as in Joshua 17:11–12; its king is counted in Joshua 12:21.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

Named among Manasseh’s allotted cities with dependent towns (11), whose inhabitants are not driven
out (12). The later imposition of forced labor (13) qualifies any inference that the defeat of its
king in Joshua 12 meant complete occupation.

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:21, paired with Taanach. No battle is narrated. Joshua 17:11–12 places it among the towns whose inhabitants Manasseh *couldn't drive out*, and Judges 1:27 (WEB checked, not yet ingested) repeats it. See [[Kings Struck and Cities Not Driven Out - Joshua 12, 15-17, and Judges 1]]. No later history or modern site is asserted here.
