# Buz

## Overview

The clan or district of Elihu, *"the Buzite"* (Job 32:2, 6). Like the friends' homelands — [[Teman]],
[[Shuah]], [[Naamah]] — it is named in Job only through a gentilic attached to one man, and the book
never describes it.

Two other verses supply its context. **Genesis 22:21** makes Buz a son of Nahor, Abraham's brother:
*"Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother"* — so Job's land and Elihu's clan stand side by side at the head of
Nahor's twelve, which [[Nahor's Line and the Burial of Sarah]] reads as a map of Aramean and north
Arabian peoples rather than a family. And **Jeremiah 25:23** puts Buz in a list of peoples who must drink
the cup of wrath: *"Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who have the corners of their beard cut off"* — beside
[[Tema]], the oasis of Job 6:19. Together they place Buz somewhere in the north Arabian desert, with no
closer location known.

The pairing with [[Uz]] is the point worth keeping. The book that begins *"There was a man in the land of
Uz"* (1:1) brings in its last human speaker from Uz's brother-clan, and he is the only speaker given a
genealogy. Whether the author meant the echo is unrecoverable; it is in the text either way.

**A collision to watch.** Buz the son of Nahor is a person in Genesis 22:21 and has no page. If one is
ever made, `AGENTS.md`'s person/place rule applies and both pages take qualifiers, as noted at [[Uz]] and
[[Shuah]].
