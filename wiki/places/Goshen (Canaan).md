# Goshen (Canaan)

## Overview

### The whole land taken — Joshua 11:16–23

The land of Goshen is named again in the summary of the regions Joshua took (16), with the hill country, the South, the lowland, and the Arabah. As in 10:41, it is the Canaan Goshen, not the Egyptian [[Goshen]].

See [[The Whole Land Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23.

The country of Goshen appears in the southern campaign’s geographical summary, extending to Gibeon (41). This Canaan setting is distinguished from the Egyptian Goshen of the Joseph narrative, [[Goshen]]. Its precise borders are not supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43. No modern identification or dynasty is assigned.
