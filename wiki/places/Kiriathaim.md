# Kiriathaim

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Listed in Reuben’s portion (19), corresponding to its building by Reuben in Numbers 32:37.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

Built by the children of Reuben (Numbers 32:37). The name means *two cities*. Genesis 14:5 has
Chedorlaomer strike *the Emim in Shaveh Kiriathaim* (stored WEB), probably the plain by the same town,
though Genesis does not say so. Joshua 13:19 lists it in Reuben's border (WEB via bible-api.com, now ingested).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
