# The Valley of Jezreel

## Overview

Valley whose Canaanite inhabitants possess iron chariots in the children of Joseph’s report
(Joshua 17:16). It is named alongside Beth Shean and its towns as an obstacle to possession.
This valley is distinct from [[Jezreel (Judah)]], the town in Joshua 15:56; no modern boundary
is asserted here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:14–18.
