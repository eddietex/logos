# The Valley of Jezreel

## Overview

### Midian's camp — Judges 6:33

*All the Midianites and the Amalekites and the children of the east assembled themselves together;
and they passed over, and encamped in the valley of Jezreel* (6:33). The Hebrew is *bəʿēmeq yizrəʿeʾl*
(WLC), *ʿēmeq*, a valley or broad lowland. The valley Joshua 17:16 described as held by Canaanites with
iron chariots is now the eastern raiders' camp. Judges 7:1 places *Midian's camp … by the hill of
Moreh, in the valley*, north of Gideon's camp at the spring of Harod (stored WEB; not yet ingested).
See [[The Fleece]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 6:33–40; 7:1; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### The children of Joseph's report — Joshua 17:16

Valley whose Canaanite inhabitants possess iron chariots in the children of Joseph’s report
(Joshua 17:16). It is named alongside Beth Shean and its towns as an obstacle to possession.
This valley is distinct from [[Jezreel (Judah)]], the town in Joshua 15:56; no modern boundary
is asserted here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:14–18.
