# Nobah (town)

## Overview

*Gideon went up by the way of those who lived in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah* (Judges 8:11).
A town east of the Jordan, named with Jogbehah as a landmark on Gideon's road to Karkor. *Lənōbaḥ*
(5025, WLC).

Numbers 32:42 says that *Nobah went and took Kenath, and its villages, and called it Nobah, after his own
name*. Judges 8:11 does not say whether its Nobah is that town, and the wiki keeps the two pages apart:
the renamed town is filed at [[Kenath]], and the man at [[Nobah (person)]]. This page is qualified
because the man's page already holds the bare name.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32:42; Judges 8:11; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
