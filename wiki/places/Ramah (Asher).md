# Ramah (Asher)

## Overview

Asher’s border *turned to Ramah, to the fortified city of Tyre* (29). Distinct from [[Ramah]] in Benjamin (18:25), [[Ramah (Naphtali)]] (19:36), and Ramah of the South (19:8, on [[Baalath Beer]]).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
