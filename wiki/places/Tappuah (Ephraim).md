# Tappuah (Ephraim)

## Overview

Starting landmark for a westward boundary segment toward the brook of Kanah and the sea (8). Kept distinct from [[Tappuah (lowland)]] in Judah and from the unidentified royal [[Tappuah]] of Joshua 12:17; no identity with that royal city is asserted.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.
