# Tappuah (Ephraim)

## Overview

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

The surrounding land belongs to Manasseh, but the border town belongs to Ephraim (8). The verse
distinguishes land from town rather than assigning the same holding exclusively to both.

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

Starting landmark for a westward boundary segment toward the brook of Kanah and the sea (8). Kept distinct from [[Tappuah (lowland)]] in Judah and from the unidentified royal [[Tappuah]] of Joshua 12:17; no identity with that royal city is asserted.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.
