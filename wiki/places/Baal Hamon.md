# Baal Hamon

## Overview

The location of [[Solomon]]'s vineyard in Song of Solomon 8:11. The vineyard is
leased to keepers, each owing a thousand shekels of silver for its fruit. The poem
provides no route, neighboring settlement, or other means of locating the site.
No modern identification or etymology is asserted. The name does not by itself
establish a cultic association in this passage. Source: stored WEB Song 8:11–12.
