# Baal Gad

## Overview

The northern limit of Joshua's conquest summary: *Baal Gad in the valley of Lebanon under Mount Hermon* (11:17). Joshua 12:7 repeats it, and 13:5 names *Baal Gad under Mount Hermon* as the start of a stretch still remaining toward the entrance of Hamath. The same place is thus the edge of what was taken and the edge of what remains. The name contains *Baal*, but the verses say nothing about a sanctuary there.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23. No modern identification is assigned.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** The northern limit of the western list, *Baal Gad in the valley of Lebanon* (12:7), repeating 11:17's pair in reverse order. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
