# Nob

## Overview

A sanctuary town where [[Ahimelech (son of Ahitub)]] and his priestly house minister *before Yahweh*, entered in the
narrative for the first time at 1 Samuel 21:1. Distinct from [[Nobah (town)]] east of the Jordan (Numbers 32:42; Judges
8:11). No precise modern site is assigned here; later books place Nob near Jerusalem in Benjamin's territory on an
Assyrian approach route (Isaiah 10:32; Nehemiah 11:32, recalled, not checked).

### Sanctuary of the show bread and the ephod — 1 Samuel 21:1–9

Fleeing Saul's court after parting from [[Jonathan (son of Saul)]], [[David]] comes *to Nob to Ahimelech the priest*
(21:1). Three details in 21:1–9 show that Nob functions as Yahweh's active sanctuary at this stage of Saul's reign:

- **The table of show bread.** Ahimelech has *no common bread*, only *the show bread that was taken from before Yahweh,
  to put hot bread in the day when it was taken away* (21:4, 6) — the weekly Sabbath service commanded for the Tent of
  Meeting in [[The Ark, the Table, and the Lampstand]] (Exodus 25:30) and [[The Lamps and the Bread Before Yahweh]]
  (Leviticus 24:5–9).
- **Standing *before Yahweh*.** [[Doeg]] the Edomite is *there that day, detained before Yahweh* (21:7), sanctuary
  language matching the presence of Yahweh's house.
- **The ephod and Goliath's sword.** *The sword of Goliath the Philistine* is kept *wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod*
  (21:9), treating the sanctuary at Nob as a sacred repository for the trophy of Elah.

**An unexplained shift from Shiloh.** Earlier in 1 Samuel, Yahweh's house and Eli's priesthood stood at [[Shiloh]] (1:3,
9; 3:3), until the ark was taken by the Philistines at Aphek (4:3–11) and lodged in Abinadab's house at
[[Kiriath Jearim]] (7:1–2). 1 Samuel never narrates Shiloh's destruction or explains when the priestly establishment of
Eli's descendants — with the table of show bread and the ephod, but without the ark — moved to Nob.

In the next chapter (22:11, 18–19, stored WEB; not yet ingested), Saul summons *the priests who were in Nob*, and after
Doeg kills eighty-five men wearing a linen ephod, *he struck Nob, the city of the priests, with the edge of the sword,
both men and women, children and nursing babies, and cattle and donkeys and sheep*.

See [[David at Nob]], [[The Bread of the Presence - David's Hunger and Matthew 12]], and
[[The Word Against the House of Eli - 1 Samuel 2.27-36 and 1 Kings 2.27]]. Source: stored WEB Exodus 25:30; Leviticus
24:5–9; 1 Samuel 1:3, 9; 4:3–11; 7:1–2; 21:1–9; 22:11, 18–19.
