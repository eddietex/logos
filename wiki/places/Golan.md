# Golan

## Overview

Golan is the city in Bashan set apart for the Manassites east of the Jordan
(Deuteronomy 4:43). It offers refuge to the unwitting killer without prior hatred
(4:42). Its precise modern site is not established by this notice.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:41–43.
