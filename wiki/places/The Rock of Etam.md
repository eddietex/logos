# The Rock of Etam

## Overview

### Samson's refuge — Judges 15:8–13

After avenging his wife, [[Samson]] *went down and lived in the cave in Etam's rock* (Judges 15:8). The
Philistines invade Judah to take him, and *three thousand men of Judah went down to the cave in Etam's
rock* to bind him and hand him over (11). They tie him with two new ropes *and brought him up from the
rock* (13).

The Hebrew is *səʿîp selaʿ ʿêṭām* (8, 11, WLC), *the cleft of the rock of Etam*. *Səʿîp* (lemma 5585)
occurs only in these two verses in the WLC files of Genesis through 2 Samuel checked; the WEB's *cave*
interprets it. Samson *went down* to it and is *brought up* from it, and the men of Judah *went down*
to it; the verbs place it below them, and say no more. Chronicles names an Etam among Judah's clans (*the
father of Etam*, 1 Chronicles 4:3) and among towns built in Judah (*He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa*,
2 Chronicles 11:6; both WEB at bible-api.com, not stored). Whether the rock is near that town Judges does
not say, and the site is not identified here.

A Danite hides in Judah, and Judah gives him up. See [[Samson Against the Philistines at Lehi]] and
[[Bound to Be Handed Over - The Men of Judah and Delilah]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 15:8–13;
1 Chronicles 4:3 and 2 Chronicles 11:6 checked at bible-api.com (WEB), 2026-11-16; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.
