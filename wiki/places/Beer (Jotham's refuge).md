# Beer (Jotham's refuge)

## Overview

### Jotham's flight — Judges 9:21

*Jotham ran away, and fled, and went to Beer, and lived there, for fear of Abimelech his brother*
(9:21). After his fable and curse on Gerizim, Gideon's youngest son leaves Shechem's country for a town
the text does not locate. *Bəʾēr* means *well*, and many places took the name. Nothing in Judges says
which one this is, or whether it lay outside Abimelech's reach. It is simply where Jotham *lived*.
He is not seen again.

The page is qualified by the person it appears with, as `AGENTS.md` does for unnamed figures, because
the wiki already has the well of Numbers 21:16, now [[Beer (well in Moab)]]. The wiki does not
identify the two. Later identifications of Jotham's Beer with a known site are not followed here.

See [[Jotham's Fable]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 9:21; Hebrew *bəʾērâ*, *to Beer*, from the WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
