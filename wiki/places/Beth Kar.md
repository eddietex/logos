# Beth Kar

## Overview

### The end of the pursuit — 1 Samuel 7:11

*The men of Israel went out of Mizpah, and pursued the Philistines, and struck them, until they came under
Beth Kar* (7:11). The WLC reads *ʿaḏ-mittaḥaṯ ləḇêṯ kār*, *as far as below Beth Kar*. The name occurs only
here in the WLC files of Genesis through 2 Kings, Ruth, and Psalms (lemma 1033), and nothing else is said of
it. It lay somewhere on the Philistines' line of retreat from [[Mizpeh]] toward the lowland; no site is
assigned here. See [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]].
