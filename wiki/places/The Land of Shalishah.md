# The Land of Shalishah

## Overview

### The second district of the search — 1 Samuel 9:4

Saul and his servant pass *through the hill country of Ephraim, and … through the land of Shalishah, but they
didn't find them* (9:4). *Šālišâ* (lemma 8031) occurs only here in the WLC files searched (Genesis through 2
Samuel, Ruth). The order of the route puts it near the hill country of Ephraim; its location is not known with
confidence. A connection with Baal Shalishah of 2 Kings 4:42 is sometimes proposed (recalled, not verified; 2 Kings
not ingested). See [[Saul Comes to the Seer]].
