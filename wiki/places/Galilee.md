# Galilee

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Kedesh, the Naphtali refuge, is located here (32).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

Joshua 20:7 locates Kedesh in Galilee, in the hill country of Naphtali, when setting it
apart as a city of refuge. This notice supplies a regional location, not a boundary
survey or a modern site identification.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 20:7.
