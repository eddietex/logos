# The Oak of Tabor

## Overview

### The second sign — 1 Samuel 10:3–4

*Then you will go on forward from there, and you will come to the oak of Tabor. Three men will meet you there going
up to God to Bethel* (10:3). They will be carrying three young goats, three loaves, and a skin of wine, and they
will give Saul two of the loaves (10:3–4). *ʾÊlôn tāḇôr* (WLC). *ʾÊlôn* is the word for the oak of Moreh at Shechem
(Genesis 12:6), the oaks of Mamre (Genesis 13:18; 18:1), and the oak of Meonenim (Judges 9:37) (lemma 436, WLC).
The tree is on the road toward Bethel, which puts it in or near Benjamin's hill country. It is kept apart here
from [[Tabor]], the mountain in Issachar of Joshua 19:22 and Judges 4, which is far to the north, because the text
does not connect them. Proposals to link this oak with the oak below Bethel where Rebekah's nurse Deborah was buried
(Genesis 35:8), or with Deborah's palm (Judges 4:5), are recalled, not checked, and are not adopted.
See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].
