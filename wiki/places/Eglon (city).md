# Eglon (city)

## Overview

The Canaanite city of Joshua 10, 12, and 15. Not to be confused with [[Eglon (person)]], the king of Moab
in Judges 3:12–30, who shares its name and, in the OpenScriptures lemma data, its lemma entry (5700).
This page was renamed from *Eglon* when the king arrived, per the person/place collision rule.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Listed in the second lowland group (39), beside Lachish. Captured in 10:34–35; now allotted to Judah.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### The southern campaign — Joshua 10:28–43

Israel captures and destroys the city on the day of its attack (34–35). Its former king Debir has already died at Makkedah; the city’s capture is a separate action.

See [[The Southern Cities Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43; comparisons as cited.

### The five kings at Makkedah — Joshua 10:16–27

Its king, [[Debir (person)]], is brought from the cave and killed at Makkedah (23–27). The city is named to identify its ruler; the king’s death is not itself a report that the city has been captured.

See [[The Five Kings in the Cave at Makkedah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:16–27.
