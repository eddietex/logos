# The Oak in Zaanannim

## Overview

Naphtali’s border runs *from Heleph, from the oak in Zaanannim* (33). A tree named as a boundary marker, like other named trees in the patriarchal narratives; nothing happens at it here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
