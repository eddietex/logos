# The Oak in Zaanannim

## Overview

### Jael's tent — Judges 4:17–22

Sisera flees *to the tent of Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite* (4:17). Verses 17–24 do not name a
place; the tent is the one 4:11 put *as far as the oak in Zaanannim, which is by Kedesh*, so the wiki
files Sisera's death here. That location rests on 4:11, not on 17–24. See [[Jael and Sisera]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:11, 17–24.

### Heber's tent — Judges 4:11

*Heber the Kenite … had pitched his tent as far as the oak in Zaanannim, which is by Kedesh* (4:11).
The Hebrew phrase is the same as in Joshua 19:33, *ʾēlôn bəṣaʿănannîm* (WLC), so the boundary marker of
Naphtali's lot is now a place where someone lives. The tent becomes the scene of Sisera's death in the
next pericope (4:17–21). See [[Heber the Kenite]] and [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Joshua 19:33; Hebrew WLC.

Naphtali’s border runs *from Heleph, from the oak in Zaanannim* (33). A tree named as a boundary marker, like other named trees in the patriarchal narratives; nothing happens at it in Joshua.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
