# Beth Haran

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

The WEB spelling Beth Haram occurs among Gad’s valley towns (27). The existing identification with Numbers 32:36’s Beth Haran rests on the paired Beth Nimrah and similar name; Joshua does not explicitly equate the spellings.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

The last of the eight towns the children of Gad build, *fortified cities, and folds for sheep* (Numbers
32:36). Joshua 13:27 lists a *Beth Haram* in Gad's valley portion beside Beth Nimrah (WEB via
bible-api.com, not yet ingested); the wiki takes them as the same town on the spelling and the pairing.
Unconnected with Haran in Mesopotamia.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
