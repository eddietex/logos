# Jezreel

## Overview

Named in Issachar’s border and city register (18–23), totaling sixteen cities with their villages and ending at the Jordan. No individual capture is narrated. Distinguished from Jezreel (Judah) in Joshua 15:56; the town is not the same page as The Valley of Jezreel.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23. No precise modern site is assigned.
