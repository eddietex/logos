# Seirah

## Overview

### Ehud's escape — Judges 3:26–27

*Ehud escaped while they waited, passed beyond the stone idols, and escaped to Seirah. When he had
come, he blew a trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim* (26–27, stored WEB). That is all the text
says. Seirah is beyond *the stone idols that were by Gilgal*, and the next verse puts Ehud in
[[The Hill Country of Ephraim]], so it lies somewhere between the Jordan valley and the hills. No more exact
location is given, and none is adopted here.

**The name.** *Haśśəʿîrātâ*, *toward the Seirah* (WLC), with the article and the ending of direction.
It occurs nowhere else in the WLC files for Genesis through 1 Samuel. *Śəʿîrâ* looks like a form of
*śāʿîr*, *hairy* or *goat*, which is also the root of *Seir*; whether it means a wooded or shaggy place
is not checked. Nothing in the text links it to [[Mount Seir (Judah border)]] or to the Seir of Edom.

See [[Ehud and Eglon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:12–30; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-13.
