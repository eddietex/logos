# The City of Salt

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the wilderness (Joshua 15:62), one of the *six cities with their villages* listed *in the wilderness* (61–62). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

The WEB renders the name as a description. The verse does not connect it with [[The Salt Sea]], and the wiki does not.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
