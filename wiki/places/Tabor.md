# Tabor

## Overview

### Mount Tabor — Judges 4:6–14

Judges calls it *Mount Tabor* (*har tābôr*, WLC; 4:6, 12, 14). Yahweh's command sends Barak there
with ten thousand of Naphtali and Zebulun; Sisera hears that Barak *was gone up to Mount Tabor* and
brings his chariots to the Kishon; and at Deborah's word *Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten
thousand men after him* (4:14). So the *Tabor* of Joshua 19:22 has a mountain of the name in the same
region; the wiki files both here without asserting they are the same point. See [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Hebrew WLC.

**Aznoth Tabor.** Naphtali’s border turns westward to [[Aznoth Tabor]] (19:34); the shared element is not explained.

Named in Issachar’s border and city register (18–23), totaling sixteen cities with their villages and ending at the Jordan. No individual capture is narrated. Joshua 19 does not identify the place as a mountain or equate it with Chisloth Tabor.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:1–23. No precise modern site is assigned.
