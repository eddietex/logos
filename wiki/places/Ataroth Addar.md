# Ataroth Addar

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

The north boundary descends here by the mountain south of lower Beth Horon (13). Joshua 16:5 names it with upper Beth Horon; these are related boundary descriptions, not an equation of the two Beth Horons.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

Eastern boundary landmark of Ephraim, named with upper Beth Horon (5). The verse does not explicitly identify it with the shorter Ataroth names in verses 2 and 7.
