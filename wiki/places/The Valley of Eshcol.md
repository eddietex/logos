# The Valley of Eshcol

## Overview

The valley near Hebron where the spies cut one cluster of grapes and carried it on a staff between
two men (Numbers 13:23). *Naḥal* is a wadi or stream valley, and *ʾeškôl* is a cluster. Verse 24
gives the etymology: *That place was called the valley of Eshcol, because of the cluster which the
children of Israel cut down from there.* Rahlfs translates the name, *Φάραγξ βότρυος*.

**The name, and an older Eshcol.** [[Eshcol]] the Amorite lived by Mamre at Genesis 14:13, in the
same district. The wiki keeps the coincidence as a coincidence: Numbers explains the valley by the
fruit, and the tags give the man and the word separate numbers (812, 811).

**It stands for the whole search.** Numbers 32:9 and Deuteronomy 1:24 name this valley as the place
they reached, and nothing beyond it. That sits beside Numbers 13:21, which goes to Rehob. *Exactly
where it is* is not recoverable from the text beyond the order of verses 22–23, Hebron first and
then the valley.
