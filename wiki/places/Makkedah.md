# Makkedah

## Overview

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

The last name of the second lowland group (41). The cave of the five kings and the city's capture (10:16–28) lie behind this entry; the list does not recall them.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### The southern campaign — Joshua 10:28–43

The city and its king are destroyed on the day of the five kings’ execution (28). The city’s own king is distinct from the five rulers imprisoned in its cave.

See [[The Southern Cities Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43; comparisons as cited.

### The five kings at Makkedah — Joshua 10:16–27

The cave at Makkedah becomes first a hiding place, then a guarded prison, and finally the burial
place of the five kings (16–27). Israel’s army returns to Joshua’s camp here in peace (21). The
capture of Makkedah itself is narrated separately in 10:28.

See [[The Five Kings in the Cave at Makkedah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:16–27.
