# The Arnon

## Overview

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

The valley and its middle mark the southern boundary in the land granted to Reuben and Gad (12, 16).

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

### Og’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

Its valley marks the southern endpoint of the combined territory taken from the two Amorite kings,
with Hermon at the other end (8).

See [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.

### Sihon’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 2:24–37

Crossing its valley introduces the command to fight Sihon (24). Aroer on its edge and an unnamed
city within the valley mark the southern end of the victories described in verse 36.

See [[The Defeat of Sihon Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:24–37.

### The first two oracles — Numbers 22:36–23:26

The City of Moab, where Balak meets Balaam, stands at the Arnon border (22:36). This boundary notice
does not itself identify that city with Ar.

See [[Balaam's First Two Oracles]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 22:36–23:26.


### Sihon and Og — Numbers 21:21–35

Southern limit of the territory Israel takes from Sihon (24), also the limit of Sihon’s earlier
conquest from Moab (26). Its high places occur in the quoted poem (28).

See [[Sihon and Og]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.


Boundary between Moab and the Amorites, with Israel camping on its other side (13). Its valleys also
occur in the cited Book of the Wars of Yahweh (14–15). The viewpoint of other side is not fixed by a
modern map here.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:10–20.
