# Jotbathah

## Overview

### Renewed tablets and onward journey — Deuteronomy 10:1–11

Deuteronomy 10:7 describes it as a land of brooks of water, reached from Gudgodah. Numbers 33:33
reaches Jotbathah from Hor Haggidgad. The names invite comparison but do not establish a modern
site.

See [[New Tablets and the Journey Onward]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 10:1–11.

The itinerary places this stage after [[Hor Haggidgad]] and before [[Abronah]].

Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49. No modern site is assigned.
