# Elealeh

## Overview

Named in the request of Reuben and Gad (Numbers 32:3) and built by the children of Reuben, next to
Heshbon (32:37). Its name is spelled *ʾelʿālēh* in 32:3 and *ʾelʿālēʾ* in 32:37 (Miqra according to the
Masorah).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
