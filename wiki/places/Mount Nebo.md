# Mount Nebo

## Overview

Mount Nebo is the mountain of Abarim in Moab opposite Jericho that Moses is commanded to
ascend, to see Canaan and die (Deuteronomy 32:49–50). Deuteronomy 34 narrates the ascent and death. No modern coordinates are assigned.

The mountain is distinct from [[Nebo (city)]]. [[Nebo (landmark)]] retains the unqualified
reference in Numbers 33:47 without deciding whether it names the city or mountain.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 32:48–52.


### The death of Moses — Deuteronomy 34:1–12

The command of 32:49–50 is fulfilled: Moses goes up to Mount Nebo, *to the top of Pisgah*, is shown the land, and dies (1–5). The verse joins the two names without explaining how they relate.

See [[The Death of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 34:1–12.
