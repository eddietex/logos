# Aroer

## Overview

### Sihon’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 2:24–37

Verse 36 specifies the Aroer at the edge of the Arnon valley. It is distinguished from the unnamed
city in the valley; the account runs from here to Gilead. This specificity does not resolve which
Aroer Gad builds in Numbers 32:34.

See [[The Defeat of Sihon Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:24–37.

One of the towns the children of Gad build (Numbers 32:34). Joshua 13 knows two Aroers east of the
Jordan: one *on the edge of the valley of the Arnon*, in Reuben's border (13:16), and *Aroer that is near
Rabbah*, in Gad's (13:25); Deuteronomy 3:12 starts the land given to Reuben and Gad *from Aroer, which is by
the valley of the Arnon* (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet ingested). Numbers 32 does not say which Aroer Gad
built. If it is the Arnon town, Numbers has Gad building in what Joshua assigns to Reuben; the wiki records
that tension on [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]] and does not resolve it. This page covers
the Transjordan Aroer(s) until the text requires separate pages.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
