# Aroer

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Two bearings distinguish the Arnon-valley Aroer in Reuben’s portion (9, 16) from Aroer near Rabbah in Gad’s (25). Numbers 32:34 does not specify which Gad built; this page retains both notices without collapsing their sites.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

The town at the edge of the Arnon valley is the southern endpoint of the summary reaching to Hermon (48). This explicit location still does not settle which Aroer Gad builds in Numbers 32:34.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

The Arnon-valley Aroer anchors the eastern grant to Reuben and Gad (12). The joint regional grant
does not settle the existing question of which Aroer Gad builds in Numbers 32:34.

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

### Sihon’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 2:24–37

Verse 36 specifies the Aroer at the edge of the Arnon valley. It is distinguished from the unnamed
city in the valley; the account runs from here to Gilead. This specificity does not resolve which
Aroer Gad builds in Numbers 32:34.

See [[The Defeat of Sihon Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:24–37.

One of the towns the children of Gad build (Numbers 32:34). Joshua 13 knows two Aroers east of the
Jordan: one *on the edge of the valley of the Arnon*, in Reuben's border (13:16), and *Aroer that is near
Rabbah*, in Gad's (13:25); Deuteronomy 3:12 starts the land given to Reuben and Gad *from Aroer, which is by
the valley of the Arnon* (stored WEB; now ingested). Numbers 32 does not say which Aroer Gad
built. If it is the Arnon town, Numbers has Gad building in what Joshua assigns to Reuben; the wiki records
that tension on [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]] and does not resolve it. This page covers
the Transjordan Aroer(s) until the text requires separate pages.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** The southern edge of Sihon's rule, *on the edge of the valley of the Arnon* (12:2); Joshua 13:9 repeats the description. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
