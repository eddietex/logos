# Lachish

## Overview

### The southern campaign — Joshua 10:28–43

Israel camps against the city and takes it on the second day (31–32). Horam of Gezer attempts relief and is defeated (33); this passage does not name another king of Lachish.

See [[The Southern Cities Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43; comparisons as cited.

### The five kings at Makkedah — Joshua 10:16–27

Its king, [[Japhia]], is brought from the cave and killed at Makkedah (23–27). The city is named to identify its ruler; the king’s death is not itself a report that the city has been captured.

See [[The Five Kings in the Cave at Makkedah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:16–27.
