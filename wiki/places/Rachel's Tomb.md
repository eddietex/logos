# Rachel's Tomb

## Overview

### The first sign — 1 Samuel 10:2

After the anointing, Samuel tells Saul, *When you have departed from me today, then you will find two men by Rachel's
tomb, on the border of Benjamin at Zelzah* (10:2). They will tell him the donkeys have been found, and that his
father is now worried about him. *Qəḇurat rāḥēl* (WLC) is the phrase Genesis uses for the pillar Jacob set up,
*maṣṣeḇeṯ qəḇurat-rāḥēl*, *the Pillar of Rachel's grave to this day* (Genesis 35:20, WLC; stored WEB). The first
of Saul's three signs comes at the grave of Benjamin's mother, to a Benjamite who has just called his tribe *the
smallest of the tribes of Israel* (9:21). See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].

**Recorded, not resolved — where it is.** Genesis says Rachel *was buried on the way to Ephrath (also called
Bethlehem)* (35:19, stored WEB). That is in Judah, south of Jerusalem. 1 Samuel 10:2 places the tomb *on the border
of Benjamin*, north of it. The disagreement and the proposed ways of reading it are set out in
[[Rachel Weeping - Her Grave, Ramah, and Bethlehem]]. The wiki adopts none of them. The traditional site on the
Bethlehem road follows Genesis. That is general knowledge, not checked here.

Source: stored WEB Genesis 35:19–20; 1 Samuel 9:21; 10:2. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
