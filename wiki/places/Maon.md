# Maon

## Overview

### A people called Maon — Judges 10:12

Yahweh names *the Maonites*, Hebrew *ûmāʿôn* (WLC), among the peoples he saved Israel from (12). The lemma (4584) is the same as this town's (Joshua 15:55, WLC). The text does not connect the two. See [[The Maonites]].

See [[Israel's Repentance Under Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 10:6–18; Joshua 15:55; Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the hill country (Joshua 15:55), one of the *ten cities with their villages* of the third hill-country group (55–57). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
