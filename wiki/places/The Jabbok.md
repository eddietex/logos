# The Jabbok

## Overview

The river that splits Gilead in two, running west from the Transjordanian highlands into the Jordan
about midway between the Sea of Galilee and the Dead Sea — the modern Zarqa. It is a border in every
later text that mentions it: Numbers 21:24 and Deuteronomy 2:37 make it the line between Ammon and the
Amorite kingdom Israel takes from Sihon, and Joshua 12:2 repeats the boundary. **In Genesis it is not
yet a political border, only a ford one man crosses alone at night**, after sending his entire
household and everything he owns over ahead of him (32:22–23). ***Yabbōq*** and ***yēʾābēq***, *he
wrestled* (32:24), sit one letter apart in Hebrew — a wordplay Genesis lets stand without commenting
on it, in the same way it lets *gilʿād* and *galʿēd* stand one chapter earlier.
