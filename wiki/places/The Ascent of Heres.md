# The Ascent of Heres

## Overview

*Gideon the son of Joash returned from the battle from the ascent of Heres* (Judges 8:13). The way back
from the battle at Karkor toward Succoth. *Maʿălēh*, *ascent*, is a road up a slope or a pass;
*heḥāres* has the article (WLC). The OpenScriptures lemma data file it as 2775a, and the *Mount Heres*
of Judges 1:35 as 2776; the consonants are the same. The wiki does not identify the two, which are far
apart in the narrative's geography. The site is not identified here.

Source: stored WEB Judges 1:35; 8:13; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
