# Moserah

## Overview

### Commanded to ascend Nebo — Deuteronomy 32:48–52

Comparison: Deuteronomy 32:50 places Aaron’s death on Mount Hor, whereas Deuteronomy 10:6 locates
his death and burial at Moserah. The discrepancy now occurs within Deuteronomy itself. Moserah is
not named in 32:48–52, and no equation of the sites is asserted.

See [[Moses Commanded to Ascend Nebo]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 32:48–52.

The destination from Beeroth Bene Jaakan, where Aaron dies and is buried and Eleazar succeeds him
(Deuteronomy 10:6). Numbers 20:28 and 33:38 instead locate Aaron’s death at Mount Hor. This
unresolved contradiction is retained. Compare Moseroth in Numbers 33:30–31; identifying the names
would also leave a reversed travel sequence.
