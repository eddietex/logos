# Moserah

## Overview

The destination from Beeroth Bene Jaakan, where Aaron dies and is buried and Eleazar succeeds him
(Deuteronomy 10:6). Numbers 20:28 and 33:38 instead locate Aaron’s death at Mount Hor. This
unresolved contradiction is retained. Compare Moseroth in Numbers 33:30–31; identifying the names
would also leave a reversed travel sequence.
