# Atrothshophan

## Overview

One of the eight towns the children of Gad build east of the Jordan (Numbers 32:35). The Hebrew writes
it as two words, *ʿaṭrōt šôpān* (Miqra according to the Masorah); the second word seems to distinguish it
from the [[Ataroth]] of the previous verse. Nothing else is said of it, and its site is not identified
here.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
