# Sibmah

## Overview

Built by the children of Reuben (Numbers 32:38). The request of 32:3 names *Sebam* (*śəbām*); Rashi
identifies the two, and the wiki follows him. Joshua 13:19 lists Sibmah in Reuben's border (WEB via
bible-api.com, not yet ingested).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
