# Lassharon

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted in Joshua 12:18 — the only occurrence of the name in the stored WEB. No battle is narrated. A relation to the region of [[Sharon]] is sometimes suggested from the name; the verse does not make it, and the wiki does not assert it.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.

## Appears in
- [[The Kings Struck Down]] — Joshua 12:1–24

## Related places

## Events here
- Its king is struck (12:18); no account of the battle is given.
