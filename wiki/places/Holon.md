# Holon

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the hill country (Joshua 15:51), one of the *eleven cities with their villages* of the first hill-country group (48–51). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Joshua 21:15 names Holon among the priests' cities (not yet ingested).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
