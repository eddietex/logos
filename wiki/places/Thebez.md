# Thebez

## Overview

After Shechem, *Abimelech went to Thebez, and encamped against Thebez, and took it. But there was a
strong tower within the city, and all the men and women of the city fled there, and shut themselves in,
and went up to the roof of the tower* (Judges 9:50–51). Abimelech comes to the door of the tower to burn
it, and *a certain woman cast an upper millstone on Abimelech's head, and broke his skull* (9:52–53).
He dies there.

*Tēbēṣ* (WLC) is named only here in Genesis–Judges. The text does not say where it is, or why Abimelech
attacks it. Perhaps it joined Shechem's revolt, but the text does not say so. Its *strong tower*,
*migdal-ʿōz*, is the phrase of Proverbs 18:10, *Yahweh's name is a strong tower*. At Thebez the
tower holds and the attacker dies.

The town is remembered by name: *Didn't a woman cast an upper millstone on him from the wall, so that he
died at Thebez?* (2 Samuel 11:21, WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested).
