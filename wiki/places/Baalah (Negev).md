# Baalah (Negev)

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the farthest cities of Judah toward the border of Edom in the South (Joshua 15:29), one of the names in the southern group that verse 32 totals as *twenty-nine* cities with their villages, though thirty-six names are listed (21–32). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Qualified because 15:9 uses Baalah as another name for [[Kiriath Jearim]] on the northern border, and 15:11 has [[Mount Baalah]]. Simeon's list has *Balah* at the corresponding point (19:3); the similarity is recorded, not turned into an identification.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
