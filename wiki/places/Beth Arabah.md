# Beth Arabah

## Overview

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Heads the six wilderness cities (61). On the border (15:6) it was a landmark; here it is a town of Judah.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

The northern line passes north of Beth Arabah before ascending to Bohan’s stone (6).
