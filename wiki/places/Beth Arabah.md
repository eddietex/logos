# Beth Arabah

## Overview

### Benjamin’s inheritance — Joshua 18:11–28

Listed among Benjamin’s first twelve cities (22–24), although Joshua 15:61 also lists it among Judah’s wilderness towns. This overlapping assignment is a textual tension; no transfer or distinct second town is asserted.

See [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; comparisons as cited.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Heads the six wilderness cities (61). On the border (15:6) it was a landmark; here it is a town of Judah.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

The northern line passes north of Beth Arabah before ascending to Bohan’s stone (6).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12. No precise modern location or unstated biography is asserted.
