# Kishon

## Overview

### *That ancient river* — Judges 5:21

*The river Kishon swept them away, that ancient river, the river Kishon. My soul, march on with
strength* (5:21). The Song names the river three times in one verse. *Gərāpām*, *swept them away*
(WLC, lemma 1640), occurs only here in the WLC files read (Genesis through Joshua, Judges, Psalms).
*Naḥal qədûmîm* (WLC), *the ancient river*, is uncertain; WEB's *ancient* is one reading. The Song
sets the river beside the stars (5:20) as the forces that fought; the prose said only *Yahweh
confused Sisera* (4:15). That the rain of 5:4 swelled the river and caught the chariots is the most
natural reading, but the Song does not narrate it. See [[The Song of Deborah]] and
[[Prose and Song - The Sea in Exodus 14-15 and the Kishon in Judges 4-5]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:15; 5:4, 20–21; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.

*The river Kishon* (*naḥal qîšôn*, WLC), where Yahweh says he will draw Sisera and his chariots
(Judges 4:7), and where Sisera brings them (4:13). *Naḥal* is a stream or wadi, often seasonal. The
battle is joined there. Judges 4 does not say what the river did; the Song says *The river Kishon swept
them away, that ancient river, the river Kishon* (5:21), so the chariots that were the oppressor's
strength may have been caught by water. That reading belongs to the Song; see the section above.

Psalm 83:9 prays *Do to them as you did to Midian, as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the river Kishon*
(WEB, checked at bible-api.com). In the WLC files read for the previous ingest (Genesis through
Joshua, Judges, 1 Samuel), the name occurred only in Judges 4–5; with Psalms added for the Song's ingest,
it occurs also at Psalm 83:10 (Hebrew numbering; WEB 83:9).

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:7, 13; 5:21; Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-13.
