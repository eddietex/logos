# Kenath

## Overview

*Nobah went and took Kenath, and its villages, and called it Nobah, after his own name* (Numbers 32:42).
*Its villages* is *bənōtêhā*, literally *its daughters* (Miqra according to the Masorah). A place called
*Nobah* appears again beside Jogbehah in Judges 8:11 (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet ingested); the text
does not say whether it is this town. The page keeps the town's first name, Kenath, so that it does not
collide with the man, [[Nobah (person)]].
