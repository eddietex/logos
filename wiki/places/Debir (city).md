# Debir (city)

## Overview

### Achsah’s springs — Joshua 15:13–19

Formerly Kiriath Sepher, Debir is attacked after Caleb’s action at Hebron. Othniel takes it and receives Achsah as wife (15–17). Joshua’s capture in 10:38–39 and removal of the Anakim in 11:21 remain in tension with this further capture; the text does not describe a reoccupation.

See [[Achsah Asks for Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:13–19.

### The whole land taken — Joshua 11:16–23

Debir is named with Hebron and Anab as a place from which Joshua cuts off the Anakim (21). This is a third Debir notice beside its destruction in 10:38–39 and Othniel’s capture in 15:15–17; the text does not set out their relationship.

See [[The Whole Land Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23.

Joshua attacks and destroys the city, its king, and its dependent cities (38–39). Distinguished from [[Debir (person)]], king of Eglon. Joshua 15:15–17 later reports Othniel taking Debir; a second conquest or overlapping account is possible, but the text does not specify the relationship.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43. No modern identification or dynasty is assigned.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Its king is counted (12:13), after its capture in 10:38–39. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
