# Michmethath

## Overview

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

The border runs from Asher to Michmethath before Shechem, then toward En Tappuah (7). This
supplements its boundary role in Joshua 16:6.

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

Northern landmark where the boundary goes westward before turning eastward toward Taanath Shiloh (6). No modern identification is assigned.
