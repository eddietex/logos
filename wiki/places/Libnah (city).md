# Libnah (city)

## Overview

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Heads the third lowland group (42). Captured in 10:29–30; now allotted to Judah. Distinct from [[Libnah (wilderness camp)]].

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

The city attacked after Makkedah and before Lachish (29–31). Yahweh delivers it and its unnamed king; Israel kills its inhabitants. It is kept distinct from [[Libnah (wilderness camp)]] in Numbers 33, since this passage does not identify the two.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43. No modern identification or dynasty is assigned.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Its king is counted (12:15), after its capture in 10:29–30. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
