# Migron

## Overview

The place of *the pomegranate tree* under which Saul sits with his six hundred men: *Saul stayed in the uttermost
part of Gibeah under the pomegranate tree which is in Migron* (1 Samuel 14:2, stored WEB). *Miḡrôn* (WLC) occurs only
here in the WLC files of Genesis–2 Samuel (morphhb lemma 4051).

Isaiah names Migron in his picture of an army approaching Jerusalem from the north: *He has come to Aiath. He has
passed through Migron. At Michmash he stores his baggage. They have gone over the pass. They have taken up their
lodging at Geba. Ramah trembles. Gibeah of Saul has fled* (Isaiah 10:28–29, checked at bible-api.com, WEB, not
stored). There Migron comes before Michmash, as if north of the pass, while 1 Samuel 14:2 sets it at *the uttermost
part of Gibeah*, south of it. Whether these are one place, two places, or whether *Migron* in 14:2 is a district on
the outskirts of Gibeah is commonly discussed (recalled, not checked); the wiki records the tension and does not
settle it. See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].
