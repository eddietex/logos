# The Hill of Moreh

## Overview

### Midian's camp — Judges 7:1

*Midian's camp was on the north side of them, by the hill of Moreh, in the valley* (7:1). The hill
stands in the valley of Jezreel, where Midian, Amalek, and the children of the east camped in 6:33,
north of Gideon's camp at the spring of Harod. The Hebrew is *miggiḇʿat hammôreh bāʿēmeq*, *from the
hill of the Moreh, in the valley* (WLC). No modern site is assigned here.

The name *môreh* is also the name of the *oak of Moreh* at Shechem, where Abram built his first altar
(Genesis 12:6), and of the *oaks of Moreh* that Deuteronomy 11:30 places near Gilgal, by Gerizim and
Ebal (both WLC *môreh*/*mōreh*). The hill is a different place, in the northern valley, and the text
does not connect them. In the WLC of Genesis–Deuteronomy, Joshua, and Judges, the name occurs only in
those three verses.
