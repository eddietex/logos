# Mount Baalah

## Overview

The northern boundary passes this mountain after Shikkeron and before Jabneel (11). The text does not equate it with Baalah/Kiriath Jearim of verse 9.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12. No precise modern location or unstated biography is asserted.
