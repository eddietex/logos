# En Gedi

## Overview

A town of Judah with a wilderness of the same name (1 Samuel 24:1). In the
wiki so far it appears only in a comparison. In [[Delight and the Unawakened Love]] the woman
calls her beloved *"a cluster of henna blossoms from the vineyards of En Gedi"* (Song 1:14).
The line assumes that En Gedi had vineyards worth naming. It does not set any scene there.

Elsewhere in Scripture, checked in the WEB but not yet ingested: Joshua lists En Gedi among
the towns of Judah (Joshua 15:62). David lives *"in the strongholds of En
Gedi"* while fleeing Saul (1 Samuel 23:29), and Saul hears he is in *"the wilderness of En
Gedi"* (24:1). 2 Chronicles 20:2 identifies Hazazon Tamar with En Gedi, where an army gathers
against Jehoshaphat. Ezekiel's vision of the healed waters has fishermen spreading nets
*"from En Gedi even to En Eglaim"* (Ezekiel 47:10). Genesis 14:7 names Hazazon Tamar without
the identification, in [[The Rescue of Lot and Melchizedek]]. Whether Genesis means the same
place rests on the Chronicler's note, so that passage is not listed under *Appears in*.

The name's sense and the site's archaeology are not discussed here. No source-language or
historical claim is made beyond the verses cited.
