# Ophrah (Benjamin)

## Overview

Named among Benjamin’s first twelve cities with their villages (23). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.

Not the Ophrah of Gideon. Judges 6 names *Ophrah of the Abiezrites* (6:24), a town of Manasseh; see
[[Ophrah (Manasseh)]]. The two pages were qualified when the second Ophrah arrived.

In 1 Samuel 13:17 the first Philistine raiding company from [[Michmash]] *turned to the way that leads to Ophrah,
to the land of Shual*. The text does not say it is the Benjamite town, but it is the Ophrah near the scene; the wiki
files it here (judgment call). See [[No Smith in Israel]] and [[The Land of Shual]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28; 1 Samuel 13:17.
