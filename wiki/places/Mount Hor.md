# Mount Hor

## Overview

### The stages of the journey — Numbers 33:1–49

Aaron dies here at Yahweh’s command on day one of month five in year forty, aged 123 (37–39). This supplies the date and age absent from Numbers 20:22–29.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49.

### The brass serpent — Numbers 21:4–9

Israel departs from Mount Hor on the way to the Red Sea (4). The snake episode belongs to the onward journey, not explicitly to the summit where Aaron died.

See [[The Brass Serpent]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:4–9.

The mountain reached after Israel leaves [[Kadesh]], described as by the border of [[Edom]]
(Numbers 20:22–23). Here [[Aaron]] dies and [[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] receives his garments.
The passage gives a narrative location, not coordinates or a secure modern identification.

## Events here
- Moses, Aaron, and Eleazar ascend before the congregation.
- Moses transfers the garments; Aaron dies on the summit.
- Moses and Eleazar descend, and Israel mourns thirty days.
