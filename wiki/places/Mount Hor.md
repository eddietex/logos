# Mount Hor

## Overview

### Commanded to ascend Nebo — Deuteronomy 32:48–52

Aaron’s death here is named as the precedent for Moses’ death on Nebo (50). Deuteronomy 32:50 agrees
with Numbers 20:28 and 33:38, but conflicts with Deuteronomy 10:6’s Moserah location. No
identification of the sites or return journey is supplied.

See [[Moses Commanded to Ascend Nebo]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 32:48–52.

### Renewed tablets and onward journey — Deuteronomy 10:1–11

Numbers 20:28 and 33:38 place Aaron’s death here. Deuteronomy 10:6 instead says he died and was
buried at Moserah. This is an unresolved location tension, not evidence that the two names are
established synonyms. Mount Hor is a comparison, not a name in Deuteronomy 10:1–11.

See [[New Tablets and the Journey Onward]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 10:1–11.

**Distinct boundary namesake:** [[Mount Hor (northern border)]] belongs to Numbers 34:7–8,
not the Edomite setting of Aaron’s death. The two textual locations are kept separate.

### The stages of the journey — Numbers 33:1–49

Aaron dies here at Yahweh’s command on day one of month five in year forty, aged 123 (37–39). This supplies the date and age absent from Numbers 20:22–29.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49.

### The brass serpent — Numbers 21:4–9

Israel departs from Mount Hor on the way to the Red Sea (4). The snake episode belongs to the onward journey, not explicitly to the summit where Aaron died.

See [[The Brass Serpent]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:4–9.

The mountain reached after Israel leaves [[Kadesh]], described as by the border of [[Edom]]
(Numbers 20:22–23). Here [[Aaron]] dies and [[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] receives his garments.
The passage gives a narrative location, not coordinates or a secure modern identification.
