# Shihor

## Overview

The Shihor before Egypt marks the starting point of the remaining territory extending northward to Ekron (Joshua 13:3). This verse does not explicitly identify it with the Nile or the brook of Egypt; neither identification is asserted here.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.
