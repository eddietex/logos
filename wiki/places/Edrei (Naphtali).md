# Edrei (Naphtali)

## Overview

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (37), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). Distinct from [[Edrei]], Og’s city east of the Jordan (12:4; 13:31).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
