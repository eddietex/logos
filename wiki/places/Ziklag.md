# Ziklag

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the farthest cities of Judah toward the border of Edom in the South (Joshua 15:31), one of the names in the southern group that verse 32 totals as *twenty-nine* cities with their villages, though thirty-six names are listed (21–32). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Simeon's list in 19:5 also names Ziklag; see [[Simeon's Lot Within Judah - Joshua 15 and 19]]. It is the town of David's later statute on sharing spoil, discussed in [[Half to the Fighters - Numbers 31 and David's Statute at Ziklag]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
