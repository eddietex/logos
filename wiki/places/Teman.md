# Teman

## Overview

An Edomite town or district, and the home of [[Eliphaz the Temanite]]. Its name means *south* or
*right hand* — the Hebrew orients by facing east, so the right hand is the south — and it is
consistently associated with Edom, with the territory south-east of the Dead Sea, and with one
thing besides: wisdom.

That association is what makes the name worth a page. Jeremiah 49:7, in the oracle against Edom,
asks *"Is wisdom no more in Teman? Has counsel perished from the prudent? Has their wisdom
vanished?"* — a question that only lands if Teman's reputation for counsel was proverbial. Obadiah
8 says God will *"destroy the wise men out of Edom, and understanding out of the mountain of
Esau"*. Baruch 3:22–23 makes the same association. Edom, and Teman within it, was to Israel what
Egypt and Babylon also were: a recognised source of the international wisdom tradition to which the
books of Job and Proverbs both belong.

The book of Job places its most confident traditionalist there, and then has him argue from
inherited wisdom kept pure of outsiders — *"which wise men have told by their fathers … to whom
alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them"* (15:18–19). Whether the author
intended the irony cannot be proven. It is available: the man from the town famous for wisdom
appeals to that wisdom, and God tells him at 42:7 that he did not speak rightly.

Genesis 36:11, 15 names Teman as the firstborn son of Esau's son Eliphaz, and 36:42 lists a chief
Teman — the standard biblical pattern of a place named for an ancestor, or an ancestor named for a
place. The overlap with the friend's name and gentilic is close enough that the book's cast is
generally read as deliberately Edomite; see the note on [[Eliphaz the Temanite]].

Location is not fixed. Proposals have included Tawilan near Petra and Bozrah, and some scholars
read Teman as a district of Edom rather than a single town, which would suit the several verses
that pair it with Bozrah as though the two were distinguishable.

## Appears in

- [[Eliphaz's Second Speech]] — carried in the gentilic, and standing behind the appeal to
  ancestral wisdom in 15:17–19.
- [[Miserable Comforters and the Witness in Heaven]] — the same, in the speaker Job answers.

## Related places

- **Edom / the mountain of Esau** — the territory Teman belongs to; gets its page when Genesis 36
  or the prophets are ingested.
- **Uz** — Job's own land, named at 1:1 and located vaguely to the east or south-east; a page
  follows when Job 1 is ingested.
- **Shuah and Naamah** — the homelands implied by [[Bildad the Shuhite]] and
  [[Zophar the Naamathite]], neither securely located, and both pointing the same direction.
- [[Sheol]] — the only other location the ingested Job passages name.

## Events here

None. Teman is never a setting in Job; the whole book takes place where Job sits. The town enters
only as an origin, and its significance is entirely what the origin implies about the man.
