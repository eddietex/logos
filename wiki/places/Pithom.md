# Pithom

## Overview

**One of the two storage cities Israel builds for Pharaoh under forced labour** — *"They built
storage cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Raamses"* (Exodus 1:11). It is named once in the Bible.

**The name is generally taken as Egyptian *Per-Atum*, *house of Atum***, the sun-god of evening
worshipped at Heliopolis — the city of [[On]], where Joseph's father-in-law was priest. Its site is
usually sought in the Wadi Tumilat, the valley running east from the Delta toward the Bitter Lakes;
Tell el-Maskhuta and Tell er-Retaba are the two candidates most often proposed. **The wiki records
the identification as the usual one and does not decide between the sites.**

***ʿĀrê miskənôt*, *storage cities***, are depots for grain and supplies. The phrase is the one
Solomon's building programme uses — *"all the storage cities that Solomon had"* (1 Kings 9:19) —
built, like these, with a levy of forced labour drawn from a subject population (9:20–21). **Egypt
stored grain under Joseph to save the world from famine (Genesis 41:48–49); here it stores it with
the labour of Joseph's family.** See [[Slavery and Servitude]] and
[[Buy Us and Our Land - Egypt Sold into Servitude and Israel After It]].
