# Irshemesh

## Overview

Named after Zorah and Eshtaol at the head of Dan’s border list (41–46), for which no city total is given (41). The second element matches [[Beth Shemesh]] on Judah’s nearby border (15:10), but the names differ and the text does not equate them.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
