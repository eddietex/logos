# Enaim

## Overview

**Where Tamar sits veiled — and the one place-name in Genesis that is a comment on the scene.**

> **14** …and sat in the gate of Enaim, which is by the way to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah was
> grown up, and she wasn’t given to him as a wife.
> **15** When Judah saw her, he thought that she was a prostitute, for she had covered her face.
> (38:14–15)

***Petaḥ ʿênayim*** — ***the opening of Enaim***. **And *ʿênayim* is the Hebrew for *two eyes*, or
*two springs*** (the same word does both, springs being the eyes of the land), so the phrase can be
read as a place name, which is how the WEB and almost every version take it, **or as a description:
*the opening of the eyes*.** Hirah is later sent to ask *"the men of her place"* about *"the prostitute
that was at Enaim by the road"* (38:21), **which wants a location**, and the wiki adopts the place-name
reading on that evidence — **while recording that the pun is there and that nothing in the Hebrew makes
the reader choose.**

**The scene it names is about eyes.** Judah *sees* her and infers a prostitute *"for she had covered
her face"*; Tamar has acted because she *saw* that Shelah was grown; and **the chapter turns four verses
from the end on *hakker-nāʾ*, *discern, please***, when he is finally shown something he can identify.
See [[Seeing and Being Seen]] and [[Judah and Tamar]].

**The gate is where business is done.** *Petaḥ*, an opening or entrance, is the standard place for
transactions and judgments in the Hebrew Bible — **Boaz settles the redemption of Ruth at a town gate
with ten elders sitting** (Ruth 4:1–2), in the story whose elders bless his marriage in
[[Tamar]]'s name. **Tamar conducts her negotiation at a gate too**, and there are no witnesses and no
elders, which is the whole of her problem. See
[[Levirate Marriage - Genesis 38, Deuteronomy 25, and Ruth]].

## Identification

**Unlocated.** The site is not identified with confidence; it is on the road from [[Adullam]] up to
[[Timnah]], in the Shephelah. **Joshua 15:34 lists an *Enam* in the same district**, between Jarmuth
and Adullam, and is the usual candidate. **Genesis names it once and nothing ever happens there
again.**
