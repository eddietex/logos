# Adam (city)

## Overview

The city beside Zarethan where the upstream Jordan waters stand in a heap, a great distance from the crossing near Jericho (Joshua 3:16). The verse supplies these relative bearings, not a modern identification. Distinguished from [[Adam (person)]]; no symbolic connection with that person is stated.
