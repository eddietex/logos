# Ar

## Overview

The dwelling toward which the slopes of the valleys incline in the quoted fragment (15), associated
with Moab’s border. Kept distinct from Moab as a whole; this verse does not equate the city with the
entire country.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:10–20.
