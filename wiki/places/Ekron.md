# Ekron

## Overview

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

Listed in Dan’s inheritance (43), after Judah’s border passed its north side (15:11) and Judah’s list assigned it with its towns and villages (15:45). Joshua 13:3 still counts it among Philistine territory remaining. See [[Dan's Towns on Judah's Border - Joshua 15 and 19]].

See [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48; comparisons as cited.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Allotted to Judah *with its towns and its villages*, together with everything *from Ekron even to the sea* beside Ashdod (45–46). The list is not a report of capture; 13:2–3 counts the Ekronites among the land that remains.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### Judah’s borders — Joshua 15:1–12

The boundary goes to Ekron’s northern side before extending to Shikkeron (11). Inclusion in the route is not a report of conquest or occupation.

See [[The Borders of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12.

Ekron marks the northern boundary of the stretch from the Shihor (Joshua 13:3). The Ekronites are also named among the five Philistine lordships whose territory remains. No capture is narrated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.
