# Ekron

## Overview

### *From Ekron even to Gath* — 1 Samuel 7:14

*The cities which the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ekron even to Gath* (7:14),
*mēʿeqrôn wəʿaḏ-gaṯ* (WLC). The two are the last cities the ark passed through (5:8–10). The phrase reads as a
line along the edge of the Philistine plain; the verse does not say whether Ekron itself was taken. See
[[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 5:8–10; 7:14. Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-17.

### The lords return — 1 Samuel 6:16–17

The five lords follow the cart *to the border of Beth Shemesh*, watch the ark received, and *returned to
Ekron the same day* (6:12, 16). Ekron is the city nearest Israel of the three the ark visited, and the one
that demanded it be sent home (5:10–11). One golden tumor is *for Ekron* (6:17). See
[[The Ark Returns to Israel]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 5:10–11; 6:12–17.

### *To kill us and our people* — 1 Samuel 5:10–12

From Gath the ark is sent to Ekron, and the Ekronites cry out as it comes: *They have brought the ark of the
God of Israel here to us, to kill us and our people* (10). The lords are gathered again and told to send it
*to its own place*; *there was a deadly confusion throughout all the city. The hand of God was very heavy
there.* Those who did not die were struck with tumors, *and the cry of the city went up to heaven* (11–12).
Judges 1:18 had said Judah took Ekron; here it is a Philistine city. See [[The Ark Among the Philistines]]
and [[Their Cry Went Up - Exodus 2.23 and 1 Samuel 5.12]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 5:10–12; Judges 1:18.

### Taken by Judah — Judges 1:18

*Also Judah took Gaza with its border, and Ashkelon with its border, and Ekron with its border* (18).
Joshua 13:2–3 had listed the city with Gaza and Ashkelon among *the land that still remains*. The next verse
says Judah could not dislodge the people of the valley, and later Judges has two of them in Philistine
hands (Ashkelon at 14:19, Gaza at 16:1–2). It is often said that the Septuagint reads verse 18 as a
negative; that claim has not been checked here. The WLC reads *wayyilkōd*, *and he took*.

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

Listed in Dan’s inheritance (43), after Judah’s border passed its north side (15:11) and Judah’s list assigned it with its towns and villages (15:45). Joshua 13:3 still counts it among Philistine territory remaining. See [[Dan's Towns on Judah's Border - Joshua 15 and 19]].

See [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48; comparisons as cited.

### Judah’s cities — Joshua 15:20–63

Allotted to Judah *with its towns and its villages*, together with everything *from Ekron even to the sea* beside Ashdod (45–46). The list is not a report of capture; 13:2–3 counts the Ekronites among the land that remains.

See [[The Cities of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63.

### Judah’s borders — Joshua 15:1–12

The boundary goes to Ekron’s northern side before extending to Shikkeron (11). Inclusion in the route is not a report of conquest or occupation.

See [[The Borders of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12.

Ekron marks the northern boundary of the stretch from the Shihor (Joshua 13:3). The Ekronites are also named among the five Philistine lordships whose territory remains. No capture is narrated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:1–7.
