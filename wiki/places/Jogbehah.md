# Jogbehah

## Overview

One of the towns the children of Gad build (Numbers 32:35). It appears again in Judges 8:11, where
Gideon goes up *by the way of those who lived in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah* on his way to
Karkor — the two names of Numbers 32's last verses side by side. See [[Ephraim, Succoth, and Penuel]].

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32; Judges 8:11.
