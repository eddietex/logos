# Jogbehah

## Overview

One of the towns the children of Gad build (Numbers 32:35). It appears again in Judges 8:11, where
Gideon goes up *by the way of those who lived in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah* (WEB via
bible-api.com, not yet ingested) — the two names of this chapter's last verses side by side.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
