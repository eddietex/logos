# Beth Millo (Shechem)

## Overview

*All the men of Shechem assembled themselves together, and all the house of Millo, and went and made
Abimelech king* (Judges 9:6). *The house of Millo*, *bêt millôʾ* (WLC), stands beside *the men of
Shechem* as a second group that makes Abimelech king. In Genesis–Judges the word *millôʾ* occurs only
here and in Jotham's curse, which names it twice: *let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the men
of Shechem, and the house of Millo; and let fire come out from the men of Shechem, and from the house
of Millo, and devour Abimelech* (9:20). See [[Jotham's Fable]].

*Millôʾ* is usually derived from *mālēʾ*, *to fill*, and taken to mean an earthwork, fill, or citadel
(reported, not checked against a lexicon). The text does not say whether *the house of Millo* is a
building, a district, or the people who lived there. Some identify it with *the tower of Shechem*
(9:46–49) (reported, not checked); the wiki does not, and gives the tower its own page,
[[The Tower of Shechem]]. If the two are different, *the house of Millo* is not named in the
fulfilment of the curse at all, and the narrator says only that *the curse of Jotham … came on them*
(9:57). See [[The Fall of Abimelech]].

The page is qualified from the start because Jerusalem also has a *Millo*: *David built around from
Millo and inward* (2 Samuel 5:9, WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). The wiki does not
assume the two are related beyond the common word.

See [[Abimelech Made King at Shechem]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 9:6, 20, 46–49; 2 Samuel 5:9 checked
at bible-api.com (WEB), 2026-11-15; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
