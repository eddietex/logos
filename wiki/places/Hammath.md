# Hammath

## Overview

One of Naphtali’s *fortified cities* (35), in Naphtali’s register, whose stated total is nineteen cities with their villages (38–39). Distinct from [[Hamath]] on the Orontes, the northern reference point of the land.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48. No precise modern site is assigned.
