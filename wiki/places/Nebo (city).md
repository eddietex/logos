# Nebo (city)

## Overview

A town named in the request of Reuben and Gad (Numbers 32:3) and built by the children of Reuben, one of
two whose names are marked *(their names being changed)* (32:38). The text does not give the new name.

*Nebo* is also the mountain from which Moses sees the land (*Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab*,
Deuteronomy 32:49) and the Babylonian god of Isaiah 46:1, *Nebo stoops* (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet
ingested). Rashi takes the renaming in 32:38 as the removal of idol names (Sefaria English). This page is
qualified *(city)* so that the mountain and the god can have their own pages without a silent collision.
