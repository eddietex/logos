# Michmash

## Overview

### Saul's camp, then the Philistines' — 1 Samuel 13:1–15

*Miḵmāś* (WLC). Saul keeps two thousand men *in Michmash and in the Mount of Bethel*, while Jonathan holds
[[Gibeah (Benjamin)]] with one thousand (13:2). After Jonathan strikes the garrison at [[Geba]], Saul goes down to
[[Gilgal]], and the Philistines come up with chariots, horsemen, and men *as the sand which is on the seashore*,
and *encamped in Michmash, eastward of Beth Aven* (13:5): they take the position Saul has left. Saul names it in his
defence, *the Philistines assembled themselves together at Michmash* (13:11). From 13:16 the two armies face each
other across the pass, Saul and Jonathan at Geba of Benjamin and the Philistines at Michmash (stored WEB; the next
pericope). The text locates it only by Bethel's hill country, Beth Aven, and the facing camp at Geba. A modern
identification is commonly given but is not used here. See [[Saul's Sacrifice at Gilgal]].
