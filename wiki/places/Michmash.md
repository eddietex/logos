# Michmash

## Overview

### Saul's camp, then the Philistines' — 1 Samuel 13:1–15

*Miḵmāś* (WLC). Saul keeps two thousand men *in Michmash and in the Mount of Bethel*, while Jonathan holds
[[Gibeah (Benjamin)]] with one thousand (13:2). After Jonathan strikes the garrison at [[Geba]], Saul goes down to
[[Gilgal]], and the Philistines come up with chariots, horsemen, and men *as the sand which is on the seashore*,
and *encamped in Michmash, eastward of Beth Aven* (13:5): they take the position Saul has left. Saul names it in his
defence, *the Philistines assembled themselves together at Michmash* (13:11). From 13:16 the two armies face each
other across the pass, Saul and Jonathan at Geba of Benjamin and the Philistines at Michmash (stored WEB; the next
pericope). The text locates it only by Bethel's hill country, Beth Aven, and the facing camp at Geba. A modern
identification is commonly given but is not used here. See [[Saul's Sacrifice at Gilgal]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:2, 5, 11, 16. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-18.

### The raiders' camp, and the pass — 1 Samuel 13:16–23

The Philistines *encamped in Michmash* (13:16), facing Saul and Jonathan at Geba, and from the camp the raiders go
out in three companies, north, west, and east on the usual reading of the place names (13:17–18). The pericope ends
with *the garrison of the Philistines* going out *to the pass of Michmash*, *maʿăḇar miḵmāś* (13:23, WLC), where
Jonathan will cross (14:1–5, stored WEB; not yet ingested). *Maʿăḇar* occurs elsewhere in the WLC of Genesis–2 Samuel
only at the ford of the Jabbok (Genesis 32:22 WEB; 32:23 WLC). See [[No Smith in Israel]].

### Across the pass — 1 Samuel 14:1–23

Jonathan goes over to the Philistine garrison *on the other side* (14:1). Between the passes stand two crags, *The one
crag rose up on the north in front of Michmash, and the other on the south in front of Geba* (14:5); the names given
are [[Bozez]] and [[Seneh]] (14:4). From the camp at Michmash the trembling spreads after Jonathan's first slaughter,
and the Philistines turn their swords on each other (14:15, 20). The pursuit runs past Beth Aven (14:23) and, in the
next pericope, *from Michmash to Aijalon* (14:31; see [[Saul's Rash Oath]]). Isaiah names Michmash in the line of
an army's approach: *At Michmash he stores his baggage. They have gone over the pass* (Isaiah 10:28–29, checked at
bible-api.com, WEB, not stored). See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].
