# Bozrah

## Overview

**Edom's chief city, and the only one of the eight royal seats in Genesis 36 that can still be pointed
at on a map.**

> **33** Bela died, and Jobab, the son of Zerah of Bozrah, reigned in his place.

**That is its whole appearance in Genesis** — a home town attached to the second king in a list. See
[[The Descendants of Esau]] and [[Jobab]]. The other seven seats are Dinhabah, the land of the
Temanites, Avith, Masrekah, [[Rehoboth by the River]], and Pau, and **none of them is securely
located**; Bozrah is identified with confidence as **Buseirah**, on a spur above the wadi in
south-western Jordan, excavated in the 1970s and showing a substantial administrative centre from
about the eighth century BC.

***Boṣrâ*** is from *bāṣar*, **to cut off, to make inaccessible** — the same root as *miḇṣār*,
**fortress**, which is the *chief Mibzar* of 36:42. **The name means something like *the
stronghold***, and the site is one: a natural promontory with steep drops on three sides.

## Why the prophets go there

**Bozrah stands for Edom the way Samaria stands for Israel** — the capital used as the name of the
country — and it does so in some of the fiercest oracles in Scripture.

- **Amos 1:12** — *"I will send a fire on Teman, and it will devour the palaces of Bozrah."* Paired
  with [[Teman]], the two names covering the country between them.
- **Jeremiah 49:13, 22** — *"Bozrah will become an astonishment, a reproach, a waste, and a curse"*;
  the oracle that also asks *"Is wisdom no more in Teman?"*
- **Isaiah 34:6** — *"Yahweh has a sword … for Yahweh has a sacrifice in Bozrah, and a great slaughter
  in the land of Edom."*
- **Isaiah 63:1–6** — the best known: *"Who is this who comes from Edom, with dyed garments from
  Bozrah? … Why is your clothing red, and your garments like him who treads in the wine vat?"*
  **The winepress imagery of Revelation 14:19–20 and 19:13 comes through this passage**, and Christian
  tradition has read the figure coming up from Bozrah as Christ since the patristic period.

**The colour is not an accident.** *ʾĔdôm* is **red** — from the stew Esau sold his birthright for
(25:30), from the *ʾadmônî* newborn five verses earlier, and from the sandstone the country is made
of. **Isaiah 63 builds its whole image on the pun Genesis planted**, and Bozrah is where it lands. See
[[Edom]] and [[The Vine and the Cup]].

## Appears in

- [[The Descendants of Esau]] — Genesis 36:33; the home town of [[Jobab]], the second king of Edom,
  and the one royal seat in the list that survives as a place rather than as a name

## Related places

- [[Edom]] — the country it is the chief city of
- [[Teman]] — the district it is paired with in the prophetic oracles, and Job's first friend's home
- [[Rehoboth by the River]] — another of the eight royal seats, and unlocated
- [[Moab (place)]] — Edom's northern neighbour

## Events here

- [[Jobab]] reigns from it as the second king of Edom — Genesis 36:33
- The target of oracles against Edom — Amos 1:12; Jeremiah 49:13, 22; Isaiah 34:6
- The city the figure in blood-stained garments comes up from — Isaiah 63:1

## Notes

**A second Bozrah** is named in Moab at Jeremiah 48:24, in a list of Moabite towns; it is not this
one. The name is a common one for a defensible site, which is what it means.
