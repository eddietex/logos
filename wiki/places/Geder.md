# Geder

## Overview

Royal city whose king is counted among the thirty-one Joshua and Israel struck west of the Jordan (Joshua 12:13). No battle at Geder is narrated, and the name occurs nowhere else in the stored WEB. Joshua 15:36 names a *Gederah* among Judah's lowland towns; the text does not equate the two, and this page does not.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 12–21 at the verses cited. No modern site is assigned.
