# Gezer

## Overview

### Not driven out — Judges 1:29

*Ephraim didn't drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, but the Canaanites lived in Gezer among them* (29). Joshua 16:10 adds forced labor and *to this day*; Judges 1:29 has neither, which makes Gezer the one city in the list whose Canaanites are not said to become forced laborers. The observation is recorded without drawing a conclusion from it.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:22–36.

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

Allocated with its suburbs from Ephraim to the remaining Kohathites (21:21).  Joshua 16:10 retains Canaanites in Gezer; this Levitical allocation does not narrate their expulsion or establish exclusive occupation.

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### Ephraim’s inheritance — Joshua 16:1–10

The Josephite boundary reaches Gezer (3). Ephraim does not drive out its Canaanites, who remain under forced labor (10). Horam’s defeat in Joshua 10:33 did not itself establish removal of Gezer’s population.

See [[The Inheritance of Ephraim]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 16:1–10.

Horam’s royal city (33). Its king and relief force are defeated while helping Lachish; this is not an explicit report of Gezer’s capture. Joshua 16:10 later records Canaanites continuing to live there.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43. No modern identification or dynasty is assigned.
