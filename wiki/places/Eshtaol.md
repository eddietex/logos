# Eshtaol

## Overview

### Samson's country — Judges 13:2, 25

*Yahweh's Spirit began to move him in Mahaneh Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol* (13:25). The pair that
heads Dan's border list (Joshua 19:41) frames Samson's story: it begins between them, and he is buried
*between Zorah and Eshtaol in the burial site of Manoah his father* (16:31). The Danites set out *from
Zorah and Eshtaol* in 18:2, 11. See [[The Announcement of Samson's Birth]], [[The Death of Samson]], and
[[Mahaneh Dan]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 13:1–25; 16:31; 18:2, 11; Joshua 19:41.

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

With Zorah at the head of Dan’s border list (41), as already in Judah’s lowland list (15:33). See [[Dan's Towns on Judah's Border - Joshua 15 and 19]].

See [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 19:24–48; comparisons as cited.

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the first group of the lowland (Joshua 15:33), one of the *fourteen cities with their villages* of the first lowland group (33–36). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Paired with [[Zorah]] here and again in Dan's border (19:41).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
