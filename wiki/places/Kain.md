# Kain

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the third group of the hill country (Joshua 15:57), one of the *ten cities with their villages* of the third hill-country group (55–57). The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

A town name; nothing connects it with the person [[Cain]].

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
