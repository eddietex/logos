# Seneh

## Overview

The second of two rocky crags at the pass between Michmash and Geba: *the name of the one was Bozez, and the name of
the other Seneh. The one crag rose up on the north in front of Michmash, and the other on the south in front of
Geba* (1 Samuel 14:4–5, stored WEB). On the usual reading of the order, Seneh is the southern crag facing [[Geba]],
the Israelite side (an inference, not stated).

*Senneh* (WLC) occurs only here in the WLC files of Genesis–2 Samuel (morphhb lemma 5573). It is commonly glossed
*thorny* and compared with *sᵊneh*, the bush that burned and was not consumed (Exodus 3:2) (recalled, not checked).
morphhb gives the bush a different lemma (5572), and the two words are pointed differently in the WLC; the comparison
is not the text's. See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].
