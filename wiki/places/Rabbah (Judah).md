# Rabbah (Judah)

## Overview

A town of Judah's inheritance, listed among the fifth group of the hill country (Joshua 15:60), one of the *two cities with their villages* of verse 60. The list is an allotment register: no event, inhabitant, or capture is narrated here.

Distinct from [[Rabbah]] of the children of Ammon (Deuteronomy 3:11; Joshua 13:25).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63. No modern site is assigned.
