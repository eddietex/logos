# Abel Meholah

## Overview

*As far as the border of Abel Meholah, by Tabbath* (Judges 7:22). The farthest point of Midian's flight
named before the pursuit. WEB's *border* is *śəpat*, the word for the *seashore* in 7:12, a lip or edge
(WLC). The name is usually glossed *meadow of dancing*; the gloss is reported, not checked.

It is the home of Elisha: *anoint Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abel Meholah to be prophet in your place*
(1 Kings 19:16, WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). The site is not identified here.

Source: stored WEB Judges 7:12, 22; 1 Kings 19:16 checked at bible-api.com (WEB); Hebrew WLC
