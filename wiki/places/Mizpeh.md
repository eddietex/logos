# Mizpeh

## Overview

### The assembly of all Israel — Judges 20:1–3

*The congregation was assembled as one man, from Dan even to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead, to
Yahweh at Mizpah* (20:1), and *the children of Benjamin heard that the children of Israel had gone up to
Mizpah* (3). The Levite tells his story there, and the tribes swear not to go home until Gibeah is
punished (4–10). The WEB spells it *Mizpah*; the Hebrew is *hammiṣpâ*, *the watchtower* (WLC).

Which Mizpah the text does not say. The wiki files it here, under Benjamin's Mizpeh (Joshua 18:26), because
the war is fought near Gibeah and Bethel; that identification is commonly made and is not stated in the
text. It is kept apart from Jephthah's [[Mizpah (Gilead)]] across the Jordan. 1 Samuel 7:5–6 gathers Israel
*to Mizpah* to fast (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not stored). The oath sworn here returns at 21:1 (stored WEB; not yet ingested).

See [[The War Against Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:26; Judges 20:1–10; 21:1; Hebrew WLC,
2026-11-17.

Named among Benjamin’s second group of fourteen cities with their villages (26). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:11–28.
