# Mizpeh

## Overview

### Repentance, prayer, and victory — 1 Samuel 7:5–16

Samuel says *Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray to Yahweh for you* (7:5). There they draw water and
pour it out before Yahweh, fast, and confess *We have sinned against Yahweh*, and *Samuel judged the children of
Israel in Mizpah* (7:6). The Philistine lords come up against the assembly; Samuel offers a burnt offering and
cries to Yahweh, and Yahweh thunders and confuses them. *The men of Israel went out of Mizpah* in pursuit (7:7–11),
and Samuel sets up the stone Ebenezer *between Mizpah and Shen* (7:12). Mizpah is then one of the three towns of
his yearly circuit, with Bethel and Gilgal (7:16). The WLC writes *hammiṣpâ*, with the article, as in Judges 20.
The wiki keeps this Mizpah on the same page as the assembly against Benjamin; 1 Samuel does not name the tribe,
and the circuit with Bethel and Gilgal is the reason for the filing. See [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]].
Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 7:5–16. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### The oaths sworn here — Judges 21:1, 5, 8

Judges 21 reports two oaths sworn at the Mizpah assembly that chapter 20 did not mention: *None of us will
give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife* (21:1), and against anyone *who didn't come up to Yahweh to
Mizpah*, *He shall surely be put to death* (21:5). The roll of the assembly finds Jabesh Gilead missing
(21:8–9). See [[Wives for Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 21:1–9.

### The assembly of all Israel — Judges 20:1–3

*The congregation was assembled as one man, from Dan even to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead, to
Yahweh at Mizpah* (20:1), and *the children of Benjamin heard that the children of Israel had gone up to
Mizpah* (3). The Levite tells his story there, and the tribes swear not to go home until Gibeah is
punished (4–10). The WEB spells it *Mizpah*; the Hebrew is *hammiṣpâ*, *the watchtower* (WLC).

Which Mizpah the text does not say. The wiki files it here, under Benjamin's Mizpeh (Joshua 18:26), because
the war is fought near Gibeah and Bethel; that identification is commonly made and is not stated in the
text. It is kept apart from Jephthah's [[Mizpah (Gilead)]] across the Jordan. 1 Samuel 7:5–6 gathers Israel
*to Mizpah* to fast; see [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]]. The oath sworn here returns at 21:1 (stored WEB; not yet ingested).

See [[The War Against Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 18:26; Judges 20:1–10; 21:1; Hebrew WLC,
2026-11-17.

Named among Benjamin’s second group of fourteen cities with their villages (26). No individual event or precise modern site is supplied.
