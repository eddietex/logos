# Azmon

## Overview

### Judah’s borders — Joshua 15:1–12

The southern boundary passes here and reaches the brook of Egypt, ending at the sea (4), as in Numbers 34:4–5.

See [[The Borders of Judah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:1–12.

A southern boundary marker after Hazar Addar, from which the border turns to the brook of Egypt.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34:4–5. No precise modern site is assigned here.
