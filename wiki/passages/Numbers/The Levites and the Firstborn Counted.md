# The Levites and the Firstborn Counted

**Book:** [[Numbers]]
**Reference:** Numbers 3:14–51

## Summary

The Levites were left out of the census of chapter 1. Now they get a census of their own, on
different terms. Yahweh tells Moses to count every male from a month old and upward (15), not men of
twenty fit for war. Moses counts them *according to Yahweh's word* (16).

The count runs house by house, and each house gets the same entry: its clans, its number, its side of
the tabernacle, its prince, and its charge.

| House | Clans | Count | Side | Prince | Charge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gershon | Libnites, Shimeites | 7,500 (22) | west (23) | [[Eliasaph (son of Lael)]] (24) | the tent, coverings, screens, hangings, cords (25–26) |
| Kohath | Amramites, Izharites, Hebronites, Uzzielites | 8,600 (28) | south (29) | [[Elzaphan (son of Uzziel)]] (30) | ark, table, lampstand, altars, vessels, screen (31) |
| Merari | Mahlites, Mushites | 6,200 (34) | north (35) | [[Zuriel (son of Abihail)]] (35) | boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, cords (36–37) |

[[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] stands over all three princes (32). The east, facing the door of the Tent,
belongs to Moses, Aaron, and Aaron's sons, and the sentence of 3:10 comes back there: *The stranger who
comes near shall be put to death* (38). The total is 22,000 (39).

Then the second count. Moses numbers every firstborn male of Israel, also from a month old (40, 42),
and the figure is 22,273 (43). Yahweh takes the Levites instead of those firstborn, and the Levites'
livestock instead of Israel's firstborn livestock (41, 45). That leaves 273 firstborn with no Levite
to stand for them. Each is redeemed for five shekels (46–47), and the 1,365 shekels go to Aaron and his
sons (48–51). The unit ends with the command carried out to the letter (51).

So what 3:11–13 stated as a principle is here done as arithmetic. A claim made on the night of the
Passover is settled name by name, and where people run out, silver makes up the difference. The camp
has also become complete: the tribes on the outside, three Levite houses inside them, and the priests
at the door.

## Notes

**Boundary.** The plan's break holds. Verse 14 opens a new command to count, and verse 51 closes on
the fulfillment formula. Chapter 4 opens with a different count, of working adults.

**From a month old.** *Mibben-ḥōdeš wāmāʿlâ* (WLC) stands at 15, 22, 28, 34, 39, 40, and 43. Chapter 1
counted fighting men from twenty. The text gives no reason for the change. The firstborn are counted
from the same age, and 18:16 sets the redemption from a month old, so the two lists could be laid side
by side, but that purpose is the wiki's inference. Leviticus 27:6 also starts a price bracket at a
month.

**One root through the whole unit.** The root of *count* is *pāqad*. Verbal and noun forms stand at 15,
16, 22, 32, 34, 36, 39, 40, 42, and 43 (WLC, by Strong's tags). At 32 and 36 WEB renders the noun
*pəquddâ* as *oversight* and *appointed duty*, so the word that counts a man also assigns him. That is
a note on the Hebrew, and the text makes no point of it.

**Moses, or Moses and Aaron?** Verses 16 and 42 have Moses alone. Verse 39 reads *whom Moses and Aaron
counted*, but the verb *pāqad* is singular, and in the WLC the word *wəʾahărōn* carries the dots
scribes place above certain words, the *puncta extraordinaria*. A singular verb before a compound
subject is not unusual, so it proves little by itself. Rashi on 3:39 (Rosenbaum–Silbermann, Sefaria,
citing Bekhorot 4a) reads the dots as saying Aaron was not among those numbered. Other witnesses are
reported to lack the name, which was not checked here. The wiki leaves WEB's reading in place.

**The sum does not add up.** The three houses come to 22,300, and verse 39 says 22,000. The Hebrew of
22, 28, 34, and 39 has the same figures as WEB (WLC). The smaller number is the one the exchange uses:
22,273 less 22,000 is the 273 of verse 46. See the tensions below and
[[Twenty-Two Thousand - The Levite Sums in Numbers 3, 4, and 26]].

**Exceeding.** The 273 are *hāʿōdpîm*, *those who exceed* (46, 48, 49, WLC). The verb *ʿādap* elsewhere
in the Pentateuch is the extra manna of Exodus 16:18, 23, the surplus curtain of Exodus 26:12–13, and
the balance owed at Leviticus 25:27. It is an accounting word, not a sacral one.

**Redemption words.** *Pədûyê* (46, 48, 49), *kesep happidyôm* (49), and *kesep happəduyim* (51) are
all from *pādâ* (WLC). The noun *pidyôn/pidyôm* occurs only here, at Exodus 21:30 (the owner of the
goring ox) and at Hebrew Psalm 49:9 (WEB 49:8), by Strong's tag. See
[[I Have Found a Ransom - Job 33.24, Psalm 49, and Mark 10.45]].

**Per skull.** WEB's *apiece* is *laggulgōlet*, *for the skull* (47, WLC), the word of the census at
Numbers 1:2, 18, 20, 22 and of the manna at Exodus 16:16. The half-shekel of Exodus 38:26 has the same
phrase.

**Who were the 273?** The text does not say how they were picked from the rest, and it does not say
whether they or their families paid. Verse 50 says only that the money was taken *from the firstborn*.

**Same price for all ages.** Verse 47 sets one price for every one of the 273, though the count ran from
a month old with no upper limit. In the table of [[Vows, Valuations, and Devoted Things]], five shekels
is the value of a male of a month to five years old, and a grown man is valued at fifty (Leviticus
27:3, 6). No text connects them. See [[Five Shekels a Head - Numbers 3, Numbers 18, and Leviticus 27]].

**The livestock.** Verses 41 and 45 exchange the animals as well, with no count and no surplus. The
text gives no accounting for them, and Exodus 13:12–13 is not said to lapse.

**Princes on the sides.** Moses and Aaron are Amramites, of the same house as the south side, but they
camp apart at the door. Eleazar's oversight at 32 is general. Numbers 4 will set Gershon and Merari
under Ithamar (4:28, 33).

**Elzaphan the prince.** The prince of Kohath is *ʾĕlîṣāpān* at 3:30, the name written *ʾelṣāpān* at
Exodus 6:22 and Leviticus 10:4 (WLC). Aaron's cousin, who carried Nadab and Abihu out of the sanctuary now
leads the house that carries the ark. That is the wiki's observation, and the text makes no comment.

**Korah's neighbors.** Kohath camps on the south (29), and so does Reuben (2:10). Numbers 16:1 will put
Korah the Izharite with Dathan, Abiram, and On of Reuben. Rashi on 16:1 (Rosenbaum–Silbermann, Sefaria)
draws that line, and adds from Midrash Tanchuma that Korah envied Elzaphan's appointment. Elzaphan's
father Uzziel is the youngest of the four at 3:19, and Korah's father Izhar is the second. Numbers says
none of this.

**Tensions left standing.**

1. **22,300 or 22,000?** The parts exceed the total by 300, and 300 is more than the 273 who had to pay.
   Rashi on 3:39, citing Bekhorot 5a, says the 300 were themselves firstborn and could not stand for
   others. The text does not say so. An error in transmission at 3:28, *šēš* for *šālōš*, has also been
   proposed, on the report of a Greek reading; that report was not checked. The WLC gives 8,600. See
   [[Twenty-Two Thousand - The Levite Sums in Numbers 3, 4, and 26]].
2. **The number of firstborn.** With 603,550 men of twenty and up in chapter 1, 22,273 firstborn males of
   all ages is about one for every twenty-seven grown men, and fewer still against all males. The text does not address it. [[The Census in the Wilderness of Sinai]] and
   [[Six Hundred Three Thousand Five Hundred Fifty - Exodus 38.26 and Numbers 1.46]] carry the wider
   question.
3. **So few Levites.** All Levi, infants included, is smaller than the smallest tribe's fighting men
   alone (Manasseh, 32,200, 1:35). No reason is given.
4. **Redeemed, or replaced?** This unit does both at once: the exchange for 22,000, and money for 273.
   See [[In Place of All the Firstborn - Exodus 13 and Numbers 3, 8, and 18]].
