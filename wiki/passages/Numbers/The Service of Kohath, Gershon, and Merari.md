# The Service of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari

**Book:** [[Numbers]]
**Reference:** Numbers 4:1–49

## Summary

Chapter 3 counted the Levites from a month old, to set them against the firstborn. This census counts
only the men who can do the work, from thirty to fifty (3, 23, 30), and its subject is how the
tabernacle travels. A sanctuary that stands at the center of the camp also has to be taken apart,
wrapped, and lifted, and this chapter says who does each part of that.

The order changes. [[Gershon]] was first in 3:17, but [[Kohath]] comes first here, because Kohath has
*the most holy things* (4). Yet Kohath's men do not begin the work. When the camp is ready to move,
[[Aaron]] and his sons go in, take down the veil, and wrap each holy object themselves (5–14). Only
then do the Kohathites come to carry, and they must not touch: *lest they die* (15).
[[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] has charge of the oil, the incense, the continual meal offering, the
anointing oil, and the oversight of the whole tabernacle (16).

Then comes a second speech, and it is the heart of the unit. Yahweh tells Moses and Aaron not to let
the Kohathites be cut off (18). The holy things that are their job could kill them, so the priests
must go in and assign each man his task and his load, and the Kohathites may not go in to look even
for a moment (19–20).

Gershon's men carry the fabric, the coverings, the screens, and the hangings of the court (24–26), and
[[Merari]]'s carry the boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, and cords (31–32). Both work under
[[Ithamar]] (28, 33). Moses, Aaron, and the princes then take the count (34–49):

| House | Count, 30 to 50 | From a month old (Numbers 3) | Load | Over them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kohath | 2,750 (36) | 8,600 | the most holy things, already wrapped (4–15) | Aaron and his sons; Eleazar's oversight (15–16, 19) |
| Gershon | 2,630 (40) | 7,500 | the fabric of tent and court (25–26) | Ithamar (28) |
| Merari | 3,200 (44) | 6,200 | the frame and its fittings (31–32) | Ithamar (33) |
| Total | 8,580 (48) | 22,000 stated | | |

Here the parts add up to the total exactly. Each man is counted *according to his service, and
according to his burden* (49). The camp that chapters 1–3 arranged at rest is now ready to move.

- [[Aaron]] — goes in with his sons to cover the holy things and assign the men (5, 15, 19).
- [[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] — has the oils, the incense, the meal offering, and the oversight (16).
- [[Ithamar]] — Gershon and Merari work under his hand (28, 33).

- [[Kohath]], [[Gershon]], [[Merari]] — the three houses and their loads.

## Places

The unit names no place. The setting is still the camp at Sinai, and the Tent of Meeting is the center
of the action.

## Themes

- [[Holiness]] — the holy things kill by touch and by sight, and the law is built to keep the bearers
  alive (15, 18–20).
- [[Priesthood]] — the priests alone handle the holy things, and they answer for the men who carry them
  (5, 19, 27).
- [[The Dwelling of God]] — the dwelling is made to move, and the chapter says how (5–15, 25–26, 31–32).
- [[Work]] — *service* and *burden*, down to each man's own load and to named instruments (19, 32, 49).
- [[Law and Obedience]] — counted *according to the commandment of Yahweh* (37, 41, 45, 49).
- [[Seeing and Being Seen]] — the sanctuary may not be looked at even for a moment (20).
- [[Life and Death]] — *that they may live, and not die* (19).

## Notes

**Boundary.** The plan's break holds. Chapter 5 opens a new subject, and 4:49 closes the count with a
fulfillment formula.

**Service as an army word.** *Enter into the service* (3) is *laṣṣābāʾ* (WLC), the noun that means
*army* or *host*. Numbers 1:3 uses the same noun for those who go out to war. It stands six times in
this chapter (3, 23, 30, 35, 39, 43), and verse 23 adds the cognate verb, *liṣbōʾ ṣābāʾ*. That verb is
rare, and Exodus 38:8 uses it of the women who served at the Tent's door. So the Levites are mustered
much as the tribes were, though for the sanctuary and with a different age limit. The comparison is the
wiki's. Their other words are *ʿăbōdâ*, *service* (twenty times in the chapter), and *maśśāʾ*, *burden*
(nine times), counted by Strong's tags.

**How each thing is wrapped.** The order, from the text:

| Object | Next to it | Over that | Outermost | Then (verse) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ark | the veil | sealskin | **blue cloth** | poles (5–6) |
| Table | blue cloth, with its vessels and the bread on it | scarlet cloth | sealskin | poles (7–8) |
| Lampstand | blue cloth | | sealskin | frame (9–10) |
| Golden altar | blue cloth | | sealskin | poles (11) |
| Vessels of ministry | blue cloth | | sealskin | frame (12) |
| The altar | purple cloth, with its vessels on it | | sealskin | poles (13–14) |

Everything except the ark travels under sealskin. The ark alone shows blue on the outside, so on the
march it would be the one object that looked different. Only the outer altar has purple, and only the
table has scarlet. The text gives no reason for any of this, and the meaning of the colors is the
reader's to supply. The Hebrew for the ark's cloth is *beged kəlîl təkēlet*, *a cloth all of blue* (6,
WLC). *Kəlîl* with *blue* occurs elsewhere only for the high priest's robe (Exodus 28:31; 39:22), by
Strong's tag. The link is the wiki's observation, and the text draws none.

**The veil wraps the ark.** At Exodus 26:33 the veil divides the holy place from the most holy. Here,
when the camp moves, it comes down and is the first thing laid on the ark (5). What hid the ark from
the room now hides it from the road. Only Aaron and his sons handle it, so even the taking down keeps
the Levites from seeing. That last step is an inference from 15 and 20. See
[[The Veil - Separation in Exodus and Access in Hebrews]].

**The bread stays on the table.** The vessels stay on the table and *the continual bread shall be on it*
(7), so the bread that is always before Yahweh keeps its place on the road. *Leḥem hattāmîd* is the
phrase (WLC).

**The basin is missing.** The chapter does not list the bronze basin of Exodus 30:18, and no Hebrew word
for it stands here (WLC). The text gives no reason. The Greek is reported to add one at verse 14, which
was not checked.

**Eleazar's list.** Verse 16 gives him the oil for the light, the sweet incense, the continual meal
offering, and the anointing oil, with *the requirements of all the tabernacle*. His word is *pəquddâ*
(WLC), the same noun as at 3:32, so this may mean oversight rather than a load. The verse does not say
he carries them.

**Kohath's priests.** The text names Ithamar over Gershon and Merari (28, 33) and names no such man over
Kohath. Aaron and his sons assign them instead (19), and Eleazar's *oversight* is general (3:32; 4:16).

**A moment.** WEB's *even for a moment* is *kəballaʿ* (20, WLC), *as a swallowing*, from *bālaʿ*,
*to swallow*. The sense of this phrase is debated, and other readings were not checked here, so WEB's
stands. In the rest of the Pentateuch *bālaʿ* is the lean ears and Aaron's rod (Genesis 41:7, 24; Exodus
7:12), the earth at the Sea (Exodus 15:12), and the earth under Korah (Numbers 16:30, 32, 34; 26:10;
Deuteronomy 11:6). Korah is a Kohathite. That link is the wiki's, and it depends on a word that is
ordinary in itself.

**One root again.** The root *pāqad* is tagged twenty-two times in the chapter, plus *pəquddâ* twice at
verse 16 (WLC). Verse 49 is hard: WEB's *Thus were they counted by him* renders *ûpəqudāyw*, a form the
Hebrew leaves abrupt. The wiki keeps WEB's sense.

**Named instruments.** *By name you shall appoint the instruments* (32). Merari's men are the only house
given this rule, and it falls on pins and cords, not on the ark. The text does not explain it.

**What was left to the priests.** *Don't cut off* (18) is said to Moses and Aaron, and the danger is the
Kohathites'. If the priests do not do their part, the bearers die. So the rule that protects the holy
things also protects the men near them. That reading is the wiki's, but verse 19 states its aim:
*that they may live, and not die*.

**Korah ahead.** The Kohathites may carry but not touch, and may not look. In chapter 16 a Kohathite
leads the claim that all the congregation is holy (16:3), and Moses answers, *Is it a small thing to
you…? Do you seek the priesthood also?* (16:9–10). The text does not link the two chapters.

**Tensions left standing.**

1. **The poles.** Exodus 25:15 says of the ark's poles, *They shall not be taken from it*. Here Aaron's
   sons *put in its poles* (6) after wrapping it. The Hebrew is *wəśāmû baddāyw* (WLC), the same phrase
   used for the table and altars. Perhaps they are adjusted rather than inserted, or perhaps the covers
   go under them, but the text says neither. See [[The Ark, the Table, and the Lampstand]].
2. **Thirty or twenty-five?** Here Levites serve from thirty, and Numbers 8:24 says twenty-five. The
   texts are not reconciled. See [[Thirty, Twenty-Five, and Twenty - The Age of Levite Service]].
3. **Who carries the ark?** Here the Kohathites carry, while the priests only wrap. Later texts have
   priests bearing it. See [[Lest They Die - The Ark Carried and the Ark Looked Into]].
4. **The order of the march.** This chapter puts Kohath first, and Numbers 10:17–21 sends Gershon and
   Merari ahead of Kohath. See [[As They Encamp, So They Set Out - Numbers 2.17 and 10.14-28]], which
   records a separate difference over 2:17.
5. **So few men.** Only 8,580 men carry the whole tabernacle, while chapter 1 counts 603,550. The text
   does not address it, and the wiki keeps the figures as written.
