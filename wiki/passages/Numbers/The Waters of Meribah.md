# The Waters of Meribah

**Book:** [[Numbers]]
**Reference:** Numbers 20:1–13

## Summary

At Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, Miriam dies and is buried. A water shortage brings the
congregation against Moses and Aaron: they wish they had died with their brothers and accuse
Moses of bringing them out of Egypt into a place without crops or drinking water (1–5).

The two leaders fall on their faces at the Tent, where Yahweh’s glory appears. Moses is to
take the rod, assemble the congregation with Aaron, and speak to the rock before them (6–8).
He takes the rod as commanded, but at the rock he addresses the people as rebels and strikes
twice. Abundant water comes for the congregation and livestock (9–11).

The miracle does not settle the question of obedience. Yahweh tells both men that they failed
to believe in him and sanctify him before Israel: they will not bring this assembly into the
land he has given it (12). The concluding name, the waters of Meribah, remembers Israel’s
contention with Yahweh and his being sanctified there (13).

## Key verses

WEB, copied from the stored Numbers source.

> **1** The children of Israel, even the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month. The people stayed in Kadesh. Miriam died there, and was buried there.

> **8** “Take the rod, and assemble the congregation, you, and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before their eyes, that it pour out its water. You shall bring water to them out of the rock; so you shall give the congregation and their livestock drink.”

> **10** Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly together before the rock, and he said to them, “Hear now, you rebels! Shall we bring water out of this rock for you?”

> **11** Moses lifted up his hand, and struck the rock with his rod twice, and water came out abundantly. The congregation and their livestock drank.

> **12** Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, “Because you didn’t believe in me, to sanctify me in the eyes of the children of Israel, therefore you shall not bring this assembly into the land which I have given them.”

> **13** These are the waters of Meribah; because the children of Israel strove with Yahweh, and he was sanctified in them.

## Notes

### What the verdict does and does not explain

The contrast between speaking and striking is visible in the WEB itself. Yahweh’s stated
charge is unbelief and failure to sanctify him (12). Whether the decisive fault is the blows,
the address to the people, the apparent claim of agency in verse 10, or their combination
is interpretation; the narrator does not isolate one of them as the exhaustive explanation.
Aaron is judged too, although only Moses is said to strike. The passage supplies no separate
account of Aaron’s offense. Verse 13’s affirmation of Yahweh’s sanctification stands beside,
not in place of, verse 12’s charge against the leaders.

### Time, place, and narrative restraint

Verse 1 gives the first month without a year. Miriam’s death precedes the water shortage,
but the text does not say that her death caused it. The rod taken from before Yahweh (9)
may recall Numbers 17, but this passage does not explicitly call it Aaron’s budding rod.
The Meribah at Kadesh is kept distinct from [[Massah and Meribah]] at Rephidim; following
the existing place convention, no second Meribah place page is created.
