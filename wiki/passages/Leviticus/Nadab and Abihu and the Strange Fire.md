# Nadab and Abihu and the Strange Fire

**Book:** [[Leviticus]]
**Reference:** Leviticus 10:1–11

## Summary

Nadab and Abihu each put fire and incense in a censer and offer before Yahweh what he has
not commanded. Fire from before Yahweh devours them. Moses explains the event to Aaron
through Yahweh’s holiness and glory; Aaron holds his peace. At Moses’ command, Mishael
and Elzaphan carry their relatives out of the camp in their coats.

Aaron, Eleazar, and Ithamar must remain at the Tent of Meeting and refrain from specified
mourning gestures because Yahweh’s anointing oil is on them. The congregation may mourn.
Yahweh then speaks directly to Aaron: priests must avoid wine and strong drink when entering
the tent, distinguish sacred and common and clean and unclean, and teach Israel his statutes.

## Key verses

> **10:1** Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, each took his censer, and put fire in it, and laid incense on it, and offered strange fire before Yahweh, which he had not commanded them.

> **10:2** Fire came out from before Yahweh, and devoured them, and they died before Yahweh.

> **10:3** Then Moses said to Aaron, “This is what Yahweh spoke of, saying, ‘I will show myself holy to those who come near me, and before all the people I will be glorified.’” Aaron held his peace.

> **10:4** Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron, and said to them, “Draw near, carry your brothers from before the sanctuary out of the camp.”

> **10:5** So they came near, and carried them in their coats out of the camp, as Moses had said.

> **10:6** Moses said to Aaron, and to Eleazar and to Ithamar, his sons, “Don’t let the hair of your heads go loose, and don’t tear your clothes; so that you don’t die, and so that he not be angry with all the congregation; but let your brothers, the whole house of Israel, bewail the burning which Yahweh has kindled.

> **10:7** You shall not go out from the door of the Tent of Meeting, lest you die; for the anointing oil of Yahweh is on you.” They did according to the word of Moses.

> **10:8** Then Yahweh said to Aaron,

> **10:9** “You and your sons are not to drink wine or strong drink whenever you go into the Tent of Meeting, or you will die. This shall be a statute forever throughout your generations.

> **10:10** You are to make a distinction between the holy and the common, and between the unclean and the clean.

> **10:11** You are to teach the children of Israel all the statutes which Yahweh has spoken to them by Moses.”

## Notes

### What made the fire strange?

Verse 1 identifies the defect as absence of Yahweh’s command. It does not identify the source
of the coals, an altered incense recipe, or the precise location within the sanctuary.
Exodus 30:9 forbids strange incense, but that does not prove the brothers changed the recipe.
The warning about drink follows the deaths (8–9); it does not state that they were drunk.
These possible explanations must remain interpretations rather than reported facts.

### Silence, mourning, and continuing service

Aaron’s silence does not tell us whether he inwardly assents, grieves, or is stunned.
Moses restricts the surviving priests’ gestures and departure while expressly allowing
Israel to mourn. The contrast concerns different responsibilities within the same bereavement.
The bodies are carried out in their coats (5); the text does not narrate their burial here.
The word “brothers” in Moses’ instruction concerns kin: verse 4 calls the carriers sons of
Aaron’s uncle, not sons of Aaron.

### Distinction and teaching

The final speech expands the picture of priesthood beyond altar work. Abstention is tied to
entry into the tent, not stated as a lifelong ban on all drinking. Holy/common and
unclean/clean are separately named pairs. Verse 11 joins discernment to teaching the statutes
given through Moses. The death warning accompanies the entry rule; it should not be
silently converted into an independently stated penalty for every teaching error.
