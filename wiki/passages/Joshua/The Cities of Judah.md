# The Cities of Judah

**Book:** [[Joshua]]
**Reference:** Joshua 15:20–63

## Summary

After the border circuit and Caleb's household story, Judah's inheritance is given a second
time, as a register of towns. The list moves through five sections: the far south toward
Edom (21–32), the lowland (33–44), the Philistine plain from Ekron to the brook of Egypt
(45–47), the hill country (48–60), and the wilderness (61–62). Most groups close with a count
— *fourteen cities with their villages*, *sixteen*, *nine* — so the list is arranged and totaled,
not merely gathered. It ends not with a total but with an exception: the children of Judah
*couldn’t drive out* the Jebusites of Jerusalem, who *live with the children of Judah at
Jerusalem to this day* (63).

## People
- [[Judah]] — *the tribe of the children of Judah according to their families* (20) receives the towns, and *the children of Judah* fail to drive out Jerusalem's Jebusites (63). The tribe, not the patriarch, is meant throughout.

## Themes
- [[Land and Sojourning]] — The inheritance is now named town by town, *with their villages*, and closes on a city not possessed (63). Allotment and occupation stay distinct: Ekron, Ashdod, and Gaza are assigned (45–47) though 13:2–3 counted them among the land that remains.
- [[Names and Naming]] — The list carries its own glosses: Kerioth Hezron *also called Hazor* (25), *Gederah (or Gederothaim)* (36), *Kiriath Sannah (which is Debir)* (49), *Kiriath Arba (also called Hebron)* (54), *Kiriath Baal (also called Kiriath Jearim)* (60). The last makes three names for one town across the chapter (9, 60).
- [[The Nations]] — The Jebusites of Jerusalem are the only people named in the unit, and they are named as remaining, living *with the children of Judah* (63).

## Notes

**A register, read slowly.** The unit has no actor but the tribe and no event but the failure
of its last verse. What it offers instead is order. The districts run south, lowland, coast,
hills, wilderness, and within each the groups are counted. The towns Joshua's southern
campaign took — Libnah, Lachish, Eglon, Makkedah, Hebron, Debir — now reappear as entries in
an inheritance, folded in without comment among dozens of places the narrative never visited.
Conquest becomes property by being written down.

**The count that does not match.** Verse 32 totals the southern group as *twenty-nine*, but
the WEB lists thirty-six names. Every other counted group adds up exactly, including the
fourteen of 33–36, which treats *Gederah (or Gederothaim)* as one entry. Common explanations
— that some pairs are compound names (*Ain, and Rimmon* is the usual candidate), or that towns
later given to Simeon (19:1–9) were added to an older list — are inferences, and the text
itself gives neither. The wiki records the difference without choosing.

**Simeon inside Judah.** Many of the southern names reappear in Simeon's lot — Beersheba,
Moladah, Hazar Shual, Ezem, Eltolad, Hormah, Ziklag, Ain, Rimmon — along with Ether and Ashan
from the lowland. Joshua 19:9 explains why: Judah's portion *was too much for them*. The
list here gives no hint of it; the comparison belongs to
[[Simeon's Lot Within Judah - Joshua 15 and 19]].

**Assigned, not held.** Ekron, Ashdod, and Gaza are given to Judah with their towns and
villages (45–47), though 11:22 left Anakim in Gaza and Ashdod and 13:3 named all three
peoples among the land that remains. The Philistine section is the only district with no
count, which fits a territory claimed rather than settled, though the text gives no reason
for the omission. Verse 63 then says outright what the rest of the list implies: a city
inside Judah's border, whose king Joshua killed (10:26), is still Jebusite *to this day*.

**Names, kept apart.** The list repeats names within itself — two Ziphs (24, 55), two Socohs
(35, 48), two Zanoahs (34, 56), two Ashnahs (33, 43) — and shares names with places elsewhere
in the wiki: Kedesh, Hazor, Tappuah, Timnah, Carmel, Mizpah, Rabbah, Naamah, Ain, Gibeah,
Jezreel, Beth Dagon, En Gannim. Each new page is qualified rather than merged, and states what
it is not identified with. Where the wiki already had a page for the same town in the same
district — Jarmuth, Adullam, Azekah, Lachish, Eglon, Makkedah, Libnah, Hebron, Debir — the
entry is added to it. *Enam* (34) and *Achzib* (44) are filed on the existing [[Enaim]] and
[[Chezib]] pages, which already proposed those identifications; both remain proposals.
*Kiriath Sannah (which is Debir)* (49) stands beside verse 15's *Kiriath Sepher* without
reconciliation.
