# Joshua's Own Inheritance

**Book:** [[Joshua]]
**Reference:** Joshua 19:49–51

## Summary

After Israel finishes distributing the land by its borders, the people give Joshua an
inheritance among them. He receives the city he requested, Timnathserah in Ephraim’s hill
country, according to Yahweh’s command; he builds it and lives there. The closing summary
names Eleazar, Joshua, and the tribal household heads as the distributors by lot before
Yahweh at the Tent of Meeting’s door in Shiloh.

The leader receives a place within the inheritance he has administered. His personal
grant concludes the tribal lists, while the final verse returns attention to shared
administration and the sanctuary setting.

## Key verses

Full pericope, WEB, copied from the stored source:

> **49** So they finished distributing the land for inheritance by its borders. The children of Israel gave an inheritance to Joshua the son of Nun among them.

> **50** According to Yahweh’s commandment, they gave him the city which he asked, even Timnathserah in the hill country of Ephraim; and he built the city, and lived there.

> **51** These are the inheritances, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers’ houses of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for inheritance by lot in Shiloh before Yahweh, at the door of the Tent of Meeting. So they finished dividing the land.

## People
- [[Joshua (son of Nun)]] — Israel gives Joshua the city he requests under Yahweh’s command (49–50). He builds it and lives there; the distributor is also a recipient among the people. Verse 51 names him with Eleazar and the tribal household heads.
- [[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] — Named first among the distributors in the closing summary (51), Eleazar shares responsibility with Joshua and the tribal household heads. The allotment takes place before Yahweh at the Tent’s door in Shiloh.
- [[Nun]] — Named as Joshua’s father (49, 51); the patronymic identifies Joshua without assigning Nun any action or presence.
- [[God]] — Joshua’s city is granted according to Yahweh’s command (50), and the division by lot takes place before Yahweh (51). No new divine speech is quoted or visible manifestation reported.
- [[Ephraim]] — The name locates Joshua’s requested city in the hill country (50), within the tribal geography. The patriarch is not a living participant; no new tribal boundary is drawn.

## Places
- [[Shiloh]] — The closing allotment summary locates the lot before Yahweh at the door of the Tent of Meeting here (51), confirming the sanctuary setting introduced in Joshua 18:1–10.
- [[The Hill Country of Ephraim]] — Timnathserah, Joshua’s requested and granted city, is located here (50). He builds and inhabits it; the verse supplies neither a modern site nor an expanded boundary for the region.
- [[Timnathserah]] — Joshua’s requested city, granted, built, and inhabited (50).

## Themes
- [[Land and Sojourning]] — Completed distribution frames Joshua’s own inheritance among Israel (49–51). The closing formula concerns allocation; it does not cancel the remaining-land report of Joshua 13:1 or the non-removal notices of 16:10 and 17:12–13.
- [[Law and Obedience]] — Israel grants Joshua his requested city according to Yahweh’s command (50). The passage asserts that authority without quoting an earlier instruction naming Timnathserah.
- [[Priesthood]] — Eleazar’s priestly role includes shared responsibility for dividing the land with Joshua and the tribal household heads (51). Priestly participation does not make the grant a unilateral priestly decision.
- [[Providence]] — The summary attributes tribal distribution to the lot before Yahweh (51). Joshua’s own grant is described as a requested city given by command (50); the text does not separately narrate a lot selecting his city.
- [[The Dwelling of God]] — The Tent’s door at Shiloh is the setting of distribution before Yahweh (51). Sanctuary and public administration meet without a report of a new glory manifestation or a stone temple.
- [[Work]] — Joshua builds the city he receives and lives there (50). Gift and labor belong together; the verse does not specify whether the work is new construction, rebuilding, or fortification.

## Notes

**What is finished.** The completed division in verses 49 and 51 must be read beside the
remaining land of Joshua 13:1 and the Canaanites not driven out in 16:10 and 17:12–13.
Those claims remain in tension with any reading of completion as universal occupation.
Here the stated activity being finished is distribution, not a final military campaign.

**Command and request.** Verse 50 grounds the grant in Yahweh’s command without quoting
its wording or dating its delivery. It does not identify an earlier speech naming
Timnathserah. Nor does the closing reference to lots establish a separate lot for Joshua’s
requested city. His last position in the list can suggest restrained leadership, but
the text does not explicitly give that motive.

**People and place names.** Nun is a patronymic, Ephraim a geographical/tribal designation,
and the unnamed household heads remain a collective. No individual biographies are
invented for that group. The city name follows the stored WEB spelling. No modern
identification, Hebrew lexical claim, or unstored-book quotation is introduced.

**Sources.** Quotations are copied from stored WEB Joshua 19:49–51. Comparisons use
stored Joshua 14:1–15; 18:1–10 and Numbers 34:17–18. The remaining-land and non-removal
notices are referenced as qualifications, not quoted from memory.
