# The Land That Remains

**Book:** [[Joshua]]
**Reference:** Joshua 13:1–7

## Summary

Yahweh addresses the aged Joshua and declares that very much land remains to be possessed.
The inventory begins with Philistine and Geshurite regions, names the five Philistine
lordships and the Avvim, then turns to northern territory associated with the Sidonians,
the Amorite border, the Gebalites, Lebanon, Hermon, and the entrance of Hamath (1–5).

The promise of future removal accompanies a present command: Joshua must allocate this land
to Israel as an inheritance (6). The recipients are nine tribes and half of Manasseh (7).
The unit therefore begins the allotment section with a distinction between assigning an
inheritance and already occupying all of it. Joshua's age limits his remaining time, but
does not cancel his responsibility.

## Key verses

> **13:1** Now Joshua was old and well advanced in years. Yahweh said to him, “You are old and advanced in years, and there remains yet very much land to be possessed.

> **13:2** “This is the land that still remains: all the regions of the Philistines, and all the Geshurites;

> **13:3** from the Shihor, which is before Egypt, even to the border of Ekron northward, which is counted as Canaanite; the five lords of the Philistines; the Gazites, and the Ashdodites, the Ashkelonites, the Gittites, and the Ekronites; also the Avvim,

> **13:4** on the south; all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah that belongs to the Sidonians, to Aphek, to the border of the Amorites;

> **13:5** and the land of the Gebalites, and all Lebanon, toward the sunrise, from Baal Gad under Mount Hermon to the entrance of Hamath;

> **13:6** all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth Maim, even all the Sidonians. I will drive them out from before the children of Israel. Just allocate it to Israel for an inheritance, as I have commanded you.

> **13:7** Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance to the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”

## Notes

**Completion and remaining land.** [[The Whole Land Taken]], Joshua 11:23, reports the
whole land taken and rest from war. Here Yahweh states that very much land remains.
Distinguishing the defeat of regional armies from comprehensive settlement helps explain
the juxtaposition, but the book does not explicitly make that distinction its explanation.
Both claims stand. Northern pursuit to Sidon and Misrephoth Maim in Joshua 11:8 does not itself
report occupation of every place reached.

**Promise and allocation.** Verse 6 joins Yahweh's future removal to Joshua's immediate duty
to allocate. Numbers 33:50–56, in [[Drive Out the Inhabitants]], had joined removal, possession,
and tribal allotment; this speech makes clear that distribution need not await completed
occupation. Exodus 23:29–30 and Deuteronomy 7:22 allow gradual removal, but Joshua 13:1–7
does not give the ecological reason supplied there, or accuse Joshua of disobedience.
His age is explicit; a numerical age or date for this speech is not.

**Names without forced identifications.** The Geshurites beside the Philistine regions (2)
are recorded on [[Geshur]] separately from the Bashan-border notices in Joshua 12:5 and
13:11–13. The text does not establish that they are the same population.
[[Aphek]] likewise holds the northern boundary notice apart from the king list's Aphek;
a shared name does not demonstrate a shared site. These remain unresolved identifications,
not asserted mergers. The Shihor is not silently identified with the Nile or the brook of
Egypt. No modern site identification or original-language etymology is supplied.

**Collective groups.** The five lords have no personal names in this unit and receive no
individual biographies. Their cities carry the references. The Avvim, Philistines,
Canaanites, Amorites, Sidonians, Geshurites, and Gebalites are treated through the places
and [[The Nations]]. Manasseh in verse 7 is a tribal designation, not an appearance of the
patriarch. Verse 8 begins the separate account of the eastern inheritance.

**Sources and boundary.** All seven verses above are copied from stored WEB Joshua 13.
Comparisons use stored WEB Joshua 11:8, 11:23, 12:5, 12:18, 13:11–13; Numbers 33:50–56;
Exodus 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 7:22. The planned 13:1–7 boundary is retained. No quotation
from an unstored book and no source-language claim is introduced.

**The allotment’s verdict.** [[Not One Good Thing Failed]], Joshua 21:43–45, now supplies the later
claim of all land given, possessed, and inhabited, with rest and every good word fulfilled.
The tension with Joshua 13:1 and the non-removal notices remains explicit; distinguishing
decisive victory from exhaustive occupation is an interpretation, not a stated resolution.
