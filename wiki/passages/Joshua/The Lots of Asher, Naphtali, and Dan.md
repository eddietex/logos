# The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan

**Book:** [[Joshua]]
**Reference:** Joshua 19:24–48

## Summary

The fifth, sixth, and seventh lots at Shiloh go to Asher, Naphtali, and Dan, closing the
distribution to the seven tribes still waiting in 18:2. Asher’s border runs along the
northern coast past Carmel, *great Sidon*, and *the fortified city of Tyre* to the sea;
Naphtali’s runs from the oak in Zaanannim to the Jordan, with a list of fortified cities;
Dan’s is a list of towns in the lowland that ends opposite Joppa. Dan alone is reported
taking land outside its lot: the tribe captures Leshem in the far north and renames it Dan.

## People
- [[Asher]] — His descendants receive the fifth lot, a boundary along the northern coast and twenty-two cities with villages (24–31). Genesis 49:20 promised rich food and Deuteronomy 33:24–25 oil and strong bars; the register says nothing about produce. The coast is named here, reaching great Sidon and Tyre, but no conquest of either is narrated.
- [[Naphtali]] — His descendants receive the sixth lot, a border ending at the Jordan and nineteen fortified cities with villages (32–39). Deuteronomy 33:23 commands Naphtali to *possess the west and the south*; the register’s own orientation is mainly west and south of its Jordan edge, but the text draws no link to the blessing.
- [[Dan (person)]] — His descendants receive the seventh and last lot, a list of lowland towns (40–46), then go beyond it and take Leshem, naming it *after the name of Dan their forefather* (47). The patriarch is present only in the name.
- [[Zebulun]] — Named as a neighbor: Asher’s border reaches Zebulun (27), and Naphtali’s reaches Zebulun on the south (34). The tribe is meant, not the patriarch.
- [[Judah]] — Naphtali’s border reaches *to Judah at the Jordan toward the sunrise* (34). Judah’s allotment (15:1–12) lies far to the south, so the phrase does not fit the rest of the book’s geography. The text does not explain it; see Notes.

## Themes
- [[Land and Sojourning]] — The last three lots complete the western distribution. Asher’s and Naphtali’s registers name cities and borders; Dan’s inheritance, uniquely, includes a city it takes by force outside its lot (47). Allotment is still not occupation: Tyre and Sidon are boundary markers, not captures.
- [[Providence]] — The fifth, sixth, and seventh lots *came out* (24, 32, 40), continuing the casting before Yahweh at Shiloh (18:10). The lot fixes each tribe’s portion; Dan’s later expansion is reported as the tribe’s own campaign, not as a further lot.
- [[Names and Naming]] — Leshem becomes Dan, named for the tribe’s forefather (47). Several names repeat across tribal lists — Ramah, Rehob, Jabneel, Beth Shemesh, Edrei, Beth Dagon — and are kept on separate pages unless the text identifies them.
- [[War]] — Dan *fought against Leshem, and took it, and struck it with the edge of the sword* (47) — the first fighting narrated in the allotment chapters since Caleb drove out the sons of Anak and Othniel took Debir (15:14–17). It is reported without a divine command or a word of judgment.

## Notes

**The last three lots.** Joshua 18:2 left seven tribes without an inheritance; with Dan the
seventh lot is cast and verse 49 can say the distribution is finished. The three registers
are uneven in kind. Asher’s is a boundary interleaved with towns; Naphtali’s is a border
followed by a list headed *the fortified cities*; Dan’s gives no border circuit, only towns,
*with the border over against Joppa*.

**Counts.** Asher’s total of twenty-two (30) has to be drawn from more than twenty-two
names, since border landmarks such as Carmel, Sidon, and Tyre are mixed in; the text does not
say which names make up the twenty-two. Naphtali’s list in verses 35–38 has sixteen names
against a stated nineteen, so either border points from verses 33–34 are counted or the
total includes cities not named. Dan’s list has no total. As with Zebulun, the wiki does not
force a counting scheme.

**Naphtali and Judah at the Jordan (34).** The WEB reads *to Judah at the Jordan toward the
sunrise*. Judah’s east border in 15:5 is *the Salt Sea, even to the end of the Jordan*, far to
the south of Naphtali. This page records the difficulty and does not resolve it. It makes no claim
about the Hebrew wording or about variant readings, because none was checked against a
Hebrew edition for this ingest.

**Dan in two places.** Dan’s towns lie in the lowland west of Benjamin and Judah, and several
were already listed for Judah: Zorah and Eshtaol (15:33), Timnah (15:10), Ekron (15:11, 45).
Verse 47 then gives Dan a second, northern home. Joshua reports it in one sentence, as
though complete; Judges 1:34 says the Amorites forced the Danites into the hill country and
would not let them come down to the valley, and Judges 18:1 says their inheritance *had not
fallen to them* — both verses checked against bible-api.com WEB, 2026-11-11, Judges not
added to raw. See [[Leshem Becomes Dan - Joshua 19 and Judges 18]] and
[[Dan's Towns on Judah's Border - Joshua 15 and 19]].

**Blessings compared.** Genesis 49:13 gave Zebulun a border on Sidon; here it is Asher whose
border reaches *great Sidon* (28). That fits the tension the
[[The Lots of Simeon, Zebulun, and Issachar]] page left open, without resolving it: Joshua
gives Sidon to Asher’s line, not Zebulun’s. Genesis 49:17’s serpent and Deuteronomy 33:22’s
lion’s cub *that leaps out of Bashan* are not cited by the narrative of Leshem’s capture; the
northern location of Dan’s city is consistent with a Bashan image, but the text draws no link.
