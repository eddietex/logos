# Get Wisdom

**Book:** [[Proverbs]]
**Reference:** Proverbs 4:1–9

## Summary

The father asks his sons to listen, then remembers being a child himself. His authority
includes a received lesson: his own father taught him to retain words in his heart, keep
commandments, and live (4:1–4). The remembered speech urges the acquisition of wisdom and
understanding, then pictures wisdom as a woman to love, esteem, and embrace. She in turn
preserves, honors, and crowns the learner (4:5–9).

**4:1–4 — The teacher was first a learner.** The address begins with sons in the plural.
The speaker then recalls himself as tender and an only child in his mother's sight; in
verse 4 his father's speech begins. As punctuated in the stored WEB, that quotation
continues through verse 9. Three generations occupy the scene: the present hearers, their
teacher, and the father whose teaching he repeats. The mother belongs to the remembered
household, though this unit quotes the father. Compare the explicit teaching of both
parents in [[Do Not Consent to Sinners]].

The collection's heading names [[Solomon]], son of [[David]], but 4:1–9 names neither.
Reading this as a recollection of David instructing Solomon is possible within that
heading; it is not an identification supplied by these verses. The language about the
mother's only child must not be turned into an independently established royal genealogy.
The passage works as an invitation to receive and transmit wisdom beyond one household.
See [[Wisdom Handed On - Proverbs 4 and Exodus 13]].

**4:5–7 — Acquisition without abandonment.** Getting wisdom is repeated, but the commands
also require not forgetting, not deviating, and not forsaking. Acquisition is a continuing
allegiance rather than a completed lesson. The WEB's verse 7 gives understanding priority
even at the cost of all one's possessions. This develops 3:14–15's ranking of wisdom above
precious goods; it does not specify a seller, a fee, or a literal transaction. Proverbs 2:6
has already described wisdom as Yahweh's gift. Effort, cost, and reception are held together;
see [[Better Than Gold - The Price of Wisdom]].

**4:6–9 — Love answered by protection and honor.** The pupil must love wisdom, not merely
know about her. Esteeming and embracing her are answered by preservation and exaltation.
Wisdom becomes the giver of a garland and crown: the ornament promised for listening to
parents in 1:9 is now placed on the learner by wisdom herself. These are images of honor,
not an account of a coronation or a promise that every pupil will become a king. Wisdom's
personification belongs on [[Wisdom]], rather than on a new historical person's page.

**Tension with Job.** Proverbs 4:6, 8–9 promises preservation, honor, and a crown. In
Job 19:9 the sufferer describes God stripping his glory and taking his crown. Job 1:1
and 2:3 establish his uprightness and continuing integrity. These claims conflict if
wisdom's promised honor is treated as an exceptionless description of present experience.
The wiki reads the exhortation as teaching what deserves lifelong allegiance; this is an
interpretation, not a removal of either the promise or the counterexample. See
[[The Crown Given and Taken - Proverbs 4 and Job 19]].

## Key verses

> **4:1** Listen, sons, to a father’s instruction. Pay attention and know understanding;

> **4:2** for I give you sound learning. Don’t forsake my law.

> **4:3** For I was a son to my father, tender and an only child in the sight of my mother.

> **4:4** He taught me, and said to me: “Let your heart retain my words. Keep my commandments, and live.

> **4:5** Get wisdom. Get understanding. Don’t forget, and don’t deviate from the words of my mouth.

> **4:6** Don’t forsake her, and she will preserve you. Love her, and she will keep you.

> **4:7** Wisdom is supreme. Get wisdom. Yes, though it costs all your possessions, get understanding.

> **4:8** Esteem her, and she will exalt you. She will bring you to honor, when you embrace her.

> **4:9** She will give to your head a garland of grace. She will deliver a crown of splendor to you.”

## People

- The father, his sons, and his remembered father and mother are unnamed. Their roles
  carry the instruction; separate biographical pages would imply identities not given.
- [[Solomon]] and [[David]] — collection-level context from 1:1, not named participants
  established by 4:1–9. Their pages record this limitation.
- Wisdom — personified as protector and giver of honor; see [[Wisdom]].

## Places

No named geographical place. The household is the setting of remembered instruction,
not an identified location requiring a place page.

## Themes

- [[Wisdom]] — acquired, retained, loved, and personified as protector.
- [[Law and Obedience]] — parental instruction received as words to keep and live by.
- [[Remembrance]] — the father recalls teaching and commands the next learner not to forget.
- [[Language and Speech]] — the teacher hands on his father's quoted words.
- [[Love]] — affection and embrace directed toward wisdom.
- [[Life and Death]] — keeping the commands is linked to life and preservation.
- [[Wealth and Possessions]] — understanding takes priority over possessions in the WEB.
- [[Favor and Grace]] — the garland is an image of gracious adornment and honor.
- [[Retribution]] — the promised honor remains in tension with Job's stripped crown.

## Notes

**Boundary.** The planned 4:1–9 unit is retained. The WEB closes the recalled father's
quotation at 4:9; a renewed address to the son at 4:10 begins the next instruction. The
following pericope has not been ingested in this pass.

**Sources and limits.** All nine verses above are copied from `raw/bible/Proverbs.md`.
Comparisons were checked in the stored WEB: Proverbs 1:8–9; 2:4–6; 3:1–3, 13–18, 21–35;
Exodus 13:8–16; 20:12; Job 1:1; 2:3; 19:6–10. Discussion of verse 7 follows the WEB's
rendering; no independent Hebrew translation or exact source-language assertion is made.
The father-to-son comparison and the opposed crowns are thematic readings, not claims
that one passage directly quotes the other. No unstored-book quotation is added.
