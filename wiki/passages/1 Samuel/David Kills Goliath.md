# David Kills Goliath

**Book:** [[1 Samuel]]
**Reference:** 1 Samuel 17:41–54

## Summary

> **41** The Philistine walked and came near to David; and the man who bore the shield went before him.
> **42** When the Philistine looked around, and saw David, he disdained him; for he was but a youth, and ruddy, and
> had a good looking face.
> **43** The Philistine said to David, “Am I a dog, that you come to me with sticks?” The Philistine cursed David by
> his gods.
> **44** The Philistine said to David, “Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the sky, and to the
> animals of the field.”
> **45** Then David said to the Philistine, “You come to me with a sword, with a spear, and with a javelin; but I come
> to you in the name of Yahweh of Armies, the God of the armies of Israel, whom you have defied.
> **46** Today, Yahweh will deliver you into my hand. I will strike you, and take your head from off you. I will give
> the dead bodies of the army of the Philistines today to the birds of the sky, and to the wild animals of the earth;
> that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel,
> **47** and that all this assembly may know that Yahweh doesn’t save with sword and spear; for the battle is
> Yahweh’s, and he will give you into our hand.”
> **48** When the Philistine arose, and walked and came near to meet David, David hurried, and ran toward the army to
> meet the Philistine.
> **49** David put his hand in his bag, took a stone, and slung it, and struck the Philistine in his forehead. The
> stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the earth.
> **50** So David prevailed over the Philistine with a sling and with a stone, and struck the Philistine, and killed
> him; but there was no sword in the hand of David.
> **51** Then David ran, stood over the Philistine, took his sword, drew it out of its sheath, killed him, and cut off
> his head with it. When the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled.
> **52** The men of Israel and of Judah arose and shouted, and pursued the Philistines as far as Gai and to the gates
> of Ekron. The wounded of the Philistines fell down by the way to Shaaraim, even to Gath and to Ekron.
> **53** The children of Israel returned from chasing after the Philistines and they plundered their camp.
> **54** David took the head of the Philistine, and brought it to Jerusalem; but he put his armor in his tent.

(The full text; from `raw/bible/1 Samuel.md`, chapter 17.)

[[Goliath]] comes on, his shield bearer before him (41). He looks and sees [[David]], and *disdained him; for he was
but a youth, and ruddy, and had a good looking face* (42). He asks *Am I a dog, that you come to me with sticks?*,
curses David *by his gods*, and promises his flesh *to the birds of the sky, and to the animals of the field* (43–44).

David answers him. *You come to me with a sword, with a
spear, and with a javelin; but I come to you in the name of Yahweh of Armies, the God of the armies of Israel, whom you
have defied* (45). He says what Yahweh will do *today*: deliver Goliath into his hand; David will strike him and *take
your head from off you*, and give the Philistine army's dead to the birds and the beasts, *that all the earth may know
that there is a God in Israel, and that all this assembly may know that Yahweh doesn't save with sword and spear; for
the battle is Yahweh's* (46–47).

The Philistine comes on, and David runs to meet him (48). One stone from the bag, slung, strikes him in the forehead;
it *sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the earth* (49). The narrator stops to sum up: *David prevailed
over the Philistine with a sling and with a stone … but there was no sword in the hand of David* (50). Then David runs,
stands over him, draws Goliath's own sword from its sheath, kills him, and cuts off his head (51). The Philistines see
*their champion* dead and flee; Israel and Judah shout and pursue them as far as Gai and the gates of Ekron, and the
wounded fall along the road to Shaaraim, to Gath and to Ekron (51–52). Israel comes back and plunders the camp (53).
David takes the head to Jerusalem and puts the armor in his tent (54).

**Where it happens.** In the [[Valley of Elah]], between the two armies (17:2–3, 19). The pursuit runs west, toward the
Philistine plain: [[Gai]], [[Ekron]], [[Shaaraim]], and [[Gath]] (52). Shaaraim is a town of Judah's lowland (Joshua
15:36), and in the Hebrew files checked the name occurs only there and here (WLC, lemma 8189). The head goes to
[[Jerusalem]] (54).

**What is going on.** Goliath sees, and judges by what he sees. The narrator gives his reason in David's own
description from 16:12, *ruddy, with a handsome face*, now as grounds for contempt; the chapter before had warned that
*man looks at the outward appearance, but Yahweh looks at the heart* (16:7, stored WEB). The two speeches then name two
kinds of power. Goliath names his gods and promises the carrion birds (43–44). David names the weapons, *sword … spear
… javelin*, and sets against them *the name of Yahweh of Armies* (45). The fight itself takes two verses.

Verse 50 is the narrator's verdict, and verse 51 tells the killing a second time, now with a sword. Both verses say
David *killed him*: *wayəmîṯēhû* (50) and *wayəmōṯəṯēhû* (51), the same verb, *mûṯ*, in two stems (WLC; lemma 4191).
Read as written, verse 50 is a summary that runs ahead, and 51 goes back to finish the act; whether the two come from
different tellings is not something the text decides (the source question is recalled, not checked). What verse 50
insists on is the point David made in 47: *there was no sword in the hand of David*. The one sword in the scene is
Goliath's, and it cuts off its owner's head.

**What is notable.**

- **Disdained.** *Wayyiḇzēhû* (42, WLC; *bāzâ*, lemma 959). In the Hebrew files checked the verb occurs eight times:
  Esau *despised his birthright* (Genesis 25:34), the one who *despised Yahweh's word* (Numbers 15:31), *those who
  despise me will be cursed* (1 Samuel 2:30), the worthless fellows who *despised* Saul (10:27), here, Michal who
  *despised* David in her heart (2 Samuel 6:16), and Nathan's charge that David *despised Yahweh's word* (2 Samuel
  12:9–10) (stored WEB for Genesis through 1 Samuel; WLC for 2 Samuel, whose WEB is not stored, so those two are
  paraphrased). See [[Ruddy - Esau and David]] and [[Seeing and Being Seen]].
- **The dog and the sticks.** *Hăḵeleḇ ʾānōḵî*, *Am I a dog?* (43, WLC; *keleḇ*, lemma 3611). The next uses in the
  files checked are all self-abasement or insult in David's story: David to Saul at 24:14 WEB (24:15 WLC), and in
  2 Samuel Abner (3:8), Mephibosheth (9:8), and Abishai of Shimei (16:9) (WLC; the 2 Samuel verses paraphrased, not
  quoted). *Sticks*, *bammaqlôṯ*, is plural; David took one *staff*, *maqlô* (17:40), and the narrator never says he
  carried it into the fight (WLC).
- **By his gods.** *Bēʾlōhāyw* (43, WLC). The form is the plural noun with a suffix, which Hebrew also uses for a single
  god and for Israel's God, so *his gods* or *his god* are both possible renderings; the WEB chooses the plural.
- **The birds and the beasts.** Goliath: *the birds of the sky, and … the animals of the field* (44). David turns it on
  the whole army, *the birds of the sky, and … the wild animals of the earth* (46). In the files checked, *ʿôp̄
  haššāmayim* with the beasts names the created order in Genesis 1–2 and 9:2, and the unburied dead in Deuteronomy
  28:26, the covenant curse, *Your dead body will be food to all birds of the sky, and to the animals of the earth*;
  then here, and at 2 Samuel 21:10, where Rizpah keeps both off the bodies of Saul's descendants (WLC; 2 Samuel paraphrased).
  *Peḡer*, *dead bodies* (46, lemma 6297), is the word for the carcasses Abram drove the birds of prey from (Genesis
  15:11) and for the wilderness generation's *dead bodies* (Numbers 14:29, 32–33) (stored WEB). See
  [[Burial and the Grave]] and [[Hanged on a Tree - Genesis 40, Deuteronomy 21, and Galatians 3]].
- **Sword, spear, and javelin.** *Ḥereḇ*, *ḥănîṯ*, and *kîḏôn* (45, WLC). The *kîḏôn* is the javelin Goliath carries
  *between his shoulders* (17:6), and in the files checked the word otherwise names the javelin Joshua held out over Ai
  (Joshua 8:18, 26) (WLC; lemma 3591). David lists Goliath's weapons back to him; 17:47 then drops the javelin and keeps
  the pair, *sword and spear*, that 13:19–22 said Israel lacked. See
  [[Yahweh Doesn't Save with Sword and Spear - 1 Samuel 13.22, 17.47, and Judges 5.8]].
- **In the name of Yahweh of Armies.** *Bəšēm YHWH ṣəḇāʾôṯ* (45, WLC). In the Hebrew files checked the phrase occurs
  only here and at 2 Samuel 6:2, 18, where the ark is called by that name and David blesses the people in it,
  both when the ark is brought up (WLC; 2 Samuel paraphrased). David gives the title its gloss: *the God of the
  armies of Israel*, *ʾĕlōhê maʿarḵôṯ yiśrāʾēl*. Goliath's own phrase was *maʿarḵôṯ yiśrāʾēl*, *the armies of Israel*
  (17:10); David makes it God's. See [[The Names of God]].
- **Whom you have defied.** *Ḥēraptā* (45, WLC). It is the fifth verse of the chapter with *ḥārap̄* (10, 25, 26, 36, 45) and
  the last; the champion who said *I defy* hears it back in the second person. See [[The Reproach of Israel - Joshua 5.9, 1 Samuel 11.2, and 17.26]] and
  [[To Defy the Living God - 1 Samuel 17 and 2 Kings 19]].
- **Deliver you into my hand.** *Yəsaggerḵā YHWH bəyāḏî* (46, WLC; *sāḡar*, *shut*, lemma 5462, in a stem that means
  *hand over*). The formula *give into the hand* returns at the end of 47, *wənāṯan ʾeṯḵem bəyāḏēnû*, and David's *my
  hand* (46) becomes *our hand* (47).
- **That all the earth may know.** *Wəyēḏəʿû kol-hāʾāreṣ kî yēš ʾĕlōhîm ləyiśrāʾēl* (46, WLC). The purpose clause is
  the Exodus refrain, *that you may know that there is no one like me in all the earth* (Exodus 9:14) and Joshua's
  *that all the peoples of the earth may know that Yahweh's hand is mighty* (Joshua 4:24) (stored WEB). *Yēš ʾĕlōhîm*,
  *there is a God*, as an adjacent pair occurs in the files checked only here (WLC). The knowledge has two audiences:
  *all the earth* (46), and *all this assembly*, *kol-haqqāhāl hazzeh* (47), Israel standing on the hill. See
  [[Who Is Yahweh - Pharaoh's Question and the Knowledge of Yahweh]].
- **The battle is Yahweh's.** *Kî laYHWH hammilḥāmâ* (47, WLC). The two words stand side by side elsewhere in the files
  checked only as *before Yahweh to the war* (Numbers 32:20, 27; Joshua 4:13), the armed men of the east-bank tribes
  crossing ahead of Israel; the possessive sentence, *the battle belongs to Yahweh*, is only here. *Yəhôšîaʿ*, *save*,
  with *sword* in the same verse otherwise only at Deuteronomy 33:29, *a people saved by Yahweh, the shield of your
  help, the sword of your excellency* (stored WEB; WLC). See [[War]] and [[Faith]].
- **Ran to meet him.** *Wayəmahēr dāwiḏ wayyārāṣ hammaʿărāḵâ* (48, WLC): the battle line again, the chapter's word, now
  the place David runs toward. He ran to the line at 17:22 to greet his brothers; he runs again in 51, to stand over
  the fallen man.
- **The bag.** *Hakkelî* (49, WLC), the *bag* of 17:40 (*biḵlî hārōʿîm*). The same noun, *kəlî* (lemma 3627), is David's
  *baggage* at 17:22 and Goliath's *armor* at 17:54. What David carries in his *kəlî* is a stone; what Goliath leaves
  behind is his.
- **The forehead.** *Miṣḥô*, twice (49, WLC; lemma 4696). In the files checked the only other place the word occurs is
  Exodus 28:38, the gold plate on Aaron's forehead, *always on his forehead, that they may be accepted before Yahweh*
  (stored WEB). The wiki records the rarity; the text draws no line between the two.
- **The stone sank.** *Wattiṭbaʿ hāʾeḇen* (49, WLC; *ṭāḇaʿ*, lemma 2883). Its one other occurrence in the files checked
  is the Song of the Sea: *His chosen captains are sunk in the Red Sea* (Exodus 15:4, stored WEB). See
  [[The Song of the Sea]].
- **Fell on his face to the earth.** *Wayyippōl ʿal-pānāyw ʾarṣâ* (49, WLC). Dagon was found *fallen on his face to the
  ground before Yahweh's ark*, his head *cut off* (1 Samuel 5:4, stored WEB; *nōp̄ēl ləp̄ānāyw ʾarṣâ*, WLC). Here the
  Philistine falls the same way, and his head is cut off (51). See
  [[Dagon Fallen - Samson's Pillars and the Ark in Ashdod]].
- **The sling.** *Wayqallaʿ*, *and slung* (49), and *baqqelaʿ* (50) (WLC). The verb *qālaʿ* (lemma 7049) occurs in the
  files checked only at Judges 20:16, the left-handed Benjamites who *could sling a stone at a hair*, here, and 25:29
  (WLC). See [[Left-Handed Men of Benjamin - Judges 3.15 and 20.16]].
- **Drew it out of its sheath.** *Wayyišləp̄āh mittaʿrāh* (51, WLC). *Taʿar* in this sense (lemma 8593, which morphhb
  shares with the razor of Numbers 6:5; 8:7) recurs at 2 Samuel 20:8. *Šālap̄*, *draw* (lemma 8025), is the verb of
  the drawn sword before Balaam and Joshua (Numbers 22:23, 31; Joshua 5:13), of Abimelech's *Draw your sword, and kill
  me* (Judges 9:54), and of Saul's to his armor bearer (1 Samuel 31:4, both stored WEB). See
  [[The Drawn Sword Before Balaam and Joshua]] and [[Draw Your Sword - The Deaths of Abimelech and Saul]].
- **Their champion.** *Gibbôrām* (51, WLC; *gibbôr*, lemma 1368). The narrator's word for Goliath was *ʾîš habbēnayim*,
  *the man of the between* (17:4, 23). Only the Philistines' view of him, after his death, calls him their *gibbôr*,
  the word for Saul's father, *a mighty man of valor* (9:1), and for David himself at 16:18 (WLC).
- **The head.** *Wayyiḵrāṯ … ʾeṯ-rōʾšô*, *cut off his head* (51, WLC). David said *I will … take your head from off
  you* (46), and does. In the files checked, *kāraṯ* with *rōʾš* in one verse is Dagon's head (5:4), this head, Saul's
  head cut off by the Philistines on Gilboa (31:9), and Sheba's at Abel (2 Samuel 20:22) (WLC; stored WEB for
  1 Samuel). See [[The Head and the Armor - Goliath at Elah and Saul at Gilboa]].
- **Israel and Judah.** *ʾAnšê yiśrāʾēl wîhûḏâ*, *the men of Israel and of Judah* (52, WLC). The musters at 11:8 and
  15:4 had already counted *the men of Judah* apart from the rest (stored WEB). The book has not divided the kingdom;
  it names Judah separately all the same.
- **Gai.** *ʿAḏ-bôʾăḵā gayʾ*, *as far as you come to Gai* (52, WLC). *Gayʾ* (lemma 1516) is the ordinary noun for
  *valley*, as in *the valley of Hinnom* (Joshua 15:8), and the WEB alone makes it a name here. The Septuagint is
  reported to read *Gath* (recalled, not checked; no Septuagint text was consulted). See [[Gai]].
- **Plundered their camp.** *Wayyāšōssû* (53, WLC; *šāsas*, lemma 8155). In the files checked the verb otherwise
  occurs only at Judges 2:14, *raiders who plundered them*, of Israel's enemies. Here the plundered are the
  Philistines. *Middəlōq*, *from chasing* (53, lemma 1814), is otherwise only Laban's *hotly pursued me* (Genesis
  31:36) (stored WEB; WLC).
- **Jerusalem, and the tent.** David *brought it to Jerusalem* (54), the only time 1 Samuel names the city
  (stored WEB). Jerusalem is still Jebusite in the books stored so
  far, *the Jebusites dwell with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day* (Judges 1:21; 19:10–12), and the
  city's capture is narrated in 2 Samuel 5:6–9 (not yet ingested; not quoted). So either the verse looks ahead, or the
  head was brought there later, or the verse comes from a later hand; those are the usual explanations (recalled, not
  checked), and the wiki does not choose. David has no tent of his own in the narrative so far. Goliath's sword turns
  up later at Nob, *wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod* (21:9, stored WEB; not yet ingested), so the armor and the
  sword do not stay together. The verse does not say how the sword got there.

**How it connects.** David's speech at 45–47 is the theology of the chapter, and the narrator confirms it at 50. The
book has set it up: Hannah's *for no man shall prevail by strength* (2:9), Jonathan's *there is no restraint on Yahweh
to save by many or by few* (14:6), and Israel without *sword nor spear* (13:22) (stored WEB). Goliath's death also
mirrors Saul's. The Philistine falls, his head is cut off by his own sword, and his armor is set in a tent; at Gilboa
Saul falls on *the sword*, and the Philistines cut off his head and put his armor *in the house of the Ashtaroth*
(31:4, 9–10, stored WEB; not yet ingested). The next pericope returns to Saul's question, *whose son is this youth?*
(17:55), and to Jonathan.

## Notes

**Judgment calls.** The pericope runs 17:41–54 as planned: it opens as the Philistine comes near in answer to David's
coming near (17:40–41) and closes when the head and the armor are put away (54); 17:55 goes back to Saul watching
David go out, the start of the next unit. One new place page, [[Gai]], because the WEB prints it as a name; the page
says the Hebrew is the common noun *valley*. The shield bearer (41) is unnamed and gets no page. One new connection,
[[The Head and the Armor - Goliath at Elah and Saul at Gilboa]], because the same three acts (the fall, the head cut
off, *kēlāyw* put in a sanctuary or tent) recur at Saul's death, in the same book, with the same words.

**Unresolved.** Whether 50 and 51 are one telling or two. Why Jerusalem (54), and whose tent. How Goliath's sword
came to Nob (21:9). Whether *bēʾlōhāyw* (43) is *his gods* or *his god*. Whether *gayʾ* (52) is a place name or *a
valley*, or a corruption of *Gath*. The contradiction with 2 Samuel 21:19 stays on
[[Goliath and Elhanan - 1 Samuel 17 and 2 Samuel 21.19]], unreconciled.
