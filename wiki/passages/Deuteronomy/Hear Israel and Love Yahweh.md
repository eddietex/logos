# Hear Israel and Love Yahweh

**Book:** [[Deuteronomy]]
**Reference:** Deuteronomy 6:1–9

## Summary

Moses carries the commission to teach into its practical purpose: Israel is to do the commands
in the land, fearing Yahweh through successive generations, with long life and increase held
before it (1–3). The address then confesses Yahweh as Israel’s God and as one (4), and commands
love with all heart, soul, and might (5).

The words must be on the hearer’s heart before being taught to children (6–7). Teaching fills
the ordinary day: at home and on the road, lying down and rising. Signs on hand and between the
eyes, and writing on door posts and gates, give the instruction a bodily and household presence
(8–9). Moses’ teaching is to continue in the speech and practices of the people themselves.

The passage joins fear, love, and obedience rather than setting them against one another. The
promised good is addressed to Israel’s covenant life in the land; it is not a warrant for
concluding that any particular sufferer must have failed to love God.

## Key verses

WEB text of the complete nine-verse unit:

> **6:1** Now this is the commandment, the statutes, and the ordinances, which Yahweh your God commanded to teach you, that you might do them in the land where you go over to possess it;

> **6:2** that you might fear Yahweh your God, to keep all his statutes and his commandments, which I command you; you, and your son, and your son’s son, all the days of your life; and that your days may be prolonged.

> **6:3** Hear therefore, Israel, and observe to do it; that it may be well with you, and that you may increase mightily, as Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has promised to you, in a land flowing with milk and honey.

> **6:4** Hear, Israel: Yahweh is our God. Yahweh is one.

> **6:5** You shall love Yahweh your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your might.

> **6:6** These words, which I command you today, shall be on your heart;

> **6:7** and you shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up.

> **6:8** You shall bind them for a sign on your hand, and they shall be for frontlets between your eyes.

> **6:9** You shall write them on the door posts of your house, and on your gates.

## Notes

**Boundary.** The planned 6:1–9 unit is retained. Verses 1–3 give the purpose of instruction;
4–9 give the confession, love command, and daily practices. Verse 10 begins the next warning,
about forgetting Yahweh amid inherited abundance, and is not ingested here.

**Reading limits.** Verse 4 is quoted as the WEB renders it. No claim about exact Hebrew syntax
or competing translations is made. Verses 8–9 command signs and writing but do not give detailed
instructions for manufacturing objects; the present page does not resolve the literal/figurative
interpretation discussed in the existing connection page.

**Sources.** All biblical quotations on this page come directly from stored WEB Deuteronomy 6.

comparisons. Gospel reception is linked through the existing connection pages and their recorded
WEB verification; no new external verse quotation or source-language assertion is introduced.
