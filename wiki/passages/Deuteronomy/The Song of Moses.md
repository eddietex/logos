# The Song of Moses

**Book:** [[Deuteronomy]]
**Reference:** Deuteronomy 31:30–32:47

## Summary

Moses speaks the song to the whole assembly (31:30). Heaven and earth are summoned to hear
instruction like rain, declaring the faithful Rock’s justice before exposing Israel’s corruption
(32:1–6). The people must remember the care that made them: their inheritance, wilderness
protection, eagle-like carrying, and abundant food (7–14).

Jeshurun grows fat and abandons the giver for new gods (15–18). Yahweh answers the provocation
with hidden presence, hunger, disease, and the sword; even infants and elders suffer (19–25).
Yet annihilation would let the enemy mistake divine judgment for its own independent power
(26–27). The poem exposes folly and false refuge through images of defeat and poisonous fruit
(28–38). Judgment gives way to compassion when the servants’ strength is gone (36), without
making rival gods effective helpers.

Yahweh alone rules death and life and promises vengeance on adversaries; nations are summoned
to rejoice with his people as their blood is avenged and land and people receive atonement
(39–43). Moses and Joshua deliver the song, then Moses insists that Israel set its heart on
these words, teach children obedience, and recognize the instruction as its life (44–47).

## Key verses

> **4** The Rock, his work is perfect, for all his ways are just. A God of faithfulness who does no wrong, just and right is he.

> **15** But Jeshurun grew fat, and kicked. You have grown fat. You have grown thick. You have become sleek. Then he abandoned God who made him, and rejected the Rock of his salvation.

> **18** Of the Rock who became your father, you are unmindful, and have forgotten God who gave you birth.

> **35** Vengeance is mine, and recompense, at the time when their foot slides; for the day of their calamity is at hand. Their doom rushes at them.”

> **36** For Yahweh will judge his people, and have compassion on his servants, when he sees that their power is gone; that there is no one remaining, shut up or left at large.

> **39** “See now that I myself am he. There is no god with me. I kill and I make alive. I wound and I heal. There is no one who can deliver out of my hand.

> **43** Rejoice, you nations, with his people, for he will avenge the blood of his servants. He will take vengeance on his adversaries, and will make atonement for his land and for his people.

> **46** He said to them, “Set your heart to all the words which I testify to you today, which you shall command your children to observe to do, all the words of this law.

> **47** For it is no vain thing for you; because it is your life, and through this thing you shall prolong your days in the land, where you go over the Jordan to possess it.”

## Notes

- **Names and speakers:** Jacob and Jeshurun name the people, not additional participants.
  Nun is named only in Joshua’s patronymic. The referents of the pronouns in 28–33 are not
  uniformly explicit; the indictment moves between Israel and enemies, and this page does
  not force every line onto one group.
- **Tensions preserved:** 5 denies the corrupt generation the status of children, while
  19 calls them sons and daughters. The tension is part of the accusation against unfaithful
  children, not grounds for deleting either statement. Compassion in 36 and vengeance in
  41–43 likewise belong to the same poem.
- **Sheol:** the fire reaches the lowest Sheol (22). This corrects the existing place page’s
  blanket statement that it has no fire, while leaving open what the poetic image implies
  about the condition of the dead.
- **Text basis:** 8 reads children of Israel in the stored WEB, and 43 supplies the closing
  call to the nations. No Hebrew or Greek edition is being collated here, so this page makes
  no judgment about variant ancient readings or original wording.
- **Scope:** the delivery frame and concluding appeal remain with the whole song as planned.
  Moses’ next ascent command is outside this unit. All quotations above use stored WEB;
  later uses are documented in the connection pages, with source verification identified.
