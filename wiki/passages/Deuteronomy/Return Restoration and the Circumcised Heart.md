# Return Restoration and the Circumcised Heart

**Book:** [[Deuteronomy]]
**Reference:** Deuteronomy 30:1–10

## Summary

Moses looks past the curses of chapters 28–29 to a time *when all these things have come on you, the
blessing and the curse* (1). Israel is scattered among the nations, and there it calls these things to
mind and returns to Yahweh, obeying his voice *with all your heart, and with all your soul*, together
with its children (1–2). Yahweh then releases Israel from captivity, has compassion, and gathers it
*from all the peoples* where he scattered it. No outcast is too far: *If your outcasts are in the
uttermost parts of the heavens, from there Yahweh your God will gather you* (3–4). He brings them into
the land their fathers possessed, does them good, and multiplies them beyond their fathers (5).

The centre of the unit is verse 6. *Yahweh your God will circumcise your heart, and the heart of your
offspring, to love Yahweh your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, that you may live.* The
curses pass to Israel's enemies and persecutors (7). Israel returns, obeys, and keeps the commands (8).
Yahweh makes the work of their hands, their children, their livestock, and their ground plentiful,
*for Yahweh will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers* (9). The unit
closes on two conditions: *if you will obey* and *if you turn to Yahweh your God with all your heart,
and with all your soul* (10).

The unit answers the chapter before it. Deuteronomy 29:4 said *Yahweh has not given you a heart to
know … to this day*; here Yahweh promises to act on the heart himself. Deuteronomy 28:63 said Yahweh
would *rejoice over you to cause you to perish*; here he *will again rejoice over you for good*. And
the scattering of 28:64 becomes the starting point for a gathering. The whole movement had been
outlined once already, in 4:29–31, at the end of the first address; see
[[Return and Rejoice Again - Deuteronomy 4.29-31, 28.63, and 30.1-10]].

## Key verses

Source: stored WEB, Deuteronomy 30:1–10. Full unit copied verbatim.

> **30:1** It shall happen, when all these things have come on you, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before you, and you shall call them to mind among all the nations, where Yahweh your God has driven you,

> **30:2** and return to Yahweh your God, and obey his voice according to all that I command you today, you and your children, with all your heart, and with all your soul;

> **30:3** that then Yahweh your God will release you from captivity, have compassion on you, and will return and gather you from all the peoples where Yahweh your God has scattered you.

> **30:4** If your outcasts are in the uttermost parts of the heavens, from there Yahweh your God will gather you, and from there he will bring you back.

> **30:5** Yahweh your God will bring you into the land which your fathers possessed, and you will possess it. He will do you good, and increase your numbers more than your fathers.

> **30:6** Yahweh your God will circumcise your heart, and the heart of your offspring, to love Yahweh your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, that you may live.

> **30:7** Yahweh your God will put all these curses on your enemies, and on those who hate you, who persecuted you.

> **30:8** You shall return and obey Yahweh’s voice, and do all his commandments which I command you today.

> **30:9** Yahweh your God will make you plenteous in all the work of your hand, in the fruit of your body, in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground, for good; for Yahweh will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers;

> **30:10** if you will obey Yahweh your God’s voice, to keep his commandments and his statutes which are written in this book of the law; if you turn to Yahweh your God with all your heart, and with all your soul.

## People

- [[God]] — Yahweh has driven and scattered Israel (1, 3), and promises to release, have compassion, gather even from *the uttermost parts of the heavens*, and bring back (3–5). He will circumcise the heart of Israel and its offspring (6), transfer the curses to enemies (7), and *again rejoice over you for good* (9).
- [[Moses]] — The speaker, still addressing the assembly in Moab: *all that I command you today* (2, 8). He speaks of the return as a future already in view; he narrates no new action.

## Places

- [[Canaan]] — Not named; *the land which your fathers possessed* (5) is the promised land in context. The exile from it and the return to it are both spoken of as future.

## Themes

- [[Covenant]] — Blessing and curse have both *come on you* (1); restoration follows return and leads to keeping *this book of the law* (10). The covenant's sanctions are not the last word, and the last verse is still conditional.
- [[Circumcision]] — *Yahweh your God will circumcise your heart, and the heart of your offspring* (6). Deuteronomy 10:16 commanded Israel to circumcise its own heart; here Yahweh is the one who acts. See [[Circumcision of the Heart]].
- [[Hardening of the Heart]] — The ungiven *heart to know* of 29:4 is answered by a promise that Yahweh will act on the heart (6). The passage does not say how the commanded return of verse 2 relates to the promised circumcision of verse 6.
- [[Love]] — The purpose of the circumcised heart is *to love Yahweh your God with all your heart, and with all your soul* (6), the words of the Shema's command (6:5).
- [[Life and Death]] — Heart-circumcision is given *that you may live* (6), which sets up the choice of *life and death* in 30:15–20, [[The Word Is Near and the Choice Is Life]].
- [[Compassion and Mercy]] — Yahweh *will … have compassion on you* (3), as 4:31 promised that *Yahweh your God is a merciful God*.
- [[Remembrance]] — Israel *shall call them to mind among all the nations* (1); return begins with remembering among the nations.
- [[Redemption]] — Release from captivity and gathering from all peoples (3–4) repeat the pattern of the exodus with the nations in place of Egypt.
- [[Land and Sojourning]] — Israel will be driven out and then brought back to *the land which your fathers possessed*, to possess it again (5).
- [[Law and Obedience]] — Obedience comes both before restoration (2) and after the circumcised heart (8), and frames the unit's closing conditions (10).
- [[Blessing]] — The blessings of 28:4 and 28:11 return: the fruit of body, livestock, and ground (9).
- [[Curse]] — *All these curses* fall on Israel's enemies and persecutors (7) after falling on Israel (1).
- [[Joy and Enjoyment]] — Yahweh *will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers* (9), reversing his rejoicing *to cause you to perish* in 28:63.
- [[Hope]] — The first long promise of restoration after exile in Deuteronomy since 4:29–31; it is spoken before Israel has even entered the land.
- [[The Nations]] — Israel is driven *among all the nations* (1) and gathered *from all the peoples* (3); enemies receive the curses (7).

## Notes

**Boundary.** The planned 30:1–10 boundary is kept. Verse 1 opens with *It shall happen, when*, a new
time frame after the closing maxim of 29:29. Verse 11 turns from what Yahweh will do to the command
itself, *not too hard for you or too distant*, which begins the next unit,
[[The Word Is Near and the Choice Is Life]].

**Seven times "return".** The Hebrew verb *šûb* (Strong's 7725) occurs seven times in these ten verses,
by the Open Scriptures WLC (morphhb) Strong's tags: verse 1 (the causative form the WEB renders *call
them to mind*, literally *bring back to your heart*), verse 2 (*return*), verse 3 twice (*release you
from captivity*, built on the idiom *šûb šəbût*, and *will return*), verse 8 (*return*), verse 9
(*again*), and verse 10 (*turn*). Israel returns in verses 2, 8, and 10; Yahweh returns in 3 and 9.
The WEB's varied renderings hide the repetition. The count rests on that edition's tagging; the
meaning of the number seven, if any, is not argued here.

**Who acts first.** Verse 2 has Israel return and obey; verse 3 has Yahweh then restore. Verse 6
has Yahweh circumcise the heart so that Israel can love, and verse 8 has Israel return and obey again.
Verse 10 ends with *if*. Read in order, return comes before restoration and obedience follows the
circumcised heart. Whether the unit means human turning to be the cause of divine renewal, or its
effect, or both, is not settled by the text, and the wiki records both orders without choosing. The
same tension is noted in [[Seen Signs and an Unknowing Heart - Deuteronomy 4 and 29]] and on
[[Circumcision of the Heart]].

**Rejoicing reversed.** In 28:63 the WEB has Yahweh *rejoiced over you to do you good* and then
*will rejoice over you to cause you to perish*. In 30:9 he *will again rejoice over you for good*.
The same verb *śûś* (Strong's 7797) is tagged in both verses in the WLC (morphhb). The disturbing
claim of 28:63 is not withdrawn; 30:9 states the reversal that follows it.

**Two words for "curse".** Verse 1 pairs *the blessing and the curse* using *qəlālâ* (7045); verse 7's
*all these curses* uses *ʾālâ* (423), the same noun as the oath-curse of 29:19–20 in that edition's
tags. The English does not show the difference. No argument is built on it.

**Uttermost parts of the heavens.** Verse 4's *uttermost parts of the heavens* is read here as
hyperbole for the farthest distance imaginable, not as a claim that exiles will be in the sky; that is
an interpretation. Compare 4:32, *from the one end of the sky to the other*, and 28:64, *from one end of
the earth to the other*.

**Prospect, not narrative.** The exile and return are spoken by Moses before Israel has crossed the
Jordan. Whether they are prediction, warning, or a later reflection placed in Moses' mouth is a
question of composition the wiki does not settle. Later texts take up the same language. Nehemiah 1:8–9
recalls the scattering and gathering (paraphrased here; Nehemiah is not yet ingested or stored).
Jeremiah 31:31–34 promises the law written on the heart, and Ezekiel 36:24–27 a gathering from the
nations with a new heart. Those two are paraphrased with references only; neither is stored in raw/,
and no direct dependence on Deuteronomy 30 is claimed.

**The heart of your offspring.** Verse 6 extends the promise to *the heart of your offspring*, matching
*you and your children* in verse 2. The covenant in Moab already included *those who are not here with
us today* (29:15).
