# Confess, Repay, and Add a Fifth - Leviticus 6 and Numbers 5

## Passages involved

- [[Restitution for Fraud and False Oaths]] — Leviticus 6:1–7.
- [[The Unclean Sent Out and Wrongs Repaid]] — Numbers 5:5–10.
- [[Confession and Offerings According to Means]] — Leviticus 5:5.
- [[The Trespass Offering for Holy Things and Unknown Guilt]] — Leviticus 5:14–19.

## Analysis

Numbers 5:5–10 looks like a shorter restatement of Leviticus 6:1–7, and they share the core: a *maʿal*
against Yahweh, repayment in full (*bərōʾšô*), an added fifth, and a ram. By Strong's tags (WLC), the
principal and the fifth are named together in these terms in only these two verses, Leviticus 6:5 (5:24
in the Hebrew numbering) and Numbers 5:7. The difference is in what each adds.

| Point | Leviticus 6:1–7 | Numbers 5:5–10 |
|---|---|---|
| The wrong | listed: deposit, bargain, robbery, oppression, lost goods, false oath (2–3) | general: *any sin that men commit* (6) |
| Who | *anyone* (2) | *a man or woman* (6) |
| Confession | not mentioned | required (7) |
| Recipient | the owner (5) | the one wronged; failing a kinsman, Yahweh and the priest (7–8) |
| Ram | brought and offered (6–7) | assumed, *the ram of the atonement* (8) |
| Forgiveness | stated (7) | not stated |

**Confession.** The Hithpael of *yādâ* stands at Leviticus 5:5; 16:21; 26:40 and Numbers 5:7 in the
Pentateuch (Strong's tag, WLC), and not in Leviticus 6:1–7. Whether Numbers adds something new or names
what Leviticus assumed is not said.

**The payment's name.** Leviticus 6:6 calls the ram the *ʾāšām*. Numbers 5:7–8 uses that noun for the
repayment, and verse 8 sets the ram beside it. WEB marks the shift by rendering *restitution for his
guilt*.

**No one to pay.** Leviticus 6 assumes the owner can be paid. Numbers 5:8 answers the case where he and
any kinsman are gone. The debt does not lapse: it goes to Yahweh, and through him to the priest. So a
wrong against a neighbor outlives the neighbor. That last sentence is the wiki's reading.

## Significance

Neither law lets the sacrifice stand in for the repayment, and Numbers adds that the wrong must be
said and that nobody profits from a victim's having no family. See [[Theft and Restitution]],
[[Guilt and Conscience]], [[Atonement]], and [[Restitution to the Neighbor and for Holy Things]].
