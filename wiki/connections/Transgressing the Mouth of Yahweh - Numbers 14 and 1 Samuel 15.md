# Transgressing the Mouth of Yahweh - Numbers 14 and 1 Samuel 15

## Passages involved

- [[The Defeat at Hormah]] — Numbers 14:40–43
- Numbers 22:18; 24:13 — Balaam *(stored WEB, not yet ingested)*
- 1 Samuel 15:11, 24 — Saul *(WEB checked, not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

By the tags, *ʿābar ʾet-pî YHWH* occurs in four verses, and Balaam's two are about what he cannot do.
That leaves one event at Kadesh and one in Saul's reign, and both are about Amalek.

| | Numbers 14 | 1 Samuel 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Confession | *ḥāṭāʾnû*, *we have sinned* (40) | *ḥāṭāʾtî*, *I have sinned* (24) |
| The transgression | *ʿōbərîm ʾet-pî YHWH* (41) | *ʿābartî ʾet-pî YHWH* (24) |
| Turning back | *šabtem mēʾaḥărê YHWH* (43) | *šāb mēʾaḥăray* (11) |
| Amalek | beats Israel (45) | spared in part, against the order |
| Result | the generation's sentence stands | the kingdom is taken |

WEB has *turned back from following* in both of the third row and *the commandment of Yahweh* in both
of the second, though with *disobey* in one and *transgressed* in the other. Saul's fear of the people, *because I feared the
people, and obeyed their voice* (15:24), resembles the fear that began Numbers 14.

**What the likeness shows.** In both, a confession arrives after the decision has been made, and it
does not reverse the decision. In both the speaker wants the earlier word to be restored by a late
act of compliance. Saul asks Samuel to turn back with him. Israel simply goes. That reading is the
wiki's, and the text gives no sign that 1 Samuel is alluding to Numbers.

**Balaam.** At 22:18 and 24:13 Balaam says he cannot *go beyond the word of Yahweh*, using the same
phrase in the negative. The foreign seer says what Israel's generation did not. That contrast is
drawn by the wiki.

## Significance

- The phrase is rare and its two narrative uses are both about failed confession.
- *Sinned* is not by itself a turn. See [[Sin]] and [[Guilt and Conscience]].
