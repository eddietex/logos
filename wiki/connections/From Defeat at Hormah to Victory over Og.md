# From Defeat at Hormah to Victory over Og

## Passages involved
- [[Sihon and Og]] — Numbers 21:21–35
- [[The Defeat at Hormah]] — Numbers 14:39–45

## Analysis

At Hormah Moses warns that Yahweh is not with the advancing people; they go anyway and are defeated
(14:41–45). Before Og, Yahweh addresses Moses, promises delivery, and invokes the victory over Sihon
as precedent (21:34). Israel then wins and takes the land (21:35). The narrative contrast concerns
divine authorization, not a claim that Israel’s military strength always guarantees success. The
earlier defeat and present victory are distinct events, not an unresolved contradiction.

## Significance

The intervening wilderness narrative changes the setting of battle. Victory is framed by assurance
where the earlier advance contradicted an explicit warning.
