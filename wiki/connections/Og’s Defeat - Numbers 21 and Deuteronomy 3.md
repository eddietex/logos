# Og’s Defeat - Numbers 21 and Deuteronomy 3

## Passages involved
- [[Sihon and Og]] — Numbers 21:33–35
- [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]] — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

## Analysis

Numbers and Deuteronomy share the approach toward Bashan, Og’s attack at Edrei, Yahweh’s assurance
to Moses, the comparison with Sihon, and victory leaving no survivors. Deuteronomy retells in the
first person what Numbers narrates in the third person.

Deuteronomy adds sixty fortified cities in Argob, unwalled villages, the explicit destruction of
women and little ones, retained livestock and plunder, and the geographical and bedstead notices
(4–11). Numbers explicitly mentions Og’s sons (35); Deuteronomy 3:3 speaks of all his people. These
differences in detail do not require two separate campaigns. The nine-by-four-cubit dimensions
belong to the bedstead, not to Og’s body.

## Significance

The expansion makes the strength of the defeated kingdom and the severity of conquest visible.
Confidence in divine delivery must not erase the civilian deaths.
