# Over the Tent and Before the People - Where the Cloud Stood

## Passages involved

- [[The Cloud over the Tabernacle]] — Numbers 9:15–23; on the tabernacle, and rising to signal departure
- [[The Crossing of the Sea]] — Exodus 13:21–22; 14:19–20; a pillar going before, then behind
- [[The Tabernacle Raised and the Glory]] — Exodus 40:34–38; on the tabernacle
- [[The Camp Around the Tent]] — Numbers 2:17; the Tent travels in the middle
- Numbers 10:11–12, 31–36 — the cloud, the ark, and Hobab on the march *(stored WEB, not yet ingested)*
- Numbers 12:5, 10 — a pillar at the door, and the cloud leaving the Tent *(stored WEB, not yet ingested)*
- Numbers 14:14 — *your cloud stands over them, and you go before them* *(stored WEB, not yet ingested)*
- Deuteronomy 1:33 — Yahweh seeking out the camping place *(WEB checked at bible-api.com, not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

The Torah puts the cloud in more than one place, and it does not explain how they fit.

| Text | Where the cloud is | Word |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 13:21–22 | Before the people | *pillar* |
| Exodus 14:19–20 | Moved behind them | *pillar* |
| Exodus 40:34–38 | On the tabernacle | *cloud* |
| Numbers 9:15–23 | On the tabernacle, rising and settling | *cloud* |
| Numbers 10:33–34 | *Over them by day*; the ark goes before | *cloud* |
| Numbers 12:5 | At the door of the Tent | *pillar of cloud* |
| Numbers 14:14 | *Over them*, and Yahweh going *before them* | both |
| Deuteronomy 1:33 | Yahweh going before, *in fire by night ... and in the cloud by day* | *cloud* |

Numbers 2:17 is what makes this a question: *Then the Tent of Meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites
in the middle of the camps.* If the cloud sits on a Tent carried in the middle, it does not lead from the front.
Numbers 9 only describes the cloud rising and settling. It does not say what the cloud did between. It has no
*ʿammûd*, *pillar*, at all (WLC tags), so nothing here says the two forms are or are not the same thing. Numbers
14:14 uses both words in one verse, which suggests the writers saw no conflict. A cloud that rose from the Tent
and moved ahead of the column would fit every line, but no text says that, and it is the wiki's suggestion.

The same question arises for the campsite. At 9:17 the cloud's settling picks it. At 10:33 *the ark ... went
before them three days' journey, to seek out a resting place for them*. Deuteronomy 1:33 gives Yahweh himself the
task, *to seek out a place for you to pitch your tents in*. Between them, at 10:31, Moses asks Hobab not to leave
*because you know how we are to encamp in the wilderness, and you can be our eyes*. This is one chapter after a
unit that gave every camping decision to Yahweh. The text does not reconcile the request with the rule. Possibly a
guide knows what the cloud does not say, such as water and pasture, but the text does not say that.

Numbers 12 offers a check on the rule itself. *The cloud departed from over the Tent* (12:10), and yet *the people
didn't travel until Miriam was brought in again* (12:15). The verb at 12:10 is *sār*, and Numbers 9 uses *ʿālâ*
(WLC), so this may be a different event from the signal. The chapter makes no comment.

## Significance

Taken together, the texts agree that Yahweh leads, and differ on the details of how. Numbers 9 cares about
the people's response and not the position of the cloud: *they kept Yahweh's command*. Each text picks the position
that suits what it is saying. That is a description of the texts, and no one of them states it. See
[[Cloud and Fire - From the Sea to the Filled Tabernacle]], [[Providence]], and [[Walking with God]].
