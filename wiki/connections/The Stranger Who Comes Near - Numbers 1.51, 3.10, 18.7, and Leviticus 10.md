## Analysis

**A short sentence comes back.** *Wəhazzār haqqārēb yûmāt* stands at Numbers 1:51, and WEB gives the
same English at 3:10, 3:38, and 18:7. It is the book's boundary law.

**The word moves with the boundary.** *Zār* does not mean foreigner here. At 1:51 it is a non-Levite
Israelite. At 3:10 and 18:7 it is anyone outside Aaron's house, which can include a Levite, as
16:40 makes clear with Korah. It means whoever is outside the circle in view.

**The wrath protects the people from their own sanctuary.** The Levites camp around the tabernacle
*that there may be no wrath on the congregation* (1:53). The Hebrew noun *qeṣep* recurs in Numbers
only at WLC 17:11 (WEB 16:46) and 18:5, in the plague after Korah and in the law it produces. The
verb stands at Leviticus 10:6. So the cordon is not only a rank. In the book's logic, one person's
wrong approach endangers everyone, and the guards are there for the camp's sake.

**Leviticus 10 is the background the text does not name.** Numbers 3:4 and 26:61 both go back to
Nadab and Abihu's *strange fire*, in the same chapters that set the priests' and Levites' lines.
Numbers 1 itself does not mention them, so the link at 1:51 is a reading. See
[[Strange Incense - Exodus 30.9, Leviticus 10, Numbers 16, and Uzziah]].
