# The First of the Ways of God - Job 40.19 and Proverbs 8.22

## Passages involved

- **Job 40:19** — of Behemoth: *"He is the chief of the ways of God. He who made him gives him his
  sword."* — *rēʾšît darkê-ʾēl* — [[Behemoth and Leviathan]]
- **Job 40:15** — *"See now, behemoth, which I made as well as you. He eats grass as an ox."*
- **Proverbs 8:22–23** — Wisdom speaking: *"Yahweh possessed me in the beginning of his work, before his
  deeds of old. I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth existed."* —
  *rēʾšît darkô* — [[Wisdom at the Creation]]
- **Job 26:14** — Job: *"Behold, these are but the outskirts of his ways"* — *qəṣôt dərākāw* —
  [[I Will Not Put Away My Integrity]]
- **Genesis 1:1, 24–26** — *bərēʾšît*, *in the beginning*; and the *livestock* (*bəhēmâ*) made on the sixth
  day — [[The Creation of the World]]
- [[The First Man and Wisdom Before the Hills]] — Job 15:7–8 and Proverbs 8, the existing thread on
  Wisdom's priority

## Analysis

**The same phrase, *the first (rēʾšît) of [God's] way(s)*, is used in the Hebrew Bible of a personified
Wisdom and of a grass-eating beast.** In Proverbs 8:22 Wisdom claims priority: she was *the beginning of his
way*, present before the mountains, beside God when he marked out the foundations of the earth (8:22–29). In
Job 40:19 the same honour is given, without explanation, to Behemoth: *"He is the chief (rēʾšît) of the ways
(darkê) of God."*

**The wiki records this as an allusion with one direction uncertain.** It cannot establish which text is
earlier, and the phrase may have been common coin. What it can say is that Job's poetry knows the
Wisdom-before-creation tradition — Eliphaz taunts Job with it at 15:7–8, and chapter 28 is a hymn to wisdom
that God alone saw at creation (28:23–27). A reader who knows that tradition hears *rēʾšît darkê-ʾēl* and
expects Wisdom. **The whirlwind gives the title to an animal.**

**What that does in the speech.** Job 28 ended by saying wisdom is not found in the land of the living and
that for man it is *"the fear of the Lord"* (28:28). The whirlwind does not dispute it. But where the wisdom
tradition reserves the place of *first of God's ways* for something only God could see, Job 40:19 puts it in
a river-bed, among the lotus and reeds, and invites Job to *look* (*"See now"*, 40:15). The masterpiece of
creation is on display, and it is a beast *"which I made as well as you"* (40:15) — *made with you*, on the
same sixth day as the man.

**Two readings of *rēʾšît* are open, and the wiki keeps both.** It may mean *first* in time (the first-made,
as Wisdom is in Proverbs 8), or *first* in rank (the masterpiece, the chief). The WEB's *chief* takes the
second. Job 26:14 had called everything Job could describe of God's power *"the outskirts (qəṣôt) of his
ways"*; 40:19 answers with the *rēʾšît* of his ways, the far end of the same phrase.

## Significance

- **It ties the Behemoth portrait to the book's wisdom question** rather than leaving it as natural history.
  The speech that asks Job *"Who has put wisdom in the inward parts?"* (38:36) ends its first creature
  portrait on the title Proverbs gives to Wisdom. See [[Wisdom]].
- **It keeps Behemoth a creature.** Whatever later tradition made of him (see the Notes on
  [[Behemoth and Leviathan]]), the text calls him *made* (40:15, 19). The first of God's ways is still one of
  God's ways.

**Update, Proverbs 8:22–36 ingested.** The Proverbs side confirms *rēʾšîṯ darkô* at 8:22 (Sefaria's
*Miqra according to the Masorah*, 2026-10-25), and shows that the two readings of *rēʾšîṯ* this page
keeps open are open there too: Wisdom may be *the beginning of his way* — the first thing made, which
suits *"I was born"* (8:24–25) — or present *at* the beginning, the WEB's *"in the beginning of his
work"*. The verb with it, *qānānî*, *"possessed me"*, is the verb of *"Get wisdom"* (4:7), and does not
by itself mean *made*. Behemoth is plainly *made* (Job 40:15); Wisdom's origin is told in the language of
birth and possession. See [[Wisdom at the Creation]] and
[[Possessed in the Beginning - Proverbs 8.22-31, John 1, and Colossians 1]].
