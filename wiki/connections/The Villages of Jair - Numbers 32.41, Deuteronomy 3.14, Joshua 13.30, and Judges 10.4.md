# The Villages of Jair - Numbers 32.41, Deuteronomy 3.14, Joshua 13.30, and Judges 10.4

## Passages involved

- [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]] — **Numbers 32:41** — *Jair the son of Manasseh
  went and took its villages, and called them Havvoth Jair.* (stored WEB)
- [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]] — **Deuteronomy 3:14** — *Jair the son of
  Manasseh took all the region of Argob, to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and
  called them, even Bashan, after his own name, Havvoth Jair, to this day.* (stored WEB)
- [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]] — **Joshua 13:30** — *… all the villages of Jair, which are
  in Bashan, sixty cities.* (stored WEB)
- [[Tola and Jair]] — **Judges 10:4** — *He had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkey colts, and they
  had thirty cities, which are called Havvoth Jair to this day, which are in the land of Gilead.*
  (stored WEB)

## Analysis

The same name, *ḥawwōt yāʾîr*, *the villages of Jair*, is explained four times. The morphhb data
tags it as a place name (lemma 2334) in Numbers 32:41, Deuteronomy 3:14, and Judges 10:4. In Joshua
13:30 the same two words are tagged as the common noun *ḥawwâ*, *village* (2333), followed by the
personal name *Jair* (2971), so Joshua reads them as a description, *the villages of Jair*, rather
than a fixed name (WLC). The accounts agree on the name and on a man called Jair. They
differ in three things:

| | Numbers 32:41 | Deuteronomy 3:14 | Joshua 13:30 | Judges 10:4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The man | Jair the son of Manasseh | Jair the son of Manasseh | (not named as actor) | Jair the Gileadite, a judge |
| When | Moses' time | Moses' time | the allotment | after Abimelech |
| Region | Gilead (32:39–41) | Argob, *even Bashan* | Bashan | *the land of Gilead* |
| Number | not given | not given | sixty cities | thirty cities |
| *To this day* | — | yes | — | yes |

Both Deuteronomy and Judges say the name lasted *to this day*, *ʿad hayyôm hazzeh* (WLC), each in its
own narrator's present.

Possible readings, none of them settled by the text: one Jair clan of Manasseh held villages across
Gilead and Bashan, and the judge was a later member of it who renewed or enlarged the holding; or the
judge's thirty cities were a part of the sixty; or two traditions of the name have been kept side by
side. These are interpretive options, reported and not checked against any commentary. The wiki
records the four accounts and chooses none.

## Significance

- **Two pages for two men.** The wiki keeps [[Jair (son of Manasseh)]] and [[Jair (the Gileadite)]]
  separate because the text separates them in time and in title. It does not decide that they are
  unrelated.
- **The number varies with the region.** Sixty cities in Bashan, thirty in Gilead. That the
  difference reflects two different districts is one reading; that it reflects two traditions is
  another.
- **Judges ties the name to sons and donkeys.** Only Judges gives the cities to sons, and spells them
  *ʿăyārîm*, the same as the *donkey colts* they ride (both lemma 5895 in the morphhb data; see
  [[Tola and Jair]]). Joshua's *sixty cities* use the ordinary *ʿîr* (lemma 5892, WLC).
- See [[Havvoth Jair]], [[Gilead]], [[Bashan]], and [[Argob]].
