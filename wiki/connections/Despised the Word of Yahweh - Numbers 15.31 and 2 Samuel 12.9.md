# Despised the Word of Yahweh - Numbers 15.31 and 2 Samuel 12.9

## Passages involved

- **Numbers 15:31** — *"Because he has despised Yahweh's word, and has broken his commandment, that soul
  shall utterly be cut off"* — [[Offerings in the Land and Sins of Ignorance]]
- **2 Samuel 12:9–10** — *"Why have you despised Yahweh's word, to do that which is evil in his sight?"*
  and *"because you have despised me"*
- **2 Samuel 12:13** — *"Yahweh also has put away your sin. You will not die"*
- **Psalm 51:16** — *"For you don't delight in sacrifice, or else I would give it"*
- **Proverbs 13:13** — *"Whoever despises instruction will pay for it"*, with another verb, *bûz*, by
  the tags

(WEB; 2 Samuel, Psalms, and Proverbs checked at bible-api.com, `translation=web`, 2026-11-02.)

## Analysis

By the tags, *bāzâ* occurs in one verse with *dəbar YHWH* only at Numbers 15:31 and 2 Samuel 12:9. Rahlfs
uses the same verb for both, *ἐφαύλισεν* and *ἐφαύλισας*. Nathan's question therefore uses the
wording that Numbers gives for the sin with no offering. The text of 2 Samuel does not cite Numbers.

David's act was not an error by any reading of the story. Numbers 15 would leave his iniquity on him,
and Nathan says that Yahweh has put it away, though the sword stays in the house (12:10). No offering
is named. Psalm 51, whose heading in WLC sets it when Nathan came to David, says that God does not
delight in sacrifice, which would fit a sin outside the offerings. That link rests on the heading.

The contradiction is flagged, not resolved. Numbers states the law, and 2 Samuel reports what Yahweh
did with a man who broke it. Neither text explains the other.

## Significance

The law leaves no procedure for this sin. Second Samuel does not add one. What it records is a word
from Yahweh through a prophet, after a confession, and with consequences that remain.
