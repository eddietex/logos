# From Healing Sign to Nehushtan - Numbers 21 and 2 Kings 18

## Passages involved
- [[The Brass Serpent]] — Numbers 21:4–9.
- 2 Kings 18:4 — verified WEB; not yet ingested.

## Analysis

Numbers attributes the serpent's manufacture to Yahweh's command and narrates life
for those who look at it. Second Kings 18:4 identifies the bronze serpent Moses made
among the objects Hezekiah destroys, because Israelites have been burning incense to it;
the verse also gives the name Nehushtan.

The tension is explicit: an object made under divine direction becomes an object
destroyed in reform. The texts give different uses, looking in response to a promise and
burning incense. Numbers does not authorize the latter or describe perpetual veneration.
Second Kings does not say that Moses disobeyed by making it. These distinctions preserve
both accounts without erasing either the commanded image or the later destruction.

The stored Numbers WEB says brass; the verified 2 Kings WEB says bronze. No claim about
the precise alloy or underlying Hebrew is made here.

Sources: stored WEB Numbers 21:4–9; bible-api.com WEB 2 Kings 18:4, verified 2026-11-04.
