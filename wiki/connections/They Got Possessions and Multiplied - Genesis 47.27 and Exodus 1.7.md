# They Got Possessions and Multiplied - Genesis 47.27 and Exodus 1.7

## Passages involved

- **Genesis 1:22, 28** — *"Be fruitful, multiply"*: the blessing on the creatures and on humankind —
  [[The Creation of the World]]
- **Genesis 8:17** — the blessing reissued to the creatures leaving the ark — [[The Waters Recede]]
- **Genesis 9:1, 7** — and to Noah and his sons, twice in one paragraph —
  [[The Covenant with Noah]]
- **Genesis 17:6; 28:3; 35:11; 48:4** — *"I will make you fruitful"*: the blessing narrowed to one
  family
- **Genesis 46:3** — *"there I will make of you a great nation"*, spoken at [[Beersheba]] on the way
  out of the land — [[Jacob's Family Settles in Egypt]]
- **Genesis 47:27** — *"they got themselves possessions therein, and were fruitful, and multiplied
  exceedingly"* — [[Joseph and the Famine]]
- **Exodus 1:7** — the same sentence in five verbs, immediately before *"there arose a new king over
  Egypt"*
- Compare **Acts 7:17** — *"the people grew and multiplied in Egypt"*, Stephen's summary
- Compare **Jeremiah 29:6** — *"multiply there, and don't be diminished"*, the same command to exiles
  in Babylon

## Analysis

***Pərû ûrəbû*** — ***be fruitful and multiply*** — **is the first thing God says to anything alive
in the Bible** (1:22), **the first thing he says to humankind** (1:28), **and the first thing he says
to the survivors of the flood** (9:1, 7). **Genesis 47:27 is the first time the book reports it as
having actually happened to Israel**, and it happens in Egypt.

> **47:27** Israel lived in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen; and they got themselves
> possessions therein, and were fruitful, and multiplied exceedingly.

**Three verbs, and each of them is a promise being cashed.**

- ***"Lived"*** — ***wayyēšeb***, *settled*, the ordinary verb of taking up residence. **It is what
  the patriarchs never quite did in [[Canaan]]**, where the standing description is *sojourning*
  (17:8; 28:4; 37:1; 47:9).
- ***"Got themselves possessions therein"*** — ***wayyēʾāḥăzû bāh***, **from the root of *ʾăḥuzzâ*,
  the word Genesis has used for exactly two things**: the cave at [[Machpelah]] (23:9, 20) and the
  grant at [[Rameses]] sixteen verses earlier (47:11). **The family's holdings in the land of the
  promise amount to a grave; its holdings in Egypt are a district.**
- ***"Were fruitful, and multiplied exceedingly"*** — ***wayyiprû wayyirbû məʾōd***, **the creation
  blessing verbatim**, with the intensifier.

**The promise of 12:2 and 46:3 — *a great nation* — is not fulfilled in the land it was made about.
It is fulfilled in Goshen, on the far side of a border God told them to cross.** See [[Covenant]] and
[[Land and Sojourning]].

### The same sentence, said twice, four hundred years apart

**Exodus opens by repeating Genesis 47:27 and enlarging it.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Genesis 47:27** | *"were **fruitful**, and **multiplied** exceedingly"* — two verbs |
| **Exodus 1:7** | *"were **fruitful**, and **increased abundantly**, and **multiplied**, and **grew exceedingly mighty**; and the land was filled with them"* — four verbs and a result clause |

***"Increased abundantly"*** is ***wayyišrəṣû***, **from *šereṣ*, the swarming creatures of Genesis
1:20–21 and 7:21.** **Exodus applies the vocabulary of teeming sea-life to a human population**, and
***"the land was filled with them"*** **is 1:28's *fill the earth* landing on a single province.**
**The creation blessing reaches its maximum expression in Scripture as a description of Israel in
Egypt**, and then:

> **Exodus 1:8** Now there arose a new king over Egypt, who didn't know Joseph. **9** He said to his
> people, "Behold, the people of the children of Israel are more and mightier than we."

**The blessing is the reason given for the persecution.** **Pharaoh's stated grievance is that the
command of Genesis 1:28 has been obeyed**, and his policy — midwives, the river, the quotas — is an
attempt to reverse it. **Exodus 1:12 reports the attempt failing in the blessing's own words**: *"But
the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and the more they spread out."*

### Where 47:27 sits in the book

**Genesis 47:27 is not a throwaway.** **It is the only verse between the arrival and the deathbed
chapters that says what happened during the seventeen years**, and it is placed immediately after the
sentence in which the Egyptians become Pharaoh's servants (47:25–26). **Genesis puts the fulfilment
of the creation blessing directly beside the enslavement of a nation and does not connect them.** See
[[Buy Us and Our Land - Egypt Sold into Servitude and Israel After It]].

**One chapter later [[Jacob]] quotes the promise back, in the form he received it at Luz**: *"Behold,
I will make you fruitful, and multiply you, and I will make of you a company of peoples"* (48:4). **He
says it in Egypt, to a grandson born in Egypt, about a land he is about to make his son swear to
carry him back to** (47:29–30).

## Significance

**The thread answers a question the Bible never puts directly: where did Israel become a nation?**
**The answer Genesis and Exodus give between them is *not in Canaan*.** **Seventy people go down
(46:27); a nation comes out.** **The multiplication that makes Abraham's offspring *as the stars* and
*as the sand* happens entirely on foreign soil, under a foreign crown, in a province granted by a
foreign king** — and then the crown that granted it tries to kill it.

**It also fixes the sense in which 47:27 is an ominous verse.** **Read alone it is pure blessing.**
**Read as the sentence Exodus 1:7 was written to echo, it is the first line of the bondage**, and
Genesis, which has just spent thirteen verses describing how Egypt came to own everyone in it, **puts
Israel's prosperity inside that country in the very next breath.** See [[Providence]] and
[[Four Hundred Years - The Sojourn and the Exodus]].
