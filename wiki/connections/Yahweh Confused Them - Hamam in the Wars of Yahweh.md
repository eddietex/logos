# Yahweh Confused Them - Hamam in the Wars of Yahweh

## Passages involved

- [[The Ark Among the Philistines]] — 1 Samuel 5:9, 11; the noun *məhûmâ*, *a very great confusion*, on Gath and Ekron.
- [[The Crossing of the Sea]] — Exodus 14:24; Yahweh *confused the Egyptian army*.
- Exodus 23:27 — *I will … confuse all the people to whom you come*.
- Deuteronomy 2:15; 7:23 — *Yahweh's hand was against them*; *will confuse them with a great confusion*.
- [[The Sun Stands Still at Gibeon]] — Joshua 10:10; *Yahweh confused them before Israel*.
- [[Deborah and Barak]] — Judges 4:15; *Yahweh confused Sisera, all his chariots, and all his army*.
- [[The Sword of Yahweh and of Gideon]] — Judges 7:19, 22; the same kind of rout told without the verb.
- [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]] — 1 Samuel 7:10; *Yahweh thundered … on the Philistines, and confused
  them*.
- [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]] — 1 Samuel 14:20; *striking each other with their swords in very great
  confusion*.

## Analysis

The verb is *hāmam* (WLC lemma 2000). A lemma search of the WLC files for Genesis, Exodus, Numbers,
Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, and 1 Samuel finds it seven times, and every time Yahweh is the subject
and an enemy army or people the object:

| Text | Subject | Object | WEB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exodus 14:24 | Yahweh | the camp of Egypt | *confused the Egyptian army* |
| Exodus 23:27 | Yahweh (promise) | all the people Israel comes to | *will confuse* |
| Deuteronomy 2:15 | Yahweh's hand | the wilderness generation's fighting men | *to destroy them* |
| Deuteronomy 7:23 | Yahweh (promise) | the nations of the land | *will confuse them with a great confusion* |
| Joshua 10:10 | Yahweh | the five kings' armies at Gibeon | *confused them* |
| Judges 4:15 | Yahweh | Sisera, his chariots, his camp | *confused* |
| 1 Samuel 7:10 | Yahweh | the Philistines | *confused them* |

**Exodus 14 and Judges 4.** The closest pair. Both are chariot armies: Pharaoh's *six hundred chosen
chariots, and all the chariots of Egypt* (Exodus 14:7), Sisera's *nine hundred chariots of iron*. In
both, Yahweh *confused* the camp (*wayyāhom … ʾet-maḥănēh*, Exodus 14:24; *wayyāhom …
ʾet-kol-hammaḥăneh*, Judges 4:15, WLC). Both end with the same clause: *lōʾ nišʾar bāhem ʿad-ʾeḥād*, *There
remained not so much as one of them* (Exodus 14:28), and *lōʾ nišʾar ʿad-ʾeḥād*, *There was not a man
left* (Judges 4:16). Both have water: the sea returns on Egypt, and the Song of Deborah says *The river
Kishon swept them away* (Judges 5:21). Both victories are followed by a song led by a woman called
*prophetess*; see [[The Prophetess - Miriam and Deborah]].

**The promise and its keeping.** Exodus 23:27 and Deuteronomy 7:23 promise the verb for the conquest.
Joshua 10:10 is the first time it is kept in the land, and Judges 4:15 the second. The verb never
belongs to Israel's army: Israel pursues and strikes, and Yahweh confuses.

**Deuteronomy 2:15 turns it around.** There the object is Israel's own fighting men, the generation
that refused to enter: *Yahweh's hand was against them, to destroy them from the middle of the camp*
(stored WEB). The WEB translates *hāmam* there as *destroy*. The same act Yahweh does to Israel's
enemies he did to Israel.

**1 Samuel 7:10 adds thunder.** *Wayyarʿēm YHWH bəqôl-gādôl … wayhummēm* (WLC): Yahweh thunders and
confuses. Judges 4 names no weather, but the Song has *the clouds dropped water* (5:4) and *From the
sky the stars fought* (5:20). The commander's name, Barak, is usually read as *lightning*; that is
reported, not verified, and the text does not make the pun.

**Judges 7 without the verb.** Gideon's battle is the next rout Judges tells, and *hāmam* does not occur
in it (WLC lemma search of Judges 7). It says instead what the confusion was: *Yahweh set every man's
sword against his fellow, and against all the army* (7:22, stored WEB). It shares one noun with Exodus
14:24. *ʾAšmōret*, the night *watch*, occurs in Genesis–Judges only there, *in the morning watch*, and at
Judges 7:19, *the beginning of the middle watch* (WLC). In both nights Yahweh acts against an enemy camp,
*maḥăneh*, at a named watch. Later accounts describe enemies striking each other in the same way,
*striking each other with their swords in very great confusion* (1 Samuel 14:20) and *everyone helped to
destroy another* (2 Chronicles 20:23) (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested; Hebrew not
checked). See [[The Sword of Yahweh and of Gideon]].

## Significance

The verb marks the battles where Israel's victory is said to be Yahweh's act and not Israel's
strength, and especially the battles against chariots. Judges 1:19 left Judah unable, or unwilling,
to face chariots of iron; Judges 4 has nine hundred of them destroyed in one day, and gives the reason
in one verb. See [[War]], [[Forest to Clear and Chariots to Face]],
[[Yahweh Fights for Israel - The Promise and the Day at Gibeon]], and [[Signs and Wonders]].

### The noun in 1 Samuel 5

The ark in Philistia brings *məhûmâ gəḏôlâ məʾōḏ*, *a very great confusion*, on Gath, and *məhûmaṯ māweṯ*, *a
deadly confusion*, on Ekron (1 Samuel 5:9, 11, WLC; stored WEB). Morphhb gives the noun its own lemma (4103),
separate from the verb *hāmam* (2000); the two are commonly treated as related, which is reported here, not
argued. In the WLC files searched, the noun occurs at Deuteronomy 7:23, *will confuse them with a great
confusion*, *wəhāmām məhûmâ gəḏōlâ*, where verb and noun stand together; at Deuteronomy 28:20 among the
curses on Israel; at 1 Samuel 5:9, 11; and at 1 Samuel 14:20 (lemma search). No Israelite army fights in 1
Samuel 5; the confusion comes with the ark alone. See
[[Tumors and Confusion - The Covenant Curses on Ashdod, Gath, and Ekron]]. Sources: stored WEB Deuteronomy
7:23; 28:20; 1 Samuel 5:9, 11; 14:20; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17, files `Gen` through
`Ruth`, `1Sam`, `2Sam`, `1Kgs`, `Ps`, `Zeph`.

### 1 Samuel 7 ingested — confusion and the hand of Yahweh

Read in place, 1 Samuel 7:10 is the battle of a prayer: the confusion comes *as Samuel was offering up the burnt
offering*, after he *cried to Yahweh for Israel* (7:9–10), and Israel only pursues (7:11). Three verses later the
narrator adds *Yahweh's hand was against the Philistines*, *wattəhî yaḏ-YHWH bappəlištîm* (7:13, WLC). Deuteronomy
2:15, the verse in the table above where Israel's own fighting men are the object, joins the same two phrases:
*Yahweh's hand was against them, to destroy them*, *yaḏ-YHWH hāyəṯâ bām ləhummām* (WLC). 1 Samuel 7 has the hand
and the confusion together again, this time against Israel's enemy. See [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]] and
[[He Will Thunder Against Them - Hannah's Song and Mizpah]]. Sources: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:15; 1 Samuel
7:9–13. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### 1 Samuel 14 ingested — the swords of Michmash

1 Samuel 14:20, cited above from bible-api.com before 1 Samuel was stored, is now stored WEB and checked in Hebrew:
*ḥereḇ ʾîš bərēʿēhû məhûmâ gəḏôlâ məʾōḏ* (WLC), *every man's sword against his fellow, very great confusion*. The
first three words are Judges 7:22's *ḥereḇ ʾîš bərēʿēhû* (WLC), and the noun is Deuteronomy 7:23's *məhûmâ gəḏōlâ*.
The verb *hāmam* is absent, and so is a stated subject: at Judges 7:22 *Yahweh set* the swords, while 1 Samuel 14:20
reports only what Saul's men found when they came to the battle. Yahweh is named in the verses around it, in
Jonathan's *It may be that Yahweh will work for us* (14:6), in *ḥerdaṯ ʾĕlōhîm*, *a trembling of God* (14:15), and
in the narrator's *So Yahweh saved Israel that day* (14:23). See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]] and
[[Yahweh Saved Israel That Day - The Sea and the Pass at Michmash]]. Sources: stored WEB Deuteronomy 7:23; Judges
7:22; 1 Samuel 14:6, 15, 20, 23. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-19.
