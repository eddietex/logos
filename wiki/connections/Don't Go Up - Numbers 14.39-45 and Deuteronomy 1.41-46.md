# Don't Go Up - Numbers 14.39-45 and Deuteronomy 1.41-46

## Passages involved

- [[The Defeat at Hormah]] — Numbers 14:39–45
- Deuteronomy 1:41–46 — Moses' retelling *(WEB checked, not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

Moses tells this story a second time on the plains of Moab, to the children of the men who went up.
The events agree, and the details do not.

| | Numbers 14 | Deuteronomy 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Confession | *for we have sinned* (40) | *We have sinned against Yahweh* (41) |
| The plan | *go up to the place* (40) | *we will go up and fight, according to all that Yahweh our God commanded us* (41) |
| Preparation | they rise early (40) | *Every man of you put on his weapons of war* (41) |
| The warning | Moses speaks (41–43) | Yahweh tells Moses what to say (42) |
| The reason | *Yahweh isn't among you* (42) | *I am not among you* (42) |
| The act | *wayyaʿpilû*, *they presumed* (44) | *wattamrû ʾet-pî YHWH … wattāzīdû* (43) |
| The enemy | Amalekite and Canaanite (45) | Amorites (44) |
| The image | none | *chased you, as bees do* (44) |
| The place | *even to Hormah* (45) | *in Seir, even to Hormah* (44) |
| Afterward | not stated | *wept before Yahweh; but Yahweh didn't listen* (45) |
| The ark and Moses | stay in the camp (44) | not mentioned |

**The command.** Deuteronomy's added phrase is the sharpest point in the comparison. The people cite
the command to go up as if it still stood, and the same retelling has just reported the sentence that
ended it (1:34–40). What was obedience the day before is rebellion today, because the word has
changed. Deuteronomy 1:43 uses for this the same phrase, *the mouth of Yahweh*, with a different verb,
*mārâ*, which Yahweh uses of Moses and Aaron at Numbers 20:24 and 27:14, *mərîtem … pî*.

**The words for presumption.** Numbers and Deuteronomy use different verbs, *ʿāpal* and *zîd*, and
WEB uses *presumed* or *presumptuous* for both. WEB also has *presumed* at Deuteronomy 1:41, where
the Hebrew is *wattāhînû*, a third verb. Rahlfs has *διαβιασάμενοι* in Numbers and *παραβιασάμενοι* at
Deuteronomy 1:43, close to one another.

**Greek.** Rahlfs adds to Numbers 14:45 that they returned to the camp. At Deuteronomy 1:44 it has
*from Seir to Herma*, against WLC's *in Seir*, and at 1:45 *καθίσαντες*, *sitting*, against
*wattāšubû*, *you returned*.

## Significance

- The two accounts agree that the people confessed, were told not to go, went, and lost.
- Numbers gives the reason as a fact about the ark and Moses. Deuteronomy gives it as Yahweh's refusal
  to hear afterward.
- The wiki keeps the differences as they stand. See [[Who Sent the Spies - Numbers 13 and Deuteronomy 1]]
  for the same retelling a few verses earlier.
