# Afraid and Dismayed - Deuteronomy 1.21, Joshua 1.9, and 1 Samuel 17.11

## Analysis

**The word.** *Ḥāṯaṯ*, *be dismayed, shattered* (morphhb lemma 2865). In the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through
2 Samuel, and Ruth) it occurs in six verses, the six above (WLC). Five are commands in the negative, *ʾal tēḥāṯ* or
*lōʾ ṯēḥāṯ*. The sixth, 1 Samuel 17:11, is narration: *wayyēḥattû wayyirʾû məʾōḏ*, *they were dismayed, and greatly
afraid* (WLC).

**The pair.** In four of the five commands *ḥāṯaṯ* stands beside *yārēʾ*, *fear*: Deuteronomy 1:21 and 31:8, Joshua
8:1 and 10:25. Joshua 1:9 pairs it with *ʿāraṣ*, *tremble*, *ʾal-taʿărōṣ wəʾal-tēḥāṯ* (WLC), which the WEB renders
*Don't be afraid*. The WEB gives *ḥāṯaṯ* as *dismayed* four times and *discouraged* once (Deuteronomy 31:8). 1 Samuel
17:11 has the same two verbs as the four, in the reverse order, as statements.

**The reasons given.** Each command gives a reason. Yahweh *has set the land before you* (Deuteronomy 1:21); *Yahweh
himself is who goes before you. He will be with you* (31:8); *Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go* (Joshua
1:9); *I have given into your hand the king of Ai* (8:1); *Yahweh will do this to all your enemies* (10:25). In 1
Samuel 17:1–11 no one says anything about Yahweh at all; neither *Yahweh* nor *God* occurs in those verses (WLC). The
first to speak of God is David, *the armies of the living God* (17:26, stored WEB; not yet ingested).

**At Kadesh, too.** Deuteronomy 1:21 is Moses' retelling of the command given before the spies, and the people's
refusal follows: *The people are greater and taller than we … Moreover we have seen the sons of the Anakim
there!* (1:28, stored WEB). In 1 Samuel 17 the man before Israel is from Gath, where the Anakim remnant stayed (Joshua 11:22). The
narrator of 1 Samuel does not cite Deuteronomy; the wiki notes the shared verbs and the shared kind of enemy.

## Significance

The command not to be dismayed is given five times in the Torah and Joshua, always with Yahweh's presence or promise
as its ground. 1 Samuel 17:11 is the one verse in these books where Israel is said to be dismayed, and it comes in a
scene where that ground is not mentioned. See [[War]], [[Saul]], and
[[Your Eyes Have Seen - Remembered Victory and Joshua’s Courage]].
