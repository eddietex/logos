# Commands Answered with Curses - Leviticus 18-19 and Deuteronomy 27

## Passages involved

- [[Forbidden Unions and the Defiled Land]] — Leviticus 18.
- [[Be Holy and Love Your Neighbor]] — Leviticus 19:3–4, 14–18.
- [[Landmarks, Witnesses, and False Testimony]] — Deuteronomy 19:14.
- [[The People Answer Amen to the Curses]] — Deuteronomy 27:15–26.

## Analysis

The curse list gives familiar obligations a responsive ceremonial form. Respect for parents
and rejection of manufactured gods (Leviticus 19:3–4) correspond to Deuteronomy 27:15–16.
The ban on putting a stumbling block before the blind (Leviticus 19:14) is comparable to the
curse on misleading the blind on the road (Deuteronomy 27:18), though the acts are described
differently. Impartial judgment and protection of the neighbor’s life (Leviticus 19:15–16)
stand beside the curses on withheld justice and secret or purchased killing (27:19, 24–25).
Deuteronomy 19:14’s landmark prohibition receives a curse in 27:17.

The father’s wife, sister, and animal prohibitions of Leviticus 18:8–9, 23 recur in
Deuteronomy 27:20–22. Leviticus 18:17 prohibits taking a woman and her daughter; Deuteronomy
27:23 names the mother-in-law. These are overlapping boundaries expressed in different
forms, not a verse-for-verse reproduction of Leviticus or a complete sexual law code.

The selected cases culminate in doing the whole law (27:26). Only verses 15 and 24 explicitly
mention secrecy, so describing every item as necessarily secret would overstate the text.

## Significance

The community must assent to obligations protecting worship, households, property, and vulnerable neighbors together.
