# As God Commanded, So He Did - The Obedience Formula

## Passages involved

- **Genesis 6:22** — *"Thus Noah did. He did all that God commanded him."* —
  [[Noah and the Flood]]
- **Exodus 7:6** — *"Moses and Aaron did so. As Yahweh commanded them, so they did."* —
  [[Aaron's Staff Before Pharaoh]]
- **Exodus 7:10, 20** — *"they did so, as Yahweh had commanded"*
- **Exodus 12:28, 50** — *"as Yahweh had commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did"* — the Passover;
  12:28 at [[The Passover]], **the first time the formula is used of the whole people**; 12:50 at
  [[The Death of the Firstborn and the Departure]], where it follows the second Passover law and is
  preceded by *"All the children of Israel did so"* — the formula twice of the whole people in one chapter
- **Exodus 39:32, 42–43; 40:16** — the finished tabernacle: *"The children of Israel did according to
  all that Yahweh commanded Moses; so they did"*; *"Moses did so. According to all that Yahweh
  commanded him, so he did."* (not yet ingested)

## Analysis

**The formula is *kəkōl ʾăšer ṣiwwâ … kēn ʿāśâ*, *"according to all that he commanded … so he
did"*.** It has two halves, and the second repeats the first: *did* at the start, *so he did* at the
end. **It is the narrator's way of saying that nothing was added and nothing left out.**

**Genesis uses the full form once, of Noah** (6:22, *kēn ʿāśâ*). Genesis 7:5 repeats the thought
with *Yahweh* and without the closing *kēn ʿāśâ*. **The obedience is the building of the ark** —
a structure built to a divine specification, with measurements.

**Exodus uses it first of Moses and Aaron, at 7:6**, after five objections at the bush, a complaint
at 5:22–23, and *"uncircumcised lips"* twice. **Its first use of Moses comes the moment he stops
objecting.** Then it is used of the whole people at the first Passover (12:28, 50), and finally of
the tabernacle (39:32, 42–43; 40:16), **where it is repeated until it becomes the structure of the
closing chapters**. (WLC search of Genesis and Exodus for *ṣiwwâ* with *kēn ʿāśâ*; Genesis 42:25
and 50:12 use the same verbs for obedience to Joseph and to Jacob, not to God.)

**The pattern is ark → plagues → Passover → tabernacle.** The formula marks the moments when a
human being does exactly what God specified — **and two of the four are buildings to a pattern**:
the ark and the tabernacle.

## Significance

**The formula connects Noah and Moses as builders of the things that carry God's people through
judgment and keep God among them.** The text draws the verbal link; the wiki notes the shared
setting — a structure built to God's dimensions — as an observation, not a claim the text makes.
See [[The Flood and Baptism]] for the ark's other afterlife.
