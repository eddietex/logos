# Six Hundred Three Thousand Five Hundred Fifty - Exodus 38.26 and Numbers 1.46

## Passages involved

- [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]] — Numbers 3:43; the 22,273 firstborn
- [[The Death of the Firstborn and the Departure]] — Exodus 12:37; *about six hundred thousand on
  foot who were men, besides children*
- [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]] — Exodus 38:25–26; *six hundred three
  thousand five hundred fifty men*
- [[The Census in the Wilderness of Sinai]] — Numbers 1:46; the same figure, by tribe
- [[The Camp Around the Tent]] — Numbers 2:32; the same figure again, by camp
- Numbers 11:21 — *six hundred thousand men on foot*, Moses speaking *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*
- Numbers 26:51 — *six hundred one thousand seven hundred thirty*, the second census *(not yet
  ingested; stored WEB)*

## Analysis

**One exact number appears in two books.** Exodus gives it as a total of payers, and Numbers as the
sum of twelve tribal figures. The twelve figures at Numbers 1:21–43 add to 603,550 exactly, and
half a shekel from each gives the 301,775 shekels of Exodus 38:25–26, assuming the 3,000-shekel
talent that the account implies. The figure is tied into both accounts by arithmetic and is not a
loose round number. It is close to the *about six hundred thousand* of Exodus 12:37 and
Numbers 11:21.

**The order of events is not explained.** Exodus 38 stands before the tabernacle is raised on the
first day of the first month (Exodus 40:17). Numbers 1 commands the count on the first day of the
second month. The two passages may be the same count told from two sides, or the silver account may
look ahead, or there may be two counts with one result. Neither text says.

**The ransom is missing from Numbers.** Exodus 30:12 ties a census to a ransom. Exodus 38:25–26
shows it collected. Numbers 1 mentions no silver. See
[[The Half Shekel - Census, Ransom, and the Temple Tax]].

**The size of the figure.** The wiki recorded three answers at
[[The Death of the Firstborn and the Departure]]: take it as it stands, read it as schematic, or
read *ʾelep* as a clan or unit. It adopts none. Any answer has to account for both sides of the
evidence: the interlocking arithmetic above reads as plain numbers, and *rāʾšê ʾalpê yiśrāʾēl* at
Numbers 1:16 shows *thousands* can also name groups led by a head. The 22,273 firstborn males of
Numbers 3:43 are hard to fit with this total on any plain reading.

## Significance

- The second census differs by only 1,820 (603,550 and 601,730), after a whole generation has died.
  See [[The Wilderness]].
- The figure counts men fit for war, so it is not the population. See [[War]].
