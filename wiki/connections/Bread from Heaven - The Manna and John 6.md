# Bread from Heaven - The Manna and John 6

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 16:4, 15** — *"Behold, I will rain bread from the sky for you"*; *"It is the bread which Yahweh has
  given you to eat."* — [[Manna and Quail]]
- **Exodus 16:2, 7–8** — the congregation *"murmured"*; *"Your murmurings are not against us, but against
  Yahweh."* — [[Manna and Quail]]
- **Psalm 78:24–25** — *"He rained down manna on them to eat, and gave them food from the sky. Man ate the bread
  of angels."* (WEB; *not yet ingested*)
- **Nehemiah 9:15** — *"and gave them bread from the sky for their hunger"* (WEB; *not yet ingested*)
- **John 6:26–58** — the discourse at Capernaum after the feeding of the five thousand (WEB; *not yet
  ingested*)

## Analysis

**John 6 is a commentary on Exodus 16, delivered in the form of an argument.** The crowd raises the manna
first, quoting Scripture: *"Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness. As it is written, 'He gave them bread
out of heaven to eat'"* (John 6:31; WEB). **The quotation matches no single verse exactly**; it is closest
to Psalm 78:24 and Nehemiah 9:15, and behind both to Exodus 16:4, 15 (WEB of all four compared; the Greek
form of the quotation is not checked in this pass). Jesus answers point by point.

| | Exodus 16 | John 6 (WEB) |
|---|---|---|
| The giver | *"the bread which **Yahweh** has given you"* (16:15) — after the people credited the exodus to Moses and Aaron: *"you have brought us out"* (16:3) | *"it wasn't **Moses** who gave you the bread out of heaven, but **my Father** gives you the true bread out of heaven"* (6:32) |
| The bread | *"bread from the sky"*; *"What is it?"* (16:4, 15) | *"I am the bread of life"* (6:35, 48); *"the living bread which came down out of heaven"* (6:51) |
| The complaint | *"The whole congregation … murmured against Moses and against Aaron"* (16:2) | *"The Jews therefore murmured concerning him"*; *"Don't murmur among yourselves"* (6:41, 43) |
| What spoils | kept overnight, *"it bred worms, and became foul"* (16:20) | *"Don't work for the food which perishes, but for the food which remains to eternal life"* (6:27) |
| Being filled | *"in the morning you shall be filled with bread"* (16:12) | *"you ate of the loaves, and were filled"* (6:26) |
| The end of it | *"The children of Israel ate the manna forty years"* (16:35) | *"Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died"* (6:49); *"He who eats this bread will live forever"* (6:58) |

**Three of the rows are explicit and three are the readers'.** John names the manna, the giver, and the
fathers' death outright. **The murmuring, the perishing food, and the filling are verbal echoes in English
and are widely read as deliberate** (reported, not decided; the Greek of John 6 and of the Greek Exodus is not
checked in this pass).

**The giver row is the closest to the text of Exodus.** Moses himself said the bread was Yahweh's gift
(16:15) and asked *"Who are we?"* (16:7–8) when the people blamed him and Aaron. **Jesus' correction — *"it
wasn't Moses"* — is the correction Moses made.** What John adds is the identity of the bread.

**The difference John insists on is death.** Exodus says the manna lasted forty years and stopped at the
border of Canaan (16:35; Joshua 5:12); it fed a generation that, in the Torah's account, died in the
wilderness (Numbers 14; *not yet ingested*). John draws that line twice (6:49, 58). **The manna is
treated as real bread from God that did what bread does, and no more.**

**Other New Testament uses** (WEB; *not yet ingested*): Paul says the fathers *"all ate the same spiritual
food"* (1 Corinthians 10:3); Hebrews puts *"a golden pot holding the manna"* in the ark (Hebrews 9:4); and the
risen Christ promises *"the hidden manna"* to the one who overcomes (Revelation 2:17), usually read as the
jar laid up *"before the Testimony"* (Exodus 16:33–34) made eschatological (reported, not decided). Deuteronomy
gives the Old Testament's own reading — *"that he might teach you that man does not live by bread only, but
man lives by every word that proceeds out of Yahweh's mouth"* (8:3) — which Jesus quotes in the wilderness
(Matthew 4:4).

## Significance

**Exodus 16 already pointed past the food.** Its purpose clauses are about knowledge — *"you shall know that
Yahweh has brought you out"*, *"you shall know that I am Yahweh your God"* (16:6, 12) — and its name for the
food is a question, *"What is it?"* (16:15). **Deuteronomy 8:3 and John 6 both answer the question the
manna was named for**, one with *"every word that proceeds out of Yahweh's mouth"*, the other with *"I am the
bread of life"*. The wiki records the typology as the New Testament's own, and the further readings —
eucharistic, and the hidden manna as the kept jar — as readings.
