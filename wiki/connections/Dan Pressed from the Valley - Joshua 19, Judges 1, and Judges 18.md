# Dan Pressed from the Valley - Joshua 19, Judges 1, and Judges 18

## Passages involved
- [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]] — Joshua 19:40–48; Dan's cities, and Leshem taken (stored WEB).
- [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]] — Judges 1:34–35 (stored WEB, now ingested).
- Judges 18:1 — the Danite migration; stored WEB, not yet ingested.

## Analysis

Three texts tell why and how Dan left its first lot, each with a different emphasis.

| Text | What it says |
|---|---|
| Joshua 19:41–42 | Dan's border list includes *Shaalabbin, Aijalon* among its cities |
| Joshua 19:47 | *The border of the children of Dan went out beyond them*; Dan takes Leshem and calls it Dan |
| Judges 1:34 | *The Amorites forced the children of Dan into the hill country, for they would not allow them to come down to the valley* |
| Judges 1:35 | The Amorites *would dwell* in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim, until the house of Joseph subjects them |
| Judges 18:1 | *Their inheritance had not fallen to them among the tribes of Israel* |

Judges 1:35's *Shaalbim* and Joshua 19:42's *Shaalabbin* carry the same lemma in the WLC's tagging,
and Aijalon is in both. So the cities the Amorites hold in Judges are cities on Dan's list in Joshua.
Dan is pushed off its own allotment.

The three accounts differ on how the move should be read. Joshua 19:47 reads as expansion: Dan's
border *went out beyond them* (*wayyēṣēʾ gəḇûl*, WLC), and the conquest of Leshem is told in the full
formula of a successful campaign, *took it, and struck it … and possessed it, and lived therein*. Judges
1:34 reads as pressure: the Amorites *pressed* (*lāḥaṣ*, WLC) Dan into the hills. Judges 18:1 reads as
lack: the inheritance *had not fallen* to them. The texts do not reconcile these. A reader can take
Joshua's verse as the outcome and Judges' as the cause; the texts do not say so.

Dan is also the one tribe in Judges 1 that is not the subject of *did not drive out*. Every other tribe
in 1:27–33 fails to act; Dan is acted on. The house of Joseph, not Dan, finally puts the Amorites to
forced labor (1:35).

Sources: stored WEB Joshua 19:40–48; Judges 1:34–35; 18:1. Hebrew of Joshua 19:42, 47 and Judges
1:34–35 read in the WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.

## Significance

The chapter's last tribe is the one that loses its land. Judges 17–18 will follow Dan north and tell
the migration in full. That account is not yet ingested, and this page will need to be revisited
when it is.
