# To Seek Out the Land - Tur in Numbers 10, 13, 15, and Ezekiel 20

## Passages involved

- [[The March from Sinai]] — Numbers 10:33; the ark goes before them *to seek out a resting place*
- [[The Twelve Spies]] — Numbers 13:2, 16, 17, 21, 25, 32
- [[The People Refuse the Land]] — Numbers 14:6, 7, 34, 36, 38; the same verb in the sequel
- [[The Tassels]] — Numbers 15:37–41; the warning about heart and eyes
- [[The Spies and the Refusal to Go Up]] — Deuteronomy 1:33, Yahweh going before them to seek out a place *(WLC; see
  [[Over the Tent and Before the People - Where the Cloud Stood]])*
- Judges 1:23 — *The house of Joseph sent to spy out Bethel* *(WEB and WLC checked, not yet
  ingested)*
- Ecclesiastes 1:13 — *to search out by wisdom* *(WEB and WLC checked, not yet ingested)*
- Ezekiel 20:6 — *a land that I had searched out for them* *(WEB and WLC checked, not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

**Numbers 15 now ingested.** The full unit makes the fringe a positive direction for sight:
look, remember, and do (39–40), grounded in the exodus (41). The Hebrew comparison below
retains the earlier entry’s WLC attribution; this ingest verifies the WEB context only.
The connection of the command with the spies remains a literary inference.

**Not the spy word.** Genesis 42 has *məraggəlîm*, and Joshua 2:1 has the same word for Joshua's two
men. Numbers 13 uses *tûr* instead. That verb has 22 verses by the tags, and most of them are in
Numbers 13–15, so WEB's *spy out* reads a military sense into a broader word of going about to look.
Rahlfs uses *κατασκέπτομαι*.

**God seeks first.** Three chapters before the spies, the ark goes *lātûr lāhem mənûḥâ* (10:33,
WLC). Deuteronomy 1:33 gives the same act to Yahweh. Ezekiel 20:6 says Yahweh himself did it: *ʾereṣ
ʾăšer-tartî lāhem*, *a land that I had searched out for them, flowing with milk and honey*. So the
search the twelve carry out for forty days has been done already, on Ezekiel's telling, and his
phrase has the same *milk and honey*.

**Then the same verb turns inward.** Numbers 15:39 comes after the sentence on the spies: *wəlōʾ
tātûrû ʾaḥărê ləbabkem wəʾaḥărê ʿênêkem*, WEB *that you don't follow your own heart and your own
eyes*. The tassels are given to stop the very thing the word has been doing. The chapter order may
be meant to connect them. The text does not say so. The link is often noted in commentary
(recalled).

**The wisdom use.** Ecclesiastes 1:13 pairs it with *dāraš*. A king's investigation of everything
under the sky ends in *a heavy burden*. A search by one's own eyes has the same limit there, though
no link with Numbers is implied.

## Significance

- The word marks a contrast in Numbers: Yahweh's seeking out goes ahead of Israel, and Israel's
  seeking out ends in *We aren't able*.
- It is lost in English, since WEB renders it *seek out*, *spy out*, *follow*, and *search out*.
