# The Basin Made and Used - Exodus 30, 38, and 40

## Passages involved

- [[The Incense Altar, the Ransom, and the Holy Oil]] — Exodus 30:17–21; vessel and washing prescribed.
- [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]] — Exodus 38:8; basin and base made from mirrors.
- Exodus 40:7, 30–32 — placement, water, and actual washing; checked in local raw WEB, not yet ingested.

## Analysis

Exodus 30:18 commands a brass basin and base between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, with water
for washing. Aaron and his sons are to wash hands and feet before entering the tent or approaching
the altar (30:19–21). The danger attached to approach gives the equipment its purpose before
manufacture is narrated.

Exodus 38:8 reports the making and adds information absent from the command: the metal comes from
the mirrors of the ministering women at the Tent of Meeting’s door. The verse describes their
ministry without defining its duties or identifying it with priesthood. It neither condemns their
mirrors nor makes their donation an act of washing. See [[Ministering Women (of Moses' time)]].

Exodus 40:30–32 reports the placement, water, and washing of hands and feet. Moses is explicitly
included with Aaron and his sons in 40:31, whereas 30:19 names Aaron and his sons. Both statements
are retained; the later account includes Moses without expressly revising the standing rule. Chapter
38 alone does not narrate any of them using the basin.

The basin does not appear in 38:30–31’s brass-use list. Its separately named mirror source may
explain separate treatment, but no basin weight or express exclusion from the total is supplied. The
omission is not grounds to deny the basin’s manufacture.

The meeting-tent reference also leaves the setting of the women’s service open: the outside tent of
33:7–11 precedes the erection in 40:17, but 38:8 does not expressly identify which stage its
designation reflects. See [[The Tent Outside the Camp and the Promised Dwelling]].

Sources: Exodus 30:17–21; 33:7–11; 38:8, 29–31; 40:7, 17, 30–32 checked in `raw/bible/Exodus.md`
(WEB), 2026-10-25. The comparison is paraphrased; no source-language or later typological claim is
made.

## Significance

[[Clean and Unclean]], [[Holiness]], and [[Priesthood]] develop through prescription, material
preparation, and actual washing. [[Work]] and [[Wealth and Possessions]] enter through the women’s
contribution. Keeping these stages distinct preserves both their service and the particular actions
assigned to Moses, Aaron, and his sons.
