## Analysis

### The command at Nebo

Deuteronomy 32:50 explicitly places Aaron’s death on Mount Hor. The location conflict is therefore
also internal to Deuteronomy: 10:6 names Moserah, while 32:50 agrees with Numbers 20:28 and 33:38.
The new witness strengthens the Mount Hor attestation but supplies no identification of Moserah with
it. Both claims remain recorded as unresolved.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 32:48–52; earlier stored passages cited below.

Deuteronomy 10:6 locates Aaron’s death and burial at Moserah after travel from Beeroth Bene
Jaakan. Numbers 20:22–29 locates his death on Mount Hor by Edom, reached from Kadesh;
Numbers 33:37–39 repeats Mount Hor and dates the death to the fortieth year.
Both accounts continue the priesthood through Eleazar, explicitly in Deuteronomy 10:6 and
through the transfer of garments in Numbers 20:25–28.

**The location claims conflict unless an additional geographic or travel explanation is supplied.**
Neither passage identifies Moserah with Mount Hor. Treating one as a district containing the
other or proposing a return journey is a possible harmonization, not information these accounts
provide. The wiki therefore retains both claims and marks the question unresolved.

The itinerary also differs. Numbers 33:30–33 orders Moseroth, Bene Jaakan, Hor Haggidgad,
Jotbathah. Deuteronomy 10:6–7 orders Beeroth Bene Jaakan, Moserah, Gudgodah, Jotbathah.
If the similar names correspond, the first two stations occur in reverse order. Separate
place pages record the possible correspondences without turning resemblance into certainty.

Sources: stored WEB Deuteronomy 10:6–7; Numbers 20:22–29; 33:30–39.

## Significance

Succession is common to the narratives; the place and travel sequence are not.
[[Aaron]], [[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]], [[Moserah]], and [[Mount Hor]] retain this distinction.
