# Filled with His Glory - Numbers 14.21, Psalm 72, Isaiah 6, and Habakkuk 2

## Passages involved

- [[The People Refuse the Land]] — Numbers 14:21
- Psalm 72:19 — *Let the whole earth be filled with his glory!* *(WEB and WLC checked, not yet
  ingested)*
- Isaiah 6:3 — *The whole earth is full of his glory!* *(WEB and WLC checked, not yet ingested)*
- Isaiah 11:9 — the earth full of the knowledge of Yahweh *(WLC checked, not yet ingested)*
- Habakkuk 2:14 — *the earth will be filled with the knowledge of Yahweh's glory* *(WEB and WLC
  checked, not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

The sentence appears four ways, and the verb's form changes the sense.

| Text | Hebrew (WLC) | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Numbers 14:21 | *wəyimmālēʾ kəbôd-YHWH ʾet-kol-hāʾāreṣ* | sworn |
| Psalm 72:19 | *wəyimmālēʾ kəbôdô ʾet-kōl hāʾāreṣ* | prayed |
| Isaiah 6:3 | *məlōʾ kol-hāʾāreṣ kəbôdô* | proclaimed as present |
| Habakkuk 2:14 | *timmālēʾ hāʾāreṣ lādaʿat ʾet-kəbôd YHWH* | promised, with *knowledge* |

**Psalm 72:19 is verbally nearest to Numbers.** Only the name has changed to a suffix. It closes a
royal psalm and a book of the Psalter. Whether it depends on Numbers cannot be shown.

**Numbers has the strangest setting.** The line comes in an oath of exclusion. A generation is told
it will not see the land, and in the same breath Yahweh swears by his life about the earth. Its
point seems to be that the refusal does not limit his purpose. That reading is the wiki's, as the
syntax of the verse is not plain. See [[The People Refuse the Land]].

**Habakkuk adds knowing.** The glory fills the earth by being known, combining Numbers' line with
the likeness of Isaiah 11:9, *as the waters cover the sea*. Exodus 40:34 had the glory fill the
tabernacle, with *mālēʾ*, and these verses extend that word from a tent to the earth. That extension
is the wiki's observation. See [[The Glory of God]].

**Sources:** Hebrew from WLC (morphhb); WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-02.

## Significance

- What the tabernacle held in Exodus 40, Numbers swears for the whole earth, and the Prophets carry
  that forward.
