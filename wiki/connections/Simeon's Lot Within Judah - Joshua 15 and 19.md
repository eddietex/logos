# Simeon's Lot Within Judah - Joshua 15 and 19

## Passages involved
- [[The Cities of Judah]] — Joshua 15:20–63; Judah's towns by district.
- Joshua 19:1–9 — Simeon's lot; read in stored WEB, not yet ingested.
- [[Jacob Blesses His Sons]] — Genesis 49:5–7; Simeon and Levi to be scattered.
- [[Simeon and Levi - The Sword at Shechem and the Deathbed Curse]] — the thread this extends.

## Analysis

Joshua 19 gives Simeon no border of its own. Its lot is a list of towns, and the list explains
itself: *Out of the part of the children of Judah was the inheritance of the children of
Simeon; for the portion of the children of Judah was too much for them* (19:9). Most of the
towns had already been listed among Judah's southern cities, and two among its lowland towns:

| Simeon (Joshua 19) | Judah (Joshua 15) |
|---|---|
| Beersheba (or Sheba), Moladah (2) | [[Beersheba]] (28), [[Moladah]] (26) |
| Hazar Shual, Balah, Ezem (3) | [[Hazar Shual]] (28), [[Baalah (Negev)]]? (29), [[Ezem]] (29) |
| Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah (4) | [[Eltolad]] (30), [[Chesil]]? (30), [[Hormah]] (30) |
| Ziklag, Beth Marcaboth, Hazar Susah (5) | [[Ziklag]] (31), [[Madmannah]]? (31), [[Sansannah]]? (31) |
| Beth Lebaoth, Sharuhen (6) | [[Lebaoth]] (32); Sharuhen not listed |
| Ain, Rimmon, Ether, Ashan (7) | [[Ain (Negev)]], [[Rimmon (Negev)]] (32); [[Ether]], [[Ashan]] (42) |

A question mark marks a match suggested only by position or similar spelling; the wiki does not
equate those names. The exact matches are enough to show that Simeon's towns were taken out of
the list in Joshua 15 rather than added beside it.

Joshua 15 does not mention Simeon. Read alone, its southern district is wholly Judah's; read with
19:1–9, part of it is Simeon's. The two lists describe the same towns from two allotments, and
the second tells the reader how to hold them together.

Genesis 49:7 had said of Simeon and Levi, *I will divide them in Jacob, and scatter them in
Israel*. Joshua 19 does not cite the oracle, and the reason it gives is Judah's surplus, not
Simeon's guilt. The [[Simeon]] page already reads the dispersal as fulfilled in a lot set
inside Judah's; this connection records the lists that show it, and leaves the link between
oracle and allotment as the reader's inference rather than the text's statement. See also
[[Simeon and Levi - The Sword at Shechem and the Deathbed Curse]].

Sources: stored WEB Joshua 15:20–63 and 19:1–9; Genesis 49:5–7.

## Significance

The tribe of Simeon is given land without a frontier. Its towns sit inside another tribe's
register, and Joshua 15's southern total of *twenty-nine* against thirty-six listed names may or
may not relate to that sharing — the text does not say. What it does show is that the allotment
lists are working documents: the same town can be counted twice, once by territory and once by
tribe.
