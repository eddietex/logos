# From Meribah to Beer - Water and the Gathered People

## Passages involved
- [[The Waters of Meribah]]
- [[The Journey to Moab]]

References: Numbers 20:1–13; 21:16–18.

## Analysis

At Meribah the congregation lacks water, quarrels, and is gathered before the rock. Moses strikes
twice, water comes, and Yahweh judges the leaders for failing to believe and sanctify him (20:8–12).
At Beer Yahweh again tells Moses to gather the people and promises water (21:16), but the narrated
response is a song about the well dug by princes and nobles. No quarrel, striking of a rock, or new
sentence is narrated. These are distinct scenes, not competing descriptions of one event.

Source: stored WEB in `raw/bible/Numbers.md` and, for the sea song,
`raw/bible/Exodus.md`.

## Significance

Water remains a divine provision after the leadership judgment. The joyful scene does not revoke the
Meribah sentence, and the text gives no warrant for treating the well song as a magical technique.
