# The Finger of God - Exodus 8.19, Exodus 31.18, and Luke 11.20

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 8:19** — the magicians, unable to make lice: *"This is God's finger"* —
  [[Frogs, Lice, and Flies]]
- **Exodus 31:18** — the tablets of the testimony, *“stone tablets, written with God’s finger”* —
  [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]]
- **Deuteronomy 9:10** — *"Yahweh delivered to me the two stone tablets written with God's finger"*
  *(not yet ingested)*
- **Luke 11:20** — *"But if I by God's finger cast out demons, then God's Kingdom has come to you"*
  *(not yet ingested)*
- Compare **Matthew 12:28** — *"But if I by the Spirit of God cast out demons, then God's Kingdom has
  come upon you"* *(not yet ingested)*

Exodus is quoted from `raw/bible/`; Deuteronomy, Luke, and Matthew from the WEB at bible-api.com. The
Hebrew of Exodus was checked in the WLC; **no claim is made about the Hebrew of Deuteronomy or the
Greek of Luke and Matthew.**

## Analysis

### In Exodus: once to strike, once to write

***ʾEṣbaʿ*, *finger*, occurs three times in Exodus** (WLC lemma search): the magicians' confession
(8:19; Hebrew 8:15), the priest's own finger putting blood on the altar's horns (29:12), and the
tablets (31:18). **Only two are God's**, and they are the two ends of the book's first half and second:

| | Exodus 8:19 | Exodus 31:18 |
|---|---|---|
| **where** | Egypt | Sinai |
| **who says it** | Pharaoh's magicians | the narrator |
| **what the finger does** | turns the dust of the earth to lice | writes the tablets of the testimony |
| **the noun for God** | *ʾĕlōhîm* — *God*, or *a god* | *ʾĕlōhîm* |

**In Egypt the finger is recognised by its effect, by people who do not know the God whose finger it
is.** At Sinai it writes the covenant for the people who do. Exodus does not link the two verses; they
share a phrase, *ʾeṣbaʿ ʾĕlōhîm*, and are twenty-three chapters apart. **The magicians name the agent of
the plagues with the words that will later name the author of the law.**

**The magicians' word is modest on purpose.** Exodus speaks of God's *"mighty hand"* (3:19), *"strong
hand"* (6:1), and *"outstretched arm"* (6:6). **The professionals who matched the rod, the blood, and the
frogs concede to a finger** — not the hand, not the arm. Whether they mean that the God of Israel is
behind it, or only that some divine power is at work that their arts cannot reach, the Hebrew does not
settle: *ʾĕlōhîm* without an article can be either. See
[[The Magicians of Egypt - Hartummim in Genesis, Exodus, and Daniel]].

### In Luke: the finger casts out demons

**Luke 11:20 puts the phrase in Jesus' mouth**, answering the charge that he casts out demons by
Beelzebul: *"But if I by God's finger cast out demons, then God's Kingdom has come to you."* (WEB)
**Matthew's parallel has *"the Spirit of God"*** (12:28, WEB). The wiki records the difference between
the two Gospels as it stands in the WEB and makes no claim about the Greek beyond that.

**The setting matches Exodus 8 in one respect that is in the texts themselves.** In both, the act is
disputed by opponents who attribute power to the wrong source, and in both the phrase *God's finger*
is the verdict. **In Exodus it is the opponents who give the verdict; in Luke it is Jesus, against
his opponents.** That Luke intends an echo of Exodus is a common reading, not something Luke says.

### The promise now delivered

The tablets promised in Exodus 24:12 are given when God finishes speaking on Sinai (31:18). The
narrator attributes their writing to God's finger; this verse does not describe their layout or
transcribe their words. The phrase now belongs to the ingested narrative. The comparison with the
plague remains an observed recurrence, not an explicit explanation by Exodus of why the phrase
occurs twice.

Exodus 31:18 was checked in raw WEB and MAM (Sefaria) on 2026-10-24. The earlier Deuteronomy and
Gospel comparisons retain their original source note.

## Significance

**The phrase marks a limit.** In Exodus 8 the magicians find the point past which imitation fails, and
call what lies beyond it *God's finger*. At Sinai the same finger writes what no one else could write.
In Luke the claim is that the same power is now present in Jesus' exorcisms, and that it means the
kingdom *"has come"*.

**It is also the first confession of God by Egyptians in Exodus** — before Pharaoh's *"Yahweh is
righteous"* (9:27) and the Egyptians' *"Let's flee from the face of Israel"* (14:25). **It is extracted,
not offered, and it changes nothing**: Pharaoh's heart *"was hardened"* in the same verse. See
[[Signs and Wonders]], [[Hardening of the Heart]], and
[[Who Is Yahweh - Pharaoh's Question and the Knowledge of Yahweh]].
