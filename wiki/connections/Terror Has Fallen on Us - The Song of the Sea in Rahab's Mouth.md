# Terror Has Fallen on Us - The Song of the Sea in Rahab's Mouth

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 15:14–16** — *"The peoples have heard. They tremble … All the inhabitants of Canaan have melted
  away. Terror and dread falls on them. By the greatness of your arm they are as still as a stone— until your
  people pass over, Yahweh"* — [[The Song of the Sea]]
- **Joshua 2:9–11** — Rahab to the spies: *"I know that Yahweh has given you the land, and that the fear of
  you has fallen upon us, and that all the inhabitants of the land melt away before you. For we have heard
  how Yahweh dried up the water of the Red Sea before you, when you came out of Egypt … As soon as we had
  heard it, our hearts melted"* (WEB; *not yet ingested*)
- **Joshua 2:24** — the spies to Joshua: *"Moreover, all the inhabitants of the land melt away before us."*
  (WEB; *not yet ingested*)
- **Genesis 15:12** — *"Now terror and great darkness fell on him"* — [[The Covenant Between the Pieces]]
- **Exodus 23:27** — *"I will send my terror before you, and will confuse all the people to whom you come"*
  — [[The Angel Sent Ahead]]

## Analysis

**Rahab's speech uses the Song's phrases, in the Song's order.** (All forms checked in the WLC.)

| Exodus 15 | Joshua 2 |
|---|---|
| *"The peoples have heard"* — ***šāməʿû ʿammîm*** (15:14) | *"we have heard"* — ***šāmaʿnû*** (2:10); *"as soon as we had heard it"* (2:11) |
| *"Terror … falls on them"* — ***tippōl ʿălêhem ʾêmātâ*** (15:16) | *"the fear of you has fallen upon us"* — ***nāpəlâ ʾêmatkem ʿālênû*** (2:9) |
| *"All the inhabitants of Canaan have melted away"* — ***nāmōgû kōl yōšbê kənāʿan*** (15:15) | *"all the inhabitants of the land melt away"* — ***nāmōgû kol-yōšbê hāʾāreṣ*** (2:9, 24) |
| *"His chosen captains are sunk in the Red Sea (yam-sûp)"* (15:4) | *"Yahweh dried up the water of the Red Sea (yam sûp)"* (2:10) |

***Mûg*, *melt*, in the niphal, occurs in the Torah only at Exodus 15:15** (WLC lemma search); **its next
occurrences are Rahab's and the spies'.** ***ʾÊmâ*** with ***nāpal***, *terror falls*, is the phrase of
Abram's covenant night — *"terror (ʾêmâ) and great darkness fell (nōpelet) on him"* (Genesis 15:12; WLC) —
and of the Song (15:16); **Rahab is the third to use it.** The WEB renders *ʾêmâ* three ways — *"terror"*,
*"terror"*, *"fear"* — but the Hebrew word is the same.

**Rahab adds one thing the Song does not have**: a second *melting* with a different verb, *"our hearts
melted"* (*wayyimmas ləbābēnû*, from *māsas*, 2:11; WLC), and a confession: *"for Yahweh your God, he is
God in heaven above, and on earth beneath"* (2:11). **The Song asked *"Who is like you, Yahweh, among the
gods?"* (15:11); a Canaanite answers it.**

**Between the Song and Rahab stands a promise.** *"I will send my terror (ʾêmātî) before you"* (Exodus
23:27; MAM). The Song sings that terror falls on Canaan's inhabitants (15:16); Sinai has God promise to
send it; Rahab reports that it has fallen (2:9). The same noun runs through all three.

## Significance

**The Song predicted what the nations would hear and feel; Joshua 2 has one of those nations report that it
happened, in the Song's words.** Forty years separate the two, and the inhabitant who says it is the one
person in Jericho who acts on it. **What the Song sang of *"the inhabitants of Canaan"* as a mass becomes,
in Rahab, a single woman who heard and believed** — the Song's *"heard … tremble"* and the people's *"feared
… believed"* at 14:31 joined in one Canaanite. **Whether Joshua 2 deliberately quotes Exodus 15 or both
draw on a shared stock of conquest language** is a question the text does not settle (reported, not
decided). See [[Canaan]], [[The Fear of God]], [[The Nations]], and
[[The Midwives and Rahab - Deceiving the King to Save Life]].
