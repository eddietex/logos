# When You Come Into the Land - The Law Given Before Arrival

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 12:25** — the Passover, *kî tābōʾû ʾel-hāʾāreṣ*, said on the way out of Egypt —
  [[The Passover]]
- **Leviticus 14:34** — *"When you have come into the land of Canaan, which I give to you for a
  possession"*, on mildew in a house
- **Leviticus 19:23** — fruit trees — [[Holy Living in Field and Community]]
- **Leviticus 23:10** — the sheaf of the first fruits — [[First Fruits and the Feast of Weeks]]
- **Leviticus 25:2** — the land's Sabbath
- **Numbers 15:2, 18** — offerings and the first of the dough —
  [[Offerings in the Land and Sins of Ignorance]]
- **Numbers 34:2** — the land's borders, with a different form of the verb *(stored WEB, not yet
  ingested)*

(WEB and WLC; Exodus through Numbers from `raw/bible/`.)

## Analysis

By the tags, *kî tābōʾû* with the land following occurs in the five places above before
Numbers, and again at Numbers 15:2. All five came before the refusal at Kadesh.

Numbers 15:2 is different because of where it stands. Kadesh has happened. The people have said they
would rather die in the wilderness, Yahweh has taken them at their word, and the forty years are
running. The formula then returns unchanged, with its present participle, *which I give to you*.
Nothing in the formula has been adjusted for what has just happened. It does not say *your children*
in place of *you*, although 14:29–31 has just drawn that line.

Verse 18 repeats it in another form: *bəbōʾăkem ʾel-hāʾāreṣ*, and there Yahweh is the one who brings
them, *where I bring you*.

## Significance

The law is a form of promise here, though the text never calls it that. A sentence of forty years does
not cancel the legislation for year forty-one. The reading depends on the chapter order, since neither
Numbers 14 nor 15 draws the link.
