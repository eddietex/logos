# Love and Hold Fast After the Campaign - Deuteronomy 10-11 and Joshua 22

## Passages involved

- [[What Yahweh Requires of Israel]] — Deuteronomy 10:12–22.
- [[Remember His Deeds and Guard Your Hearts]] — Deuteronomy 11:1–32, especially 13 and 22.
- [[The Eastern Tribes Sent Home]] — Joshua 22:1–9, especially 5.

## Analysis

Joshua's discharge is framed by Moses' instruction. In the stored WEB, Deuteronomy 10:12–13
joins walking in Yahweh's ways, loving him, serving with heart and soul, and keeping his
commandments; 10:20 adds clinging to him. Deuteronomy 11:13 joins love and wholehearted
service, and 11:22 joins diligent commandment keeping, love, walking, and clinging.

Joshua 22:5 gathers these demands into the farewell to the eastern tribes. This is a
comparison of the verified WEB wording and instruction, not an assertion of exact Hebrew
identity or a decision about literary composition. Joshua names the law commanded by Moses
and applies it after the warriors' particular obligation has been fulfilled (22:2–4).

The settings differ. Deuteronomy addresses Israel before western possession; Joshua addresses
the eastern tribes as they return to their grant. Neither possession nor an honored record
of service is presented as making continued fidelity unnecessary.

## Significance

The military endpoint and the continuing obligation to love Yahweh belong together.
Joshua's commendation is real, and so is his urgent charge for what comes next. The shared
law also joins communities whose homes lie on different sides of the Jordan.
