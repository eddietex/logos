# The Congregation's Error - Leviticus 4 and Numbers 15

## Passages involved

- **Leviticus 4:13–21** — the whole congregation — [[The Sin Offering for Priest and Congregation]]
- **Leviticus 4:27–35** — one of the common people — [[The Sin Offering for Ruler and Individual]]
- **Numbers 15:22–29** — the congregation and one person —
  [[Offerings in the Land and Sins of Ignorance]]

(WEB and WLC, from `raw/bible/` and morphhb.)

## Analysis

| | Leviticus 4 | Numbers 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Fault | *any of the things which Yahweh has commanded not to be done* (13, 27) | *don't observe all these commandments* (22) |
| Hidden | *mēʿênê haqqāhāl* (13) | *mēʿênê hāʿēdâ* (24) |
| Congregation brings | young bull, sin offering (14) | young bull, burnt offering, with meal and drink; male goat, sin offering (24) |
| One person brings | female goat or lamb, without defect (28, 32) | female goat, a year old (27) |
| Ranks | anointed priest, congregation, ruler, common person | congregation, one person |
| Outcome | *wənislaḥ*, forgiven (20, 26, 31, 35) | *wənislaḥ*, forgiven (25, 26, 28) |
| Stranger | not named | named, and included (26, 29) |

The two laws share the error verb, the idiom of the eyes, the word for one person, and the outcome.
They differ on the offering for the congregation, the fault, and who is covered.

The text does not explain the difference. Two readings are open. The laws may be two different
cases, one of doing and one of leaving undone. Or they may be two forms of one law, with Numbers
restating it after the congregation's offerings have been extended by verses 1–16. The wiki leaves
both open. Rahlfs adds *ἄμωμον* to the bull at Numbers 15:24, which brings it nearer Leviticus 4:14,
where Rahlfs also has it.

## Significance

Numbers 15 does not cite Leviticus or say that it changes it. Its practical result is a wider
provision: the stranger is expressly forgiven, and the congregation's offering is made to include a
burnt offering *for a pleasant aroma*. The contradiction is flagged here and on the passage page.
