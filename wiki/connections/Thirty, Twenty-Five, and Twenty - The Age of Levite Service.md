# Thirty, Twenty-Five, and Twenty - The Age of Levite Service

## Passages involved

- [[The Service of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari]] — Numbers 4:3, 23, 30, 47; from thirty to fifty
- Numbers 8:24–26 — from twenty-five, retiring at fifty *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*
- 1 Chronicles 23:3, 24, 26–27 — thirty, then twenty *(not yet ingested; WEB verified)*
- 2 Chronicles 31:17 — from twenty *(not yet ingested; WEB verified)*
- Ezra 3:8 — from twenty *(not yet ingested; WEB verified)*

## Analysis

**The figures.**

| Text | Starting age | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Numbers 4:3 | 30 | carrying the tabernacle in the wilderness |
| Numbers 8:24 | 25 | *the service in the work of the Tent of Meeting* |
| 1 Chronicles 23:3 | 30 | David's count, 38,000 men |
| 1 Chronicles 23:24, 27 | 20 | *by the last words of David* |
| 2 Chronicles 31:17 | 20 | Hezekiah's register |
| Ezra 3:8 | 20 | oversight of the rebuilding |

Numbers 1:3 counts soldiers from twenty, so the age of twenty is not new to the Bible, only to the
Levites.

**What the texts say.** Numbers 4 and 8 stand four chapters apart with no comment. Both end the service
at fifty, and 8:26 lets older men *minister with their brothers* but *perform no service*.

Chronicles alone gives a reason for a change: *the Levites will no longer need to carry the tabernacle
and all its vessels for its service* (1 Chronicles 23:26). The next verse then lowers the age. That
would account for twenty, but not for the two ages inside Numbers. The same chapter also keeps
thirty at verse 3 and twenty at verses 24 and 27, with no note on the difference.

**Readings not adopted.** The difference in Numbers has been explained by a period of training from
twenty-five, with full service from thirty, a view reported from rabbinic sources. It has also been
explained by different tasks, heavy carrying in chapter 4 and work at the Tent in chapter 8, since 8:24
does not mention burdens. Greek witnesses are reported to read twenty-five in chapter 4. None of these
reports was checked, and the WLC reads *thirty* at 4:3, 23, 30, 35, 39, 43, and 47.

## Significance

Whatever the explanation, the Bible treats the age limit as something that can change with the work.
The numbers follow the task. See [[Work]] and [[Priesthood]].
