# Dan's Towns on Judah's Border - Joshua 15 and 19

## Passages involved
- [[The Lots of Asher, Naphtali, and Dan]] — Joshua 19:40–48; Dan’s list.
- [[The Borders of Judah]] — Joshua 15:1–12; Judah’s northern border past Beth Shemesh, Timnah, and Ekron.
- [[The Cities of Judah]] — Joshua 15:20–63; Judah’s lowland towns.
- [[Simeon's Lot Within Judah - Joshua 15 and 19]] — the parallel case in the south.

## Analysis

### The last three lots — Joshua 19:24–48

Dan’s lot has no border circuit. It is a list of towns, and several of them already appear in
Judah’s chapter:

| Dan (Joshua 19) | Judah (Joshua 15) |
|---|---|
| Zorah, Eshtaol (41) | [[Zorah]], [[Eshtaol]] — lowland towns (33) |
| Irshemesh (41) | [[Beth Shemesh]]? — on the border (10) |
| Timnah (43) | [[Timnah]] — the border *passed along by Timnah* (10) |
| Ekron (43) | [[Ekron]] — the border passes its north side (11); listed with its towns (45) |
| Baalath (44) | Mount Baalah? — on the border near Ekron (11) |

A question mark marks a match suggested only by a shared element of the name; the wiki does
not equate those names. Zorah, Eshtaol, Timnah, and Ekron are exact.

Unlike Simeon’s lot (19:9), Dan’s gives no explanation for the overlap. Two readings are
available and the text chooses neither: Judah’s list may name towns later assigned to Dan,
or towns on Judah’s northern border may be counted from the side of each tribe. Ekron adds a
third layer, since 13:3 counts it among the Philistine lordships still remaining, so a town
can be in Judah’s list, Dan’s list, and the unconquered land at once.

Sources: stored WEB Joshua 13:3, 15:10–11, 33, 45, and 19:41–44.

## Significance

The allotment lists are written as titles, not as records of occupation. Dan’s lowland towns
sit on a line already drawn for Judah and inside land still Philistine, and verse 47’s move
north reads differently once that is seen. See
[[Leshem Becomes Dan - Joshua 19 and Judges 18]].
