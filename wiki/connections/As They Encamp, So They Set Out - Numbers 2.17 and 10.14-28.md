# As They Encamp, So They Set Out - Numbers 2.17 and 10.14-28

## Passages involved

- [[The Camp Around the Tent]] — Numbers 2:9, 16–17, 24, 31; the order commanded
- [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]] — Numbers 3:23, 29, 35, 38; where the Levite clans camp
- Numbers 4 — what each clan carries *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*
- Numbers 10:14–28 — the order carried out *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*

## Analysis

Verse 17 gives one rule: *Then the Tent of Meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites in the
middle of the camps. As they encamp, so shall they set out*. It sits between the second camp and the
third, so the Tent is in the middle of the column as it was in the middle of the camp.

Numbers 10 reports the same four camps in the same order, and the same princes. The Levites are not
where verse 17 seems to put them:

| Numbers 2 | Numbers 10 |
|---|---|
| Judah's camp | Judah's camp (14–16) |
| | Gershon and Merari with the tabernacle (17) |
| Reuben's camp | Reuben's camp (18–20) |
| The Tent and the Levites (17) | Kohath *bearing the sanctuary* (21) |
| Ephraim's camp | Ephraim's camp (22–24) |
| Dan's camp | Dan's camp (25–27) |

Numbers 10:21 gives a reason of its own: *The others set up the tabernacle before they arrived*. The
structure goes ahead and is standing when the holy things come. Kohath, carrying those things, is at
the position verse 17 names. So the two chapters agree on Kohath and differ on Gershon and Merari,
unless verse 17 is taken as a general statement of the Levites' place and chapter 10 as its detail.
The text does not say which. The wiki records the difference and does not resolve it.

## Significance

The rule that rest and march have one shape is the chapter's point, and it holds for the twelve
tribes without exception. The difference is confined to how a sanctuary is moved, which is the
subject of Numbers 4. See [[The Dwelling of God]].
