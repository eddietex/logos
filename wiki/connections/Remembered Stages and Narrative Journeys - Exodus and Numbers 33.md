# Remembered Stages and Narrative Journeys - Exodus and Numbers 33

## Passages involved
- [[The Stages of the Journey]] — Numbers 33:1–49.
- [[The Crossing of the Sea]] — Exodus 13:17–14:31.
- [[Bitter Water at Marah]] — Exodus 15:22–27.
- [[Water from the Rock]] — Exodus 17:1–7.
- [[Miriam and Aaron Against Moses]] — Numbers 12:1–16.
- [[The Journey to Moab]] — Numbers 21:10–20.


## Analysis

Numbers 33 preserves the departure route of Exodus but adds intermediate camps: a Red Sea
camp after Elim, and Dophkah and Alush before Rephidim. It remembers Elim’s springs and palms
and Rephidim’s lack of water without retelling the murmuring and remedies. The difference is
one of selection; a narrative’s omission need not deny an intermediate stop.

Other differences need to remain explicit. Exodus 15:22 names Shur where Numbers 33:8 names
Etham. Numbers 12:16 names Paran after Hazeroth, where 33:18 names Rithmah. Region and campsite
may account for these differences, but neither passage expressly identifies them. The itinerary
in Numbers 21:11–20 names Zered, the Arnon, Beer, Mattanah, Nahaliel, Bamoth, and Pisgah after
Iyeabarim; Numbers 33:44–48 instead gives Iyim, Dibon Gad, Almon Diblathaim, and Abarim before
the plains of Moab. The two selections should not be silently converted into a single map.

Aaron’s death is unusually precise amid mostly undated stages. Numbers 20:22–29 explains the
sentence and succession; 33:38–39 supplies date and age. The king of Arad’s hearing in 33:40
recalls 21:1, without repeating the battle and vow. These expansions and compressions make the
list a selective remembrance rather than a replacement for the narratives.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 13–17 and Numbers 12, 20–21, 33. Comparisons are paraphrases;
no claim about Hebrew wording or modern geography is made.

## Significance

The itinerary holds liberation, need, provision, and the death of a leader within one journey.
Its written form preserves places that the fuller narratives omit, while the narratives give
meaning to events the list abbreviates. Both witnesses remain available without forcing every
name into a confident modern location.
