# Without Revelation and Without Restraint - Proverbs 29.18 and Exodus 32.25

## Passages involved

- [[Where There Is No Vision]] — Proverbs 29:1, 18.
- [[The Golden Calf]] — Exodus 32:7–9, 25.

## Analysis

Proverbs 29:18 pairs the absence of revelation with people casting off restraint, then blesses the
one who keeps the law. Read in full, the contrast concerns receiving and obeying guidance, not
merely possessing an ambitious plan. The passage title preserves the planned wording “vision”; the
stored WEB reads “revelation.”

Exodus 32:25 describes people who had broken loose because Aaron let them loose. Earlier Yahweh
identifies their conduct as departure from what he commanded (32:7–8), and calls them stiff-necked
(9). That narrative supplies a comparison for both the lack of restraint in Proverbs 29:18 and the
stubborn neck of 29:1.

The settings also differ. Israel had received commands; the calf episode is not an absence of
prior revelation. Proverbs describes what happens without revelation and contrasts law-keeping
with unrestrained conduct. The connection concerns neglected guidance and failed restraint; it
does not establish that Proverbs quotes Exodus or that every word has an identical Hebrew
counterpart. No source-language identity is claimed.

Sources: stored WEB Proverbs 29 and the cited Exodus and Job passages; no source-language identity
or literary dependence is asserted.

## Significance

Revelation and obedience belong together. [[Prophecy and Vision]] cannot be reduced to foresight, and
[[Law and Obedience]] cannot be replaced by possession of information. The comparison also joins
[[Hardening of the Heart]] to refusal of correction.
