# Taken by Lot - Achan, Saul, and Jonathan

## Passages involved

- [[The Sin of Achan]] — **Joshua 7:14, 16–18** — *the tribe which Yahweh selects shall come near by families. The
  family which Yahweh selects shall come near by households. The household which Yahweh selects shall come near
  man by man* (14) … *Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was
  selected* (18). Stored WEB.
- [[Saul Chosen by Lot at Mizpah]] — **1 Samuel 10:20–22** — *the tribe of Benjamin was chosen. He brought the
  tribe of Benjamin near by their families; and the family of the Matrites was chosen. Then Saul the son of Kish
  was chosen; but when they looked for him, he could not be found. Therefore they asked of Yahweh further … "Behold,
  he has hidden himself among the baggage."* Stored WEB.
- **1 Samuel 14:41–42** — *Therefore Saul said to Yahweh, the God of Israel, "Show the right." Jonathan and Saul
  were chosen, but the people escaped. Saul said, "Cast lots between me and Jonathan my son." Jonathan was
  selected.* Stored WEB; not yet ingested.

## Analysis

**One verb, three scenes.** In each scene Israel is narrowed down before Yahweh until one man is left, and each
time the narrator marks the result with the passive of *lāḵaḏ*, *capture, take*: *wayyillāḵēḏ*, *and he was
taken* (Joshua 7:16, 17, 18; 1 Samuel 10:20, 21; 14:41, 42, WLC). The WEB translates it *was selected* in Joshua 7
and 1 Samuel 14 and *was chosen* in 1 Samuel 10. In the WLC files searched (Genesis–2 Samuel and Proverbs), these
are the only passages where the passive is used of someone picked out before Yahweh. Its only other passive uses
there are in Proverbs, of a person *trapped* (6:2; 11:6, stored WEB); the active is the ordinary verb for capturing a
city (lemma 3920). The procedure is also shared. The people are
*brought near*, *qāraḇ* (Joshua 7:14, 16–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21, WLC), first by tribes and then by families.

| | Joshua 7 | 1 Samuel 10 | 1 Samuel 14 |
|---|---|---|---|
| the question | who took the devoted things | who is to be king | who broke Saul's oath |
| the steps | tribe, family, household, man | tribe, family, man | Saul and Jonathan on one side, the people on the other; then the two |
| the man taken | Achan | Saul | Jonathan |
| what was hidden | the plunder, *hidden in the ground* in his tent (7:21) | Saul himself, *hidden among the baggage* | nothing; Jonathan says *I certainly did taste a little honey* (14:43) |
| the outcome | stoned and burned (7:25) | acclaimed king | spared: *the people rescued Jonathan* (14:45) |

(Stored WEB; 1 Samuel 14:43–45 not yet ingested. Hebrew WLC.)

**What is the same and what is reversed.** Achan is taken and found to be guilty. Saul is taken and found to be
hidden, but the selection makes him king, not a culprit. In chapter 14 the procedure that made Saul king points to
his own son. Saul has sworn that the guilty man will die even if it is Jonathan (14:39, stored WEB; not yet
ingested), and the lot finds Jonathan. The three scenes do not refer to each other, and the wiki does not claim the
narrator of Samuel had Joshua 7 in view. What the shared verb and procedure show is narrower: Israel had one way
of finding a man before Yahweh, and it was used to expose a thief, to choose a king, and to expose a king's son.

**The means is named only once.** Joshua 7 says *the tribe which Yahweh selects*, with *lāḵaḏ* in the active
voice and Yahweh as subject (7:14, WLC), and never says how. 1 Samuel 10 does not say either; the people *asked
of Yahweh* to find the hidden man (10:22). Only 14:42 says *Cast lots*, *hap̄pîlû* (WLC), and 14:41's *Show the
right*, *hāḇâ ṯāmîm* (WLC), is commonly connected with the Thummim. That connection depends on a longer Greek
text of the verse, which is recalled and was not checked. The wiki does not carry the means from 14:42 back into
the other two scenes. See [[The Lot Is Cast into the Lap - Proverbs 16.33 and the Casting of Lots]] and
[[Urim and Thummim - The Breastplate of Judgment and Later Inquiry]].

## Significance

- **Saul's kingship begins and is tested by the same procedure.** The selection that made him king at Mizpah
  turns against his house at Michmash. The wiki notes the symmetry. The text does not draw it.
- **Hiding is the link between Achan and Saul.** One hides what he took, and the other hides from what he is
  given. Both are found by asking Yahweh. See [[Providence]] and [[Call and Election]].
- **The selection is presented as Yahweh's choice in all three scenes.** That is the claim of Proverbs 16:33,
  *its every decision is from Yahweh* (stored WEB), and Samuel states it outright at 10:24: *him whom Yahweh has
  chosen*.
