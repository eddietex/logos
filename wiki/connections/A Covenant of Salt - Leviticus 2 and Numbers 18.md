# A Covenant of Salt - Leviticus 2 and Numbers 18

## Passages involved
- [[The Meal Offering]] — Leviticus 2:13.
- [[The Duties and Portions of Priests and Levites]] — Numbers 18:1–32.

## Analysis

Leviticus 2:13 requires salt with offerings and associates it with God’s covenant.
Numbers 18:19 calls the lasting grant of holy portions to Aaron and his offspring
a covenant of salt. One passage regulates the offering; the other secures the
priestly household’s share. Neither explains salt’s meaning through its physical
properties or describes a separate covenant-making salt ritual.

Sources: stored WEB passages cited above.

## Significance

Covenant language joins worship to the continuing provision of its servants.
The shared image is explicit; an explanation of its origin would require further evidence.
