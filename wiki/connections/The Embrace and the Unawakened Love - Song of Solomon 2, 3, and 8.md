# The Embrace and the Unawakened Love - Song of Solomon 2, 3, and 8

## Passages involved

- [[Delight and the Unawakened Love]] — Song of Solomon 2:6–7.
- [[Seeking Him by Night]] — Song of Solomon 3:4–5.
- [[Come into the Fields, My Beloved]] — Song of Solomon 8:1–4.

## Analysis

The embrace of 2:6 immediately precedes the adjuration of 2:7. In the stored WEB,
8:3 repeats the two hands as a wished-for embrace, followed by the charge at 8:4.
The refrain also closes the successful night search at 3:5. At 8:4 the roes and
hinds invoked in 2:7 and 3:5 are absent, while the instruction not to stir up or
awaken love until it desires remains.

The repeated charge can be read as resistance to forcing love prematurely or to
interrupting the lovers. These contexts keep both possibilities in view; the wiki
does not turn the refrain into a newly stated marriage rule. The 8:3 embrace is
conditional in the WEB, so the repeated image does not prove a completed encounter.

## Significance

[[Oaths and Vows]] and [[Love]] meet in a refrain that gives the audience a limit.
The woman's eager invitation and her insistence on love's timing belong together.
