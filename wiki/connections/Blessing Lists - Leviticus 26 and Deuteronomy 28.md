# Blessing Lists - Leviticus 26 and Deuteronomy 28

## Passages involved
- [[Blessings for Obedience (Leviticus)]] — Leviticus 26:1–13.
- [[Blessings for Obedience (Deuteronomy)]] — Deuteronomy 28:1–14.

## Analysis

Both units attach agricultural abundance and victory to obedience. Leviticus 26:3–10 promises
rain, yield, security, fertility, and overflowing stores; Deuteronomy 28:1–12 expands the
catalogue across city and field, household food, offspring, livestock, barns, and seasonal rain.
The enemies’ defeat is expressed by unequal numbers in Leviticus 26:8 and by flight in seven
directions in Deuteronomy 28:7. Neither is a report of a completed battle.

Their emphases differ. Leviticus ends with Yahweh’s dwelling and walking among the people and
the remembered breaking of Egyptian bondage (11–13). Deuteronomy stresses holy identity,
recognition by other nations, lending without borrowing, and elevated standing (9–13).
Its repeated obedience clauses culminate in the ban on following other gods (14). These are
parallel covenant promises, not identical lists or proof of a particular direction of borrowing.

## Significance

The comparison keeps material provision, relationship to Yahweh, and public national standing together without importing one account’s distinctive details into the other.
