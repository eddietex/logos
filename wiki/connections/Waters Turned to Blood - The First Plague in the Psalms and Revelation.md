# Waters Turned to Blood - The First Plague in the Psalms and Revelation

## Passages involved

- Exodus 4:9 — the third sign at the bush: river water poured on the dry land *"will become blood on
  the dry land"* — [[The Burning Bush]]
- Exodus 7:14–25 — the Nile and all the waters of Egypt turned to blood — [[Water Turned to Blood]]
- Exodus 1:22 — *"You shall cast every son who is born into the river"* — [[The Oppression in Egypt]]
- Psalm 78:44; 105:29 — the plague in Israel's retellings *(not yet ingested)*
- Wisdom of Solomon 11:6–7 — blood *"to rebuke the decree for the slaying of babes"*
  *(deuterocanonical; not in the wiki's canon list)*
- Revelation 8:8; 11:6; 16:3–6 — the trumpet, the two witnesses, and the second and third bowls
  *(not yet ingested)*

All texts outside Genesis, Exodus, and Job are quoted from the WEB at bible-api.com and were not
checked in Hebrew or Greek.

## Analysis

**The first plague is told three times in Exodus before it is retold anywhere else.** It is a sign in
a cup at the bush (4:9), a forecast to Pharaoh (7:17–18), and an event (7:20–21), and the event repeats
the forecast nearly word for word. **The later retellings keep two details above all: the rivers, and
the fish.**

| text | WEB | what it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 7:17–21 | *"they shall be turned to blood. The fish that are in the river shall die"* | the source |
| Psalm 78:44 | *"he turned their rivers into blood, and their streams, so that they could not drink"* | the rivers, the streams, the drinking (7:19, 21, 24) |
| Psalm 105:29 | *"He turned their waters into blood, and killed their fish"* | the waters and the fish (7:18, 21) |
| Revelation 8:8 | *"One third of the sea became blood"* | the water, partially, at a trumpet |
| Revelation 11:6 | the two witnesses *"have power over the waters, to turn them into blood"* | the power, given to prophets |
| Revelation 16:3–4 | the sea *"became blood as of a dead man. Every living thing in the sea died"*; then *"the rivers and springs of water … became blood"* | sea and rivers, and the death of what lives in them |

**The psalms list it, and do not explain it.** Both Psalm 78 and Psalm 105 retell the plagues as a
catalogue of God's acts; neither says why the river was the first target. Psalm 105 puts the darkness
first and the blood second, so the psalms do not even agree on the order — the wiki records that as a
fact of the WEB texts and does not resolve it.

**Revelation turns it into a pattern of judgment on the world, not on Egypt.** The witnesses of 11:6
are given the power of Moses (blood) and of Elijah (shut the sky). The bowls widen the Nile to *"the
sea"* and *"the rivers and springs of water"*. **And Revelation 16:5–6 gives a reason the Exodus text
does not**: *"they poured out the blood of the saints and prophets, and you have given them blood to
drink. They deserve this."*

**That reason is the one Wisdom of Solomon gives for Exodus 7.** *"When the enemy were troubled with
clotted blood instead of a river's ever-flowing fountain, to rebuke the decree for the slaying of
babes"* (11:6–7). **Wisdom reads the first plague as an answer to Exodus 1:22**: the river that took
the Hebrew sons is made to run with blood. Exodus never says so. Its vocabulary allows it — *hayəʾōr*,
*"the river"*, is the same word in 1:22 and 7:17–25 — but the connection is the later reader's.

**Exodus itself gives a different stated purpose**: *"In this you shall know that I am Yahweh"* (7:17).
**The blood is a lesson before it is a retribution.** The retributive reading of Wisdom and
Revelation is an addition to that purpose, not a replacement of it.

## Significance

**The first plague becomes the template for judgment by water.** The Nile was what Egypt lived by (see
[[The Nile]]); striking it struck the country at its root. Revelation keeps that logic and enlarges the
river to every water there is — which Exodus 7:19 had already begun, by extending the blow from the
river to *"all their ponds of water"* and the jars in the house.

**It is also the first place the measure-for-measure reading of the plagues attaches itself.** Wisdom
11 and Revelation 16 both ask why blood, and both answer: because blood was shed. The wiki records the
reading as theirs. See [[Retribution]], [[Blood]], and [[Signs and Wonders]].

**A later contrast is often drawn with John 2.** Moses' first public sign turns water to blood; the
sign John calls *"this beginning of his signs"* turns water to wine (John 2:11, WEB). Neither text
refers to the other, and the contrast belongs to its interpreters.
