# They Turned Aside Quickly - The Golden Calf and the Cycle of Judges

## Passages involved

- [[The Golden Calf]] — **Exodus 32:8** — *They have turned away quickly out of the way which I
  commanded them* (stored WEB)
- [[Remember the Calf and Moses' Intercession]] — **Deuteronomy 9:12, 16** — *They have quickly turned
  away from the way which I commanded them*; *You had quickly turned away from the way which Yahweh
  had commanded you* (stored WEB)
- [[Another Generation Arose]] — **Judges 2:17** — *They quickly turned away from the way in which their
  fathers walked, obeying Yahweh's commandments* (stored WEB)

## Analysis

The Hebrew is the same three words in all four verses (all WLC):

| Verse | Hebrew | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 32:8 | *sārû mahēr min-hadderek ʾăšer ṣiwwîtīm* | *they have made themselves a molten calf* |
| Deuteronomy 9:12 | *sārû mahēr min-hadderek ʾăšer ṣiwwîtīm* | *they have made a molten image* |
| Deuteronomy 9:16 | *sartem mahēr min-hadderek ʾăšer ṣiwwâ YHWH ʾetkem* | Moses to Israel, second person |
| Judges 2:17 | *sārû mahēr min-hadderek ʾăšer hālkû ʾăbôtām* | *obeying Yahweh's commandments. They didn't do so.* |

The difference is in the relative clause. At Sinai it is *the way which I commanded them*. In Judges
it is *the way in which their fathers walked*. The way is the same, but it is now described as the
fathers' way. The fathers in Judges 2 are the generation of Joshua and the elders, who *served
Yahweh* (2:7). The generation of the calf was the one that died in the wilderness. Judges does not
comment on that; it only uses the sentence.

A second word links the passages. Deuteronomy 9:12 says the people *have corrupted themselves*,
*šiḥēt*. Judges 2:19 says each generation after a judge *dealt more corruptly than their fathers*,
*wəhišḥîtû*. Both are forms of *šāḥat* (WLC).

## Significance

- **Sinai's sin becomes Israel's pattern.** At Sinai the turning-aside happened once, while Moses was on
  the mountain. In Judges 2:17 it happens after every judge, and *quickly* each time.
- **The leader is absent both times.** The calf was made while Moses was away (Exodus 32:1). In Judges
  the turning happens when the judge is dead (2:19). The parallel is in the narratives; the text of
  Judges does not mention the calf.
- **The speed is the point.** *Mahēr*, *quickly*, also appears at Judges 2:23: Yahweh did not drive the
  nations out *hastily*. Israel turns from Yahweh quickly; Yahweh does not remove the nations quickly.
- Later Judges returns to made objects of worship: Gideon's ephod, after which *all Israel played the
  prostitute with it* (8:27), and Micah's *carved image and a molten image* (17:4; both stored WEB).
  Whether their Hebrew echoes Exodus 32 will be checked when those pericopes are ingested.
