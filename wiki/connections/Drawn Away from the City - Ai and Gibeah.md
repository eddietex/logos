# Drawn Away from the City - Ai and Gibeah

## Passages involved

- [[The Capture of Ai]] — Joshua 8:5–6, 14–22: the ambush west of the city, the feigned flight,
  *"They flee before us, like the first time"*, the city left open, and the smoke that *"ascended up
  to heaven"* (stored WEB).
- [[The Fall of Abimelech]] — Judges 9:25, 32–49: Abimelech's ambushes at Shechem, the gate held, and
  the stronghold set on fire (stored WEB).
- [[The War Against Benjamin]] — Judges 20:13–48; Israel against Benjamin at Gibeah (stored WEB).

## Analysis

The two battles follow the same plan, and Judges tells it in Joshua's vocabulary:

| | Ai (Joshua 8) | Gibeah (Judges 20) |
|---|---|---|
| A prior defeat | the rout of 7:4–5 | two days of defeat, 20:21, 25 |
| The ambush | *Set an ambush for the city behind it* (8:2) | *Israel set ambushes all around Gibeah* (20:29) |
| The defenders' confidence | *They flee before us, like the first time* (8:6) | *They are struck down before us, as at the first* (20:32) |
| The drawing away | *until we have drawn them away from the city* (8:6); *were drawn away from the city* (8:16) | *were drawn away from the city* (20:31); *draw them away from the city to the highways* (20:32) |
| Bethel on the road | the ambush between Bethel and Ai (8:9, 12) | *the highways, of which one goes up to Bethel* (20:31) |
| The smoke | *the smoke of the city ascended up to heaven* (8:20) | the appointed sign, *a great cloud of smoke* (20:38) |
| The defenders unaware | *he didn't know that there was an ambush* (8:14) | *they didn't know that disaster was close to them* (20:34) |

All WEB; the Judges column from bible-api.com, 2026-11-10, with 20:39–48 not yet checked beyond the
summary given here.

The wiki has not compared the Hebrew of Judges 20 with Joshua 8 in this pass, so the shared English
phrases are not claimed as shared Hebrew phrases. Whether one narrative was shaped on the other is
not something the texts state.

**Shechem between them.** Judges 9 uses the same vocabulary for a war between an Israelite king and an
Israelite town. *ʾĀrab*, *lie in wait*, is the verb of Joshua 8:2 and of Judges 9:25, 32, 34, 43 (WLC).
Abimelech's companies hold the gate while the others strike *all who were in the field* (9:44). Ai's
people were cut off between the ambush and Joshua's army (Joshua 8:20–22). Then he burns a stronghold
in the city. *Wayyaṣṣîtû … bāʾēš*, *set … on fire*, is in Genesis–Judges otherwise only Joshua 8:8, 19, at
Ai (WLC). There is no feigned flight at Shechem, so the shape is closer to Ai's ambush than to its
drawing away. See [[The Fall of Abimelech]].

## Significance

At Ai the stratagem is Yahweh's (*Set an ambush*, 8:2) and is used against a Canaanite city after
Israel has dealt with sin in its own camp. At Gibeah the same stratagem is used by Israel against
one of its own tribes, after Benjamin refused to give up the men of Gibeah (Judges 20:13). The
parallel shows the conquest's methods turned inward at the end of Judges — a shape the wiki
notes now and will revisit when Judges is ingested.

### Judges 20 now ingested: the Hebrew compared

The page above compared English only. With Judges 20 ingested, the Hebrew of both chapters was read in the
WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17. The shared English is shared Hebrew in these places:

| | Joshua 8 | Judges 20 |
|---|---|---|
| the ambush | *ʾōrēḇ* (2) | *ʾōrəḇîm* (29); *ʾōrēḇ* (33, 36–38) |
| *as at the first* | *kaʾăšer bāriʾšōnâ* (5, 6) | *kəḇāriʾšōnâ* (32); *kammilḥāmâ hāriʾšōnâ* (39) |
| drawn away | *hattîqēnû* (6); *wayyinnātəqû* (16) — *nātaq* | *hontəqû* (31); *ûnəṯaqqənuhû* (32) — *nātaq* |
| not knowing | *wəhûʾ lōʾ yāḏaʿ kî* (14) | *wəhēm lōʾ yāḏəʿû kî* (34) |
| the appointed | *lammôʿēḏ*, *at the time appointed* (14) | *wəhammôʿēḏ*, *the appointed sign* (38) |
| looking back | *wayyip̄nû … ʾaḥărêhem* (20) | *wayyip̄en binyāmin ʾaḥărāyw* (40) |
| the smoke going up | *ʿālâ ʿăšan hāʿîr haššāmaymâ* (20) | *ʿālâ ḵəlîl-hāʿîr haššāmaymâ* (40) |
| the flight | *derek hammiḏbār* (15) | *derek hammiḏbār* (42) |

*Nātaq* in this sense occurs in the WLC files of Genesis through Judges at Joshua 8:6, 16 and Judges 20:31,
32; its other uses there are the cords Samson *broke* (Judges 16:9, 12), Joshua 4:18, and Leviticus 22:24
(lemma 5423). The closest single phrase is the smoke: Joshua's *the smoke of the city went up to the
heavens*, and Judges' *the whole of the city* — *kālîl*, the word of the whole burnt offering and of the
condemned city of Deuteronomy 13:16 (Hebrew 13:17) — *went up to the heavens*. See
[[The Whole City Went Up - Deuteronomy 13 and Gibeah]].

The verbal overlap is now shown in Hebrew; whether Judges 20 was written with Joshua 8 in view is still
not stated by either text. One difference stands out: at Ai Yahweh gives the plan (*Set an ambush*, 8:2);
at Gibeah Israel sets the ambushes after Yahweh's promise (20:28–29), and the plan is not attributed to
him. See [[The War Against Benjamin]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 8; Judges 16:9; 20:29–42.
