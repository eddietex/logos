# Lifted Up - The Brass Serpent and the Son of Man

## Passages involved
- [[The Brass Serpent]] — Numbers 21:4–9.
- John 3:14–17 — verified WEB; not yet ingested.

## Analysis

John 3:14 explicitly compares Moses lifting up the wilderness serpent with the Son of Man
being lifted up. The comparison leads into the promise of eternal life to the believer (15–16).
Numbers narrates preservation from a deadly bite through looking at the appointed sign;
John develops belief and eternal life. The shared pattern is a divinely provided rescue
made available through the lifted figure, not an assertion that Jesus is morally like a snake.

Numbers does not itself name the Son of Man or call its episode a prediction. This is
John's explicit typological use of the earlier narrative, rather than a fulfillment claim
supplied by Numbers. Nor should John's language be silently inserted into Numbers:
the stored WEB of Numbers 21:8–9 speaks of seeing and looking.

Sources: stored WEB Numbers 21:4–9; bible-api.com WEB John 3:14–17, verified 2026-11-04.
