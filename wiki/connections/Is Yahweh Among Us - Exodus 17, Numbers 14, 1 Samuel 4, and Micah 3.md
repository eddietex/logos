## Analysis

The question runs through Israel's history with the same Hebrew word, *qereb*, *inward part* or
*midst* (tags).

| Text | Who speaks | Words (WLC) |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 17:7 | the people, testing | *hăyēš YHWH bəqirbēnû*, *Is Yahweh among us?* |
| Exodus 33:3 | Yahweh | *lōʾ ʾeʿĕleh bəqirbəkā*, *I will not go up among you* |
| Numbers 11:20 | Yahweh | *YHWH ʾăšer bəqirbəkem*, *Yahweh who is among you* |
| Numbers 14:14 | Moses | *bəqereb hāʿām hazzeh* |
| Numbers 14:42 | Moses | *kî ʾên YHWH bəqirbəkem*, *for Yahweh isn't among you* |
| Deuteronomy 31:17 | the people, foreseen | *ʾên ʾĕlōhay bəqirbî*, WEB *our God is not among us* |
| 1 Samuel 4:3 | the elders | *wəyābōʾ bəqirbēnû*, of the ark |
| Micah 3:11 | the leaders | *hălôʾ YHWH bəqirbēnû*, *Isn't Yahweh among us?* |

**The going up.** Exodus 33:3 joins the same verb and noun that Numbers 14:42 joins: Yahweh will not
go up in their midst, and the people must not go up, since he is not in their midst. In Exodus Moses
intercedes and is told *My presence will go with you* (33:14). In Numbers the people do not wait.

**The ark.** Numbers 14:44 and 1 Samuel 4 are the two sides of one mistake. In Numbers the people go
without the ark and are struck. In 1 Samuel the elders, after a defeat, send for *the ark of Yahweh's
covenant*, the same title, so that *it may come among us, and save us*. The Philistines say *God has
come into the camp*. Israel is defeated worse, and the ark is taken (4:10–11). Having the object was
no more a guarantee than lacking it was the cause. In Numbers the ark's absence stands beside Yahweh's
not being among them, and 1 Samuel shows that presence cannot be fetched. That pairing is the wiki's.

**The same question, turned to confidence.** Exodus 17 asks it in doubt. Micah has it asked in
security, by leaders who take bribes: *No disaster will come on us.* Numbers 14 has neither form. It
has the answer *no*, from Moses, on the morning they are most sure.

## Significance

- The word for *among* marks a claim Israel can neither test nor assume.
- Yahweh's presence stays with the ark and Moses in the camp at Numbers 14:44. The people who leave
  it leave him.
