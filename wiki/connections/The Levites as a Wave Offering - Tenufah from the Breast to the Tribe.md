## Analysis

**The word's range.** *Tənûpâ* stands in 28 verses in the WLC tags. Most are portions of animals, or
grain, or oil: Exodus 29; Leviticus 7–10, 14, 23; Numbers 6:20; 18:11, 18. Exodus 35:22 and 38:24, 29
use it of gold and bronze given for the sanctuary. Isaiah 19:16 and 30:32 use it of Yahweh's action against nations.
Numbers 8 is the only place it is used of human beings. There it comes four times in eleven verses.

**A warning for English readers.** WEB's *wave offering* in Numbers 18 is mostly a different Hebrew
word, *tərûmâ* (18:8, 19, 24, 26–29). Only 18:11 and 18:18 have *tənûpâ*. A search of the English would
join these wrongly.

**What is given, and to whom.** At Leviticus 7:30–34 the offerer brings the breast and it goes to the
priests. Numbers 8 follows the same line. Israel brings, the thing is presented before Yahweh, and it ends
with Aaron and his sons (8:19). That matches Leviticus 7:34, where Yahweh takes the breast *from the
children of Israel* and gives it to Aaron and his sons. This is the wiki's comparison, and Numbers 8 does
not draw it. It would mean that the Levites are to the tribes what the waved breast is to a peace
offering: the part set aside for the priests.

**Offering without death.** Verses 10–12 put the two side by side. Israel's hands go on the Levites and
the Levites live. The Levites' hands go on the bulls and the bulls die. Paul's *living sacrifice* (Romans
12:1) and *the offering up of the Gentiles* (Romans 15:16), and Isaiah's brothers brought *for an offering
to Yahweh* (66:20), also treat people as an offering. None of them cites Numbers 8, and the wiki claims
no dependence. It records Numbers 8 as the earliest case it has read where living people are presented in
sacrificial terms.

**A term or a gesture?** The Hebrew has the cognate verb and noun, *wəhēnîp … tənûpâ* (8:11, WLC). What
the priest physically did is not said. The same noun with gold at Exodus 35:22 is a further reason not
to press a motion, though the gold and the men need not be alike in this.

## Significance

The Levites stand where a gift stands. They belong to Israel, they are handed over before Yahweh, and they
serve as the priests' share. See [[Sacrifice and Offering]], [[Priesthood]], [[Birthright and the Firstborn]],
[[In Place of All the Firstborn - Exodus 13 and Numbers 3, 8, and 18]], and
[[Cleansed, Not Anointed - The Levites of Numbers 8 and the Priests of Leviticus 8]].
