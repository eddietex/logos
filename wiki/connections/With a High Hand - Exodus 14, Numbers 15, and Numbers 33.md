# With a High Hand - Exodus 14, Numbers 15, and Numbers 33

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 14:8** — *"the children of Israel went out with a high hand"* — [[The Crossing of the Sea]]
- **Numbers 15:30** — *"But the soul who does anything with a high hand, whether he is native-born or a
  foreigner, the same blasphemes Yahweh"* — [[Offerings in the Land and Sins of Ignorance]]
- **Numbers 33:3** — *"the children of Israel went out with a high hand in the sight of all the
  Egyptians"* *(stored WEB, not yet ingested)*
- **Deuteronomy 32:27** — *"Our hand is exalted, Yahweh has not done all this"*, the enemy's boast

(WEB; Deuteronomy checked at bible-api.com, `translation=web`, 2026-11-02.)

## Analysis

*Bəyād rāmâ* is the wording in all three of the first passages (WLC). The morphhb tags number the last
word differently at Numbers 15:30 (7411 b) than at Exodus 14:8 and Numbers 33:3 (7311), though the
written word is the same. The wiki takes the phrase to be the same and has not checked the tag
difference in a lexicon.

Used of Israel in Egypt, the phrase is open and unafraid, *in the sight of all the Egyptians*. Used of
a person in Numbers 15, it has the same openness, now toward Yahweh. Rahlfs treats the two as
different: *ἐν χειρὶ ὑψηλῇ* in Exodus and Numbers 33, *ἐν χειρὶ ὑπερηφανίας* in Numbers 15.

Deuteronomy 32:27 has *yādēnû rāmâ* (WLC) and shows the danger in it: the raised hand
takes credit for what Yahweh did. In Exodus the hand goes up with Yahweh's act, and in Numbers 15 it
goes up against his word. That is the wiki's reading.

## Significance

The same words name the people's liberation and the sin that has no offering. The difference is whom
the hand is raised against, and only Numbers 15:31 says that: the word of Yahweh.
