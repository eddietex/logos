# Ammon Protected and Ammonite Land Allotted

## Passages involved
- [[Around Seir and Past Moab and Ammon]] — Deuteronomy 2:19.
- [[The Defeat of Sihon Recounted]] — Deuteronomy 2:37.
- [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]] — Deuteronomy 3:16.
- [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]] — Joshua 13:25–27.

## Analysis

Deuteronomy 2:19 forbids Israel to contend with Ammon and promises none of its land to Israel,
because it has been given to Lot’s descendants. After Sihon’s defeat, 2:37 reports that Israel
stayed away from Ammon’s land and the prohibited riverbanks and hill-country towns.
Deuteronomy 3:16 describes the Jabbok as Ammon’s border in the eastern allocation.

Joshua 13:25 nevertheless includes half the land of the children of Ammon within Gad’s
portion, extending to Aroer near Rabbah. Verses 26–27 connect the allocation with Heshbon
and the remainder of Sihon’s kingdom. A possible reconciliation distinguishes land once
Ammonite but now held by Sihon from territory still under Ammonite control. These verses do
not narrate that transfer, however, and the proposal must remain an inference rather than
the supplied explanation. The prohibition, report of avoidance, and allotment wording all stand.

Rabbah is a geographical bearing in Joshua 13:25, not a city whose capture is narrated.
No later war account is imported to settle the issue.
