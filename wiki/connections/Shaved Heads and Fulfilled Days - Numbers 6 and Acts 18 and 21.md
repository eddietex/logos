# Shaved Heads and Fulfilled Days - Numbers 6 and Acts 18 and 21

## Passages involved

- [[The Nazirite Vow]] — Numbers 6:5, 9, 13–18.
- Acts 18:18 — *"He shaved his head in Cenchreae, for he had a vow."* *(WEB, bible-api.com; not yet
  ingested)*
- Acts 21:23–24, 26 — *"We have four men who have taken a vow ... pay their expenses for them, that they
  may shave their heads."* Paul goes into the temple *"declaring the fulfillment of the days of
  purification, until the offering was offered for every one of them."* *(same)*

## Analysis

Acts never uses the word *Nazirite* in these verses, and the identification is an inference.

Acts 21 is the closer match. It has men under a vow, heads to be shaved, a term of days, an offering for
each one, and a place at the sanctuary. Paying for someone else's offerings would make sense given how
long the list at Numbers 6:14–15 is, though Acts does not say that this is why. Acts also speaks of
*purification*, and Paul purifies himself with them, which Numbers 6 mentions only for a defiled vow
(9–12).

Acts 18 is looser. The shaving is at Cenchreae, not at a sanctuary, and Numbers 6:18 puts the end of the
vow at the door of the Tent. WEB reads Paul as the one who shaved; the Greek was not checked in this pass.
The verse may refer to some other kind of vow, and the wiki leaves this open.

## Significance

If these are Nazirite vows, the law was still in practice and valued among believers, and its cost was a
public matter. Acts is not yet ingested, so this page is provisional. See [[Oaths and Vows]].
