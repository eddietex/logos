# Wisdom Handed On - Proverbs 4 and Exodus 13

## Passages involved

- [[Get Wisdom]] — Proverbs 4:1–9, especially the teacher's memory in 4:3–4.
- [[The Consecration of the Firstborn]] — Exodus 13:8–16, a parent's explanation to a son.
- [[Do Not Consent to Sinners]] — Proverbs 1:8–9, instruction from father and mother.

## Analysis

In Proverbs 4 the teacher briefly places himself where his hearers stand. He was a
vulnerable child receiving instruction, and his current lesson includes the words his
father gave him. Wisdom is passed across generations through remembered speech and
present attention. The command not to forget (4:5) concerns keeping that teaching in force.

Exodus 13 gives the household a related responsibility. The parent tells a son why the
observance is kept (13:8); a future son's question about it receives an account of rescue
from bondage and the firstborn (13:14–15). Practices invite questions, and words attach
the practices to their meaning. The comparison is between two forms of instruction:
Exodus explains remembered deliverance and its rites; Proverbs transmits counsel for life.
Proverbs 4 does not mention Egypt or require the rites of Exodus 13.

The mother's place also needs precision. Proverbs 1:8 explicitly names her teaching;
4:3 remembers the speaker as a child in her sight, but 4:4 introduces the father's words.
The later unit does not cancel the earlier mother's teaching, nor does it quote her here.

Sources: the cited passages checked in the local WEB Exodus and Proverbs, 2026-10-25.
This is a thematic parallel, without a claim of direct literary dependence or shared
Hebrew wording.

## Significance

[[Remembrance]], [[Language and Speech]], and [[Law and Obedience]] meet in an ordinary
household. The instruction's continuity depends on a learner becoming a teacher who
still acknowledges having received it. This supplements
[[Honor Your Father and Mother - Exodus 20 and Ephesians 6]]: the parent's responsibility
to teach stands beside the child's responsibility to listen.
