# Gift and Judgment - The Two Quail Stories of Exodus 16 and Numbers 11

## Passages involved

- [[Manna and Quail]] — Exodus 16:13; *"In the evening, quail came up and covered the camp"*
- [[The Seventy Elders and the Quail]] — Numbers 11:31–34; the wind, the two days, and the plague
- **Psalm 105:40** — *"They asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of the sky."* (WEB) *(not yet ingested)*
- **Psalm 106:14–15** — *"but gave in to craving in the desert, and tested God in the wasteland. He gave them their request, but
  sent leanness into their soul."* (WEB) *(not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

**The word *śəlāw*, *quail*, occurs in only these four places** in the Hebrew Bible, by the tags: Exodus 16:13, Numbers 11:31, 32, and
Psalm 105:40. *Śəlāw* does not occur in Psalm 106.

| | Exodus 16 | Numbers 11 |
|---|---|---|
| when | fifteenth of the second month, first year (16:1) | after the twentieth of the second month, second year (10:11) |
| before Sinai or after | before | after |
| the complaint | hunger, *"to kill this whole assembly"* | craving meat, with manna on hand |
| how quail come | *came up* | a wind from Yahweh, from the sea |
| how much is told | one clause | a whole scene |
| measure | an omer a head, *according to his eating* (manna) | the least has ten homers |
| *hammamʿît* | *had no lack* (16:18) | *ten homers* (11:32) |
| outcome | *you shall know that I am Yahweh* (16:12) | a very great plague |

**The difference the text gives is motive, not need.** In Exodus they have no food, and Numbers 11 says the manna is falling. Yahweh's
stated reason is *you have rejected Yahweh who is among you* (11:20). The covenant and tabernacle lie between the two, and the wiki notes
that sequence, though neither passage cites it.

**The psalms split the memory.** Psalm 105:40 keeps a gift with no shadow, and its *satisfied* with bread looks to Exodus 16.
Psalm 106:14 uses *wayyitʾawwû taʾăwâ*, the same doubled phrase as Numbers 11:4 (WLC), and it does not say *quail*. So the two psalms
sit side by side, and each takes one story.

## Significance

A gift and a judgment can look identical from outside: birds around the camp. What differs is what the request said about Yahweh.
Numbers 11 also shows God giving what was asked as the penalty, as Psalm 106:15 states it. See [[Judgment]], [[Famine and Plenty]], and
[[Murmuring]].
