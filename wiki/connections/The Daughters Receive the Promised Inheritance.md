# The Daughters Receive the Promised Inheritance

## Passages involved
- [[The Petition of Zelophehad's Daughters]] — Numbers 27:1–11.
- [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]] — Joshua 17:1–13.

## Analysis

Numbers 27:1–11 records the petition, Moses’ referral to Yahweh, and the ruling that a
sonless man’s inheritance passes to his daughter. Joshua 17:3–4 names the same five
sisters approaching Eleazar, Joshua, and the princes and invoking that command.
The inheritance is now granted among their father’s brothers. This is an explicit appeal
to an earlier command, not merely a thematic resemblance.

Joshua 17:5–6 gives ten parts beside Gilead and Bashan and explains the daughters’ inclusion.
A reconstruction of five other family shares plus five shares for the sisters is possible,
but the text does not itemize those ten parts or identify the sisters’ parcels.
The grant should not be expanded into a claim that all daughters inherit equally with sons:
Zelophehad’s lack of sons is expressly repeated (3).

Source: stored WEB Numbers 27:1–11 and Joshua 17:1–13.

## Significance

A ruling given under Moses survives the succession of leadership and becomes an actual
allotment under Joshua. Women’s named claims are part of the territorial account itself.
See [[Inheritance Kept Within the Tribe - Zelophehad's Daughters and the Jubilee]] for the
marriage restriction recorded before this grant.
