# Holy Ground at the Bush and Before Jericho

## Passages involved

- [[The Burning Bush]] — Exodus 3:1–6.
- [[The Commander of Yahweh's Army]] — Joshua 5:13–15.

## Analysis

In Exodus 3:2–6 the angel appears in the bush, God calls Moses, and the voice tells him to
remove his sandals because the ground is holy. In Joshua 5:13–15 the armed commander
tells Joshua to remove his shoes because the place is holy. Both accounts order a human
leader's approach before the work ahead. This is a close narrative parallel visible in
the stored WEB, without an independent claim about exact Hebrew wording.

Exodus explicitly moves between the angel, Yahweh, and God; Joshua names the commander
of Yahweh's army without explaining his identity. The similarity supports continuity
between the leaders' encounters but does not by itself establish that the speakers are
the same figure. Moses' questions and extended commission also differ from Joshua's
brief question and immediate obedience.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 3:1–6 and Joshua 5:13–15; comparisons in paraphrase.

## Significance

Entry into the land continues the demand for reverent approach introduced before the
exodus. Joshua's authority remains subordinate to the presence he encounters.
