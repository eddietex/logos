# Like Grasshoppers - Numbers 13.33 and Isaiah 40.22

## Passages involved

- [[The Twelve Spies]] — Numbers 13:33
- Isaiah 40:22 — *its inhabitants are like grasshoppers* *(WEB and WLC checked, not yet ingested)*
- Leviticus 11:22 — the *ḥāgāb* among the clean insects
- Joshua 2:9 — *the fear of you has fallen upon us* *(WEB; see
  [[Terror Has Fallen on Us - The Song of the Sea in Rahab's Mouth]])*

## Analysis

***Ḥāgāb* occurs in five verses by the tags**: Leviticus 11:22, Numbers 13:33, 2 Chronicles 7:13,
Ecclesiastes 12:5, and Isaiah 40:22. Only two of them use it as a comparison for people, and both
have the form *kaḥăgābîm*.

**In Numbers the spies are the grasshoppers.** *We were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we
were in their sight.* The measure is the size of the Anakim.

**In Isaiah everyone is.** *It is he who sits above the circle of the earth, and its inhabitants are
like grasshoppers.* *Yōšəbêhā*, *its inhabitants*, is the same word as *the land that eats up its
inhabitants* of Numbers 13:32. So the comparison the spies made between themselves and the
Canaanites is true, Isaiah says, of all of them together, from a greater height. Sons of Anak and
spies have the same size from there. Whether Isaiah has Numbers in mind cannot be shown. The wiki's
point rests on the shared words.

**The second clause is the one the spies could not know.** Rahab's report at Joshua 2:9 says the
Canaanites feared Israel, so the spies' reading of the Canaanites' sight was wrong, or at least
wrong by Jericho's time.

## Significance

- Fear shrank the spies, and Isaiah 40 uses the same image to enlarge God.
- The comfort of Isaiah 40 depends on the reader being small too.
