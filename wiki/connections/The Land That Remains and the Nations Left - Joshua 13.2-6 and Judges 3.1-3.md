# The Land That Remains and the Nations Left - Joshua 13.2-6 and Judges 3.1-3

## Passages involved

- [[The Land That Remains]] — Joshua 13:1–7; *This is the land that still remains*.
- [[The Nations Left to Test Israel]] — Judges 3:1–6; *these are the nations which Yahweh left*.

## Analysis

Two lists of the same unfinished edges, given two different reasons.

| | Joshua 13:2–6 | Judges 3:3 |
|---|---|---|
| The Philistines | *all the regions of the Philistines … the five lords of the Philistines* | *the five lords of the Philistines* |
| The Canaanites | *all the land of the Canaanites* | *all the Canaanites* |
| The Sidonians | *Mearah that belongs to the Sidonians … even all the Sidonians* | *the Sidonians* |
| The north | *all Lebanon … from Baal Gad under Mount Hermon to the entrance of Hamath* | *the Hivites who lived on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal Hermon to the entrance of Hamath* |

(All stored WEB.)

**The shared words.** *Ḥămēšet sarnê pəlištîm*, *the five lords of the Philistines*, is the same in
both verses (Joshua 13:3; Judges 3:3, WLC). So is *ʿad-ləbôʾ ḥămāt*, *to the entrance of Hamath*
(Joshua 13:5; Judges 3:3, WLC). Joshua names the five lords by city: Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and
Ekron. Judges does not.

**The differences.** Joshua's southern point is *Baal Gad under Mount Hermon*; Judges has *Mount Baal
Hermon*. The OpenScriptures lemma data gives them separate entries (`1171` and `1179`). The text does not
say whether they are the same place, and the wiki keeps them apart: [[Baal Gad]] and [[Hermon]]. Joshua
does not name the people of the north; Judges calls them Hivites. Joshua also lists the Geshurites, the
Avvim, the Gebalites, Aphek, and Misrephoth Maim, none of which Judges repeats.

**The two reasons.** In Joshua 13 the land is still remaining because the work is unfinished, and
Yahweh promises to finish it: *I will drive them out from before the children of Israel. Just allocate
it to Israel for an inheritance* (13:6). In Judges 3 the same peoples are there because Yahweh *left*
them: *to test Israel by them*, and *to teach them war* (3:1–2, 4). Between the two lists stand
Joshua's warning (Joshua 23:12–13), the verdict at Bochim (Judges 2:3), and Yahweh's speech in Judges
2:20–23, *I also will no longer drive out*. The promise of Joshua 13:6 is not withdrawn in so many
words. Judges 2:20–23 says why it is not being carried out: *this nation transgressed my covenant*.

**Judah's three cities.** Judges 1:18 says *Judah took Gaza … Ashkelon … and Ekron*, three of the five
cities Joshua 13:3 lists. Judges 3:3 still counts *the five lords*. The wiki records both statements
and does not decide what *took* meant in 1:18.

## Significance

The lists show that the borders of Joshua 13 were still open in Judges 3. Joshua frames the open edges
as land to be divided before it is taken. Judges frames the same edges as nations kept in place to test
the next generation. Neither list reports a conquest of the north or of the Philistine coast. The
Philistines of this list will be Samson's enemies.
