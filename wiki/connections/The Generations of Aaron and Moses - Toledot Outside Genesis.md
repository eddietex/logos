# The Generations of Aaron and Moses - Toledot Outside Genesis

## Passages involved

- [[The Generations of Aaron and the Levites Given]] — Numbers 3:1; *the history of the generations
  of Aaron and Moses*
- [[The Census in the Wilderness of Sinai]] — Numbers 1:20–42; *their generations*, tribe by tribe
- [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]] — Exodus 6:16, 19; *according to their generations*
- [[The Priestly Garments]] — Exodus 28:10; the names on the stones
- Ruth 4:18 — *the history of the generations of Perez* *(not yet ingested; WEB verified at
  bible-api.com)*
- 1 Chronicles 1:29 — the same phrase with a suffix, of Ishmael's line *(not yet ingested; Hebrew
  checked in the WLC only)*

## Analysis

**The heading Genesis is built on nearly stops after Genesis.** *Tôlədōt* stands in thirty-nine
verses of the WLC by Strong's lemma: Genesis thirteen, Numbers thirteen, 1 Chronicles nine, Exodus
three, and Ruth one. Most uses outside Genesis are a bookkeeping form with a suffix, *by their
generations*. Twelve of Numbers' thirteen are the census of chapter 1.

**The headed form survives three times.** Outside Genesis, *ʾēlleh tôlədōt* or *wəʾēlleh tôlədōt*
followed by a name stands only at Numbers 3:1, Ruth 4:18, and 1 Chronicles 1:29 (suffixed), by a
search of the WLC. See [[The Other Line First - The Order of the Toledot in Genesis]].

**So Numbers 3:1 sounds like a Genesis heading.** That heading has introduced a line and a story in
Genesis. In Numbers it introduces a list of four names, of whom two are dead and childless, then
hands over to the Levites. No later line of Moses follows, and Aaron's line is the only one given.

**Ruth's use goes the other way.** Ruth 4:18 starts from Perez and ends at David (4:22). The heading
there opens a line that arrives somewhere; the one at Numbers 3:1 opens a line that has already been
cut in half. That contrast is a reading.
