# Forty-Eight Cities Commanded and Allotted

## Passages involved
- [[The Levitical Cities and the Cities of Refuge]] — Numbers 35:1–8.
- [[The Division of the Land Begins]] — Joshua 14:1–5.
- [[The Cities of Refuge Appointed]] — Joshua 20:1–9.
- [[The Cities of the Levites]] — Joshua 21:1–42.

## Analysis

Numbers 35 commands forty-eight cities, including six refuges, with surrounding land for
livestock. Joshua 14:4 explicitly reserves cities and suburbs for Levi despite the absence
of a territorial share. Joshua 21 turns the command into a request at Shiloh and an allocation
by lot: thirteen cities for Aaron’s descendants, ten for the other Kohathites, thirteen for
Gershon’s descendants, and twelve for Merari’s descendants. The total is forty-eight (41).

The six refuges designated in Joshua 20 occur within this allocation. Five are expressly
called refuges here (13, 21, 27, 32, 38); Bezer appears in the Reuben list (36) without that
label. Its refuge status comes from Joshua 20:8, not from an added phrase in 21:36.

This is fulfillment of a settlement command, not a claim that each city was exclusively
occupied by Levites. Gezer’s surviving Canaanites in Joshua 16:10 and Manasseh’s unremoved
inhabitants in 17:11–13 remain relevant. Nor does the list explain Numbers 35’s two different
measurements for the surrounding land: Joshua supplies no new dimensions.

## Significance

The distribution gives concrete form to Israel’s obligation to house Levi within the other
tribes. It joins priestly and Levitical provision with refuge for the unwitting killer while
preserving the distinction between a tribal territorial inheritance and local property.
