# I Brought You Up from Egypt - The Indictments at Bochim and in Gideon's Day

## Passages involved
- [[The Angel at Bochim]] — Judges 2:1–3. Yahweh's angel: *I brought you out of Egypt … But you have
  not listened to my voice* (stored WEB).
- **Judges 2:20–21** — Yahweh, speaking without a messenger: *Because this nation transgressed my
  covenant which I commanded their fathers, and has not listened to my voice, I also will no longer
  drive out any of the nations that Joshua left when he died* (stored WEB; *not yet ingested*).
- **Judges 6:8–10** — a prophet: *I brought you up from Egypt, and brought you out of the house of
  bondage … I said to you, "I am Yahweh your God. You shall not fear the gods of the Amorites, in
  whose land you dwell." But you have not listened to my voice* (stored WEB; *not yet ingested*).
- [[The Angel Sent Ahead]] — Exodus 23:21–22. *Listen to his voice … if you indeed listen to his
  voice, and do all that I speak* (stored WEB). The condition the three Judges speeches say was
  broken.

## Analysis

**Three speeches, one pattern.** Each recalls what Yahweh did, quotes what he said, and ends with the
same charge. All three have *lōʾ šəmaʿtem bəqōlî* or its third-person form, *you have not listened to
my voice* (2:2; 6:10) and *they have not listened to my voice* (2:20, *lōʾ šāməʿû ləqôlî*; all WLC,
OpenScriptures morphhb, 2026-11-13).

| | Speaker | What Yahweh did | What he said | The charge | The result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2:1–3 | Yahweh's angel | *brought you out of Egypt … to the land* | no covenant, break their altars | *you have not listened to my voice* | *I will not drive them out* |
| 2:20–21 | Yahweh | (the covenant *commanded their fathers*) | — | *has not listened to my voice* | *I also will no longer drive out* |
| 6:8–10 | a prophet | *brought you up from Egypt … out of the house of bondage … drove them out … gave you their land* | *You shall not fear the gods of the Amorites* | *you have not listened to my voice* | none stated; the angel comes to Gideon next (6:11) |

**Different messengers.** At Bochim the angel speaks in Yahweh's first person with no introduction.
At 6:8 the speaker is *ʾîš nābîʾ*, *a man, a prophet*, and he uses the formula *kōh ʾāmar YHWH*,
*thus says Yahweh* (WLC). At 2:20 no messenger is named. The book uses all three ways of delivering
the same charge. The difference is in the text; what it implies about the angel's identity is left
open, as on [[The Angel of Yahweh]].

**Different verbs for "brought up".** At 2:1 the form is *ʾaʿăleh*, parsed as hiphil imperfect
(`HVhi1cs`). At 6:8 it is *heʿĕlêtî*, hiphil perfect (`HVhp1cs`; both WLC morphology). Both are
rendered as past in the WEB. The grammar of 2:1 is noted on [[The Angel at Bochim]] and not settled.

**Different verbs for "drive out".** At 2:3 Yahweh says *lōʾ ʾăgārēš*, from *gāraš*, the verb of his
promise in Exodus 23:28–31 and 34:11. At 2:21 he says *lōʾ ʾôsîp ləhôrîš*, *I will no longer drive
out*, from *yāraš*, the verb Judges 1 used of the tribes (WLC). Joshua 23:13 has the same phrase as
2:21, *lōʾ yôsîp … ləhôrîš* (WLC), in the third person.

## Significance
- Judges states its verdict three times, each time by a different voice. The wording of the charge is
  the same, and it answers the condition set in Exodus 23:22.
- The speeches put the exodus first. Each charge is set against what Yahweh had already done.
- See also [[Little by Little - The Hornet, the Snare, and the Land Not Taken]], for the reasons given
  in Scripture for the peoples who remained.

### Judges 2:20–21 now ingested

[[Another Generation Arose]] gives the second speech its setting: it comes at the end of the summary
of the judges period, after the anger, the raiders, the judges, and each relapse. The charge is in
two parts: *this nation transgressed my covenant which I commanded their fathers* and *has not
listened to my voice* (2:20). The first part repeats Joshua 23:16, which joined *ʿābar* with *bərît*
and *ṣiwwâ* in its warning (WLC). Israel is called *haggôy hazzeh*, *this nation*, with the word for the
nations. The speech then adds a purpose Bochim did not give: the nations stay *that by them I may test
Israel* (2:22). Sources: stored WEB Judges 2:20–22 and Joshua 23:16; Hebrew from the Westminster
Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.
