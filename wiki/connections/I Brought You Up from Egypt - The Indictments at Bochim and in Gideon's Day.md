# I Brought You Up from Egypt - The Indictments at Bochim and in Gideon's Day

## Passages involved
- [[The Angel at Bochim]] — Judges 2:1–3. Yahweh's angel: *I brought you out of Egypt … But you have
  not listened to my voice* (stored WEB).
- **Judges 2:20–21** — Yahweh, speaking without a messenger: *Because this nation transgressed my
  covenant which I commanded their fathers, and has not listened to my voice, I also will no longer
  drive out any of the nations that Joshua left when he died*
  (stored WEB; see [[Another Generation Arose]]).
- [[Midian's Oppression and the Prophet]] — Judges 6:8–10. A prophet: *I brought you up from Egypt,
  and brought you out of the house of bondage … I said to you, "I am Yahweh your God. You shall not
  fear the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you dwell." But you have not listened to my voice* (stored WEB).
- [[The Call of Gideon]] — Judges 6:13. Gideon to the angel: *Where are all his wondrous works which
  our fathers told us of, saying, 'Didn't Yahweh bring us up from Egypt?' But now Yahweh has cast us
  off* (stored WEB).
- [[The Angel Sent Ahead]] — Exodus 23:21–22. *Listen to his voice … if you indeed listen to his
  voice, and do all that I speak* (stored WEB). The condition the three Judges speeches say was
  broken.
- [[Israel's Repentance Under Ammon]] — Judges 10:11–14. Yahweh, to Israel: *Didn't I save you from the Egyptians … Therefore I will save you no more* (stored WEB).

## Analysis

**Three speeches, one pattern.** Each recalls what Yahweh did, quotes what he said, and ends with the
same charge. All three have *lōʾ šəmaʿtem bəqōlî* or its third-person form, *you have not listened to
my voice* (2:2; 6:10) and *they have not listened to my voice* (2:20, *lōʾ šāməʿû ləqôlî*; all WLC,
OpenScriptures morphhb, 2026-11-13).

| | Speaker | What Yahweh did | What he said | The charge | The result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2:1–3 | Yahweh's angel | *brought you out of Egypt … to the land* | no covenant, break their altars | *you have not listened to my voice* | *I will not drive them out* |
| 2:20–21 | Yahweh | (the covenant *commanded their fathers*) | — | *has not listened to my voice* | *I also will no longer drive out* |
| 6:8–10 | a prophet | *brought you up from Egypt … out of the house of bondage … drove them out … gave you their land* | *You shall not fear the gods of the Amorites* | *you have not listened to my voice* | none stated; the angel comes to Gideon next (6:11) |

**Different messengers.** At Bochim the angel speaks in Yahweh's first person with no introduction.
At 6:8 the speaker is *ʾîš nābîʾ*, *a man, a prophet*, and he uses the formula *kōh ʾāmar YHWH*,
*thus says Yahweh* (WLC). At 2:20 no messenger is named. The book uses all three ways of delivering
the same charge. The difference is in the text; what it implies about the angel's identity is left
open, as on [[The Angel of Yahweh]].

**Different verbs for "brought up".** At 2:1 the form is *ʾaʿăleh*, parsed as hiphil imperfect
(`HVhi1cs`). At 6:8 it is *heʿĕlêtî*, hiphil perfect (`HVhp1cs`; both WLC morphology). Both are
rendered as past in the WEB. The grammar of 2:1 is noted on [[The Angel at Bochim]] and not settled.

**Different verbs for "drive out".** At 2:3 Yahweh says *lōʾ ʾăgārēš*, from *gāraš*, the verb of his
promise in Exodus 23:28–31 and 34:11. At 2:21 he says *lōʾ ʾôsîp ləhôrîš*, *I will no longer drive
out*, from *yāraš*, the verb Judges 1 used of the tribes (WLC). Joshua 23:13 has the same phrase as
2:21, *lōʾ yôsîp … ləhôrîš* (WLC), in the third person.

## Significance
- Judges states its verdict three times, each time by a different voice. The wording of the charge is
  the same, and it answers the condition set in Exodus 23:22.
- The speeches put the exodus first. Each charge is set against what Yahweh had already done.
- See also [[Little by Little - The Hornet, the Snare, and the Land Not Taken]], for the reasons given
  in Scripture for the peoples who remained.

### Judges 2:20–21 now ingested

[[Another Generation Arose]] gives the second speech its setting: it comes at the end of the summary
of the judges period, after the anger, the raiders, the judges, and each relapse. The charge is in
two parts: *this nation transgressed my covenant which I commanded their fathers* and *has not
listened to my voice* (2:20). The first part repeats Joshua 23:16, which joined *ʿābar* with *bərît*
and *ṣiwwâ* in its warning (WLC). Israel is called *haggôy hazzeh*, *this nation*, with the word for the
nations. The speech then adds a purpose Bochim did not give: the nations stay *that by them I may test
Israel* (2:22). Sources: stored WEB Judges 2:20–22 and Joshua 23:16; Hebrew from the Westminster
Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.

### Judges 6:8–10 now ingested

[[Midian's Oppression and the Prophet]] gives the third speech its setting. It comes after the cry,
which the narrator tells twice (6:6–7), and before any deliverer. The prophet's history is longer than
the angel's: *I delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all who oppressed
you, and drove them out from before you, and gave you their land* (6:9). His *drove them out* is
*wāʾăgārēš*, from *gāraš* (WLC), the verb Bochim used negatively: what Yahweh did then is what he said
at Bochim he would no longer do. His quoted command, *You shall not fear the gods of the Amorites, in
whose land you dwell* (6:10), repeats the clause of Joshua 24:15 (WLC). Of the three speeches, his is
the only one with no stated result; the angel of 6:11 comes instead. See
[[The Prophet (of Gideon's time)]]. Sources: stored WEB Judges 6:6–11; Joshua 24:15; Hebrew from the
Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### Judges 6:13 — the recital answered

Three verses after the prophet's *I brought you up from Egypt* (*heʿĕlêtî*, 6:8), Gideon quotes his
fathers: *Didn't Yahweh bring us up from Egypt?* (*heʿĕlānû*, 6:13). The verb is the same, *ʿālâ* in
the hiphil (WLC), and it is the verb of Bochim's *I brought you up* (2:1). The three speeches gave the
history as grounds for a charge; Gideon gives it as grounds for a complaint, *now Yahweh has cast us
off*. The prophet's answer to *why then has all this happened to us?* was *you have not listened to my
voice* (6:10). The text sets the two speeches next to each other and does not say whether Gideon had
heard the prophet. See [[The Call of Gideon]]. Sources: stored WEB Judges 6:11–24; Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### Judges 6:25–32 — the charge acted on

Bochim's charge had a specific content: *You shall break down their altars.' But you have not listened
to my voice* (2:2). The prophet of 6:10 repeated *you have not listened to my voice*. The same night as
his call, Gideon is sent to do what Bochim said Israel had not done. He throws down his father's altar
of Baal and cuts down its Asherah (6:25–28). The verb of the report, *nātaṣ*, is the verb of 2:2 (WLC).
The prophet's command was *You shall not fear the gods of the Amorites* (6:10). Gideon does the deed
by night *because he feared his father's household and the men of the city* (6:27), so he fears the
god's worshipers and not the god. See [[Gideon Throws Down Baal's Altar]] and
[[Break Down Their Altars - Exodus 34.13, Deuteronomy 7.5, Judges 2.2, and Judges 6.25-32]]. Sources:
stored WEB Judges 2:2; 6:10, 25–28; Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### Judges 10:11–14 — the fourth speech, and a refusal

[[Israel's Repentance Under Ammon]] adds a fourth speech, and it is different from the others. *Yahweh said
to the children of Israel* (10:11), with no angel or prophet. It answers a cry that, for the first time,
has words: *We have sinned against you* (10:10). The history is a list of seven peoples, beginning with
*the Egyptians* (10:11–12). The charge is not *you have not listened to my voice* but *you have forsaken
me, and served other gods* (10:13). And the result is stated in the shape of 2:21: *lōʾ-ʾôsîp ləhôšîaʿ
ʾetkem*, *I will save you no more* (10:13), beside *lōʾ ʾôsîp ləhôrîš*, *I also will no longer drive out*
(2:21; both WLC). At 2:21 Yahweh stopped driving out the nations; at 10:13 he says he will stop saving
Israel from them.

| | Speaker | What Yahweh did | The charge | The result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10:11–14 | Yahweh, to Israel | *save you from the Egyptians … I saved you out of their hand* | *you have forsaken me, and served other gods* | *I will save you no more. Go and cry to the gods which you have chosen* |

Unlike the earlier three, this speech is answered. Israel confesses again and puts away its gods
(10:15–16). See [[I Will Save You No More - Deuteronomy 32.37-38, Joshua 24, and Judges 10.13-14]].
