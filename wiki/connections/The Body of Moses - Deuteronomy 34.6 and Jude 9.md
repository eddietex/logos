# The Body of Moses - Deuteronomy 34.6 and Jude 9

## Passages involved
- [[The Death of Moses]] — Deuteronomy 34:5–6; *"He buried him in the valley in the land of Moab
  opposite Beth Peor, but no man knows where his tomb is to this day."*
- **Jude 9** — *"But Michael, the archangel, when contending with the devil and arguing about the
  body of Moses, dared not bring against him an abusive condemnation, but said, ‘May the Lord
  rebuke you!’"* (WEB, verified at bible-api.com, 2026-11-10) *(not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

**What Deuteronomy says.** Moses dies *according to Yahweh's word*, and *he buried him*. The
Hebrew verb is singular (*wayyiqbōr*, MAM via Sefaria), and the nearest named actor is Yahweh;
the verse does not name the subject outright. The grave's place is unknown *to this day*. No
angel, no dispute, and no further fate of the body appears in the Torah.

**What Jude adds.** Jude refers to a dispute between Michael and the devil over Moses' body, as
something his readers already know. Deuteronomy supplies no such episode. Ancient Christian
writers linked Jude 9 to a lost or partly preserved work often called the *Assumption* (or
*Testament*) *of Moses*; that attribution is recalled from secondary scholarship and has not been
verified for this page. Jude's own point is the restraint of Michael's speech, not the burial.

**The two do not contradict.** Jude does not locate the grave or say the body was taken up.
Deuteronomy's unknown tomb leaves room for a tradition about the body; it does not confirm one.

## Significance

The hidden grave is the detail of Deuteronomy 34 that later tradition built on most. The wiki
keeps the Torah's statement and Jude's allusion side by side without merging them. See
[[Burial and the Grave]], [[Moses]], and
[[Aaron on Hor and Moses on Nebo - Numbers 20 and Deuteronomy 34]].
