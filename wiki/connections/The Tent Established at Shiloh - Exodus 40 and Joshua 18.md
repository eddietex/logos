# The Tent Established at Shiloh - Exodus 40 and Joshua 18

## Passages involved
- [[The Tabernacle Raised and the Glory]] — Exodus 40:17–19.
- [[The Land Surveyed at Shiloh]] — Joshua 18:1–10.

## Analysis

Exodus 40:17–19 describes Moses erecting the tabernacle with its structural parts and coverings.
Joshua 18:1 describes the assembled congregation setting up the Tent of Meeting at Shiloh.
The sanctuary that accompanied the wilderness narrative now stands at the center of an
allotment assembly in the land.

Joshua repeatedly locates the casting of lots before Yahweh at Shiloh (6, 8, 10). That spatial
connection joins worship and public administration. Unlike the close of Exodus 40, this unit
does not report the cloud covering the Tent or glory filling the sanctuary; it should not acquire that event by analogy.

This is narrative continuity, not an explicit prophecy/fulfillment quotation. Joshua 18 supplies
neither construction details nor a claim that the Tent has become a stone temple.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 40:17–19, 34–35 and Joshua 18:1–10; comparisons are paraphrases.

## Significance

[[The Dwelling of God]] and [[Land and Sojourning]] meet at a named place: the sanctuary
anchors the assembly where the remaining inheritance is divided.
