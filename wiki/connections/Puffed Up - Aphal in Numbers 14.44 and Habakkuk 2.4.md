# Puffed Up - Aphal in Numbers 14.44 and Habakkuk 2.4

## Passages involved

- [[The Defeat at Hormah]] — Numbers 14:44
- Habakkuk 2:4 — *his soul is puffed up* *(WEB checked, not yet ingested)*
- Hebrews 10:38 — *If he shrinks back* *(WEB checked, not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

Strong's 6075 has two entries in the morphhb tags, and they cover only these two places: *wayyaʿpilû*
at Numbers 14:44, marked 6075 b, a hiphil, and *ʿuppəlâ* at Habakkuk 2:4, marked 6075 a, a pual. The
letters show the tagging treats them as distinct, so a link between them is not established. The
identification with *ʿōpel*, *mound*, and a sense of swelling, is recalled and was not checked in a
lexicon.

**If they are the same word.** Numbers has a people who go up, and Habakkuk has a soul that is lifted.
Habakkuk sets it against *the righteous will live by his faith*, and Numbers 14 is the chapter of
*how long will they not believe in me* (14:11), with Joshua and Caleb living (14:38). The likeness is
suggestive, but it is the wiki's, and it depends on a root connection the sources here do not
prove.

**The Greek.** Rahlfs has *ἐὰν ὑποστείληται*, *if he shrinks back*, at Habakkuk 2:4, and WEB's Hebrews
10:38 has the matching English; the Greek of Hebrews was not checked for this page. It has *διαβιασάμενοι*, *forcing their way*, at Numbers 14:44. The two
Greek words point in opposite directions. So the Greek tradition does not support the link.

## Significance

- This is a thin link, kept because the word is so rare and because the setting is the same question
  of faith. It should not be cited as a demonstrated connection.
- See [[Pride and Self-Exaltation]] and [[Faith]].
