# Tumors and Confusion - The Covenant Curses on Ashdod, Gath, and Ekron

## Passages involved
- [[Blessing and the Removal of the Nations]] — **Deuteronomy 7:23** — *Yahweh your God will deliver them up
  before you, and will confuse them with a great confusion, until they are destroyed.* (stored WEB)
- [[Curses for Disobedience]] — **Deuteronomy 28:20** — *Yahweh will send on you cursing, confusion, and
  rebuke*; **28:27** — *Yahweh will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with the tumors, with the scurvy, and
  with the itch, of which you can not be healed.* (stored WEB)
- [[The Ark Among the Philistines]] — **1 Samuel 5:6, 9, 11–12** — *struck them with tumors*; *a very great
  confusion*; *a deadly confusion throughout all the city*; *the men who didn't die were struck with the
  tumors* (stored WEB)
- **1 Samuel 6:4–5, 11, 17** — *five golden tumors* as the Philistines' trespass offering (stored WEB; not yet
  ingested)

## Analysis

Two rare nouns tie 1 Samuel 5 to Deuteronomy.

**Tumors.** At Deuteronomy 28:27 and at 1 Samuel 5:6, 9, 12 the consonantal text (ketiv) writes *ʿŏp̄ālîm*
and the Masoretic reading (qere) is *ṭəḥōrîm*; the WLC records both. In the WLC files searched (Genesis
through Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, Psalms, Zephaniah) *ṭəḥōrîm* (lemma 2914) occurs only at
Deuteronomy 28:27 and 1 Samuel 5:6, 9, 12; 6:4, 5, 11, 17, and *ʿŏp̄ālîm* in this sense (lemma 6076 b) only
at Deuteronomy 28:27 and 1 Samuel 5:6, 9, 12; 6:4, 5 (lemma search). The ketiv/qere pair is the same in both
books.

**Confusion.** *Məhûmâ* (lemma 4103), *confusion, panic*, occurs in the same files at Deuteronomy 7:23 and
28:20, at 1 Samuel 5:9, 11, and at 1 Samuel 14:20 (lemma search). It is related to the verb *hāmam*, *to
confuse*, which Yahweh does to Israel's enemies (see
[[Yahweh Confused Them - Hamam in the Wars of Yahweh]]); morphhb gives the noun its own lemma.

| | Deuteronomy | 1 Samuel 5 |
|---|---|---|
| *məhûmâ* promised against the nations | 7:23, *a great confusion* | 5:9, *a very great confusion* |
| *məhûmâ* threatened against Israel | 28:20 | — |
| *ṭəḥōrîm* threatened against Israel | 28:27 | 5:6, 9, 12, on the Philistines |
| the agent | *Yahweh will strike you*, *yakkəḵâ* (28:27) | *he struck them*, *wayyaḵ* (5:6, 9) |

Deuteronomy 7:23 promised the confusion for Israel's enemies; Deuteronomy 28 turned the same confusion and
the tumors against a disobedient Israel. In 1 Samuel 4 the curse has fallen on Israel, *struck before your
enemies* (see [[Struck Before Your Enemies - Leviticus 26, Numbers 14, Deuteronomy 28, and 1 Kings 8]]); in 1
Samuel 5 the curse's diseases fall on the enemy who holds the ark. The narrator does not cite Deuteronomy.

## Significance

The Philistines receive what Deuteronomy called *the boils of Egypt*, and in the next chapter their priests
compare themselves to Egypt and Pharaoh (6:6). The pairing shows the covenant curses as Yahweh's weapons,
not Israel's fate only, and the ark as their bearer even in captivity. See
[[The Diseases of Egypt - The Plagues in Deuteronomy 28]], [[Curse]], and [[Judgment]]. The identification
of the disease is not given by the text.
