# The Dance of Mahanaim - Genesis 32.1-2 and Song of Solomon 6.13

## Passages involved

- [[Wrestling at the Jabbok]] — Genesis 32:1–2.
- [[Return, Return, Shulammite]] — Song of Solomon 6:13.

## Analysis

Genesis 32:1–2 narrates angels meeting Jacob and his naming the place Mahanaim.
Song 6:13 uses Mahanaim in a question comparing the gaze at the Shulammite with a dance.
The shared name in the stored WEB permits a cross-reference, but the two passages do
different things: one explains a place name after an encounter; the other evokes a dance.

The Song does not name Jacob or angels, narrate travel to the place, or explain the dance.
It cannot establish that the woman reenacts Jacob's meeting, that angels are dancing,
or that a specific ancient choreography has been recovered. This page compares the
English texts without deciding alternative translations or Hebrew etymology.

Sources: stored WEB Genesis 32:1–2 and Song of Solomon 6:13.

## Significance

[[Mahanaim]] moves from a named encounter to an image in love poetry. Keeping the
connection limited preserves both the recurrence and the lack of an explicit allusion.
See [[Seeing and Being Seen]] and [[Praise and Song]].
