# A Blessing and a Curse Set Before You - Deuteronomy 11.26-28 and 30.15-20

## Passages involved

- [[Set These Words Before You]] — Deuteronomy 11:26–28; *"Behold, I set before you today a
  blessing and a curse"*: the blessing if Israel listens, the curse if it turns aside to other gods.
- **Deuteronomy 30:15–20** *(stored WEB, not yet ingested)* — *"Behold, I have set before you today
  life and prosperity, and death and evil"* (15); *"I have set before you life and death, the
  blessing and the curse. Therefore choose life"* (19); and to *"cling to him; for he is your life,
  and the length of your days"* (20).
- **Deuteronomy 4:26** *(ingested)* — *"I call heaven and earth to witness against you today"*, the
  same witness formula 30:19 uses. See [[No Form Seen and No Image Made]].

## Analysis

The two passages close two sections of Deuteronomy. Chapter 11 ends the exhortation that began at
4:44, just before the statutes of chapters 12–26. Chapter 30 ends the covenant made in Moab,
before Moses' succession and death. Both reduce what precedes to a pair set before the people
*today*.

**What stays the same.** The verb phrase *"set before you today"* in the WEB; a pair of outcomes;
and the condition of listening to Yahweh's commandments against turning away to other gods
(11:27–28; compare 30:16–17 in the stored WEB).

**What is added in chapter 30.** Chapter 11 names only *"a blessing and a curse"*. Chapter 30 first
names *"life and prosperity, and death and evil"* (15) and then puts the two pairs together: *"life
and death, the blessing and the curse"* (19). It also calls heaven and earth as witnesses and gives
an explicit command, *"choose life"*. Chapter 11 states the condition but does not say *choose*.

**What links the frame.** Clinging to Yahweh is part of the condition at 11:22, and at 30:20
clinging is joined to *"he is your life, and the length of your days"*. Chapter 11 likewise ties
obedience to days *"multiplied … as the days of the heavens above the earth"* (11:21). Long life in
the land is the shared horizon of both. This is an English-level comparison in the WEB; no claim is
made about the Hebrew verbs.

## Significance

The choice that 11:26 frames as a blessing and a curse becomes, by chapter 30, explicitly a choice
of life or death. Read together, the two passages bracket the law code and the covenant ceremony.
