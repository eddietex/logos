# The Continual Burnt Offering - Exodus 29, Numbers 28, and Daniel 8

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 29:38–42** — two lambs a year old *"day by day continually"*, morning and evening,
  *"a continual burnt offering throughout your generations"* — [[The Ordination of the Priests]].
- **Numbers 28:1–8** — the same offering at the head of the festival calendar, *"ordained in Mount
  Sinai"* *(verified WEB; not yet ingested)*.
- **Daniel 8:11–13** — in a vision, a power takes away *"the continual burnt offering"*, and a holy
  one asks how long the vision will last *(verified WEB; not yet ingested)*.

## Analysis

**Exodus sets the offering beside the ordination.** The priests are installed in 29:1–37, and the
next instruction is what they will offer on the altar every day (29:38–41). The site is the door of
the Tent of Meeting, *"where I will meet with you, to speak there to you"* (29:42). In MAM the phrase
is *ʿōlat tāmîd*, a burnt offering of *continuity*. Its basic schedule matches the lamp of
[[From Evening to Morning - The Lamp in Exodus 27 and Leviticus 24]]: Exodus fills both ends of the
day with priestly service.

**Numbers repeats the measures and names their source.** Numbers 28:3–8 gives the same two male lambs
a year old, one in the morning and one at evening, a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a
fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a fourth of a hin for the drink offering. Numbers adds that the
lambs are *"without defect"* and that the drink is *"strong drink"* poured *"in the holy place"*.
It calls this *"a continual burnt offering, which was ordained in Mount Sinai"* (28:6), pointing back to
the Sinai instruction. The festival offerings of Numbers 28–29 are then stated as additions to this
daily base, a structure noted here as context only.

**Daniel makes its removal a sign of catastrophe.** In Daniel 8:11–13 the power of the vision
*"took away from him the continual burnt offering, and the place of his sanctuary was cast down"*.
The question beginning *"How long will the vision"* concerns the continual burnt offering and the sanctuary. Daniel's
historical referent is not decided here, and Daniel 8 was not read beyond verses 11–13 for this
page.

## Significance

**The daily lamb measures whether the arrangement of Exodus 29:42–46 is in force.** In Exodus the
offering marks the place where God meets and dwells. Numbers keeps it as the base of the calendar.
Daniel treats its interruption as the sanctuary's ruin. The thread is the same English phrase in the
WEB in all three passages; whether the Hebrew of Numbers and the Hebrew and Aramaic setting of
Daniel use *tāmîd* in the same construction was not checked for this page. See
[[Sacrifice and Offering]], [[Priesthood]], and [[The Dwelling of God]].
