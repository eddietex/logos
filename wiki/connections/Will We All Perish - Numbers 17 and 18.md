# Will We All Perish - Numbers 17 and 18

## Passages involved
- [[Aaron's Rod Buds]] — Numbers 17:12–13.
- [[The Duties and Portions of Priests and Levites]] — Numbers 18:1–32.

## Analysis

Israel closes Numbers 17 fearing that everyone approaching the tabernacle dies.
Numbers 18:1–7 immediately assigns responsibility to Aaron and his sons, with
Levites assisting within stated limits. Verse 5 names the purpose: no further wrath
on Israel. The sequence reads as an answer to the people’s fear, although the
chapter does not explicitly introduce itself as a reply to their question.

The answer neither abolishes danger nor declares all approach fatal. It differentiates
service, restricts unauthorized approach, and provides for those who bear the duty.
The gifts of service (6–7) and material support (8–32) belong to the same arrangement.

Sources: stored WEB passages cited above.

## Significance

The chosen priesthood protects the community while carrying a responsibility of its
own. Israel’s fearful generalization must not be mistaken for the law that follows.
