# Cities You Did Not Build - Deuteronomy 6.10-12 and Joshua 24.13

## Passages involved

- [[Do Not Forget When You Are Full]] — Deuteronomy 6:10–12: *"great and goodly cities, which you
  didn't build, and houses full of all good things, which you didn't fill, and cisterns dug out,
  which you didn't dig, vineyards and olive trees, which you didn't plant, and you shall eat and
  be full; then beware lest you forget Yahweh, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of
  the house of bondage."*
- [[The Covenant at Shechem]] — Joshua 24:13: *"I gave you a land whereon you had not labored, and
  cities which you didn't build, and you live in them. You eat of vineyards and olive groves which
  you didn't plant."*

## Analysis

**Promise, then report.** Moses speaks before the crossing and describes what Israel will find.
Joshua, at the end of his life, speaks in Yahweh's first person and says it has happened: *you
live in them*. In the WEB the shared terms are cities *which you didn't build* and vineyards and
olive trees or groves *which you didn't plant*. Joshua omits Moses' houses and cisterns and adds
*a land whereon you had not labored*.

**The warning comes in the same order.** Deuteronomy 6 follows the list with *beware lest you
forget Yahweh* (12), *You shall fear Yahweh your God; and you shall serve him* (13), *You shall not
go after other gods* (14), and *Yahweh your God among you is a jealous God* (15). Joshua 24
follows verse 13 with *fear Yahweh, and serve him* (14), the choice between Yahweh and other gods
(15), and *He is a jealous God* (19). Both texts say the dangerous moment is not hunger but
fullness. Deuteronomy 6:11 says it outright: *you shall eat and be full*.

**Both tie the gift to the exodus.** Deuteronomy 6:12 names Yahweh *who brought you out of the
land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage*. The people at Shechem answer in nearly the same
words: *who brought us and our fathers up out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage*
(Joshua 24:17). The phrase also opens the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:2, stored WEB).

## Significance

- The land is presented as inherited labor, not earned possession, in both texts. That is the
  wiki's recurring theme of gift in [[Land and Sojourning]]; see also
  [[Not for Your Righteousness - The Ancestral Oath and the Gift of the Land]].
- Joshua 24 makes a Mosaic warning into a present test. The condition Moses described is now
  Israel's condition, so the warning applies now. See [[Remembrance]] and [[Idolatry]].
- [[Wealth and Possessions]]: the good things in themselves are not condemned. Forgetting the giver
  is.
