# Twenty-Two Thousand - The Levite Sums in Numbers 3, 4, and 26

## Passages involved

- [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]] — Numbers 3:22, 28, 34, 39; the three houses and the stated total
- Numbers 3:43, 46 — the 22,273 firstborn, and the 273 over
- [[The Service of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari]] — Numbers 4:36, 40, 44, 48; the working adults
- Numbers 26:62 — the second Levite total *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*

## Analysis

**The figures.**

| House | Males from a month (Numbers 3) | Aged 30–50 (Numbers 4) |
|---|---|---|
| Gershon | 7,500 | 2,630 |
| Kohath | 8,600 | 2,750 |
| Merari | 6,200 | 3,200 |
| Sum of the parts | 22,300 | 8,580 |
| Total stated | 22,000 (3:39) | 8,580 (4:48) |

The sums of chapter 4 add up exactly, and the Hebrew of 4:36, 40, 44, and 48 has the same figures
(WLC). The sum of chapter 3 is off by 300, and the Hebrew of the four
verses has the same figures as WEB (WLC). Numbers 26:62 later gives 23,000, a rounded number on its
face, with no parts.

**The stated total is the working one.** The exchange reckons 22,273 less 22,000, which gives the 273 of
3:46. If the parts had been used there would be no surplus at all, and Levites left over. So the
difference has consequences in the story: 1,365 shekels depend on it.

**Three accounts, none in the text.**

1. Rashi on 3:39, citing Bekhorot 5a, says the 300 were firstborn Levites, already claimed, who could
   not also stand for someone else. The wiki reads this in the Rosenbaum–Silbermann English at Sefaria.
   It explains the shape of the numbers, but Numbers never says it.
2. A copying slip at 3:28 is a common proposal, *šēš*, six, for *šālōš*, three, which would make Kohath
   8,300 and the parts 22,000. It is reported to have Greek support. That report was not checked here,
   and the WLC has *šēš*.
3. The total may simply be rounded, but the exact subtraction that follows makes that an awkward fit.

The wiki records the difference and adopts no account.

**A proportion worth noting.** Merari is the smallest house counted from a month old and the largest
among working adults: 52 percent of Merari is of working age, 35 percent of Gershon, and 32 percent of
Kohath, against the 8,600 as written. That is arithmetic from the table, and the text makes no comment.

## Significance

The unit is careful with numbers, down to five shekels on 273 men, which is why the gap stands out. See
[[Six Hundred Three Thousand Five Hundred Fifty - Exodus 38.26 and Numbers 1.46]] for the larger
question of Numbers' figures, and [[Five Shekels a Head - Numbers 3, Numbers 18, and Leviticus 27]] for the money.
