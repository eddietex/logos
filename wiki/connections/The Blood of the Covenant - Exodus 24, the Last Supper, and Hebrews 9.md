# The Blood of the Covenant - Exodus 24, the Last Supper, and Hebrews 9

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 24:6–8** — *"Look, this is the blood of the covenant, which Yahweh has made with you
  concerning all these words"* — [[The Blood of the Covenant]]
- **Zechariah 9:11** — *"because of the blood of your covenant, I have set free your prisoners from
  the pit in which is no water"* (WEB; *not yet ingested*)
- **Jeremiah 31:31–33** — *"I will make a new covenant … not according to the covenant that I made
  with their fathers in the day that I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of
  Egypt"* (WEB; *not yet ingested*)
- **Matthew 26:27–28; Mark 14:23–24** — *"this is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out

- **Hebrews 12:24; 1 Peter 1:2** — *"the blood of sprinkling that speaks better than that of

## Analysis

**Moses' sentence at Sinai is repeated, in two forms, in the New Testament.** The source-language
texts (checked 2026-10-23):

| Text | Edition | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 24:8 | MAM | *hinnēh dam-habbərît ʾăšer kārat YHWH ʿimmākem* — *behold, the blood of the covenant which Yahweh has cut with you* |
| Exodus 24:8 | LXX (Rahlfs) | *idou to haima tēs diathēkēs hēs dietheto kyrios pros hymas* |
| Matthew 26:28 | SBLGNT | *touto gar estin to haima mou tēs diathēkēs to peri pollōn ekchynnomenon eis aphesin hamartiōn* |
| Mark 14:24 | SBLGNT | *Touto estin to haima mou tēs diathēkēs to ekchynnomenon hyper pollōn* |
| Hebrews 9:20 | SBLGNT | *Touto to haima tēs diathēkēs hēs eneteilato pros hymas ho theos* |

Matthew and Mark, in the SBLGNT, have *to haima mou tēs diathēkēs*, *my blood of the covenant* —
the Septuagint's *to haima tēs diathēkēs* with *my* added. **The WEB's *"of the new covenant"* in
both Gospels reflects a textual variant**: the SBLGNT marks a variant at that point and prints no
*kainēs*, *new*. With the shorter text, the Gospel sentence is closer to Exodus 24:8; with the
longer, it also echoes Jeremiah 31:31. Luke 22:20 and 1 Corinthians 11:25 say *new covenant* in the
WEB; their Greek was not checked in this pass. This page records the variant and does not settle
it.

**Hebrews quotes the verse itself**, with *touto*, *this*, where the Septuagint has *idou*,
*behold*, and *eneteilato*, *commanded*, where it has *dietheto*, *covenanted*. Hebrews 9:19 also adds
details Exodus 24 does not have — water, scarlet wool, hyssop, and the book itself sprinkled. The WEB
adds *"and the goats"*; the SBLGNT reads only *calves* and marks a variant. The extra details may come
from other rites in the Torah — cedar wood, scarlet, and hyssop appear together in the cleansing of
Leviticus 14:4 and the red heifer of Numbers 19:6 (WEB) — but this page notes the difference without
deciding its source.

**Zechariah 9:11** uses *the blood of your covenant* as the reason God frees prisoners from a
waterless pit. It is the only other Old Testament verse named here that joins *blood* and
*covenant*; its Hebrew was not checked in this pass.

## Significance

- **The words over the cup put Jesus where Moses stood, and his blood where the sacrifices' blood
  was.** In Exodus 24 the blood binds a people to God *"concerning all these words"*; at the table
  the blood is *"poured out for many"*, and in Matthew *"for the remission of sins"*. Exodus 24 says
  nothing about forgiveness; the Gospel adds it.
- **Both are meals.** Exodus 24:11 ends the ceremony with the leaders eating and drinking before God;
  the Gospel sentence is spoken at a meal. The wiki notes the shape and does not press it further.
- **Hebrews reads the Sinai rite as the precedent for its whole argument** — *"apart from shedding of
  blood there is no remission"* (9:22). See also [[Passing Between the Pieces]] and [[Blood]].
- **Jeremiah sets the new covenant against the one made *"in the day that I took them by the hand to
  bring them out of the land of Egypt"***, and names its failure: *"which covenant of mine they
  broke"*. Exodus 24 is the making of that first covenant; Exodus 32 is its breaking.
