# The Head and the Armor - Goliath at Elah and Saul at Gilboa

## Passages involved
- [[David Kills Goliath]] — **1 Samuel 17:49, 51, 54** — *The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to
  the earth* (49); *took his sword, drew it out of its sheath, killed him, and cut off his head with it. When the
  Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled* (51); *David took the head of the Philistine, and brought it
  to Jerusalem; but he put his armor in his tent* (54). Stored WEB.
- **1 Samuel 31:4, 9–10** (not yet ingested) — *Therefore Saul took his sword, and fell on it* (4); *They cut off his
  head, stripped off his armor, and sent into the land of the Philistines all around, to carry the news to the house of
  their idols, and to the people* (9); *They put his armor in the house of the Ashtaroth, and they fastened his body to
  the wall of Beth Shan* (10). Stored WEB.
- [[The Ark Among the Philistines]] — **1 Samuel 5:4** — *Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before Yahweh's
  ark; and the head of Dagon and both the palms of his hands were cut off on the threshold.* Stored WEB.
- [[The Sword of Yahweh and of Gideon]] — **Judges 7:25** — *Then they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon
  beyond the Jordan.* Stored WEB.

## Analysis

**Three acts, in the same words.** Each death in 1 Samuel 17 and 31 ends with the same sequence: a fall, the head cut
off, and the armor put in a house.

| | Goliath, 1 Samuel 17 | Saul, 1 Samuel 31 |
|---|---|---|
| the fall | *wayyippōl ʿal-pānāyw ʾarṣâ*, *he fell on his face to the earth* (49) | *wayyippōl ʿālêhā*, *and fell on it*, his own sword (4) |
| whose sword | Goliath's own, drawn by David from its sheath (51) | *haḥereḇ*, *the sword*, after the armor bearer refuses to draw his; the WEB's *his sword* makes it Saul's, and the Hebrew does not say (4) |
| the head | *wayyiḵrāṯ … ʾeṯ-rōʾšô*, *cut off his head* (51) | *wayyiḵrəṯû ʾeṯ-rōʾšô*, *They cut off his head* (9) |
| the armor | *wəʾeṯ-kēlāyw śām bəʾohŏlô*, *he put his armor in his tent* (54) | *wayyāśîmû ʾeṯ-kēlāyw bêṯ ʿaštārôṯ*, *They put his armor in the house of the Ashtaroth* (10) |
| the news | *the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled* (51) | sent *to carry the news to the house of their idols, and to the people* (9) |

All Hebrew WLC. In the files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, and Ruth), *kāraṯ* and *rōʾš* occur together in a
verse at 1 Samuel 5:4; 17:51; 31:9; and 2 Samuel 20:22, Sheba's head thrown over the wall to Joab (WLC; 2 Samuel
paraphrased, its WEB not stored). Judges 7:25 brings the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon with a different verb; the
heads are carried, not said to be cut off.

**Israel's champion and Israel's king.** Israel's champion beheads the Philistines' at Elah; at Gilboa the
Philistines behead Israel's king. In 17:54 the armor goes to a tent and the head to Jerusalem; in 31:9–10 the head and
the news go round the land of the Philistines, and the armor to the house of a goddess.

**Dagon first.** Before either man, Dagon had fallen *on his face to the ground* and lost his head (5:4, *nōp̄ēl
ləp̄ānāyw ʾarṣâ*, WLC). Goliath's fall in 17:49 uses the same three elements (*nāp̄al*, *pānîm*, *ʾarṣâ*). In 1
Chronicles 10:10, Saul's head is fastened *in the house of Dagon* (WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17, per
[[Dagon Fallen - Samson's Pillars and the Ark in Ashdod]]; not stored).

**Swords.** Goliath is killed with his own sword; Saul falls on *the sword*. Saul and Jonathan were the only men in
Israel with swords at 13:22. Goliath's turns up at Nob, *wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod*, and is given to David
(21:9, stored WEB; not yet ingested), so the champion's weapon goes into Yahweh's sanctuary while the king's armor goes
into the Ashtaroth's.

## Significance

The parallel is in the narrative and the vocabulary. The text does not say that Saul's death answers Goliath's or
draw the comparison itself; the wiki records the shared words and sequence. It will be revisited when 1 Samuel 21 and
31 are ingested. See [[Goliath]], [[Saul]], [[War]], and [[Draw Your Sword - The Deaths of Abimelech and Saul]].
