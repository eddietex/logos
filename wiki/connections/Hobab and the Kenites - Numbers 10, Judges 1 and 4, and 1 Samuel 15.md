# Hobab and the Kenites - Numbers 10, Judges 1 and 4, and 1 Samuel 15

- Numbers 24:21–22 — Balaam's word to the Kenite *(stored WEB; now ingested in [[Balaam's Last Oracles]])*
- [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]] — Judges 1:16; *the children of the Kenite, Moses' brother-in-law*, go up with Judah *(stored WEB, now ingested)*

## Analysis

### Judges 4:17–22 ingested

[[Jael and Sisera]] now holds the verse: *there was peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the house
of Heber the Kenite* (4:17). The household that *separated* from Hobab's people (4:11) is at peace with
Israel's oppressor, and Sisera dies in its tent at the hand of Heber's wife, [[Jael]]. So one house of
the family tied to Moses by marriage is on both sides at once: treaty with Jabin, and the blow that ends
his general. The text does not say which side Heber himself took. The Song blesses Jael *above women in
the tent* (5:24). The question left above, what a Kenite household is doing at the point of Israel's
rescue, now has this answer: it is where the rescue is completed.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:11, 17–22; 5:24.

### Judges 4:11 ingested

[[Deborah and Barak]] now holds the verse. The WLC reads *wəḥeber haqqênî nipərād miqqayin mibbənê
ḥōbāb ḥōtēn mōšeh*: Heber *separated* from *Qayin*, the collective name, *from the children of
Hobab*. It is the only verse that names Hobab as the Kenites' ancestor, and the identification of
1:16's unnamed *Kenite, Moses' ḥōtēn* with Hobab rests on it. The verse also shows the Kenites
divided: the family of 1:16 in the far south, Heber's household by Kedesh in the north. Within 4:1–16
nothing happens at the tent; 4:17 is the next pericope. See [[Heber the Kenite]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.

### Judges 1:16 ingested

[[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]] now holds the verse. The WLC reads *ûḇənê qênî ḥōtēn mōšeh*, without the article on *qênî*, and the
man remains unnamed. *The city of palm trees* they leave is Jericho by Deuteronomy 34:3. They settle
*in the Negev of Arad* (*bəneḡeḇ ʿărāḏ*) and *lived with the people* (*ʾet-hāʿām*), which the wiki reads
as Judah. The verse is still the only link between Numbers 10's offer and the Kenites in the land, and
it still does not name Hobab.

Source: stored WEB Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.

### Balaam’s last oracles

The Kenite oracle in Numbers 24:21–22 is now ingested. Strong dwelling and a rocky nest do not avert captivity by Asshur. The text addresses the group, not Hobab personally, and supplies no date. See [[Kenites]]; the oracle is not evidence that Hobab’s personal fate was captivity.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.


Numbers 10 ends the conversation without Hobab's answer. The later texts do not supply one directly, but they place
people connected to him in the land.

| Text | Who | Where | Stated relation to Moses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numbers 10:29 | Hobab, son of Reuel the Midianite | At Sinai | *ḥōtēn* (WEB *father-in-law*), attached to Hobab or Reuel |
| Judges 1:16 | *the children of the Kenite* | With Judah, south of Arad | *ḥōtēn* (WEB *brother-in-law*) |
| Judges 4:11 | Heber the Kenite, from *the children of Hobab* | Near Kedesh | *ḥōtēn* (WEB *brother-in-law*) |
| 1 Samuel 15:6 | The Kenites | Among the Amalekites | Not stated |

The WLC has the same word, *ḥōtēn mōšeh*, in all three texts that give a relation. WEB renders it differently, and that
is a translator's decision about Numbers and Judges, not a difference in the Hebrew.

**Midianite or Kenite.** Numbers calls Hobab's father *the Midianite*. Judges calls the family *the Kenite*. The texts do
not explain how the two relate, whether as a clan within a people or otherwise. Judges 4:11 and 1:16 together are the
only link, and they make the Kenites Hobab's people only if *Kenite, ḥōtēn mōšeh* in 1:16 is the same family as *Hobab,
ḥōtēn mōšeh* in 4:11, which seems likely.

**Whether the promise was kept.** Moses says *whatever good Yahweh does to us, we will do the same to you* (Numbers 10:32).
Judges 1:16 has the Kenite going up with Judah, and living *with the people*. 1 Samuel 15:6 has Saul saying *you showed
kindness to all the children of Israel, when they came up out of Egypt*, using *ḥesed* (WLC). The verse does not say what
that kindness was or name Hobab. Reading it as his guidance through the wilderness is possible and is the wiki's
suggestion, as is reading Judges 1:16 as Moses' offer honoured.

**The land they entered.** Genesis 15:19 lists the Kenites first among the peoples whose land Abraham's offspring will
receive. Balaam addresses them apart from Israel at Numbers 24:21–22. So the name stands both on the list of those
displaced and, in Judges, among those who settle with Judah. The texts do not comment on this.

**Jael.** Sisera flees to the tent of *Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite* (Judges 4:17), and Judges notes Heber's separation
from the Kenites at 4:11, just before. 4:17 adds that this house was at peace with Jabin. If the reading above
holds, a household of Hobab's people stands, a long time later, at the point of Israel's rescue. This will need revisiting when Judges is ingested.

**Blessed in the Song.** Judges 5:24 blesses *Jael … the wife of Heber the Kenite* *above women*.
The Kenite household that Judges 4:17 placed at peace with Jabin is, in the Song, on Israel's side,
blessed in the verse after an Israelite town is cursed (5:23). See [[The Song of Deborah]].

## Significance

These links are fragmentary. No text traces the Kenites from Sinai to Judah, and the Hobab of Numbers 10 is never said
to have gone. What the texts do show is a foreign family attached to Israel by marriage, who stay in Israel's story for
generations. See [[Hobab]], [[Jethro]], and [[The Nations]].
