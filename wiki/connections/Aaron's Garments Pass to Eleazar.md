# Aaron's Garments Pass to Eleazar

## Passages involved
- [[The Ordination of the Priests]] — Exodus 29:29–30.
- [[The Death of Aaron]] — Numbers 20:25–28.
- [[The Holiness of the Priests]] — Leviticus 21:10–12.
- [[The Burials of Joshua, Joseph, and Eleazar]] — Joshua 24:33.

## Analysis

Exodus 29:29–30 assigns Aaron’s holy garments to his sons after him and prescribes seven
days of wearing them for the son who ministers in his place. Numbers 20 identifies that
successor as Eleazar: Moses removes Aaron’s garments and puts them on his son before Aaron
dies. The enacted succession gives concrete form to the earlier legal provision.

The narrative does not repeat every ordination instruction. It reports no anointing or
seven-day observance on the mountain, but does not deny them or announce a revised law.
Likewise, Leviticus 21:10–12 forbids the high priest’s defilement for a dead parent, while
Numbers places Eleazar with Aaron when he dies. This is a question of narrative and legal
scope, not proof of contradiction: no contact with the corpse or burial action is recorded.
Neither an exemption nor disobedience should be invented.

## Significance

The garments outlast their first wearer. Aaron’s death under judgment ends his own service,
while the commanded transfer preserves the priesthood for Israel.

Eleazar's own death is reported in one sentence: *Eleazar the son of Aaron died. They buried him
in the hill of Phinehas his son* (Joshua 24:33). Nothing is said of garments passing to Phinehas or
of any successor being installed; the transfer that this page traces is narrated once only.
