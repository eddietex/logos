# The Secret Things - Proverbs 25.2 and Deuteronomy 29.29

## Passages involved

- [[The Glory of Kings]] — Proverbs 25:2–3: *"It is the glory of God to conceal a thing, but the glory
  of kings is to search out a matter. As the heavens for height, and the earth for depth, so the hearts
  of kings are unsearchable."*
- Deuteronomy 29:29 — *"The secret things belong to Yahweh our God; but the things that are revealed
  belong to us and to our children forever, that we may do all the words of this law."* Verified WEB,
  not yet ingested.
- [[The Glory of Kings]] — Proverbs 25:9, *"don't betray the confidence of another"*, and 25:23, *"a
  backbiting tongue"*.
- [[The King's Heart]] — Proverbs 21:1, *"The king's heart is in Yahweh's hand"*.

## Analysis

**One root for what is hidden.** Proverbs 25:2 gives God the glory of *concealing* — *hastēr*, from
*sāṯar*, to hide. Deuteronomy 29:29 (29:28 in Hebrew numbering) calls *"the secret things"*
*hannistārōṯ*, from the same root, and assigns them to Yahweh. Both passages agree that some things
are hidden by God and remain his.

**They divide the rest differently.** Deuteronomy sets *"the things that are revealed"* (*hanniḡlōṯ*,
from *gālāh*) against the hidden, and gives them to Israel *"that we may do all the words of this
law"* — the revealed is enough to obey. Proverbs sets *searching* against concealment and gives it
to kings: *"the glory of kings is to search out a matter."* Deuteronomy stops at the boundary of what
is disclosed; Proverbs makes inquiry into what is not yet disclosed an honourable office. The two
are not in contradiction — Proverbs' *matter* (*dāḇār*) may be a case at law or the order of things,
not God's own counsel — but they lean differently, and the wiki records both rather than merging
them: reverent limits in the law, royal investigation in wisdom.

**The same roots inside Proverbs 25.** The chapter uses *gālāh* at 25:9 — *"don't betray"*, literally
*don't reveal*, *the confidence of another* — and *sāṯar* again at 25:23, where *"a backbiting
tongue"* is *ləšôn sāṯer*, *a tongue of secrecy*. Among people, revealing a confidence and speaking in
secret are both faults; what is God's glory in 25:2 becomes, in human mouths, betrayal and slander.
That observation is the wiki's, drawn from the shared roots; Proverbs does not draw it.

**The unsearchable king.** 25:3 then says the king who searches cannot himself be searched (*ʾên
ḥēqer*). 21:1 had placed that same heart *"in Yahweh's hand"*. Unsearchable to subjects, it is not
hidden from God. See
[[The King's Heart in Yahweh's Hand - Proverbs 21.1, Exodus, and Ezra 6.22]].

## Significance

The pair marks the edges of [[Wisdom]] and of [[The Glory of God]]: there is a hiddenness that
belongs to God and is not trespassed, and a searching that belongs to rulers and to the wise. See
also [[Kingship and Empire]] and [[Language and Speech]].
