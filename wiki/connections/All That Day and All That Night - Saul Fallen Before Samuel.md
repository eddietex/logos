# All That Day and All That Night - Saul Fallen Before Samuel

## Passages involved

- [[Saul Among the Prophets at Naioth]] — **1 Samuel 19:24** — *He also stripped off his clothes, and he also
  prophesied before Samuel, and lay down naked all that day and all that night.* Stored WEB.
- **1 Samuel 28:20** — *Then Saul fell immediately his full length on the earth, and was terrified, because of
  Samuel's words. There was no strength in him; for he had eaten no bread all day long or all night long.* Stored WEB;
  not yet ingested.
- [[Locusts and Darkness]] — **Exodus 10:13** — *Yahweh brought an east wind on the land all that day, and all night*.
  Stored WEB.
- [[The Seventy Elders and the Quail]] — **Numbers 11:32** — *The people rose up all that day, and all of that night,
  and all the next day, and gathered the quails.* Stored WEB.

## Analysis

**Two falls before Samuel.** Saul falls in Samuel's presence twice in 1 Samuel, and the two verses share their verb and
their measure of time (WLC):

| | 1 Samuel 19:24 | 1 Samuel 28:20 |
|---|---|---|
| the fall | *wayyippōl ʿārōm*, *he fell naked* | *wayyippōl mǝlōʾ-qômāṯô ʾarṣâ*, *fell his full length on the earth* |
| Samuel | *lip̄nê šǝmûʾēl*, *before Samuel*, alive and silent | *middiḇrê šǝmûʾēl*, *because of Samuel's words*, brought up at Endor |
| the time | *kol-hayyôm hahûʾ wǝḵol-hallaylâ*, *all that day and all that night* | *kol-hayyôm wǝḵol-hallaylâ*, *all day long or all night long* |
| what came before | the Spirit of God came on him, and he prophesied | Samuel's sentence: *tomorrow you and your sons will be with me* (28:19) |

At Naioth Samuel says nothing, and Saul lies before him for a day and a night. At Endor Samuel speaks, and Saul, who
has fasted for a day and a night, falls at his words. The first scene is the last time Saul and the living Samuel are
together (Samuel's death is reported at 25:1); the second is their last meeting of all.

**The phrase elsewhere.** In the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, Ruth), *kol-hayyôm hahûʾ
wǝḵol-hallaylâ* with the demonstrative occurs only at Exodus 10:13, Numbers 11:32, and 1 Samuel 19:24. The Exodus
verse is the east wind before the locusts; the Numbers verse is the people gathering quail, in the chapter whose
first half is the Spirit resting on the seventy elders so that *they prophesied* (11:25). 28:20 has the phrase without
*hahûʾ*. The wiki records the Numbers 11 setting because it joins prophecy and the phrase in one chapter, and claims
no allusion.

## Significance

- **Saul's relation to Samuel ends lying down.** 15:35 says *Samuel came no more to see Saul until the day of his
  death*, *wǝlōʾ-yāsap̄ šǝmûʾēl lirʾôṯ ʾeṯ-šāʾûl* (WLC). In both falls it is Saul who comes to Samuel, once at Naioth
  and once through the medium. **Recorded, not resolved:** whether 19:24 conflicts with 15:35 depends on how *see* is
  read; the wiki notes both and does not decide. See [[Is Saul Also Among the Prophets - 1 Samuel 10.11-12 and 19.24]].
- **Without strength.** At 28:20 the narrator says *There was no strength in him*. At 19:24 the narrator gives no
  reason for the fall beyond the Spirit and the prophesying. The two scenes frame the king's last years as a king
  brought low before the prophet who anointed him.
- **The Endor half is provisional.** 1 Samuel 28 is not yet ingested. This page will be revised when it is, and the
  comparison may change.
