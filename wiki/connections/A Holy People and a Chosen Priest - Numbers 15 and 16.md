# A Holy People and a Chosen Priest - Numbers 15 and 16

## Passages involved
- [[The Tassels]]
- [[The Rebellion of Korah]]
- [[The Generations of Aaron and the Levites Given]]

## Analysis

Numbers 15:40 commands Israel to be holy to God. Immediately afterward, the rebels appeal to the
congregation’s holiness and Yahweh’s presence as grounds to challenge Moses and Aaron (16:3). Moses’
response distinguishes shared belonging from the divine choice of who may approach in priestly
service (16:5–10). Numbers 3:5–10 had already distinguished the Levites’ service from Aaron and his
sons’ priesthood.

The apparent tension is real but not a direct contradiction of the command to Israel: the narrative
rejects the inference that everyone’s holiness confers the same office. The later fate of the
censers further distinguishes an object’s holiness from approval of its offerer (16:37–40).

## Significance

Holiness is shared without being an interchangeable authorization for every sanctuary task.
