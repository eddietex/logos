# Three Gates to a Side - Numbers 2, Ezekiel 48, and Revelation 21

## Passages involved

- [[The Camp Around the Tent]] — Numbers 2:1–34; four camps of three around the Tent
- Ezekiel 48:30–34 — *the gates of the city shall be named after the tribes of Israel* *(not yet
  ingested; WEB verified at bible-api.com)*
- Revelation 21:12–13 — *On the east were three gates; and on the north three gates; and on the south
  three gates; and on the west three gates* *(not yet ingested; WEB verified at bible-api.com)*

## Analysis

**The same figure three times.** Numbers sets three tribes on each of four sides of the place where
Yahweh speaks. Ezekiel gives the last city three gates to a side, each named for a tribe. Revelation
keeps three gates to a side and the names, and leaves the gates unassigned.

**The sides do not match.**

| Side | Numbers 2 | Ezekiel 48 |
|---|---|---|
| East | Judah, Issachar, Zebulun | Joseph, Benjamin, Dan |
| South | Reuben, Simeon, Gad | Simeon, Issachar, Zebulun |
| West | Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin | Gad, Asher, Naphtali |
| North | Dan, Asher, Naphtali | Reuben, Judah, Levi |

Simeon alone keeps its side. Issachar and Zebulun stay together, moving from east to south, and Asher
and Naphtali stay together, moving from north to west. Ezekiel's list also includes Levi and folds Ephraim and Manasseh
back into Joseph, which is the opposite of the camp, where Levi is in the center and Joseph is split.
Neither text gives a reason for its order. Ezekiel 48 does not cite Numbers.

**What does carry over is the shape.** In all three the twelve names are set out on four sides. In
Numbers, the arrangement holds the people back from the center. In the two later
texts the names are on gates, which are for going in. That contrast is a reading and neither text
states it.

## Significance

The wilderness camp is the first time the twelve are laid out on four sides around God's dwelling,
and Revelation's city keeps three to a side. See [[The Dwelling of God]] and
[[The Twelve Sons and the Lists That Reorder Them]].
