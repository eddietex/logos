# Urim and Thummim - The Breastplate of Judgment and Later Inquiry

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 28:30** — *"You shall put in the breastplate of judgment the Urim and the Thummim;
  and they shall be on Aaron's heart, when he goes in before Yahweh"* — [[The Priestly Garments]].
- **Leviticus 8:8** — *"He placed the breastplate on him; and in the breastplate he put the Urim
  and the Thummim"* *(verified WEB, not yet ingested)*.
- **Numbers 27:21** — Joshua is to stand before Eleazar the priest, *"who shall inquire for him by
  the judgment of the Urim before Yahweh"* *(verified WEB, not yet ingested)*.
- **Deuteronomy 33:8** — *"Your Thummim and your Urim are with your godly one, whom you proved at
  Massah"*, in the blessing of Levi *(verified WEB, not yet ingested)*.
- **1 Samuel 28:6** — *"Yahweh didn't answer him by dreams, by Urim, or by prophets"*
  *(verified WEB, not yet ingested)*.
- **Ezra 2:63** — the most holy things withheld *"until a priest stood up to serve with Urim and
  with Thummim"* *(verified WEB, not yet ingested)*.
- [[The Priestly Garments Made]] — Exodus 39:1–31; Breastplate manufactured without a notice about Urim and Thummim.

## Analysis

**Exodus introduces the objects without describing them.** Every other item in Exodus 28 has a
material, a measurement, or a maker. The Urim and the Thummim have none. They are simply *put
in* the breastplate, as things already known or separately supplied (28:30). Leviticus 8:8
repeats the act of placing them and adds nothing about their form.

**Their function is named by the breastplate before any later text uses them.** The breastplate
is *"of judgment"* (28:15, 29, 30), and with the Urim and Thummim in it Aaron *"shall bear the
judgment of the children of Israel on his heart before Yahweh continually"* (28:30). Numbers
27:21 is the first verse in this list that shows the Urim used: a priest *inquires* by it
for the leader, and Israel goes out and comes in at its word.

**The later texts track it as a priestly, then a lost, resource.** Deuteronomy 33:8 gives the
Thummim and Urim to Levi as the tribe's possession. 1 Samuel 28:6 lists Urim with dreams and
prophets as a channel of divine answer that falls silent for Saul. Ezra 2:63 defers a question
of priestly status *"until a priest stood up to serve with Urim and with Thummim"* — so in that
text such a priest is not available.

**What the texts do not say.** None of the verses above describes the objects, says how many
there are, or explains how an answer was received. Proposals that they were lots, marked stones,
or a means of yes-or-no answers are interpretations; they may be reasonable, but they are not
stated in these passages, and this page does not adopt one. No etymology of the two words is
asserted here; the Hebrew was not checked in this pass.

### The priestly garments made — Exodus 39:1–31

Exodus 39:8–21 describes making, setting, and securing the breastplate without mentioning
the Urim and Thummim. Chapter 28:30 commands putting them in it, not making them alongside the
stones. Their absence from the manufacturing report therefore does not show disobedience or loss,
and does not identify them with the twelve stones. The later placement account already documented
above remains a separate stage; no new later-source quotation is introduced here.

## Significance

Exodus 28 joins two things that could have been kept separate: carrying Israel's names *"for a
memorial"* and carrying Israel's *judgment*. The same breastplate holds both, so the priest who
represents the tribes before God is also the channel by which a decision is sought for them.
When Numbers, Deuteronomy, Samuel, and Ezra are ingested, this page is where the later uses
should be read against the bare introduction of Exodus 28:30, not the other way round.
