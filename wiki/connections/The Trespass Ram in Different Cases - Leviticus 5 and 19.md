# The Trespass Ram in Different Cases - Leviticus 5 and 19

## Passages involved

- [[The Trespass Offering for Holy Things and Unknown Guilt]] — Leviticus 5:14–19.
- [[Holy Living in Field and Community]] — Leviticus 19:20–22.

## Analysis

Leviticus 5:14–19 and 19:20–22 prescribe a ram, priestly atonement, and forgiveness.
The cases differ. Chapter 5 treats unwitting trespass in holy things and liability
despite lack of knowledge. Chapter 19 treats intercourse with an unfreed slave woman
pledged to another man and does not label the offense unwitting in the stored WEB.

The holy-things case adds restitution and a fifth (5:16). Chapter 19 does not repeat
that schedule: it prescribes punishment without execution because the woman was not
free, then the man's ram and forgiveness. The form of punishment is unspecified in
the WEB. Nor does the text say that the offering purchases the woman's freedom.

This qualifies any claim that every trespass offering is restricted to an accident.
It does not establish that all deliberate offenses can be resolved by a ram. The
particular cases, consequences, and promises must remain distinct.

Sources: stored WEB Leviticus 5:14–19 and 19:20–22. No source-language claim is made.


## Significance

[[Atonement]] and [[Forgiveness]] do not erase the prescribed punishment or the
unequal legal status in [[Slavery and Servitude]]. A shared sacrificial form does
not make these offenses or their consequences identical.
