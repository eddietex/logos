# The Counsel of Balaam - Numbers 31.16 and Its Later Readings

## Passages involved

- [[Balaam's Last Oracles]] — Numbers 24:25; *Balaam rose up, and went and returned to his place*.
- [[Baal Peor and the Zeal of Phinehas]] — Numbers 25:1–18; Peor narrated, with no mention of Balaam.
- [[The War Against Midian]] — Numbers 31:8, 16; Balaam killed among Midian, and named as the source of
  *the counsel* at Peor.
- Deuteronomy 23:4 — Moab and Ammon excluded *because they hired against you Balaam the son of Beor …
  to curse you* (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet ingested).
- Joshua 13:22 — *The children of Israel also killed Balaam the son of Beor, the soothsayer, with the
  sword, among the rest of their slain* (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet ingested).
- 2 Peter 2:15 — false teachers *followed the way of Balaam the son of Beor, who loved the wages of
  wrongdoing* (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet ingested).
- Revelation 2:14 — *the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to throw a stumbling block before the
  children of Israel, to eat things sacrificed to idols, and to commit sexual immorality* (WEB via
  bible-api.com, not yet ingested).

## Analysis

**Numbers tells the Balaam story twice, and the second telling changes him.** Chapters 22–24 end with
Balaam having blessed Israel three times and gone home (24:25). Chapter 25 follows with Peor and does not
name him. Only at 31:16 does Moses say the Midianite women caused the trespass *bidbar bilʿām*, *by the word
of Balaam* (Miqra according to the Masorah, via Sefaria), and 31:8 has already put him among the dead of
Midian. The narrative never shows the counsel; it is reported once, in Moses' speech.

**What the later texts take from it.** Deuteronomy 23:4 remembers only the hiring to curse. Joshua 13:22
repeats his death and calls him *the soothsayer* (WEB). 2 Peter 2:15 makes him the man *who loved the
wages of wrongdoing*, a reading of the rewards Balak offered (Numbers 22:7, 17), though Numbers never says
Balaam took them. Revelation 2:14 is the fullest reading of 31:16: Balaam *taught Balak* to put a stumbling
block before Israel, idol food and sexual immorality — the two sins of Numbers 25:1–2. Revelation is the
first of these to join Balak's name to the Peor counsel; Numbers 31:16 does not mention Balak.

**What stays unresolved.** Numbers 24:25 sends Balaam home; 31:8 finds him with Midian. The text does not
say whether he returned, never left, or was there for another reason. Nor does it say what the counsel was.
The wiki keeps the two Numbers portraits side by side — the seer who says only what God gives him
(22:38; 23:12) and the adviser behind Peor — and does not read either into the other.

## Significance

The later tradition's Balaam is mostly built on one verse. Reading Numbers 22–24 without 31:16, as the
wiki's Balaam passage pages do, shows how much of his reputation comes from this retrospective line.
See [[Balaam]].
