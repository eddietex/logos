# The Portion of a Wicked Man - Job 20.29 and Job 27.13

## Passages involved

- **Job 20:29** — *"This is the portion of a wicked man from God, the heritage appointed to him by
  God."* — Zophar's closing line — [[Zophar's Second Speech]]
- **Job 27:13** — *"This is the portion of a wicked man with God, the heritage of oppressors, which
  they receive from the Almighty."* — in Job's mouth, as received — [[I Will Not Put Away My Integrity]]
- **Job 21:7–18** — *"Why do the wicked live, become old, yes, and grow mighty in power? … How often
  is it that they are as stubble before the wind, as chaff that the storm carries away?"* —
  [[Why Do the Wicked Live]]
- **Job 27:14–23** — the children for the sword, the silver for the innocent, the moth's house, the
  storm by night, the east wind, the clapping and hissing — [[I Will Not Put Away My Integrity]]
- **Job 18:21** — *"Surely such are the dwellings of the unrighteous. This is the place of him who
  doesn't know God."* — Bildad's closing formula of the same kind — [[Bildad's Second Speech]]
- **Job 5:27** — *"Look this, we have searched it, so it is"* — Eliphaz's closing formula —
  [[Eliphaz's First Speech]]

## Analysis

**Two lines, seven chapters apart, that differ in two words.** Zophar's second speech ends *zeh
ḥēleq-ʾādām rāšāʿ mēʾĕlōhîm wənaḥălat ʾimrô mēʾēl* — this is the portion of a wicked man *from God*,
and the heritage *of his word* (WEB *"appointed to him"*) *from God*. Job 27:13 reads *zeh
ḥēleq-ʾādām rāšāʿ ʿim-ʾēl wənaḥălat ʿārîṣîm miššadday yiqqāḥû* — *with God*, the heritage *of
oppressors*, which they receive *from the Almighty*. The opening clause is identical but for one
preposition; the second clause keeps *heritage* and *from [God]* and changes the inheritors. **It is
a quotation, and it is a quotation of a closing line** — the friends' habit is to end a portrait of
the wicked with a summary formula of exactly this shape (Eliphaz at 5:27, Bildad at 18:21, Zophar at
20:29).

**The difference is where the formula stands.** In Zophar's speech it closes the portrait; at 27:13 it
*opens* one. Ten verses follow that could have been written by any of the three friends — and that
contradict, sometimes word for word, what Job said in reply to Zophar:

- 21:8, the wicked man's children *established* around him; 27:14, his children multiplied *for the
  sword*.
- 21:18, *"how often"* are they *"as chaff that the storm carries away?"* (*gənābattû sûpâ*); 27:20,
  *"A storm steals him away in the night"* (*laylâ gənābattû sûpâ*).
- 21:13, they spend their days in prosperity and go down to Sheol *"in an instant"*, peacefully;
  27:19, *"He lies down rich, but he shall not do so again"*.

**What the parallel proves depends on what one thinks happened to the third cycle.** On the
reconstruction that gives 27:13–23 to Zophar, the repeated formula is the strongest single piece of
evidence: it is Zophar's signature, and a heading has fallen out before it. On the readings that keep
the section in Job's mouth, the quotation is deliberate — Job either turns Zophar's formula on his
accusers (*"Let my enemy be as the wicked"*, 27:7), or concedes Zophar's end-point while denying his
inference, or recites the doctrine back to the men who taught it to him. The passage page records all
four ([[I Will Not Put Away My Integrity]], Notes). **Every reading has to account for the quotation;
none of them makes it accidental.**

## Significance

- **It is the most precise internal echo in the dialogue**, and the textual case for a lost speech of
  Zophar rests largely on it.
- **It is where the wiki flags a contradiction in Job's own mouth**, as the text stands: 21:7–18
  against 27:13–23. The flag is carried on [[Retribution]], [[The Prosperity of the Wicked]] and
  [[Job (person)]] as well.
- **It is the dialogue's last statement of the doctrine of retribution as a portrait.** After 27:23
  no one in the book describes the wicked man's ruin again until Elihu, and when God speaks he
  describes wild animals instead. The formula *"this is the portion"* is the last word of the
  friends' genre, whoever says it — and the next word on the subject is God's verdict at 42:7.

**A parallel in Proverbs.** Job 27:16–17's silver divided by the innocent states the same transfer
as Proverbs 13:22, *"the wealth of the sinner is stored for the righteous"*. See
[[Stored for the Righteous - Proverbs 13.22 and Job 27.16-17]].
