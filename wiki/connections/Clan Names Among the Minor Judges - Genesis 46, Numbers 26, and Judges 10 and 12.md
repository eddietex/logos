# Clan Names Among the Minor Judges - Genesis 46, Numbers 26, and Judges 10 and 12

## Passages involved

- [[Jacob's Family Settles in Egypt]] — **Genesis 46:13–14** — *The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puvah,
  Iob, and Shimron. The sons of Zebulun: Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.* (stored WEB)
- [[The Second Census]] — **Numbers 26:23, 26** — *of Tola, the family of the Tolaites; of Puvah, the
  family of the Punites*; *of Elon, the family of the Elonites*. (stored WEB)
- [[Tola and Jair]] — **Judges 10:1** — *Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar*.
  (stored WEB)
- [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]] — **Judges 12:11** — *Elon the Zebulunite*. (stored WEB)

## Analysis

Two of the five minor judges carry the names of their own tribe's founding clans.

| Judge | Tribe | Name in Genesis 46 | Clan in Numbers 26 | WLC lemma |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tola, son of Puah | Issachar (*a man of Issachar*, 10:1) | Tola and Puvah, sons of Issachar (46:13) | the Tolaites, the Punites (26:23) | 8439; 6312 |
| Elon | Zebulun (*the Zebulunite*, 12:11) | Elon, son of Zebulun (46:14) | the Elonites (26:26) | 356 |

The lemma numbers are the morphhb tagging of the Westminster Leningrad Codex. In each case the judge's
name and the clan-founder's name share one lemma. The spellings differ in small ways: the father of
Tola is *pûʾâ* in Judges 10:1 and *puwwâ* in Genesis 46:13 and Numbers 26:23; *Elon* is *ʾêlôn* at
Judges 12:11, *ʾēlôn* (without the *y*) at 12:12, and *ʾēlôn* in Genesis 46:14 and Numbers 26:26.

The other three minor judges show no such pattern in the stored text. *Jair* the Gileadite shares a
name with *Jair the son of Manasseh*, who took the villages of Gilead (Numbers 32:41), but that is a
man, not a clan of the census, and the wiki treats it separately (see
[[The Villages of Jair - Numbers 32.41, Deuteronomy 3.14, Joshua 13.30, and Judges 10.4]]). *Ibzan*
and *Abdon* do not appear in the clan lists of Numbers 26.

## Significance

- **Three explanations, none given by the text.** The judge may be named after the clan by custom; the
  notice may remember a clan as if it were a man; or family names may simply recur. Interpreters have
  argued each. The text states the names and nothing more, and the wiki does not choose.
- **The judges belong to their tribes.** The cycle judges are often introduced by tribe (Othniel of
  Judah's line, Ehud the Benjamite, Gideon of Manasseh). In these two minor notices, the tribal tie is
  carried by the name itself as well as by the epithet.
- **Elon's grave.** Elon is buried in *Aijalon in the land of Zebulun*, a place spelled with the same
  consonants as his name at 12:11. A clan name and a place name may coincide in the text. See
  [[Aijalon (Zebulun)]].
