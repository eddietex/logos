# The Word Against the House of Eli - 1 Samuel 2.27-36 and 1 Kings 2.27

## Passages involved

- [[A Man of God Against the House of Eli]] — 1 Samuel 2:27–36 *(stored WEB)*
- 1 Samuel 3:11–14 — the word repeated to Samuel: *In that day I will perform against Eli all that I have
  spoken concerning his house, from the beginning even to the end* (3:12) *(stored WEB; not yet ingested)*
- 1 Samuel 4:11, 18 — *the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were slain*; Eli falls and dies *(stored
  WEB; not yet ingested)*
- 1 Samuel 22:18–20 — Doeg kills *eighty-five people who wore a linen ephod*; *One of the sons of
  Ahimelech, the son of Ahitub, named Abiathar, escaped* *(stored WEB; not yet ingested)*
- 1 Kings 2:26–27, 35 — *So Solomon thrust Abiathar out from being priest to Yahweh, that he might fulfill
  Yahweh's word, which he spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh*; *the king put Zadok the priest in
  the place of Abiathar* *(WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17; not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

The oracle names its sign and leaves its long sentence open. Its parts are fulfilled at different
distances:

| Oracle, 1 Samuel 2 | Fulfilment |
|---|---|
| *in one day they will both die* (34) | Hophni and Phinehas slain at the ark's capture (4:11) |
| *there will not be an old man in your house* (31–32) | Eli dies at ninety-eight the same day (4:15, 18); no later descendant is said to grow old (an argument from silence) |
| *the man of yours, whom I don't cut off from my altar* (33) | commonly read as Abiathar, the one who escapes Nob (22:20) |
| *I will raise me up a faithful priest* (35) | read as Samuel (3:20), or Zadok installed *in the place of Abiathar* (1 Kings 2:35) |
| *everyone who is left in your house will … bow down to him* (36) | no narrated fulfilment |

**The genealogy that joins them.** The chain from Eli to Abiathar is given in 1 Samuel itself: Ahijah is
*the son of Ahitub, Ichabod's brother, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli* (14:3), and Abiathar is *one
of the sons of Ahimelech, the son of Ahitub* (22:20) (stored WEB). That Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob is
of Eli's line rests on the shared father's name Ahitub; the text does not say in so many words that
Ahijah and Ahimelech are brothers, or the same man. 1 Kings 2:27 then makes the link explicit for
Abiathar.

**Shiloh named in Kings.** 1 Kings 2:27 locates the original word *concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh*,
though 1 Samuel 2 does not name the place of the oracle; it is implied by Eli's seat at Shiloh (1:9).

**Ithamar and Eleazar.** 1 Chronicles 24:3 sets *Zadok of the sons of Eleazar* beside *Ahimelech of the
sons of Ithamar* (WEB checked). If Eli's house is Ithamar's, Abiathar's removal moves the priesthood from
Ithamar's line to Eleazar's, the line of Phinehas's *covenant of an everlasting priesthood* (Numbers
25:13). Neither Samuel nor Kings draws that conclusion; it is assembled from Chronicles and reported as an
inference.

## Significance

- The word spans the whole of 1–2 Samuel and reaches into Kings; it is one of the longest-range prophecy
  and fulfilment pairs in the Former Prophets, and Kings marks its end explicitly with *that he might
  fulfill Yahweh's word*.
