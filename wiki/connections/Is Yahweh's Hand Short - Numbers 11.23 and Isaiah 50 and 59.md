# Is Yahweh's Hand Short - Numbers 11.23 and Isaiah 50 and 59

## Passages involved

- [[The Seventy Elders and the Quail]] — Numbers 11:23; *"Has Yahweh's hand grown short?"*
- **Isaiah 50:2** — *"Is my hand shortened at all, that it can't redeem?"* (WEB) *(not yet ingested)*
- **Isaiah 59:1** — *"Behold, Yahweh's hand is not shortened, that it can't save; nor his ear dull, that it can't hear."* (WEB) *(not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

**The idiom is the same.** Numbers has *hăyad yhwh tiqṣār*, Isaiah 50:2 *hăqāṣôr qāṣərâ yādî*, and Isaiah 59:1 *lōʾ-qāṣərâ yad-yhwh* (WLC). In
the Torah, the tags find *qāṣar* with this sense only here and at Numbers 21:4 (*wattiqṣar*, of the people's soul). In Numbers the question answers a man's arithmetic about food. In Isaiah it answers Israel's doubt about rescue.

**Isaiah 50:2 goes on to the fish.** The same verse says the rivers dry up and *their fish stink*, using *dāgâ* (WLC), the word of Numbers
11:5, and Moses asked about the fish of the sea at 11:22 with a related form. The wiki notes the sharing and does not claim
Isaiah had this chapter in mind.

**Ear as well.** Isaiah 59:1 adds *his ear*. *In the ears of Yahweh* frames Numbers 11 too (1, 18). The likeness is loose.

## Significance

Numbers asks the question and gives no argument, only *now you will see*. The answer is an event. Isaiah keeps the question for people
who have not yet seen the event. See [[Faith]] and [[Signs and Wonders]].
