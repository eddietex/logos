# The Hearing Ear and the Seeing Eye - Proverbs 20.12 and Exodus 4.11

## Passages involved

- [[Wine Is a Mocker]] — Proverbs 20:5, 8–12, 27.
- [[The Glory of Kings]] — Proverbs 25:12.
- [[The Burning Bush]] — Exodus 4:10–12.

## Analysis

Proverbs 20:12 credits Yahweh with making the hearing ear and seeing eye. The surrounding
sayings describe discernment: understanding draws out counsel (5), the king sees evil in
judgment (8), and conduct makes even a child known (11). The capacities used to recognize
character are received from God; possessing them does not make a person's judgment infallible.

Exodus 4:10–12 puts the claim in a commission. Moses objects to his ability to speak. Yahweh
answers by claiming the making of the mouth and of people who are mute, deaf, seeing, or blind,
then promises to be with Moses' mouth. Proverbs praises hearing and seeing; Exodus includes
their absence within its difficult statement of divine agency. Neither text makes disability
evidence of personal sin, and Proverbs does not itself explain disability.

Verse 27 adds a different image: the human spirit as Yahweh's lamp searching the inward person.
This concerns inward disclosure, not simply physical sight. The comparison is thematic; no
identity of Hebrew terms or direction of borrowing is asserted. Sources: stored WEB Proverbs
20:5, 8–12, 27 and Exodus 4:10–12.

### The obedient ear — Proverbs 25:12

*"As an earring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold, so is a wise reprover to an obedient ear."* The
WEB's *"obedient ear"* is, in Sefaria's *Miqra according to the Masorah* (read 2026-10-26), *ʾōzen
šōmaʿaṯ* — the *"hearing ear"* that 20:12 says Yahweh made. The organ Yahweh made is here the place
where reproof becomes an ornament; whether the ear *hears* in the sense of obeying is the WEB's
interpretive choice, which the Hebrew participle allows. See [[The Glory of Kings]].

Source: stored WEB Proverbs 20:12; 25:12.

## Significance

Creation underlies the act of understanding, but the two passages have different purposes: an
observation in wisdom and an answer to a reluctant commission. See [[Creation]], [[Seeing and Being Seen]],
[[Wisdom]], and [[Guilt and Conscience]].
