# Goliath and Elhanan - 1 Samuel 17 and 2 Samuel 21.19

## Passages involved
- [[Goliath Defies Israel]] — **1 Samuel 17:4, 7** — *A champion out of the camp of the Philistines named Goliath, of
  Gath …* ; *The staff of his spear was like a weaver's beam*. Stored WEB.
- [[David Kills Goliath]] — **1 Samuel 17:50–51** — David kills *the Philistine* and cuts off his head. Stored WEB.
- **2 Samuel 21:19** — *There was again war with the Philistines at Gob; and Elhanan the son of Jaare-Oregim the
  Bethlehemite killed Goliath the Gittite's brother, the staff of whose spear was like a weaver's beam.* WEB checked
  at bible-api.com, 2026-11-21; not stored.
- **1 Chronicles 20:5** — *Again there was war with the Philistines; and Elhanan the son of Jair killed Lahmi the
  brother of Goliath the Gittite, the staff of whose spear was like a weaver's beam.* WEB checked at bible-api.com,
  2026-11-21; not stored.

## Analysis

**The Hebrew of 2 Samuel 21:19.** The WLC reads *wayyaḵ ʾelḥānān ben-yaʿrê ʾōrəḡîm bêṯ hallaḥmî ʾēṯ golyaṯ haggittî
wəʿēṣ ḥănîṯô kimnôr ʾōrəḡîm*: *Elhanan son of Jaare-Oregim the Bethlehemite struck Goliath the Gittite, and the shaft
of his spear was like a weaver's beam*. There is no word for *brother*; *ʾēṯ* is the object marker. The WEB's
*Goliath the Gittite's brother* supplies the word from 1 Chronicles 20:5. The WEB does not mark the addition in the
text served by bible-api.com.

**What the two passages share.** A man named Goliath, *the Gittite* (2 Samuel) or *of Gath* (1 Samuel 17:4, *miggaṯ*),
and the same phrase for his spear, *kimnôr ʾōrəḡîm*, which in the Hebrew files checked (Genesis through 2 Samuel, and
Ruth) occurs only at 1 Samuel 17:7 and 2 Samuel 21:19 (WLC; morphhb lemma 4500). Both killers are Bethlehemites:
David, *son of Jesse the Bethlehemite* (16:18), and Elhanan, *bêṯ hallaḥmî* (WLC). The word *ʾōrəḡîm*, *weavers*,
appears twice in 2 Samuel 21:19, once in Elhanan's patronymic and once in the spear.

**What differs.** In 1 Samuel the killer is David, the place the valley of Elah, the time Saul's reign. In 2 Samuel
21:19 the killer is Elhanan, the place Gob, the time a later war in David's reign. Chronicles has Elhanan son of *Jair*
kill *Lahmi the brother of Goliath*.

**Explanations offered.** That the text of 2 Samuel 21:19 is damaged and Chronicles preserves a better reading; that
Chronicles harmonizes a difficulty; that there were two Goliaths; that Elhanan is another name of David; or that a
deed of Elhanan's was later told of David (all recalled, not checked). The wiki does not choose among them. It
records that the Hebrew of 2 Samuel 21:19 says Goliath, that the WEB's English says his brother, and that 1 Samuel
17 says David.

## Significance

The contradiction is recorded on [[Goliath]] and [[Goliath Defies Israel]], and will be revisited when 1 Samuel
17:41–54 and 2 Samuel 21 are ingested. It also bears on how the wiki uses the WEB: in 2 Samuel 21:19 the English adds a
word the Hebrew does not have, and a reader relying on the English alone would not see the difficulty.

### 1 Samuel 17:41–54 ingested

The account of the kill names Goliath only as *the Philistine*: in 17:41–54 the name *golyaṯ* does not occur (WLC;
the WEB likewise has *the Philistine* throughout). The chapter names him at 17:4 and 17:23 only. That observation is
recorded; whether it bears on the Elhanan problem is a matter of reading the wiki does not settle. The contradiction
is unchanged: 1 Samuel 17 has David kill the champion of 17:4; 2 Samuel 21:19 in the Hebrew has Elhanan kill *Goliath
the Gittite*. See [[David Kills Goliath]]. Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 17:4, 23, 41–54; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-21.
