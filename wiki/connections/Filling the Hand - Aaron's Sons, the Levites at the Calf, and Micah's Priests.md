# Filling the Hand - Aaron's Sons, the Levites at the Calf, and Micah's Priests

## Passages involved

- [[The Priestly Garments]] — **Exodus 28:41** — *You shall put them on Aaron your brother, and on his sons
  with him, and shall anoint them, and consecrate them, and sanctify them, that they may minister to me in
  the priest's office.* *Consecrate them* renders *ûmilleʾtā ʾet-yādām*, *fill their hand* (stored WEB;
  WLC). The idiom recurs at 29:9, 33, 35.
- [[The Golden Calf]] — **Exodus 32:29** — *Moses said, "Consecrate yourselves today to Yahweh, yes, every
  man against his son, and against his brother; that he may give you a blessing today."* *Milʾû yedkem*,
  *fill your hand* (stored WEB; WLC), said to the sons of Levi after they kill the calf's worshipers.
- [[Aaron and His Sons Consecrated]] — **Leviticus 8:33** — the seven days of ordination (stored WEB; WLC
  *yəmallēʾ ʾet-yedkem*). Also Leviticus 16:32; 21:10; Numbers 3:3.
- [[Micah's Idol and Levite]] — **Judges 17:5, 12** — Micah *consecrated one of his sons, who became his
  priest* (5) and *consecrated the Levite, and the young man became his priest* (12); both *filled the
  hand* (stored WEB; WLC *wayəmallēʾ ʾet-yad*).

## Analysis

**The idiom.** *Millēʾ yād*, *to fill the hand*, is the Hebrew for installing a priest; the WEB translates
it *consecrate*. In the WLC files of Genesis through Judges the verb *mālēʾ* with *yād* in this sense
occurs at Exodus 28:41; 29:9, 33, 35; 32:29; Leviticus 8:33; 16:32; 21:10; Numbers 3:3; and Judges 17:5,
12. Every case before Judges is done by Moses at Yahweh's command, or is the high priest's office handed
on, except Exodus 32:29, where the Levites fill their own hands by their zeal. What the hand was filled
with, portions of the offering (as Exodus 29:22–24 and Leviticus 8:25–28 place them in the priests' hands)
or something else, is debated; the wiki reports the question.

**Who fills whose hand.** In Exodus and Leviticus Moses fills Aaron's sons' hands. At the calf the Levites
fill their own. In Judges 17 a householder fills the hand of his own son, an Ephraimite, and then of a
hired Levite, with no altar, offering, or command named. Numbers 3:10 had said *Aaron and his sons … shall
keep their priesthood. The stranger who comes near shall be put to death* (stored WEB). The narrator of
Judges does not cite it.

**The calf and the image.** The chapter in which the Levites filled their hands is the chapter of the
molten calf (Exodus 32:4, 29). In Judges 17 a Levite's hand is filled to serve a molten image (17:3–4,
12). The pairing is a reading of the two chapters together; Judges does not refer to Exodus 32.

## Significance

The phrase measures how far Micah's household has moved from the ordination of Exodus 29 and Leviticus 8
while keeping its vocabulary. The priest is real in name and in form, and nothing in the law authorizes
him. See [[Priesthood]], [[Micah's Levite]], and
[[A Carved Image and a Molten Image - Deuteronomy 27.15 and Micah's House]].
