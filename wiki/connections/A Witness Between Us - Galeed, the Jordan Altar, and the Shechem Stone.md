# A Witness Between Us - Galeed, the Jordan Altar, and the Shechem Stone

## Passages involved

- [[Jacob's Flight from Laban]] — Genesis 31:44–52; the heap and pillar in Gilead (stored WEB).
- [[The Altar of Witness by the Jordan]] — Joshua 22:26–28, 34; the altar that is *not for burnt
  offering, nor for sacrifice* (stored WEB).
- Joshua 24:26–27 — the great stone under the oak at Shechem (stored WEB; not yet ingested).

## Analysis

Three times an object is set up and called a witness. In each case the object stands in the
place of a party who may not be present later.

| | Genesis 31:44–52 | Joshua 22:26–28, 34 | Joshua 24:26–27 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Object | a heap of stones and a pillar | an altar, *the pattern of Yahweh's altar* | *a great stone* |
| Between | Laban and Jacob | the eastern and western tribes, and their *generations after us* | Joshua and the people (*a witness against us … against you*) |
| Purpose | that neither *pass over this heap … for harm* (52) | that *your children may not tell our children … "You have no portion in Yahweh"* (27) | *lest you deny your God* (27) |
| Location | Gilead, east of the Jordan | by the Jordan, placed by the WEB *in the land of Canaan* | Shechem, *under the oak that was by the sanctuary of Yahweh* |
| Named | Galeed, Mizpah; Laban's *Jegar Sahadutha* (47–49) | *A Witness Between Us that Yahweh is God* (34) | not named |

**Galeed marks a line; the Jordan altar denies one.** Laban's heap is a boundary marker. It
divides two households, and each party swears not to cross it for harm. The altar of Joshua 22
is built against the opposite danger. The builders fear the Jordan will be read as a border
that puts their children outside Yahweh's people: *Yahweh has made the Jordan a border between
us and you* (25). Galeed's heap is in [[Gilead]]. The altar's builders are going home to Gilead.
Joshua 22 does not mention Galeed, and the wiki does not claim that the altar deliberately
alludes to it. The shared formula, *a witness between me and you* (Genesis 31:44, 48) and *a
witness between us and you* (Joshua 22:27, 28), appears in the stored WEB of both passages.

**What the witness testifies.** At Galeed the heap testifies to a sworn agreement, and Laban
adds that *God is witness between me and you* (Genesis 31:50). At the Jordan the altar testifies
to a shared worship it does not itself carry out. It is a copy that points to the altar before
the tabernacle (Joshua 22:28–29). At Shechem the stone *has heard all Yahweh's words* (Joshua
24:27). The text speaks of the stone as a hearer. The wiki takes this as personification in a
covenant setting and does not assert any belief about the stone.

**Witness between and witness against.** Galeed and the Jordan altar witness *between* two
parties of equal standing. The Shechem stone witnesses *against* the people, *against us* and
then *against you*. It stands with the other witnesses Deuteronomy calls *against* Israel: heaven
and earth, the song, and the book of the law. See
[[Heaven and Earth as Witnesses - Deuteronomy 4, 30, and 31]].

Sources: stored WEB Genesis 31:44–53; Joshua 22:10–34; 24:26–27. Joshua 24 is quoted from the
stored WEB before its ingest. No claim is made about the Hebrew wording of any of the three
naming or witness formulas.

## Significance

All three objects are set up for the time when the people who made the agreement are gone. They
speak to later generations, the children of Joshua 22:24–28 and the people who might *deny your
God* in Joshua 24:27. [[Remembrance]] here is not only something a people does. It is built in
stone, so that the question can be asked again later. See also [[Oaths and Vows]] and
[[Brotherhood]].
