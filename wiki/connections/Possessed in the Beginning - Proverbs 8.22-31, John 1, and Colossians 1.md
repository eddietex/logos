# Possessed in the Beginning - Proverbs 8.22-31, John 1, and Colossians 1

## Analysis

**What Proverbs 8 says.** Wisdom speaks of herself as having an origin with Yahweh before the
world: possessed (*qānānî*) at *rēʾšîṯ*, the beginning, set up *"from everlasting"*, born before the
depths and hills (8:22–25). She was present at each act of making — heavens, the circle on the
deep, clouds, springs, the sea's boundary, the earth's foundations — and beside God as *ʾāmôn*,
*craftsman* or *nursling*, delighting before him and in humanity (8:27–31). In 3:19 the same wisdom
is the means by which Yahweh founded the earth.

**What the New Testament texts say.** Several describe Christ with the same elements:

| Proverbs 8 (and 3:19) | New Testament |
|---|---|
| *rēʾšîṯ*, *"in the beginning of his work"* (8:22) | *"In the beginning was the Word"* (John 1:1); *"the Beginning of God's creation"* (Revelation 3:14) |
| before the earth, the depths, the hills (8:23–26) | *"He is before all things"* (Colossians 1:17) |
| born, brought forth (8:24–25) | *"the firstborn of all creation"* (Colossians 1:15) |
| *"I was there"*, *"by his side"* (8:27, 30) | *"the Word was with God"* (John 1:1–2) |
| *"By wisdom Yahweh founded the earth"* (3:19); *ʾāmôn* as *craftsman* (8:30) | *"All things were made through him"* (John 1:3); *"through whom also he made the worlds"* (Hebrews 1:2) |
| the sea held by his decree (8:29) | *"in him all things are held together"* (Colossians 1:17); *"upholding all things by the word of his power"* (Hebrews 1:3) |
| Wisdom personified | *"Christ is … the wisdom of God"* (1 Corinthians 1:24) |

**What the texts do not say.** None of these passages quotes Proverbs 8 or names Wisdom as its
source. John's prologue opens with Genesis 1:1's words and calls its subject *the Word*, not Wisdom
(see [[In the Beginning - Genesis 1 and John 1]]). Colossians and Hebrews use *firstborn*, *image*,
and *radiance*, which are not in Proverbs 8. 1 Corinthians 1:24 names Christ as God's wisdom, but
in an argument about the cross, not about creation. That the writers drew on Proverbs 8 — directly,
or through later Jewish writing that developed the figure of Wisdom — is a common scholarly
reading. It is a reading. The resemblance is clear; the dependence is not stated in the texts.

**Where the comparison strains.** Proverbs 8 says Wisdom was *possessed* and *born*: it gives her
an origin. John 1:1 says the Word *"was God"*. If Proverbs 8 is read as a description of the Son,
its origin language must be explained; and in the fourth century, as the passage page reports, a
Greek reading of 8:22 as *created* was used to argue that the Son was a creature — the wiki has not
read the primary sources for that history. Colossians 1:15's *firstborn* raises the same question.
The wiki does not resolve it. It notes that *qānâ* in Hebrew does not require *created* (see the
passage page on Genesis 14:19 and 4:1), and that Proverbs 8 is poetry personifying an attribute of
God, which the New Testament texts do not claim to be interpreting.

**Wisdom's delight and the incarnation.** Proverbs 8:31 ends the creation account with Wisdom's
delight *"with the sons of men"*, and 8:4 has her calling to them at the gates. John 1:14 says the
Word *"became flesh and lived among us"*. The resemblance is thematic only: both texts move from
presence at creation to presence among humans. The wiki notes it without claiming that John
intended it. The Gospel sayings in which Jesus speaks of wisdom — *"wisdom is justified by her
children"* (Matthew 11:19) — are a separate thread; see
[[Wisdom Sends Prophets - Proverbs 1.20-33, Luke 11.49, and Matthew 23.34-37]].

## Significance

- **It is the main Old Testament text behind the New Testament's language of a pre-existent agent of
  creation**, if the common reading is right. Proverbs 3:19 and 8:22–31 are the passages that give
  God's wisdom a voice, a place beside him, and a part in the making of the world.
- **It shows how far the wiki can go on the texts alone.** The table is what the texts say. Whether
  John or Paul was thinking of Proverbs 8, and whether Wisdom is a person, is interpretation; the
  wiki records both readings and decides neither.
- **Revisit when John 1, Colossians 1, Hebrews 1, and 1 Corinthians 1 are ingested.** Their passage
  pages should test the table against their full contexts.
