# Between Ramah and Bethel - Deborah and Samuel as Judges

## Passages involved

- [[Deborah and Barak]] — Judges 4:4–5; *Deborah, a prophetess … judged Israel at that time. She lived under
  Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim; and the children of Israel came
  up to her for judgment.*
- [[Yahweh Calls Samuel]] — 1 Samuel 3:20; *Samuel was established to be a prophet of Yahweh.*
- [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]] — 1 Samuel 7:15–17; *Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life. He
  went from year to year in a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah … His return was to Ramah, for his house
  was there; and he judged Israel there.*
- [[Another Generation Arose]] — Judges 2:16–18; the pattern of the judges, *all the days of the judge*.
- [[Samuel's Farewell Address]] — 1 Samuel 12:11; *Yahweh sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel* (stored WEB). See [[Bedan]].

## Analysis

**The same two towns.** Deborah judges *between Ramah and Bethel*, *bên hārāmâ ûḇên bêṯ-ʾēl* (Judges 4:5,
WLC). Samuel's circuit includes Bethel, and his seat is Ramah (1 Samuel 7:16–17). Judges often names where a
judge lived or was buried; in Judges and 1 Samuel 1–7 these are the two judges for whom it names where they
*judged*, and the places are the same pair. Whether Deborah's Ramah is
Samuel's is part of the open question on [[Ramah]]: 1 Samuel 1:1 places Samuel's town in *the hill country of
Ephraim*, which is also where Judges 4:5 places the palm.

**Prophet and judge.** They are the only two figures in Judges and 1 Samuel 1–7 who are called both prophet and
judge: [[Deborah (prophetess)]], *a prophetess*, *ʾiššâ nəḇîʾâ*, who *judged Israel*, *šōp̄ṭâ ʾeṯ-yiśrāʾēl* (Judges 4:4, WLC);
Samuel *a prophet of Yahweh* (1 Samuel 3:20) who *judged Israel all the days of his life* (7:15). For the
others, Judges uses *judged* of military deliverers or gives only a notice of years.

**Judging as hearing cases.** Israel *came up to her for judgment*, *lammišpāṭ* (Judges 4:5, WLC). Samuel
travels instead, *year by year in a circuit*, and *judged Israel in all those places* (7:16). In both, *judge*
reads as settling disputes at a fixed seat, not only as leading in war, though the text does not say what
cases they heard.

**A battle neither of them fights.** Deborah summons Barak and goes with him, but *Yahweh confused Sisera*
(Judges 4:15) and Barak's men do the pursuing (4:16). Samuel prays and offers while Yahweh *thundered … and
confused them*, and *the men of Israel* pursue (1 Samuel 7:10–11). The verb in both is *hāmam* (WLC); see
[[Yahweh Confused Them - Hamam in the Wars of Yahweh]]. The Song of Deborah gives the battle weather (Judges
5:4, 20–21); 1 Samuel 7:10 gives thunder. Both deliverances end with the enemy *subdued*, *kānaʿ* (Judges
4:23; 1 Samuel 7:13, WLC).

**The formula of the judges.** Judges 2:18 says Yahweh *saved them out of the hand of their enemies all the
days of the judge*, *kōl yəmê haššôp̄ēṭ* (WLC). 1 Samuel 7:13 says *Yahweh's hand was against the Philistines
all the days of Samuel*, *kōl yəmê šəmûʾēl* (WLC). Samuel is presented as a judge of the book of Judges, and his
own speech counts himself among them (1 Samuel 12:11). Barak is not in that list in the Masoretic text; the name
*Bedan* is unexplained there, and some ancient versions are commonly reported to read *Barak* (recalled, not
checked).

### What the page does not claim

It does not claim that 1 Samuel was written to recall Deborah, or that the two Ramahs are proved to be one. The
shared places, titles, and verbs are in the text; the intention is not stated.

## Significance

- **Samuel is the last judge as well as the first prophet of the monarchy.** The pairing places him in the line
  of Judges' deliverers, at its best: a judge who prays, under whom the land is delivered without a king. See
  [[Kingship and Empire]].
- **The hill country between Ramah and Bethel is where Israel goes for judgment.** It is the setting of both
  judgeships named by place, and of the assembly against Benjamin at Mizpah and Bethel (Judges 20:1, 26). See
  [[Bethel]] and [[Mizpeh]]. Deborah's seat is [[Deborah's Palm Tree]]; Samuel's is his house at [[Ramah]].
- **It sharpens the demand for a king.** The elders come to Samuel *to Ramah* to ask for *a king to judge us*
  (1 Samuel 8:4–5, stored WEB; not yet ingested), at the seat where he has judged them.
