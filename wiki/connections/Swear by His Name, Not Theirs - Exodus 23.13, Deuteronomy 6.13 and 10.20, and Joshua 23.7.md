# Swear by His Name, Not Theirs - Exodus 23.13, Deuteronomy 6.13 and 10.20, and Joshua 23.7

## Passages involved

- [[Justice, Sabbaths, and Feasts]] — Exodus 23:13: *"don't invoke the name of other gods or even
  let them be heard out of your mouth."*
- [[Do Not Forget When You Are Full]] — Deuteronomy 6:13–14: *"You shall fear Yahweh your God;
  and you shall serve him, and shall swear by his name. You shall not go after other gods"*.
- [[What Yahweh Requires of Israel]] — Deuteronomy 10:20: *"You shall fear Yahweh your God; you
  shall serve him; and you shall cling to him, and you shall swear by his name."*
- [[Joshua's Farewell to the Leaders]] — Joshua 23:7–8: *"neither make mention of the name of
  their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow down yourselves to them;
  but hold fast to Yahweh your God"*.

## Analysis

**Two halves of one rule.** Exodus 23:13 gives the negative half: other gods' names are not to be
invoked or even heard from Israel's mouth. Deuteronomy gives the positive half twice: Israel is
to swear by Yahweh's name, and in both verses swearing stands beside fearing and serving him
(6:13; 10:20). Joshua 23:7–8 brings both halves into one sentence. He first forbids the
nations' gods four acts: mentioning their names, causing anyone to swear by them, serving them,
and bowing to them. Then he sets against them a single positive: *hold fast to Yahweh*.

| Text | Forbidden | Commanded |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 23:13 | invoking other gods' names, letting them be heard | being careful to do all Yahweh said |
| Deuteronomy 6:13–14 | going after other gods | fear, serve, swear by his name |
| Deuteronomy 10:20 | — | fear, serve, cling, swear by his name |
| Joshua 23:7–8 | mention, swear by, serve, bow to their gods | hold fast to Yahweh |

**Speech as allegiance.** In all four texts, what a person says counts as worship. An oath calls on
a god as witness and guarantor, so the god sworn by is the god trusted. That is why the oath sits
beside fear and service in Deuteronomy. It is also why Joshua moves from naming and swearing to
serving and bowing without a break. The list moves from the mouth to the body.

**The setting sharpens it.** Exodus and Deuteronomy speak before the land is entered. Joshua speaks
with *these nations that remain among you* (23:7), so the other gods are now neighbours whose
names could easily come up in ordinary dealings, and who could be sworn by in a treaty or a
marriage (23:12). In Joshua 9 Israel had already sworn to the Gibeonites *by Yahweh, the God of
Israel* (9:19, stored WEB); there the oath was by the right name, even though the treaty itself
was made by deception. See [[The Gibeonite Deception]].

**Cling and hold fast.** Deuteronomy 10:20's *cling to him* and Joshua 23:8's *hold fast to Yahweh*
are different English verbs in the WEB. The wiki makes no claim about whether one Hebrew verb lies
behind both, because that was not checked. The parallel claimed here is one of thought and
setting, not of exact wording.

## Significance

- The thread treats [[Idolatry]] as something that can happen in speech before it happens in ritual.
  Mentioning a god's name and swearing by it come first in Joshua's list.
- It puts [[Oaths and Vows]] inside exclusive worship: an oath is a confession of which god is
  trusted.
- It shows Joshua passing on Mosaic instruction to a generation now living among the peoples that
  instruction warned about. See [[Law and Obedience]], [[The Names of God]], and [[The Nations]].
