# Sacrifice to Yahweh Alone - Exodus 22 and Leviticus 17

## Passages involved

- [[Laws of Holiness and Compassion]] — Exodus 22:20.
- [[The Peace Offering]] — Leviticus 3:1–17.
- [[The Place of Sacrifice and the Life in the Blood]] — Leviticus 17:1–9.

## Analysis

Exodus 22:20 excludes sacrifice to any god other than Yahweh. Leviticus 17 adds
a spatial and priestly requirement: the named livestock must be brought to the tent’s
door; field sacrifices become peace offerings, and goat-idol worship must cease (3–7).
The blood and fat procedure corresponds to Leviticus 3.

Exclusive allegiance and the sanctuary procedure support one another but are not
identical commands. Leviticus 17:8–9 expressly includes resident foreigners in its
offering rule. The passage gives no names to the goat idols and no narrated example
of the law’s enforcement. It should not be converted into a biography of a demon.

## Significance

The location of sacrifice serves the stated rejection of rival worship. See [[Idolatry]],
[[Priesthood]], and [[The Dwelling of God]]. Sources: stored WEB Exodus 22 and
Leviticus 3; 17. Comparisons are paraphrases.
