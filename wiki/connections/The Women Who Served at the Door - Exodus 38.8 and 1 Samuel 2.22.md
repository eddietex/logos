# The Women Who Served at the Door - Exodus 38.8 and 1 Samuel 2.22

## Passages involved

- [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]] — Exodus 38:8: *He made the basin of brass, and
  its base of brass, out of the mirrors of the ministering women who ministered at the door of the Tent
  of Meeting.*
- [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]] — 1 Samuel 2:22: *Now Eli was very old; and he heard all that his sons did
  to all Israel, and how that they slept with the women who served at the door of the Tent of Meeting.*

## Analysis

**The same participle at the same door.** Exodus 38:8 has *hassōḇəʾōṯ ʾăšer ṣāḇəʾû peṯaḥ ʾōhel môʿēḏ*;
1 Samuel 2:22 has *hannāšîm haṣṣōḇəʾôṯ peṯaḥ ʾōhel môʿēḏ* (WLC). Both use the feminine plural participle
of *ṣāḇāʾ* and both place the women *at the door of the Tent of Meeting*. In the WLC files of Genesis
through 2 Samuel, the verb *ṣāḇāʾ* has women as its subject only in these two verses. Elsewhere in those
files it is used of the Levites' *service* in the tent (Numbers 4:23; 8:24) and of men *going to war*
(Numbers 31:7, 42). (Lemma search, OpenScriptures morphhb XML.)

**What the service was is not said.** Neither verse describes the women's work, appointment, or status.
Proposals in the commentary tradition range from cleaning and assembly duties to prayer and fasting;
those are recalled, not checked, and the texts settle none of them. The shared verb with the Levites'
service suggests an organised role at the sanctuary rather than casual presence, but that is inference.

**Two groups, not one.** The wiki keeps separate pages, [[Ministering Women (of Moses' time)]] and
[[Ministering Women (of Eli's time)]], because nothing in 1 Samuel says the women at Shiloh continue an
institution from the wilderness. The repeated phrase allows that reading; it does not require it.

**Textual note.** The second half of 1 Samuel 2:22, with the women, is commonly reported to be absent
from the Septuagint and from the Qumran scroll 4QSamᵃ, and to have entered the Hebrew text under the
influence of Exodus 38:8. That report is recalled from standard commentary and was not checked against
either witness. If correct, the parallel would be a scribal echo rather than the narrator's.

## Significance

- In Exodus the women give their mirrors to make the basin where priests wash before serving. In 1 Samuel
  priests lie with the women who serve at the same door. The sanctuary's purity, which the basin
  symbolised, is violated by its priests, at its entrance.
- It adds the sexual charge of 2:22 to the sacrificial charge of 2:13–17 as an offence at the sanctuary
  itself. See [[Sexual Sin]], [[Priesthood]], and [[The Dwelling of God]].
