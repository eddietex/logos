# Jannes and Jambres - The Magicians Who Opposed Moses

## Passages involved

- **Exodus 7:11–12** — *"They also, the magicians of Egypt, did the same thing with their
  enchantments … but Aaron's rod swallowed up their rods"* — [[Aaron's Staff Before Pharaoh]]
- **Exodus 7:22; 8:7, 18–19; 9:11** — the magicians match the blood and the frogs, fail at the lice,
  and cannot stand because of the boils (not yet ingested)
- **2 Timothy 3:8–9** — *"Even as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so do these also oppose the truth
  … their folly will be evident to all men, as theirs also came to be"* (WEB, quoted from
  bible-api.com; not yet ingested)

## Analysis

**Exodus never names the magicians.** It gives them three titles in one verse — *wise men*,
*sorcerers*, *ḥarṭummîm* — and never a number or a name (7:11; WLC). **2 Timothy 3:8 names two of
them, Jannes and Jambres, as if the reader already knows who they are.** The letter does not explain
the names or cite a source. **It presupposes a tradition about the Exodus that the book of Exodus
does not contain.**

**The tradition is older than the letter and wider than the church.** Jewish sources are usually
said to know the pair: the Aramaic Targum Pseudo-Jonathan names them at Exodus 7:11, and the
Damascus Document (CD 5:17–19) speaks of *Yohanah and his brother* raised up by Belial against Moses
and Aaron. A lost work called *Jannes and Jambres* is also reported. **These attestations are cited
from memory and were not checked here**; the spelling of the names varies across them.

**What the letter takes from Exodus is the shape of the contest, not its detail.** In Exodus the
magicians do what Aaron does, for a while (7:11, 22; 8:7). **2 Timothy compares them to people who
*"holding a form of godliness, but having denied its power"* (3:5, WEB)** — imitators of a real
thing. And its *"they will proceed no further"* (3:9) matches the Exodus arc: the magicians match
three signs and then stop (8:18–19). See
[[The Magicians of Egypt - Hartummim in Genesis, Exodus, and Daniel]].

**The comparison is generous to the magicians in one respect.** Exodus never calls their success a
trick. **Neither does 2 Timothy**: their fault is that they *"opposed"*, not that they faked.

## Significance

**The New Testament here names figures Exodus leaves unnamed** — a small, clear instance of the New
Testament reading the Torah through Jewish tradition rather than through the bare text. The wiki
records it as an allusion to Exodus 7, with the names flagged as extra-biblical in origin.
