# Searched Out, Struck, and Burned - Deuteronomy 13 and Laish

## Passages involved
- Deuteronomy 13:12–16 — the law of the city that serves other gods (stored WEB; the wiki has no
  passage page that covers it).
- [[The Danite Migration]] — Judges 18:2, 7, 27–28; Laish searched out, struck, burned, and rebuilt.

## Analysis

Deuteronomy 13 ends with a law for one of Israel's own cities. If *certain wicked fellows* draw its
people away, *saying, "Let us go and serve other gods"* (13:13), then *you shall inquire, and make
search, and ask diligently* (13:14); if the report is true, *you shall surely strike the inhabitants of
that city with the edge of the sword, destroying it utterly* (13:15), gather its plunder, *and shall burn
with fire the city … It shall be a heap forever. It shall not be built again* (13:16) (stored WEB).

Judges 18 uses three of the law's actions, in the same order, against the wrong city.

| Deuteronomy 13:14–16 (English) | Judges 18 |
|---|---|
| *Wəḥāqartā*, *make search* (13:15 Hebrew) | *Ûləḥoqrāh*, *to search it* (18:2) |
| *Hakkēh takkeh … ləp̄î-ḥāreḇ*, *strike … with the edge of the sword* (13:16 Hebrew) | *Wayyakkû ʾôṯām ləp̄î-ḥāreḇ* (18:27) |
| *Wəśārap̄tā bāʾēš ʾet-hāʿîr*, *burn with fire the city* (13:17 Hebrew) | *Wəʾet-hāʿîr śārəp̄û bāʾēš* (18:27) |
| *Lōʾ tibbāneh ʿôḏ*, *it shall not be built again* (13:17 Hebrew) | *Wayyiḇnû ʾet-hāʿîr*, *they built the city* (18:28) |

(WLC. The Hebrew verse numbers of Deuteronomy 13 run one ahead of the English.)

**The verb *ḥāqar*.** In the WLC files of Genesis through Judges it occurs only at Deuteronomy 13:15
(Hebrew) and twice at Judges 18:2. *Striking with the edge of the sword* and *burning with fire* are
common in the conquest narratives; *search* is not. The rare verb is what ties the two passages; the
rest of the sequence would not, by itself.

**The inversion.** The law's city is guilty of other gods, and it is searched out so that the guilt is
certain. Laish is searched for its weakness: *quiet and secure*, with no one *possessing authority*
(18:7). Nothing is said of its gods. The ones carrying a *carved image and a molten image* are the
Danites, who took them from Micah on the way (18:14–20) and set them up in the rebuilt city (18:30).
The law forbade rebuilding the burned city; Dan builds it and makes it the home of the image.

**What the text does not say.** The narrator does not cite Deuteronomy 13, and Judges never calls the
Danites' act a breach of it. The connection is lexical and structural, and it is the wiki's reading;
another reader may take *search* in 18:2 as ordinary reconnaissance vocabulary and see no allusion.

## Significance

Judges strikes and burns other cities — Jerusalem at 1:8, the towns of Benjamin at 20:48 (stored WEB) —
but Laish is the only one *searched out* first, and it is a city with no recorded idolatry, taken by a
tribe carrying idols. Read beside
Deuteronomy 13, the Danites do to a foreign town what the law said Israel should do to an apostate
Israelite one, and then do what the law forbade. See
[[The Land Is Very Good - The Spies of Numbers 13-14 and Judges 18]] and
[[A Carved Image and a Molten Image - Deuteronomy 27.15 and Micah's House]].
