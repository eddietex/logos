## Analysis

Proverbs 13:12 is unusual among its neighbours because it makes no moral contrast. The sick heart
of the first line belongs to no fool, sluggard, or sinner; it belongs to anyone whose hope is
*deferred*. The saying describes what waiting does to a person, and then what fulfilment does — it
becomes *"a tree of life"*, the image Proverbs has already given to wisdom (3:18) and to righteous
fruit (11:30). Proverbs 13:19 repeats the sweetness of longing fulfilled before turning to the fool.

The book of Job is, among other things, an extended record of hope deferred. Job longs for a
request God will not grant (6:8), asks how he can wait (6:11), and finds his days spent *"without
hope"* (7:6). He uses the tree for the opposite of Proverbs' figure: a felled tree has hope of
sprouting again, while of God he says *"So you destroy the hope of man"* (14:7, 19), and later *"My hope he has
plucked up like a tree"* (19:10). Proverbs makes fulfilled longing a tree of life; Job finds his hope
uprooted like one. At 17:15 he asks where it has gone and who will see it.

The two texts do not contradict each other: Proverbs says what deferral does to the heart, and Job
is the case of a heart made sick by it. Proverbs 13:12 gives Job's condition a name and offers no
timetable for the second line. Job's own restoration (42:10–17) comes late and after the losses,
which the wiki does not treat as cancelling them.

No literary dependence is claimed; the shared word *hope* in the WEB is a link in English
translation, and no identity of Hebrew terms is asserted here. Sources: quotations copied from
stored WEB Proverbs 13:12, 19 and Job 6:8, 11; 7:6; 14:7, 19; 17:15; 19:10.

**Proverbs 23:18 promises the opposite of Job 19:10.** *"Your hope will not be cut off"* is said to
the son who fears Yahweh instead of envying sinners; Job's *"My hope he has plucked up like a tree"*
is said by a man who did fear him. Proverbs 24:14 repeats 23:18's promise. The wiki keeps both: the
proverb's hope rests on an *ʾaḥărît*, an end not yet seen (Hebrew read in Sefaria's *Miqra according
to the Masorah*, 2026-10-26), and Job speaks from inside the delay. See [[Do Not Envy Sinners]].

### Honey and hope — Proverbs 24:14

*"so you shall know wisdom to be to your soul; if you have found it, then there will be a reward,
your hope will not be cut off."* 23:18's promise is repeated, now attached to finding wisdom, which is
compared to honey (24:13). The tree of life of 13:12 and the honey of 24:13 are both images of desire
fulfilled.
