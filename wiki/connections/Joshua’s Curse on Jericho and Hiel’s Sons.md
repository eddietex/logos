# Joshua’s Curse on Jericho and Hiel’s Sons

## Passages involved
- [[The Fall of Jericho]] — Joshua 6:26.
- 1 Kings 16:34 — WEB verified from bible-api.com, 2026-11-10; not yet ingested.

## Analysis

Joshua pronounces loss of firstborn and youngest son at the foundation and gates of the rebuilt
city. First Kings 16:34 names Hiel the Bethelite as builder, Abiram as firstborn, and Segub as
his youngest son, explicitly attributing the outcome to Yahweh’s word spoken through Joshua.
This is the later narrator’s stated fulfillment, not merely a resemblance inferred by the wiki.

Both WEB passages describe loss of sons. Neither verse describes a foundation sacrifice or says
that Hiel intentionally killed his children. The mechanism must remain unspecified. The curse
concerns rebuilding, not an explicit rule that nobody can ever pass through or live in the area.

## Significance

The oath at the conquest remains consequential in the later kingdom narrative. The new connection
records that explicit fulfillment without opening or bulk-fetching the book of Kings.
