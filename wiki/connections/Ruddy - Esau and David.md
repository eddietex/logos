# Ruddy - Esau and David

## Passages involved
- [[Jacob and Esau and the Sold Birthright]] — **Genesis 25:25** — *The first came out red all over, like a hairy garment. They named
  him Esau.* Stored WEB.
- [[Samuel Anoints David]] — **1 Samuel 16:12** — *He sent, and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, with a handsome face
  and good appearance. Yahweh said, "Arise! Anoint him, for this is he."* Stored WEB.
- **1 Samuel 17:42** (not yet ingested) — *When the Philistine looked around, and saw David, he disdained him; for he
  was but a youth, and ruddy, and had a good looking face.* Stored WEB.

## Analysis

**One word, three times.** *ʾAḏmônî* (WLC). In the OpenScriptures morphhb files of Genesis through 2 Samuel, and Ruth,
the adjective occurs only in these three verses (lemma 132). The WEB renders it *red* at Genesis 25:25 and *ruddy* in
1 Samuel. Lexicons relate it to *ʾāḏōm*, *red* (recalled, not checked; morphhb gives the two separate lemma numbers, 132 and 122), the word that gives Esau his other name: *Please feed me with that same
red stew … Therefore his name was called Edom* (Genesis 25:30, stored WEB). Whether *ʾaḏmônî* describes hair or skin is
not settled by these verses (the debate is recalled, not checked). At Esau's birth it goes with *like a hairy garment*;
in both of David's verses it goes with good looks.

**Two firstborn reversals.** Esau is the elder twin, born first, and the oracle had said *the elder will serve the
younger* (Genesis 25:23). David is the youngest of Jesse's sons, chosen over the eldest. The one word is used of the
elder who is passed over and of the youngest who is chosen. The narrator does not draw the connection; it is
recorded here as a verbal link, not as a claim that 1 Samuel is alluding to Genesis.

**Seen and despised.** At 17:42 the word is in Goliath's eyes: *he looked … and saw David, and despised him*, because
he was *a youth, and ruddy, and had a good looking face*. Esau *despised his birthright* (Genesis 25:34, stored WEB).
The verb is *bāzâ* in both places (WLC). The man described as ruddy despises at Genesis 25:34; at 1 Samuel 17:42 the
ruddy man is despised.

## Significance

The link is lexical and rare. It joins two scenes about the order of brothers, an eldest passed over and a youngest
chosen, and in 1 Samuel it belongs to a chapter about what is seen (16:7). See [[Esau]], [[David]],
[[Birthright and the Firstborn]], [[Seeing and Being Seen]], and
[[The Elder Will Serve the Younger - Genesis 25.23 in Malachi and Romans]].
