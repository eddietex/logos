# From Perez to David - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, Matthew 1, and Luke 3

## Passages involved

- **Ruth 4:18–22** — [[The Generations of Perez]]; *Now this is the history of the generations of Perez*,
  ten names ending on David
- **1 Chronicles 2:9–15** — Hezron's three sons, then Ram to Jesse and *David the seventh* (not yet
  ingested; WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17)
- **Matthew 1:3–6** — from Judah to *King David*, with four mothers named (not yet ingested; WEB checked at
  bible-api.com, 2026-11-17)
- **Luke 3:31–33** — the same names in reverse, from David back to Judah (not yet ingested; WEB checked at
  bible-api.com, 2026-11-17)

## Analysis

**Four books give one line.** From Perez to David the names agree in order in all four: Perez, Hezron,
Ram, Amminadab, Nahshon, Salmon, Boaz, Obed, Jesse, David. Each writer frames it differently.

| | starts at | ends at | the WEB's spelling | what it adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ruth 4:18–22 | Perez, under a *tôlədôṯ* heading | David | Salmon (twice) | nothing; ten names, one verb |
| 1 Chronicles 2:9–15 | Hezron's three sons | *David the seventh* | Salma | *Nahshon, prince of the children of Judah*; Jesse's seven sons by order of birth |
| Matthew 1:3–6 | Judah, *by Tamar* | *King David*, then Solomon | Salmon | Tamar, Rahab, Ruth, *her who had been Uriah's wife* |
| Luke 3:31–33 | David, going upward | Judah, and on to Adam | Aram for Ram; Salmon | nothing in this stretch |

**The spelling of Salmon.** In the Hebrew of Ruth the name is *śalmâ* at the end of 4:20 and *śalmôn* at
the start of 4:21; Chronicles has *śalmāʾ* (WLC, lemmas 8009, 8012, 8007). The WEB normalizes Ruth's to
*Salmon* and keeps Chronicles' *Salma*. The Greek of Matthew and Luke was not consulted; the WEB has
*Salmon* in both.

**Ram and Aram.** Ruth, Chronicles, and Matthew have *Ram*; the WEB of Luke 3:33 has *Aram*. Manuscripts of
Luke are reported to differ at this point (recalled, not checked against a Greek edition), so the wiki
records only the WEB reading.

**What each adds.** Ruth adds nothing to the names; its framing is the heading, which makes the list a
Genesis-style *history of the generations*. Chronicles adds a title and a count: Nahshon is *prince of the
children of Judah* (2:10), as Numbers 2:3 calls him, and David is Jesse's *seventh* son (2:15). Matthew
adds the mothers, including *Salmon became the father of Boaz by Rahab* and *Boaz became the father of Obed
by Ruth* (1:5), and the title *King* (1:6). Luke adds nothing in this stretch, but carries the line past
Judah to Adam; see [[From Adam to Jesus - The Genealogy of Genesis 5 in Chronicles and Luke]].

**Rahab appears only in Matthew.** Joshua narrates Rahab at Jericho and her household living in Israel
*to this day* (Joshua 6:25), and never names a husband; neither spelling of Salmon's name occurs in the
WLC of Joshua. Matthew does not say his Rahab is the woman of Jericho, though she is usually taken to be.
If she is, the timing fits the list, since Salmon's father Nahshon was the prince of Judah in the
wilderness. See [[Rahab]] and [[Salmon]].

**The gaps.** Genesis 46:12 takes Perez and Hezron into Egypt; Numbers 1:7 has Nahshon at Sinai; Exodus
12:40 gives four hundred thirty years in Egypt. Only Hezron, Ram, and Amminadab stand between them in any
of the four lists. That the lists agree on the names does not settle whether the line is complete or
selective; none of the four says.

## Significance

- Ruth is the earliest book in canonical order to give the line. The others repeat it, and none of them
  differs from it on the names, so the book of a Moabite widow supplies the backbone of the royal
  genealogy. See [[Kingship and Empire]].
- The writers differ in what they put beside the names. Ruth's list, which ends a book about a woman, names
  no woman; Matthew's names Ruth. Chronicles names Nahshon's office; Ruth does not. See
  [[Tamar in the Genealogies - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, and Matthew 1]].
