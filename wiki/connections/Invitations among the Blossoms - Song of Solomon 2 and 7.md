# Invitations among the Blossoms - Song of Solomon 2 and 7

## Passages involved

- [[Rise Up, My Love]] — Song of Solomon 2:8–17.
- [[Come into the Fields, My Beloved]] — Song of Solomon 7:10–13.

## Analysis

In 2:10–13 he invites her to come away as winter passes and vines blossom. In
7:11–12 she invites him to go out, lodge in villages, and inspect vines and flowering
pomegranates. His invitation to see and hear her (2:14) is answered at the level of
the book's repeated imagery by her promise to give him her love (7:12).
The changed speaker gives both lovers initiative. Neither passage supplies a complete
itinerary or proves that the later invitation narrates the fulfillment of the earlier one.

## Significance

The landscape is shared language for desire. Repetition joins poems without forcing
them into a single continuous journey; see [[Love]].
