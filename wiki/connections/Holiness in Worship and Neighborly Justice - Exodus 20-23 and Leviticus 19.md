# Holiness in Worship and Neighborly Justice - Exodus 20-23 and Leviticus 19

## Passages involved

- [[Holy Living in Field and Community]] — Leviticus 19:19–37.

- [[The Ten Commandments]] — Exodus 20:1–17.
- [[Justice, Sabbaths, and Feasts]] — Exodus 23:1–19.
- [[Be Holy and Love Your Neighbor]] — Leviticus 19:1–18.

## Analysis

### Leviticus 19:19–37 now ingested

Leviticus 19:30 repeats Sabbath keeping beside sanctuary reverence. Verses 33–34 develop the
protection of the alien in Exodus 23:9 into an explicit command to love the resident foreigner as
oneself. Both appeal to Israel’s experience in Egypt; the latter also requires treatment as
native-born.

Source for this addition: stored WEB Leviticus 19; Exodus 23:9 and Proverbs 11:1
where compared. Existing wider reception material is retained.


Leviticus 19:3–4, 11–12 revisits parents, Sabbath, idols, theft, and false speech,
all matters treated in Exodus 20. Its order and elaborations differ: mother precedes
father in 19:3, and the speech continues through harvests, wages, disability, courts,
and the disposition of the heart. This is a thematic comparison, not a claim that
every commandment is reproduced in sequence.

Exodus 23:3, 6 excludes both favoritism toward a poor litigant and denial of justice
to the poor. Leviticus 19:15 likewise rejects partiality toward poverty or greatness.
Both passages pair impartial courts with material protection: the seventh-year produce
feeds the poor in Exodus 23:10–11; harvest edges and gleanings serve poor people and
foreigners in Leviticus 19:9–10. The provisions differ in timing and mechanism.

Exodus 23:4–5 requires help for an enemy’s straying or burdened animal. Leviticus
19:17–18 forbids hatred, vengeance, and grudges and commands love. These are related
restraints on hostility, not identical wording or interchangeable laws.

## Significance

Holiness integrates worship, resources, judgment, and conflict. Help for poor people does not
authorize a biased court, and truthful rebuke does not authorize hatred. Sources: stored WEB
Exodus 20, 23 and Leviticus 19.
