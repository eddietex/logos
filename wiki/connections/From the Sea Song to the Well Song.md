# From the Sea Song to the Well Song

## Passages involved
- [[The Song of the Sea]]
- [[The Journey to Moab]]

References: Exodus 15:1–21; Numbers 21:16–18.

## Analysis

The stored WEB introduces both songs by reporting that Israel sang, with Moses also named in Exodus
15:1. At the sea the song addresses and praises Yahweh for victory and deliverance. At Beer the
people address the well and celebrate the leaders who dug it, while the prose immediately before the
song gives Yahweh’s promise of water. The comparison concerns narrative form and communal song; no
claim of exact Hebrew verbal identity is made.

Source: stored WEB in `raw/bible/Numbers.md` and, for the sea song,
`raw/bible/Exodus.md`.

## Significance

Israel’s remembered journey includes both deliverance from enemies and the provision needed to keep
traveling. The well song belongs within divine provision without being a direct address to God in
its recorded words.
