# Wholly Given - Netunim in Numbers and the Temple Servants

## Passages involved

- [[The Generations of Aaron and the Levites Given]] — Numbers 3:9; *wholly given to him*
- Numbers 8:16, 19 — *wholly given to me*, and *as a gift to Aaron* *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*
- Numbers 18:6 — *a gift to you, dedicated to Yahweh* *(not yet ingested; stored WEB)*
- 1 Chronicles 6:48 (WLC 6:33) — *Their brothers the Levites were appointed* *(not yet ingested; WEB
  verified at bible-api.com)*
- 1 Chronicles 9:2; Ezra 2:43; 8:20 — the *temple servants* *(not yet ingested; WEB verified at
  bible-api.com)*

## Analysis

**The doubled word.** Numbers 3:9 reads *nətûnim nətûnim hēmmâ lô*, and 8:16 reads *nətunîm nətunîm
hēmmâ lî* (WLC; spelled differently, same passive participle of *nātan*). WEB's *wholly given*
translates the repetition. In the WLC the plural participle of this verb for a person given to
sanctuary service is used of the Levites at 3:9, 8:16, 8:19, and 18:6, and it recurs at 1 Chronicles
6:48 (WLC 6:33), which WEB renders *appointed*.

**Given to whom?** 3:9 says *to him*, and the context makes that Aaron. 8:16 says *to me*, and the
speaker is Yahweh. 8:19 then says Yahweh gives them to Aaron. 18:6 joins the two: *a gift to you,
dedicated to Yahweh*. Read together, they describe a chain, in which Israel gives them up, Yahweh takes
them, and Yahweh hands them to the priests. Israel's part is the *from* in 3:9 and 8:16. Ancient
witnesses are reported to read *to me* at 3:9; that report is unchecked here and the wiki follows the
WLC and WEB.

**A later class with a similar name.** Ezra, Nehemiah, and 1 Chronicles 9:2 have the *nətînîm*
(Strong's H5411 in the WLC tags), WEB's *temple servants*. Ezra 8:20 says *David and the princes had
given* them *for the service of the Levites*. So the word that made the Levites a gift to the priests
has a relative naming a group given to the Levites. The form is different, and the two groups are
different. The likeness is the wiki's observation, and the etymological link is the usual one, not
checked against a named lexicon.

## Significance

- A tribe's standing is stated in the passive: the Levites hold their place by being given. See
  [[Levi]] and [[Priesthood]].
