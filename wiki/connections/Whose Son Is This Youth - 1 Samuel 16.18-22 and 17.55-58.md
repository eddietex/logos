# Whose Son Is This Youth — 1 Samuel 16:18–22 and 17:55–58

## Passages involved

- **1 Samuel 16:18–22** — [[David Plays the Harp for Saul]]; Saul's servant names *a son of Jesse the
  Bethlehemite*, Saul sends to Jesse for *David your son*, *loved him greatly*, makes him his armor bearer, and asks
  Jesse's leave to keep him.
- **1 Samuel 17:12–15** — [[David Comes to the Camp]]; David introduced again, *the son of that Ephrathite of
  Bethlehem Judah*, going *back and forth from Saul to feed his father's sheep*.
- **1 Samuel 17:31–39** — [[David Comes to the Camp]]; David speaks with Saul face to face, and Saul dresses him in
  his own clothing.
- **1 Samuel 17:55–58** — [[Jonathan Loves David]]; Saul asks Abner *whose son is this youth?*, Abner does not know,
  and David answers *I am the son of your servant Jesse the Bethlehemite*.
- **1 Samuel 17:25** — the reward that touches the father: *make his father's house free in Israel*.

## Analysis

The two scenes tell how David came to stand before Saul, and they do not fit together as written.

| | 16:18–22 | 17:55–58 |
|---|---|---|
| **who names David's father** | Saul's servant: *a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite* (16:18) | David: *the son of your servant Jesse the Bethlehemite* (17:58) |
| **what Saul knows** | his name and his father; he sends to Jesse twice (16:19, 22) | not whose son he is; he asks three times (17:55, 56, 58) |
| **Saul's relation to him** | *He loved him greatly; and he became his armor bearer* (16:21) | *Saul took him that day, and would let him go no more home* (18:2) |
| **the go-between** | the servant who *has seen* him (16:18) | Abner, who swears *I can't tell* (17:55) |

All quotations stored WEB. The phrase *Jesse the Bethlehemite*, *yišay bêṯ hallaḥmî* (WLC), is the same in both
scenes and in Yahweh's word to Samuel at 16:1.

Chapter 17 has its own bridge: 17:15 says David *went back and forth from Saul* to the sheep, which reads as an
attempt to place the shepherd of 17 in the court of 16; and 17:31–39 has David speak with Saul and wear his armor
before the fight. Even within chapter 17, then, Saul has met David before he asks whose son he is.

**What the text gives.** Only the question, and the reward of 17:25, which was promised to the killer's *father's
house*. Saul's word is *whose son*, *ben-mî*, all three times (WLC), never *who is he*. That fits a reading in which
the question is about the family the king has now bound himself to free, rather than about the man. It does not fit
Abner's ignorance as well, and the text does not say it.

**Readings offered** (recalled, not checked):

- Saul asks about the father, because of 17:25.
- Saul, troubled by the evil spirit, does not recognize his musician; or the commander of the army did not know the
  court's harpist.
- Two accounts of David's arrival at court, from different tellings, have been placed side by side. The Septuagint
  in Codex Vaticanus is reported to lack 17:12–31 and 17:55–18:5, which would leave a chapter 17 without the second
  introduction and without the question. No Septuagint text was consulted.

The wiki records both accounts and does not choose. See also
[[Goliath and Elhanan - 1 Samuel 17 and 2 Samuel 21.19]], the other unresolved tension around chapter 17.

## Significance

The doublet sits where the book moves David from Jesse's house into Saul's. Both scenes end the same way: Saul keeps
him. In 16:22 he asks Jesse for it; in 18:2 he does not ask, and David does *not go home to his father's house*. The
second scene also gives David his first words to the king about who he is, and they are his father's name and his
town. See [[David]], [[Saul]], and [[Jesse]].
