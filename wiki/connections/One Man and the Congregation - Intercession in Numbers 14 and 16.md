# One Man and the Congregation - Intercession in Numbers 14 and 16

## Passages involved
- [[The People Refuse the Land]]
- [[The Rebellion of Korah]]

## Analysis

In Numbers 14:11–20 Moses answers the threat against Israel by appealing to Yahweh’s reputation and
mercy. In Numbers 16:20–24 Moses and Aaron plead against anger at the entire congregation because of
one man’s sin. Both threats are followed by a plea and a differentiated judgment rather than the
destruction first announced.

The limits matter. Numbers 14 still sentences the refusing generation; Numbers 16 still includes
households in the swallowing scene, after naming wives, sons, and little ones (27–33). The plea
against collective destruction cannot be flattened into a claim that only individually demonstrated
offenders die. Nor is the singular wording of the plea a claim that only one person participated in
the rebellion.

## Significance

Intercession preserves a congregation without removing judgment or resolving every question of
shared consequences.
