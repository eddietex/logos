# The Sons of Levi - Genesis 46, Exodus 6, and Numbers 3 and 26

## Passages involved

- **Genesis 46:11** — *"The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari."* —
  [[Jacob's Family Settles in Egypt]]
- **Exodus 6:16–25** — Levi's sons *"according to their generations"*, their sons, and Kohath's line
  through Amram to Aaron, Moses, and Phinehas — [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]]
- **Numbers 3:17–20** — the same three sons and eight grandsons, listed *"according to their
  families"* for the tabernacle charge (by reference; Hebrew checked, not yet ingested)
- **Numbers 26:57–61** — the second census's Levite clans, with Jochebed *"born to Levi in Egypt"*
  and the death of Nadab and Abihu (by reference; Hebrew checked, not yet ingested)
- **1 Chronicles 6** — the Levite and high-priestly lines restated (by reference; not checked)

## Analysis

**The list grows each time it is given, and what it grows for changes.**

| | Genesis 46:11 | Exodus 6:16–25 | Numbers 3:17–20 | Numbers 26:57–61 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **what the list is for** | who went down to Egypt | who Moses and Aaron are | who carries what in the tabernacle | who was counted in the plains of Moab |
| **Levi's sons** | Gershon, Kohath, Merari | the same | the same | the same, as clans |
| **grandsons** | — | Libni, Shimei; Amram, Izhar, Hebron, Uzziel; Mahli, Mushi | the same eight, same order | five clans: Libnites, Hebronites, Mahlites, Mushites, Korahites |
| **lifespans** | — | Levi 137, Kohath 133, Amram 137 | — | — |
| **Moses' family** | — | Amram + Jochebed → Aaron, Moses | — | Amram + Jochebed → Aaron, Moses, *and Miriam* |
| **Aaron's sons** | — | Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, Ithamar | (given at 3:2–4) | the same four, and the death of the first two |
| **Korah** | — | son of Izhar; his sons *"the families of the Korahites"* | — | the *Korahites* a clan; *"the sons of Korah did not die"* (26:11) |

(Hebrew of Genesis 46:11, Exodus 6:16–25, Numbers 3:17–20, and Numbers 26:11, 57–61 checked in the
Westminster Leningrad Codex. The Numbers columns are the wiki's own summaries of the Hebrew, written
before Numbers reached `raw/`; every quoted phrase from Numbers on this page is the wiki's own
rendering of the checked Hebrew, not the WEB. Numbers 3:2–4 is now checked at
[[The Generations of Aaron and the Levites Given]]; 3:21–37 is cited from memory.)

**Genesis records a family.** The three sons are one clause, with no titles and no future, on the
road into Egypt.

**Exodus records a pedigree.** It takes Genesis's clause, adds Genesis's own structuring word —
*lətōlədōtām*, *"according to their generations"*, the first *tôlədôt* since Genesis 37:2 — and
gives it a direction: down one line, Kohath's, to two men, and past one of them to his grandson. The
other branches get one generation each and stop.

**Numbers records an institution.** The same eight grandsons, in the same order, are now the heads
of the clans that carry the tabernacle: the Gershonites its fabric, the Kohathites its holy things,
the Merarites its frame (Numbers 3:21–37, by reference). **The names Exodus lists to place Moses are
the names Numbers lists to organise a camp.**

**And the second census shows what the list has lost and kept.** Numbers 26:58 names five clans, not
eight: Libnites, Hebronites, Mahlites, Mushites, and Korahites. Shimei, Izhar, and Uzziel are not
named as clans; Amram is named in the next clause as Kohath's son; and Korah, a grandson, is.
It adds Miriam to Amram's children (26:59), which Exodus 6 did not, and states in a single verse what
happened to the first two of Aaron's four sons: *"Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire
before Yahweh"* (26:61, the wiki's rendering of *bəhaqrîbām ʾēš zārâ lipnê YHWH*).

### Agreement, and what it implies

**Across four lists in three books, the names agree.** The three sons and eight grandsons do not vary
in Genesis, Exodus, or Numbers 3 — unlike Simeon's sons, who are given differently at Numbers 26:12–13
(see [[Shaul (son of Simeon)]]). The wiki notes the stability without drawing a conclusion from it.

**The lists do not agree on how long it all took.** Exodus 6 gives three lifespans for four
generations; Numbers 26:59 makes Jochebed Levi's own daughter; Exodus 12:40–41 gives four hundred
thirty years. That tension is set out in [[Four Hundred Years - The Sojourn and the Exodus]].

## Significance

The tribe Jacob cursed with dispersal (Genesis 49:7) is the one whose genealogy Scripture keeps in the
greatest detail. **Exodus 6 is the hinge**: the first place the list is followed downward on purpose,
and the first place it is made to end in a priest. See [[Levi]], [[Priesthood]], and
[[Simeon and Levi - The Sword at Shechem and the Deathbed Curse]].
