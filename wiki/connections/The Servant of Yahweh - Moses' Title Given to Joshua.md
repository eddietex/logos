# The Servant of Yahweh - Moses' Title Given to Joshua

## Passages involved

- **Deuteronomy 34:5** — *So Moses the servant of Yahweh died there in the land of Moab* —
  [[The Death of Moses]]
- **Joshua 1:1–2** — *after the death of Moses the servant of Yahweh … Joshua the son of Nun, Moses'
  servant*; *Moses my servant is dead* — [[After the Death of Moses]]
- **Exodus 24:13** — *Moses rose up with Joshua, his servant* — [[The Blood of the Covenant]]
- **Joshua 24:29** — *Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Yahweh, died* —
  [[The Burials of Joshua, Joseph, and Eleazar]]
- **Judges 2:8** — the same words *(not yet ingested)*

## Analysis

Throughout the book of Joshua, *the servant of Yahweh* belongs to Moses. The stored WEB of Joshua
uses the phrase *Moses the servant of Yahweh* fourteen times, and Yahweh speaks of *Moses my
servant* twice (1:2, 7). Joshua's own title is different. The first verse calls him *Moses'
servant* (1:1), as Exodus 24:13 did, *Joshua, his servant*.

The title moves once, at the end. Moses' death notice reads *Moses the servant of Yahweh died*
(Deuteronomy 34:5). Joshua's reads *Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Yahweh, died* (Joshua
24:29). The two notices frame the book: it opens on the first death and closes on the second.
Joshua leads the people, divides the land, and speaks for Yahweh at Shechem, but the narrator
gives him his master's title only when his life is complete.

The book also marks how the two men differ. Moses was buried by Yahweh in an unknown grave in Moab
(Deuteronomy 34:6); Joshua is buried in his own inheritance. Moses died at one hundred twenty
(34:7), and Israel wept for him thirty days (34:8); Joshua dies at one hundred ten, with no mourning
recorded.

## Significance

The servant of a servant becomes a servant of Yahweh. The book does not explain the change. One
reading is that the title is given for a finished life, not for an office; another is that it
marks Joshua as Moses' true successor, which Deuteronomy 34:9 had already begun to say (*the
children of Israel listened to him*). The wiki records both without choosing. The same words are
repeated at Judges 2:8, so the title stays with Joshua beyond his own book.
