# God Does Not Repent - Balaam and the Golden Calf

## Passages involved
- [[Balaam's First Two Oracles]] — Numbers 23:19–20.
- [[The Golden Calf]] — Exodus 32:9–14.

## Analysis

Numbers 23:19 denies divine lying and repentance immediately before the declaration that Balaam
cannot reverse the blessing (20). Exodus 32:14 says that Yahweh repented of the harm announced
against Israel after Moses interceded. These statements pull in different directions if either is
treated as an unrestricted description of all divine action.

The narrative settings differ: Balak seeks a hostile reversal of blessing; Moses seeks preservation
after Israel’s sin. Reading Numbers as an assurance of fidelity in this dispute is a contextual
interpretation, not a claim that Exodus uses no language of change. The wiki retains both claims and
does not resolve their exact source-language relation without checking a named Hebrew edition.

## Significance

Divine reliability and responsiveness to intercession must both remain visible. See
[[Divine Grief and Relenting]] and [[Blessing]]. Sources: stored WEB Numbers 23:19–20 and
Exodus 32:9–14; comparison in paraphrase.
