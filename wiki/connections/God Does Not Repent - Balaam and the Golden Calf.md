# God Does Not Repent - Balaam and the Golden Calf

## Passages involved
- [[Balaam's First Two Oracles]] — Numbers 23:19–20.
- [[The Golden Calf]] — Exodus 32:9–14.
- [[Saul Rejected over Amalek]] — 1 Samuel 15:11, 29, 35.

## Analysis

Numbers 23:19 denies divine lying and repentance immediately before the declaration that Balaam
cannot reverse the blessing (20). Exodus 32:14 says that Yahweh repented of the harm announced
against Israel after Moses interceded. These statements pull in different directions if either is
treated as an unrestricted description of all divine action.

The narrative settings differ: Balak seeks a hostile reversal of blessing; Moses seeks preservation
after Israel’s sin. Reading Numbers as an assurance of fidelity in this dispute is a contextual
interpretation, not a claim that Exodus uses no language of change. The wiki retains both claims and
does not resolve their exact source-language relation without checking a named Hebrew edition.

## Significance

Divine reliability and responsiveness to intercession must both remain visible. See
[[Divine Grief and Relenting]] and [[Blessing]]. Sources: stored WEB Numbers 23:19–20 and
Exodus 32:9–14; comparison in paraphrase.

### 1 Samuel 15 — both claims in one chapter

1 Samuel 15 puts the denial and the narrated regret side by side. The narrator has Yahweh say *It grieves me*,
*niḥamtî* (15:11), and closes *Yahweh grieved*, *niḥām* (35); between them Samuel says *the Strength of Israel will
not lie nor repent; for he is not a man, that he should repent*, *lōʾ yinnāḥēm … ləhinnāḥēm* (29) (WLC). Checked
against the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb, 2026-11-20): all four forms are niphal of *nāḥam*
(lemma 5162), as is Exodus 32:14, *wayyinnāḥem*; Numbers 23:19 has the hitpael *wəyiṯneḥām*. So 1 Samuel 15:29 uses
the same stem as the narrated regret around it and as Exodus 32:14, while Balaam's line uses another. Samuel's
sentence otherwise follows Balaam's closely, *not a man … to lie … to repent*. Like Numbers 23:19, verse 29 stands
beside an irrevocable word, here the sentence on Saul just spoken (28). The chapter does not explain how 11 and 35
relate to 29; this page records them together. See [[Saul Rejected over Amalek]] and
[[Divine Grief and Relenting]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:19; Exodus 32:14; 1 Samuel 15:11, 28–29, 35.
