# Judah and Benjamin Share a Boundary - Joshua 15 and 18

## Passages involved
- [[The Borders of Judah]] — Joshua 15:1–12.
- [[The Cities of Judah]] — Joshua 15:20–63.
- [[The Inheritance of Benjamin]] — Joshua 18:11–28.

## Analysis

Judah’s north border runs from the Salt Sea and Jordan junction westward past Bohan’s stone,
En Shemesh, En Rogel, the south side of Jerusalem, Hinnom, Nephtoah, and Kiriath Jearim
(15:5–9). Benjamin’s south border traverses these shared landmarks in the opposite general
direction, from Kiriath Jearim to the Jordan and Salt Sea junction (18:15–19). The correspondence
explains adjoining allotments; it does not provide a modern surveyed map.

The lists differ. Gilgal opposite Adummim in 15:7 corresponds to Geliloth opposite Adummim
in 18:17, but neither passage expressly equates those names. Beth Arabah belongs to Judah’s
wilderness cities in 15:61 and Benjamin’s cities in 18:22. This is an unresolved overlapping
assignment, not a narrated transfer. The wiki retains both claims rather than inventing a
second town or redrawing the boundary to eliminate the difficulty.

Kiriath Jearim is expressly Judah’s city (18:14), whereas Jerusalem is listed for Benjamin
(18:28). Judah’s failure to expel Jerusalem’s Jebusites (15:63) concerns occupation; the city’s
assignment here does not report a successful expulsion. Kiriath in 18:28 is not expressly
identified with Kiriath Jearim.

Sources: stored WEB Joshua 15:5–10, 60–63; 18:11–28. All comparisons are paraphrases.

## Significance

The adjacent inheritances join the allotment accounts without erasing their differences.
[[Land and Sojourning]] distinguishes a defined share from possession; [[Names and Naming]]
requires explicit identifications to be distinguished from conjectural ones.
