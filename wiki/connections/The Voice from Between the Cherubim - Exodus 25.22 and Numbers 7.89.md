# The Voice from Between the Cherubim - Exodus 25.22 and Numbers 7.89

## Passages involved

- [[The Ark, the Table, and the Lampstand]] — Exodus 25:22; the promise
- [[The Offerings of the Twelve Princes]] — Numbers 7:89; the promise in use
- [[The Tabernacle Raised and the Glory]] — Exodus 40:34–35; Moses unable to enter
- Exodus 29:42–43 — *"where I will meet with you, to speak there to you"*
- Exodus 33:9–11 — the earlier tent, where *"Yahweh spoke to Moses face to face"*
- Leviticus 1:1 — *"Yahweh called to Moses, and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting"*
- Numbers 1:1 — *"Yahweh spoke to Moses … in the Tent of Meeting"*
- [[Miriam and Aaron Against Moses]] — Numbers 12:8; *"With him, I will speak mouth to mouth"*
- [[The Day of Atonement]] — Leviticus 16:2; the same place, and *"lest he die"*

## Analysis

The wording of the two verses matches closely.

> **Exodus 25:22** There I will meet with you, and I will tell you from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubim which are on the ark of the testimony, all that I command you for the children of Israel.

> **Numbers 7:89** When Moses went into the Tent of Meeting to speak with Yahweh, he heard his voice speaking to him from above the mercy seat that was on the ark of the Testimony, from between the two cherubim: and he spoke to him.

In the WLC both have *mēʿal hakkappōret* and *mibbên šənê hakkərubîm*, with a small change of
order, and both have *ʾărōn hāʿēdut*. Every element of the location comes back, so the verse reads as
the promise kept. The text does not call it that. It says nothing about Exodus 25, and the link is drawn from the shared words.

The two verses are one-sided in a way that needs noticing. Exodus promises that God will *tell* Moses all
he commands. Numbers reports the voice and gives no words. Moses *heard* (*wayyišmaʿ*, WLC), and nothing
is said about seeing. Exodus 33:9–11 describes a different tent and calls the speech *face to face*, and
Numbers 12:8 calls it *mouth to mouth*. Numbers 7:89 has only a voice. Whether these descriptions are the same event in
different language is not stated.

Exodus 40:35 shuts Moses out, Leviticus 1:1 calls to him from the Tent, and Numbers 7:89 has him inside.
No verse says when access opened.

The same spot is dangerous to anyone else. Aaron may not come *at all times*, *lest he die: for I will
appear in the cloud on the mercy seat* (Leviticus 16:2). Moses goes in as a matter of course here, as far
as the verse shows, though the verse does not say he went to the mercy seat itself, and the veil's place
in this scene is not mentioned.

**Numbers 12 now ingested.** The phrase is used of speech, and it adds that Moses *shall see Yahweh's form*, which Numbers 7:89 does not claim. See [[Mouth to Mouth - Numbers 12.6-8, Exodus 33, and Deuteronomy 34]].

## Significance

The mercy seat's function as a place of speech is promised at Exodus 25 and shown once, in this verse.
*Kappōret* appears in 22 tagged verses of the WLC, and this is the only one outside Exodus, Leviticus, and
1 Chronicles 28:11. What Moses hears here is what Numbers keeps reporting as *Yahweh spoke to Moses*. The
verse therefore tells the reader, once, where that speech is coming from. That last step is an inference,
since the text does not apply 7:89 to each later instance.

See [[The Mercy Seat - Kapporet and Hilasterion from Leviticus 16 to Romans 3]] for the other function of
the same object.
