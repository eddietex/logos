# Kings Struck and Cities Not Driven Out - Joshua 12, 15-17, and Judges 1

## Passages involved
- [[The Kings Struck Down]] — Joshua 12:1–24; thirty-one western kings counted.
- [[The Southern Cities Taken]] — Joshua 10:28–43; Horam of Gezer struck (10:33).
- [[The Five Kings in the Cave at Makkedah]] — Joshua 10:16–27; the king of Jerusalem killed.
- Joshua 13:13; 15:63; 16:10; 17:11–13 — read in stored WEB, not yet ingested.
- Judges 1:21, 22–25, 27, 29 — WEB checked at bible-api.com, not yet ingested.

## Analysis

Joshua 12 counts kings, not cities held. Set beside the allotment chapters, five of the
royal cities it lists — and both of the east-bank border peoples it names — reappear as places
whose inhabitants Israel did not drive out:

| Joshua 12 | Later notice in Joshua | Judges 1 |
|---|---|---|
| *the king of Jerusalem, one* (10) | Judah *couldn't drive them out*; the Jebusites live there *to this day* (15:63) | Benjamin *did not drive out* the Jebusites (1:21) |
| *the king of Gezer, one* (12) | The Canaanites of Gezer not driven out; put to forced labor (16:10) | Ephraim *didn't drive out* the Canaanites in Gezer (1:29) |
| *the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one* (21) | Manasseh *couldn't drive out the inhabitants* (17:11–12) | Manasseh *did not drive out* Taanach and Megiddo (1:27) |
| *the king of Dor in the height of Dor, one* (23) | The same notice (17:11–12) | The same notice (1:27) |
| Og's border *to the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites* (5) | *Geshur and Maacath live within Israel to this day* (13:13) | — |

Bethel is a different case. Its king is counted (12:16), but Joshua narrates no capture of the
city; Judges 1:22–25 has the house of Joseph take it after Joshua's death.

None of these is a contradiction in the narrow sense. The list says the kings were *struck*; it
does not say the cities were emptied or occupied. Joshua 17:13 itself supplies one pattern —
*when the children of Israel had grown strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, and didn't
utterly drive them out* — and 16:10 the same for Gezer. What the comparison does show is that the
book's summary forms (11:23, *Joshua took the whole land*; 12:24, *all the kings thirty-one*)
measure defeat of rulers, and its allotment notices measure possession, and the two are not the
same measurement. The narrative keeps both.

The wording differs in a way the wiki records without resolving: Joshua 15:63 and 17:12 say
*couldn't*, Joshua 16:10 and Judges 1:21, 27, 29 say *didn't* or *did not*. Whether that difference
reflects inability, unwillingness, or only English rendering is not decided here; no source-language
comparison was made.

## Significance

The list of thirty-one kings is the conquest's high-water mark, and the allotment chapters are its
qualification, written into the same book. Read together they make the book's double verdict —
nothing failed, and very much land remains — concrete, city by city. See also
[[The Southern Conquest and the Land Still Remaining]] and
[[Hazor Burned and the Northern Land Still Remaining]].
