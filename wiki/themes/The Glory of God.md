# The Glory of God

## Definition / overview

**God's presence made visible — *kəbôd YHWH*, *Yahweh's glory*.** The noun ***kābôd*** comes from the root
***kbd***, *be heavy*, and its range runs from *weight* through *wealth* and *honour* to *glory* (WLC; the
semantic range is the usual lexical report, not checked in this pass). **Exodus uses the root for both sides
of its contest**: Pharaoh's heart is *kābēd*, *heavy* (7:14), and God says he will *"get honor"*
(*ʾikkābdâ*) over Pharaoh at the sea (14:4, 17) (WLC). See [[Hardening of the Heart]].

**The phrase *kəbôd YHWH* first occurs in the wilderness of Sin**, over a complaining congregation:
*"in the morning, then you shall see Yahweh's glory"* (Exodus 16:7), and *"they looked toward the wilderness,
and behold, Yahweh's glory appeared in the cloud"* (16:10) (WLC lemma search, Genesis–Exodus). **Before
that, Genesis uses the noun only of people** — Jacob's *"wealth"* (Genesis 31:1), Joseph's *"all my glory in
Egypt"* (45:13), and Jacob's *"My glory, don't be united to their assembly"* (49:6) (WLC).

Three features hold from the first appearance:

1. **It is seen, not only spoken of.** *"You shall see"* (16:7); *"appeared"* (*nirʾâ*, 16:10).
2. **It is seen in the cloud.** The cloud of the pillar (13:21–22) is where the glory shows itself, and the
   pair cloud-and-glory runs to the last chapter of Exodus.
3. **It comes in answer to complaint, not to worship.** The first glory is shown to people who have just said
   they wish they had died in Egypt (16:3), and it comes with food.

**Exodus 19 develops the visible-presence theme without using the WEB phrase “Yahweh's glory.”**
The mountain is covered by cloud and smoke as Yahweh descends in fire (19:16–20). These are
the narrative setting for the explicitly named glory in 24:16–17, not grounds for pretending
that chapter 19 already uses that expression. The statement here concerns the checked WEB
English, not an unverified Hebrew word search. See [[Israel at Mount Sinai]].

## Development across Scripture

**Exodus traces a movement of the glory — appearing, settling, filling.**

| | Where | What the text says |
|---|---|---|
| **16:7, 10** | the wilderness of Sin | *"you shall see Yahweh's glory"*; *"Yahweh's glory appeared in the cloud"* |
| **24:16–17** | Sinai | *"Yahweh's glory settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days … like devouring fire"* — [[The Blood of the Covenant]] |
| **29:43** | the tent | *"There I will meet with the children of Israel; and the place shall be sanctified by my glory"* *(not yet ingested)* |
| **33:18, 22** | the cleft of the rock | *"Please show me your glory"*; *"while my glory passes by"* *(not yet ingested)* |
| **40:34–35** | the tabernacle | *"the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting, and Yahweh's glory filled the tabernacle"* *(not yet ingested)* |

(Exodus 29:43 and 33:18, 22 are cited from the WLC lemma search and the WEB of `raw/bible/Exodus.md`; they
are ahead of the ingested text.)

**The glory Pharaoh's heaviness provoked, Israel is shown.** God's *"I will get honor over Pharaoh"* (14:4) is
the root as action against an enemy; *"you shall see Yahweh's glory"* (16:7) is the root as presence for a
people. See [[The Crossing of the Sea]] and [[Manna and Quail]].

**Ahead** (by reference, *not yet ingested*): the glory at the tabernacle's first offerings (Leviticus 9:23–24),
the glory that appears at the murmurings of Numbers 14 and 16, the glory filling Solomon's temple (1 Kings
8:11), Isaiah's *"the whole earth is full of his glory"* (Isaiah 6:3), Ezekiel's vision of the glory leaving
the temple and returning (Ezekiel 10; 43), and John's *"we saw his glory"* (John 1:14).

### The glory settles on Sinai — Exodus 24:15–18

*"Yahweh's glory settled on Mount Sinai"* (24:16). The verb is *šākan* (MAM), the root of *miškān*,
the tabernacle, and of God's *"that I may dwell among them"* at 25:8 (MAM). For six days cloud covers
the mountain, on the seventh God calls Moses from it, and to Israel below the glory looks *"like
devouring fire"* (24:16–17). **Exodus 24 is the hinge between the glory that appears (16:10) and the
glory that fills the tent (40:34–35).** See [[The Blood of the Covenant]].

### The priestly garments — Exodus 28:1–43

The holy garments are *"for glory and for beauty"* (Exodus 28:2, 40). The glory here is given to
the priest by his clothing; the chapter does not tie it to the glory that settled on Sinai
(24:16–17), and this page does not claim the same Hebrew word is used in both places. See [[The Priestly Garments]].

## Key passages

- [[Manna and Quail]] — Exodus 16:7, 10; **the first *kəbôd YHWH* in Scripture.** *"In the morning, then you
  shall see Yahweh's glory; because he hears your murmurings"*; *"behold, Yahweh's glory appeared in the
  cloud"* — shown to a complaining congregation, and followed by quail and bread
- [[The Crossing of the Sea]] — Exodus 14:4, 17; **the root before the noun.** *"I will get honor over
  Pharaoh"* (*ʾikkābdâ*, WLC)
- [[Israel at Mount Sinai]] — Exodus 19:9, 16–20; cloud and fire at the public descent, ahead of 24:16–17
- [[The Blood of the Covenant]] — Exodus 24:16–17; **the glory settles** (*šākan*), cloud for six days, devouring fire on the summit
- [[The Offering for the Sanctuary]] — Exodus 25:8–9; **the dwelling announced.** The glory that *"settled"* (*šākan*) on
  Sinai (24:16) is to *dwell* (*šākan*) among the people, in a *miškān* built to God's pattern (MAM). The
  Septuagint has *"I will be seen among you"* (Rahlfs). See [[The Dwelling of God]]
- [[The Priestly Garments]] — Exodus 28:1–43; garments *"for glory and for beauty"*.
