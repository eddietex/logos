# The Spirit of God

## Definition / overview

*Rûaḥ* means wind, breath, and spirit, and the context has to say which. English capital letters are a translator's decision, and
the wiki follows WEB's capitals when it quotes, without treating them as evidence. This page tracks the places where the text
speaks of the Spirit as God's. It opened with Numbers 11, when the Spirit is first shared out from one person to others, and it
is not yet a full survey of the wiki's earlier books.

## Development across Scripture

**Creation.** *God's Spirit was hovering over the surface of the waters* (Genesis 1:2). Job 33:4 says, *The Spirit of God has
made me*. See [[Creation]].

**Limit.** *My Spirit will not strive with man forever* (Genesis 6:3). See [[The Sons of God and the Corruption of the Earth]].

**In a person, recognized by a foreigner.** Pharaoh asks about Joseph, *a man in whom is the Spirit of God* (Genesis 41:38). See
[[A Man in Whom Is the Spirit of God]].

**For skilled work.** Bezalel is filled *with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding* (Exodus 31:3; 35:31).

**Wisdom's promise.** *I will pour out my spirit on you* (Proverbs 1:23). See
[[I Will Pour Out My Spirit - Proverbs 1.23, Joel 2.28, and Acts 2.17]], which also records that *rûaḥ* there may not be the same
thing.

### Shared out — Numbers 11

Yahweh says to Moses, *I will take of the Spirit which is on you, and will put it on them* (11:17). This is the first time the
Spirit passes from one person to others. It is not described as moving away from Moses, and it has a purpose, to share the
burden. It also has a visible sign, prophecy, that happens once (25). Then it goes where it was not sent. Eldad and Medad did not go
to the Tent, and it rests on them in the camp (26). The verb for that is *nûaḥ*, which at Isaiah 11:2 is used of the coming ruler,
*wənāḥâ* (WLC), WEB's *Yahweh's Spirit will rest on him*.

**Moses' wish.** *I wish that all Yahweh's people were prophets, that Yahweh would put his Spirit on them!* (11:29). Here the Spirit
is called *his*. The wish is for no limit at all. See [[Would That All Were Prophets - Numbers 11, Joel 2, and Acts 2]].

**The word that follows.** Verse 31 has *rûaḥ* again, as the wind that brings the quail. See
[[The Seventy Elders and the Quail]].

Source: stored WEB and WLC; Isaiah 11:2 WEB at bible-api.com.

### Ahead

The Spirit comes on Balaam (Numbers 24:2), and Joshua is *a man in whom is the Spirit* (27:18). Those sections are written as their
pericopes arrive.
