# Work

## Definition / overview

Labour as a human vocation. The Bible's placement of it is the whole argument: **work appears
before the fall, not after it.** *"Yahweh God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to
cultivate and keep it"* (2:15) comes fourteen verses before the prohibition is broken and a
chapter before the ground is cursed. What Genesis 3 adds is not work but *futility* — thorns,
thistles, sweat, and a harvest that fights back. Toil is the curse; work is the commission.

Three things frame it in Genesis 1–2:

1. **God works.** Six days of it, and the seventh day is defined by what it rests *from*. Human
   work is patterned on divine work before it is anything else.
2. **Work is dominion in practice.** Genesis 1:28 grants rule over the creatures; Genesis 2:19–20
   shows the man exercising it by naming them — an act of ordering and knowing, not of force.
3. **Work is service and guardianship.** The pair *ʿābad* / *šāmar*, "cultivate and keep", is
   used elsewhere of the Levites tending and guarding the tabernacle (Numbers 3:7–8, 18:5–7).
   *ʿābad* is also the ordinary verb for serving and for worshipping. The garden is worked the way
   a sanctuary is kept.

Work and rest are a matched pair, not opposites: the same account that gives the man a job gives
the seventh day a blessing. See [[Sabbath]].

## Development across Scripture

- **Genesis 1:28; 2:15** — the mandate to fill, subdue, cultivate, and keep.
- **Genesis 3:17–19** *(not yet ingested)* — the ground cursed; the same work now yields thorns
  and is done by sweat.
- Strands to trace as ingestion reaches them: the sabbath and jubilee legislation that limits
  work and protects workers (Exodus 20:9–10, Leviticus 25); the craftsmen of the tabernacle filled
  with the Spirit for their skill (Exodus 31:1–11); Ecclesiastes on the vanity and the gift of
  labour (2:18–24, 3:13); Proverbs on diligence and sloth; Jesus as a *tektōn* (Mark 6:3); Paul's
  tentmaking and his rules for it (Acts 18:3, 1 Thessalonians 4:11, 2 Thessalonians 3:10);
  Colossians 3:23.
