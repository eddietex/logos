| [[Light and Darkness]] | The Bible's first made thing and its first act of ordering — light spoken into being and divided from a darkness that was already there and is never said to be created; both are named and bounded rather than one abolished, until Revelation removes night altogether. In Job the pair turns hostile: chapter 3 asks for a day back, *light* comes to mean simply *being alive*, Bildad makes extinguished light a sentence, and the whirlwind opens by accusing Job of darkening counsel;
