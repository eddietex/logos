# Numbers

## Overview

Numbers takes Israel from the camp at Sinai to the plains of Moab. It opens with the army counted
around the tabernacle and closes with the next generation counted on the edge of the land. Between
those two censuses the first generation refuses the land and dies in the wilderness, as 26:64–65
says outright. The Hebrew name of the book is taken from its first verse, *in the wilderness*.

[[The Census in the Wilderness of Sinai]] (1:1–54) sets the frame. A month after the tabernacle was raised, Moses and Aaron
count 603,550 men of fighting age by tribe, and the Levites are set apart to carry and guard the
tabernacle instead. Israel is arranged as a marching army with a sanctuary at its center.

The book mixes narrative with law, and it is not in strict date order: 9:1 goes back to the first
month. It names Moses as the writer of the journey list (33:2), but says nothing about the book as a
whole.

## Structure

- **At Sinai (1:1–10:10)** — census, camp, Levites, purity, dedication, and the signals for moving.
- **Sinai to Kadesh (10:11–12:16)** — the march, and the complaints that begin at once.
- **Kadesh and the wilderness years (13:1–19:22)** — the spies, the refusal, the sentence, Korah,
  and the laws around them.
- **Kadesh to Moab (20:1–25:18)** — Meribah, the deaths of Miriam and Aaron, the Transjordan
  victories, Balaam, and Baal Peor.
- **The plains of Moab (26:1–36:13)** — the second census, inheritance, offerings, Midian, the
  eastern tribes, and the borders of the land.

## Key themes

- [[The Wilderness]] — the setting of the book and the name it carries in Hebrew
- [[War]] — Israel is mustered as an army
- [[The Dwelling of God]] — the tabernacle at the center of a moving camp
- [[Holiness]] — the danger of approach, and the cordon around the sanctuary
- [[Priesthood]] — Levites and priests set apart from the tribes
- [[Law and Obedience]] — *as Yahweh commanded*

## Key people

- [[Moses]] — leader, counter, and recipient of the speeches
- [[Aaron]] — counts with Moses
- [[The Twelve Tribal Princes]] — the man from each tribe
- [[Nahshon]] — Judah's man
