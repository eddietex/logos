# Salmon

## Overview

### Sixth in the generations of Perez — Ruth 4:20–21

*Nahshon became the father of Salmon, and Salmon became the father of Boaz* (Ruth 4:20–21). He is the son
of [[Nahshon]], the wilderness prince of Judah, and the father of [[Boaz]]. Ruth says nothing else of him.
See [[The Generations of Perez]].

**Three spellings.** The WEB gives *Salmon* twice, but the Hebrew of Ruth spells the name two ways in
consecutive clauses: *śalmâ* closing verse 20 and *śalmôn* opening verse 21 (WLC; Strong's 8009 and
8012, each found only in that verse in the files searched). 1 Chronicles 2:11 has a third form,
*śalmāʾ*, which the WEB renders *Salma*. Matthew 1:4–5 and Luke 3:32 have *Salmon* (WEB at
bible-api.com; the Greek was not consulted).

**Rahab.** Matthew 1:5 reads *Salmon became the father of Boaz by Rahab* (WEB at bible-api.com). Neither
Joshua nor Ruth connects him with her; a WLC search finds no form of his name in Joshua. Matthew does not
say that his Rahab is the woman of Jericho, though that is the usual reading; see [[Rahab]]. If she is,
Salmon belongs to the generation of the conquest, which fits his father's place in the wilderness. See
[[From Perez to David - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, Matthew 1, and Luke 3]].
