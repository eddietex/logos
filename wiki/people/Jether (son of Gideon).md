# Jether (son of Gideon)

## Overview

### The firstborn who did not draw — Judges 8:20

*He said to Jether his firstborn, "Get up, and kill them!" But the youth didn't draw his sword; for he was
afraid, because he was yet a youth* (8:20). It is the only verse in Judges 8 that names him. Gideon gives
his firstborn the death of the two kings of Midian, who had killed Gideon's brothers at Tabor (8:18–19).
The text does not say why. The kings' answer, *Rise and fall on us; for as the man is, so is his
strength* (8:21), suggests that death by a boy would have shamed them.

He is *afraid*, *yārēʾ*, the word of the proclamation that sent twenty-two thousand home (7:3) and of
Yahweh's *if you are afraid* to Gideon (7:10). He is called *naʿar*, *youth*, twice, the word used of
[[Purah]] (7:10–11). He *didn't draw his sword*, *lōʾ šālap ḥarbô* (WLC), the verb of the fifteen
thousand *who drew sword* (8:10). The father who had been afraid now commands, and the son is afraid.

The page is qualified because the name recurs for other men; *Amasa the son of Jether* is named at
1 Kings 2:5 (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). In the WLC lemma data the same name, *Yeter* (3500), is how Moses' Midianite father-in-law is
written at Exodus 4:18, where WEB reads *Jethro*. The wiki notes the coincidence and draws nothing from
it; see [[Jethro]].

Gideon has seventy sons (8:30), and Abimelech kills *his brothers the sons of Jerubbaal, being seventy
persons, on one stone*, all but Jotham (9:5) (stored WEB; not yet ingested). Jether is not named there.
