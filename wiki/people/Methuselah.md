# Methuselah

## Overview

The longest-lived human being in the Bible, and the son of the one man in his chapter who never
died. Nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and the entry ends the way all of them end: *"All the days
of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty-nine years, then he died"* (5:27). He is the eighth generation
from Adam, born to [[Enoch (son of Jared)]] at 65 — the birth that begins Enoch's three hundred
years of walking with God — father of [[Lamech (father of Noah)]] at 187, and grandfather of
[[Noah]].

Two facts about him are entirely a matter of arithmetic, because the text never states either.

**He dies in the year of the flood.** Counting from Adam's creation as year 0, Methuselah is born in
687 and dies in 1656. Noah is born in 1056 and the flood comes in his six hundredth year (7:11) —
1656. The longest life in Scripture ends exactly at the waters. Genesis does not say he drowned and
does not say he was spared; it does not mention him again. Old rabbinic tradition reads the
seven-day delay before the rain (7:4) as the week of mourning for him, which resolves the question
in the gentler direction. The alignment is exact in the Hebrew text and does not survive the
Septuagint's different figures, so it is a feature of one textual tradition (see
[[From Adam to Noah]]).

**The famous gloss on his name is not sound.** The reading *"when he dies, it shall be sent"* —
taking *mût* (die) plus *šelaḥ* (send), and "it" as the flood — is homiletic reconstruction, and it
requires a verb form the name does not have. The defensible parsings are *"man of the dart"* or
*"man of the weapon"* (*metû* + *šelaḥ*, a missile or javelin), or *"man of Shelah"* if the second
element is a name. The coincidence of dates is real; the name is not evidence for it.

He is one of the two names in the Sethite list that survives, altered, into the line of Cain, where
the corresponding figure is *Methushael* (4:18) — same first element, different second. See
[[Two Lines from Adam - Cain's Genealogy and Seth's]]. Elsewhere in Scripture he appears only in the
lists: 1 Chronicles 1:3, Luke 3:37.

## Timeline / key events

- Born to Enoch in Enoch's sixty-fifth year; his birth is the point from which Enoch is said to walk
  with God — [[From Adam to Noah]]
- Fathers Lamech at 187, then other sons and daughters — [[From Adam to Noah]]
- Outlives his father by some 669 years, having watched him be taken rather than buried —
  [[From Adam to Noah]]
- Dies at 969, the longest recorded human life — by the chapter's own numbers, in the year the flood
  comes — [[From Adam to Noah]]
