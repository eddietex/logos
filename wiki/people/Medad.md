# Medad

## Overview

The second of the two men left in the camp (Numbers 11:26). Everything said of [[Eldad]] is said of him, and he appears only
beside Eldad, at Numbers 11:26–27 (tags). Rahlfs's Greek spells him *Μωδαδ*. Nothing else is known of him, and the
text gives him no words.

## Timeline / key events

- Stays in the camp, and prophesies there — Numbers 11:26, [[The Seventy Elders and the Quail]]
