# Asherah

## Overview

### The Asheroth — Judges 3:7–11

*They … served the Baals and the Asheroth* (3:7, stored WEB). The Hebrew is *hāʾăšērôt* (WLC). This is
the first time the wiki meets the word as something Israel *served*, beside a god. Before this, the
law treated an Asherah as an object.

**An object in the law.** *Cut down their Asherah poles* (Exodus 34:13); *cut down their Asherah
poles* (Deuteronomy 7:5); *burn their Asherah poles with fire* (Deuteronomy 12:3); and *You shall not
plant for yourselves an Asherah of any kind of tree beside Yahweh your God's altar* (Deuteronomy 16:21;
all stored WEB). *Plant*, *cut down*, and *burn* fit a wooden pole or a tree.

**A deity, or her objects?** Judges 3:7 sets the word in the plural beside *the Baals*, as 2:13 set
*the Ashtaroth*. That makes it look like the name of a goddess. It is commonly held that Asherah was a
Canaanite goddess and the pole her symbol. That is reported here, not checked. This verse alone cannot
settle whether *the Asheroth* are goddesses, poles, or both, and the wiki does not decide.

**The form.** In the WLC files read (Exodus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges; OpenScriptures
lemma `842`), the plural elsewhere ends in *-îm* (Deuteronomy 7:5; 12:3), and only Judges 3:7 has
*-ôt*, the ending of *ʿaštārôt*. Some explain 3:7 as a blending with *Ashtaroth*; reported, not
checked. Other ancient witnesses for 3:7 were not consulted.

Gideon will be told to *cut down the Asherah* beside his father's altar of Baal (Judges 6:25, stored
WEB), the singular with the article.

The page is filed under `people/` as `type: person`, as [[Baal (deity)]] and [[Ashtoreth (deity)]]
are. No collision is expected for the name, so it has no qualifier.

See [[Othniel the First Judge]]. Source: stored WEB Exodus 34:13; Deuteronomy 7:5; 12:3; 16:21; Judges
2:13; 3:7; 6:25. Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.
