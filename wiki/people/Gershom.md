# Gershom

## Overview

**Moses' firstborn son, born in Midian to Zipporah, and named for his father's exile.** *"She bore a
son, and he named him Gershom, for he said, 'I have lived as a foreigner in a foreign land'"*
(Exodus 2:22; repeated at 18:3). ***Gēr hāyîtî bəʾereṣ nokriyyâ*** — the name is heard as ***gēr
šām***, ***a sojourner there***. See [[Moses Flees to Midian]].

**The name carries the word of the Abrahamic prophecy.** *Gēr* is what God told Abram his offspring
would be — *"your offspring will live as foreigners in a land that is not theirs"* (Genesis 15:13) —
and Moses is the first of that offspring to name a child with it. **Which land is *foreign* the
speech does not say**: Midian, where Moses is living, or Egypt, where he was raised and from which
he fled. Readers have taken both. And the word becomes the ground of Israel's law of the stranger:
*"You shall not oppress an alien, for you know the heart of an alien, since you were aliens in the
land of Egypt"* (Exodus 23:9). See
[[A Foreigner in a Foreign Land - Gershom and the Law of the Stranger]] and [[Land and Sojourning]].

**The name sets Moses beside Joseph**, who also named his sons in exile — Manasseh for forgetting,
Ephraim for fruitfulness *"in the land of my affliction"* (Genesis 41:51–52). **Joseph's names both
name God; Gershom's does not.** The God-name in Moses' family is given to the second son, Eliezer,
*"My father's God was my help and delivered me from Pharaoh's sword"* (Exodus 18:4), after the
deliverance.

### His line

- **1 Chronicles 23:15–16** — *"The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer. The sons of Gershom: Shebuel
  the chief."* Moses' sons are counted among the Levites, not the priests; **the priesthood goes to
  Aaron's line, and Moses' descendants receive no office of their own.**
- **Judges 18:30** — *"Jonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, and his sons were priests to
  the tribe of the Danites"* at the image of Dan. **The Masoretic text writes *Moses* (*mšh*) with a
  suspended *nun* above the line, making it read *Manasseh* (*mnšh*)** — a scribal intervention
  widely understood as shielding Moses' name from an idolatrous grandson. The WEB reads *Moses*. The
  wiki records the textual fact and will take it up when Judges 18 is reached.
- **Gershom the son of Moses is distinct from *Gershon* the son of Levi** (Genesis 46:11; Exodus
  6:16–17), whose name is near-identical and whose clan, the Gershonites, is a Levitical division.

### The lodging place (Exodus 4:20–26)

**Set on a donkey with his mother for the journey to Egypt** (4:20, *"his sons"*, plural, though only
he has been named) **and — if he is the son meant — circumcised by his mother at the lodging place**
(4:25). The text says only *"her son"*, and the Hebrew of 4:24 does not say whether Yahweh's attack was
on Moses or on the boy. **Moses' firstborn is cut three verses after God names Israel his firstborn and
announces the death of Pharaoh's** (4:22–23). See [[Moses Returns to Egypt]].

**He is not in the genealogy of 6:14–27.** The list names Aaron's wife, sons, and grandson, and
stops Moses' line at Moses. See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]].

### What is ahead

Sent back to Midian at some point not narrated, and brought again by Jethro (18:2–6).
