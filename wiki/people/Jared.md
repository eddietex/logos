# Jared

## Overview

The sixth generation from Adam, father of the one man in Genesis 5 who does not die, and himself
the second-longest-lived person in the Bible. His entry is otherwise ordinary: born to
[[Mahalalel]] at 65, father of [[Enoch (son of Jared)]] at 162, other sons and daughters, dead at
962 (5:15–20). Only [[Methuselah]] outlasts him, by seven years.

His name, *Yered*, is from *yārad*, "to go down" — *descent*. Genesis makes nothing of it. Later
apocalyptic literature made a great deal: *1 Enoch* 6:6 and the *Book of Jubilees* 4:15 both take
the name as a marker of the generation in which the watchers *descended* to earth — the tradition
that grows out of Genesis 6:1–4 and reads the "sons of God" as fallen angels. That reading is a
second-century-BC construction working backwards from a name; it is worth knowing because it shapes
how Enoch was read for centuries, not because Genesis 5 supports it.

In Cain's line the counterpart is *Irad* (4:18) — the same consonants but for the first letter. See
[[Two Lines from Adam - Cain's Genealogy and Seth's]]. He is named again at 1 Chronicles 1:2 and
Luke 3:37.
