# Arpachshad

## Overview

The first person in the Bible born after the flood, and the man the line to Abraham runs through.

He is Shem's third-listed son at Genesis 10:22 — *"The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad,
Lud, and Aram"* — and the only one of the five whose descendants
[[The Table of Nations]] traces further. In [[From Shem to Abram]] he gets the numbers: *"Shem was
one hundred years old when he became the father of Arpachshad two years after the flood"* (11:10),
then thirty-five years old at Shelah's birth and four hundred three after that.

**That "two years after the flood" is the most load-bearing clause in the register**, and it is
attached to him. It is the only fixed point in Genesis 11 — every other entry is relative to the one
before it — and it is what ties the post-flood chronology to the pre-flood one. It also produces a
small friction the text does not resolve: Genesis 5:32 has Noah fathering Shem, Ham, and Japheth at
five hundred, and the flood comes in Noah's six hundredth year (7:11), so a Shem who is a hundred
two years *after* the flood was born when his father was five hundred and two. Either 5:32 is a
round figure for the beginning of Noah's fathering, or Shem is not the eldest of the three — which
is a question Genesis 9:24 and 10:21 raise independently.

**The name is unexplained and unclear.** *ʾArpakšad* has no Hebrew etymology and Genesis offers
none. The proposals — a link to *Arrapḫa*, the Hurrian city at modern Kirkuk; a reading of the final
element as *kaśed*, the root behind *Kaśdîm*, "Chaldees"; a Hurrian or Elamite personal name — are
guesses of varying seriousness and none has carried the field. He is one of the few names in either
register that Genesis makes no play on at all.

**The Septuagint puts a son of his into the Bible that the Hebrew does not have.** Between Arpachshad
and [[Shelah]], the Greek text inserts **Cainan**, with a hundred and thirty years of his own, and
Luke 3:36 follows it: *"the son of Shelah, the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad."* The Hebrew of
Genesis 10:24 and 11:12–13 has Arpachshad fathering Shelah directly, as does 1 Chronicles 1:18, 24.
It is the clearest single instance in Scripture of the genealogies disagreeing about how many
generations there were.

He does nothing, says nothing, and goes nowhere. On the register's own arithmetic he dies in the year
2096 from creation, twenty-seven years before Abraham — the first of the ten to be outlived by his
own descendants.

## Timeline / key events

- Named third among Shem's five sons — Genesis 10:22, [[The Table of Nations]]
- Born two years after the flood, when Shem was a hundred — Genesis 11:10, [[From Shem to Abram]]
- Fathers [[Shelah]] at thirty-five and lives four hundred three years after — Genesis 11:12–13
- Named in the Chronicler's list (1 Chronicles 1:17–18, 24) and in Luke's genealogy of Jesus
  (Luke 3:36, with Cainan interposed)

## Appears in

- [[The Table of Nations]] — Genesis 10:22, 24; a name in Shem's branch, and the one it follows
- [[From Shem to Abram]] — Genesis 11:10–13; the register's first entry after Shem, and its only
  absolute date
