# Mamre (person)

## Overview

An Amorite, Abram's covenant-partner, and the man the grove Abram lives in is named for — or the
man who is named for it; Genesis does not say which way round it goes.

*"At that time, he lived by the oaks of Mamre, the Amorite, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner;
and they were allies of Abram"* (Genesis 14:13). The verse uses the name twice over in one breath —
once as a place Abram camps at and once as a person who has brothers — and offers no comment on the
overlap. See [[The Rescue of Lot and Melchizedek]] and [[Mamre (place)]].

**The Hebrew for *allies* is stronger than the English.** *Baʿălê bərît ʾAbrām* — **masters of a
covenant of Abram**, covenant-partners. This is the first occurrence of *bərît* in the Abraham
story, and it is a mutual-defense treaty with three Amorite brothers, not a covenant with God.
Genesis will not use the word of Abram and Yahweh until 15:18. That the promised land's future
dispossessed are, at this moment, the promise-bearer's sworn allies is one of the quieter tensions
in the cycle: 15:16 has Yahweh telling Abram his descendants will return *"in the fourth
generation … for the iniquity of the Amorite is not yet full."*

**He goes to war, and he takes his share.** The three brothers are among *"the men who went with
me"* (14:24), and Abram — having refused to accept a thread from the king of Sodom — insists on
their portion in the same sentence in which he refuses his own: *"Let them take their portion."* He
does not bind allies who did not swear his oath. It is a small, exact piece of ethics, and it is the
last the Bible says about any of the three.

## The name

*Mamrēʾ* is of uncertain derivation; *strength* or *fatness* have both been proposed, and neither is
secure. Whether the grove took the man's name as its owner, or the man took the grove's as a local
chief, is not recoverable from the text, and the same doubling happens elsewhere in Genesis with
[[Enoch (city)]] and, in the other direction, with the register names that are also towns near
[[Haran (city)]] — [[Serug]], [[Nahor (son of Serug)]], [[Terah]], [[Reu]].

**On the two pages.** `AGENTS.md` requires that a name held by two pages be qualified on both, so
this page is `Mamre (person)` and the oak grove is [[Mamre (place)]]. The collision was flagged when
the grove's page was created at Genesis 13:18 and both pages were renamed in this pass, as scheduled.
The qualifier is written out in links and never piped away.
