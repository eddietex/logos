# Elon the Zebulunite

## Overview

### The judge of Zebulun — Judges 12:11–12

*After him Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel; and he judged Israel ten years. Elon the Zebulunite
died, and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun* (12:11–12).

The shortest of the five minor-judge notices: a name, a tribe, ten years, a grave. No family is
given. He is the only judge of the book from Zebulun, the tribe Barak called to Tabor (4:6, 10) and
the Song praised as *a people that jeopardized their lives to the death* (5:18).

**A clan name.** *Elon* is the name of Zebulun's second son (*Sered, Elon, and Jahleel*, Genesis
46:14) and of a Zebulunite clan, *the family of the Elonites* (Numbers 26:26). The WLC lemma is the
same, 356. Tola, the first minor judge, likewise bore the names of Issachar's clans. See
[[Clan Names Among the Minor Judges - Genesis 46, Numbers 26, and Judges 10 and 12]].

**Elon and Aijalon.** He is buried in *Aijalon in the land of Zebulun*, *bəʾayyālôn* (WLC). His name at
12:11, *ʾêlôn*, and the town's name share the same consonants, *ʾylwn*; at 12:12 his name is written
without the *y*. The morphhb lemma data keeps them apart (356 for the man, 357 for the place). The
text does not explain the likeness. See [[Aijalon (Zebulun)]].

**Not the Hittite.** The same name belongs to [[Elon the Hittite]], Esau's father-in-law (Genesis
26:34; 36:2), and to a town in Dan (Joshua 19:43). They are unrelated to the judge.

See [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 12:11–12; Genesis 46:14; Numbers 26:26;
Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.
