# Saul's Uncle

## Overview

### The question Saul does not fully answer — 1 Samuel 10:14–16

When [[Saul]] comes back from prophesying to the high place, *Saul's uncle*, *dôḏ šāʾûl* (10:14, WLC), asks him and
his servant, *Where did you go?* Saul tells him about the donkeys and Samuel. The uncle presses: *Please tell me
what Samuel said to you* (10:15). Saul answers only that *He told us plainly that the donkeys were found*, and the
narrator adds, *But concerning the matter of the kingdom, of which Samuel spoke, he didn't tell him* (10:16). The
uncle's questions are the first time anyone in Saul's family hears of the trip, and the answer keeps the anointing
secret. See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].

**Not identified.** The text does not name him. 1 Samuel 14:50 names *Abner the son of Ner, Saul's uncle*, and
14:51 says *Ner the father of Abner was the son of Abiel*, as Kish was (9:1) (stored WEB; not yet ingested). The
phrase at 14:50 can be read with either Ner or Abner as the uncle. Identifying the uncle of 10:14 with Ner, or with
Abner, is a common proposal. It is recalled, not checked, and the wiki does not make it. *Dôḏ* in the Torah is a
father's brother (Leviticus 10:4; 25:49, WLC; lemma 1730).
