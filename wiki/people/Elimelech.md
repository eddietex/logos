# Elimelech

## Overview

*A certain man of Bethlehem Judah* (Ruth 1:1), husband of [[Naomi]] and father of [[Mahlon]] and
[[Chilion]], *Ephrathites of Bethlehem Judah* (1:2). When famine comes in the days of the judges he takes
his family *to live*, *lāgûr*, to sojourn, in the country of Moab, the verb Genesis uses of Abram's descent
to Egypt in a famine (Genesis 12:10; WLC). He dies there in a single clause (1:3). The text gives no cause
of death and passes no judgment on his leaving.

He does nothing else in the book, but his name stays in it. The kinsman Boaz is *of the family of
Elimelech* (2:1, 3), the land Naomi sells *was our brother Elimelech's* (4:3), and Boaz buys *all that was
Elimelech's* (4:9). The redemption of chapter 4 is the recovery of what he left.

**The name.** *ʾĔlîmeleḵ* reads naturally as *my God is king*. The book that precedes Ruth in the English
canon ends *In those days there was no king in Israel* (Judges 21:25). The text makes nothing of the
name; the observation is a reader's (see [[Names and Naming]]).

See [[Famine and Death in Moab]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 1:1–3; 2:1, 3; 4:3, 9; Genesis 12:10; Judges
21:25; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
