# Abiel (father of Kish)

## Overview

### Saul's grandfather — 1 Samuel 9:1; 14:51

Abiel heads Kish's pedigree, *Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of Aphiah*
(9:1), and he is named again as Ner's father: *Ner the father of Abner was the son of Abiel* (14:51). He is
therefore Saul's grandfather and Abner's, and the ancestor through whom the king and his commander are cousins.
*ʾĂḇîʾēl* (WLC, both verses). Qualified because an Abiel appears among David's mighty men (recalled, not checked).

1 Chronicles 8:33 and 9:39 name no Abiel; there *Ner became the father of Kish*, and the father of Gibeon in 9:35 is
*Jeiel* (WEB at bible-api.com). The two genealogies are compared on [[Ner]] and [[Kish (father of Saul)]]; whether
Abiel and Jeiel are the same man is proposed (recalled, not checked) and not decided here. See
[[Saul Comes to the Seer]] and [[Saul's Wars and Household]].
