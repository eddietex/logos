# Hirah the Adullamite

## Overview

**Judah's friend, and the only friend any patriarch is given in Genesis.**

> **1** At that time, Judah went down from his brothers, and visited a certain Adullamite, whose name
> was Hirah. (38:1)
> **12** …and went up to his sheep shearers to Timnah, he and his friend Hirah, the Adullamite. (38:12)

***Rēʿēhû*** — *his friend*, *his companion* — at 38:12 and 38:20. **Genesis gives the patriarchs
wives, servants, allies, rivals, and hosts; it gives Judah a friend**, and it is a Canaanite in the
Shephelah. The nearest earlier comparison is the Amorite brothers [[Mamre (person)]], [[Eshcol]], and
[[Aner]], who are *"allies of Abram"* (14:13) and are named only in a list.

**He is present for all three of the chapter's social transactions and complicit in none of them.** He
is where Judah goes when he leaves his brothers; he is at the shearing when Judah walks past
[[Enaim]]; and **he is the one sent back with the goat.**

> **20** Judah sent the young goat by the hand of his friend, the Adullamite, to receive the pledge
> from the woman’s hand, but he didn’t find her.
> **21** Then he asked the men of her place, saying, “Where is the prostitute, that was at Enaim by the
> road?” They said, “There has been no prostitute here.”

***"Where is the prostitute?"*** **And the word he uses is not the word the narrator used.** At 38:15
Judah takes her for a ***zônâ***, an ordinary prostitute; **Hirah goes looking for a *qədēšâ*** — a
*consecrated woman*, from *qādaš*, *to be holy*, a class of personnel attached to a sanctuary and
forbidden to Israelites by name at Deuteronomy 23:17–18. **The errand has been dignified in the
telling**, and the wiki takes the shift as the chapter's own quiet comment on how such a commission
gets described by the man running it. **Whether *qədēšâ* implies sacred prostitution at all is
disputed**, and the wiki does not settle it. See [[Language and Speech]].

**He carries back a true answer that is useless.** *"There has been no prostitute here"* — **and there
had not been**; there had been a widow in a veil. He reports it in the townsmen's own words, and Judah
closes the matter: *"Let her keep it, **lest we be shamed**."* **The pronoun is plural.** **Hirah is
inside the embarrassment and is never mentioned again**, in Genesis or anywhere else in Scripture.

**The name.** ***Ḥîrâ*** is probably related to a root for *nobility* or *splendour*, and it is one
consonant from ***Ḥîrām***, Hiram of Tyre — which is why this page is qualified as *the Adullamite*
from the start rather than left bare. **Genesis itself identifies him that way both times after the
first**, so the qualifier is the text's own.

## Timeline / key events

- Judah goes down and *visits* him at [[Adullam]] — Genesis 38:1, [[Judah and Tamar]]
- Accompanies Judah to the shearing at [[Timnah]] as *his friend* — Genesis 38:12
- Sent back with the young goat to redeem the pledge; asks the men of [[Enaim]] for a *qədēšâ* and is
  told there was none — Genesis 38:20–22
- Not mentioned again in Scripture
