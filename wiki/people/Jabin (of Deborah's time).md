# Jabin (of Deborah's time)

## Overview

*Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor* (Judges 4:2), into whose hand Yahweh *sold* Israel after
Ehud's death. He has nine hundred chariots of iron and *mightily oppressed the children of Israel for
twenty years* (4:3). He does nothing on the page himself; his captain Sisera fights for him. 4:17 adds
that there was peace between him and the house of Heber the Kenite, and 4:23–24 report his defeat and
destruction (next pericope).

**Two Jabins of Hazor.** Joshua 11 names *Jabin king of Hazor* as the king who summoned the northern
coalition; Joshua took Hazor, *struck its king with the sword*, and burned it (Joshua 11:1, 10–13).
Judges 4 has a king of the same name reigning in the same city. The text does not explain this. The
possibilities usually raised are a rebuilt city with a later king of the same name, a dynastic name,
or two traditions of one king; the wiki does not choose. This page is keyed to the narrative, by the
rule for figures whose identity the text does not fix. See [[Jabin (of Joshua's time)]].

**"King of Canaan."** No other king in the books read so far is called king of the whole land. The
title may mean he led the Canaanite cities of the north, as Hazor *used to be the head of all those
kingdoms* (Joshua 11:10). That is a reading, not something the text says.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Joshua 11:1–13.
