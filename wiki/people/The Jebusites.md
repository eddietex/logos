# The Jebusites

## Overview

### Lived among — Judges 3:1–6

Last of the six peoples Israel *lived among* (5). Judges 1:21 had already said they *dwell with the
children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day*. Israel marries their daughters and serves their gods
(6).

See [[The Nations Left to Test Israel]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:1–6.

A people of the land, named on most of the Pentateuch's lists of nations to be driven out, and the
inhabitants of Jerusalem. No individual Jebusite is named in the texts the wiki has ingested so far.
The page was created at Judges 1:21, after the name had appeared on many passage pages without a page
of its own.

**Descent.** The Table of Nations makes them sons of Canaan: *Canaan became the father of Sidon (his
firstborn), Heth, the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites* (Genesis 10:15–16). See
[[Canaan (son of Ham)]].

**On the lists.** They close the list of peoples whose land is promised to Abram (Genesis 15:21), and
they recur in the lists of Exodus 3:8, 17; 13:5; 23:23; 33:2; 34:11, Deuteronomy 7:1; 20:17, and Joshua
3:10; 24:11, as nations Yahweh will drive out or has given into Israel's hand, or that Israel must
devote to destruction. The spies place them *in the hill country*
with the Hittite and the Amorite (Numbers 13:29).

**Jerusalem.** Joshua's border lists call the city by their name: *the side of the Jebusite (also
called Jerusalem)* (Joshua 15:8; 18:28; compare 18:16). Its king is among those struck (Joshua 12:10). But Joshua
15:63 records that *the children of Judah couldn't drive them out; but the Jebusites live with the
children of Judah at Jerusalem to this day*.

### Remaining with Benjamin — Judges 1:21

*The children of Benjamin did not drive out the Jebusites who inhabited Jerusalem, but the Jebusites
dwell with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day* (21). The same chapter says Judah *took*
Jerusalem and *set the city on fire* (1:8). The Hebrew of 1:21 is *lōʾ hôrîšû*, *they did not drive
out*. Joshua 15:63 has *lōʾ yāḵəlû … ləhôrîšām*, *they were not able to drive them out* (both WLC). The
texts give no account of how the Jebusites stayed, nor of how the capture in 1:8 relates to it.

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 10:15–16; 15:21; Numbers
13:29; Joshua 12:10; 15:8, 63; 18:16, 28; Judges 1:8, 21.
