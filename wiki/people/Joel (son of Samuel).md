# Joel (son of Samuel)

## Overview

### A judge in Beersheba — 1 Samuel 8:1–5

Joel is [[Samuel]]'s firstborn. When Samuel is old he makes his sons judges over Israel: *the name of his
firstborn was Joel; and the name of his second, Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba* (8:2). They *didn't
walk in his ways, but turned away after dishonest gain, took bribes, and perverted justice* (8:3), the two
things Deuteronomy forbids a judge, *You shall not pervert justice … You shall not take a bribe*
(Deuteronomy 16:19, stored WEB; the verbs *nāṭâ mišpāṭ* and *lāqaḥ šōḥaḏ* are shared, WLC). The elders cite
the sons as one of the two reasons for asking for a king (8:5). Joel is not said to do anything individually;
the charge is made against both brothers together, and neither is mentioned again in 1 Samuel. See
[[Israel Asks for a King]] and [[Not in His Ways - The Sons of Eli and the Sons of Samuel]].

**Name.** *Yôʾēl* (WLC), commonly glossed *Yahweh is God* (a conventional gloss, not verified here). In the
WLC files searched (Genesis through 2 Kings, Ruth, Psalms) the name occurs only at 1 Samuel 8:2. The prophet
of the book of Joel bears the same name and is a different man; this page is qualified by his father.

**Later.** 1 Chronicles 6:28 names *the sons of Samuel: the firstborn, Joel, and the second, Abijah*, and 6:33
makes Heman the singer *the son of Joel, the son of Samuel* (WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17; not yet
ingested). If that Joel is this one, Samuel's line continues in the temple singers. The Chronicles genealogy is
also the evidence for a Levite line discussed under [[Elkanah (father of Samuel)]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 8:1–5; Deuteronomy 16:19. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
