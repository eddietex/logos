# Nun

## Overview

### The Song of Moses — Deuteronomy 31:30–32:47

Nun is named only as Joshua’s father (44); the verse gives him no action or presence.

See [[The Song of Moses]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 31:30–32:47.


### The song as witness — Deuteronomy 31:14–23

Nun is named as Joshua's father in the commissioning notice (23). No action or attendance is
attributed to Nun.

See [[A Song to Witness Against Israel]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 31:14–23.

### The sentence and the ascent — Deuteronomy 1:34–46

Named as Joshua’s father (38); no action or presence is attributed to him.

See [[The Sentence and the Presumptuous Ascent]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:34–46; Numbers 14 and 20 for comparisons.

### The borders of the land — Numbers 34:1–29

Named as Joshua’s father in the appointment of the allotment officials (17); no action is assigned to Nun.

See [[The Borders of the Land]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 34.

### Joshua commissioned — Numbers 27:12–23

Named in Joshua's patronymic when Yahweh designates him (18); no action is attributed to Nun.

See [[Joshua Commissioned]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 27:12–23.


### The second census — Numbers 26:1–65

Named as Joshua’s father in the closing survival notice (65); no action is assigned to Nun.

See [[The Second Census]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 26.


Joshua’s father, named in Exodus 33:11. The verse identifies Joshua as Moses’ young servant
who remains at the tent; it attributes no action to Nun and does not say whether Nun is alive
or present. This page records the named relationship without supplying an unreported biography.
