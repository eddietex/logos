# Amram

## Overview

**The father of Moses, who in the story of his son's birth does one thing — marry — and is not
mentioned again.** *"A man of the house of Levi went and took a daughter of Levi as his wife"*
(Exodus 2:1). He has no name in the narrative; Exodus supplies it in the genealogy of Moses and
Aaron: [[Kohath]]'s son (6:18), who *"took Jochebed his father's sister to himself as wife; and she
bore him Aaron and Moses"*, and who lived *"a hundred and thirty-seven years"* (6:20).

**The birth narrative gives every act of rescue to women** — the mother, the sister, Pharaoh's
daughter — and the father vanishes after the first verse. Hebrews restores him: Moses *"was hidden
for three months by his parents"* (Hebrews 11:23), and Stephen says he was *"nourished three months
in his father's house"* (Acts 7:20). See [[The Birth of Moses]].

**His marriage is to his aunt**, on the literal reading of *"a daughter of Levi"* (2:1) that Numbers
26:59 adopts — a union Leviticus 18:12 forbids. See [[Jochebed]].
