# Mahalalel

## Overview

The fifth generation from Adam, and the only man in the line whose name is a sentence about God.
*Mahălalʾēl* is *"praise of God"*, or *"God shines"* if the first element is taken from *hālal* in
its sense of shining rather than boasting — either way, the divine element *ʾēl* is in it. He is
one of only two names in Genesis 5 built that way; the register otherwise runs on names meaning
*man*, *appointed*, *descent*, and the like.

That is all there is. His entry is the standard one: born to [[Kenan]] at 70, father of [[Jared]]
at 65, other sons and daughters, dead at 895 (5:12–17). His 895 years are the shortest of the eight
recorded deaths in the chapter, which is the sort of fact the register produces and never uses.

In Cain's line the nearest name is *Mehujael* (4:18) — close in sound, and likewise ending in
*-ʾēl*. See [[Two Lines from Adam - Cain's Genealogy and Seth's]]. He is named again at 1
Chronicles 1:2 and Luke 3:37.
