# Kish (father of Saul)

## Overview

A Benjamite, *a mighty man of valor*, *gibbôr ḥāyil* (1 Samuel 9:1, WLC), and the father of [[Saul]]. Qualified
because another Kish, an ancestor of Mordecai, appears at Esther 2:5 (not yet stored; cited by reference only).

### The lost donkeys — 1 Samuel 9:1–3

9:1 gives Kish a five-generation pedigree, *the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of
Aphiah, the son of a Benjamite*, one generation longer than Elkanah's at 1:1. The phrase *gibbôr ḥāyil* is said
of Gideon, Jephthah, and Boaz (Judges 6:12; 11:1; Ruth 2:1, WLC); the WEB renders it *a mighty man of wealth* for
Boaz, and whether it means valor or standing for Kish is not settled. His donkeys are lost, and he sends Saul with
one of the servants to look for them (9:3). He does not appear again in the pericope, but Saul's first words are
about him: *lest my father stop caring about the donkeys, and be anxious for us* (9:5). See
[[Saul Comes to the Seer]].

**Recorded, not resolved — Kish's father.** 1 Samuel 9:1 names Abiel. 1 Samuel 14:51 reads *Kish was the father
of Saul; and Ner the father of Abner was the son of Abiel* (stored WEB; not yet ingested). 1 Chronicles 8:33 and
9:39 read *Ner became the father of Kish* (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). Harmonizations (two
men named Kish; Ner as an earlier ancestor; a scribal slip) are recalled, not verified; the wiki does not choose
between them.

### Anxious for his son — 1 Samuel 10:2, 11

The first of Samuel's signs is news of Kish: *your father has stopped caring about the donkeys, and is anxious for
you, saying, "What shall I do for my son?"* (10:2). The fear Saul voiced at 9:5 has come true, with the same verb
*dāʾaḡ*, *be anxious* (WLC). When Saul prophesies, the people ask *What is this that has come to the son of Kish?*
(10:11), and someone answers *Who is their father?* (10:12). See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:1; 9:1–5; 10:2, 11–12; 14:51; Judges 6:12; 11:1; Ruth 2:1. 1 Chronicles 8:33; 9:39 checked at
bible-api.com (`translation=web`), 2026-11-17. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### *Saul the son of Kish* — 1 Samuel 10:21

At Mizpah the lot takes Benjamin, then the family of the [[Matrites]], and *then Saul the son of Kish was chosen*
(10:21). The step for Kish's household, which Joshua 7:14, 18 includes, is not narrated. The text does not say
whether Kish was present. See [[Saul Chosen by Lot at Mizpah]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 10:20–21; Joshua 7:14, 18.
