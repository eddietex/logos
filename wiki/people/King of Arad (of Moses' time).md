# King of Arad (of Moses' time)

## Overview

### The stages of the journey — Numbers 33:1–49

His hearing of Israel’s arrival is recalled (40). Numbers 21:1–3 supplies the attack and victory omitted from this brief itinerary notice.

See [[The Stages of the Journey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 33:1–49.

The unnamed Canaanite king associated with [[Arad]], living in [[The Negev]].
He attacks Israel after hearing of its approach by [[Atharim]] and takes captives
(Numbers 21:1). The subsequent defeat is told of the Canaanites collectively; the text
does not separately narrate his death. The qualifier identifies the narrative period,
not a proposed historical identity.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Joshua 12:14 counts *the king of Arad, one* among the kings *Joshua and the children of Israel struck* west of the Jordan. The link to this page is a pointer to the earlier account, not an identification: whether 12:14 means this king, a successor, or Moses' southern victory folded into Joshua's list is not stated. See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
