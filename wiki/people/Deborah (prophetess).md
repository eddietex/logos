# Deborah (prophetess)

## Overview

### The woman's hand fulfilled — Judges 4:21–22

*Yahweh will sell Sisera into a woman's hand* (4:9) is fulfilled by Jael, not Deborah: *Jael Heber's
wife took a tent peg, and took a hammer in her hand* (4:21). Deborah does not appear in 4:17–24. The
open question this page left at 4:9 is answered: the prophecy did not name the woman, and the woman
was not the prophet. Her word was fulfilled in a way Barak did not expect. See [[Jael]], [[Jael and Sisera]],
and [[Into a Woman's Hand - Judges 4.9, 4.21, and 9.53-54]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:9, 17–24.

*Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, judged Israel at that time* (Judges 4:4). She is the
fourth deliverer-figure of Judges, after Othniel, Ehud, and Shamgar, and the only one introduced
already at work. The others are *raised up* after Israel cries; she *was judging* (*šōpṭâ*, a
participle, WLC) when the cry came. She sits *under Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in
the hill country of Ephraim*, and *the children of Israel came up to her for judgment* (4:5). She is
the one judge in the book who hears cases.

She is also a prophet: the first in Judges, and the first woman called *prophetess* (*nəbîʾâ*) since
Miriam (Exodus 15:20), in the WLC files for Genesis through Judges and 1 Samuel. She summons Barak and
quotes Yahweh's command to him (4:6–7). When he will not go without her, she goes, and tells him that
*Yahweh will sell Sisera into a woman's hand* (4:9). She gives the signal for battle and the reason:
*this is the day … Hasn't Yahweh gone out before you?* (4:14). She does not fight. See
[[The Prophetess - Miriam and Deborah]].

**The woman of 4:9.** Her prophecy does not say which woman. She is the obvious candidate when it is
spoken, and the honor goes to Jael (4:17–22; see the section above).

**Her name.** *Dəbôrâ*, the ordinary Hebrew word for *bee*, the name of Rebekah's nurse (Genesis 35:8).
The two women are different and have different pages. The nurse was buried *below Bethel under the
oak*; the prophetess judged *under Deborah's palm tree* between Ramah and Bethel. The text does not
connect them. See [[Deborah (Rebekah's nurse)]] and [[Deborah's Palm Tree]].

**Her husband.** *ʾĒšet lappîdôt* (4:4, WLC). The WEB reads Lappidoth as a name. *Lappîd* is the word
for *torch*, and some read the phrase as *woman of torches*; that is reported, not verified.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-13.

## Timeline / key events

- Judges 4:4–5 — judging Israel under her palm.
- Judges 4:6–7 — summons Barak and gives him Yahweh's command.
- Judges 4:8–10 — agrees to go, foretells *a woman's hand*, and goes up with him to Kedesh.
- Judges 4:14 — gives the word for battle.
- Judges 5 — sings with Barak (not yet ingested).
