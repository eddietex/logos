# Amminadab

## Overview

**The father of Nahshon and of Elisheba, and the fourth name in the line of Perez.**

He is known only as a father. Exodus names him to place Aaron's wife: *Elisheba, the daughter of
Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon* (Exodus 6:23). Numbers names him five times, always in *Nahshon the son
of Amminadab* (Numbers 1:7; 2:3; 7:12, 17; 10:14). See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]] and
[[Elisheba Daughter of Amminadab - Aaron's Marriage into Judah]].

### Fourth in the generations of Perez — Ruth 4:19–20

*Ram became the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab became the father of Nahshon* (Ruth 4:19–20). The
genealogy places him between [[Ram (son of Hezron)]] and [[Nahshon]], four generations from Perez and six
above David. Through him the priestly house and the royal line touch: his daughter married [[Aaron]], and
his son's line runs to [[David]]. 1 Chronicles 2:10, Matthew 1:4, and Luke 3:33 keep him (WEB at
bible-api.com). See [[The Generations of Perez]].

**Other men of the name.** *ʿAmmînāḏāḇ* (WLC lemma 5992) also names a Levite in 1 Chronicles 6:7 and
15:10–11 (WLC search; not yet ingested). If that man gets a page, both take qualifiers.
