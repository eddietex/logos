# Shemuel

## Overview

Son of Ammihud, appointed to represent the tribe associated with [[Simeon]] in the
division of Canaan (Numbers 34:20). His commission is specified, but no speech,
individual allotment, or further biography is given here. The patronymic is retained
without identifying him with another bearer of the name.

The Hebrew name, *Šəmûʾēl* (WLC lemma 8050), is the name the WEB spells *Samuel* in 1 Samuel 1:20; see
[[Samuel]]. Nothing links the two men but the name. Source: Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-17.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 34.
