# The Hittites

## Overview

One of the peoples of Canaan. The Table of Nations makes Heth a son of Canaan (Genesis 10:15), and
Genesis calls the people *the children of Heth*. They are on the list of peoples whose land is promised
to Abram (15:20) and on nearly every later list of the land's nations (Exodus 3:8, 17; Deuteronomy 7:1;
20:17).

In Genesis they are neighbors with names. Abraham buys the cave of Machpelah from *Ephron the Hittite*
in the hearing of *the children of Heth* (23:10). Esau marries two Hittite women, *Judith, the daughter
of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, the daughter of Elon the Hittite* (26:34), and that marriage
grieves his parents. The spies report *The Hittite, the Jebusite, and the Amorite dwell in the hill
country* (Numbers 13:29). Joshua 1:4 uses *all the land of the Hittites* for the northern reach of the
promised border, and Judges 1:26 sends the man of Luz to build a new city *into the land of the
Hittites*. The Hittites of the lists and *the land of the Hittites* in the far north may not be the
same people; the text does not say, and the wiki does not decide.

Judges 3:5 puts the Hittites among the six peoples Israel *lived among*, and 3:6 says Israel married
their daughters. Esau's Hittite wives in Genesis 26:34–35 were the first such marriage in the wiki.
See [[Not from the Daughters of the Canaanites]].
