# Elkanah (father of Samuel)

## Overview

### A man of Ramathaim Zophim — 1 Samuel 1:1–28

*A certain man of Ramathaim Zophim, of the hill country of Ephraim, and his name was Elkanah, the son of
Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephraimite* (1:1). He has two wives,
[[Hannah]] and [[Peninnah]], and goes up *from year to year* to sacrifice at [[Shiloh]] (1:2–3). At the
sacrificial meal he gives Hannah a special portion *for he loved Hannah* (1:5); the WEB's *double
portion* renders an obscure phrase, *mānâ ʾaḥaṯ ʾappāyim* (WLC). His one attempt at comfort is a
question: *Am I not better to you than ten sons?* (1:8). He neither prays for her, as Isaac did for
Rebekah (Genesis 25:21), nor answers in anger, as Jacob did to Rachel (Genesis 30:2).

He is not present at Hannah's vow, but he goes up to offer *the yearly sacrifice, and his vow* (1:21) — a
vow the text has not recorded, or Hannah's made his own. When she asks to keep the child until he is
weaned, he agrees: *Do what seems good to you … only may Yahweh establish his word* (1:23), which is the
husband's confirming word that Numbers 30 provides for. *Elkanah knew Hannah his wife; and Yahweh
remembered her* (1:19). See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]] and
[[May Yahweh Establish His Word - Numbers 30 and 1 Samuel 1]].

**Ephraimite or Levite — two claims, flagged.** 1 Samuel 1:1 calls him *ʾep̄rāṯî*, which the WEB renders
*an Ephraimite*, and places his home in the hill country of Ephraim. 1 Chronicles 6:33–38 lists *Heman the
singer, the son of Joel, the son of Samuel, the son of Elkanah, the son of Jeroham, the son of Eliel, the
son of Toah, the son of Zuph* among the Kohathites, back to *Korah, the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath,
the son of Levi* (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). The two lists share Jeroham and Zuph;
*Elihu, Tohu* in 1 Samuel appear as *Eliel, Toah* in Chronicles. 1 Samuel never calls the family Levites.
A Levite could live in Ephraim (Joshua 21:5 gives Kohathites cities there), so the claims need not
conflict, but the text of 1 Samuel does not make that harmonization, and the wiki records both rather
than choosing. See [[Korah (son of Izhar)]].

**Name.** *ʾElqānâ* (WLC) is usually glossed *God has acquired* or *God has created*; this is a
conventional lexical gloss, not verified here. Exodus 6:24 names an Elkanah among *the sons of Korah*;
this page carries a qualifier because 1 Chronicles 6 has several Elkanahs.

**Home to Ramah.** After Hannah's song, *Elkanah went to Ramah to his house* (2:11), leaving the boy at
Shiloh. The verse names only him; Hannah is assumed to go with him. See [[The Song of Hannah]].

### Blessed by Eli — 1 Samuel 2:19–21

He goes up with Hannah *to offer the yearly sacrifice* (2:19). *Eli blessed Elkanah and his wife*: *May
Yahweh give you offspring from this woman for the petition which was asked of Yahweh* (2:20). The WLC
parses *šāʾal* in that clause as active masculine singular, *he asked*; some read the subject as Elkanah,
others impersonally, and the verse does not say. Hannah then bears three sons and two daughters (2:21).
See [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]].

## Timeline / key events

- Goes up yearly to Shiloh with his two wives — 1 Samuel 1:3–7.
- *Am I not better to you than ten sons?* — 1:8.
- Hannah conceives; Samuel is born — 1:19–20.
- Offers the yearly sacrifice *and his vow*; consents to Hannah's waiting — 1:21–23.
- Goes home to Ramah, leaving Samuel with Eli — 2:11.
- Blessed by Eli at the yearly sacrifice; five more children are born — 2:19–21.
