# Nahshon

## Overview

**The brother of Aaron's wife, and the head of the tribe of Judah in the wilderness.**

> **23** Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, as his wife … (Exodus
> 6:23)

**Exodus names him only to identify his sister.** In Numbers he is Judah's census head (1:7), the
*nāśîʾ* of Judah's camp on the east side (2:3), the first of the tribal heads to bring his offering at
the altar's dedication (7:12), and the commander of the camp that marches first (10:14) — Hebrew
checked in the WLC; Numbers 1 is ingested and the later chapters are not yet. **He is also in the royal line**: Ruth 4:20 and 1 Chronicles
2:10–11 put him between Amminadab and Salmon, five generations above David, and Matthew 1:4 and Luke
3:32 keep him (all by reference). See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]],
[[Elisheba Daughter of Amminadab - Aaron's Marriage into Judah]], and
[[Tamar in the Genealogies - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, and Matthew 1]].

### The census — Numbers 1:1–54

The census names him as Judah's man (7), son of Amminadab, and Judah's 74,600 is the largest figure (27). Numbers has now reached him; the references ahead (2:3; 7:12; 10:14) remain to be ingested.

See [[The Census in the Wilderness of Sinai]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 1.
