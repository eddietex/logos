# Abdon (son of Hillel)

## Overview

### The judge of Pirathon — Judges 12:13–15

*After him Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel. He had forty sons and thirty sons'
sons, who rode on seventy donkey colts. He judged Israel eight years. Abdon the son of Hillel the
Pirathonite died, and was buried in Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of the
Amalekites* (12:13–15).

The last of the five minor judges. His father and his town are given at his introduction and repeated
in full at his death. The name is qualified here because *Abdon* is also a Levitical city of Asher
(Joshua 21:30), the same word and lemma in the WLC (5658); see [[Abdon (city)]]. *Abdon* is often
linked to *ʿebed*, *servant*, and *Hillel* to *hll*, *to praise*; both derivations are reported, not
checked.

**Seventy riders.** Forty sons and thirty grandsons, *who rode on seventy donkey colts*. His is the only
notice of the five to count three generations. The *ʿăyārim*, *donkey colts*, are the animals of Jair's
thirty sons (10:4), and riding them marks standing (5:10). The number seventy recalls Gideon's seventy
sons (8:30) and Abimelech's seventy brothers killed *on one stone* (9:5). The text draws no comparison;
the echo is the wiki's observation.

**An Ephraimite judge after the fords.** Pirathon is *in the land of Ephraim*. His notice follows
directly on Ephraim's defeat at the Jordan, where forty-two thousand fell (12:6). The text does not
comment on the sequence. *In the hill country of the Amalekites* puts an Amalekite name inside
Ephraim, as the Song did (5:14). See [[Pirathon]] and [[Amalek]].

His notice is the last before the Philistine oppression and Samson (13:1).
