# The Two Spies Sent by Joshua

## Overview

The two unnamed men Joshua secretly sends from Shittim to inspect the land and Jericho (1). They receive Rahab’s protection and bind themselves to protect her household under stated conditions. They are a group page; no identification with named Israelites is implied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 2:1–24.

## Timeline / key events

- Are concealed on Rahab’s roof (1–8).
- Promise reciprocal protection and specify the scarlet sign, household boundary, and secrecy (14–20).
- Hide in the mountain three days, then cross back and report to Joshua (22–24).
