# The Hivites

## Overview

One of the peoples of Canaan, descended from Canaan in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:17), and a
fixed name in the lists of the land's nations from the promise at the burning bush on (Exodus 3:8,
17). Unlike most names on those lists, the Hivites have stories of their own. Hamor, prince of the
land at Shechem, and his son Shechem are Hivites (Genesis 34:2). One of Esau's wives, Oholibamah, is
the granddaughter of *Zibeon, the Hivite* (36:2). The Gibeonites who deceive Joshua are Hivites:
*The men of Israel said to the Hivites, "What if you live among us?"* (Joshua 9:7), and they are the
one city that *made peace with the children of Israel* (11:19). In the north, *the Hivite under Hermon
in the land of Mizpah* joins Jabin's coalition (11:3).

Judges 3 names them twice. Verse 3 puts *the Hivites who lived on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal Hermon
to the entrance of Hamath* among the nations Yahweh left *to test Israel*. Verse 5 puts them among the
six peoples Israel *lived among*, and verse 6 says Israel married their daughters. Hivites had made
both offers before. In Genesis 34 Hamor proposed it: *Give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for
yourselves* (34:9). The proposal ended in a massacre. In Joshua 9 the Gibeonites asked to live among Israel, and the
request ended in a sworn treaty. In Judges 3 Israel lives among them and marries them, and no story is
told of how.

Sources: stored WEB Genesis 10:17; 34:2, 9; 36:2; Exodus 3:8, 17; Joshua 9:7; 11:3, 19; Judges 3:3, 5–6.
