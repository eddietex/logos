# Shua's Daughter

## Overview

**[[Judah]]'s Canaanite wife, mother of all three of his first sons, named only as her father's
daughter.**

> **2** Judah saw there a certain Canaanite man named Shua’s daughter. He took her, and went in to her.
> (38:2)

**The Hebrew is *bat-šûaʿ*, and 1 Chronicles 2:3 reads it as a name** — *"Bath-shua the Canaanitess"*
— which is what happens to a patronymic left standing long enough. **Genesis never gives her one.** She
is *"Shua's daughter"* at 38:2 and *"Shua's daughter, the wife of Judah"* at 38:12, where she dies in
half a clause.

**Three verbs bring her into the book and none of them is hers.** *He saw*, *he took*, *he went in to
her* — **the same sequence Genesis uses at 6:2** of the sons of God and the daughters of men, and there
is no betrothal scene, no negotiation, no family, and no consent recorded. **Compare the four chapters
Genesis spends fetching [[Rebekah]].** The wiki notes the contrast and notes that the book passes no
verdict on either.

**She is the rule being broken.** *"You shall not take a wife for my son of the daughters of the
Canaanites"* (24:3), repeated by Isaac to Jacob at 28:1 — **and the line that produces David and the
genealogies of both Gospels begins with the son who ignores it**, through this woman's three sons and
then through [[Tamar]]. **Genesis reports the marriage in a sentence and never comes back to it**,
exactly as it reports Esau's Hittite wives and Joseph's Egyptian one. See
[[Not from the Daughters of the Canaanites]] and [[Marriage]].

**She names two of her three sons**, which is the ordinary pattern in Genesis and worth noticing here
only because **the three names are the chapter's first set of wordplays**: [[Er]], whose name is *raʿ*
reversed; [[Onan]], named for generative strength; [[Shelah (son of Judah)]], born at [[Chezib]],
*deception*. See [[Names and Naming]].

**Her death is what sets the plot going.** *"After many days, Shua's daughter, the wife of Judah,
died. Judah was comforted, and went up to his sheep shearers to Timnah"* (38:12) — **and it is the
mourning ending, not the mourning, that puts him on the road past [[Enaim]].** The verb is
***wayyinnāḥem***, *he was comforted*; **the previous chapter closed on his father refusing exactly
that** (37:35), and still refusing twenty-two years later.

## Timeline / key events

- Married by [[Judah]] at [[Adullam]]; *"he took her, and went in to her"* — Genesis 38:2,
  [[Judah and Tamar]]
- Bears [[Er]], [[Onan]], and [[Shelah (son of Judah)]] — Genesis 38:3–5
- Dies; Judah is comforted and goes up to [[Timnah]] — Genesis 38:12
- 1 Chronicles 2:3 — *"Bath-shua the Canaanitess"*, the patronymic hardened into a name

## Appears in

- [[Judah and Tamar]] — Genesis 38:2–5, 12; **four appearances, no words, and no name.** She is taken
  in three verbs, bears the three sons the chapter is about, and dies in the clause that starts the
  plot
