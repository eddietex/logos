# Ner

## Overview

### Abner's father, son of Abiel — 1 Samuel 14:50–51

*Abner the son of Ner, Saul's uncle* (14:50); *Kish was the father of Saul; and Ner the father of Abner was the son
of Abiel* (14:51). 1 Samuel 9:1 had made Kish *the son of Abiel*. Read together, Ner and Kish are brothers, and Ner
is Saul's father's brother, his *dôḏ* (WLC). The WEB's *Saul's uncle* in 14:50 can be read of Ner or of Abner. See
[[Saul's Wars and Household]].

**Recorded, not resolved — Chronicles.** 1 Chronicles 8:33 and 9:39 read *Ner became the father of Kish. Kish became
the father of Saul* (WEB at bible-api.com). On that reading Ner is Saul's grandfather, not his uncle. 1 Chronicles
9:36 also lists a Ner among the sons of Jeiel of Gibeon, beside a Kish (WEB at bible-api.com). Harmonizations (two
Ners; *father* for a more distant ancestor; a scribal slip) are recalled, not checked; the wiki does not choose. The
same tension is recorded on [[Kish (father of Saul)]]. Whether Ner is the unnamed uncle of 10:14–16 is not said;
see [[Saul's Uncle]].
