# Gaal

## Overview

### The newcomer at Shechem — Judges 9:26–41

*Gaal the son of Ebed came with his brothers, and went over to Shechem; and the men of Shechem put their
trust in him* (Judges 9:26). He arrives after God has *sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the men
of Shechem* (9:23). The text gives him no tribe, no town, and no reason for coming. He has *brothers*
with him, and nothing more is said of them.

At the vintage festival, in *the house of their god*, the men of Shechem eat, drink, and curse
Abimelech. Gaal makes the speech: *Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Isn't
he the son of Jerubbaal? Isn't Zebul his officer? Serve the men of Hamor the father of Shechem, but why
should we serve him? I wish that this people were under my hand! Then I would remove Abimelech* (9:28–29).
He calls the town back to its founders, the house of [[Hamor]] (Genesis 33:19; 34), and against the son
of the man who tore down Baal's altar. He wants what Abimelech wanted, one man ruling the town.

His father's name, *ʿEbed*, is the common Hebrew word for *servant* or *slave* (WLC). Whether that is the
narrator's irony, a *son of a slave* scorning the man Jotham called *the son of his female servant*
(9:18), or simply a name, is disputed (reported, not checked).

[[Zebul]], the city's ruler, reports him to Abimelech, and at dawn stands beside him at the gate while
Abimelech's men come down. *You see the shadow of the mountains as if they were men*, Zebul says (9:36).
When Gaal sees them clearly, Zebul throws his words back at him: *Now where is your mouth?* (9:38).
Gaal goes out *before the men of Shechem*, fights, flees, and is driven out with his brothers (9:39–41).
He is not mentioned again.

See [[The Fall of Abimelech]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 33:19; Judges 9:18, 23–41; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
