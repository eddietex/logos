# The Philistines

## Overview

A people of the southern coastal plain, ruled in Joshua and Judges by *five lords*. The Table of
Nations traces them to the Casluhim, *which the Philistines descended from* (Genesis 10:14). In
Genesis they are the people of Gerar: Abraham *lived as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines
many days* (21:34), and Isaac goes *to Abimelech king of the Philistines, to Gerar* (26:1), where
*The Philistines envied him* (26:14). At the Exodus, God does not lead Israel *by the way of the land
of the Philistines, although that was near*, lest they *see war* and turn back (Exodus 13:17); the
Song of the Sea has *the inhabitants of Philistia* tremble (15:14).

Joshua 13:2–3 puts *all the regions of the Philistines* and *the five lords of the Philistines* first
in *the land that still remains*, naming them by city: Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron. Judges
1:18 says Judah *took* Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron. Yet Judges 3:3 opens its list of the nations Yahweh
left *to test Israel* with *the five lords of the Philistines*. Both statements stand in the text; the
wiki does not decide how far Judah's taking reached. The tension is recorded on
[[The Nations Left to Test Israel]].

The Philistines are the enemy of Samson's story (Judges 13–16, not yet ingested), where *the lords of
the Philistines* come to Delilah (16:5).

This page covers the people. The region is [[The Land of the Philistines]]; the rulers of Genesis are
[[Abimelech (of Abraham's time)]] and [[Abimelech (of Isaac's time)]].
