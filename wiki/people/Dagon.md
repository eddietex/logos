# Dagon

## Overview

### *Dagon their god* — Judges 16:23–30

*The lords of the Philistines gathered them together to offer a great sacrifice to Dagon their god, and to
rejoice; for they said, "Our god has delivered Samson our enemy into our hand"* (16:23). The people praise
*their god* in the same words (24). Dagon is the god of the Philistines in the text, and the feast is held
in his house, which falls when Samson pulls down the pillars (25–30). The narrator never says Dagon did
anything; the claim that he delivered Samson is the Philistines'. The reader has been told that Samson was
given up by his own secret, a paid woman, and Yahweh's departure (16:17–20).

In the WLC files of Genesis through Judges, *dāgôn* (lemma 1712) as a god occurs only in Judges 16:23. The
name appears earlier inside the place-name Beth Dagon, *house of Dagon*, once in Judah's lowland and once on
Asher's border (Joshua 15:41; 19:27); those verses say nothing about worship there. See
[[Beth Dagon (Judah)]] and [[Beth Dagon (Asher)]].

What kind of god Dagon was, Judges does not say. A derivation from *dāg*, *fish*, is popular, and one from
*dāgān*, *grain*, is also offered; both are reported, not checked, and the wiki builds nothing on either.

The house's location is not named in 16:23–30; Samson is brought to it *out of the prison* (25), and the
prison is in Gaza (21). See [[Gaza]] and [[The Death of Samson]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 16:17–30; Joshua 15:41; 19:27; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.

### Fallen before the ark — 1 Samuel 5:1–7

The Philistines bring the captured ark *into the house of Dagon* at [[Ashdod]] and set it *by Dagon* (5:2).
The next morning Dagon is found *fallen on his face to the ground before Yahweh's ark*, and his people *set
him in his place again*; the morning after, he has fallen again, and *the head of Dagon and both the palms
of his hands were cut off on the threshold* (3–4). The WEB's *Only Dagon's torso was intact* renders *raq
dāḡôn nišʾar ʿālāyw*, *only Dagon was left on him* (WLC); readers who take the name from *dāḡ*, *fish*, read
it as the fish part, which depends on the derivation reported above. The priests of Dagon and his
worshippers do not step on the threshold *to this day* (5). The men of Ashdod say Yahweh's *hand is severe
on us, and on Dagon our god* (7). In the WLC files searched (Genesis through Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1
Kings, Psalms, Zephaniah), *dāḡôn* as a god occurs at Judges 16:23 and eleven times in 1 Samuel 5:2–7 (lemma
search). See [[The Ark Among the Philistines]] and [[Dagon Fallen - Samson's Pillars and the Ark in Ashdod]].

Ahead, 1 Chronicles 10:10 says the Philistines *fastened [Saul's] head in the house of Dagon* (WEB checked at
bible-api.com; not stored).

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 5:1–7; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

## Timeline / key events
- Credited by the Philistines with delivering Samson into their hand; a great sacrifice and feast held to
  him (Judges 16:23–24).
- His house falls on the lords and the people (16:30).
- Falls twice before the ark in his house at Ashdod; his head and hands cut off on the threshold (1 Samuel 5:2–5).
