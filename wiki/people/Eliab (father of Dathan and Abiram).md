# Eliab (father of Dathan and Abiram)

## Overview

### Remembered deeds and the rain-fed land — Deuteronomy 11:1–17

Named again only to identify Dathan and Abiram as "the sons of Eliab, the son of Reuben" (6). In this verse the lineage is Eliab directly to Reuben; Numbers 26:5–9 supplies Pallu between them, so "son of Reuben" here reads as descent, not immediate parentage.

See [[Remember His Deeds and Guard Your Hearts]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 11:1–17.

### The second census — Numbers 26:1–65

Named as Pallu’s son and father of Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram (8–9). Nemuel is added to the family
information of Numbers 16:1.

See [[The Second Census]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 26.


Named as the father of Dathan and Abiram in Numbers 16:1, 12. His sons are the actors; the chapter
supplies no action or judgment concerning Eliab himself. The qualifier distinguishes him from Eliab
son of Helon, the Zebulunite prince in Numbers 1:9.
