# Eldad

## Overview

One of two men left in the camp when the seventy elders went to the Tent. The Spirit rested on him there, and he
prophesied (Numbers 11:26). He is named only at Numbers 11:26–27 (tags), always with [[Medad]], and he says nothing that the
text records. He was *of those who were written*, on a list the chapter does not otherwise describe, and why he stayed
away is not given.

His case prompts Moses' wish. Joshua tells Moses to stop the two, and Moses answers, *I wish
that all Yahweh's people were prophets* (11:29). Eldad's importance is that the Spirit reached him away from the Tent and
outside the planned group, and that no one was allowed to stop him.

The meaning of the name is not stated in the text. No etymology was checked for this page.
