# Ministering Women (of Eli's time)

## Overview

### At the door of the Tent of Meeting at Shiloh — 1 Samuel 2:22

The unnamed women with whom Eli's sons *slept*: *the women who served at the door of the Tent of Meeting*
(1 Samuel 2:22). The Hebrew, *hannāšîm haṣṣōḇəʾôṯ peṯaḥ ʾōhel môʿēḏ* (WLC), repeats the participle and
place of Exodus 38:8, where *the ministering women who ministered at the door of the Tent of Meeting*
gave their mirrors for the basin; in the WLC files of Genesis through 2 Samuel these are the only two
verses with women as subject of *ṣāḇāʾ*. The verse does not say what their service was, whether they
consented, or whether they were wronged; it reports the act as part of what Eli *heard* of his sons. The
period qualifier separates them from [[Ministering Women (of Moses' time)]] without asserting continuity.
The clause is commonly reported to be absent from the Septuagint and 4QSamᵃ (recalled, not checked). See
[[The Sins of Eli's Sons]] and [[The Women Who Served at the Door - Exodus 38.8 and 1 Samuel 2.22]].
