# The Sidonians

## Overview

The people of [[Sidon (city)]], on the northern coast. Sidon is Canaan's firstborn in the Table of
Nations (Genesis 10:15). The Sidonians themselves are first named for a word: *The Sidonians call
Hermon Sirion* (Deuteronomy 3:9). Joshua 13 lists *Mearah that belongs to the Sidonians* (4) and ends
the land still remaining with *all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth Maim,
even all the Sidonians*, whom Yahweh says *I will drive … out* (6).

Judges 3:3 names *the Sidonians* among the nations Yahweh left *to test Israel*. They are not among the
six peoples of 3:5 whom Israel lived among. Judges 1:31 had already said Asher did not drive out the
inhabitants of Sidon.

Sources: stored WEB Genesis 10:15; Deuteronomy 3:9; Joshua 13:4, 6; Judges 1:31; 3:3.
