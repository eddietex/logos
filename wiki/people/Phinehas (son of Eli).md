# Phinehas (son of Eli)

## Overview

### Priest at Shiloh — 1 Samuel 1:3

Named once in 1 Samuel 1, with his brother: *The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, priests to Yahweh,
were there* (1:3). He bears the name of Aaron's grandson, [[Phinehas (son of Eleazar)]], the priest of the
covenant of peace (Numbers 25:11–13); the WLC spells both *Pînəḥās*. Whether Eli's line descends from that
Phinehas, 1 Samuel does not say. Eli's sons are *wicked men* (2:12), and both die *in one day* (2:34;
4:11; not yet ingested); this Phinehas's wife dies in childbirth naming her son Ichabod (4:19–22; not yet
ingested). See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:3; 2:12, 34; 4:11, 19–22; Numbers 25:11–13. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Wicked men who didn't know Yahweh — 1 Samuel 2:12–25

With his brother [[Hophni]], one of *the sons of Eli*, *wicked men* who *didn't know Yahweh* (2:12). The
pericope does not distinguish the brothers. Their servant seizes the worshippers' meat by fork and by
threat, before the fat is burned (2:13–16), *for the men despised the offering of Yahweh* (2:17); they lie
with the women who served at the door of the Tent of Meeting (2:22); and they ignore their father's rebuke,
*because Yahweh intended to kill them* (2:25). The name he shares with Aaron's grandson sharpens the
contrast: that Phinehas *was jealous with my jealousy* against an Israelite who brought a Midianite woman
before the congregation weeping *at the door of the Tent of Meeting* (Numbers 25:6–13), and this one lies
with women who serve at that door. The comparison is a reader's. See [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]] and
[[Two Priests Stop the Plague - Aaron and Phinehas]].
