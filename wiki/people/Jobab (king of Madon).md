# Jobab (king of Madon)

## Overview

King of Madon summoned by Jabin (1). The coalition’s kings are destroyed (12), but no individual death scene is given for him. He is distinct from [[Jobab]], the Edomite son of Zerah in Genesis 36:33–34.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Counted by city as *the king of Madon, one* (12:19). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
