# Zebul

## Overview

### Abimelech's officer in Shechem — Judges 9:28–41

Gaal names him first, as an insult: *Isn't he the son of Jerubbaal? Isn't Zebul his officer?* (Judges
9:28). *Officer* is *pāqîd*, a word that occurs in Genesis–Judges only here and at Genesis 41:34, for the
*overseers* Joseph advises Pharaoh to appoint (WLC). The narrator calls him *the ruler of the city*, *śar
hāʿîr* (9:30, WLC). Abimelech lives at [[Arumah]] (9:41), and Zebul holds Shechem for him.

When he hears Gaal's words, *his anger burned*. He sends messengers to Abimelech *craftily* (*bətormâ*,
read by some as *in Tormah*; reported, not checked), and he gives him the plan: come by night, lie in
wait in the field, rush the city at sunrise, and *do to them as you shall find occasion* (9:30–33).

At dawn he stands with Gaal at the gate. When Gaal sees men coming down from the mountains, Zebul says
*You see the shadow of the mountains as if they were men* (9:36). When Gaal sees them again, Zebul
drops the pretence: *Now where is your mouth, that you said, "Who is Abimelech, that we should serve
him?" Isn't this the people that you have despised? Please go out now and fight with them* (9:38). After
the battle he drives Gaal and his brothers out of the city (9:41). He is not mentioned again. The text
does not say whether he died in the city's destruction the next day (9:42–45).

See [[The Fall of Abimelech]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 41:34; Judges 9:28–45; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
