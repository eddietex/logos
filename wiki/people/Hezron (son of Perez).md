# Hezron (son of Perez)

## Overview

**A name in a subordinate clause, and the line from it runs to David and to Jesus.**

> **12** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, and Zerah; but Er and Onan died in the land of
> Canaan. The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul. (46:12)

**[[Judah]]'s branch is the only one in the register of the seventy that runs to a fourth generation**,
and Hezron and [[Hamul]] are it. **Genesis tells us nothing else about either of them** — no age, no
wife, no act, no death.

**Ruth ends on him.** *"Now this is the history of the generations of Perez"* (Ruth 4:18) begins a
ten-name genealogy that runs Perez — **Hezron** — Ram — Amminadab — Nahshon — Salmon — Boaz — Obed —
Jesse — **David**. **Matthew 1:3 and Luke 3:33 both carry him**, so the two New Testament genealogies
of Jesus pass through this clause. **Numbers 26:21 counts the Hezronites as a clan of Judah**, and
1 Chronicles 2 gives him three sons and builds most of its Judahite material on them.

**He is the wiki's standing example of what a register is for.** **The man is nothing but a link, and
the link is load-bearing.** See [[Kingship and Empire]] and
[[Tamar in the Genealogies - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, and Matthew 1]].

### The other Hezron

**[[Reuben]]'s third son, in the same register, has the same name** — *"the sons of Reuben: Hanoch,
Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi"* (46:9), with the Hezronites of Reuben counted at Numbers 26:6. **Two men
named Hezron enter Egypt in the same paragraph.** **That is why this page carries the qualifier**:
a bare `Hezron` would resolve to one of them by an unstated rule and the failure would be silent.
Reuben's Hezron has no page.
