# The Levite's Concubine

## Overview

### Bethlehem to Gibeah — Judges 19:1–30

She is never named and never speaks. She is *a concubine out of Bethlehem Judah*, *ʾiššâ pîleḡeš*, *a
woman, a concubine* (19:1, WLC), but her father is called the Levite's *father-in-law* and he the father's
*son-in-law* (19:4–5, 9): the text treats the union as a marriage. The narrator calls her *the young lady*,
*hannaʿărâ*, in her father's house (19:3–9).

*His concubine played the prostitute against him, and went away from him to her father's house* (19:2).
Some ancient versions are reported to read *she was angry with him* — reported, not checked. She stays
four months, until her husband comes *to speak kindly to her* (19:3).

At Gibeah the old man offers her with his own daughter — *Humble them, and do with them what seems good to
you* — and *the man laid hold of his concubine, and brought her out to them; and they had sex with her, and
abused her all night until the morning* (19:24–25). At dawn she comes back to the house *where her lord
was* and falls at the door, *with her hands on the threshold* (19:26–27). When he says *Get up*, *no one
answered* (19:28). Whether she was dead then the Hebrew does not say; the Greek is reported to add *for she
was dead* (not checked). He cuts her into twelve pieces and sends them through Israel (19:29).

The next chapter calls her *the woman who was murdered*, *hāʾiššâ hanniršāḥâ* (20:4, WLC), with the verb of
*You shall not murder* (Exodus 20:13), and her husband says *she is dead* (20:5). Her death starts the war
against Benjamin; she is not named in it again after verse 6. See [[The War Against Benjamin]]. The tradition the wiki has stored does not mourn her by name;
[[Jephthah's Daughter]] got four days a year (11:40), and she gets no notice at all. The wiki notes the
contrast; the text does not draw it.

Her story runs parallel to [[Lot's Daughters]], offered to the men of Sodom by their father's *do to them
what seems good to you* (Genesis 19:8); there the visitors intervened, and here nobody does. See
[[Gibeah and Sodom - Judges 19 as a Retelling]].

## Timeline / key events
- Taken as concubine from Bethlehem Judah; leaves for her father's house (19:1–2).
- Fetched by her husband after four months (19:3–10).
- Offered and put out to the men of Gibeah; abused all night (19:24–25).
- Found at the threshold at dawn; cut in twelve pieces (19:26–29).
- *The woman who was murdered* (20:4–6).
