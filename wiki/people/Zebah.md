# Zebah

## Overview

### King of Midian — Judges 8:5–21

One of *the kings of Midian*, always named first with [[Zalmunna]] (8:5, 12). Oreb and Zeeb were
*princes*, *śārîm* (7:25); Zebah and Zalmunna are *kings*, *malkê midyān* (8:5, 12, WLC). They are not
named until Gideon crosses the Jordan after them (8:4–5). They have escaped the rout with fifteen thousand
men, *all who were left of all the army of the children of the east* (8:10), and rest at [[Karkor]].
Gideon comes on the camp by the tent-dwellers' road while it *was secure*, and takes them (8:11–12).

The princes of Succoth use the kings' names to refuse Gideon bread, *Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna
now in your hand?* (8:6), and Gideon later shows the kings to Succoth (8:15). Then he asks about the men
they killed *at Tabor*, and they answer *They were like you. Each one resembled the children of a king*
(8:18). Those men were Gideon's brothers. Gideon swears *As Yahweh lives, if you had saved them alive, I
would not kill you* (8:19). When Gideon's son is too young to strike, the kings ask to die by the father:
*Rise and fall on us; for as the man is, so is his strength* (8:21). Gideon kills them and takes the
crescents from their camels.

*Zebaḥ* is spelled like the common noun *zebaḥ*, *sacrifice*; whether the name means that is not
settled, and the wiki adopts no meaning. Psalm 83:11 asks God to make Israel's enemies' *princes like Zebah
and Zalmunna* (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). See
[[The Day of Midian - Oreb and Zeeb in Psalm 83 and Isaiah 9-10]].

See [[Ephraim, Succoth, and Penuel]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 7:25; 8:4–21; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-15.
