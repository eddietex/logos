# Pharaoh's Daughter (of Moses' time)

## Overview

**The daughter of the king who ordered every Hebrew son drowned, who draws one out of the river,
spares him knowing exactly what he is, and makes him her son.** She is never named in Exodus. The
page takes the schema's narrative-keyed qualifier because Scripture has at least two other
daughters of a Pharaoh — Solomon's Egyptian wife (1 Kings 3:1) and *"Bithiah the daughter of
Pharaoh, whom Mered took"* (1 Chronicles 4:18) — and a bare title would collide.

**She is the decree's nearest neighbour and its undoing.** Her father is
[[Pharaoh (of the oppression)]], whose order was *"You shall cast every son who is born into the
river"* (Exodus 1:22). She *"came down to bathe at the river"* (2:5) — the same river — sees the basket, sends her
servant for it, opens it, sees the child, and hears him cry. Then: *"She had compassion on him, and
said, 'This is one of the Hebrews' children'"* (2:6). **The pity comes before the recognition in
the sentence, and the recognition does not reverse it.** Her verb is *ḥāmal*, *to spare* — the verb
of one who has the power to kill and does not. See [[The Birth of Moses]] and
[[Compassion and Mercy]].

**She accepts the sister's offer of a Hebrew nurse with one word, *"Go"*** (2:8), pays the nurse
wages from her father's house (2:9), and when the child is weaned *"he became her son"* — the
language of adoption — *"She named him Moses, and said, 'Because I drew him out of the water'"*
(2:10). **She is the only person in the passage who names anyone, and she names Israel's
deliverer.** See [[Adoption]] and [[Names and Naming]].

**Whether she knew the nurse was the mother the text does not say.** Readers have often assumed she
did and chose not to ask; the narrative leaves it open.

**She is the passage's Gentile rescuer, and the narrator gives her no motive but pity** — in a
chapter where every act of rescue belongs to a woman, she is the one who was supposed to be on the
other side. Later tradition honoured her: Josephus names her *Thermuthis* (*Antiquities* 2.9.5), and rabbinic
tradition identifies her with the *Bithiah* of 1 Chronicles 4:18, *daughter of Yah*, who married
into Judah. **Neither name is in Exodus, and the wiki does not adopt either.**

## Timeline / key events

- Comes down to bathe at the river; finds the basket among the reeds — Exodus 2:5,
  [[The Birth of Moses]]
- Opens it, sees the crying child, pities him, and recognizes him as a Hebrew — Exodus 2:6
- Accepts the sister's offer of a nurse; hires the child's mother — Exodus 2:7–9
- Receives the weaned child, adopts him, and names him *Moses* — Exodus 2:10
- *(ahead)* The son *"refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter"* — Hebrews 11:24
