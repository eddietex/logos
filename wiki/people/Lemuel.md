# Lemuel

## Overview

The king named in Proverbs 31:1 and addressed again in 31:4. The heading associates the
words with him and says his mother taught them to him. No kingdom, father, dynasty, date, or
biographical event is supplied. He is not identified with [[Solomon]] on this evidence.

His role is to receive instruction. His mother warns against dissipating royal strength and
against drink that could make a ruler forget the law. She ends with the duty to speak for the
mute and serve justice to the poor. Nothing in the passage reports whether Lemuel followed her
counsel. Source: stored WEB Proverbs 31:1–9.

## Timeline / key events

- Proverbs 31:1–2 — named as king and as the son taught by [[Lemuel's Mother]].
- Proverbs 31:3–7 — instructed about royal strength, drink, and impaired judgment.
- Proverbs 31:8–9 — charged to speak and judge for the poor and needy; no subsequent action is
  narrated.
