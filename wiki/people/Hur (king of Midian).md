# Hur (king of Midian)

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Named among the five chiefs of Midian, princes of Sihon, whom Moses struck (21). Numbers 31:8 calls the same five kings of Midian. The descriptions differ in title and political framing; neither text explains their precise relationship to Sihon.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

One of *the five kings of Midian* killed in the war against Midian (Numbers 31:8). He is named only in
the list — *Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba* — and no act or word of his is narrated. Joshua 13:21 lists
the same five as *the chiefs of Midian … the princes of Sihon* (WEB via bible-api.com, not yet
ingested); Numbers calls them kings, and the wiki keeps both titles.

The page is qualified because two other Hur pages exist: [[Hur (companion of Moses)]] and
[[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]]. Nothing connects this Midianite king with either.
