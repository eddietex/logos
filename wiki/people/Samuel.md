# Samuel

## Overview

### Asked of Yahweh, and given — 1 Samuel 1:19–28

Samuel is born to [[Hannah]] and [[Elkanah (father of Samuel)]] after Hannah's vow at Shiloh. *Yahweh
remembered her* (1:19); *she named him Samuel, saying, "Because I have asked him of Yahweh"* (1:20). The
explanation is a sound-play, not an etymology: *šəʾiltîw*, *I asked him*, from *šāʾal*, does not
obviously yield *Šəmûʾēl* (WLC), which is more often glossed *name of God* or *heard of God*
(conventional glosses, not verified here). The participle Hannah uses at the dedication, *šāʾûl*, *asked*
or *given* (1:28), has the consonants of Saul's name (9:2, WLC). See
[[Asked of Yahweh - Samuel's Name and Saul's]].

Hannah keeps him until he is weaned and then brings him to [[Eli]] at [[Shiloh]] to *appear before Yahweh,
and stay there forever* (1:22). The vow's terms are lifelong: *all the days of his life, and no razor shall
come on his head* (1:11). The Masoretic text does not call him a Nazirite; see
[[Nazirite by Vow, Nazirite from the Womb - Numbers 6, Judges 13, and 1 Samuel 1]]. *The child was
young*, literally *the child was a child* (1:24, WLC). See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]].

**Name.** [[Shemuel]], the Simeonite of Numbers 34:20, bears the same Hebrew name, *Šəmûʾēl* (WLC lemma
8050); the WEB spells it differently there. 1 Chronicles 6:28, 33 names Samuel's sons Joel and Abijah and
places him in a Levite line (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested); see
[[Elkanah (father of Samuel)]] for the Ephraimite-or-Levite question.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:11, 19–28; 9:2; Numbers 34:20. 1 Chronicles 6:28, 33 at bible-api.com,
2026-11-17. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

**Serving before Eli.** When his parents go home, *the child served Yahweh before Eli the priest* (2:11).
*Məšārēṯ* (WLC) is the participle of the verb for priestly ministry at the sanctuary (for example Numbers
3:6; Deuteronomy 10:8). His mother's song, sung over him, ends on *his king* and *his anointed* (2:10); he
will be the one who anoints the first two kings (10:1; 16:13, stored WEB; not yet ingested). See
[[The Song of Hannah]] and [[His King and His Anointed - Hannah's Song and David's]].

### Growing beside Eli's sons — 1 Samuel 2:18–26

The narrative alternates between Eli's sons and the boy. *But Samuel ministered before Yahweh, being a
child, clothed with a linen ephod* (2:18); the phrase *ḥāḡûr ʾēp̄ôḏ bāḏ*, *girded with a linen ephod*,
recurs in the WLC files of Genesis through 2 Samuel only of David before the ark (2 Samuel 6:14). Each year
his mother brings him *a little robe*, *məʿîl* (2:19, WLC), the noun of the robe Saul will tear (15:27) and
the robe the medium sees on the risen old man (28:14) (stored WEB; not yet ingested). *The child Samuel
grew before Yahweh* (2:21), literally *with* Yahweh (*ʿim-YHWH*, WLC), and *grew on, and increased in
favor both with Yahweh, and also with men* (2:26). Eli's sons *didn't know Yahweh* (2:12); Samuel *didn't
yet know Yahweh* (3:7, stored WEB; not yet ingested). Eli's question, *who will intercede for him?* (2:25),
finds its answer later in Samuel himself, who prays for Israel at Mizpah (7:5) and calls it sin to cease
praying for them (12:23) (stored WEB; not yet ingested). See [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]],
[[Favor with God and Man - Proverbs 3.4, 1 Samuel 2.26, and Luke 2.52]], and
[[They Didn't Know Yahweh - Pharaoh, the Generation After Joshua, and Eli's Sons]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:12, 18–26; 3:7; 7:5; 12:23; 15:27; 28:14. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### The faithful priest? — 1 Samuel 2:35

Samuel is not named in the man of God's oracle, but he is one of the usual candidates for its *faithful
priest*, *kōhēn neʾĕmān*, who *will do according to that which is in my heart* (2:35). He is growing at
the same altar (2:18, 26), and at 3:20 all Israel knows he *was established to be a prophet of Yahweh*,
where *established* is the same *neʾĕmān* (WLC; stored WEB; not yet ingested). Against the reading, his own
sons *didn't walk in his ways* (8:3), and his house is not made *sure*; Zadok, installed *in the place of
Abiathar* (1 Kings 2:35, WEB checked at bible-api.com), is the other common identification. The wiki
records both. See [[A Man of God Against the House of Eli]] and
[[A Sure House - The Faithful Priest and the House of David]].

2026-11-17. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Called at Shiloh — 1 Samuel 3:1–4:1a

*The child Samuel ministered to Yahweh before Eli* in days when *Yahweh's word was precious* (3:1). Lying in
*Yahweh's temple, where God's ark was*, before *God's lamp* had gone out, he hears his name and runs to Eli
three times: *Here I am; for you called me* (3:3–8). The narrator explains: *Samuel didn't yet know Yahweh,
neither was Yahweh's word yet revealed to him* (3:7), *ṭerem*, *not yet*, where Eli's sons simply *didn't
know Yahweh* (2:12, WLC). Eli teaches him the answer; when *Yahweh came, and stood, and called as at other
times, "Samuel! Samuel!"*, he says *Speak; for your servant hears* (3:9–10), without the *Yahweh* of Eli's
version (WLC). He says *hinnēnî* to the first single call and to Eli, not to the doubled call (3:4–8, 16);
see [[Jacob, Jacob - The Doubled Name and Hinneni]], corrected on this point.

His first word from Yahweh is the sentence on the house that raised him: *I will perform against Eli all
that I have spoken concerning his house* (3:11–14). He is *afraid to show Eli the vision*, opens the doors
of Yahweh's house in the morning as usual, and when Eli binds him with an oath tells him *every bit*
(3:15–18). Then the summary of a career: *Samuel grew, and Yahweh was with him, and let none of his words
fall to the ground*; *all Israel from Dan even to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established*, *neʾĕmān*,
*to be a prophet of Yahweh*; and *the word of Samuel came to all Israel* (3:19–4:1a, WLC). It is the first
*nāḇîʾ*, *prophet*, in 1 Samuel. *Neʾĕmān* is the word of the *faithful priest* of 2:35, which keeps
Samuel among the candidates for that figure; see [[A Sure House - The Faithful Priest and the House of David]].
See [[Yahweh Calls Samuel]] and
[[Both His Ears Will Tingle - 1 Samuel 3.11, 2 Kings 21.12, and Jeremiah 19.3]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:12; 3:1–4:1. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Absent from the battle — 1 Samuel 4:1b–22

The verse that closes his call, *The word of Samuel came to all Israel* (4:1a), is followed at once by
Israel going to war, and Samuel is not mentioned again until 7:3 (stored WEB). No one in chapter 4 asks
Yahweh or his prophet before the elders send for the ark (4:3). The defeat, the ark's capture, and the deaths
of Eli and his sons are the fulfilment of the word Samuel himself received (3:11–14). See
[[The Ark Is Captured]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 3:11–14; 4:1–22; 7:3.

### Judge at Mizpah — 1 Samuel 7:2–17

After *twenty years* with the ark at Kiriath Jearim, Samuel speaks for the first time since his call: *If you
are returning to Yahweh with all your heart, then put away the foreign gods and the Ashtaroth … and serve him
only* (7:3). Israel does, and he gathers them to Mizpah: *I will pray to Yahweh for you* (7:5). They fast and
confess, and *Samuel judged the children of Israel in Mizpah* (7:6), the first time *šāp̄aṭ* is used of him
(WLC). When the Philistines come up, the people beg him *Don't stop crying to Yahweh our God for us* (7:8). He
offers a suckling lamb as *a whole burnt offering*, *cried to Yahweh for Israel; and Yahweh answered him* with
thunder (7:9–10). He names the stone of victory *Ebenezer*, *Yahweh helped us until now* (7:12). The chapter
gives him every office at once: prophet, intercessor, priest at the altar, and judge. It is the first answer in
the book to Eli's question, *who will intercede for him?* (2:25), and it ends with the notice of a judge's
career: *Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life*, on a yearly circuit of Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah,
from his house at Ramah, where *he built an altar to Yahweh* (7:15–17). He offers sacrifice in person here and
the narrator raises no question of descent; see [[Elkanah (father of Samuel)]] for the Ephraimite-or-Levite
question. See [[Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah]],
[[He Will Thunder Against Them - Hannah's Song and Mizpah]], and
[[Between Ramah and Bethel - Deborah and Samuel as Judges]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:25; 7:2–17. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Old, with sons who do not walk in his ways — 1 Samuel 8:1–22

*When Samuel was old, he made his sons judges over Israel* (8:1): [[Joel (son of Samuel)]] and
[[Abijah (son of Samuel)]], at Beersheba. They *turned away after dishonest gain, took bribes, and perverted
justice* (8:3). The elders come to him at Ramah with both facts, *you are old, and your sons don't walk in
your ways*, and ask for *a king to judge us like all the nations* (8:5). Like Eli, *very old* when he heard
what his sons were doing (2:22), Samuel is introduced in old age beside sons who abuse their office; unlike
Eli, he is not rebuked for them. See [[Not in His Ways - The Sons of Eli and the Sons of Samuel]].

*The thing displeased Samuel*, literally *was evil in Samuel's eyes* (8:6, WLC), and he prays. Yahweh's
answer takes the hurt out of his hands, *they have not rejected you, but they have rejected me as the king
over them* (8:7), and tells him three times to *listen to their voice* (7, 9, 22). Samuel is the go-between
in both directions: he tells *all Yahweh's words to the people who asked him for a king* (10), and *rehearsed*
the people's words *in the ears of Yahweh* (21). His warning, *the way of the king*, is a list of what a king
takes (11–17); in his farewell he will ask the people to witness that he has taken nothing (12:3, stored WEB;
not yet ingested). He ends the assembly without naming a king: *Everyone go to your own city* (22). See
[[Israel Asks for a King]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:22; 8:1–22; 12:3. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

## Timeline / key events

- Born after Hannah's vow; named *because I have asked him of Yahweh* — 1 Samuel 1:20.
- Weaned, and given to Yahweh at Shiloh under Eli — 1:22–28.
- Stays at Shiloh and *served Yahweh before Eli the priest* — 2:11.
- Ministers in a linen ephod; grows *with Yahweh and with men* beside Eli's sons — 2:18–26.
- Not named, but one common identification of the man of God's *faithful priest* — 2:35.
- Called four times by Yahweh at night in the temple at Shiloh; receives and tells the word against Eli's house; known *from Dan even to Beersheba* as a prophet — 3:1–4:1a.
- Absent from the narrative of the ark's capture, which fulfils the word given to him — 4:1b–22.
- Calls Israel to put away the Baals and the Ashtaroth; prays at Mizpah; offers a whole burnt offering; Yahweh thunders; sets up Ebenezer; judges Israel all his life from Ramah — 7:2–17.
- Old, appoints his sons judges at Beersheba; the elders ask for a king; he prays, is told to listen, warns of *the way of the king*, and sends the people home — 8:1–22.
