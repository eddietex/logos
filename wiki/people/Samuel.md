# Samuel

## Overview

### Asked of Yahweh, and given — 1 Samuel 1:19–28

Samuel is born to [[Hannah]] and [[Elkanah (father of Samuel)]] after Hannah's vow at Shiloh. *Yahweh
remembered her* (1:19); *she named him Samuel, saying, "Because I have asked him of Yahweh"* (1:20). The
explanation is a sound-play, not an etymology: *šəʾiltîw*, *I asked him*, from *šāʾal*, does not
obviously yield *Šəmûʾēl* (WLC), which is more often glossed *name of God* or *heard of God*
(conventional glosses, not verified here). The participle Hannah uses at the dedication, *šāʾûl*, *asked*
or *given* (1:28), has the consonants of Saul's name (9:2, WLC). See
[[Asked of Yahweh - Samuel's Name and Saul's]].

Hannah keeps him until he is weaned and then brings him to [[Eli]] at [[Shiloh]] to *appear before Yahweh,
and stay there forever* (1:22). The vow's terms are lifelong: *all the days of his life, and no razor shall
come on his head* (1:11). The Masoretic text does not call him a Nazirite; see
[[Nazirite by Vow, Nazirite from the Womb - Numbers 6, Judges 13, and 1 Samuel 1]]. *The child was
young*, literally *the child was a child* (1:24, WLC). See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]].

**Name.** [[Shemuel]], the Simeonite of Numbers 34:20, bears the same Hebrew name, *Šəmûʾēl* (WLC lemma
8050); the WEB spells it differently there. 1 Chronicles 6:28, 33 names Samuel's sons Joel and Abijah and
places him in a Levite line (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested); see
[[Elkanah (father of Samuel)]] for the Ephraimite-or-Levite question.

**Serving before Eli.** When his parents go home, *the child served Yahweh before Eli the priest* (2:11).
*Məšārēṯ* (WLC) is the participle of the verb for priestly ministry at the sanctuary (for example Numbers
3:6; Deuteronomy 10:8). His mother's song, sung over him, ends on *his king* and *his anointed* (2:10); he
will be the one who anoints the first two kings (10:1; 16:13, stored WEB; not yet ingested). See
[[The Song of Hannah]] and [[His King and His Anointed - Hannah's Song and David's]].

### Growing beside Eli's sons — 1 Samuel 2:18–26

The narrative alternates between Eli's sons and the boy. *But Samuel ministered before Yahweh, being a
child, clothed with a linen ephod* (2:18); the phrase *ḥāḡûr ʾēp̄ôḏ bāḏ*, *girded with a linen ephod*,
recurs in the WLC files of Genesis through 2 Samuel only of David before the ark (2 Samuel 6:14). Each year
his mother brings him *a little robe*, *məʿîl* (2:19, WLC), the noun of the robe Saul will tear (15:27) and
the robe the medium sees on the risen old man (28:14) (stored WEB; not yet ingested). *The child Samuel
grew before Yahweh* (2:21), literally *with* Yahweh (*ʿim-YHWH*, WLC), and *grew on, and increased in
favor both with Yahweh, and also with men* (2:26). Eli's sons *didn't know Yahweh* (2:12); Samuel *didn't
yet know Yahweh* (3:7, stored WEB; not yet ingested). Eli's question, *who will intercede for him?* (2:25),
finds its answer later in Samuel himself, who prays for Israel at Mizpah (7:5) and calls it sin to cease
praying for them (12:23) (stored WEB; not yet ingested). See [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]],
[[Favor with God and Man - Proverbs 3.4, 1 Samuel 2.26, and Luke 2.52]], and
[[They Didn't Know Yahweh - Pharaoh, the Generation After Joshua, and Eli's Sons]].

### The faithful priest? — 1 Samuel 2:35

Samuel is not named in the man of God's oracle, but he is one of the usual candidates for its *faithful
priest*, *kōhēn neʾĕmān*, who *will do according to that which is in my heart* (2:35). He is growing at
the same altar (2:18, 26), and at 3:20 all Israel knows he *was established to be a prophet of Yahweh*,
where *established* is the same *neʾĕmān* (WLC; stored WEB; not yet ingested). Against the reading, his own
sons *didn't walk in his ways* (8:3), and his house is not made *sure*; Zadok, installed *in the place of
Abiathar* (1 Kings 2:35, WEB checked at bible-api.com), is the other common identification. The wiki
records both. See [[A Man of God Against the House of Eli]] and
[[A Sure House - The Faithful Priest and the House of David]].

## Timeline / key events

- Born after Hannah's vow; named *because I have asked him of Yahweh* — 1 Samuel 1:20.
- Weaned, and given to Yahweh at Shiloh under Eli — 1:22–28.
- Stays at Shiloh and *served Yahweh before Eli the priest* — 2:11.
- Ministers in a linen ephod; grows *with Yahweh and with men* beside Eli's sons — 2:18–26.
- Not named, but one common identification of the man of God's *faithful priest* — 2:35.
