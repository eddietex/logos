# Ishvi (son of Saul)

## Overview

### Second of Saul's sons — 1 Samuel 14:49

*Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchishua* (14:49). *Yišwî* (WLC) is named only here, in the
summary of Saul's reign, and does nothing. Qualified because an Ishvi son of Asher appears in Genesis 46:17 and
Numbers 26:44 (stored WEB). See [[Saul's Wars and Household]].

**Recorded, not resolved — who Ishvi is.** Three other lists of Saul's sons do not have his name. At Gilboa the
Philistines kill *Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul* (31:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested).
1 Chronicles 8:33 and 9:39 list *Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal* (WEB at bible-api.com). Abner makes
*Ishbosheth the son of Saul* king (2 Samuel 2:8, WEB at bible-api.com). Ishvi may be another name for Abinadab, or
for Ishbosheth/Eshbaal, or a son not otherwise recorded. Each identification is proposed in the literature
(recalled, not checked); the text does not make one, and the wiki does not.
