# Malchishua

## Overview

### Third of Saul's sons — 1 Samuel 14:49

*Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchishua* (14:49). The name is written as two words in the WLC,
*malkî-šûaʿ* (14:49; 31:2). He does nothing in the summary. He is one of the three sons the Philistines kill with
Saul at Gilboa, *Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua* (31:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested), and the only son besides
Jonathan who is in all four lists of Saul's sons (14:49; 31:2; 1 Chronicles 8:33; 9:39, WEB at bible-api.com). The
gloss *my king is salvation* or *my king is wealth* is commonly given (recalled, not checked against a lexicon).
See [[Saul's Wars and Household]].
