# Shelah

## Overview

The third generation after the flood, and one of the emptiest entries in Scripture: a father, a son,
two numbers, and nothing else.

*"Shelah lived thirty years, and became the father of Eber. Shelah lived four hundred three years
after he became the father of Eber, and became the father of more sons and daughters"* (Genesis
11:14–15). He appears once before that, in [[The Table of Nations]] at 10:24, in the same position
and without the numbers. He is in the Chronicler's list (1 Chronicles 1:18, 24) and in Luke's
genealogy of Jesus (Luke 3:35).

**The name.** *Šelaḥ* is from a root meaning *to send*; as a noun it can mean a missile or weapon,
and it is the second element of *Metûšelaḥ*, [[Methuselah]] — one of several near-echoes between the
two ten-generation registers. It is also the name of the pool of Siloam (*šilōaḥ*, "sent", the
conduit; Nehemiah 3:15, John 9:7) and, in a different vocalization, of Judah's third son in Genesis
38. None of these is the same person, and Genesis draws no connection between any of them.

**The two 403s.** His remainder of four hundred three years is identical to his father
[[Arpachshad]]'s — the only repeated figure in the register apart from Peleg's and Reu's matching
239s. Genesis 11 has four pairs of identical or near-identical numbers in nine entries, which is
part of why the figures have been read as schematic rather than as raw record.

**The Septuagint makes him a grandson rather than a son of Arpachshad**, inserting Cainan between
them, and Luke 3:36 follows the Greek. The Hebrew text has him fathered by Arpachshad directly, both
here and at 10:24.

On the register's arithmetic he lives from 1693 to 2126 from creation — dying three years after
Abraham, whom he outlives by the length of one of his own descendants' childhoods.
