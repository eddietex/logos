# Adah (wife of Esau)

## Overview

**Esau's Hittite wife in the register of Genesis 36, and the same woman Genesis 26:34 calls
Basemath** — or a different woman entirely, depending on which of three explanations you take.

> **2** Esau took his wives from the daughters of Canaan: Adah the daughter of Elon, the Hittite …
> **4** Adah bore to Esau Eliphaz. …

**The patronymic is what holds the two lists together.** [[Elon the Hittite]] has a daughter in both
registers, and only the daughter's name changes:

| father | **Genesis 26:34; 28:9** | **Genesis 36:2–3** |
|---|---|---|
| Elon the Hittite | **Basemath** | **Adah** |
| Beeri the Hittite | **Judith** | — |
| Anah / Zibeon | — | **Oholibamah** |
| Ishmael | **Mahalath** (28:9) | **Basemath** |

**The difficulty is not that Elon's daughter has two names — it is that the spare name is in use.**
*Basemath* is attached to [[Ishmael]]'s daughter in this chapter and to Elon's in the last one, so a
two-names-per-woman harmonisation has to move the name *Basemath* from one woman to another between
the registers. **The wiki flags the disagreement and adopts none of the explanations**; the full
treatment is at [[The Names of Esau's Wives - Genesis 26 and Genesis 36]], and the existing page
[[Basemath (daughter of Elon)]] holds the same woman under the earlier register's name.

***ʿĀdâ*** means **ornament**, from *ʿādâ*, to adorn — and *Basemath* means **spice** or **balsam**.
Both are names of the perfumed-luxury kind, which is at least consistent.

## What she is in the chapter

**The mother of the senior branch.** Her son [[Eliphaz (son of Esau)]] is Esau's firstborn, and his
line produces seven of the fourteen chiefs at 36:15–16 — half the register, against four from
[[Reuel (son of Esau)]] and three from [[Oholibamah]]. **[[Amalek]] is counted among her sons**
(36:12) though [[Timna]] bore him, which is the ordinary reckoning of a concubine's child to the
wife's line. See [[The Descendants of Esau]].

**She is also, with Oholibamah, the reason the chapter's opening sentence is a verdict.** *"Esau took
his wives from the daughters of Canaan"* — the marriage [[Abraham]] made a servant swear against at
24:3 and [[Rebekah]] used at 27:46 to get her other son out of the country. See
[[Not from the Daughters of the Canaanites]] and [[Isaac in Gerar]].

## A note on the filename

**There is another Adah in Genesis** — the first of the two wives [[Lamech (son of Methushael)]] takes
at 4:19, mother of Jabal and Jubal, and one of the first two women named in the Bible after Eve. She
has no page yet; **if a later pass makes one it must be `Adah (wife of Lamech)`**, so that neither
page holds the bare name. Both qualifiers are required by the same rule, and the failure they prevent
is silent at both ends: `scripts/link-check.sh` resolves by basename and would pass two files called
`Adah.md`, while Obsidian would pick one of them by an unstated rule.

## Timeline / key events

- Married by Esau in Canaan, daughter of [[Elon the Hittite]] — Genesis 36:2; called *Basemath* at
  Genesis 26:34
- Grieves Isaac's and Rebekah's spirits, with the other Hittite wife — Genesis 26:35
- Bears [[Eliphaz (son of Esau)]] in the land of Canaan — Genesis 36:4–5
- Moves with the household to the hill country of Seir — Genesis 36:6–8
- Her line reckoned at seven chiefs, [[Amalek]] among them — Genesis 36:15–16

## Appears in

- [[The Descendants of Esau]] — Genesis 36:2, 4, 10, 12, 16; **named four times in one chapter and
  differently from the way the same woman was named ten chapters earlier.** The mother of the senior
  Edomite branch, and half the chief-list descends from her
- [[Isaac in Gerar]] — Genesis 26:34–35, under the name **Basemath**; taken at forty with no journey,
  no servant, and no oath, and reported in one sentence with its effect on his parents in the next
