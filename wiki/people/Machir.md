# Machir

## Overview

**The last person named in Genesis.**

> **23** Joseph saw Ephraim's children to the third generation. The children also of Machir, the son
> of Manasseh, were born on Joseph's knees. (50:23)

**[[Manasseh]]'s son and [[Joseph]]'s grandson**, named once in the book and never given a word, an
act, or a description. **What Genesis records about him is that his children were held by his
grandfather**, and then the book ends three verses later.

**The name means roughly *sold* or *bought*** — the root *mākar*, **which is the verb Genesis used
of his grandfather at 37:28**, *"they sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty pieces of silver"*,
and which the brothers repeat back at 45:4–5. **The wiki notes the coincidence and does not build
on it**: Genesis supplies no etymology for this name, as it does for [[Manasseh]] and [[Ephraim]]
one chapter earlier (41:51–52), and a name-meaning the text does not gloss is not the text's point.
See [[Names and Naming]].

***"Born on Joseph's knees."*** ***ʿAl-birkê yōsēp*** — **the adoption idiom, and Genesis uses it
exactly twice**: here, and at 30:3, where [[Rachel]] gives [[Bilhah]] to [[Jacob]] *"that she may
bear on my knees, and I also may obtain children by her"* (in [[The Birth of Jacob's Children]]).
**Whether the gesture at 50:23 is legal adoption or a great-grandfather's lap, Genesis does not
say**, and the two uses are far enough apart in tone that the wiki reports the idiom rather than
deciding what it does here. See [[Adoption]].

## What becomes of him

**Genesis leaves him an infant's father in Egypt. The rest of the Torah makes him a clan and then a
territory**, and none of it is in this book:

- **Numbers 26:29 makes him the only son of Manasseh**, and the whole tribe descends through him
- **Numbers 32:39–40** — *"The children of Machir the son of Manasseh went to Gilead, took it, and
  dispossessed the Amorites who were therein"* — **and Moses gives them Gilead**
- **Joshua 17:1** calls him *"the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead, because he was a man
  of war"*
- **Judges 5:14**, in the Song of Deborah, has *"out of Machir governors came down"* — **the clan
  name standing in for the tribe two centuries later**

**The last name in a book about a promised land belongs to the family that settles east of the
Jordan, outside it.** **Genesis does not know that and the wiki records the irony as an irony**, not
as anything the chapter is doing.
