# Baal Peor

## Overview

The deity to whom Israel joins itself in Numbers 25:3, 5. Sacrificial meals and bowing to the gods
frame this allegiance (1–3). This page follows the existing convention of person pages for named
deities such as Chemosh; it does not identify Baal Peor with Chemosh or with the height [[Peor]].

Deuteronomy 4:3–4 recalls the judgment: all the men who followed Baal Peor were destroyed, while
those who held fast to Yahweh are alive. Its *because of Baal Peor* could name either the deity or
the incident; Deuteronomy does not separate the executions of Numbers 25:4–5 from the plague of
25:9.
