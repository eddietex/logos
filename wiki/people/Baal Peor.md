# Baal Peor

## Overview

The deity to whom Israel joins itself in Numbers 25:3, 5. Sacrificial meals and bowing to the gods
frame this allegiance (1–3). This page follows the existing convention of person pages for named
deities such as Chemosh; it does not identify Baal Peor with Chemosh or with the height [[Peor]] .
