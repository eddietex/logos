# The Amorites

## Overview

### Lived among — Judges 3:1–6

Third of the six peoples Israel *lived among* (5). Israel marries their daughters and serves their gods
(6). The list is in the order of Exodus 3:8, 17.

See [[The Nations Left to Test Israel]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:1–6.

A people of the land, named on most of the Pentateuch's lists of nations to be driven out, and
sometimes used for the land's inhabitants generally. The page was created at Judges 1:22–36, where the
Amorites are the last actors in the chapter. No individual Amorite is named in that pericope.

**Descent.** The Table of Nations lists *the Amorites* among Canaan's descendants (Genesis 10:16).

**The iniquity of the Amorite.** Yahweh tells Abram that his descendants will return in the fourth
generation, *for the iniquity of the Amorite is not yet full* (Genesis 15:16). Here *the Amorite*
stands for the land's population as a whole.

**Kings and gods.** Sihon is *king of the Amorites* east of the Jordan (Numbers 21:21). West of it,
*the five kings of the Amorites*, Jerusalem's, Hebron's, Jarmuth's, Lachish's, and Eglon's, go up against
Gibeon (Joshua 10:5), and Joshua's sun-and-moon prayer is spoken *in the day when Yahweh delivered up the
Amorites* (10:12). At Shechem, Joshua sets *the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you dwell* beside
the gods beyond the River as the alternative to Yahweh (Joshua 24:15). The spies place them *in the
hill country* (Numbers 13:29).

### Pressing Dan — Judges 1:34–36

*The Amorites forced the children of Dan into the hill country, for they would not allow them to come
down to the valley* (34). The verb is *lāḥaṣ*, *to press, oppress* (WLC). In this chapter it is the
only case of the land's people acting against a tribe rather than being left. *The Amorites would dwell
in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim* (35), the same *yāʾal*, *determined*, as the Canaanites in
1:27. Then *the hand of the house of Joseph prevailed*, and they became forced laborers. The chapter
ends on *the border of the Amorites … from the ascent of Akrabbim, from the rock, and upward* (36). That
places Amorites in the far south as well as near Dan, and the text does not explain it. A reading
*Edomite* here is reported elsewhere; it has not been checked.

Hill country and valley are reversed from the spies' report. In Numbers 13:29 the Amorite is in the hill
country; in Judges 1:34 the Amorites hold the valley and keep Dan in the hills.

See [[The Tribes That Did Not Drive Out]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 10:16; 15:16; Numbers 13:29;
21:21; Joshua 10:5, 12; 24:15; Judges 1:34–36. Hebrew of Judges 1:34–36 read in the WLC.
