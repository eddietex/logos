# Er

## Overview

**[[Judah]]'s firstborn, given a wife and a verdict, and nothing else.**

> **6** Judah took a wife for Er, his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.
> **7** Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in Yahweh’s sight. Yahweh killed him. (38:6–7)

**No offence is specified.** Genesis says ***raʿ bəʿênê YHWH*** — *evil in the eyes of Yahweh* — and
stops. **1 Chronicles 2:3 repeats the sentence almost verbatim and adds nothing**: *"Er, the firstborn
of Judah, was evil in Yahweh's sight; and he killed him."* **The book that gives a reason for the
flood, for Sodom, and for Babel gives none here**, and the wiki records the silence rather than filling
it. Later tradition supplies guesses — that he wronged [[Tamar]] as his brother did, or that he
refused to father children on her — and **none of them is in the text.**

**His is one of two individual killings the narrator attributes to Yahweh in Genesis**, and the other
is his brother [[Onan]], three verses later. **God destroys a world and two cities elsewhere in the
book; nowhere else does the narrator say he killed a particular man.** The two who are killed are the
two men standing between Tamar and an heir. See [[Retribution]] and [[Judgment]].

**The name.** ***ʿĒr*** is the consonants of ***raʿ***, *evil*, reversed — **the word the next clause
uses of him** — and it sits one letter from ***ʿărîrî***, *childless*, which is how he dies. **Genesis
does not remark on either**, as it does not remark on [[Chezib]] eight verses earlier. The wiki
records the wordplay as a feature of the Hebrew and does not claim the narrator intended it. See
[[Names and Naming]].

**He is the first firstborn in Judah's line and he leaves nothing.** The chapter that opens with him
closes with a birth in which the order of the firstborn is marked, reversed, and recorded the second
way — and the line runs through [[Perez]], the fourth son of his father by a different mother. See
[[Birthright and the Firstborn]].

## Timeline / key events

- Born to [[Judah]] and [[Shua's Daughter]]; *"he named him Er"* — Genesis 38:3, [[Judah and Tamar]]
- Given [[Tamar]] as a wife — Genesis 38:6
- *"Wicked in Yahweh's sight. Yahweh killed him"* — Genesis 38:7
- Listed among those who went down to Egypt, with the note that he *"died in the land of Canaan"* —
  Genesis 46:12, [[Jacob's Family Settles in Egypt]]; Numbers 26:19
