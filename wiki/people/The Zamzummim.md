# The Zamzummim

## Overview

The Ammonite name for the former Rephaim inhabitants of their territory, described as great,
numerous, and tall like the Anakim (Deuteronomy 2:20–21). Yahweh destroyed them before the
Ammonites. Identification with the Zuzim of Genesis 14:5 is not explicit in either passage.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.
