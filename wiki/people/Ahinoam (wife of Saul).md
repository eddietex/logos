# Ahinoam (wife of Saul)

## Overview

### Saul's wife — 1 Samuel 14:50

*The name of Saul's wife was Ahinoam the daughter of Ahimaaz* (14:50). She is the only wife of Saul named in 1
Samuel, and she does nothing. The text does not say which of the five children of 14:49 are hers. Qualified because
David also takes *Ahinoam of Jezreel* (25:43, stored WEB; not yet ingested); the text does not identify the two
women, and the wiki keeps them apart. See [[Saul's Wars and Household]] and [[Ahimaaz (father of Ahinoam)]].

When Saul curses Jonathan at the new moon he speaks of *the shame of your mother's nakedness* (20:30,
[[Jonathan's Covenant and the Arrows]]), without naming her; the text does not explicitly identify Ahinoam as
Jonathan's mother. Saul's concubine Rizpah appears in 2 Samuel 3:7 (recalled, not checked).

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 14:49–50; 20:30; 25:43. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb).
