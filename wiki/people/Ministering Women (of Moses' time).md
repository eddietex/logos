# Ministering Women (of Moses' time)

## Overview

The unnamed women whose mirrors supply the brass basin and its base in Exodus 38:8. The WEB
describes them as ministering at the door of the Tent of Meeting. No individual names, duties,
formal appointment, or priestly status are given.

The period qualifier distinguishes this group without asserting continuity with women in later
sanctuary accounts. Their identity with the skilled spinners of Exodus 35:25–26 is not stated. Nor
does the verse condemn the mirrors as vanity. The relationship between the meeting tent in 33:7–11
and the sanctuary erected in 40:17 leaves the precise setting of their service open; see
[[The Tent Outside the Camp and the Promised Dwelling]].

Source: Exodus 33:7–11; 35:25–26; 38:8; 40:17, checked in local raw WEB on 2026-10-25. No
source-language claim is made.
