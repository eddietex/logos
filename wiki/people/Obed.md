# Obed

## Overview

### Born to Ruth, *born to Naomi* — Ruth 4:13–17

The son of [[Boaz]] and [[Ruth (person)]], born after *Yahweh enabled her to conceive* (Ruth 4:13). He
does nothing and says nothing; he is spoken about. The women of Bethlehem call him Naomi's *near kinsman*
(*gōʾēl*, 4:14) — the word that named Boaz in chapters 2–4, now given to a newborn — and say he will be to
her *a restorer of life* and *sustain you in your old age* (15). [[Naomi]] lays him in her bosom and becomes
his nurse (16). The neighbor women name him, saying *A son is born to Naomi* (17); in the narratives the
wiki has read, he is the only child named by neighbors rather than by a parent.

**The name.** *ʿÔḇēḏ* (WLC) is the participle of *ʿāḇaḏ*, *one who serves* or *works*. The book gives no
reason for it; the women's saying stands where a naming explanation usually stands in Genesis.

**Whose son.** The levirate purpose was a son *to raise up the name of the dead* (4:5, 10), Mahlon; the
women say *A son is born to Naomi* (4:17); the genealogy says *Boaz became the father of Obed* (4:21). The
book records all three without harmonizing them. See
[[Levirate Marriage - Genesis 38, Deuteronomy 25, and Ruth]].

**The line.** *He is the father of Jesse, the father of David* (4:17), so [[David]]'s grandfather. The
genealogy of 4:18–22 places him eighth of its ten names, counting Perez as first.

See [[The Birth of Obed]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 4:5, 10, 13–17, 21; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Eighth in the generations of Perez — Ruth 4:21–22

*Boaz became the father of Obed, and Obed became the father of Jesse* (Ruth 4:21–22). The genealogy credits
him to Boaz, the third of the book's three attributions of the child. See [[The Generations of Perez]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 4:18–22; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

**Correction.** An earlier version of this page said the genealogy places Obed *tenth from Perez*; the
list has him eighth of ten names (WEB and WLC, Ruth 4:18–22). Corrected 2026-11-17.
