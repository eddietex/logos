# Achsah

## Overview

Daughter of Caleb and wife of Othniel. After Caleb’s offer of marriage to the conqueror of Kiriath Sepher is fulfilled, she initiates a request for a field and asks Caleb directly for springs (16–19). Her request links household land to water provision; the narrator records no rebuke.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:13–19.

## Timeline / key events

- Caleb gives her to Othniel after the capture; she gets off her donkey and obtains upper and lower springs.
