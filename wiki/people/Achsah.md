# Achsah

## Overview

### The springs again — Judges 1:12–15

Judges retells her request almost word for word. She *got him to ask her father for a field* (14;
the same Hebrew verb, *wattəsîtēhû*, *she urged him*, that Joshua 15:18 has), dismounts, and asks
Caleb directly: *Give me a blessing; because you have set me in the land of the South, give me also
springs of water* (15). Judges has *the field* with the article (*haśśādeh*), Joshua *a field*
(WLC). Her blessing is water, and she receives two sources of it. See
[[Achsah and the Springs - Joshua 15 and Judges 1]].

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

Daughter of Caleb and wife of Othniel. After Caleb’s offer of marriage to the conqueror of Kiriath Sepher is fulfilled, she initiates a request for a field and asks Caleb directly for springs (16–19). Her request links household land to water provision; the narrator records no rebuke.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:13–19.
