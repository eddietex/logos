# Jotham (son of Gideon)

## Overview

### The one who was left — Judges 9:5

*He went to his father's house at Ophrah, and killed his brothers the sons of Jerubbaal, being seventy
persons, on one stone: but Jotham the youngest son of Jerubbaal was left; for he hid himself* (9:5). He
is the only one of Gideon's seventy sons the book names apart from [[Jether (son of Gideon)]], the
firstborn of 8:20. Jether was too young to strike the kings. Jotham, *the youngest*, *haqqāṭōn*,
survives his brother's killing by hiding.

*Was left*, *wayyiwwātēr*, is the niphal of *yātar*, *to remain over*. *He hid himself*, *neḥbāʾ*, is
the verb of Adam in the garden (Genesis 3:10) and of the five kings in the cave at Makkedah (Joshua
10:16) (WLC). The kings who hid were found and killed. Jotham is not found. In 2 Kings 11:2 another
king's son is hidden while the royal sons are killed; see
[[Seventy Sons and One Hidden - Judges 9 and 2 Kings 10-11]].

The page is qualified because Judah later has a king named Jotham, *Jotham his son reigned in his
place* (2 Kings 15:7, WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested).

### What is ahead — Judges 9:7–21, 57 (stored WEB; not yet ingested)

When he hears that Abimelech has been made king, he stands *on the top of Mount Gerizim* and tells the
men of Shechem the fable of the trees and the bramble (9:7–15). He charges them with the killing of his
father's sons *on one stone* (9:18), curses them and Abimelech with fire, and flees to Beer *for fear of
Abimelech his brother* (9:21). The chapter's last words are *the curse of Jotham the son of Jerubbaal
came on them* (9:57). These will be written up when those verses are ingested.
