# Cozbi

## Overview

Daughter of Zur, a Midianite leader (Numbers 25:15). Zimri brings her into the camp in public view;
Phinehas kills both (6–8). The closing oracle names her in the charge of Midianite deception (18),
but supplies neither her speech nor a separate account of her intentions.
