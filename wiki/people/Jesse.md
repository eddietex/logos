# Jesse

## Overview

### Named in the line — Ruth 4:17

The son of [[Obed]] and the father of [[David]]. He enters the wiki in the last words of
[[The Birth of Obed]]: *He is the father of Jesse, the father of David* (Ruth 4:17). The genealogy that
follows repeats it, *Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David* (4:22). Ruth
says nothing else about him. His household at Bethlehem and the anointing of his youngest son belong to
1 Samuel 16–17; 16:1–13 is now ingested below.

*Yišay* (WLC). In the wiki's existing pages David is already *son of Jesse* (see [[David]]).

### Ninth in the generations of Perez — Ruth 4:22

*Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David* (Ruth 4:22). 1 Chronicles 2:12–15
names his seven sons in order, Eliab the firstborn to *David the seventh* (WEB at bible-api.com,
2026-11-17; not yet ingested). See [[The Generations of Perez]] and [[From Perez to David - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, Matthew 1, and Luke 3]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 4:18–22; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### The sacrifice at Bethlehem — 1 Samuel 16:1–13

Yahweh sends Samuel *to Jesse the Bethlehemite; for I have provided a king for myself among his sons* (16:1). Samuel
sanctifies Jesse and his sons and calls them to the sacrifice (16:5). Jesse makes his sons pass before Samuel, Eliab,
Abinadab, Shammah, and then seven in all, and none is chosen (16:6–10). He has not brought the youngest: *There
remains yet the youngest. Behold, he is keeping the sheep* (16:11). He sends for him, and David is anointed *in the
middle of his brothers* (16:12–13). Jesse says nothing about the anointing. See [[Samuel Anoints David]].

**How many sons — a contradiction, recorded.** 1 Samuel 16:10 has *seven of his sons* pass before Samuel before David
is sent for, and 17:12 says *he had eight sons* (stored WEB). 1 Chronicles 2:13–15 names seven, *Eliab*, *Abinadab*,
*Shimea*, *Nethanel*, *Raddai*, *Ozem*, and *David the seventh* (WEB checked at bible-api.com). The two books differ
by one son, and Chronicles gives the third son's name as *Shimea* where Samuel has *Shammah* (16:9; 17:13). Neither
text explains the difference. The wiki records both counts and does not choose between them.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 16:1–13; 17:12–14. 1 Chronicles 2:13–15 checked at bible-api.com (`translation=web`),
2026-11-20. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-20.

### David sent to Saul — 1 Samuel 16:19–22

Saul sends messengers to Jesse, *Send me David your son, who is with the sheep* (16:19). Jesse sends him with *a
donkey loaded with bread, and a bottle of wine, and a young goat* (16:20), the same three things the men going up to
Bethel carried at Saul's anointing (10:3). Saul sends again: *Please let David stand before me; for he has found favor
in my sight* (16:22). Jesse's answer is not recorded; by 17:15 David goes back and forth between Saul and the sheep.
See [[David Plays the Harp for Saul]].
