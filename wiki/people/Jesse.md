# Jesse

## Overview

### Named in the line — Ruth 4:17

The son of [[Obed]] and the father of [[David]]. He enters the wiki in the last words of
[[The Birth of Obed]]: *He is the father of Jesse, the father of David* (Ruth 4:17). The genealogy that
follows repeats it, *Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David* (4:22). Ruth
says nothing else about him. His household at Bethlehem and the anointing of his youngest son belong to
1 Samuel 16–17, not yet ingested; the page will grow there.

*Yišay* (WLC). In the wiki's existing pages David is already *son of Jesse* (see [[David]]).

Source: stored WEB Ruth 4:17, 22; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
