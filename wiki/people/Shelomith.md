# Shelomith

## Overview

Israelite woman identified as the daughter of [[Dibri]], of the tribe of
[[Dan (person)]]. Her unnamed son has an Egyptian father and is executed for
blasphemy (Leviticus 24:10–23). Her naming identifies his family; the account
assigns no wrongdoing to her.
