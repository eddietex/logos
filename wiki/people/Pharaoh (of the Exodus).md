# Pharaoh (of the Exodus)

## Overview

**The king of Egypt to whom Moses is sent — and whose refusal God announces before the first request is
made.** He is not [[Pharaoh (of the oppression)]], who *"died"* in the course of Moses' years in
Midian (Exodus 2:23); Exodus gives neither man a personal name, and the wiki keys the page to the
narrative rather than to a dynasty. *Pharaoh* is a title — Egyptian *pr-ʿȝ*, *the great house*. The
identification with any known king (Ramesses II is the most often proposed, Thutmose III or
Amenhotep II on an earlier chronology) is not recoverable from Scripture, and the wiki adopts none.
This page grows as Exodus 5–14 is ingested.

**He first appears only in God's speech.** *"I will send you to Pharaoh"* (3:10); *"Who am I, that I
should go to Pharaoh?"* (3:11). And then the forecast: *"I know that the king of Egypt won't give you
permission to go, no, not by a mighty hand. I will reach out my hand and strike Egypt with all my
wonders which I will do among them, and after that he will let you go"* (3:19–20). **The request for a
three days' journey is to be made to a king whose refusal is already known.** The Hebrew of 3:19 can
also be read *unless by a mighty hand*, as the Septuagint and many versions take it; both readings are
recorded on [[The Burning Bush]].

**The contest is named before it begins.** God sends Moses so that Pharaoh will send Israel — *šālaḥ*
both times — and Moses, *"heavy of mouth"* (*kəbad-peh*, 4:10), will face a king whose heart is
*heavy* (*kābēd*, 7:14).

**His hardening and his loss are both announced before he appears.** On Moses' road back, God says
*"I will harden his heart and he will not let the people go"* (4:21) — *ʾăḥazzēq*, *I will make
strong* — the first hardening statement in Exodus; and gives Moses the message that will end the
contest: *"Israel is my son, my firstborn … Let my son go, that he may serve me; and you have refused
to let him go. Behold, I will kill your son, your firstborn"* (4:22–23). **The refusal is put in the
past tense before it has happened, and the tenth plague is announced before the first.** See
[[Moses Returns to Egypt]] and [[Hardening of the Heart]].

**His first words are a question, and the rest of his story answers it.** *"Who is Yahweh, that I
should listen to his voice to let Israel go? I don't know Yahweh, and moreover I will not let Israel
go"* (5:2) — *lōʾ yādaʿtî ʾet-YHWH*, the construction of his predecessor's *"who didn't know Joseph"*
(1:8). He hears the request for a feast as a labour stoppage — *"you make them rest"*, ***hišbattem***,
the Sabbath verb (5:5) — and that same day withdraws the straw and keeps the quota (5:6–9). **He
calls the request idleness twice** (*"You are idle! You are idle!"*, 5:17) **and the word of Yahweh
*"lying words"*** (5:9). His orders go out as *kōh ʾāmar parʿōh*, *"This is what Pharaoh says"* (5:10)
— the messenger formula Moses had just used for Yahweh. **Yahweh's reply is about him, not to him**:
*"Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh, for by a strong hand he shall let them go"* (6:1). See
[[Bricks Without Straw]] and
[[Who Is Yahweh - Pharaoh's Question and the Knowledge of Yahweh]].

**In 6:2–13 he is the destination of a command he does not hear.** God's answer to his *"I don't know
Yahweh"* (5:2) is spoken to Israel: *"you shall know that I am Yahweh your God"* (6:7). It promises
deliverance *"from under the burdens of the Egyptians"*, which answers his *"Get back to your
burdens!"* (5:4), and judgment through *"great judgments"* (6:6). Moses doubts that Pharaoh will listen
when Israel has not (6:12). See [[I Am Yahweh]].

### What is ahead

The ten plagues; the release; the pursuit; and the sea. These are written as
their pericopes arrive.

## Timeline / key events

- Named as the king to whom Moses is sent; his refusal foretold, and his compulsion by God's hand —
  Exodus 3:10–11, 18–20, [[The Burning Bush]]
- His heart's hardening foretold; the death of his firstborn announced as the price of refusing God's
  firstborn — Exodus 4:21–23, [[Moses Returns to Egypt]]
- Refuses Moses and Aaron: *"Who is Yahweh? … I don't know Yahweh"*; withdraws the straw and keeps the
  quota; calls the people idle — Exodus 5:1–18, [[Bricks Without Straw]]
- His compulsion foretold: *"by a strong hand he shall let them go"* — Exodus 6:1
- The object of a renewed command: *"Go in, speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the children of
  Israel go out of his land"* — Exodus 6:10–13, [[I Am Yahweh]]
