# Baal Berith

## Overview

The god Israel turns to after Gideon's death: *As soon as Gideon was dead, the children of Israel
turned again, and played the prostitute following the Baals, and made Baal Berith their god* (Judges
8:33). This page follows the convention of person pages for named deities, as with [[Baal Peor]] and
[[Baal (deity)]]. It does not decide whether Baal Berith is a local form of Baal or a separate god.

The name is *baʿal bərît* (WLC), *lord of the covenant*. That *bərît* is the Hebrew word for
*covenant* is not in doubt; how the title was understood by those who used it is the usual explanation,
reported, not checked. In the Genesis–Judges WLC the name occurs only at Judges 8:33 and 9:4. Judges 9:4
places his *house* at [[Shechem (city)]], and its silver pays Abimelech's men: *They gave him seventy
pieces of silver out of the house of Baal Berith, with which Abimelech hired vain and light fellows*
(9:4). So the first thing the book shows the new god's house doing is paying for the killing of
Gideon's seventy sons. Shechem's leading men are *baʿălê šəkem*, *the lords of Shechem* (WLC), the
same word as the god's title. See [[Abimelech Made King at Shechem]]. 9:46 names *the house of
Elberith*, *ʾēl bərît*, *God of the covenant*, at Shechem's tower; see below.

Shechem is where Joshua *made a covenant with the people* (Joshua 24:25). The book does not draw the
contrast between that covenant and this god's title; the wiki records it in
[[The Altar at Shechem and the Covenant at Shechem]].

The verb of 8:33, *wayyāśîmû*, *they set* him as their god, is the verb Gideon used when he *named* his
son Abimelech two verses earlier (8:31, WLC).

See [[Gideon's Ephod and Death]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 8:31, 33; 9:2, 4, 46; Joshua 24:25; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### The house of their god, and El Berith — Judges 9:27, 46–49

At the vintage festival the men of Shechem *went into the house of their god, and ate and drank, and
cursed Abimelech* (9:27). The god is not named there; *their god* is most naturally Baal Berith, whose
house is the only one at Shechem the chapter has mentioned (9:4). After the city falls, the men of the
tower of Shechem *entered into the stronghold of the house of Elberith* (9:46), and Abimelech burns it
over them, about a thousand men and women (9:49).

*ʾĒl bərît*, *God of the covenant*, occurs only at 9:46 in Genesis–Judges; *baʿal bərît* only at 8:33
and 9:4 (WLC). This page treats them together, as most readers do, but the text does not say that El
Berith and Baal Berith are one god or that the *house* of 9:4 is the *house* of 9:46. That *ʾēl* can be
used of Israel's God and of other gods is the wider usage; whether Shechem's worshippers meant Baal or
something older by *El Berith* is not recoverable from the text. The god whose silver paid for the
seventy cannot shelter the people who flee to its house. See [[The Fall of Abimelech]] and
[[The Tower of Shechem]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 8:33; 9:4, 27, 46–49; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-15.
