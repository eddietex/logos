# Rahab

## Overview

A prostitute living in Jericho whose house is on the wall (1, 15). She shelters Joshua’s spies, confesses Yahweh’s sovereignty, and negotiates protection for her household. Her occupation is stated; the story does not reduce her to it. She is not the poetic Rahab mentioned in Job.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 2:1–24.

## Timeline / key events

- Hides the spies under flax and misdirects the king’s messengers (4–7).
- Confesses what she has heard and asks an oath for her family (9–14).
- Lowers the spies through her window, directs their escape, and ties the scarlet line (15–21).
