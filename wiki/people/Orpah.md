# Orpah

## Overview

A Moabite woman, one of the two *wives of the women of Moab* taken by Elimelech's sons (Ruth 1:4). She is
named first, before Ruth. The text does not say which son she married; Ruth 4:10 makes Ruth the wife of
[[Mahlon]], so Orpah was presumably [[Chilion]]'s. She is widowed in Moab after about ten years (1:5).

On the road to Judah she sets out with Naomi, refuses with Ruth to leave her (*No, but we will return with
you to your people*, 1:10), and then, after Naomi's second appeal, turns back: *Orpah kissed her
mother-in-law, but Ruth joined with her* (1:14). Naomi says she *has gone back to her people, and to her
god* (1:15). She does what Naomi urged three times over, and the narrator gives her a kiss, not a rebuke;
the contrast is between *kissed* and *clung*, not between a good and a bad daughter-in-law. Later
traditions that judge her harshly are not in the text. Naomi calls her *yəḇimtēḵ*, *your sister-in-law*
(15), a noun found elsewhere in the WLC files read only in the levirate law (Deuteronomy 25:7, 9). She does
not appear again.

See [[Famine and Death in Moab]] and [[Ruth Clings to Naomi]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 1:4–5, 8–15; 4:10; Deuteronomy 25:7, 9;
Hebrew WLC, 2026-11-17.
