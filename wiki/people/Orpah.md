# Orpah

## Overview

A Moabite woman, one of the two *wives of the women of Moab* taken by Elimelech's sons (Ruth 1:4). She is
named first, before Ruth. The text does not say which son she married; Ruth 4:10 makes Ruth the wife of
[[Mahlon]], so Orpah was presumably [[Chilion]]'s. She is widowed in Moab after about ten years (1:5).

On the road to Judah she will turn back: *Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, but Ruth joined with her* (1:14),
and Naomi will say she *has gone back to her people, and to her god* (1:15; not yet ingested).

See [[Famine and Death in Moab]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 1:4–5, 14–15; 4:10.
