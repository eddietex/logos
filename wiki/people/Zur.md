# Zur

## Overview

### Eastern allotments — Joshua 13:8–33

Named among the five chiefs of Midian, princes of Sihon, whom Moses struck (21). Numbers 31:8 calls the same five kings of Midian. The descriptions differ in title and political framing; neither text explains their precise relationship to Sihon.

See [[The Inheritance Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 13:8–33; comparisons as cited.

### The war against Midian — Numbers 31:1–54

*Zur* is one of *the five kings of Midian* killed in the war of vengeance for Peor (Numbers 31:8). The
wiki takes him to be the same man as Cozbi's father: the name is the same, and Numbers 25 has already
ranked him among Midian's leaders. The text does not say so in as many words, and the identification is
the wiki's. Joshua 13:21 names the five as *the chiefs of Midian … the princes of Sihon* (WEB via
bible-api.com, not yet ingested); Numbers calls them kings.

See [[The War Against Midian]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 31.

### Baal Peor — Numbers 25:1–18

Cozbi’s father, head of the people of a fathers’ house in Midian (Numbers 25:15); verse 18 calls him
a prince of Midian. No personal act of his is narrated here.
