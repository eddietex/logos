# Ichabod

## Overview

### Born the day the ark was taken — 1 Samuel 4:19–22

Son of [[Phinehas (son of Eli)]] and [[Phinehas' Wife]], grandson of [[Eli]]. He is born on the day the
news comes from the battle: his mother, near her time, hears that *God's ark was taken, and that her
father-in-law and her husband were dead*, goes into labor, and dies (4:19–20). *She named the child Ichabod,
saying, "The glory has departed from Israel"; because God's ark was taken, and because of her father-in-law
and her husband* (21). Her last words repeat the reason with the ark alone (22).

**Name.** *ʾÎ-ḵāḇôḏ* (4:21, WLC; written as one word, *ʾîḵāḇôḏ*, at 14:3). The second element is *kāḇôḏ*,
*glory*, the word the mother's explanation uses. The first is disputed: *no glory*, or *where is the
glory?* Morphhb tags it as a particle; the rare negative *ʾî* is discussed under Job 22:30 at
[[Eliphaz's Third Speech]]. Either way the name records the day, not the child. Rachel had named her dying
son Benoni in the same way (Genesis 35:18); his father renamed him, and Ichabod's father is already dead.
See [[Do Not Be Afraid, You Have a Son - Rachel and Phinehas' Wife]] and [[Names and Naming]].

**Later.** He is named once more, as a point of reference in the priestly line: *Ahijah, the son of Ahitub,
Ichabod's brother, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli* (14:3, stored WEB; not yet ingested). Nothing else
is said of him.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 4:19–22; 14:3; Genesis 35:17–18. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-17.
