# Adoni-Zedek

## Overview

King of Jerusalem, one of the five Amorite kings who attack Gibeon. Organizes the coalition after
hearing of Ai’s destruction and Gibeon’s peace with Israel (1–4).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No personal history or dynasty is supplied here.
