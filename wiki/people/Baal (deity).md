# Baal (deity)

## Overview

The god Israel serves in place of Yahweh in the cycle of Judges. Judges 2:11 has *the Baals*,
*habbəʿālîm*, plural with the article; 2:13 has the singular, *Baal* (*labbaʿal*), with *the
Ashtaroth* (WLC; stored WEB). *Baʿal* is an ordinary Hebrew word for *owner, master, husband*, and as
a divine title it was attached to many local gods. The plural is usually read as the Baal of many
places rather than many unrelated gods; that reading is reported here, not settled.

Before Judges, the name has appeared in the wiki only in compounds: the god of Peor (see
[[Baal Peor]]) and places named for a Baal, such as [[Baal Zephon]], [[Baal Gad]], and
[[Baal Meon]]. Judges 2 is the first place the god is named by himself, and the summary makes him the
standing alternative to Yahweh for the whole book. Judges 3:7 pairs *the Baals* with *the
Asheroth* (stored WEB); see [[Asherah]].

### An altar in Ophrah — Judges 6:25–32

The first Baal altar the wiki meets belongs to an Israelite: *the altar of Baal that your father has*
(6:25), kept by Joash at Ophrah with an Asherah beside it. Gideon throws it down at night, and the men of
the town demand his death for it (6:30). Joash answers: *Will you contend for Baal? Or will you save
him? … If he is a god, let him contend for himself* (6:31), and names his son *Jerub-Baal*, *Let Baal
contend against him* (6:32). The verb is *rîb*, *contend* or *plead a case* (WLC). Baal is challenged
by name, and the narrative never shows him answering. After Gideon's death Israel turns to *the Baals*
again and makes *Baal Berith* their god (8:33). See [[Gideon Throws Down Baal's Altar]].

The page is qualified `(deity)` from the start because the name is expected to collide later:
1 Chronicles is recalled as naming both a man and a town called Baal (not yet stored, so not checked).
Qualifying now means no bare `Baal.md` is ever created.

Source: stored WEB Judges 2:11–13; 3:7; 6:25–32; 8:33. Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-13.

## Timeline / key events

- **Judges 2:11–13** — the new generation serves *the Baals*; they abandon Yahweh and serve *Baal and
  the Ashtaroth*.
- **Judges 3:7** — Israel *forgot Yahweh* and served *the Baals and the Asheroth*; the first oppression follows.
- **Judges 6:25–32** — Joash's Baal altar at Ophrah thrown down by Gideon; *If he is a god, let him
  contend for himself*; the name *Jerub-Baal*.
