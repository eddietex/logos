# Serah

## Overview

**The only granddaughter named in the register of the seventy, and the Bible never says why.**

> **17** The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and Serah their sister. The sons of Beriah:
> Heber and Malchiel. (46:17)

**The register at 46:8–27 lists about fifty grandsons and great-grandsons of [[Jacob]] and exactly one
granddaughter.** **It does not give her a husband, a child, a story or an epithet** — only *their
sister*, appended to a list of [[Asher]]'s sons. **[[Dinah]] is the other woman in the count** (46:15),
and she is [[Jacob]]'s own daughter and has eleven chapters behind her; **Serah has nothing behind her
at all.**

**And yet she is listed twice more.** **Numbers 26:46 interrupts the census of Asher to say that the
name of his daughter was Serah** — a sentence with no census function whatever — **and 1 Chronicles
7:30 repeats the Genesis clause almost exactly.** **Three registers, across three books, none of which
needed her, and all three of which kept her.** **The wiki records that pattern and does not explain
it**, because Scripture does not.

**Later Jewish tradition builds a great deal on the silence** — that she survived to identify Joseph's
coffin, or never died at all — **and none of it is in the text.** It is noted here as tradition and
not adopted. See [[Names and Naming]].
