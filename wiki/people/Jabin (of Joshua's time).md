# Jabin (of Joshua's time)

## Overview

### A second Jabin of Hazor — Judges 4:2

Judges 4:2 has *Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor*, oppressing Israel after Ehud's death,
though Joshua killed Hazor's king and burned the city (Joshua 11:10–13). The text does not explain
the repeated name. The wiki keeps a separate page, [[Jabin (of Deborah's time)]], and does not
identify the two. See [[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; Joshua 11:1–13.
