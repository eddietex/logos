# Jabin (of Joshua's time)

## Overview

King of Hazor who summons the northern coalition (1). Hazor heads these kingdoms (10); Joshua captures it and kills its king. The narrative setting distinguishes this ruler without identifying him with later namesakes.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:1–15.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Counted by city as *the king of Hazor, one* (12:19). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
