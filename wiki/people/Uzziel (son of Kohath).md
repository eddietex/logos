# Uzziel (son of Kohath)

## Overview

Exodus 6:18 names Uzziel among Kohath’s sons; 6:22 names Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri as his sons. Leviticus 10:4 identifies him as Aaron’s uncle when Moses summons two of those sons to remove the dead priests. Uzziel himself is mentioned genealogically, not reported acting in the scene.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 6:18–23 and Leviticus 10:4–5.

### A clan and a prince — Numbers 3:14–51

Last of Kohath's sons (19), head of the Uzzielites (27), and father of the Kohathite prince (30).

See [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 3.
