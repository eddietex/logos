# Jonathan (son of Gershom)

## Overview

*A young man out of Bethlehem Judah, of the family of Judah, who was a Levite* (Judges 17:7, stored WEB),
*sojourning there*, *gār šām* (WLC). He leaves *to live where he could find a place* and comes to Micah's
house in the hill country of Ephraim (17:8). Micah offers *Dwell with me, and be to me a father and a
priest, and I will give you ten pieces of silver per year, a suit of clothing, and your food*; he is
*content*, becomes *as one of his sons*, and Micah *consecrated* him, literally *filled his hand* (17:10–12,
WLC). See [[Micah's Idol and Levite]].

**A Levite of the family of Judah.** How he can be both the text does not explain. Readers take it as
residence in Judah, descent through his mother, or *Levite* as an office; the wiki records all three.

**Against Deuteronomy 18.** Deuteronomy 18:6–7 provides for a Levite *where he lives as a foreigner* to
come *to the place which Yahweh shall choose* and *minister in the name of Yahweh his God* (stored WEB).
This one finds a private house and a wage. See
[[The Priests of the High Places - Deuteronomy 18.6-8 and 2 Kings 23.9]] and
[[Filling the Hand - Aaron's Sons, the Levites at the Calf, and Micah's Priests]].

### Priest to a tribe — Judges 18

The five Danite spies *knew the voice of the young man the Levite* (18:3) and ask him three questions:
*Who brought you here? What do you do in this place? What do you have here?* His answer is a wage: *he
has hired me, and I am become his priest* (4). Asked to *ask counsel of God*, he says, *Go in peace. Your
way in which you go is before Yahweh* (6) — an answer the narrator neither confirms nor condemns.

When the six hundred come back and the five take the image, ephod, teraphim, and molten image, he
protests once, *What are you doing?* (18). They tell him to *put your hand on your mouth* and offer him
Micah's own terms, *be to us a father and a priest*, with a promotion: *Is it better for you to be priest
to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and a family in Israel?* (19). *The priest's heart was
glad* (*wayyîṭaḇ*, WLC), the verb of Micah's *Yahweh will do good to me* (17:13); he carries the ephod,
the teraphim, and the image himself, and goes *with the people* (20). Micah's complaint names him with the
idols: *You have taken away my gods which I made, and the priest* (24).

*Jonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites
until the day of the captivity of the land* (30). See [[The Danite Migration]].

**Is he the same man?** Verse 30 does not say *the Levite was Jonathan*. But the Levite goes with the tribe
as its priest (19–20), and verse 30 names the tribe's priest; most readers identify them, and the wiki
does. The page was filed as `Micah's Levite` until the ingest of Judges 18 and renamed then.

**Moses or Manasseh.** The Westminster Leningrad Codex writes his grandfather's name *mənaššeh* with the
*nun* suspended above the line, and its lemma tagging reads it as *Manasseh*; without the raised letter
the consonants are *mšh*, *Moses*. The WEB reads *Moses*. The usual explanation — that scribes raised the
letter to keep Moses' name from an idolatrous grandson — is reported, not checked, as are the Greek and
Latin readings. See [[Gershom]].

**A grandson of Moses this late?** If *son of Moses* means grandson, the story belongs in the first
generations after the conquest, which fits the epilogue's other marker: Phinehas, Aaron's grandson,
stands before the ark in the war with Benjamin (20:28, stored WEB). Readers take Judges 17–21 as set
early and placed at the end, or take *son* as *descendant*. The wiki records both.

**The irony of the line.** Moses' firstborn was named *a sojourner there* (Exodus 2:22); his grandson is
introduced *sojourning there* (*gār šām*, 17:7, WLC), looking for any place, and ends as the hereditary
priest of an image at the northern edge of the land — the thing his grandfather's law cursed (Deuteronomy
27:15). The narrator does not draw the line; the wiki records the sound and the descent.

## Timeline / key events

- Leaves Bethlehem Judah looking for a place to live — Judges 17:7–9
- Hired and consecrated as Micah's priest — 17:10–13, [[Micah's Idol and Levite]]
- Recognized by the Danite spies; gives them an oracle — 18:3–6, [[The Danite Migration]]
- Leaves Micah with the image, ephod, and teraphim to be priest to the tribe — 18:18–20
- He and his sons priests of the image at Dan *until the day of the captivity of the land* — 18:30
