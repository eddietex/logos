# Eleazar (son of Abinadab)

## Overview

### Consecrated to keep the ark — 1 Samuel 7:1

The men of Kiriath Jearim bring the ark into the house of his father [[Abinadab (of Kiriath Jearim)]] *and
consecrated Eleazar his son to keep Yahweh's ark* (7:1). The verb is *qiddəšû*, *they consecrated* (WLC), the
Piel of *qādaš*, *be holy*; *to keep* is *lišmōr*. He is not called a priest or a Levite, and nothing is
said of what his keeping involved. The narrative has just shown what happens to those who *look into* the ark
at Beth Shemesh (6:19), so the consecration answers the question asked there, *Who is able to stand before
Yahweh, this holy God?* (6:20), with a single set-apart keeper. See [[The Ark Returns to Israel]].

He is the second Eleazar the wiki records in charge of holy things. [[Eleazar (son of Aaron)]] was to have *the oversight
of those who keep the requirements of the sanctuary* (Numbers 3:32, stored WEB), where *keep* is again
*šāmar* (*šōmərê*, WLC). The two men are not connected by the text, and the wiki draws no link beyond the
shared name and verb.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 6:19–7:1; Numbers 3:32. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
