# Zimri (son of Salu)

## Overview

A prince of a fathers’ house among the Simeonites, named after his death (Numbers 25:14). He brings
Cozbi before Moses and the weeping congregation; Phinehas follows and kills them both (6–8). The
text does not narrate his words or motive.
