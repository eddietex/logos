# Aaron

## Overview

**Moses' elder brother, Israel's first high priest — and, at his first appearance, a name God offers
in anger to a man who has refused to go.** He enters Scripture at Exodus 4:14, inside God's answer to
Moses' fifth objection, *"Oh, Lord, please send someone else"* (4:13). This page grows as Exodus and
the rest of the Torah are ingested; what is ahead is marked as ahead.

**He is introduced by three facts and a movement.** *"What about Aaron, your brother, the Levite? I
know that he can speak well. Also, behold, he comes out to meet you. When he sees you, he will be glad
in his heart"* (4:14). **He is already travelling before Moses has agreed to anything** — the help is
on the road before the call is accepted — and they meet *"on God's mountain"* (4:27). See
[[The Burning Bush]].

**Why *the Levite*, when Moses is a Levite too, is not explained.** It may anticipate the priesthood
that will be his line's; it may reflect a use of *Levite* for a function as well as a descent; or it
may be plain full identification. The wiki records all three and adopts none.

**His office is defined before he has one.** *"He will be your spokesman to the people; and it will
happen, that he will be to you a mouth, and you will be to him as God"* (4:16). **Moses is to put
words in his mouth as God puts them in Moses'**, a chain the text makes explicit at 7:1: *"I have made
you as God to Pharaoh; and Aaron your brother shall be your prophet."* **Joseph had spoken to his
brothers through an interpreter (Genesis 42:23); Aaron is the first person in Scripture whom God
appoints to be another man's mouth.** A rabbinic tradition (Babylonian Talmud,
Zevachim 102a) holds that the priesthood passed to Aaron's line because of Moses' refusal here; the
text does not say so.

**Brother without rivalry, at first.** After Cain and Abel, Isaac and Ishmael, Jacob and Esau, and
Joseph and his brothers, **Aaron is the first brother in Scripture whose coming is announced as
gladness** (4:14). The rivalry, when it comes (Numbers 12), is over prophetic standing and not an
inheritance. See [[Brotherhood]].

**He meets Moses as foretold, and speaks for him at once.** *"Yahweh said to Aaron, 'Go into the
wilderness to meet Moses.' He went, and met him on God's mountain, and kissed him"* (4:27). **His first
act in Scripture is to go out and kiss his brother**, with no quarrel to get past — unlike Esau's (Genesis 33:4) or Joseph's
(45:15). Moses tells him *"all Yahweh's words"* and the signs; and before the elders **it is Aaron who
*"spoke all the words which Yahweh had spoken to Moses, and did the signs in the sight of the
people"*** (4:30) — signs given into Moses' hand. *"The people believed"* (4:31). See
[[Moses Returns to Egypt]].

**At the first audience he is beside Moses at every step and has not one word assigned to him.**
*"Moses and Aaron came, and said to Pharaoh"* (5:1); *"They said"* (5:3); Pharaoh rebukes *"you, Moses
and Aaron"* (5:4); and the beaten officers find *"Moses and Aaron, who stood in the way"*, and curse
them both (5:20–21). **Then it is Moses alone who returns to Yahweh** (5:22). The mouth appointed at
4:16 speaks with Moses here rather than for him, and the text does not say which of them said what.
See [[Bricks Without Straw]].

**At 6:13 Yahweh speaks to him and to Moses together for the first time.** *"Yahweh spoke to Moses and
to Aaron, and gave them a command"* (*ʾel-mōšeh wəʾel-ʾahărōn*). God had spoken to Aaron once before,
alone, to send him to meet Moses (4:27). Here the command is given to the two of them as a pair,
directly after Moses' objection that he has *"uncircumcised lips"* (6:12). The command is not
explained, and it does not answer the objection; it adds a second person to it. See [[I Am Yahweh]].

### What is ahead

The rod that becomes a *tannîn* (7); the genealogy that names his parents, his wife Elisheba,
and his sons (6:20–25); the plagues; Sinai, where he goes up with the elders (24); the priestly
garments and ordination (28–29); **the golden calf, which he makes** (32); and his death on Mount Hor
(Numbers 20). These sections are written as their pericopes arrive.

## Timeline / key events

- Named by God as Moses' brother, *"the Levite"*, who *"can speak well"*, already coming to meet him,
  and to be his *mouth* — Exodus 4:14–16, [[The Burning Bush]]
- Sent into the wilderness to meet Moses; meets him on God's mountain and kisses him —
  Exodus 4:27, [[Moses Returns to Egypt]]
- Speaks Yahweh's words and does the signs before the elders and the people, who believe — Exodus
  4:29–31
- Goes in to Pharaoh with Moses; cursed with him by the Israelite officers — Exodus 5:1–21,
  [[Bricks Without Straw]]
- Commanded by Yahweh together with Moses for the first time, to bring Israel out — Exodus 6:13,
  [[I Am Yahweh]]

## Appears in

- [[The Burning Bush]] — Exodus 4:14–16; **named, not present.** Offered by God when Moses asks for
  someone else; Moses is to be *"to him as God"*, and he is to be Moses' mouth
- [[Moses Returns to Egypt]] — Exodus 4:27–31; **the mouth, in use.** Meets Moses on God's mountain
  and kisses him; speaks all the words and does the signs before the people, and they believe
- [[Bricks Without Straw]] — Exodus 5:1–21; **present, and unassigned.** Speaks *with* Moses before
  Pharaoh — every verb is plural — and is cursed with him; absent from the complaint to God
- [[I Am Yahweh]] — Exodus 6:13; **addressed for the first time with Moses.** Named in the command,
  given after Moses' *"uncircumcised lips"*, to both the people and Pharaoh
