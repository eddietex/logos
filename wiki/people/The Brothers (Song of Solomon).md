# The Brothers (Song of Solomon)

## Overview

A collective literary-role page for the woman's unnamed mother's sons in Song 1:6,
and the likely family speakers of 8:8–9. They are angry in the first passage and
assign her vineyard work; the later plural voice plans for a little sister's future.
The second group is not explicitly called brothers or identified with the first.
This page records that probable connection without turning it into a proved biography.

Their protective or controlling plans meet the woman's mature self-description in
8:10. That tension should not be resolved by treating their assessment as the poem's
last word. No names, ages, or particular brother are supplied.
Source: stored WEB Song of Solomon 1:6; 8:8–12.

## Timeline / key events

- Song 1:6 — angry mother's sons make the woman keeper of vineyards.
- Song 8:8–9 — probable family voices plan adornment or enclosure for a sister;
  the poem does not establish chronological distance from 1:6.
