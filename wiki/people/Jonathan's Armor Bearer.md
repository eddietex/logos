# Jonathan's Armor Bearer

## Overview

The unnamed young man, *hannaʿar nōśēʾ ḵēlāyw*, *the young man who bore his armor* (1 Samuel 14:1, WLC), who goes
over the pass with [[Jonathan (son of Saul)]]. He is never named, and he is the only other person who knows that
Jonathan has gone (14:1, 3). Named here by his role and his master, so that the page cannot collide with Saul's
armour-bearer (31:4–6, stored WEB; not yet ingested) or David's appointment as one (16:21, stored WEB; not yet
ingested).

### Over the pass — 1 Samuel 14:1–23

When Jonathan proposes to go over to *the garrison of these uncircumcised*, saying *It may be that Yahweh will work
for us* (14:6), the armour-bearer answers, *Do all that is in your heart. Turn and, behold, I am with you according to
your heart* (14:7). The phrase *kilḇāḇeḵā* (WLC) is the one Samuel had used of the man Yahweh sought *after his own
heart*, *kilḇāḇô* (13:14). He climbs after Jonathan, and *they fell before Jonathan; and his armor bearer killed them
after him* (14:13): about twenty men (14:14). Saul's roll call finds them both missing (14:17). After verse 17 he is
not mentioned again. See [[Jonathan's Attack at Michmash]].
