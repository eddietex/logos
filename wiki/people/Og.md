# Og

## Overview

### Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan — Numbers 32:1–42

*The kingdom of Og king of Bashan* is given with Sihon's to Gad, Reuben, and half of Manasseh (33).

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.

King of Bashan who brings his people to fight Israel at Edrei (33). Yahweh assures Moses of victory;
Og, his sons, and his people are struck until no survivors remain, and Israel possesses the land
(34–35). This unit gives no physical description of Og.
