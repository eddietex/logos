# Og

## Overview

### The Gibeonite deception — Joshua 9:1–27

The envoys cite the defeat of Og king of Bashan, who was at Ashtaroth, with Sihon (10). Their appeal
uses remembered victories to explain their alleged long journey.

See [[The Gibeonite Deception]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 9:1–27; comparisons as cited.


### Rahab and the spies — Joshua 2:1–24

Rahab names him with Sihon among the two Amorite kings whose destruction has terrified Jericho (10–11). The significance here is the report’s effect west of the Jordan, not a further conquest.

See [[Rahab and the Spies]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 2:1–24.

### Courage and succession — Deuteronomy 31:1–8

His destruction alongside Sihon is recalled to encourage the people facing the crossing (4). The defeated kings are both identified as Amorite rulers.

See [[Be Strong as Joshua Takes the Lead]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 31:1–8; Deuteronomy 31:16–18 and 34:7 where compared.

### The covenant in Moab — Deuteronomy 29:1–29

The king of Bashan is recalled with Sihon as defeated before the eastern land was allotted (7–8). No new battle takes place.

See [[The Covenant in Moab and the Hidden Things]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 29:1–29.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

His former kingdom of Bashan is included with Sihon’s territory in Israel’s acquired land (47). The two are explicitly called kings of the Amorites.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

His former kingdom, Bashan, is assigned to half of Manasseh (13). He is one of the two defeated
kings whose fate supplies encouragement for Joshua (21), not a living participant in the allocation.

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

### Og’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

Deuteronomy identifies Og as the remaining survivor of the Rephaim remnant and describes his iron
bedstead at Rabbah as nine cubits by four (11). These are the bedstead’s dimensions, not a
measurement of his height. This adds information absent from Numbers 21:33–35.

See [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

His defeat also precedes the address (4). The WEB names Bashan, Ashtaroth, and Edrei; Numbers 21:33
locates his battle at Edrei. No new campaign is narrated here.

See [[The Words of Moses Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

### Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan — Numbers 32:1–42

*The kingdom of Og king of Bashan* is given with Sihon's to Gad, Reuben, and half of Manasseh (33).

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.

King of Bashan who brings his people to fight Israel at Edrei (33). Yahweh assures Moses of victory;
Og, his sons, and his people are struck until no survivors remain, and Israel possesses the land
(34–35). This unit gives no physical description of Og.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Recalled as *of the remnant of the Rephaim*, living at Ashtaroth and Edrei and ruling Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all Bashan to the border of the Geshurites and Maacathites (12:4–5). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
