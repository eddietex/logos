# Gershon

## Overview

**[[Levi]]'s firstborn, named once in Genesis and never again in it.**

> **11** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. (46:11)

**That clause is the whole of his appearance in Genesis** — a list item in the register of the seventy
who went down into Egypt. **Everything the Bible does with him happens after the Exodus**, when Levi's
three sons have become the three houses that carry the tabernacle: **the Gershonites take the fabric**
— the curtains, the tent, its coverings, the screen of the door, the hangings of the court (Numbers
3:25–26) — while [[Kohath]]'s house takes the ark and the holy vessels and [[Merari]]'s takes the
frames and the sockets.

**Chronicles spells him *Gershom***, which is also the name of Moses' firstborn son and is a different
man; the wiki keeps the Genesis spelling. **The singer Asaph is traced to his line** (1 Chronicles
6:39–43), as Heman is to Kohath's and Ethan's to Merari's — **so the three brothers listed in one
clause here end up dividing the tabernacle's freight and, later, its music.**

**Genesis knows none of this and says none of it.** What it says is that a man named Gershon walked
into Egypt with his father and his two brothers, in a party of seventy. See [[Priesthood]].
