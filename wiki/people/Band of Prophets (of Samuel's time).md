# Band of Prophets (of Samuel's time)

## Overview

The first group of prophets in the books stored so far. They are qualified by period because companies of prophets
recur later. This page covers the band Saul meets at the hill of God, and the *company of the prophets* at Naioth
with Samuel at its head (19:20, stored WEB; not yet ingested). The text does not say whether these are the same
group.

### Coming down from the high place — 1 Samuel 10:5–13

Samuel tells Saul that at [[The Hill of God]] he *will meet a band of prophets coming down from the high place with
a lute, a tambourine, a pipe, and a harp before them; and they will be prophesying* (10:5). The noun is *ḥeḇel*
(WLC), elsewhere in the files searched a cord or a measured region (Deuteronomy 3:4; 32:9; Joshua 2:15). Its use for
a group of people is found only here (10:5, 10; lemma 2256). Their prophesying is described by its music and its
visible activity. No words are reported. When they meet Saul, *the Spirit of God came mightily on him, and he
prophesied among them* (10:10), and people who knew him ask *Is Saul also among the prophets?* A man of the place
answers, *Who is their father?* (10:11–12). That question may concern who leads the band, but the text does not
say. See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].

The only earlier group prophesying in the files searched is the seventy elders, with Eldad and Medad (Numbers
11:25–27). See [[Would That All Were Prophets - Numbers 11, Joel 2, and Acts 2]].
