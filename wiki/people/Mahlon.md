# Mahlon

## Overview

Son of [[Elimelech]] and [[Naomi]], named first of the two sons (Ruth 1:2), an *Ephrathite of Bethlehem
Judah*. He marries a Moabite woman in Moab and dies there after about ten years, with his brother [[Chilion]]
(1:4–5). Ruth 1:4 does not say which son married which woman; Ruth 4:10 does: *Ruth the Moabitess, the
wife of Mahlon*. No child of his is reported, and Boaz marries Ruth *to raise up the name of the dead on
his inheritance* (4:10).

**The name.** Often linked with a root meaning *to be sick*; the etymology is disputed and is reported
here, not built on.

See [[Famine and Death in Moab]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 1:2–5; 4:9–10.
