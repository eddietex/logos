# Hobab

## Overview

### The unnamed brother-in-law — Judges 1:16

Judges 1:16 sends *the children of the Kenite, Moses' brother-in-law* up with Judah, without a name.
Judges 4:11 names *the children of Hobab, Moses' brother-in-law*, and on that basis the wiki reads 1:16
as Hobab's family. If so, it is the nearest the text comes to showing Moses' promise of Numbers 10:32
kept, *whatever good Yahweh does to us, we will do the same to you*. The text does not say so.

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

**A Midianite relative of Moses, asked to go with Israel from Sinai as its guide.** Numbers 10:29 names him *Hobab, the
son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law*, and that is nearly everything the Torah says. The name comes only
here and at Judges 4:11 (WLC, by the tags).

**The conversation.** Moses invites him to the place Yahweh promised, offering to treat him well (29). He refuses, and
says he will go to his own land and relatives (30). Moses asks again, because he knows how to camp in the wilderness and
can be Israel's *eyes*, and promises him a share of whatever good Yahweh does (31–32). No answer is given, and the
march goes on (33). See [[The March from Sinai]].

**Who he is.** *Ḥōtēn mōšeh* (WLC) can attach to Hobab or to Reuel at Numbers 10:29. If it means Reuel, Hobab is Zipporah's
brother, as WEB's *brother-in-law* takes it at Judges 4:11. If it means Hobab, he is Moses' father-in-law, and the
question is how he relates to [[Jethro]]. The wiki keeps him on a separate page and adopts neither. The readings are at
[[Jethro]] under *The names*.

**Afterwards.** Numbers does not say what he did. Judges 1:16 has *the children of the Kenite, Moses' brother-in-law* in
the land with Judah, and Judges 4:11 has *the children of Hobab*, so his people, or some of them, ended up in Canaan. See
[[Hobab and the Kenites - Numbers 10, Judges 1 and 4, and 1 Samuel 15]].
