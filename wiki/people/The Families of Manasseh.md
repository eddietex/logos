# The Families of Manasseh

## Overview

Collective page for the ancestral families named in Joshua 17:2: Abiezer, Helek, Asriel,
Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. They identify descendant groups receiving the western lot;
the text does not narrate six living men appearing before Joshua. Gilead, son of Machir,
also belongs to the genealogy: Hepher’s son Zelophehad descends through Gilead and Machir
from Manasseh (3). Gilead the ancestor is distinct from [[Gilead]] the region; this Shechem
is not [[Shechem (son of Hamor)]] or [[Shechem (city)]], and Hepher is not [[Hepher (city)]].

The five daughters receive their father’s inheritance within these families (3–6).
The six family names and ten parts are not accompanied by a detailed allocation table.
Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–6.
