# Delilah

## Overview

### The woman of the valley of Sorek — Judges 16:4–22

*It came to pass afterward, that he loved a woman in the valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah* (16:4).
She is the only woman in Samson's story whose name is given; his mother, the Timnite wife, her sister,
and the prostitute at Gaza are all unnamed. The text gives her a home, [[The Valley of Sorek]], and no
father, no people, and no age. Whether she is a Philistine or an Israelite it does not say.

*The lords of the Philistines* come up to her with an offer: *Entice him, and see in which his great
strength lies … and we will each give you eleven hundred pieces of silver* (5). She asks Samson plainly
four times, binds him three times with what he names, and cries *The Philistines are on you, Samson!*
each time, with an ambush waiting in the inner room (6–14). Then she accuses him: *How can you say, 'I
love you,' when your heart is not with me?* (15), and *pressed him daily with her words* until *his soul
was troubled to death* (16). When he tells her *all his heart*, she sees it, sends for the lords, *makes
him sleep on her knees*, calls a man to shave the seven locks, and *began to afflict him* (17–19). The
lords come *and brought the money in their hand* (18). She disappears from the text after verse 20.

**What the text says and does not say.** It says Samson loved her (4). It never says she loved him, and
it never says she did not. It gives her a motive, the silver, and no other; it passes no verdict on her.
She does what the Timnite wife did under threat of fire (14:15–17), for money and under no threat. See
[[Entice Him - The Timnite Wife and Delilah]].

**The sum.** *Eleven hundred pieces of silver* from each lord is the sum Micah steals from his mother in
the next chapter (17:2), stolen inside an Israelite house and made into an idol. The phrase occurs in
Judges only in these two places (WLC). See
[[Eleven Hundred Pieces of Silver - Delilah and Micah's Mother]].

**Jael's mirror.** A woman, a sleeping man, and a pin, *yātēd*, fastened, *tāqaʿ* (16:14, WLC): the words
of Jael's killing of Sisera (4:21). Jael is blessed *above women* (5:24); Delilah is paid. See
[[Jael and Sisera]].

**Her name.** *Dəlîlâ* (WLC lemma 1807). Proposed meanings, often linked to *dal*, *weak, low*, or
*dallâ*, *hair, thrum*, or *laylâ*, *night*, are reported, not checked, and the text gives none.

See [[Samson and Delilah]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 4:21; 5:24; 14:15–17; 16:4–22; 17:2; Hebrew WLC
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.

## Timeline / key events
- Loved by Samson, in the valley of Sorek (Judges 16:4).
- Offered eleven hundred pieces of silver by each of the lords of the Philistines (16:5).
- Binds Samson with green cords, new ropes, and the loom; each time he breaks free (16:6–14).
- Presses him daily until he tells her the secret of the razor (16:15–17).
- Has his seven locks shaved while he sleeps on her knees; the lords pay her (16:18–20).
