# Chilion

## Overview

Son of [[Elimelech]] and [[Naomi]], named second of the two sons (Ruth 1:2), an *Ephrathite of Bethlehem
Judah*. He marries a Moabite woman and dies in Moab with his brother [[Mahlon]] (1:4–5). The text never says
whom he married; since Ruth 4:10 makes Ruth Mahlon's wife, [[Orpah]] was presumably his. That is an
inference, not a statement of the text. Boaz buys *all that was Chilion's and Mahlon's* (4:9).

**The name.** Often linked with a root meaning *to fail* or *come to an end*; the etymology is disputed
and is reported here, not built on.

See [[Famine and Death in Moab]]. Source: stored WEB Ruth 1:2–5; 4:9–10.
