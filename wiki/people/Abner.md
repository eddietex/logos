# Abner

## Overview

### Captain of Saul's army — 1 Samuel 14:50–51

*The name of the captain of his army was Abner the son of Ner, Saul's uncle* (14:50), *śar-ṣəḇāʾô* (WLC). Verse 51
adds *Ner the father of Abner was the son of Abiel*. Read with 9:1, where Kish is also *the son of Abiel*, Ner and
Kish are brothers, Ner is Saul's uncle, and Abner is Saul's first cousin. The phrase *Saul's uncle* in 14:50 stands
after both names and can be read of either; Abner as the uncle is also proposed (recalled, not checked). See
[[Ner]] and [[Kish (father of Saul)]] for the Chronicles genealogy, which differs.

The name is spelled *ʾăḇînēr* here and *ʾaḇnēr* at 14:51 and everywhere after (WLC; morphhb lemma 74 for both).
He is the first named Israelite to hold the title. In the WLC of Genesis–1 Samuel, *śar ṣāḇāʾ* is otherwise said
of foreign commanders, Phicol to Abimelech (Genesis 21:22, 32; 26:26) and Sisera to Jabin (Judges 4:2, 7; 1 Samuel
12:9); of the *commander of Yahweh's army* before Joshua (Joshua 5:14–15); and in the plural, unnamed, of the
*captains of armies* Deuteronomy appoints for war (20:9) (stored WEB; WLC). See
[[Saul's Wars and Household]].

**Ahead (not yet ingested).** He sleeps beside Saul in the camp David enters, and is
mocked by David for not guarding the king (26:5–15) (stored WEB). After Saul's death he makes *Ishbosheth the son
of Saul* king (2 Samuel 2:8, WEB at bible-api.com).

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 9:1; 14:50–51; 20:25; 26:5–15; Judges 4:2, 7. 2 Samuel 2:8 checked at
bible-api.com (`translation=web`), 2026-11-19. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb).

### I can't tell — 1 Samuel 17:55–57

His first act in the narrative is to not know. Saul, watching David go out, asks him *Abner, whose son is this
youth?*, and he answers on oath, *As your soul lives, O king, I can't tell* (17:55). *Ḥê-nap̄šəḵā … ʾim-yāḏāʿtî*,
literally *if I know*, is the form of oath that swears a negative (WLC); Hannah had used *as your soul lives* to Eli
(1:26, stored WEB). Told *Inquire whose son the young man is!*, he takes David as he returns from the killing and
brings him *before Saul with the head of the Philistine in his hand* (17:56–57). The captain of the army does not
know the king's armor bearer of 16:21; the tension is recorded on
[[Whose Son Is This Youth - 1 Samuel 16.18-22 and 17.55-58]], not reconciled. See [[Jonathan Loves David]] and
[[By the Life of Pharaoh - Oaths Sworn by a King]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:26; 16:21; 17:55–57. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-21.

### By Saul's side at the new moon — 1 Samuel 20:25

At the new-moon feast, *The king sat on his seat, as at other times, even on the seat by the wall; and Jonathan stood
up, and Abner sat by Saul's side, but David's place was empty* (20:25). Abner speaks no word and takes no part in
Saul's outburst against Jonathan; his seat beside the king marks his standing as one of the four regular diners at
Saul's table. See [[Jonathan's Covenant and the Arrows]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 20:25.
