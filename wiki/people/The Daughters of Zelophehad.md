# The Daughters of Zelophehad

## Overview

Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah are named together in Numbers 26:33. Their father
has no sons. They are preserved as a collective page because this unit assigns them no
individual acts. Noah is not the flood patriarch, Milcah is not Nahor’s wife, and Tirzah
is not the city. Their petition belongs to the next pericope, not this census.
