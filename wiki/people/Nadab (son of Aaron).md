# Nadab (son of Aaron)

## Overview

**[[Aaron]]'s firstborn, and the first of the two sons who do not survive.**

> **23** Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, as his wife; and she
> bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. (Exodus 6:23)

**He is named first of four**, in a list that pairs him with [[Abihu]]. The pair goes up Sinai with
Moses, Aaron, and the seventy elders: *"Then Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of the elders of
Israel went up"* (24:9). **Then they die for offering *"strange fire"* before Yahweh** — told in
Leviticus 10, and summarised in Numbers 26:61 (the wiki's rendering of *ʾēš zārâ*, checked in the
WLC). The priesthood passes through his younger brothers. See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]].

The wiki qualifies the page because a later king of Israel has the same name (1 Kings 15).
