# Othniel

## Overview

### Caleb's younger brother — Judges 1:11–13

Judges repeats the capture of Kiriath Sepher and the marriage to Achsah, adding one phrase: Othniel
the son of Kenaz, *Caleb's younger brother* (13). Joshua 15:17 has only *ʾăḥî kālēb*; Judges 1:13 adds
*haqqāṭōn mimmennû*, *the younger than he* (WLC). The WEB attaches *younger* to Othniel, as it does at
3:9. The Hebrew could also be read as describing Kenaz, which would make Othniel Caleb's nephew; the
wiki does not decide. Two chapters later he is the first judge, *Yahweh raised up a savior … even
Othniel the son of Kenaz* (3:9, stored WEB).

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

Son of Kenaz who captures Kiriath Sepher (Debir) and receives Caleb’s daughter Achsah as wife (17). Verse 18 presents Achsah as having him ask her father for a field. The WEB places the brother-of-Caleb description beside his patronymic; this page does not settle the family tree beyond the translated wording.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 15:13–19.
