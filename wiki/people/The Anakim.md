# The Anakim

## Overview

### The whole land taken — Joshua 11:16–23

Joshua *cut off the Anakim* from Hebron, Debir, Anab, and the hill country of Judah and of Israel, so that none remained except in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod (21–22). This stands beside Caleb driving out the three sons of Anak at Hebron (15:13–14) and Caleb’s own reference to the Anakim still *there* (14:12); all are kept, not harmonized.

See [[The Whole Land Taken]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 11:16–23.

### Possession without merit — Deuteronomy 9:1–6

Their great stature and the saying about who can withstand them sharpen the threat (2). Moses answers with Yahweh’s advance (3), not by denying their size. The stored WEB here supplies no Nephilim genealogy.

See [[Not Because of Your Righteousness]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 9:1–6.


### Protected neighbors and the wilderness generation — Deuteronomy 2:1–23

The Anakim serve as the measure of the stature of the Emim and Zamzummim (10–11, 21). The WEB also
classes the Anakim with the Rephaim (11). These comparisons do not supply a genealogy or identify
every named group as identical.

See [[Around Seir and Past Moab and Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.

The people at Hebron whom the spies feared most. They enter at Numbers 13:22: *Ahiman, Sheshai, and
Talmai, the children of Anak, were there*. Numbers 13 never uses the plural form *Anakim*, which
first occurs at Deuteronomy 1:28 by the WLC tags. It has *yəlîdê hāʿănāq* (22, 28) and *bənê ʿănāq*
(33), with the article in the first of these, so *ʿănāq* may be a title or class word more than a
man's name. Its likely link with the word for a neck or necklace is recalled, not checked.

**Three names, always in a group.** Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai appear together at Numbers 13:22,
Joshua 15:14, and Judges 1:10. Sheshai occurs nowhere else by the tags. The Talmai of 2 Samuel 3:3
and the Ahiman of 1 Chronicles 9:17 are other men.

**The spies link them to the Nephilim.** *There we saw the Nephilim, the sons of Anak, who come from
the Nephilim* (13:33). This is the only link anywhere, it comes from the men the chapter condemns,
and Rahlfs's Greek lacks the clause (*καὶ ἐκεῖ ἑωράκαμεν τοὺς γίγαντας*, with no Enach). See
[[The Nephilim]] and [[The Nephilim and the Giants of Canaan]].

**In Deuteronomy's retelling.** At Deuteronomy 1:28 the people quote what *our brothers* said: *we
have seen the sons of the Anakim there!* They are the last and climactic item in the list of fears,
after the taller people and the walled cities. See [[The Spies and the Refusal to Go Up]].

**Other notices.** Deuteronomy 9:2, now ingested, preserves the saying quoted in the passage.
Deuteronomy 2:10–11 and 2:21 use them as the measure of other tall peoples. Joshua 11:21–22 is now
ingested in [[The Whole Land Taken]]. The following Joshua notices remain not yet ingested. Joshua 15:13
calls Arba *the father of Anak*, and Joshua 14:15 has him the greatest man among them, giving
Hebron's old name.
Joshua 15:14 and Judges 1:20 have Caleb drive out the three, and Judges 1:10 has Judah strike them.
See [[Caleb and Hebron - Numbers 13, Joshua 14-15, and Judges 1]].
