# Michal

## Overview

### The younger daughter — 1 Samuel 14:49

*The name of the firstborn Merab, and the name of the younger Michal* (14:49). *Haqqəṭannâ*, *the younger* (WLC),
is the word Laban used of Rachel (Genesis 29:26, stored WEB). She does nothing in the summary; she is named here so
that she is known when she acts. See [[Saul's Wars and Household]].

**Ahead (not yet ingested).** *Michal, Saul's daughter, loved David* (18:20), and Saul gives her to him for a bride
price of Philistine foreskins (18:25–27). She helps David escape from Saul's house (19:11–17). While David is a
fugitive, Saul gives her to *Palti the son of Laish, who was of Gallim* (25:44) (stored WEB). Her story continues
in 2 Samuel.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 14:49; 18:20–27; 19:11–17; 25:44; Genesis 29:26. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb).
