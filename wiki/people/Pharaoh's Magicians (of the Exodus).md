# Pharaoh's Magicians (of the Exodus)

## Overview

**The professionals of Pharaoh's court who answer Aaron's sign with the same sign — and lose their
rods to his.** Exodus never names them or counts them. At 7:11 Pharaoh *"called for the wise men
and the sorcerers"*, and the text then calls them *"the magicians of Egypt"*, ***ḥarṭummê
miṣrayim***, the exact phrase of Genesis 41:8. A single page holds the group, because the text
never separates its members; the precedent is [[Shiphrah and Puah]] and [[Job's Children]]. The
qualifier follows the rule for figures known only by a title: they appear with
[[Pharaoh (of the Exodus)]], and are not the guild that failed [[Pharaoh (of Joseph's time)]].

**Three words describe them in one verse** (7:11): ***ḥăkāmîm***, *wise men*, the word used of the
man Egypt needed and found in Joseph (Genesis 41:33, 39); ***məkaššəpîm***, *sorcerers*, a root
that occurs elsewhere in Genesis, Exodus, and Job only in the law *"You shall not allow a sorceress
to live"* (22:18); and ***ḥarṭummîm***, probably from an Egyptian title for a chief lector priest.
**They are a learned, salaried establishment, not street conjurors.** See
[[The Magicians of Egypt - Hartummim in Genesis, Exodus, and Daniel]].

**Their first act is a success, and the text does not explain it away.** *"They also … did the same
thing with their enchantments"* (7:11) — *bəlahăṭêhem*, a word the morphology tags as *flame* and
many lexicons read as *secret arts*. **Each casts down his rod and it becomes a *tannîn*.** Then
*"Aaron's rod swallowed up their rods"* (7:12). **They lose nothing but their staffs**, and Pharaoh
draws no conclusion from it (7:13). See [[Aaron's Staff Before Pharaoh]].

**Later tradition gave two of them names.** 2 Timothy 3:8 says *"Even as Jannes and Jambres opposed
Moses, so do these also oppose the truth"* — names Exodus does not contain. See
[[Jannes and Jambres - The Magicians Who Opposed Moses]].

**At the first plague they match again, and their match makes things worse.** *"The magicians of
Egypt did the same thing with their enchantments"* (7:22) — *wayyaʿăśû-kēn*, the words used of Moses
and Aaron's obedience two verses earlier, and *bəlāṭêhem*, the spelling usually read *secret arts*.
**What they "do so" is make more blood.** They cannot undo the plague, only repeat it, and the text
does not ask where they found clean water. Pharaoh takes their match as permission to go home (7:23).
See [[Water Turned to Blood]].

**At the frogs they match a third time** — *"brought up frogs on the land of Egypt"* (8:7) — and
again the match adds to the plague. **What they cannot do is take the frogs away**; Pharaoh has to ask
Moses for that (8:8).

**At the lice they fail.** *"The magicians tried with their enchantments to produce lice, but they
couldn't"* (8:18). **The text uses the same *wayyaʿăśû-kēn*, *"they did so"*, that recorded their
successes**, and adds *to bring out the lice, and they could not*. Then they speak, for the only time
in Exodus: ***"This is God's finger"*** (8:19) — *ʾeṣbaʿ ʾĕlōhîm hîʾ*. **They say *ʾĕlōhîm*, not the
name Moses has preached**, and they say *finger*, not *hand*. It is the first confession of God by
Egyptians in the book, and Pharaoh's heart is hardened in the same verse. **They are not mentioned at
the flies.** See [[Frogs, Lice, and Flies]] and
[[The Finger of God - Exodus 8.19, Exodus 31.18, and Luke 11.20]].

**At the boils they cannot even stand.** ***"The magicians couldn't stand before Moses because of
the boils; for the boils were on the magicians, and on all the Egyptians"*** (9:11) — *lōʾ-yākəlû …
laʿămōd lipnê mōšeh*. **This time they do not try to copy the plague at all.** At the lice they
confessed in words; now the plague is on their own bodies. **Moses and Aaron *"stood before
Pharaoh"* (9:10), and the magicians cannot stand before Moses.** God then tells Pharaoh, with the same
verb, *"for this cause I have made you stand"* (9:16).

**This is their last appearance.** *Ḥarṭummîm* does not occur in Exodus after 9:11 (WLC lemma
search). **They are not dismissed, defeated in a contest, or converted; they simply drop out.** Later
tradition names two of them (2 Timothy 3:8); see
[[Jannes and Jambres - The Magicians Who Opposed Moses]]. See [[Livestock, Boils, and Hail]].

## Timeline / key events

- Summoned by Pharaoh as *"the wise men and the sorcerers"*; match the sign of the rod with their
  enchantments; their rods are swallowed by Aaron's — Exodus 7:11–12,

  [[Water Turned to Blood]]

  [[Frogs, Lice, and Flies]]

  [[Livestock, Boils, and Hail]]
