# Pharaoh's Magicians (of the Exodus)

## Overview

**The professionals of Pharaoh's court who answer Aaron's sign with the same sign — and lose their
rods to his.** Exodus never names them or counts them. At 7:11 Pharaoh *"called for the wise men
and the sorcerers"*, and the text then calls them *"the magicians of Egypt"*, ***ḥarṭummê
miṣrayim***, the exact phrase of Genesis 41:8. A single page holds the group, because the text
never separates its members; the precedent is [[Shiphrah and Puah]] and [[Job's Children]]. The
qualifier follows the rule for figures known only by a title: they appear with
[[Pharaoh (of the Exodus)]], and are not the guild that failed [[Pharaoh (of Joseph's time)]].

**Three words describe them in one verse** (7:11): ***ḥăkāmîm***, *wise men*, the word used of the
man Egypt needed and found in Joseph (Genesis 41:33, 39); ***məkaššəpîm***, *sorcerers*, a root
that occurs elsewhere in Genesis, Exodus, and Job only in the law *"You shall not allow a sorceress
to live"* (22:18); and ***ḥarṭummîm***, probably from an Egyptian title for a chief lector priest.
**They are a learned, salaried establishment, not street conjurors.** See
[[The Magicians of Egypt - Hartummim in Genesis, Exodus, and Daniel]].

**Their first act is a success, and the text does not explain it away.** *"They also … did the same
thing with their enchantments"* (7:11) — *bəlahăṭêhem*, a word the morphology tags as *flame* and
many lexicons read as *secret arts*. **Each casts down his rod and it becomes a *tannîn*.** Then
*"Aaron's rod swallowed up their rods"* (7:12). **They lose nothing but their staffs**, and Pharaoh
draws no conclusion from it (7:13). See [[Aaron's Staff Before Pharaoh]].

**Later tradition gave two of them names.** 2 Timothy 3:8 says *"Even as Jannes and Jambres opposed
Moses, so do these also oppose the truth"* — names Exodus does not contain. See
[[Jannes and Jambres - The Magicians Who Opposed Moses]].

### What is ahead

They match the water turned to blood (7:22) and the frogs (8:7); they fail at the lice and say
*"This is God's finger"* (8:18–19); and they cannot stand before Moses because of the boils (9:11).
These sections are written as their pericopes arrive.
