# The Caphtorim

## Overview

People from Caphtor who destroyed and replaced the Avvim in villages as far as Gaza (Deuteronomy
2:23). This verse supplies their origin and action but does not expressly name Yahweh as the agent
or identify them with another people.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.
