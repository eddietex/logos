# Shaul (son of Simeon)

## Overview

**The one man in the register of the seventy whose mother is flagged, in a list full of foreign
mothers.**

> **10** The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanite
> woman. (46:10)

***Ben-hakkənaʿănît*** — ***the son of the Canaanite woman.*** **The register gives no other man in
seventy names an ethnic annotation**, and the annotation is not obviously needed: **[[Judah]]'s five
sons in the next verse but one are the children of [[Shua's Daughter]], a Canaanite** (38:2), **and
the register does not say so**; **[[Joseph]]'s two sons are the children of [[Asenath]], an Egyptian
priest's daughter, and the register gives her a full and honourable pedigree** (46:20).

**Three foreign mothers, three different treatments, and Genesis supplies no rule.** **The wiki does
not invent one.** What can be said is that the note is not a passing slip: **Exodus 6:15 reproduces it
word for word** when it repeats Simeon's sons, **so whatever the reason, the tradition carried the
clause deliberately across two books.**

**He outlasts his brothers in the lists.** **Simeon's sons are given differently at Numbers 26:12–13 —
Nemuel, Jamin, Jachin, Zerah, Shaul, with Ohad absent and two names respelled** — **and Shaul is the
one name that stands unchanged in every list** (Genesis 46:10; Exodus 6:15; Numbers 26:13;
1 Chronicles 4:24), **always last, and at Numbers 26:13 heading a clan.**

**He is not Saul of 1 Samuel — the same Hebrew name — and he is not the Shaul who reigned in Edom**
(36:37); **the wiki qualifies his
page for that reason.** See [[The Nations]] and
[[Not from the Daughters of the Canaanites]].
