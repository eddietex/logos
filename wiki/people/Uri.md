# Uri

## Overview

The father of [[Bezalel]] and son of [[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]], named in the craftsman's
Judahite ancestry (Exodus 31:2). This verse assigns him no craft or office. Exodus 35:30 and 38:22
repeat the genealogy (local WEB; not yet ingested). 1 Chronicles 2:20 also gives Hur–Uri–Bezalel
(WEB verified from bible-api.com on 2026-10-24; not yet ingested). No name meaning or further
biography is inferred.
