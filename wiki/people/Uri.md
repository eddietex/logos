# Uri

## Overview

The father of [[Bezalel]] and son of [[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]], named in the craftsman's
Judahite ancestry (Exodus 31:2). This verse assigns him no craft or office. Exodus 35:30 repeats the
genealogy in Moses' public announcement; 38:22 repeats it in the inventory’s credit for work done; see
[[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]]. 1 Chronicles 2:20 also gives Hur–Uri–Bezalel
(WEB verified from bible-api.com on 2026-10-24; not yet ingested). No name meaning or further
biography is inferred.

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The makers and the surplus — Exodus 35:30–36:7

Moses' public announcement repeats Bezalel's descent through Uri to Hur (35:30). Uri still
identifies the craftsman rather than being given a craft himself. No new biographical event
for Uri is narrated.

See [[Bezalel's Workmen and the Surplus]].

### The outer equipment and inventory — Exodus 38:1–31

Exodus 38:22 repeats his place as Bezalel’s father in the inventory’s attribution. No work, office,
or speech is assigned to Uri; the maker is his son.

See [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]].
