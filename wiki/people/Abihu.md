# Abihu

## Overview

**[[Aaron]]'s second son, never named without [[Nadab (son of Aaron)]].**

> **23** … and she bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. (Exodus 6:23)

His name is usually read *he is my father*. **Every time he appears it is in a pair**: at his birth
(6:23), on the mountain with the elders (24:1, 9), and in his death for *"strange fire"* (Leviticus
10:1–2; Numbers 26:61, checked in the WLC). Numbers' summary of Aaron's sons is a single clause about
the two of them. See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]].

### The lamp service prescribed — Exodus 27:1–21

Exodus 27:21 assigns the lamp outside the veil to Aaron and his sons collectively. Abihu
is included by the family list of 6:23 and the explicit appointment list of 28:1; he is not
individually named in 27:21. This is a prescribed duty, not a report that his service has
begun. See [[The Altar and the Court]].

### The priestly garments prescribed — Exodus 28:1–43

Exodus 28:1 names Abihu individually among Aaron's sons brought near *"that he may minister to
me in the priest's office"* — the first time Exodus appoints the four by name. Coats, sashes,
headbands, and linen breeches are prescribed for the sons (28:40–43), and Moses is to anoint
and consecrate them (28:41). The ordination is commanded in chapter 29; no service is yet
narrated. See [[The Priestly Garments]].

### The ordination prescribed — Exodus 29:1–46

Exodus 29 includes Abihu among *"his sons"* — not by name — who are washed, clothed in
coats, belts, and headbands, and sprinkled with the altar's blood and the anointing oil with their
garments (29:4, 8–9, 21). The sons share in laying hands on the offerings, in the blood on the right
ear, thumb, and big toe, and in the meal of the ram of consecration (29:10, 20, 32). The breast and
thigh of later peace offerings are assigned to Aaron and his sons *"forever"* (29:28). Only Aaron's
head receives the poured oil (29:7). See [[The Ordination of the Priests]].

Exodus 30 includes Abihu among Aaron's *"sons"* — not by name — who wash hands and feet at the
basin before entering the tent or approaching the altar, *"that they not die"* (30:19–21), and who
are anointed with the holy oil (30:30). See
[[The Incense Altar, the Ransom, and the Holy Oil]]. The same chapter forbids *"strange incense"*
(30:9); Leviticus 10:1 (verified WEB) will narrate Nadab and Abihu offering *"strange fire"*. See
[[Strange Incense - Exodus 30.9, Leviticus 10, Numbers 16, and Uzziah]].

### Garments included in the makers' commission — Exodus 31

Exodus 31:10 includes Aaron's sons collectively among those for whom garments must be made. The
individual identification comes from 28:1; this verse does not name each son or narrate his service.
Their clothing is part of the workers' commission in 31:6–11.

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The freewill offering — Exodus 35:1–29

Exodus 35:19 includes Aaron's sons collectively in the public commission to make priestly
garments. The individual identification comes from 28:1, not from a personal name in this
verse. The people's offerings support the holy garments (35:21), but no action by this son,
completed vesting, or ordination is reported here.

See [[The Freewill Offering]].
