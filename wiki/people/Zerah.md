# Zerah

## Overview

**The twin with the scarlet thread on his hand — first by the evidence and second by the birth.**

> **28** When she travailed, one put out a hand, and the midwife took and tied a scarlet thread on his
> hand, saying, “This came out first.”
> **30** Afterward his brother came out, that had the scarlet thread on his hand, and his name was
> called Zerah. (38:28, 30)

***Zeraḥ*** is from ***zāraḥ***, **to *rise* or *shine*, as the sun does** — *"the sun rises"*
(Ecclesiastes 1:5), and the word behind *"the sun of righteousness will arise"* (Malachi 4:2).
**Plausibly named for the scarlet that showed first**, the dawn-colour at the edge of the birth, though
Genesis does not gloss it as it glosses his brother's name. See [[Names and Naming]].

**He is the chapter's second displaced firstborn**, and the displacement is done by the facts rather
than by anyone's decision. The midwife tied the thread *"saying, 'This came out first'"* — **a legal
record, not an ornament**, since inheritance turns on order of birth. **Then the hand went back in.**
A hand is not a birth; [[Perez]] came out; and **the thread became a record of something that had not
happened.** See [[Two Sets of Twins - Jacob and Esau, Perez and Zerah]] and
[[Birthright and the Firstborn]].

**On the scarlet.** ***Šānî*** is the thread of the tabernacle hangings and the priestly vestments
(Exodus 25:4; 28:5–6) and of the cleansing rites for leprosy and for the red heifer (Leviticus 14:4;
Numbers 19:6). **Rahab's cord at Joshua 2:18 is a different word** — *tiqwat ḥûṭ haššānî*, where the
*šānî* is present but the cord is a *tiqwâ* — **and the wiki draws no connection between the two
stories**, noting only that the association is a standing one in later Christian reading and is not
made by either text.

### The line

**His descendants are a real clan and their most famous member is a disaster.**

- **Numbers 26:20.** The Zerahites, counted in Judah beside the Perezites and the Shelanites.
- **Joshua 7:1, 18.** *"Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of
  Judah"* — **the man whose theft from the devoted things gets Israel beaten at Ai**, and the one
  person in Joshua identified by four generations of ancestry. The genealogy is given precisely
  because the lot has to narrow to him.
- **1 Chronicles 2:6** gives him five sons, among them **Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara** — names that
  reappear as the wise men Solomon is measured against at 1 Kings 4:31 and in the titles of Psalms 88
  and 89.
- **Matthew 1:3** names him with Perez in the genealogy of Jesus, though the line runs through his
  brother. **Matthew keeps the twin who is not the ancestor**, which is what Genesis does too.

**Two other men named Zerah** are worth keeping apart from him: a son of Reuel in the Edomite register
([[Reuel (son of Esau)]]'s line, Genesis 36:13, 17, 33), and Zerah the Ethiopian of 2 Chronicles 14:9.
**The Edomite Zerah is already on the wiki inside [[The Descendants of Esau]]** and has no page of his
own, so **no collision arises here**; if he is ever given one, both take a qualifier.
