# Perez

## Overview

**The twin who came second and was born first, and the ancestor of David.**

> **29** As he drew back his hand, behold, his brother came out, and she said, “Why have you made a
> breach for yourself?” Therefore his name was called Perez. (38:29)

***Mah-pāraṣtā ʿāleykā pāreṣ*** — and ***pāraṣ*** **is a violent verb**: to *break through* a wall, to
*burst out*. It is the word for a breached city, and for what Yahweh does to Uzzah at 2 Samuel 6:8,
which leaves the place called *Perez-uzzah*, *the breaking-out against Uzzah*. **The midwife's
exclamation becomes the name**, which is how most of the namings in this chapter work. See
[[Names and Naming]].

**The wall he broke through was his brother.** [[Zerah]] put out a hand, the midwife tied a scarlet
thread on it to record the order of birth, **the hand went back in, and Perez came out.** A hand is not
a birth, and primogeniture turns on the birth — **so the thread is the Bible's first attempt at
documentary evidence and it is contradicted within a verse.** See
[[Two Sets of Twins - Jacob and Esau, Perez and Zerah]] and [[Birthright and the Firstborn]].

**He is the fourth younger-over-elder in Genesis and the last.** Isaac over Ishmael, Jacob over Esau,
Ephraim over Manasseh still to come — and **here the reversal happens inside the act of birth itself**,
with no oracle, no bargain, no blind father, and no deception. It is the only one of the four that
nobody arranges.

### The line

**Everything the rest of the Bible does with Genesis 38 runs through him.**

- **Ruth 4:18–22.** *"Now this is the history of the generations of Perez"* — Hezron, Ram, Amminadab,
  Nahshon, Salmon, Boaz, Obed, Jesse, **David.** The book of Ruth ends on this genealogy, and it starts
  it at Perez rather than at Judah.
- **Ruth 4:12.** The elders of Bethlehem bless Boaz's marriage in his name: *"let your house be like
  the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah."* **The comparison between the two stories is the
  text's own, not the wiki's.**
- **Numbers 26:20–21.** The Perezites, with his sons Hezron and Hamul, in the census of Judah.
- **1 Chronicles 2:4–5** and **1 Chronicles 9:4; Nehemiah 11:4–6** — the register, and the children of
  Perez resettling Jerusalem after the exile.
- **Matthew 1:3; Luke 3:33.** In both genealogies of Jesus. **Matthew names [[Tamar]] with him.**

See [[Tamar in the Genealogies - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, and Matthew 1]] and [[Kingship and Empire]].

**What that means for the chapter is worth stating plainly.** The line that produces the kingship and
the genealogies of both Gospels **descends from a woman who had to trick her father-in-law into
honouring an obligation, and from the child of that night.** Genesis attaches no comment; Ruth,
Chronicles, and Matthew all name her anyway. **The wiki records that the text does not apologise for
this and does not celebrate it either.**

## Appears in

- [[Judah and Tamar]] — Genesis 38:27–30; **the second twin out, named by the midwife for the wall he
  came through.** The chapter opened on a firstborn who died childless and closes on a firstborn
  decided twice — **and the second decision is the one the rest of Scripture builds on**
- [[Jacob's Family Settles in Egypt]] — Genesis 46:12; ***"The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul."*** **His is the
  only branch of the register that runs to a fourth generation**, and **Ruth 4:18 starts its genealogy
  to David from exactly this point.** [[Hezron (son of Perez)]] carries the line to David and into both
  Gospel genealogies; [[Hamul]] carries a clan and stops
