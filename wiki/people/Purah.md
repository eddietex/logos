# Purah

## Overview

### Gideon's servant — Judges 7:10–11

*But if you are afraid to go down, go with Purah your servant down to the camp* (7:10). Purah is Gideon's
*naʿar* (WLC), his young man or attendant, and the only companion of Gideon's named anywhere in his
story. He exists in the text because of Gideon's fear. Yahweh offers him as company for the frightened
commander, and *Then went he down with Purah his servant* (7:11). That is how the reader learns
Gideon was afraid. Purah says nothing and does nothing that is reported. He is with Gideon at the edge
of the enemy camp when the dream is told (7:13–14), and the text does not mention him again.

The name occurs in Genesis–Judges only in these two verses (WLC). Its meaning is not settled here.
The same word, *naʿar*, is used later in Judges for Gideon's son Jether, who does not draw his sword
*for he was afraid, because he was yet a youth* (8:20), and for Abimelech's armor-bearer (9:54) (stored
WEB; not yet ingested).
