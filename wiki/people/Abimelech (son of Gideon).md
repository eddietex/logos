# Abimelech (son of Gideon)

## Overview

### Born in Shechem — Judges 8:31

*His concubine who was in Shechem also bore him a son, and he named him Abimelech* (8:31). He is
introduced after Gideon's *seventy sons conceived from his body* (8:30), as one more son by a different
mother who lives in [[Shechem (city)]], not in Ophrah. Gideon names him himself: *wayyāśem ʾet-šəmô*,
*he set his name* (WLC).

The name is the one borne by the Philistine kings of Gerar in Genesis, which the wiki treats as very
likely a royal title; see [[Abimelech (of Abraham's time)]] and [[Abimelech (of Isaac's time)]]. It is
usually glossed *my father is king* (reported, not checked). Gideon gives it a few verses after he has
told Israel *I will not rule over you, neither shall my son rule over you. Yahweh shall rule over you*
(8:23). The narrator does not explain the choice.

The page is qualified from the start, as the wiki's convention for this name requires: Genesis already
has two Abimelechs.

### Made king at Shechem — Judges 9:1–6

He goes to Shechem, to *his mother's brothers*, and has them ask the town: *Is it better for you that
all the sons of Jerubbaal, who are seventy persons, rule over you, or that one rule over you? Remember
also that I am your bone and your flesh* (9:2). The verb is *māšal*, the verb of his father's *neither
shall my son rule over you* (8:23, WLC). Shechem's heart *inclined to follow* him, *for they said, "He is
our brother"* (9:3), Judah's words of Joseph (Genesis 37:27).

They pay him *seventy pieces of silver out of the house of Baal Berith*, and he hires *vain and light
fellows* (9:4). He goes to *his father's house at Ophrah* and kills *his brothers the sons of
Jerubbaal, being seventy persons, on one stone*. Only Jotham, the youngest, is left, *for he hid
himself* (9:5). Then *all the men of Shechem … and all the house of Millo* make him king *by the oak of
the pillar that was in Shechem* (9:6). He is the first person in Genesis–Judges who is *made king*,
the hiphil of *mālak* (WLC). His name has *melek*, *king*, in it, and Shechem now makes it true. No word
of Yahweh is spoken in the scene. See
[[Your Bone and Your Flesh - Genesis 29.14, Judges 9.2, and 2 Samuel 5.1]] and
[[Seventy Sons and One Hidden - Judges 9 and 2 Kings 10-11]].

His three years as *prince over Israel* (9:22) and his death are ahead (stored WEB; not yet ingested).

See [[Gideon's Ephod and Death]] and [[Abimelech Made King at Shechem]]. Source: stored WEB Genesis 37:27; Judges 8:23, 30–31; 9:1–7, 22; Hebrew of Judges 8:31; 9:2–6 from the WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

### The bramble — Judges 9:7–21

Jotham tells Shechem the fable of the trees from Mount Gerizim. The olive, fig, and vine will not leave
their fruit to be king. The bramble will: *come and take refuge in my shade; and if not, let fire come
out of the bramble, and devour the cedars of Lebanon* (9:15). Jotham never says *Abimelech is the
bramble*, but he applies the bramble's terms to him. Abimelech is *the son of his female servant*, made
king *because he is your brother* (9:18). If Shechem dealt *truly and righteously*, let it rejoice in
him. If not, *let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the men of Shechem … and let fire come out
from the men of Shechem … and devour Abimelech* (9:20). Abimelech does not answer. Jotham flees
*for fear of Abimelech his brother* (9:21). See [[Jotham's Fable]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 9:7–21.
