# Hophni

## Overview

### Priest at Shiloh — 1 Samuel 1:3

Named once in 1 Samuel 1, with his brother: *The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, priests to Yahweh,
were there* (1:3). Nothing more is said of them in the chapter. The next chapter calls *the sons of Eli*
*wicked men*, *bənê ḇəlîyāʿal* (2:12, WLC) — the term [[Hannah]] had asked [[Eli]] not to apply to her
(1:16) — and later both die *in one day* (2:34; 4:11; not yet ingested). The name is commonly taken to be
Egyptian, like his brother's; recalled, not verified. See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:3, 16; 2:12, 34; 4:11. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
