# Hophni

## Overview

### Priest at Shiloh — 1 Samuel 1:3

Named once in 1 Samuel 1, with his brother: *The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, priests to Yahweh,
were there* (1:3). Nothing more is said of them in the chapter. The next chapter calls *the sons of Eli*
*wicked men*, *bənê ḇəlîyāʿal* (2:12, WLC) — the term [[Hannah]] had asked [[Eli]] not to apply to her
(1:16) — and later both die *in one day* (2:34; 4:11; not yet ingested). The name is commonly taken to be
Egyptian, like his brother's; recalled, not verified. See [[Hannah Asks Yahweh for a Son]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 1:3, 16; 2:12, 34; 4:11. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Wicked men who didn't know Yahweh — 1 Samuel 2:12–25

With his brother, one of *the sons of Eli*, who *were wicked men* and *didn't know Yahweh* (2:12). The
pericope does not name them or separate their acts. Their servant takes whatever the three-pronged fork
brings up from the worshippers' pots, and demands raw meat before the fat is burned, *if not, I will take
it by force* (2:13–16): *the men despised the offering of Yahweh* (2:17). They lie with *the women who
served at the door of the Tent of Meeting* (2:22). Eli rebukes them; *they didn't listen to the voice of
their father, because Yahweh intended to kill them* (2:25). *Didn't know Yahweh* is said in the WLC of
Genesis through 1 Samuel only of them, Pharaoh (Exodus 5:2), and the generation after Joshua (Judges
2:10). See [[The Sins of Eli's Sons]],
[[They Didn't Know Yahweh - Pharaoh, the Generation After Joshua, and Eli's Sons]], and
[[Pleased to Kill - Manoah's Wife and Eli's Sons]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:12–25; Exodus 5:2; Judges 2:10. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-17.

### The sign — 1 Samuel 2:34

Named, with his brother [[Phinehas (son of Eli)]], in the sign the [[Man of God (of Eli's time)]] gives Eli: *This will be
the sign to you, that will come on your two sons, on Hophni and Phinehas: in one day they will both die*
(2:34). It is the first time the brothers are named since 1:3. The oracle charges their father with
honouring them above Yahweh and fattening on *the best of all the offerings* (2:29). The sign is fulfilled
at the ark's capture: *the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were slain* (4:11, stored WEB; not yet
ingested). See [[A Man of God Against the House of Eli]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:29, 34; 4:11.

### *His sons brought a curse on themselves* — 1 Samuel 3:13

Not named in the chapter, the brothers are the cause of the sentence Yahweh repeats to the boy Samuel: Eli's
house is judged *because his sons brought a curse on themselves, and he didn't restrain them* (3:13).
*Məqalləlîm lāhem* (WLC), literally *cursing for themselves* or *making themselves contemptible*, is
commonly reported to be a scribal softening of *cursing God*, with the Septuagint reading *speaking evil
of God* (recalled from standard commentary, not checked). The iniquity of the house *shall not be removed
with sacrifice or offering forever* (3:14): the priests who abused the sacrifices (2:13–17) are told the
sacrifices cannot cover them. See [[Yahweh Calls Samuel]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 2:13–17; 3:13–14. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
