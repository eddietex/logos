# Manoah's Wife

## Overview

### The one who sees — Judges 13:2–24

She is never named. *His wife was barren, and childless* (13:2). [[The Angel of Yahweh]] appears to
her, not to her husband, and tells her she will bear a son who will be a Nazirite *from the womb* and
will *begin to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines* (13:3–5). The food rules are hers: no
wine or strong drink, nothing unclean (13:4).

Her report to [[Manoah]] is exact about the visitor and free with the message. *A man of God came to
me, and his face was like the face of the angel of God, very awesome. I didn't ask him where he was
from, neither did he tell me his name* (13:6). She repeats the son and the rules, leaves out the razor
and *begin to save*, and adds *to the day of his death* (13:7). Whether the change is a foreshadowing or
a mother's reading, the text does not say.

The second visit also comes to her, *as she sat in the field* (13:9). She *hurried and ran* to bring
her husband (13:10). When the angel speaks to Manoah, he only repeats what she was told: *Of all that I
said to the woman let her beware* (13:13).

After the angel goes up in the altar flame, Manoah says *We shall surely die, because we have seen God*.
She answers with three reasons: *If Yahweh were pleased to kill us, he wouldn't have received a burnt
offering and a meal offering at our hand, and he wouldn't have shown us all these things, nor would he
have told us such things as these at this time* (13:22–23). The narrator does not comment, and the
next verse proves her right: *The woman bore a son, and named him Samson* (13:24). She, not her husband,
gives the name.

She stands in the line of barren women whose sons are announced: Sarah (Genesis 18:9–15), Rebekah
(25:21), Rachel (30:22), and after her Hannah (1 Samuel 1). Of the women the text calls *barren*,
*ʿăqārâ*, in Genesis and Judges (Genesis 11:30; 25:21; 29:31; Judges 13:2–3, WLC), she is the only one
with no name. See [[The Barren Matriarchs]] and [[Barrenness]].

Later tradition is reported to give her a name; that is recalled, not verified, and not used here.

Source: stored WEB Genesis 18:9–15; 25:21; 30:22; Judges 13:2–24.

## Timeline / key events
- Barren; visited by the angel of Yahweh and promised a son (Judges 13:2–5).
- Reports the visit to her husband (13:6–7).
- Visited again in the field; brings Manoah (13:9–10).
- Answers her husband's fear of death (13:23).
- Bears and names Samson (13:24).
