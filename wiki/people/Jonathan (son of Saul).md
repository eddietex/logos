# Jonathan (son of Saul)

## Overview

The son of [[Saul]], introduced without a patronymic as the commander of one thousand men at
[[Gibeah (Benjamin)]] (13:2); 13:16 calls him *Jonathan his son* (stored WEB). Not to be confused with
[[Jonathan (son of Gershom)]], the Levite priest of the Danites' image (Judges 18:30). *Yônāṯān* (WLC) is
usually glossed *Yahweh has given* (the gloss is not checked against a lexicon). The page grows as 1 Samuel is
ingested.

### First blow at Geba — 1 Samuel 13:1–15

Jonathan's first act is the one that starts the war: he *struck the garrison of the Philistines that was in Geba*
(13:3), *nəṣîḇ pəlištîm* (WLC). The next verse reports what *all Israel heard*: *Saul had struck the garrison*
(13:4). The text records the change of name without comment. The Philistines answer with a great army at
[[Michmash]], Israel scatters, and at [[Gilgal]] Saul, waiting for Samuel, offers the burnt offering and is told
his kingdom will not continue (13:5–14). Jonathan is not at Gilgal. The dynasty forfeited there would have been
his, and the next chapter makes him the hero of Michmash (14:1–23, stored WEB; not yet ingested). See
[[Saul's Sacrifice at Gilgal]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:1–16; Judges 18:30. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-18.

### One of two swords — 1 Samuel 13:16–23

Called *Jonathan his son* (13:16), he holds Geba with Saul while the Philistine raiders go out from Michmash. With no
smith in Israel, *neither sword nor spear was found in the hand of any of the people … but Saul and Jonathan his son
had them* (13:22). The verse sets up his attack on the garrison at *the pass of Michmash* (13:23; 14:1, stored WEB;
not yet ingested). His sword later passes to David with his robe (18:4, stored WEB; not yet ingested). See
[[No Smith in Israel]] and
[[Yahweh Doesn't Save with Sword and Spear - 1 Samuel 13.22, 17.47, and Judges 5.8]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 13:16–23; 14:1; 18:4.

## Timeline / key events

- Commands a thousand of Saul's three thousand at Gibeah of Benjamin — 1 Samuel 13:2.
- Strikes the Philistine garrison at Geba; the blow is reported as Saul's — 13:3–4.
- Holds Geba with Saul; one of the two armed men in Israel's army — 13:16, 22.
