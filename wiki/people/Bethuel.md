# Bethuel

## Overview

The eighth and last of Milcah's sons, listed without an epithet, and the only one of the twelve the
narrative ever needs.

He arrives at the end of a run of names — *"Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of
Aram, Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel"* (Genesis 22:22) — and then the register stops
being a register for one clause: ***"Bethuel became the father of Rebekah"*** (22:23). **That
sentence is why the genealogy exists.** Everything in front of it is the road to it. See
[[Nahor's Line and the Burial of Sarah]].

**His position in the family is worth stating carefully**, because Genesis will lean on it. He is the
son of [[Nahor (brother of Abram)]] and [[Milcah]], and Milcah is [[Haran (son of Terah)]]'s
daughter — so both his parents are [[Terah]]'s descendants, and he is [[Abraham]]'s nephew twice
over. His daughter [[Rebekah]] is therefore Abraham's great-niece, and when Abraham sends his
servant *"to my country, and to my relatives"* (24:4) for a wife for [[Isaac]], the servant is being
sent to Bethuel's house. Bethuel's son Laban then supplies both of Jacob's wives a generation later.
**Two of the three patriarchal marriages come out of this one man's household.**

### What he does, and what he does not

Genesis calls him *"Bethuel the Syrian"* — *hāʾărammî*, **the Aramean** — at 25:20 and 28:5, which
is the book's way of marking the branch that stayed in Mesopotamia as having become a different
people. See [[The Nations]].

**He is almost absent from the chapter that turns on his daughter.** In Genesis 24 the negotiation is
run by Rebekah's brother Laban; Bethuel is named twice (24:15, 24:24), speaks once and only jointly
— *"Then Laban and Bethuel answered, 'The thing proceeds from Yahweh. We can't speak to you bad or
good.'"* (24:50) — and by 24:53 the gifts go *"to her brother and her mother"*, with no
mention of a father at all. The oddity is old and unexplained; readings range from Bethuel being
elderly or ill, to a household where the brother held authority over a sister's marriage, to the
name in 24:50 being a later insertion. **Genesis simply does not say, and the wiki does not settle
it.**

**The name itself** is usually read as *bêt ʾēl*, **house of God**, or as *bətû ʾēl*, *man of God* —
neither derivation is secure, and *Bethuel* is also a place name in the Simeonite south
(1 Chronicles 4:30; compare *Bethul*, Joshua 19:4), which is the pattern the rest of Nahor's sons
follow too.

## Timeline / key events

- Born to [[Nahor (brother of Abram)]] and [[Milcah]], eighth of their eight sons — Genesis 22:22,
  [[Nahor's Line and the Burial of Sarah]]
- Becomes the father of [[Rebekah]] — Genesis 22:23
- Named as Rebekah's father when Abraham's servant meets her at the well — Genesis 24:15, 24,
  [[A Wife for Isaac]]
- Answers the servant jointly with Laban, in the only words attributed to him — Genesis 24:50
- Not named in the verse that distributes the gifts, nor in the one that asks for a delay —
  Genesis 24:53, 55
- Called *the Aramean*, and named as the father of Rebekah and of Laban — Genesis 25:20; 28:5
  *(not yet ingested)*
