# The Emim

## Overview

Earlier inhabitants of Moab, described as great, numerous, and tall like the Anakim, and counted as
Rephaim (Deuteronomy 2:10–11). Emim is the name attributed to the Moabites. These verses do not
explicitly recount their destruction; Genesis 14:5 also names Emim in the eastern campaign.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.
