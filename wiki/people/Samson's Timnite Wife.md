# Samson's Timnite Wife

## Overview

### The woman of Timnah — Judges 14:1–20

*A woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines* (14:1), never named. [[Samson]] sees her and
tells his parents to get her for him; they object that she is of *the uncircumcised Philistines*, and he
answers *she pleases me well*, literally *she is right in my eyes* (14:3, WLC *yāšərâ bəʿênāy*). The
narrator adds that *it was of Yahweh; for he sought an occasion against the Philistines* (14:4). She
does not speak until the feast.

At the seven-day feast the thirty companions threaten her: *Entice your husband, that he may declare to
us the riddle, lest we burn you and your father's house with fire* (14:15). She weeps before him, *You
just hate me, and don't love me*, and weeps *the seven days*; on the seventh he tells her *because she
pressed him severely*, and she tells *the children of her people* (14:16–17). Samson's answer to the
solvers calls her *my heifer* (14:18). The chapter ends with her *given to his companion who had been
his friend* (14:20).

She is caught between a husband who keeps his secrets and her own people who threaten to burn her. The
text gives her one speech, a complaint of being unloved, and no verdict. Her pressing of Samson, and the
Philistines' *Entice*, return almost word for word with Delilah (16:5, 16, WLC *pattî*, *hēṣîqâ*). See
[[Entice Him - The Timnite Wife and Delilah]].

See [[Samson's Riddle]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 14:1–20; 16:5, 16; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-16.

### Given away, then burned — Judges 15:1–6

*In the time of wheat harvest* Samson comes back with a young goat to visit her, and her father will not
let him in: *I most certainly thought that you utterly hated her; therefore I gave her to your
companion*. He offers her younger sister instead (15:1–2). Samson burns the Philistines' grain, and when
they ask who did it they are told it was *the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he has taken his wife,
and given her to his companion*. *The Philistines came up, and burned her and her father with fire*
(15:6). The threat of 14:15, *lest we burn you and your father's house with fire*, is carried out after
she had done what it demanded. She does not speak in chapter 15.

Her father, *the Timnite*, gets no page of his own: he speaks once, to refuse Samson and offer the sister,
and dies with her. *Hate*, his reason for giving her away (15:2), is her own word from 14:16, *You just
hate me*. See [[Samson Against the Philistines at Lehi]] and [[We Will Burn Your House - Judges 12.1, 14.15, and 15.6]].
Source: stored WEB Judges 14:15–16; 15:1–6; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.

## Timeline / key events
- Seen by Samson in Timnah; chosen against his parents' objection (Judges 14:1–3, 7).
- Threatened by the thirty companions; weeps seven days and draws out the riddle (14:15–17).
- Given to Samson's companion (14:20).
- Withheld from Samson by her father, who offers her younger sister (15:1–2).
- Burned with her father by the Philistines (15:6).
