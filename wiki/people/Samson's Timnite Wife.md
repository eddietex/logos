# Samson's Timnite Wife

## Overview

### The woman of Timnah — Judges 14:1–20

*A woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines* (14:1), never named. [[Samson]] sees her and
tells his parents to get her for him; they object that she is of *the uncircumcised Philistines*, and he
answers *she pleases me well*, literally *she is right in my eyes* (14:3, WLC *yāšərâ bəʿênāy*). The
narrator adds that *it was of Yahweh; for he sought an occasion against the Philistines* (14:4). She
does not speak until the feast.

At the seven-day feast the thirty companions threaten her: *Entice your husband, that he may declare to
us the riddle, lest we burn you and your father's house with fire* (14:15). She weeps before him, *You
just hate me, and don't love me*, and weeps *the seven days*; on the seventh he tells her *because she
pressed him severely*, and she tells *the children of her people* (14:16–17). Samson's answer to the
solvers calls her *my heifer* (14:18). The chapter ends with her *given to his companion who had been
his friend* (14:20).

She is caught between a husband who keeps his secrets and her own people who threaten to burn her. The
text gives her one speech, a complaint of being unloved, and no verdict. Her pressing of Samson, and the
Philistines' *Entice*, return almost word for word with Delilah (16:5, 16, WLC *pattî*, *hēṣîqâ*). See
[[Entice Him - The Timnite Wife and Delilah]].

See [[Samson's Riddle]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 14:1–20; 16:5, 16; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-16.

### Ahead

At 15:1–2 Samson returns with a young goat to visit her and her father refuses him, offering her younger
sister. At 15:6 the Philistines *burned her and her father with fire*, the threat of 14:15 carried out
after all. Not yet ingested (stored WEB).
