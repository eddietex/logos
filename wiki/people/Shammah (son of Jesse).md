# Shammah (son of Jesse)

## Overview

### *Yahweh has not chosen this one, either* — 1 Samuel 16:9

The third son of [[Jesse]]. *Then Jesse made Shammah to pass by. He said, "Yahweh has not chosen this one, either"*
(16:9). He is not described. See [[Samuel Anoints David]].

**Later.** He is *the third* among the three eldest who follow Saul to the battle (17:13, stored WEB; not yet
ingested).

**Name.** *Šammâ* (WLC). 1 Chronicles 2:13 calls Jesse's third son *Shimea* (WEB checked at bible-api.com). The
lists agree on his place in the order and differ in the form of the name. The wiki treats them as one man, which the
order supports, and records the difference. The qualifier keeps him apart from the other men named Shammah in
Scripture, including the Edomite chief of Genesis 36:13.
