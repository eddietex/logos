# King of Ai (of Joshua's time)

## Overview

### At Gibeon — Joshua 10:1–15

His defeat is recalled in Adoni-Zedek’s intelligence report (1). He is not a participant in the new
coalition.

See [[The Sun Stands Still at Gibeon]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15.


### The capture of Ai — Joshua 8:1–29

The unnamed ruler of [[Ai]] in the conquest narrative. Qualified by the period, as the wiki does for
unnamed rulers known only by title (compare [[King of Jericho (of Joshua's time)]]), because the
text gives no personal name.

Yahweh names him first in the promise — *I have given into your hand the king of Ai, with his
people, his city, and his land* (8:1) — and orders that he be treated *as you did to Jericho and her
king* (2). When he sees Israel's army in the valley north of the city, he and his men hurry out
early *at the time appointed, before the Arabah*, and the narrator adds: *he didn't know that there
was an ambush against him behind the city* (14). His confidence is the confidence Joshua's plan had
predicted — the memory of Israel's first rout (7:4–5; 8:5–6). He is captured alive and brought to
Joshua (23). After the city is destroyed he is hanged on a tree until evening, taken down at
sundown, thrown at the entrance of the city gate, and covered with *a great heap of stones that
remains to this day* (29).

He is the first king in the conquest whose death the text narrates, and the first execution in
Joshua carried out with the timing of Deuteronomy 21:22–23. The text does not say how he was
killed; the *hanging* displays a body (see
[[Hanged on a Tree - Genesis 40, Deuteronomy 21, and Galatians 3]]). He reappears in the summary
list of defeated kings, *the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel* (12:9).

See [[The Capture of Ai]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 8:1–29; 12:9; WLC (morphhb) where Hebrew is
named on the passage page.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Counted second, *the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one* (12:9). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].

## Timeline / key events

- Named in Yahweh's promise to Joshua (8:1–2).
- Sees Israel's army and leads his men out early, unaware of the ambush (14).
- Captured alive after the ambush takes and burns the city (23).
- Hanged until evening, taken down at sundown, buried under a heap of stones at the gate (29).
- Joshua 12:9 — counted.
