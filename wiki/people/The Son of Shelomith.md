# The Son of Shelomith

## Overview

Unnamed son of [[Shelomith]] and an unnamed Egyptian father. He fights with
an Israelite man, blasphemes the Name, and is brought to [[Moses]]. After custody
pending Yahweh’s ruling, the congregation stones him outside the camp
(Leviticus 24:10–23). This relational title supplies no personal name.

His mixed parentage is explicit; the claim that mixed ancestry caused his offense
or disqualified him from Israel is not. The stated capital offense is blasphemy,
and the ruling applies to foreigner and native-born alike.

## Timeline / key events

- Leviticus 24:10–12: fight, curse, and custody.
- Leviticus 24:13–23: divine ruling, Moses’ announcement, and execution.
