# King of Ammon (of Jephthah's time)

## Overview

### The claim to Gilead — Judges 11:12–28

The unnamed king of the children of Ammon whose war brings Jephthah back from Tob (11:4–5). Jephthah
asks him by messengers what quarrel he has with Israel (11:12). He answers with a claim: *Israel took
away my land, when he came up out of Egypt, from the Arnon even to the Jabbok, and to the Jordan. Now
therefore restore that territory again peaceably* (11:13).

Those are the bounds Numbers gives for the land Israel took from Sihon king of the Amorites (Numbers
21:24), land Sihon had taken from *the former king of Moab* (21:26). The king of Ammon claims it as his
own. Jephthah answers that the land was Amorite and that Yahweh gave it to Israel (11:15–23); he names
*Chemosh your god* to this king, though Chemosh is called Moab's god elsewhere (Numbers 21:29). See the
passage for the readings.

*However the king of the children of Ammon didn't listen to the words of Jephthah which he sent him*
(11:28). The text gives him no name and no further words. Judges 11:32–33 says Yahweh delivered Ammon
into Jephthah's hand, and Ammon was *subdued*; the king himself is not mentioned again. See
[[Jephthah's Vow]].

The qualifier follows the rule for figures known by a title: keyed to the narrative, not to a dynasty.

See [[Jephthah's Message to the King of Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 11:4–5, 12–28, 32–33;
Numbers 21:24, 26, 29.

## Timeline / key events
- Makes war on Israel in Gilead — Judges 11:4
- Claims the land from the Arnon to the Jabbok and demands its return — Judges 11:13
- Does not listen to Jephthah's second message — Judges 11:28
- His people are struck and subdued from Aroer to Minnith — Judges 11:32–33
