# Ram (son of Hezron)

## Overview

### Third in the generations of Perez — Ruth 4:19

*Hezron became the father of Ram, and Ram became the father of Amminadab* (Ruth 4:19). That clause is all
Ruth says of him: no wife, no age, no act. He stands third in the ten-name line that runs from [[Perez]]
to [[David]], between [[Hezron (son of Perez)]] and [[Amminadab]]. See [[The Generations of Perez]].

**Elsewhere in the line.** 1 Chronicles 2:9 names him as one of Hezron's three sons — *Jerahmeel, Ram, and
Chelubai* — and 2:10 repeats Ruth's link to Amminadab. Matthew 1:3–4 keeps him as *Ram*; Luke 3:33 in the
WEB reads *Aram* at this place (WEB at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17; manuscripts are reported to differ here,
recalled and not checked against a Greek edition). See
[[From Perez to David - Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 2, Matthew 1, and Luke 3]].

**Why the qualifier.** *Rām* (WLC lemma 7410) also names a son of Jerahmeel, this Ram's nephew
(1 Chronicles 2:25, 27), and Elihu is *of the family of Ram* (Job 32:2; see [[Elihu the Buzite]]). A bare
`Ram` page would collide with them and with the animal in many page titles.

Source: stored WEB Ruth 4:19; 1 Chronicles 2:9–10, Matthew 1:3–4, and Luke 3:33 from the WEB at
bible-api.com; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
