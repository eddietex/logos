# Abinadab (son of Jesse)

## Overview

### *Yahweh has not chosen this one, either* — 1 Samuel 16:8

The second son of [[Jesse]]. At the sacrifice in Bethlehem, *Jesse called Abinadab, and made him pass before Samuel.
He said, "Yahweh has not chosen this one, either"* (16:8). The text says nothing else about him in this scene. The
verb *made him pass*, *wayyaʿăḇirēhû* (WLC), is repeated for Shammah and for the seven (9–10). See
[[Samuel Anoints David]].

**Later.** He is *next to* Eliab among the three eldest who follow Saul to the battle (17:13, stored WEB; not yet
ingested). 1 Chronicles 2:13 lists him as *the second* (WEB checked at bible-api.com).

**Name.** Two other men named Abinadab appear in 1 Samuel: [[Abinadab (of Kiriath Jearim)]], in whose house the ark
stayed (7:1), and a son of Saul who dies on Gilboa (31:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested). Hence the qualifier.
