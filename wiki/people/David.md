# David

## Overview

Son of Jesse, second king of Israel, and the father of [[Solomon]]. The narrative of his life belongs
to 1 and 2 Samuel and the opening of 1 Kings, none of which has yet been ingested, so this page is a
placeholder that grows as the wiki reaches him. He enters the wiki by name in the heading of Proverbs,
where he is mentioned only to identify Solomon: *"The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of
Israel"* (Proverbs 1:1). See [[The Purpose of the Proverbs]].

That patronym carries weight even in passing. It places the book in the line of the dynasty to which
God promised, through Nathan, *"I will set up your offspring after you … He will build a house for my
name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever"* (2 Samuel 7:12–13; WEB, checked against
bible-api.com). Ecclesiastes uses the same identification for its Preacher, *"the son of David, king in
Jerusalem"* (Ecclesiastes 1:1). In the New Testament *"son of David"* becomes a messianic title.

Earlier wiki pages mention him in connection pages — his eating of the bread of the Presence and his
confession in Psalm 51 — without a page to link to: see
[[The Bread of the Presence - David's Hunger and Matthew 12]] and
[[Sin Against God - Joseph at 39.9 and David at Psalm 51]].

Proverbs 4:3–4 recalls a teacher receiving instruction from his father. The heading at
1:1 makes a David–Solomon association possible, but 4:1–9 names neither man.
[[Get Wisdom]] records the scene as unnamed family instruction, not an established event in
David's biography. The mother's only child in the WEB (4:3) is not independent evidence
for the composition of the royal household. Source: stored WEB Proverbs 1:1; 4:1–9.
