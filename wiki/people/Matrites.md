# Matrites

## Overview

The family, *mišpāḥâ*, within [[Benjamin]] that the choosing at Mizpah takes before it takes Saul: *He brought
the tribe of Benjamin near by their families; and the family of the Matrites was chosen. Then Saul the son of Kish
was chosen* (1 Samuel 10:21). This is a collective page.

*Hammaṭrî*, *the Matrite*, occurs only here in the WLC files searched (lemma 4309). No ancestor named Matri
appears in Benjamin's genealogies in the books stored so far (Genesis 46:21; Numbers 26:38–41), and the wiki does
not identify the family with any family named there. In 9:1 Saul's line runs through [[Kish (father of Saul)]] to
Abiel, and 9:21 calls Saul's family *the least of all the families of the tribe of Benjamin*. The text does not
say whether that family is the Matrites.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 9:1, 21; 10:20–21; Genesis 46:21; Numbers 26:38–41. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-17.
