# Zeeb

## Overview

### Prince of Midian — Judges 7:25

*Zeeb they killed at Zeeb's wine press* (7:25). Zeeb is the second of *the two princes of Midian*, taken
with [[Oreb]] by the men of Ephraim at the end of the pursuit. He dies at a wine vat, *yeqeb*, that bears
his name, [[The Winepress of Zeeb]], and his head is brought to Gideon with Oreb's. He never speaks.

His name is the common noun *zəʾēb*, *wolf*, the word of Jacob's blessing, *Benjamin is a ravenous
wolf* (Genesis 49:27) (WLC). As a personal name it occurs in Genesis–Judges only at Judges 7:25 and
8:3 (WLC).

Gideon's story began with wheat beaten out *in the wine press, to hide it from the Midianites* (6:11).
A Midianite prince dies at a wine press. The Hebrew nouns differ, *gat* in 6:11 and *yeqeb* in 7:25
(WLC), and the text does not draw the comparison.

Psalm 83:11 names him beside Oreb, *Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb* (WEB checked; not yet
ingested). See [[The Day of Midian - Oreb and Zeeb in Psalm 83 and Isaiah 9-10]].
