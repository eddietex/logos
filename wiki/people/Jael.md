# Jael

## Overview

*Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite* (Judges 4:17), the woman into whose hand Yahweh *sold* Sisera, as
Deborah had said (4:9). She has not been mentioned before 4:17, and the tent where she kills Sisera is
called hers, not her husband's.

**What she does.** Sisera, running on foot from the rout at the Kishon, comes to her tent *for there was
peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite* (4:17). She goes out to meet
him and invites him in: *Turn in, my lord, turn in to me; don't be afraid* (4:18). She covers him with a
rug, gives him milk when he asks for water, and covers him again (4:19). When he is in a deep sleep she
takes a tent peg and a hammer, goes *softly* to him, and drives the peg through his temple *into the
ground* (4:21). When Barak comes in pursuit she goes out to meet him too and shows him *the man whom
you seek* (4:22).

**What the text says about her.** Judges 4 reports her actions and gives no verdict on them, and it
never says what she was thinking or whether she planned the killing when she invited him in. Judges 5
calls her *blessed above women … above women in the tent* (5:24, next pericope). The narrator of 4:23
gives the victory to God: *God subdued Jabin*.

**The open question.** She is a host who kills her guest, from a household at peace with the king
whose general he is. Against that stand Sisera's twenty years of oppression (4:3), Yahweh's word about
a woman's hand (4:9), and the Song's blessing. The text sets out both and does not decide between them.
The wiki records both and does not choose. See [[Hospitality]] and [[Deception]].

**Account to compare.** Judges 4:21 has Sisera asleep when struck; Judges 5:27 has him *bowed* and
*fell* *at her feet*, which some read as a man standing. Recorded for the next ingest
([[Jael and Sisera]], Notes).

**Her name.** *Yāʿēl* is also the word for *mountain goat*; reported, not verified.
