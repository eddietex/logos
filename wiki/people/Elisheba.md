# Elisheba

## Overview

**[[Aaron]]'s wife, and the woman in the genealogy with the fullest pedigree.**

> **23** Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, as his wife; and she
> bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. (Exodus 6:23)

**She is the only wife in the list given both a father and a brother**, and both are men of Judah:
Amminadab, and [[Nahshon]], who will lead Judah's camp in the wilderness. **Through her the first
priestly house is joined by marriage to the line that runs from Perez to David.** Exodus says nothing
more about her, and she does not appear again by name. See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]] and
[[Elisheba Daughter of Amminadab - Aaron's Marriage into Judah]].

Her name is usually read *my God is an oath* or *my God is abundance*. **Its Greek form is *Elisabet*,
the name of the priest's wife in Luke 1:5** (from memory, not checked).
