# Eglon (person)

## Overview

### The fat king of Moab — Judges 3:12–30

*Yahweh strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done that which was evil
in Yahweh's sight* (12). He gathers *the children of Ammon and Amalek*, strikes Israel, possesses *the
city of palm trees* (Jericho; Deuteronomy 34:3), and Israel serves him eighteen years (13–14). He is
the second oppressor of the judges cycle, and the first whose story is told.

*Now Eglon was a very fat man* (17), *ʾîš bārîʾ məʾōd* (WLC), the adjective used of the fat cattle of
Pharaoh's dream (Genesis 41:2, WLC). He receives Israel's tribute, dismisses his attendants at Ehud's
word, *Keep silence!* (19), and is alone *in the cool upper room* when Ehud says *I have a message from
God to you*. *He arose out of his seat* (20), the only act the text reports him choosing for himself.
Ehud's sword goes in with the handle, *and the fat closed on the blade* (22). His servants, finding
the doors locked, assume *he is covering his feet* (24), an idiom the WEB translates as *relieve
himself* in 1 Samuel 24:3, and wait *until they were ashamed*. When they open the doors, *their lord
had fallen down dead on the floor* (25).

**"Strengthened."** *Wayḥazzēq YHWH ʾet-ʿeglôn* (12, WLC): the same verb and stem as *Yahweh hardened
the heart of Pharaoh* (Exodus 9:12, WLC). With Pharaoh the object is his heart; here it is Eglon
himself. See [[Hardening of the Heart]].

**The name.** *ʿEglôn* (WLC) shares its root letters with *ʿēgel*, *calf*, and is often read as a jibe:
a fatted calf receiving an offering (*minḥâ*, the tribute of 15–18). That is reported, not checked.
The same name, under the same OpenScriptures lemma, belongs to a Canaanite city in Joshua 10 and 15;
see [[Eglon (city)]]. Nothing in the text links the king to the city.

See [[Ehud and Eglon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:12–30; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-13.

## Timeline / key events

- **Judges 3:12–14** — Strengthened by Yahweh; allied with Ammon and Amalek; takes the city of palm
  trees; rules Israel eighteen years.
- **Judges 3:17–20** — Receives Israel's tribute; dismisses his attendants for a *secret message*.
- **Judges 3:21–25** — Killed by Ehud in his upper room; found dead by his servants.
