# Hur (companion of Moses)

## Overview

**The man who held up Moses' other hand.** Hur enters Exodus without introduction, third in a list — *"Moses,
Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill"* (Exodus 17:10) — and his one act is the whole of his
significance: when Moses' hands grew heavy during the battle with Amalek, ***"Aaron and Hur held up his hands,
the one on the one side, and the other on the other side. His hands were steady until sunset"*** (17:12).
**He says nothing**, and the text gives him no father, tribe, or office. See [[The Battle with Amalek]].

**Exodus names him once more in the narrative**, again beside Aaron and again as Moses' support: when Moses goes
up the mountain he tells the elders, *"Behold, Aaron and Hur are with you. Whoever is involved in a dispute can go
to them"* (24:14; see [[The Blood of the Covenant]]). **He is left in charge of the camp with Aaron** — and the camp Aaron is
left in charge of is the one that makes the calf (32). **Hur is not mentioned there.**

**Other Hurs.** *"Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah"* (31:2; also 35:30; 38:22) is the
tabernacle's craftsman, and Chronicles makes that Hur a son of Caleb by Ephrath (1 Chronicles 2:19–20; WEB; WLC;
*not yet ingested*). **Whether he is the Hur of the hill the text never says**; the identification is possible and
is not adopted here. **A Midianite king named Hur** (Numbers 31:8; WLC) is certainly a different man. Later Jewish
tradition says more about the Hur of Exodus 17 — including that he was Miriam's husband — but **those sources have
not been checked here and are not relied on.**

### The other identification now reached — Exodus 31:2

The craftsman's genealogy now has its own page, [[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]]. **Whether that Hur
is this companion remains unresolved.** Exodus supplies neither a genealogy for Moses' companion nor
an explicit identification at 31:2. The cross-reference below records the question; it does not
count this man as a certain participant in the pericope.

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The makers and the surplus — Exodus 35:30–36:7

The Hur named in Moses' public announcement is Bezalel's grandfather (35:30), treated under
[[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]]. Nothing in the renewed genealogy identifies him with this
companion of Moses. This cross-reference records the unresolved identity, not a certain
appearance or a new action by the companion.

See [[Bezalel's Workmen and the Surplus]].
