# Kenites

## Overview

### *The wife of Heber the Kenite* — Judges 5:24

The Song blesses a Kenite woman *above women* (5:24). The tribes of Israel who stayed home are
shamed (5:15–17) and the town of Meroz cursed (5:23), and the one non-Israelite named on Israel's
side is blessed. See [[The Song of Deborah]] and
[[Hobab and the Kenites - Numbers 10, Judges 1 and 4, and 1 Samuel 15]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 5:15–17, 23–24.

### Jael's tent — Judges 4:17–22

*The house of Heber the Kenite* was at peace with Jabin (4:17), and Sisera dies in the tent of Heber's
wife, Jael (4:21). The Kenites stand in Israel's story as outsiders tied to Israel by Moses' marriage,
and here one Kenite household is at peace with Israel's enemy while one Kenite woman kills his general.
The Song calls her *blessed above women* (5:24). See [[Jael]] and [[Jael and Sisera]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:17–24; 5:24.

### Heber's separation — Judges 4:11

*Heber the Kenite had separated himself from the Kenites, even from the children of Hobab, Moses'
brother-in-law* (4:11). This is the verse that names Hobab as the family's ancestor, and it shows the
Kenites not all in one place: most with Judah in the south (1:16), one household by Kedesh in the far
north. 4:17 adds that this household was at peace with Jabin. See [[Heber the Kenite]] and
[[Deborah and Barak]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16; 1:16; 4:17.

### Going up with Judah — Judges 1:16

*The children of the Kenite, Moses' brother-in-law, went up out of the city of palm trees with the
children of Judah into the wilderness of Judah, which is in the south of Arad; and they went and lived
with the people* (16). The Hebrew is *qênî ḥōtēn mōšeh* (WLC), the relation Numbers 10:29 gives Hobab
or Reuel, which the WEB there renders *father-in-law*. *The city of palm trees* is Jericho
(Deuteronomy 34:3). The man is unnamed; Judges 4:11 names *Hobab*. The people listed among the
nations of the promised land (Genesis 15:19) now settle inside Judah.

See [[Judah's Conquests and Achsah's Springs]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:8–21; comparisons as cited.

The collective people addressed as the Kenite in Numbers 24:21, with Kain in the following verse.
Their strong rocky dwelling will not prevent captivity by Asshur. This page treats the group
collectively; it does not identify Kain with Cain of Genesis 4.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.
