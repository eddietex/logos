# Abijah (son of Samuel)

## Overview

### A judge in Beersheba — 1 Samuel 8:1–5

Abijah is [[Samuel]]'s second son, appointed with his brother [[Joel (son of Samuel)]] as a judge in
[[Beersheba]] when Samuel is old (8:1–2). Both *turned away after dishonest gain, took bribes, and perverted
justice* (8:3), and the elders give their conduct as a reason for asking for a king (8:5). Nothing is said of
Abijah apart from his brother. See [[Israel Asks for a King]] and
[[Not in His Ways - The Sons of Eli and the Sons of Samuel]].

**Name.** *ʾĂḇiyyâ* (WLC), commonly glossed *my father is Yahweh* (a conventional gloss, not verified here).
In the WLC files searched (Genesis through 2 Kings, Ruth, Psalms) the name occurs here and at 1 Kings 14:1,
of a son of Jeroboam; this page is qualified by his father so that later Abijahs do not collide with it.
1 Chronicles 6:28 names him second after Joel (WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-17; not yet ingested).
