# Commander of Yahweh's Army (of Joshua's time)

## Overview

The armed figure Joshua meets by Jericho identifies himself as commander of Yahweh's
army. The WEB introduces him as a man and subsequently calls him commander and prince.
He commands Joshua to remove his shoes because the place is holy. No personal name,
origin, or composition of his army is given.

Joshua's worship and the holy-ground command support a reading as divine presence, but
the verses do not expressly equate him with Yahweh, [[The Angel of Yahweh]], or Christ.
This page preserves the distinction between what the scene says and later identification.
The period qualifier identifies the encounter, not a dynasty or separate immortal being.
