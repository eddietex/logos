# The Maonites

## Overview

A people named once, in Yahweh's list of oppressors he saved Israel from: *The Sidonians also, and
the Amalekites, and the Maonites, oppressed you; and you cried to me, and I saved you out of their
hand* (Judges 10:12). The Hebrew is simply *ûmāʿôn*, *and Maon* (WLC, lemma 4584). The WEB supplies
*Maonites*.

No oppression by them is narrated anywhere in the books stored so far (Genesis through Judges). The
same lemma is used for the town of [[Maon]] in Judah's hill country (Joshua 15:55, WLC). The text does
not connect the people with the town.

Some Greek manuscripts are reported to read *Midian* in this verse. That would put Gideon's enemy in
the list, which otherwise omits Midian. This reading is reported, not checked; no Greek text was
consulted.

Sources: stored WEB Judges 10:11–12; Joshua 15:55; Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex
(OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.
