# Zuriel (son of Abihail)

## Overview

Prince of the fathers' house of the Merarite families (Numbers 3:35). He leads the smallest of the
three Levite houses, which camps on the north and keeps the tabernacle's boards, bars, pillars, and
sockets (35–37). Nothing else is told of him, and the text gives no descent from Mahli or Mushi.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 3.
