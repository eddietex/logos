# On (son of Peleth)

## Overview

Named among the Reubenite associates at the opening of the rebellion (Numbers 16:1). He is not named
again in this unit. It neither explains his disappearance nor explicitly reports his death; a rescue
or withdrawal story should not be supplied as though narrated here.
