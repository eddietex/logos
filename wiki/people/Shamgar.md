# Shamgar

## Overview

### The ox goad — Judges 3:31

*After him was Shamgar the son of Anath, who struck six hundred men of the Philistines with an ox
goad. He also saved Israel* (31). That verse is his whole story. He comes *after* [[Ehud]], fights
[[The Philistines]] with a farm tool, and *also saved Israel*, the verb used of the judges in 2:16 and
of Othniel in 3:9.

**What the text withholds.** None of the cycle's other parts are given for him. No sin of Israel, no
oppressor's reign, no cry, and no *Yahweh raised up*. Yahweh is not named in the verse. No Spirit
comes on him; he is not called *savior* (*môšîaʿ*) as Othniel and Ehud are, only said to have *saved*
(*wayyōšaʿ gam-hûʾ*, WLC); he is never said to *judge*. No rest follows, and his death is not
reported. The next verse dates Israel's relapse from Ehud's death (*when Ehud was dead*, 4:1), not
his.

**His name.** In the WLC files read (Genesis through Joshua, Judges, 1 Samuel), *Šamgar* and
*ʿĂnāt* occur only in Judges 3:31 and 5:6. *Shamgar* is commonly taken as a non-Hebrew, perhaps
Hurrian, name, and *Anath* as the name of a Canaanite war goddess known from Ugarit. Both are
reported, not checked. *Son of Anath* might then be a father's name, a warrior's title, or a link to a
place such as [[Beth Anath]]. The text does not say which.

**In the Song of Deborah.** *In the days of Shamgar the son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the
highways were unoccupied. The travelers walked through byways* (5:6, stored WEB; not yet ingested).
The song uses his name as a date, for a time when the roads were too dangerous to use. The prose
says he *saved Israel*; the song says the roads were empty in his days. The book gives both, and
neither is said to correct the other.

**Before Samson.** He is the first in Judges to fight the Philistines. Samson is later promised as one
who *shall begin to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines* (13:5), and kills *a thousand men*
with a donkey's jawbone (15:15). See
[[One Man Against the Philistines - Shamgar's Ox Goad and Samson's Jawbone]].
