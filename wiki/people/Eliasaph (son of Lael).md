# Eliasaph (son of Lael)

## Overview

Prince of the fathers' house of the Gershonites (Numbers 3:24), so he leads the clans who camp behind
the tabernacle on the west and keep its tent, coverings, screens, and hangings (23–26). This is his
only appearance in the stored text of Numbers. He is not the Eliasaph of [[The Twelve Tribal Princes]], the prince of Gad, who is the son
of Deuel or Reuel (1:14; 2:14) and camps on the south.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 1–3.
