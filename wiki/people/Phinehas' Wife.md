# Phinehas' Wife

## Overview

### The mother of Ichabod — 1 Samuel 4:19–22

*His daughter-in-law, Phinehas' wife, was with child, near to be delivered* (4:19). The text gives her no
name; she is identified by Eli first and her husband second, *kallāṯô ʾēšeṯ-pînəḥās* (WLC). She hears *that
God's ark was taken, and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead*, and *she bowed herself and gave
birth; for her pains came on her* (19). Her husband is [[Phinehas (son of Eli)]], killed at the battle that
morning; her father-in-law is [[Eli]], dead at the gate of Shiloh.

*About the time of her death the women who stood by her said to her, "Don't be afraid; for you have given
birth to a son." But she didn't answer, neither did she regard it* (20). *Wəlōʾ šāṯâ libbāh*, *did not set
her heart* (WLC). She names the boy [[Ichabod]], *The glory has departed from Israel* (21). The narrator's
reason lists the ark, her father-in-law, and her husband; her own last words name only the ark: *The glory
has departed from Israel; for God's ark has been taken* (22). She is the one person in the chapter who
speaks of *glory*, and the last in the house of Eli whose words are recorded.

The women's words repeat those of Rachel's midwife (*Don't be afraid*, *ʾal-tîrʾî*, followed by *a son*;
Genesis 35:17, WLC), and the two women die naming a son for their grief. See
[[Do Not Be Afraid, You Have a Son - Rachel and Phinehas' Wife]].

The page is named in the WEB's form, on the model of [[Lot's Wife]] and [[Manoah's Wife]]. The earlier
priest [[Phinehas (son of Eleazar)]] has no wife named or mentioned in the text, so the name is not
ambiguous; it would be qualified if one appeared.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 4:19–22; Genesis 35:17–18. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.
