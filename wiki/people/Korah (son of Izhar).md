# Korah (son of Izhar)

## Overview

### The daughters’ inheritance — Numbers 27:1–11

The daughters explicitly deny that Zelophehad belonged to Korah's company (3). This retrospective
reference distinguishes their father's unspecified sin from that rebellion; it does not claim he
was sinless.

See [[The Petition of Zelophehad's Daughters]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 27:1–11.


### The second census — Numbers 26:1–65

The retrospective explicitly places him in the earth’s swallowing (10), while his sons survive (11)
and Korahites remain a Levitical family (58). The sweeping household language in Numbers 16:32 must
not erase this stated exception.

See [[The Second Census]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 26.


### Between the dead and the living — Numbers 16:41–50

Korah is mentioned retrospectively in the death count (49). The 14,700 who die in this plague
are additional to those who died in his affair; he is not an active participant in this new scene.

See [[Aaron Stands Between the Dead and the Living]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 16:41–50; comparisons as cited.

### The rebellion of Korah — Numbers 16:1–40

Korah leads the challenge to Moses and Aaron with Reubenite allies and 250 recognized leaders. Moses
identifies his demand as seeking priesthood beyond the Levites’ given service (1–11). He gathers the
congregation at the Tent (19). Chapter 16 describes the earth swallowing his men and the fire
consuming the incense offerers; Numbers 26:10 explicitly includes Korah in the swallowing, while
26:11 preserves his sons. These later verses are now ingested in [[The Second Census]].

See [[The Rebellion of Korah]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 16:1–40; comparisons as cited.

**Moses' and Aaron's first cousin, and the man who will challenge Aaron's priesthood.**

> **21** The sons of Izhar: Korah, and Nepheg, and Zichri. (Exodus 6:21)

He is the son of Izhar, Kohath's second son, so Izhar's brother [[Amram]] is his uncle and [[Moses]]
and [[Aaron]] are his cousins. **He is the only one of Amram's nephews whose own sons are listed**:
*"The sons of Korah: Assir, and Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the families of the Korahites"*
(6:24). **The list gives him a clan before it gives him a story.** See
[[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]].

### The revolt and its later retrospective

In Numbers 16 he leads a revolt of Levites, with Reubenites, against Moses and Aaron, and the ground
swallows the rebels' households. **Numbers 26:11 adds that *"the sons of Korah did not die"*** (the
wiki's rendering of *ûbənê-qōraḥ lōʾ-mētû*, checked in the WLC), and the *sons of Korah* are later
named in the headings of a group of psalms (cited from memory; not checked). The revolt is now
covered in [[The Rebellion of Korah]] ; the census and psalm sections
remain ahead.

**He is not the Korah of Genesis 36:5, 14, 18**, Esau's son by [[Oholibamah]], nor the chief Korah
listed under Eliphaz at 36:16; the wiki qualifies this page for that reason.

### The Izharites — Numbers 3:14–51

He is not named, but his father's clan is, in the Kohathite house (19, 27), which camps on the south beside Reuben (29; 2:10). Elzaphan of the younger line is its prince (30). Both facts bear on Numbers 16, where Rashi draws on them; the text of chapter 3 makes no comment.

See [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 3.
