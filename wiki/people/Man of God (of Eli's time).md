# Man of God (of Eli's time)

## Overview

### The oracle against the house of Eli — 1 Samuel 2:27–36

*A man of God came to Eli* (2:27). He is not named, given no town, tribe, or ancestry, and never
reappears. He speaks only in Yahweh's voice: *Yahweh says*. He recounts Yahweh's choice of Eli's
ancestral house in Egypt and the grant of the altar and its offerings (27–28); charges Eli with kicking at
Yahweh's sacrifice and honouring his sons above him (29); revokes the promise that the house would *walk
before me forever*, *for those who honor me I will honor, and those who despise me will be cursed* (30);
and announces the sentence: no old man in the house, one survivor at the altar to grieve, Hophni and
Phinehas dead *in one day* as the sign, and *a faithful priest* with *a sure house* to replace them
(31–36). His word is confirmed to the boy Samuel (3:11–14) and its sign fulfilled at Aphek (4:11; stored
WEB; not yet ingested); 1 Kings 2:27 records Abiathar's removal as fulfilling *Yahweh's word, which he
spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh* (WEB checked at bible-api.com).

**The title.** *ʾÎš ʾĕlōhîm* (2:27, WLC). In the WLC files of Genesis through 1 Kings it is used before
this only of Moses (Deuteronomy 33:1; Joshua 14:6) and of the messenger who came to Manoah's wife (Judges
13:6, 8). After it, Saul's servant uses it of Samuel (9:6–10), and 1 Kings of the unnamed man of God from
Judah (1 Kings 13) and of Elijah (17:18, 24). The page is qualified by period, like other unnamed figures
known only by a title, because those later men of God will need their own pages; nothing in the text
links this one to any of them. Some rabbinic tradition is reported to identify him with Elkanah;
recalled, not verified, and not supported by anything in 1 Samuel.
