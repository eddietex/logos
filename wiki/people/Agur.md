# Agur

## Overview

Speaker named in Proverbs 30:1, the son of [[Jakeh]] , addressing [[Ithiel]] and [[Ucal]] in the
stored WEB. The text supplies no date, country, occupation, or identification with [[Solomon]] . His
separate heading is evidence that Proverbs preserves more than one attributed voice.

This page follows the WEB’s naming and does not claim to settle alternative readings of the
source-language heading. Source: raw/bible/Proverbs.md, 30:1–14.

## Timeline / key events

- He confesses limited understanding, asks questions about creation, commends God’s words, and prays
  for truthfulness and sufficient food (30:2–9). The following sayings warn against slander,
  parental dishonor, self-deception, pride, and oppression (10–14).
