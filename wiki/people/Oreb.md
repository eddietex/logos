# Oreb

## Overview

### Prince of Midian — Judges 7:25

*They took the two princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb. They killed Oreb at Oreb's rock* (7:25). Oreb is one
of *the two princes*, *śārîm*, of Midian's army. He is captured and killed by the men of Ephraim, who had
been summoned to cut off the Jordan crossings (7:24), and his head is brought to Gideon *beyond the
Jordan*. The place of his death is called [[The Rock of Oreb]]. The text does not say whether the rock
had the name before. He never speaks.

His name is the common noun *ʿōrēb*, *raven*, the bird of Genesis 8:7 (WLC). His fellow prince is
[[Zeeb]], *wolf*. As a personal name *Oreb* occurs in Genesis–Judges only at Judges 7:25 and 8:3 (WLC).
In 8:3 Gideon reminds Ephraim that *God has delivered into your hand the princes of Midian, Oreb and
Zeeb* (stored WEB; not yet ingested).

Later Scripture uses the name as a byword: *Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb* (Psalm 83:11), and *as
in the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb* (Isaiah 10:26) (WEB checked; not yet ingested). See
[[The Day of Midian - Oreb and Zeeb in Psalm 83 and Isaiah 9-10]].
