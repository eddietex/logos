# Kohath

## Overview

**The second son of [[Levi]], and the ancestor of Moses and Aaron — which Genesis does not mention.**

> **11** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. (46:11)

**He appears in Genesis exactly once, in the middle of a list, with no epithet and no story.** The
register at 46:8–27 is counting the household that crossed into Egypt, and he is one of the seventy.

**What the rest of the Pentateuch makes of him is disproportionate to that.** **Kohath's son Amram
fathers Aaron and Moses** (Exodus 6:18, 20), **so the Exodus, the priesthood and the law all descend
from the middle name of this clause.** **The Kohathite house is given the most dangerous charge in the
camp**: the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars and the vessels of the sanctuary (Numbers
4:4–15), **carried on the shoulder rather than in a cart** (Numbers 7:9), **and not to be touched or
looked at on pain of death.** The singer Heman is traced to his line (1 Chronicles 6:33–38).

**Genesis is not withholding any of this; it does not know it yet.** **The point worth recording is
the shape**: **the man through whom Israel gets its lawgiver enters Scripture as an unmarked entry in
a travel document**, three names after [[Reuben]] and forty before the end of the list. See
[[Priesthood]] and [[Providence]].
