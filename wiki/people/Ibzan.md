# Ibzan

## Overview

### The judge of Bethlehem — Judges 12:8–10

*After him Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel. He had thirty sons; and thirty daughters he sent
outside his clan, and thirty daughters he brought in from outside his clan for his sons. He judged
Israel seven years. Ibzan died, and was buried at Bethlehem* (12:8–10).

The first of the three minor judges after Jephthah. Like the other two, he is introduced with the
judging verb itself, *wayyišpōṭ ʾaḥărāyw*, *and after him judged* (WLC), not with *arose* as Tola and
Jair were (10:1, 3). In the WLC books of Genesis, Numbers, Joshua, and Judges his name, *ʾibṣān*
(lemma 78), occurs only in this notice.

**Which Bethlehem.** The text says only *Bethlehem*. Elsewhere in Judges the town is always *Bethlehem
Judah* (17:7–9; 19:1–2, 18; WLC *bêt leḥem yəhûdâ*). Joshua 19:15 lists a Bethlehem in Zebulun, and
the next judge is a Zebulunite. The wiki records both possibilities and chooses neither. A later
Jewish tradition identifies Ibzan with Boaz of Bethlehem; it is recalled (Bava Batra 91a), not
verified, and Judges makes no such link.

**Sixty marriages.** His thirty daughters are *sent outside*, and thirty daughters are *brought in from
outside* for his sons. The WEB's *his clan* interprets the Hebrew *haḥûṣâ* / *min-haḥûṣ*, *outside*;
outside of what, the text does not say. The marriages are an exchange in equal numbers, and the text
gives them no comment. Like Jair's thirty sons (10:4), they show a large and connected household.

See [[Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 12:8–10; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-16.
