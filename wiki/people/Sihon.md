# Sihon

## Overview

### The covenant in Moab — Deuteronomy 29:1–29

His defeat alongside Og is recalled as the prelude to the eastern inheritance (7–8). He is identified as king of Heshbon, not a participant in the present assembly.

See [[The Covenant in Moab and the Hidden Things]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 29:1–29.

### Setting of the instruction — Deuteronomy 4:44–49

His former land contains the valley where Moses speaks. He is identified as king of the Amorites, resident at Heshbon, and defeated by Moses and Israel (46–47); no new battle occurs.

See [[The Setting of the Covenant Instruction]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 4:44–49.

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

Although not named in this unit, he is the other of the two kings recalled in verse 21, alongside Og
(compare 2:24–3:11). Their defeats become the precedent for Joshua’s future campaigns.

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

### Og’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 3:1–11

His earlier defeat supplies both the promise’s precedent (2) and the comparison for destroying the
inhabitants (6). Verse 8 gathers his and Og’s territories into a single account of the two Amorite
kings.

See [[The Defeat of Og Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:1–11.

### Sihon’s defeat recalled — Deuteronomy 2:24–37

Sihon refuses passage; Moses attributes that refusal to Yahweh’s hardening for the purpose of
delivering him to Israel (30). He fights at Jahaz, and he, his sons, and his people are struck
(32–34). Numbers 21:21–26 narrates the refusal and defeat without the hardening explanation.

See [[The Defeat of Sihon Recounted]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:24–37.

### Moses’ address in Moab — Deuteronomy 1:1–5

His defeat precedes the address (4). He is identified as king of the Amorites, resident in Heshbon;
this is retrospective dating, not a second battle.

See [[The Words of Moses Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 1:1–5.

### Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan — Numbers 32:1–42

*The kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites* is given to Gad, Reuben, and half of Manasseh (33). Several of
the towns rebuilt in the chapter — Heshbon, Dibon, Jazer — were taken in the war against him (21:21–35).

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
