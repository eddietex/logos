# Zelophehad

## Overview

### Manasseh’s inheritance — Joshua 17:1–13

His five daughters receive the inheritance commanded through Moses (3–4). The deceased father’s line
remains represented in the western allotment.

See [[The Inheritance of Manasseh]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:1–13.

### Marriage and tribal inheritance — Numbers 36:1–13

His inheritance remains with his daughters within their father's tribe through their marriages to
paternal cousins (2, 10–12). He is recalled as the deceased owner, not a participant.

See [[The Marriage of the Daughters of Zelophehad]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 36; comparisons as cited.

### The daughters’ inheritance — Numbers 27:1–11

His daughters report that he died in his own sin in the wilderness, not in Korah's company (3).
The sin is unspecified; identifying him with the Sabbath breaker would go beyond this text. His
lack of sons does not extinguish the family's inheritance: Yahweh grants it to his daughters
(7–8).

See [[The Petition of Zelophehad's Daughters]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 27:1–11.


Son of Hepher in Manasseh’s Gileadite line, with no sons and five named daughters (33).
The census notice prepares the inheritance question resolved in Numbers 27:1–11.
