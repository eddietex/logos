# Zelophehad

## Overview

### The daughters’ inheritance — Numbers 27:1–11

His daughters report that he died in his own sin in the wilderness, not in Korah's company (3).
The sin is unspecified; identifying him with the Sabbath breaker would go beyond this text. His
lack of sons does not extinguish the family's inheritance: Yahweh grants it to his daughters
(7–8).

See [[The Petition of Zelophehad's Daughters]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 27:1–11.


Son of Hepher in Manasseh’s Gileadite line, with no sons and five named daughters (33).
The census notice prepares the inheritance question resolved in Numbers 27:1–11.
