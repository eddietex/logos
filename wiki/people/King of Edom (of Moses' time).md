# King of Edom (of Moses' time)

## Overview

The unnamed king to whom Moses sends messengers from Kadesh (Numbers 20:14). Edom’s
replies reject transit and threaten the sword; the nation then mobilizes in force (18–20).
The narrative does not supply a dynasty, personal name, or motive. The period qualifier
distinguishes this ruler without identifying him with the earlier kings of Genesis 36.

## Timeline / key events

- Receives Israel’s request; refusal and armed opposition lead Israel to turn away (20:14–21).
