# Eshcol

## Overview

One of the three Amorite brothers who are Abram's sworn allies in the war of the four kings.

*"He lived by the oaks of Mamre, the Amorite, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner; and they were
allies of Abram"* (Genesis 14:13). He appears twice, both times in this chapter: in the list of
Abram's covenant-partners, and in the last clause Abram speaks — *"the portion of the men who went
with me: Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. Let them take their portion"* (14:24). He says nothing and does
nothing on his own account. See [[The Rescue of Lot and Melchizedek]].

**What the three of them are is stated in a technical phrase.** *Baʿălê bərît ʾAbrām*, **masters of
a covenant of Abram** — treaty partners, bound by a mutual-defense pact. It is the first *bərît* in
the Abraham cycle, and it is with Amorites rather than with God. See [[Mamre (person)]] and
[[Aner]].

**And what Abram does for them is the point of the mention.** Having sworn not to take *"a thread
nor a sandal strap"* from the king of Sodom, he immediately excepts his allies' share from his own
vow. They did not swear it; he does not impose it on them.

## The name

*ʾEshkōl* is the ordinary Hebrew word for a **cluster** of grapes. The Valley of Eshcol near
[[Hebron]] carries the same name in Numbers 13:23–24, where the spies cut down a single cluster so
large it has to be carried on a pole between two men, and the text supplies its own etymology:
*"That place was called the valley of Eshcol, because of the cluster which the children of Israel
cut down from there."* Numbers is naming the valley from the fruit, not from this man, and the wiki
records the coincidence of name and district without asserting a connection — though a local chief
and a nearby valley sharing a name in the same region as [[Mamre (place)]] is at least suggestive.
