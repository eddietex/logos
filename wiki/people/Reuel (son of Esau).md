# Reuel (son of Esau)

## Overview

**Esau's second son, by [[Mahalath]] — whom this register calls Basemath — and the head of the junior
of the two branches that have descendants.**

> **4** … Basemath bore Reuel.
> **13** These are the sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These were the sons of
> Basemath, Esau’s wife.

**Four sons, four chiefs (36:17), and nothing else.** He is born in Canaan before the household moves
to Seir (36:5), and he is never mentioned outside Genesis 36 and the Chronicler's copy of it at
1 Chronicles 1:35, 37. See [[The Descendants of Esau]].

***Rəʿûʾēl*** is **friend of God**, or **God is a friend** — *rēaʿ*, companion, plus *ʾēl*. It is a
name of the same confessional class as his half-brother [[Eliphaz (son of Esau)]]'s, which is worth
noting in a family Genesis has spent ten chapters describing as outside the covenant: **the Edomite
register is full of El-names, and Genesis never remarks on it.**

## The name elsewhere

**Reuel is also the name of Moses' father-in-law** at Exodus 2:18 and Numbers 10:29, the Midianite
priest called **Jethro** at Exodus 3:1 and elsewhere. **They are not the same man**, and the wiki
keeps them apart by filename — this page is qualified *(son of Esau)* so that a later
`Reuel (father-in-law of Moses)` can be created without either holding the bare name.

**The overlap is still worth a line.** [[Midian]] and Edom are neighbours in the same Transjordanian
world, and Genesis 36:35 has an Edomite king striking Midian in the field of Moab. **The same stock of
names circulates across the whole region**, which is the plainest thing the chapter demonstrates and
the reason the book of Job's cast sounds Edomite — see [[Jobab of Bozrah and the Septuagint's Job]].

## His sons

**Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.** *Zerah* is the one that recurs: **[[Jobab]], the second king
of Edom, is *"the son of Zerah of Bozrah"*** (36:33), and the Septuagint's appendix to the book of Job
uses exactly that link to make Job a great-grandson of Esau through Reuel. **Whether the Zerah of
36:13 and the Zerah of 36:33 are the same man, Genesis does not say** — it never connects the chief
list to the king list at all, which is one of the signs that the chapter is an anthology of separate
registers rather than a single document.

## Timeline / key events

- Born in Canaan to Esau and [[Mahalath]], called Basemath in this register — Genesis 36:4–5, 10
- Father of Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah — Genesis 36:13
- His four sons made chiefs in the land of Edom — Genesis 36:17
- Copied by the Chronicler — 1 Chronicles 1:35, 37
