# Abiram

## Overview

Son of Eliab and brother of Dathan, among the Reubenites allied with Korah (1). He shares Dathan’s
refusal and accusation and appears with his household at the tents before the earth swallows the
rebels (12–14, 27–33). No distinct speech or independent motive is assigned to him.
