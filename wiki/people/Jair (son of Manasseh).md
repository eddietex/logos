# Jair (son of Manasseh)

## Overview

### Eastern inheritance — Deuteronomy 3:12–22

Jair takes Argob to the Geshurite and Maacathite border and names the territory Havvoth Jair after
himself (14). Numbers 32:41 gives the village-taking and naming more briefly. The differing later
account associated with a judge named Jair remains unresolved on [[Havvoth Jair]].

See [[The Eastern Inheritance and the Charge to Joshua]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 3:12–22.

*Jair the son of Manasseh went and took its villages, and called them Havvoth Jair* (Numbers 32:41). One
of three Manassite conquerors at the end of Numbers 32, after the children of Machir and before Nobah. The
text gives him one verse and no speech.

*Son of Manasseh* may mean a descendant rather than a son; the chapter does not give his father. Deuteronomy
3:14 also calls him *Jair the son of Manasseh* and has him take all the region of Argob in Bashan
(stored WEB, now ingested).

The page is qualified because Judges 10:3–4 has a later *Jair, the Gileadite*, a judge of twenty-two years
whose thirty sons held *thirty cities, which are called Havvoth Jair to this day* (WEB via bible-api.com, not
yet ingested). Whether the two accounts of the name are one tradition or two is recorded on
[[Havvoth Jair]] without being decided.

See [[Reuben, Gad, and the Land Beyond the Jordan]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 32.
