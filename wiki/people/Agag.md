# Agag

## Overview

The king used as a comparison for Israel’s future king in Numbers 24:7. The verse gives no ancestry
or chronology for Agag. Identity with the Agag of 1 Samuel 15 is not established here, nor does this
ingest assume that Agag is a dynastic title.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.
