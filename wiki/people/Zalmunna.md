# Zalmunna

## Overview

### King of Midian — Judges 8:5–21

The second of *the kings of Midian*, never named without [[Zebah]] and always after him (8:5, 6, 7, 10,
12, 15, 18, 21). The two share everything the text tells: the escape to [[Karkor]] with fifteen thousand
men (8:10), the capture (8:12), the question about the men killed at Tabor and the answer, *They were like
you* (8:18), and the request to die by Gideon's own hand, *Rise and fall on us; for as the man is, so is
his strength* (8:21). Neither king is ever given a separate act or word.

The name is not explained in the text, and the wiki adopts no meaning for it. Psalm 83:11 names him with
Zebah as a byword (WEB checked at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). See
[[The Day of Midian - Oreb and Zeeb in Psalm 83 and Isaiah 9-10]].

See [[Ephraim, Succoth, and Penuel]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 8:5–21; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures
morphhb), 2026-11-15.

After the kings' deaths their goods are counted with Gideon's gold: *the crescents, and the pendants, and
the purple clothing that was on the kings of Midian, and besides the chains that were about their camels'
necks* (8:26). The verse lists them *besides* the weight; it does not say they went into the ephod. See
[[Gideon's Ephod and Death]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 8:26.
