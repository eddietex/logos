# Balaam

## Overview

### The first two oracles — Numbers 22:36–23:26

His promised restriction becomes narrated action: Yahweh puts the words in his mouth twice (23:5,
16). He blesses Israel despite Balak’s demand, and refuses both reversal and silence (23:20, 25–26).
His wish for a righteous death (23:10) is a wish, not a verdict on his own eventual end.

See [[Balaam's First Two Oracles]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 22:36–23:26.


### Balaam’s donkey — Numbers 22:22–35

Balaam strikes the donkey three times without seeing the angel she sees. Once Yahweh opens his eyes, he bows, confesses sin, and offers to return. Permission to continue is renewed with his speech restricted to the given word (23–35). His confession does not explain away the tension between permission in 22:20 and anger in 22:22.

See [[Balaam's Donkey]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 22:22–35.

Son of [[Beor (father of Balaam)]] , summoned from [[Pethor]] by [[Balak]] to curse Israel. He
consults Yahweh twice and asserts that even a house full of wealth cannot authorize him to exceed
the divine word (8–20). The narrator reports the rewards brought by the first delegation but does
not yet say that Balaam accepted payment.
