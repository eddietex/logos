# Ahimaaz (father of Ahinoam)

## Overview

### Saul's father-in-law — 1 Samuel 14:50

*Ahinoam the daughter of Ahimaaz* (14:50). He is named only to identify his daughter, Saul's wife, and nothing else
is told of him: no tribe, no town. Qualified because a later Ahimaaz, son of Zadok the priest, runs with news in
David's reign (2 Samuel 15–18, recalled, not checked). See [[Saul's Wars and Household]].
