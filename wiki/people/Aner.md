# Aner

## Overview

The third of the Amorite brothers allied to Abram, and the thinnest of them.

*"At that time, he lived by the oaks of Mamre, the Amorite, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner;
and they were allies of Abram"* (Genesis 14:13). Like [[Eshcol]] he occurs twice, both times in
Genesis 14, and both times in a list: once among Abram's covenant-partners, once among the men whose
portion Abram protects — *"Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. Let them take their portion"* (14:24). He has no
recorded word or act. See [[The Rescue of Lot and Melchizedek]].

**The one thing the text does with him is refuse to bind him.** Abram swears off the spoil of Sodom
and in the same breath reserves the share of three men who took no such oath. Whatever else the
chapter is doing with foreigners — a Canaanite priest blesses Abram, an Elamite king is routed by
him, a Sodomite king is refused by him — it is not treating Abram's vow as something he can impose
on people outside it.

**The name is unexplained.** *ʿĀnēr* has no clear Hebrew etymology; *boy* or *sprout* have been
suggested and neither is secure. A Levitical town called Aner appears in Manasseh in
1 Chronicles 6:70, in a list where the parallel at Joshua 21:25 reads *Taanach* instead; the
relationship, if any, is textual rather than genealogical, and the wiki draws no line between the
town and the man.
