# Bezalel

## Overview

The craftsman of Judah whom Yahweh calls by name and fills with the Spirit of God for wisdom,
understanding, knowledge, and work in metal, stone, and wood (Exodus 31:2–5). He is the son of
[[Uri]] and grandson of [[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]]. [[Oholiab]] and a company of wise-hearted
workers share his commission (31:6–11). God addresses [[Moses]] about him; no speech or completed
work by Bezalel is narrated here. The passage neither explains his training nor limits the duration
of the gift.

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The makers and the surplus — Exodus 35:30–36:7

Moses repeats his name, ancestry, and Spirit-given ability to Israel (35:30–33), then names
teaching as a gift shared with Oholiab (35:34). Moses summons both men with the other skilled,
willing workers, and they receive the contributions (36:1–3). The makers report a surplus
collectively (36:4–5); no individual speech is assigned to Bezalel. Teaching is part of the gift,
without an account of his prior training or a claim that he alone makes everything.

See [[Bezalel's Workmen and the Surplus]].

### The enclosure made — Exodus 36:8–38

The enclosure account opens with the wise-hearted workers collectively (36:8), after Bezalel's
commission and summons in 36:1–2. Its subsequent singular making descriptions may be read under
his leadership; he is not named within 36:8–38 itself. The report does not establish that he
personally made every component alone. The next unit names him explicitly for the ark (37:1).

See [[The Tabernacle Built]].
