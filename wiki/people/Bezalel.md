# Bezalel

## Overview

The craftsman of Judah whom Yahweh calls by name and fills with the Spirit of God for wisdom,
understanding, knowledge, and work in metal, stone, and wood (Exodus 31:2–5). He is the son of
[[Uri]] and grandson of [[Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)]]. [[Oholiab]] and a company of wise-hearted
workers share his commission (31:6–11). God addresses [[Moses]] about him in chapter 31,
before any completed work is narrated there. Chapter 37 now names him as the ark maker; see
[[The Furnishings Made]]. The commissioning passage neither explains his training nor limits
the duration of the gift.

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The makers and the surplus — Exodus 35:30–36:7

Moses repeats his name, ancestry, and Spirit-given ability to Israel (35:30–33), then names
teaching as a gift shared with Oholiab (35:34). Moses summons both men with the other skilled,
willing workers, and they receive the contributions (36:1–3). The makers report a surplus
collectively (36:4–5); no individual speech is assigned to Bezalel. Teaching is part of the gift,
without an account of his prior training or a claim that he alone makes everything.

See [[Bezalel's Workmen and the Surplus]].

### The enclosure made — Exodus 36:8–38

The enclosure account opens with the wise-hearted workers collectively (36:8), after Bezalel's
commission and summons in 36:1–2. Its subsequent singular making descriptions may be read under
his leadership; he is not named within 36:8–38 itself. The report does not establish that he
personally made every component alone. The next unit names him explicitly for the ark (37:1).

See [[The Tabernacle Built]].

### The furnishings made — Exodus 37:1–29

Exodus 37:1 explicitly names him as the ark's maker. The account continues with singular
making descriptions for the mercy seat, table, lampstand, incense altar, oil, and incense
(37:2–29). This advances his story from appointment to specified work. The shared commission
of 36:1–2 remains in force; the attribution does not establish that he made every component
without assistance.

See [[The Furnishings Made]]. Source: the cited Exodus verses checked in local raw WEB, 2026-10-24.

### The outer equipment and inventory — Exodus 38:1–31

Exodus 38:22 credits him with making all that Yahweh commanded Moses and repeats his ancestry
through Uri and Hur in Judah. Oholiab is explicitly with him (38:23), while 36:1–2 supplies the
wider workforce. The summary credit is not a claim of unaided manufacture; the garments and the
formal completion report still follow in chapter 39.

See [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]].

### The priestly garments made — Exodus 39:1–31

The garment account belongs to the company commissioned in 36:1–2 and credited in
38:22–23. Its WEB alternates singular and plural making descriptions, but does not name Bezalel
in 39:1–31. His leadership supplies context; it does not establish that he alone made the ephod,
cut the gold into wires, or engraved every stone. The new detail is the work performed, not a
new allocation of individual tasks.

See [[The Priestly Garments Made]]. Source: cited Exodus verses checked in local raw WEB, 2026-10-25.

### The work completed — Exodus 39:32–43

The work led by Bezalel in the surrounding narrative is now brought to Moses and approved.
Exodus 39:32, 42 credits the children of Israel collectively, while 38:22 names Bezalel’s work
and 36:1–2 names the wider company. These are complementary levels of attribution. He is not
named in 39:32–43, and the blessing is not restricted to him or to two master craftsmen.

See [[The Work Completed]]. Source: cited passages checked in the stored WEB Genesis and Exodus
texts, 2026-10-25.

### The same three gifts in Proverbs and Kings

The three nouns of Exodus 31:3 — *wisdom*, *understanding*, *knowledge* (*bəḥoḵmāh ûḇiṯḇûnāh
ûḇəḏaʿaṯ* in Sefaria's *Miqra according to the Masorah*, read 2026-10-26) — are the three by which
Yahweh founds the world in Proverbs 3:19–20, by which *"a house is built"* in Proverbs 24:3–4, and with
which Hiram of Tyre is filled for the temple's bronze work in 1 Kings 7:14. The wiki records the
shared vocabulary without claiming that any of the later texts alludes to Bezalel. See
[[Wisdom, Understanding, and Knowledge - The Builder's Gifts in Proverbs, Exodus, and Kings]] and
[[Through Wisdom a House Is Built]].
