# Elzaphan (son of Uzziel)

## Overview

Son of [[Uzziel (son of Kohath)]], named in Exodus 6:22. With [[Mishael (son of Uzziel)]], he carries the dead priests outside the camp at Moses’ command (Leviticus 10:4–5). The passage assigns him this act without appointing him to Aaron’s priestly office.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 6:18–23 and Leviticus 10:4–5.

## Timeline / key events

- Exodus 6:18–22 — family identification.
- Leviticus 10:4–5 — the family named in the removal of the dead priests.
