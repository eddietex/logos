# Elzaphan (son of Uzziel)

## Overview

Son of [[Uzziel (son of Kohath)]], named in Exodus 6:22. With [[Mishael (son of Uzziel)]], he carries the dead priests outside the camp at Moses’ command (Leviticus 10:4–5). The passage assigns him this act without appointing him to Aaron’s priestly office.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 6:18–23 and Leviticus 10:4–5.

### Prince of the Kohathites — Numbers 3:30

Written *Elizaphan* here (*ʾĕlîṣāpān*, against *ʾelṣāpān* at Exodus 6:22 and Leviticus 10:4, WLC). He is prince of the Kohathite families, the house charged with the ark. Rashi on 16:1, following Midrash Tanchuma, makes this appointment the cause of Korah's envy, since Uzziel was the youngest of Kohath's four sons; Numbers does not say so.

See [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 3.
