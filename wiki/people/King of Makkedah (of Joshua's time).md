# King of Makkedah (of Joshua's time)

## Overview

Unnamed ruler destroyed with Makkedah (28). His fate is compared to that of Jericho’s king. He is not one of the five kings from other cities hidden in Makkedah’s cave.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43. No modern identification or dynasty is assigned.
