# The Woman of Thebez

## Overview

### The millstone — Judges 9:50–55

Abimelech takes [[Thebez]], but *there was a strong tower within the city, and all the men and women of
the city fled there, and shut themselves in, and went up to the roof of the tower*. He comes *near to the
door of the tower to burn it with fire*, as he had burned the tower of Shechem (Judges 9:49–52). *A
certain woman cast an upper millstone on Abimelech's head, and broke his skull* (9:53).

She is *ʾiššâ ʾaḥat*, *one woman* (WLC), unnamed and given no family. The page is named for her town,
as the wiki names other unnamed figures by who or where they appear with. *Cast*, *wattašlēk*, is the
verb of Jotham's *my father … risked his life*, literally *threw* it (9:17; WLC). *An upper millstone*,
*pelaḥ rekeb*, is the moving stone of a hand mill, the thing Deuteronomy 24:6 forbids taking in pledge
*for he takes a life in pledge* (WLC; stored WEB).

Abimelech's last words are about her: *Draw your sword, and kill me, that men not say of me, "A woman
killed him"* (9:54). His armor bearer does it, but the narrator has already said who struck the blow.
She is the second woman in Judges to kill an enemy of the narrative with a blow to the head, after
[[Jael]] (4:21; 5:26). Deborah had said *Yahweh will sell Sisera into a woman's hand* (4:9). No one
foretells this woman, and the narrator's verdict follows at once: *Thus God repaid the wickedness of
Abimelech* (9:56).

Joab remembers her a generation later: *Who struck Abimelech the son of Jerubbesheth? Didn't a woman
cast an upper millstone on him from the wall, so that he died at Thebez?* (2 Samuel 11:21, WEB checked
at bible-api.com; not yet ingested). See [[Into a Woman's Hand - Judges 4.9, 4.21, and 9.53-54]].

See [[The Fall of Abimelech]]. Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 24:6; Judges 4:9, 21; 9:17, 49–56; 2 Samuel
