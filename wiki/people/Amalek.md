# Amalek

## Overview

**A grandson of [[Esau]] by a concubine, named in nine words at the bottom of a list — and the
ancestor of the one nation the Bible commands Israel to destroy utterly.**

> **12** Timna was concubine to Eliphaz, Esau’s son; and she bore to Eliphaz Amalek. These are the
> sons of Adah, Esau’s wife.

**Genesis says nothing else about him.** No act, no character, no territory, no death. He is a name in
the third tier of [[The Descendants of Esau]] — son of [[Eliphaz (son of Esau)]] by [[Timna]], counted
among the sons of [[Adah (wife of Esau)]] because a concubine's child is reckoned to the wife's line —
and then, four verses later, *chief Amalek* among the chiefs of Eliphaz (36:16). **That is the whole
of his biblical biography.** Everything else the Bible has to say about Amalek is about the people.

## The people, and the sentence on them

**The Amalekites are the first nation to attack Israel after the Exodus**, and the Torah treats that
priority as decisive.

- **Exodus 17:8–16** — at Rephidim, *"Amalek came and fought with Israel"*, before Sinai, before the
  law, before anything. Moses' hands are held up by Aaron and Hur; Joshua fights. The episode ends
  with the only oath of its kind in Scripture: *"Yahweh will have war with Amalek from generation to
  generation."*
- **Deuteronomy 25:17–19** — the charge, and it is a charge of cowardice rather than of war: *"how he
  met you by the way, and struck the rearmost of you, all who were feeble behind you, when you were
  faint and weary; and he didn't fear God."* Then: *"you shall blot out the memory of Amalek from
  under the sky. You shall not forget."* **The command is to forget them and to remember to**, which
  is a knot the tradition has never untied.
- **1 Samuel 15** — Saul is sent to carry it out, spares Agag the king and the best of the livestock,
  and loses the kingdom for it. **The one command Saul is destroyed for disobeying is this one.**
- **Esther 3:1** — Haman is *the Agagite*, and the book's plot is a last attempt to do to the Jews
  what Deuteronomy 25 had ordered done to his ancestor's people. **The Bible's most famous pogrom is
  framed as the tail of Genesis 36:12.**

**Numbers 24:20** gives Balaam's verdict in one line — *"Amalek was the first of the nations, but his
latter end shall come to destruction"* — and 1 Chronicles 4:43 records the last remnant killed in
Hezekiah's reign. **The Bible follows the family from a subordinate clause in Genesis to an
extermination notice in Chronicles.**

## The thing Genesis does not do

**It does not explain, foreshadow, or condemn.** A book that cannot introduce the Canaanites without
[[Curse|a curse]], or Moab and Ammon without a cave, introduces Israel's archetypal enemy as a
routine entry in a genealogy — **and the genealogy's whole tendency is generous**, granting Esau's
line chiefs, kings, and a country without a word of disparagement. See
[[The Other Line First - The Order of the Toledot in Genesis]].

**And the descent complicates the sentence rather than justifying it.** Amalek is Esau's great-
grandson, which makes the Amalekites kin — and Deuteronomy 23:7, in the same book that orders their
memory blotted out, says *"You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother."* **The Torah
protects the uncle and proscribes the nephew.** Whether the Amalekites were in fact an Edomite
offshoot is doubtful on other grounds: they range the Negev and Sinai rather than Seir, Genesis 14:7
mentions *"all the country of the Amalekites"* generations before this Amalek is born — an
anachronism of the same kind as *Dan* at 14:14 — and Numbers 24:20 calls them *the first of the
nations*. **Genesis 36:12 may be an Israelite explanation of a neighbour rather than a record of
one.**

## Timeline / key events

- Born to [[Timna]], concubine of [[Eliphaz (son of Esau)]], and reckoned among the sons of
  [[Adah (wife of Esau)]] — Genesis 36:12, [[The Descendants of Esau]]
- Listed as *chief Amalek* among the chiefs descended from Eliphaz — Genesis 36:16
- His people attack Israel at Rephidim and are put under permanent war — Exodus 17:8–16
- Ordered blotted out for striking the stragglers — Deuteronomy 25:17–19
- Spared by Saul, at the cost of Saul's kingdom — 1 Samuel 15
- Haman the Agagite — Esther 3:1

## Appears in

- [[The Descendants of Esau]] — Genesis 36:12, 16; **nine words and a chief's title.** The Bible's
  most comprehensively condemned nation enters Scripture as the son of a concubine in the third
  generation of a register that is otherwise entirely peaceable, and Genesis neither comments nor
  looks ahead
