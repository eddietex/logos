# Mishael (son of Uzziel)

## Overview

Son of [[Uzziel (son of Kohath)]], named in Exodus 6:22. With [[Elzaphan (son of Uzziel)]], he obeys Moses and carries Nadab and Abihu in their coats from the sanctuary to outside the camp (Leviticus 10:4–5). The patronymic distinguishes this Mishael from other bearers of the name.

Sources: stored WEB Exodus 6:18–23 and Leviticus 10:4–5.

## Timeline / key events

- Exodus 6:18–22 — family identification.
- Leviticus 10:4–5 — the family named in the removal of the dead priests.
