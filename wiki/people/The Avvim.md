# The Avvim

## Overview

People who lived in villages extending as far as Gaza, destroyed and replaced by Caphtorim from
Caphtor (Deuteronomy 2:23). They enter as a comparison in the territorial retrospect, not as
opponents Israel fights in this unit.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.
