# Saul's Servant (of the lost donkeys)

## Overview

The unnamed young man, *one of the servants*, *ʾaḥaḏ mēhannəʿārîm* (1 Samuel 9:3, WLC), whom [[Kish (father of Saul)]]
sends with [[Saul]] to look for the lost donkeys. Qualified by his errand because Saul's servants recur as a group
from 16:15 (stored WEB; not yet ingested).

### The one who knows the way — 1 Samuel 9:3–22

When Saul wants to turn back in the land of Zuph, the servant knows that *in this city* there is *a man of God …
held in honor. All that he says surely happens*, and proposes asking him the way (9:6). When Saul objects that they
have no present, he has *the fourth part of a shekel of silver* and offers it (9:8). The chapter's turn comes from
him: the future king follows his servant's suggestion to the prophet who is waiting to anoint him. Samuel seats him
with Saul at the head of the guests (9:22). In the next pericope Samuel tells Saul to send him on ahead (9:27,
stored WEB; not yet ingested), and Saul's uncle questions them both (10:14, stored WEB; not yet ingested). See
[[Saul Comes to the Seer]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 9:3–27; 10:14; 16:15. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-17.

### Sent ahead — 1 Samuel 9:27; 10:14

At the edge of the city Samuel tells Saul, *Tell the servant to go on ahead of us*, and *He went ahead* (9:27). The
one who brought Saul to the prophet does not see the anointing. He is with Saul again when Saul's uncle asks them
both, *Where did you go?* (10:14), and Saul answers for both of them: *we came to Samuel*. Whether the servant knew
what happened, the text does not say. See [[Samuel Anoints Saul]].

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 9:27; 10:14.
