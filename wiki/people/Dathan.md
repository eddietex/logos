# Dathan

## Overview

Son of Eliab and a Reubenite ally of Korah. With Abiram he refuses Moses’ summons, accuses him of
failed deliverance and domination, and stands at his tent with his household before the earth opens
(1, 12–14, 27–33). The narrative includes wives and children in the scene without assigning them
separate speeches or offenses.
