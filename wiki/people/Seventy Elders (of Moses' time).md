# Seventy Elders (of Moses' time)

## Overview

A group, not a person, filed here as the wiki files [[Ministering Women (of Moses' time)]]. No one among them is named,
unless [[Eldad]] and [[Medad]] count.

Seventy elders appear twice with Moses. At Exodus 24:1, 9–11 *seventy of the elders of Israel* go up the mountain, see the God
of Israel, and eat and drink ([[The Blood of the Covenant]]). At Numbers 11:16–25 Yahweh tells Moses to gather seventy men
*whom you know to be the elders of the people, and officers over them*. The Spirit on Moses is shared with them, and they
prophesy, once. **The text does not say that the two groups are the same men.**

Their purpose is stated: *they shall bear the burden of the people with you* (11:17). The chapter never shows them doing it.
Verse 30 sends them back to the camp, and they are not mentioned at the plague. Whether they are the judges of
Exodus 18 is taken up at [[Too Heavy for You Alone - Exodus 18, Numbers 11, and Deuteronomy 1]], and that page treats them as a
different body.

Seventy elders recur at Ezekiel 8:11, with censers in the wrong place, and the Lord appoints *seventy others* at Luke 10:1.
Neither verse points back to Moses, and the wiki claims no link. Later institutions called on this number are not treated here.
