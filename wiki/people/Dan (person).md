# Dan (person)

## Overview

**Bilhah's first son, Jacob's fifth, and a name that is a legal verdict.**

> **6** Rachel said, “God has judged me, and has also heard my voice, and has given me a son.”
> Therefore called she his name Dan. (30:6)

***Dānannî ʾĕlōhîm*** — from ***dîn***, *to judge* — **and *Dān*.** The sense is not *God condemned
me* but *God found for me*: a ruling in her favour, in a dispute whose other party is her sister.
**[[Rachel]]'s two naming speeches are both scoreboard entries**, which is what makes them different in
kind from [[Leah]]'s — Leah names her sons for what she is still not getting, Rachel names hers for what
she has just won. **He is named by a woman who did not bear him, and [[Bilhah]] says nothing at his
birth.** See [[The Birth of Jacob's Children]] and [[Names and Naming]].

**The page is qualified `(person)` because [[Dan (city)]] already exists** — the northern town Abram
pursues to at 14:14, which Judges 18:29 says was renamed *"after the name of Dan their father"* when
the tribe captured it. **The city is named for this man**, five generations later, which is why Genesis
14:14 is the clearest anachronism in the book.

### What is still ahead

This page grows as those pericopes arrive; what follows is the shape of it.

Dan is listed among the twelve at 35:25 and 46:23 — where he has **one son**, Hushim, the smallest
entry in the register — and Jacob's blessing takes his name back to the root it came from:
*"**Dan** will **judge** his people, as one of the tribes of Israel. Dan will be a serpent in the way,
a viper in the path, that bites the horse's heels, so that his rider falls backward"* (49:16–17).
**Samson, the one judge the tribe produces, is a Danite** (Judges 13:2).

**And the tribe's later history is the darkest of the twelve.** Judges 18 has six hundred armed Danites
migrate north, steal a household's idols and its Levite on the way, and put up a graven image at Laish
which they rename Dan — *"the children of Dan set up the engraved image for themselves"* (18:30–31).
Jeroboam later puts one of his two golden calves there (1 Kings 12:29). **Dan is missing from the
tribal list in Revelation 7:5–8**, where Manasseh takes the twelfth slot, and the omission has never
been explained by any text. See
[[The Twelve Sons and the Lists That Reorder Them]] and
[[Bethel from Jacob's Pillar to Jeroboam's Calf]].
