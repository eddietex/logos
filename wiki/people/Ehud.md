# Ehud

## Overview

### The left-handed savior — Judges 3:12–30

*Yahweh raised up a savior for them, Ehud the son of Gera, the Benjamite, a left-handed man* (15). He
is the second savior of the judges cycle, raised up after Israel has served [[Eglon (person)]], king of
Moab, eighteen years. Israel sends its tribute to Eglon *by him*, so the man Yahweh has raised up is
also the one carrying Israel's payment.

He makes his own weapon, a two-edged sword *a cubit in length* (16; the Hebrew unit *gōmed* occurs
only here in the WLC files read, and its exact length is not known), and wears it *on his right
thigh*, where a left-handed man would draw it. He delivers the tribute, sends the carriers away, turns
back at *the stone idols that were by Gilgal*, and gains a private audience with two sentences: *I have
a secret message for you, O king*, and, alone with him in the upper room, *I have a message from God
to you* (19–20). Then *Ehud put out his left hand, and took the sword from his right thigh, and thrust
it into his body* (21). He locks the doors, escapes while the servants wait outside, blows a trumpet in
the hill country of Ephraim, and leads Israel down to hold the fords of the Jordan: *Follow me; for
Yahweh has delivered your enemies the Moabites into your hand* (28). About ten thousand Moabites die,
and *the land had rest eighty years* (30).

**What the text gives him and withholds.** He is called *môšîaʿ*, *savior* (15, WLC), but unlike
[[Othniel]] he is never said to have *judged Israel*, and the Spirit is never said to come on him. He
has a plan instead, and he does every step of it himself. The narrator does not comment on the
deception, and does not say whether *a message from God* was a lie, a grim truth, or both.

**Son of the right hand, restricted in the right hand.** *Ben-hayəmînî ʾîš ʾiṭṭēr yad-yəmînô* (15,
WLC). [[Benjamin]] means *son of the right hand*, and Ehud's right hand is *restricted*. The same
phrase describes Benjamin's seven hundred left-handed slingers in Judges 20:16. Whether it means a
disability, natural left-handedness, or a trained skill is not settled. See
[[Left-Handed Men of Benjamin - Judges 3.15 and 20.16]].

**His father.** *Gera* is one of Benjamin's sons in Genesis 46:21 (stored WEB), so *son of Gera* may
name a clan rather than a father. Gera has no page.

**After him.** *The children of Israel again did that which was evil in Yahweh's sight, when Ehud was
dead* (4:1, stored WEB). Shamgar is placed *after him* (3:31), though 4:1 dates the next relapse from
Ehud's death, not Shamgar's.

See [[Ehud and Eglon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 3:12–31; 4:1; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-13.

## Timeline / key events

- **Judges 3:15** — Raised up as savior; sent with Israel's tribute to Eglon.
- **Judges 3:16** — Makes a two-edged sword and straps it to his right thigh.
- **Judges 3:19–22** — Gains a private audience and kills Eglon with his left hand.
- **Judges 3:23–26** — Locks the doors and escapes past the stone idols to Seirah.
- **Judges 3:27–29** — Blows the trumpet, leads Israel to take the fords of the Jordan; ten thousand
  Moabites die.
- **Judges 3:30** — Moab subdued; eighty years of rest.
- **Judges 4:1** — His death is the marker for Israel's next relapse.
