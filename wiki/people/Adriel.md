# Adriel

## Overview

### Given Merab — 1 Samuel 18:19

*At the time when Merab, Saul's daughter, should have been given to David, she was given to Adriel the Meholathite as
wife* (18:19). He is named and does nothing; the verb is passive, *nittǝnâ*, *she was given* (WLC), and no reason is
offered for the change. *The Meholathite*, *hammǝḥōlāṯî*, makes him a man of Meholah, commonly taken to be
[[Abel Meholah]] (recalled, not checked). He is the man [[Merab]] goes to while her younger sister [[Michal]] goes to
[[David]]. See [[David Marries Michal]].

**Ahead (not yet ingested).** He is named again at 2 Samuel 21:8, as *Adriel the son of Barzillai the Meholathite*,
whose five sons David hands to the Gibeonites. The WLC there reads *the five sons of Michal the daughter of Saul, whom
she bore to Adriel* (WLC; paraphrased), while 2 Samuel 6:23 says Michal had no child to the day of her death (WLC;
paraphrased). Many translations read *Merab* at 21:8 (recalled, not checked). The tension is recorded on [[Michal]] and
[[Merab]] and not resolved. The wiki does not identify this Barzillai with any other man of the name.
