# Hamul

## Overview

**[[Perez]]'s second son, and one of the shortest careers in Scripture.**

> **12** … The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul. (46:12)

**He is named three times in the Bible and does nothing in any of them.** Genesis 46:12 lists him
among the seventy; **Numbers 26:21 counts the Hamulites as a clan of Judah**; 1 Chronicles 2:5
repeats the Genesis clause. **There is no fourth mention.**

**His brother [[Hezron (son of Perez)]] carries the line to David and the two Gospel genealogies.**
**Hamul carries a clan and then stops**, which is the ordinary fate of the great majority of the
seventy names in the register — **and the reason this wiki gives pages to only a handful of them.**
He has one here because he is one of the two people in the fourth generation of the list, and the
list only goes that deep once. See [[Names and Naming]].
