# Joash (father of Gideon)

## Overview

*Joash the Abiezrite*, of the clan of Abiezer in Manasseh, is Gideon's father. He is introduced by
what he owns. Yahweh's angel sits *under the oak which was in Ophrah, that belonged to Joash the
Abiezrite* (Judges 6:11), and the next pericope gives him an altar to Baal and an Asherah beside it
(6:25). The man whose oak hosts Yahweh's angel also keeps the town's Baal altar. In 6:31 he defends his
son against the town: *If he is a god, let him contend for himself*. That belongs to the next
pericope.

The name is qualified because later books have other men named Joash, kings of Judah and Israel among
them.

Source: stored WEB Judges 6:11, 25, 31.
