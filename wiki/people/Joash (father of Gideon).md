# Joash (father of Gideon)

## Overview

*Joash the Abiezrite*, of the clan of Abiezer in Manasseh, is Gideon's father. He is introduced by
what he owns. Yahweh's angel sits *under the oak which was in Ophrah, that belonged to Joash the
Abiezrite* (Judges 6:11), and the next pericope gives him an altar to Baal and an Asherah beside it
(6:25). The man whose oak hosts Yahweh's angel also keeps the town's Baal altar.

When Gideon throws that altar down by night with his father's bull, the town comes to Joash: *Bring out
your son, that he may die* (6:30). Joash does not hand him over. He turns on them: *Will you contend for
Baal? Or will you save him? He who will contend for him, let him be put to death by morning! If he is a
god, let him contend for himself, because someone has broken down his altar!* (6:31). Then he names his
son *Jerub-Baal*, *Let Baal contend against him* (6:32). The text does not say why he changed sides. He
may have been persuaded, or protecting his son, or turning the town's logic against it. His answer puts
the town's own death sentence, in the law's form *yûmat* (WLC), back on whoever pleads for Baal. A man
who kept a Baal altar is the first in Judges to say aloud that Baal cannot defend himself. See
[[Gideon Throws Down Baal's Altar]] and
[[Bring Him Out - Genesis 19.5, Deuteronomy 17.5, Judges 6.30, and Judges 19.22]].

The name is qualified because later books have other men named Joash, kings of Judah and Israel among
them.

Source: stored WEB Judges 6:11, 25–32. Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-15.

**Named in the enemy's camp (7:14).** The Midianite who interprets the dream of the barley loaf calls
the coming danger *the sword of Gideon the son of Joash, a man of Israel* (7:14). Joash's name is known
in Midian's camp, and the son he defended at Ophrah is named by it. See [[The Dream of the Barley Loaf]].
Source: stored WEB Judges 7:14.

## Timeline / key events

- Judges 6:11 — owner of the oak at Ophrah under which the angel sits.
- Judges 6:25 — owner of the Baal altar, the Asherah, and the bull.
- Judges 6:30–32 — refuses to give up his son; *If he is a god, let him contend for himself*; names him
  *Jerub-Baal*.
- Judges 7:14 — named by the Midianite interpreter: *Gideon the son of Joash*.
- Judges 8:13, 19 — *Gideon the son of Joash*; the brothers killed at Tabor are *the sons of my mother*.
