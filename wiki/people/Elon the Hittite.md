# Elon the Hittite

## Overview

**The father of Esau's second wife — and the one name in Genesis 26:34 that Genesis 36 keeps.**

> **34** When Esau was forty years old, he took as wife Judith, the daughter of Beeri the Hittite,
> and Basemath, the daughter of Elon the Hittite.

**He is named twice in the Bible and both times as somebody's father-in-law**, and the two verses do
not agree about which daughter he gave. At 26:34 she is **Basemath**; at 36:2 she is **Adah the
daughter of Elon the Hittite**. **The patronymic is stable and the name is not**, which is why the
divergence is usually taken as a matter of the registers rather than of the man. See
[[The Names of Esau's Wives - Genesis 26 and Genesis 36]].

***ʾÊlôn*** is **oak** or **terebinth** — the same word as the great trees Genesis pitches its scenes
under, *the oaks of Mamre* (13:18; 18:1) and *the oak of Moreh* (12:6). It is also a place name in
Dan's territory (Joshua 19:43), the name of a son of Zebulun (46:14), and the name of one of the
minor judges (Judges 12:11–12). **Nothing connects those to this man but the word.** See
[[The Trees of the Patriarchs]].

**Everything else about him is what the gentilic carries**: he is one of the *bənê-Ḥēt*, the resident
people of the hill country whom Abraham dealt with courteously for the cave at Machpelah and whose
daughters the marriage rule of 24:3 was designed to exclude. See [[The Children of Heth]] and
[[Not from the Daughters of the Canaanites]].
