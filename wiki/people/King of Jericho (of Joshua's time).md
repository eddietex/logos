# King of Jericho (of Joshua's time)

## Overview

### The fall of Jericho — Joshua 6:1–27

Yahweh includes Jericho’s king and mighty men in the announced delivery of the city (2). The account reports the city’s destruction (21) but does not give a separate scene of the king’s death or name him.

See [[The Fall of Jericho]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 6:1–27; comparisons identified above.


The unnamed ruler informed of Israelite spies in Jericho (2–3). He orders Rahab to hand them over. His agents pursue the supposed fugitives toward the Jordan, while she shelters them on her roof. No personal name or historical dynasty is supplied.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 2:1–24.

## Timeline / key events

- Receives intelligence and demands surrender of the spies (2–3).
- The pursuit fails after Rahab’s misleading report (4–7, 22).
