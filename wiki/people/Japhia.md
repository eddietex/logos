# Japhia

## Overview

King of Lachish, one of the five Amorite kings who attack Gibeon. Receives Adoni-Zedek’s appeal and
joins the five-king attack (3–5).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No personal history or dynasty is supplied here.
