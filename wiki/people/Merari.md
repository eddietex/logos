# Merari

## Overview

### Levitical cities — Joshua 21:1–42

His descendants receive twelve cities from Zebulun, Reuben, and Gad (7, 34–40).

See [[The Cities of the Levites]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 21:1–42.

### The second census — Numbers 26:1–65

The Merarites appear in the separate Levitical census (57).

See [[The Second Census]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 26.


**[[Levi]]'s third son, and the third name in the only clause Genesis gives him.**

> **11** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. (46:11)

**His name is built on the root *mrr*, *to be bitter*** — the root behind *Marah*, the undrinkable
water of Exodus 15:23, and behind Naomi's *call me Mara* in Ruth 1:20. **Genesis offers no naming
speech and no explanation**, which is unusual for the book; most of the names it stops to account for
belong to [[Jacob]]'s own sons, and the grandsons are simply listed.

**The Merarite house draws the tabernacle's heavy freight**: the frames, the bars, the pillars, the
sockets and the pegs (Numbers 3:33–37; 4:29–33) — **the parts that are neither holy enough to be
carried on the shoulder like [[Kohath]]'s ark nor light enough to be folded like [[Gershon]]'s
curtains.** They are given wagons and oxen for it (Numbers 7:8, [[The Offerings of the Twelve Princes]]). **The singer Ethan is traced to his
line** (1 Chronicles 6:44–47).

**In Genesis he is a name in a list of seventy, going into Egypt with his father.** See
[[Priesthood]].

### The Merarite house — Numbers 3:14–51

The Mahlites and Mushites number 6,200 (20, 33–34). They camp on the north under [[Zuriel (son of Abihail)]] (35) and keep the boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, and cords (36–37). Theirs is the smallest house here, and chapter 4 will show it the largest in working adults.

See [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 3.

### The frame — Numbers 4:29–33

Its 3,200 working men carry the boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, and cords, under the hand of [[Ithamar]] (31–33). It is the smallest house from a month old and the largest here, and only its instruments are assigned *by name* (32).

See [[The Service of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 4.

### Four wagons — Numbers 7:8

The Merarites receive four wagons and eight oxen, the largest share, for the frames, bars, and sockets of 4:31–32, under Ithamar.

See [[The Offerings of the Twelve Princes]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 7.

### The tabernacle moves — Numbers 10:17

His sons take the tabernacle after Judah's camp has gone, alongside Gershon's sons (17), and so travel ahead of the holy things.

See [[The March from Sinai]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 10.
