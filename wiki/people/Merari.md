# Merari

## Overview

**[[Levi]]'s third son, and the third name in the only clause Genesis gives him.**

> **11** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. (46:11)

**His name is built on the root *mrr*, *to be bitter*** — the root behind *Marah*, the undrinkable
water of Exodus 15:23, and behind Naomi's *call me Mara* in Ruth 1:20. **Genesis offers no naming
speech and no explanation**, which is unusual for the book; most of the names it stops to account for
belong to [[Jacob]]'s own sons, and the grandsons are simply listed.

**The Merarite house draws the tabernacle's heavy freight**: the frames, the bars, the pillars, the
sockets and the pegs (Numbers 3:33–37; 4:29–33) — **the parts that are neither holy enough to be
carried on the shoulder like [[Kohath]]'s ark nor light enough to be folded like [[Gershon]]'s
curtains.** They are given wagons and oxen for it (Numbers 7:8). **The singer Ethan is traced to his
line** (1 Chronicles 6:44–47).

**In Genesis he is a name in a list of seventy, going into Egypt with his father.** See
[[Priesthood]].

### The Merarite house — Numbers 3:14–51

The Mahlites and Mushites number 6,200 (20, 33–34). They camp on the north under [[Zuriel (son of Abihail)]] (35) and keep the boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, and cords (36–37). Theirs is the smallest house here, and chapter 4 will show it the largest in working adults.

See [[The Levites and the Firstborn Counted]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 3.
