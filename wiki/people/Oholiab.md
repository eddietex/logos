# Oholiab

## Overview

The son of [[Ahisamach]], of the tribe named for [[Dan (person)]], appointed by Yahweh alongside
[[Bezalel]] to make the sanctuary and its equipment (Exodus 31:6–11). The naming of a second worker
and the wider wise-hearted company prevents treating the work as Bezalel's alone. Chapter 31 gives
no separate list of Oholiab's special skills. Exodus 35:34–35 now adds teaching and skilled
making; 38:23 now describes his engraving and embroidery in [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]].

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The makers and the surplus — Exodus 35:30–36:7

Exodus 35:34 names him with Bezalel in the capacity to teach; 35:35 describes their skilled
workmanship. Moses then calls both with the wise-hearted workers (36:1–2). Oholiab belongs to
the team receiving the contributions, while the surplus report is the makers' collective act
(36:3–5). The new description supplies teaching and shared skills beyond his appointment in 31:6.

See [[Bezalel's Workmen and the Surplus]].

### The enclosure made — Exodus 36:8–38

The company commissioned with Oholiab in 36:1–2 now makes the curtains and framework (36:8–38).
He is not named in this unit, and no particular curtain, carving, or casting is assigned to him.
His participation follows the shared commission; it should not be turned into an invented
division of labor between the two named makers.

See [[The Tabernacle Built]].

### The furnishings made — Exodus 37:1–29

The furnishing account names Bezalel at 37:1 and continues with singular descriptions.
Oholiab remains part of the workforce commissioned in 36:1–2, but is not named in chapter 37.
Neither a particular furnishing nor the perfumer's work is assigned to him. Shared commission
and individual narrative credit must both be retained.

See [[The Furnishings Made]]. Source: the cited Exodus verses checked in local raw WEB, 2026-10-24.

### The outer equipment and inventory — Exodus 38:1–31

The inventory names him beside Bezalel as Ahisamach’s son of Dan, describing engraving, skilled
workmanship, and embroidery in blue, purple, scarlet, and linen (38:23). His specified abilities
enlarge the credit without assigning him a particular court hanging or reducing the wider workforce
to two men.

See [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]].

### The priestly garments made — Exodus 39:1–31

The embroidery and textile work reported in 39:1–31 fit the skills attributed to Oholiab
in 35:34–35 and 38:23. Nevertheless this pericope does not name him or assign him a particular
garment. His participation belongs to the shared commission of 36:1–2; the sash’s embroidery
(39:29) should not be turned into an individually signed work.

See [[The Priestly Garments Made]]. Source: cited Exodus verses checked in local raw WEB, 2026-10-25.

### The work completed — Exodus 39:32–43

The shared commission of 36:1–2 reaches the completion and inspection of 39:32–43.
Oholiab is not individually named here; the narrator credits the children of Israel. His earlier
place alongside Bezalel (38:23) supplies context without making him the sole presenter of the
finished articles or an individually named recipient of Moses’ blessing.

See [[The Work Completed]]. Source: cited passages checked in the stored WEB Genesis and Exodus
texts, 2026-10-25.
