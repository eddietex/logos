# Chemosh

## Overview

### *Chemosh your god* — Judges 11:24

*Won't you possess that which Chemosh your god gives you to possess? So whoever Yahweh our God has
dispossessed from before us, them will we possess* (11:24). Jephthah says this to **the king of Ammon**,
though Numbers 21:29 calls Moab *people of Chemosh*. In the WLC files read (Genesis–Deuteronomy, Joshua,
Judges) Chemosh occurs only in those two verses. **The text does not explain the attribution.** Possible
readings, none stated: the disputed land was once Moab's (Numbers 21:26), so Jephthah answers a Moabite
claim with Moab's god; Ammon and Moab act together elsewhere (Judges 3:13; Deuteronomy 23:3–4); or the
speaker's or narrator's naming is loose. Ammon's god is named elsewhere as Milcom or Molech (recalled
from 1 Kings 11; not stored, not verified). The argument grants Chemosh nothing: Judges 10:6 counts *the
gods of Moab* among Israel's sins. See [[Jephthah's History of the Journey - Numbers 20-22, Deuteronomy 2, and Judges 11]].

See [[Jephthah's Message to the King of Ammon]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 11:12–28; Numbers 21:26, 29; Judges 3:13; 10:6; Deuteronomy 23:3–4.

The deity whose people Moab is called in the quoted Heshbon poem (29). The poem portrays him as
giving sons into flight and daughters into captivity to Sihon. This is speech within the cited poem,
not an independent assertion that Chemosh acts alongside Yahweh.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.
