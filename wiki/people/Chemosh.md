# Chemosh

## Overview

The deity whose people Moab is called in the quoted Heshbon poem (29). The poem portrays him as
giving sons into flight and daughters into captivity to Sihon. This is speech within the cited poem,
not an independent assertion that Chemosh acts alongside Yahweh.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 21:21–35.
