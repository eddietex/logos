# Horam

## Overview

King of Gezer who comes to help Lachish. Joshua defeats him and his people, leaving him no remaining force (33). The verse does not narrate an assault upon Gezer itself.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:28–43. No modern identification or dynasty is assigned.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Counted as *the king of Gezer, one* (12:12), though Gezer's Canaanites remain (16:10). See [[Kings Struck and Cities Not Driven Out - Joshua 12, 15-17, and Judges 1]] and [[The Kings Struck Down]].
