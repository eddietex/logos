# Samson

## Overview

### Announced before conception — Judges 13:1–25

The last judge of the book is promised before he exists. Israel is *in the hand of the Philistines
forty years* (13:1), and for the first time in Judges no one cries out. [[The Angel of Yahweh]]
appears to the barren wife of [[Manoah]], a Danite of [[Zorah]], and tells her she will bear a son:
*No razor shall come on his head; for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb. He shall
begin to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines* (13:5). His mother reports it with *to the day
of his death* (13:7). His father asks what the child's *way of life and mission* will be, and gets no
answer (13:12–14).

He is born, *named … Samson*, and *The child grew, and Yahweh blessed him* (13:24). The chapter's last
verse is his first act of the Spirit: *Yahweh's Spirit began to move him in Mahaneh Dan, between Zorah
and Eshtaol* (13:25). *Began* is the verb of the promise, *he shall begin to save* (13:5, WLC
*ḥālal*). He is the only judge in the book whose birth is narrated, and the only one promised a
beginning rather than a rest.

Three things about him are fixed before he acts: he is a Nazirite by God's choice and not his own; he
is a Danite, of a tribe pressed into the hill country (1:34); and his work is half a work. See
[[The Announcement of Samson's Birth]],
[[Nazirite by Vow, Nazirite from the Womb - Numbers 6, Judges 13, and 1 Samuel 1]], and
[[He Shall Begin to Save - Judges 13.5 and the Philistine Wars of Samuel]].

*Šimšôn* (WLC lemma 8123) is usually connected with *šemeš*, *sun*; the connection is reported, not
checked, and the text gives no reason for the name.

### The Timnite marriage and the riddle — Judges 14:1–20

His first act is to see. *Samson went down to Timnah, and saw a woman … of the daughters of the
Philistines*, and tells his parents to get her for him (14:1–2). To their objection, *a wife of the
uncircumcised Philistines*, he answers *she pleases me well*, *hîʾ yāšərâ bəʿênāy*, *she is right in my
eyes* (14:3, WLC), the root of the book's last verse, *Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes*
(21:25). The narrator adds what no one in the story knows: *it was of Yahweh; for he sought an occasion
against the Philistines* (14:4).

On the road *Yahweh's Spirit came mightily on him*, and he tears a young lion with his bare hands (14:6).
Later he takes honey from its carcass, eats, and gives some to his parents, and tells them neither thing
(14:6, 9). He holds the wedding feast, puts a riddle to thirty Philistine companions, *Out of the eater
came out food. Out of the strong came out sweetness* (14:14), loses it when they threaten his wife and
she presses him for seven days, and pays the wager with the clothes of thirty men he strikes at
[[Ashkelon]] (14:15–19). *His anger burned*, he goes home, and his wife is given to his companion
(14:19–20).

The chapter shows the pattern of his life: a woman he chooses by sight, a secret, a woman who presses it
out of him, and a blow struck in anger that the narrator counts against the Philistines. The Spirit that
*began to move him* (13:25) now *came mightily on him*, *ṣālaḥ* (14:6, 19, WLC), a verb used in Judges
only of Samson and in 1 Samuel of Saul and David. Whether he broke the Nazirite rules at the carcass or
the feast, the text does not say. See [[Samson's Riddle]], [[Samson's Timnite Wife]],
[[Entice Him - The Timnite Wife and Delilah]], and
[[The Spirit Rushed on Him - Samson, Saul, and David]].

Source: stored WEB Judges 13:25; 14:1–20; 21:25; Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb), 2026-11-16.

### Ahead

His story runs to 16:31: the jawbone at Lehi (15), Gaza's gates
(16:1–3), Delilah (16:4–22), and his death in the temple of Dagon (16:23–31). He *judged Israel twenty
years in the days of the Philistines* (15:20; 16:31) and is buried *between Zorah and Eshtaol in the
burial site of Manoah his father* (16:31). Hebrews 11:32 names him among the faithful. These pages are
not yet ingested.

## Timeline / key events
- Announced to his mother by the angel of Yahweh; a Nazirite from the womb (Judges 13:3–7).
- Born and named; grows, and Yahweh blesses him (13:24).
- *Yahweh's Spirit began to move him* in Mahaneh Dan (13:25).
- Sees and chooses a Philistine woman of Timnah over his parents' objection (14:1–4).
- Tears a young lion when the Spirit comes on him; later eats honey from its carcass (14:5–9).
- Loses the wedding riddle, strikes thirty men of Ashkelon, and loses his wife (14:10–20).
