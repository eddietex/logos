# Abinadab (of Kiriath Jearim)

## Overview

### The ark in his house on the hill — 1 Samuel 7:1

When the men of [[Beth Shemesh]] send for help after the slaughter at the ark, *the men of Kiriath Jearim
came, and took Yahweh's ark, and brought it into Abinadab's house on the hill, and consecrated Eleazar his son
to keep Yahweh's ark* (7:1). That is all the text says of him: his house, its location *on the hill*,
*baggiḇʿâ* (WLC), and his son [[Eleazar (son of Abinadab)]]. He is not called a priest or a Levite, and the
text gives no reason why his house was chosen. See [[The Ark Returns to Israel]].

**Later.** The ark stays in Kiriath Jearim *twenty years* (7:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested). When David
brings it out, it is *out of Abinadab's house that was on the hill*, on a new cart driven by *Uzzah and Ahio,
the sons of Abinadab* (2 Samuel 6:3, WEB checked at bible-api.com; WLC *baggiḇʿâ* again). Whether Uzzah and
Ahio are brothers of Eleazar or the name stands for the household is not stated.

**Name.** *ʾĂḇînāḏāḇ*, conventionally *my father is noble* or *generous*. Two other men in 1 Samuel bear it,
Jesse's second son (16:8) and a son of Saul (31:2, stored WEB; not yet ingested); hence the qualifier.
