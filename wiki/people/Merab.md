# Merab

## Overview

### The firstborn daughter — 1 Samuel 14:49

*The names of his two daughters were these: the name of the firstborn Merab, and the name of the younger Michal*
(14:49). *Habbəḵîrâ*, *the firstborn*, and *haqqəṭannâ*, *the younger* (WLC), are the words Laban used of Leah and
Rachel: *It is not done so in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn* (Genesis 29:26, stored WEB;
WLC). Merab does nothing in the summary. See [[Saul's Wars and Household]].

**Ahead (not yet ingested).** Saul offers her to David, *Only be valiant for me, and fight Yahweh's battles*, and
privately hopes the Philistines will kill him; at the time she should have been given, *she was given to Adriel
the Meholathite as wife* (18:17–19, stored WEB). Then her younger sister loves David (18:20). As with Leah and
Rachel, the elder is given and the younger is loved, though here the elder goes to another man.

Source: stored WEB 1 Samuel 14:49; 18:17–20; Genesis 29:26. Hebrew WLC (OpenScriptures morphhb).
