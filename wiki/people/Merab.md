# Merab

## Overview

### The firstborn daughter — 1 Samuel 14:49

*The names of his two daughters were these: the name of the firstborn Merab, and the name of the younger Michal*
(14:49). *Habbəḵîrâ*, *the firstborn*, and *haqqəṭannâ*, *the younger* (WLC), are the words Laban used of Leah and
Rachel: *It is not done so in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn* (Genesis 29:26, stored WEB;
WLC). Merab does nothing in the summary. See [[Saul's Wars and Household]].

### Offered, and given to another — 1 Samuel 18:17–19

Saul offers her to David as *my elder daughter Merab*, *bittî haggǝḏôlâ* (18:17, WLC), the narrator's word for Leah
(Genesis 29:16), on the condition *Only be valiant for me, and fight Yahweh's battles*, and privately hopes *the hand
of the Philistines* will kill him (18:17). David answers *Who am I … that I should be son-in-law to the king?* (18:18).
Then, *at the time when Merab, Saul's daughter, should have been given to David, she was given to Adriel the
Meholathite as wife* (18:19). The verb is passive, *nittǝnâ* (WLC); no giver and no reason is named. Her younger sister
loves David (18:20) and is given to him (18:27). As with Leah and Rachel, the elder is given and the younger is loved,
though here the elder goes to another man. See [[David Marries Michal]], [[Adriel]], and
[[The Younger Before the Firstborn - Jacob and Laban]].

**2 Samuel 21:8.** The WLC names *Michal* as the mother of Adriel's five sons; many translations read *Merab* (recalled,
not checked). It is recorded on [[Michal]] and here, and not resolved.

**The king's daughter promised.** At the battle line Israel's soldiers say the king *will give him his daughter* to
the man who kills Goliath (17:25); the daughter is not named. Merab is the first Saul offers David (18:17), but 18:17
gives a different condition, *be valiant for me*, and does not refer back to 17:25 (stored WEB). See [[David Comes to the Camp]].
