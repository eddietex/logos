# Jochebed

## Overview

**The mother of Moses, who obeys Pharaoh's decree to the letter and defeats it.** In the passage
where she acts she has no name — she is *"a daughter of Levi"* (Exodus 2:1), *"the woman"* (2:2, 9),
*"the child's mother"* (2:8). Exodus gives her name only in the genealogy: *"Amram took Jochebed his
father's sister to himself as wife; and she bore him Aaron and Moses"* (6:20). The wiki uses the name
for the page and keeps what 2:1–10 says of her distinct from what 6:20 and Numbers 26:59 add.

**Everything she does in 2:1–10 is an act of concealment or construction.** She *sees that he is
good* — *kî-ṭôb*, the verdict of the creation week (Genesis 1:4) — and hides him three months. When
she can no longer hide him she makes a *tēbâ*, an ark, of papyrus, seals it with tar and pitch, and
*puts* him into the river Pharaoh ordered him *cast* into (1:22; 2:3). **She does what the law says
and not what it means.** Then she is summoned by her daughter, handed her own son by Pharaoh's
daughter, and paid to nurse him (2:9). **No text says she told the princess who she was.** See
[[The Birth of Moses]] and [[Deception]].

**Hebrews gives the hiding to both parents and calls it faith**: *"By faith, Moses, when he was born,
was hidden for three months by his parents, because they saw that he was a beautiful child, and they
were not afraid of the king's commandment"* (Hebrews 11:23). Exodus gives it to her alone and gives
no reason beyond what she saw. See [[Faith]].

**Hers is the first personal name in Scripture that appears to contain the divine name** if, as is
usually proposed, *Yôkebed* means *Yahweh is glory* — a name borne by Moses' mother before the name *Yahweh* is revealed to Moses at
the bush (3:14–15; 6:3). The etymology is the usual one and is not certain; the wiki records it as
such. See [[The Names of God]].

### Daughter of Levi, and her husband's aunt

**Numbers 26:59 reads 2:1 literally**: *"The name of Amram's wife was Jochebed, the daughter of Levi,
who was born to Levi in Egypt."* On that reading she is [[Levi]]'s own daughter and Jacob's
granddaughter, and Exodus 6:20 makes her the sister of Amram's father [[Kohath]] — **a marriage
to an aunt that Leviticus 18:12 later forbids.** The Torah records the marriage without comment.

The literal reading also compresses the sojourn: Levi's daughter, born in Egypt, bearing the man who
leads Israel out, leaves very little room for Exodus 12:40's *"four hundred thirty years"*. The
tension is set out, not resolved, in [[Four Hundred Years - The Sojourn and the Exodus]].

### Exodus 6:20 — named

**The genealogy gives her name for the first time**, as Amram's wife and the mother of *"Aaron and
Moses"*. ***Dōdātô***, *"his father's sister"* in the WEB, is literally *his aunt*; in Leviticus the
word's two uses name an uncle's wife (18:14; 20:20), and the father's sister has its own phrase
(18:12) (all checked in the WLC). **WEB's rendering follows Numbers 26:59**, which makes her Levi's
daughter. **Either reading puts the marriage inside a degree Leviticus forbids.** She is one of three
wives the list names, and the only one identified by her relation to her husband rather than by her
father. See [[The Heads of the Fathers' Houses]].

## Timeline / key events
