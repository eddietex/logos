# Cushite Woman (of Moses' time)

## Overview

The wife of Moses named as the reason Miriam and Aaron spoke against him (Numbers 12:1). She has no name in the verse, she speaks no words, and she appears only as
a phrase. The verse says twice that he married her: *the Cushite woman whom he had married; for he had married a Cushite woman*.

**Whether she is [[Zipporah]] is not settled.** She may be Moses' Midianite wife under another description, as the pairing of Cushan and Midian at Habakkuk 3:7 suggests, or a second wife otherwise unmentioned.
This page is kept apart from Zipporah's so that the filename does not decide the point. See [[Who Is the Cushite Woman - Numbers 12.1, Exodus 2, and Habakkuk 3]].

**What was wrong with the marriage is not said.** Yahweh's reply does not mention her at all. It deals only with the claim of verse 2.
