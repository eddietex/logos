# The Perizzites

## Overview

### Judah goes up first — Judges 1:1–7

Yahweh delivers *the Canaanites and the Perizzites* into Judah's hand at Bezek, ten thousand men
struck (4–5). The pairing goes back to *The Canaanites and the Perizzites lived in the land at that
time* (Genesis 13:7) and to Jacob's fear of being *odious … among the Canaanites and the Perizzites*
after Simeon and Levi's sword (Genesis 34:30). Simeon now fights them beside Judah.

See [[Judah Goes Up First]]. Source: stored WEB Judges 1:4–5; comparisons as cited.

### The covenant at Shechem — Joshua 24:1–28

Listed among the peoples who fought against Israel with *the men of Jericho* (11) and were delivered into Israel's hand.

See [[The Covenant at Shechem]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 24:1–28; comparisons as cited.

A people whose land Joshua names alongside that of [[The Rephaim]] as forest for Joseph’s
descendants to clear (Joshua 17:15). No individual is named and no new encounter is narrated.

Source: stored WEB Joshua 17:14–18.
