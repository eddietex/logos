# Kemuel (son of Shiphtan)

## Overview

Son of Shiphtan, appointed to represent the tribe associated with [[Ephraim]] in the
division of Canaan (Numbers 34:24). His commission is specified, but no speech,
individual allotment, or further biography is given here. The patronymic is retained
without identifying him with another bearer of the name.
