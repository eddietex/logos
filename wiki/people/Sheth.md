# Sheth

## Overview

Named in the WEB expression “all the sons of Sheth” in Numbers 24:17, among those the future ruler
will strike. The identity and scope are uncertain in this passage. This page does not silently
equate Sheth with Seth of Genesis 4–5.

Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.
