# Bedan

## Overview

A deliverer named once, in Samuel's farewell, between Gideon and Jephthah: *Yahweh sent Jerubbaal, Bedan,
Jephthah, and Samuel, and delivered you out of the hand of your enemies on every side; and you lived in safety*
(1 Samuel 12:11, stored WEB). The Hebrew is *Bədān* (WLC). No judge of that name appears in Judges, and in the WLC
files of Genesis through 2 Samuel the name occurs only here (lemma 917). A Bedan is listed in a Manassite
genealogy at 1 Chronicles 7:17 (recalled, not checked; 1 Chronicles is not stored), which says nothing of
judging.

The text does not explain the name, and the wiki does not resolve it. Explanations commonly reported, all recalled
and not checked here, with no version consulted:

- Some ancient versions, including the Septuagint and Syriac, are said to read *Barak*, which would give the list
  Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, Samuel, with the first two out of Judges' order. See [[Barak]].
- Some have proposed *Abdon* (Judges 12:13–15), whose name shares letters with *Bedan*. See
  [[The Minor Judges - Judges 10.1-5 and 12.8-15]].
- A rabbinic reading takes *Bedan* as *ben-Dan*, *the son of Dan*, meaning Samson of the tribe of Dan. See
  [[Samson]].

What the text does show is that Samuel's list is not a copy of Judges: it names *Jerubbaal*, not Gideon; it puts
Sisera before the king of Moab (12:9); and it ends with the speaker himself.
