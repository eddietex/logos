# Beor (father of Balaam)

## Overview

### Balaam’s last oracles — Numbers 23:27–24:25

Balaam identifies himself as Beor’s son in both oracle introductions (24:3, 15). Beor is a
patronymic reference, not an actor in the scene.

See [[Balaam's Last Oracles]]. Source: stored WEB Numbers 23:27–24:25.
