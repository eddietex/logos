# The Horites

## Overview

Earlier inhabitants of Seir, displaced and destroyed before Esau’s descendants (Deuteronomy 2:12,
22). Verse 22 attributes their removal to Yahweh; verse 12 describes the successors’ action. This
collective page is distinct from Seir the Horite, the named ancestor in Genesis 36.

Source: stored WEB Deuteronomy 2:1–23.
