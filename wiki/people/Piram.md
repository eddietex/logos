# Piram

## Overview

### The five kings at Makkedah — Joshua 10:16–27

One of the five kings hidden in the cave at Makkedah, brought before Joshua, humiliated beneath the
commanders’ feet, killed, and displayed on a tree until evening (16–27). The bodies are returned to
the cave under great stones. Verse 23 identifies the rulers by their cities; their personal names
come from 10:3.

See [[The Five Kings in the Cave at Makkedah]]. Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:16–27.


King of Jarmuth, one of the five Amorite kings who attack Gibeon. Receives Adoni-Zedek’s appeal and
joins the five-king attack (3–5).

Source: stored WEB Joshua 10:1–15. No personal history or dynasty is supplied here.

**Joshua 12:1–24 — the list of kings.** Counted by city as *the king of Jarmuth, one* (12:11). See [[The Kings Struck Down]].
