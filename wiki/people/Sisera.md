# Sisera

## Overview

### In the Song — Judges 5:20, 26–30

The Song names Sisera three times. *From the sky the stars fought. From their courses, they fought
against Sisera* (5:20). *With the hammer she struck Sisera. She struck through his head* (5:26), and
*at her feet he bowed, he fell* (5:27). And *Sisera's mother* waits for him at the window, imagining
*to Sisera a plunder of dyed garments* (5:28–30). The Song never names his master Jabin; its enemy
is *the kings of Canaan* (5:19). How the Song's account of his death relates to 4:21, where he is
asleep, is weighed on [[The Song of Deborah]] and [[Jael]] and left open.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:21; 5:19–30.

### The tent — Judges 4:17–22

Sisera runs on foot *to the tent of Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite; for there was peace between
Jabin the king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite* (4:17). He goes where his king has a treaty.
Jael invites him in, *don't be afraid*, covers him, and gives him milk when he asks for water (4:18–19).
He tells her to stand at the tent door and say *No* if anyone asks *Is there any man here?* (4:20). The
general who commanded nine hundred chariots ends giving orders to one woman, and the lie he gives her
turns out true. *He was in a deep sleep*, and she drives a tent peg through his temple into the ground
(4:21). Barak arrives to find him *dead, and the tent peg was in his temples* (4:22). Deborah's word
that *Yahweh will sell Sisera into a woman's hand* (4:9) is fulfilled. The Song tells the death
differently (5:26–27; next pericope); see [[Jael and Sisera]], Notes.

See [[Jael and Sisera]] and [[Into a Woman's Hand - Judges 4.9, 4.21, and 9.53-54]]. Source: stored
WEB Judges 4:17–24; 5:26–27; Hebrew WLC.

*The captain of [Jabin's] army*, *who lived in Harosheth of the Gentiles* (Judges 4:2), with *nine
hundred chariots of iron* (4:3, 13). He is the real power in the oppression; Jabin is named, and
Sisera acts. Yahweh's command says *I will draw to you, to the river Kishon, Sisera* (4:7), and when
Sisera hears that Barak has gone up Tabor he gathers his chariots to the Kishon (4:12–13), doing what
Yahweh said he would make him do. *Yahweh confused Sisera, all his chariots, and all his army* (4:15).
*Sisera abandoned his chariot and fled away on his feet*. His whole army falls; he escapes.

Deborah's word that *Yahweh will sell Sisera into a woman's hand* (4:9) hangs over the end of the
pericope. He is running toward the tent of Heber the Kenite (4:17; next pericope).

Later texts remember him: 1 Samuel 12:9 has Yahweh *sold them into the hand of Sisera, captain of the
army of Hazor*, and Psalm 83:9 prays *as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the river Kishon* (both WEB,
checked at bible-api.com). The name does not look Hebrew; no derivation is offered here.

Source: stored WEB Judges 4:1–16.
