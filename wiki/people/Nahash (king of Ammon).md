# Nahash (king of Ammon)

## Overview

The Ammonite who besieges Jabesh Gilead at the start of Saul's reign. 1 Samuel 11 calls him only *Nahash the
Ammonite*, *nāḥāš hāʿammônî* (11:1–2, WLC); Samuel's farewell calls him *Nahash the king of the children of Ammon*
(12:12, stored WEB). The page is qualified because 2 Samuel 17:25 names a different Nahash, the father of Abigail
(WEB checked at bible-api.com, 2026-11-18). The name is spelled with the same letters as *nāḥāš*, *serpent*
(Genesis 3:1, WLC), though the lexical data gives the proper name its own lemma (5176); the text makes nothing of
it.

### The right eyes of Jabesh — 1 Samuel 11:1–11

He comes up and camps against [[Jabesh Gilead]]. Offered the town's service under a covenant, he sets as its
condition *that all your right eyes be gouged out. I will make this dishonor all Israel* (11:2). His aim, as he
states it, is the *reproach*, *ḥerpâ*, of all Israel, not only the town (WLC). He grants the elders seven days
to look for someone *to save* them (11:3); the narrative does not say why he allows it. Saul's army breaks into
his camp in the morning watch and strikes the Ammonites *until the heat of the day*, so that *no two of them were
left together* (11:11). Nahash's own fate is not told. See [[Saul Rescues Jabesh Gilead]] and
[[The Reproach of Israel - Joshua 5.9, 1 Samuel 11.2, and 17.26]].

**Recorded, not resolved — the occasion of the request for a king.** Samuel will say *When you saw that Nahash the
king of the children of Ammon came against you, you said to me, 'No, but a king shall reign over us'* (12:12,
stored WEB; not yet ingested). In the narrative order, the elders asked for a king at 8:1–5 because Samuel was
old and his sons were corrupt, and Nahash first appears after Saul is chosen (10:17–27). The text leaves both
accounts standing, and so does the wiki.

**Ahead** (not yet ingested): at 2 Samuel 10:1–2 *the king of the children of Ammon died, and Hanun his son
reigned*, and David says *I will show kindness to Hanun the son of Nahash, as his father showed kindness to me*;
at 17:27 *Shobi the son of Nahash of Rabbah of the children of Ammon* brings supplies to David (WEB checked at
bible-api.com, 2026-11-18). Whether that Nahash is this one the text does not say; the wiki will decide when it
reaches 2 Samuel 10.
