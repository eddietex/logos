# Kenan

## Overview

The fourth generation from Adam, and one of the six names in the Sethite register that has a
near-twin in the line of Cain. He does nothing. The whole of him is a single entry in the machinery
of Genesis 5: born to [[Enosh]] when Enosh was 90, father of [[Mahalalel]] at 70, dead at 910
(5:9–14). No act, no word, no place, no comment.

His name is the interesting thing about him. *Qênān* shares its consonantal root with *Qayin* —
[[Cain]] — which puts the name of the first murderer, in a slightly extended form, in the fourth
slot of the line of promise. The same root in cognate Semitic languages carries the sense of
*smith* or *metalworker*, which is also where the name Tubal Cain comes from. Whether the overlap
between the two genealogies is transmission, design, or a small ancient name-pool is set out on
[[Two Lines from Adam - Cain's Genealogy and Seth's]]; what is not in dispute is that Genesis 5
records the name without a flicker of acknowledgement that the reader has met something very like
it two paragraphs earlier.

He appears in the Bible three more times, always as a name in a list: 1 Chronicles 1:2, and Luke
3:37 (as *Cainan*), where he is a link in the chain running from Jesus back to Adam.
