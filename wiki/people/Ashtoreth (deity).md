# Ashtoreth (deity)

## Overview

A goddess, named in the plural as *the Ashtaroth*, *ʿaštārôt*, beside Baal in Judges 2:13 (WLC;
stored WEB): *They abandoned Yahweh, and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.* The plural is commonly
taken, like *the Baals*, as the goddess under many local forms. She is usually identified with the
goddess known in Greek as Astarte. Both points are reported as common readings and are not checked
here. The singular form, *Ashtoreth*, is used as the page title for the goddess herself. It does not
occur in the text ingested so far; that later books use it (Kings is the usual citation) is recalled,
not checked, since those books are not yet stored.

The same word is the name of a city: [[Ashtaroth]], Og's royal city in Bashan (Joshua 12:4; 13:12,
31). The city and the goddess are kept on separate pages, and this one is qualified `(deity)` so that
neither takes the bare name of the other.

Source: stored WEB Judges 2:13. Hebrew from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (OpenScriptures morphhb),
2026-11-13.
