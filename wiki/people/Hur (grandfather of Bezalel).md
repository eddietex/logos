# Hur (grandfather of Bezalel)

## Overview

The grandfather of [[Bezalel]] and father of [[Uri]], in the genealogy identifying the appointed
craftsman with Judah (Exodus 31:2). **His identity with [[Hur (companion of Moses)]] is possible but
unproved:** Exodus does not connect this genealogy to the companion of 17:10–12 and 24:14. The
separate page preserves that uncertainty rather than asserting two certainly distinct men.

1 Chronicles 2:19–20 names Caleb and Ephrath as this Hur's parents and repeats the line
Hur–Uri–Bezalel (WEB verified from bible-api.com, 2026-10-24; not yet ingested). It does not
expressly identify him as Moses' companion.

See [[Bezalel, Oholiab, and the Sabbath Sign]].

### The makers and the surplus — Exodus 35:30–36:7

Moses repeats Hur's place as Bezalel's grandfather in the Judahite genealogy (35:30).
The repetition supplies no identification with Moses' companion from 17:10–12 and 24:14.
The earlier uncertainty remains; neither a new office nor a role in the work is assigned to Hur.

See [[Bezalel's Workmen and the Surplus]].

### The outer equipment and inventory — Exodus 38:1–31

Exodus 38:22 repeats the lineage Bezalel–Uri–Hur. It gives Hur no action and supplies no
identification with Hur, Moses’ companion in 17:10–12 and 24:14. That possible identity remains
unproved.

See [[The Altar, the Basin, the Court, and the Inventory]].
